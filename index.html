  FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?
We support MP3, WAV, OGG for voice; JPG, PNG, WEBP for images; MP4, MOV, AVI for video; and raw text or PDF for text analysis. URL scanning works on any public web link.
How accurate is the detection?
Our ensemble models achieve over 99% accuracy on standard benchmark datasets. We continuously update models to detect the latest generative AI techniques including GPT-4, Sora, Stable Diffusion, and ElevenLabs cloning.
Do you store my uploaded files?
No. All uploaded media is processed in memory and immediately discarded after analysis. We never store your files or use your data for model training. Your privacy is our priority.
Can I integrate VoiceCheck into my own application?
Yes — our Enterprise plan includes full REST API access with authentication, webhooks, and SDKs for Node.js, Python, and Go. Check our API documentation for endpoint details.
How fast is the analysis?
Most text and image scans complete in under 2 seconds. Audio clips up to 60s typically complete in 3–5 seconds. Video analysis depends on file size but usually finishes in 10–30 seconds.
Is there a free tier?
Yes — the Starter plan is completely free with 10 scans per month and no credit card required. Create an account and start detecting immediately.
