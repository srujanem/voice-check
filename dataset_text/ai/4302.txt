There are a few reasons why artists might still release singles, even though people can listen to individual songs on streaming services.\n\nOne reason is that releasing a single can help an artist build excitement for an upcoming album. If an artist releases a single, it can be featured on music charts and get played on the radio, which can help the artist gain new fans.\n\nFinally, releasing a single can also be a way for an artist to experiment with new sounds or styles.
