A racket is a type of illegal business or scheme that is typically run by a group of people. Rackets often involve fraud, bribery, or other types of dishonest or unethical behavior. One example of a racket is a protection racket, where a group of people threaten to cause harm to a business or individual unless they pay the group a fee for "protection." Another example is a price-fixing racket, where a group of businesses collude to artificially inflate the prices of goods or services. Rackets can be difficult to detect and disrupt because they often involve people in positions of power or authority who use their influence to cover up their illegal activities. However, law enforcement agencies and regulatory bodies work to investigate and prosecute individuals and organizations involved in racketeering.
