Contemporary philosophy is the study of ideas and issues that are important in the present day. There are many different areas of philosophy, and many different problems that philosophers are interested in. Some philosophers might be interested in questions about the nature of reality, or what it means for something to be true. Others might be interested in questions about how we should live our lives, or how we should treat other people. There is no one common problem that a lot of people are trying to solve, but rather many different problems that different philosophers are interested in.
