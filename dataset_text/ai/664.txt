Athletes who attack other athletes in the middle of games are usually punished by the rules of the game and the governing bodies of their sports. For example, in hockey, if a player hits another player in the nuts with their stick, they might receive a penalty or be ejected from the game. These punishments are meant to deter players from engaging in dangerous or unsportsmanlike behavior. 
In some cases, an athlete's actions during a game might be considered criminal, such as if they intentionally hurt another player in a way that goes beyond the rules of the game. In these situations, the police might be called to investigate and the athlete could potentially face criminal charges. However, it is not common for athletes to be arrested on the spot during a game. 
Overall, the goal of punishing athletes who engage in dangerous or unsportsmanlike behavior is to keep the game safe and fair for all players and to maintain the integrity of the sport.
