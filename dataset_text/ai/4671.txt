A mechanical watch is a type of timepiece that uses a mechanical movement to keep time. This means that it has a series of gears and springs inside of it that work together to power the watch and keep it running.\n\nOver time, these gears and springs can become worn out or lose some of their tension. When this happens, the watch may not run as accurately as it did when it was new. This can cause the watch to lose time or gain time, depending on the specific issue with the movement.\n\nIn addition, external factors such as temperature, humidity, and even the position of the watch can also affect the accuracy of a mechanical watch. For example, if a watch is stored in a very cold place, the oils used to lubricate the movement may become thicker and cause the watch to run slower.\n\nOverall, the reason a mechanical watch can change its time over a long period of time is due to the mechanical movement inside of it and the various factors that can affect its accuracy.
