Being high is a feeling that people sometimes get when they use certain drugs. These drugs are called psychoactive drugs because they can change the way a person thinks, feels, and behaves. When someone is high, they might feel very happy, relaxed, or dizzy. They might see things that are not really there or hear things that are not really happening. They might also feel hungry or have trouble remembering things. The scientific explanation for being high is that the drug changes the way certain chemicals in the brain work. These chemicals, called neurotransmitters, are responsible for carrying messages between nerve cells. When someone is high, the drug can interfere with the way these neurotransmitters work, which can change the way the person thinks and feels. It's important to note that drugs can be very dangerous, and it is never safe to use them without a doctor's supervision.
