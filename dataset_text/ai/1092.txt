Manga is a style of Japanese comics and graphic novels that originated in Japan in the late 19th century. The characters in manga often have exaggerated facial features, such as large eyes, as a way to express emotion and personality. This artistic style is known as "big eyes, small mouth," and it is a common feature of manga and anime characters. 
The large eyes of manga characters are not meant to be realistic, but rather they are used to convey the inner thoughts and emotions of the characters. Some people find this style of art appealing because it allows the characters to express a wide range of emotions through their facial expressions. Others may not like this style as much because it is not as realistic as other forms of art. 
Overall, the appearance of manga characters is a matter of personal preference, and different people will have different opinions on whether they like the style or not.
