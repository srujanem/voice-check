Money laundering is the process of hiding or disguising the proceeds of illegal activities as legitimate money. It is often done to make it difficult for law enforcement agencies to trace the source of the money, which could be used as evidence in a criminal investigation or prosecution.\n\nBusinesses can sometimes benefit from money laundering because they may receive the illegal proceeds as payment for goods or services, which can increase their profits. However, it is important to note that participating in money laundering is illegal and can have serious consequences for businesses and individuals, including fines and imprisonment.
