Google has a searchable version of the internet because they have special computers called "servers" that are always connected to the internet. These servers are really powerful and can do a lot of things really quickly. When you search for something on Google, the servers go and look for websites that might have the answer you're looking for. The servers are also really good at remembering things. They can save a copy of almost every website on the internet so that they can look at it again later if they need to. This way, when you search for something on Google, the servers can look at the copy of the website they saved instead of having to go out onto the internet and find the website all over again. This is how Google can have a searchable version of the internet that isn't the same size as the internet. It's kind of like how you might have a bunch of books in your room that you can read anytime you want, instead of having to go to the library every time you want to read something.
