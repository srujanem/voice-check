Fractals are shapes that repeat themselves over and over again at different scales. They can be found in many different places in the natural world, such as in the shapes of trees, mountains, rivers, and even in the patterns of leaves on a plant. One way that fractals are used in the real world is in the design of computer graphics and animations. Fractals can be used to create realistic-looking landscapes, trees, and other objects that are difficult to create using traditional geometric shapes. Fractals are also used in the field of medicine to study the shapes of blood vessels and other structures in the body. By understanding how these structures are organized, doctors can better understand how the body works and how to treat diseases. Fractals are also used in engineering and architecture to design buildings and other structures that are more resistant to wind, earthquakes, and other natural disasters. By using fractal-based designs, these structures can be stronger and more stable. Overall, fractals are a powerful tool that can be used to understand and model complex systems in the natural world, and they have many practical applications in fields such as computer graphics, medicine, and engineering.
