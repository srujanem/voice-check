American football is a sport played by two teams of eleven players each. The teams take turns trying to move an oval-shaped ball down a long field by either throwing it or running with it. The team with the ball is trying to score points by getting the ball into the other team's end zone, while the other team is trying to stop them and take control of the ball. Each team has four chances, or "downs," to move the ball at least ten yards. If they succeed, they get four more downs to try and move the ball another ten yards. If they don't succeed, the other team gets a chance to try and move the ball. Points can be scored in a few different ways: A touchdown is worth six points and is scored when a player carries the ball across the opponent's goal line or catches a pass in the end zone. A field goal is worth three points and is scored when a player kicks the ball through a set of goalposts. An extra point is worth one point and is scored by kicking the ball through the goalposts after a touchdown. The team with the most points at the end of the game wins.
