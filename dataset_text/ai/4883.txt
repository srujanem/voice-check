The dent or semi-circle at the bottom of a bottle is called a punt. It is there to help strengthen the bottle and make it more stable. It also helps to distribute the weight of the liquid more evenly, which can help to prevent the bottle from tipping over or falling over. The punt is not there to prevent the bottle from exploding. However, it can help to make the bottle stronger and more resistant to damage, which may help to reduce the risk of the bottle breaking or cracking.
