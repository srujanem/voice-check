House cats can have sudden bursts of energy for a variety of reasons. They might be feeling playful or curious, or they might be trying to burn off excess energy. Sometimes they do this because they are instinctually hunting, even if they are well-fed domestic pets and don't actually need to hunt for food. These bursts of energy are completely normal and are just a part of a cat's nature. Large, exotic cats like tigers can also have sudden bursts of energy, but it's important to remember that they are wild animals and should be treated with caution and respect.
