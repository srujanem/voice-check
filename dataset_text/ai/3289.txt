If an astronaut goes into space without a space suit, they would not survive for very long. There is no air in space, so the astronaut would begin to suffocate almost immediately. They would also be exposed to the harsh elements of space, including extreme temperatures and radiation, which would also be harmful or lethal. To explain this in simpler terms: an astronaut needs a space suit to breathe and stay safe in space. Without a space suit, an astronaut would not be able to breathe and would quickly become very sick or die.
