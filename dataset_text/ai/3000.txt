Free web browsers like Opera generate income through advertising and partnerships. When you use a free web browser, you may see ads displayed on the browser or on websites you visit. These ads are paid for by companies who want to promote their products or services. When you click on one of these ads, the company pays the web browser a small fee. This is one way that free web browsers like Opera can generate income. Opera may also have partnerships with other companies. For example, they may agree to include links to a particular company's website in the browser, or to feature that company's products or services in some way. In exchange, the company will pay Opera a fee. Additionally, some web browsers offer premium features that users can pay for. For example, Opera has a feature called Opera VPN, which allows users to browse the internet securely and privately. Users can pay a fee to access this feature. Overall, free web browsers like Opera generate income through a combination of advertising, partnerships, and paid features.
