There are a few reasons why humans have not gone back to the moon in the same way that they did during the Apollo missions of the 1960s and 1970s. One reason is that the Apollo program was a very expensive and ambitious undertaking that was funded by the United States government. After the last Apollo mission in 1972, the government decided to focus on other priorities and funding for moon missions was greatly reduced. Another reason is that sending humans back to the moon is a very complex and difficult task. It requires a lot of resources, including money, technology, and trained personnel. There are also many safety considerations that must be taken into account when sending humans into space. Despite these challenges, there has been some renewed interest in going back to the moon in recent years. In 2019, NASA announced plans to send humans back to the moon by 2024 as part of a program called Artemis. However, these plans are still in the early stages and it is not yet clear when or if humans will actually return to the moon.
