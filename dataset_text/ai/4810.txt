Inflation is when the prices of goods and services go up over time. It can happen for a variety of reasons, but one of the main reasons is when a country is printing too much money. When a country has a lot of money in circulation, but not enough goods and services to match, it can lead to higher prices because people are willing to pay more for the same goods and services. This is especially true if the country is in economic trouble and people are worried that their money won't be worth as much in the future. In the case of Venezuela, their inflation rate is so high because the government has been printing a lot of money to try to fix their economic problems, but it has only made things worse.
