Nikolai Tesla and Thomas Edison were both famous scientists and inventors who lived in the late 1800s and early 1900s. Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor who is known for his contributions to the design of the modern alternating current (AC) electricity supply system. Edison was an American inventor who is known for his development of the incandescent light bulb and the phonograph, among other things. Tesla and Edison were not always enemies, but they did have a famous rivalry. This happened because they had different ideas about how electricity should be generated and transmitted. Tesla believed in using AC electricity, while Edison believed in using direct current (DC) electricity. They both worked on developing their own systems and tried to convince others that their way was the best. Despite their differences, both Tesla and Edison are remembered as some of the most important and influential inventors in history. They are famous for their many inventions and innovations, which have had a huge impact on the world we live in today.
