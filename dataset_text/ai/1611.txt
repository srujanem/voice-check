Embezzlement is the act of stealing money or property that has been entrusted to you. For example, if you are a city official or mayor and you are in charge of managing the city's money, you might embezzle money by taking some of it for yourself instead of using it for the purposes it was intended for. There are many ways that someone might embezzle money from a city or company. For example, they might create fake invoices or receipts and submit them for payment, or they might transfer money from the company's account into their own personal account. Embezzlement is a serious crime and is considered fraud. If someone is caught embezzling money, they can be charged with a crime and may face fines and imprisonment. It is important to be honest and trustworthy when handling money and property, and to use it for the purposes it was intended for.
