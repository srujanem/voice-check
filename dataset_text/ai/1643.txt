Adolf Hitler became the dictator of Germany through a combination of his own charisma and the weakness of other political parties in the country. After World War I, Germany was in a difficult situation. The economy was struggling, and many people were angry and frustrated. Hitler and his political party, the Nazi Party, promised to solve these problems and make Germany great again. To win support, Hitler used a variety of tactics, including giving powerful speeches, spreading propaganda, and using violence to intimidate his opponents. He also took advantage of the fact that other political parties were divided and not very popular. Eventually, Hitler and the Nazi Party gained enough support to take control of the government. Once in power, Hitler used his position to consolidate his control over the country, suppressing opposition and establishing himself as the absolute ruler of Germany. So to sum it up, Hitler became dictator of Germany by using his charisma, propaganda, and violence to win the support of the people and take control of the government.
