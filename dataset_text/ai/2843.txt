Existentialism is a way of thinking about the world that says that each person is responsible for making their own meaning in life. It is a philosophy that emphasizes individual freedom and choice, and the idea that we are all responsible for creating our own purpose and meaning in life. Here is a simple explanation: Imagine you are playing a game with your friends, and you get to choose what the game is and how you want to play it. Existentialism is like that, but instead of a game, it's about your whole life. You get to decide what you want to do with your life and how you want to live it, and it's up to you to find meaning and purpose in your choices. Existentialism can be a difficult concept to understand, but the main idea is that each person is in charge of their own life and it is up to them to make the most of it.
