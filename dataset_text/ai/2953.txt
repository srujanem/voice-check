Monitors and TVs are wider and not taller for a few reasons. One reason is that when people watch TV or use a computer, they usually sit farther away from the screen than they do when they read a book or look at a painting. If the screen was taller, it would be harder to see the whole thing because it would be too far above or below the viewer's line of sight. But if the screen is wider, it can still be seen easily even when it is farther away. Another reason is that most movies and TV shows are filmed in a wide-screen format, which means that they have more width than height. This allows the filmmakers to show more of the scene at once, which can be helpful for showing landscapes, action scenes, or other wide shots. When you watch a movie or TV show on a wider screen, it can help you feel more immersed in the story because you can see more of the surroundings. Finally, most monitors and TVs are rectangular because that is the shape that fits best in most homes and offices. Rectangular screens can be placed on a desk or mounted on a wall, and they don't take up too much space. I hope that helps to explain why monitors and TVs are wider and not taller!
