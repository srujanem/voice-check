Train tracks have rocks, or ballast, along the rail lines for a few reasons. First, the ballast helps to keep the tracks in place and prevent them from shifting or moving around. When a train travels on the tracks, the weight of the train can cause the tracks to sink or shift slightly. The ballast helps to distribute the weight of the train and keep the tracks stable. Second, the ballast helps to drain water away from the tracks. When it rains or snows, the water can accumulate on the tracks and cause them to become slippery or unstable. The ballast helps to drain the water away from the tracks, which helps to keep the tracks safe for the trains to travel on. Overall, the ballast helps to keep the tracks in good condition and make sure that the trains can travel safely on them.
