Fixed income refers to a type of investment or financial product that provides a regular, predictable income. This can include things like bonds, annuities, and certain types of pension plans.\n\nWhen you invest in a fixed income product, you are essentially lending money to a company or government in exchange for regular payments. For example, if you buy a bond, the company or government will pay you a set amount of interest each year until the bond matures.\n\nWhen people say that they are on a fixed income, it usually means that they are receiving regular payments from a source that they cannot change or increase, such as a pension or a social security check. It can also refer to a situation where someone has a limited amount of money available to them each month and cannot change that amount.\n\nBudgets can be helpful for people living on a fixed income because they allow them to plan their spending and make sure that they have enough money to cover their expenses each month.\n
