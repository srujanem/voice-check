Being a good critical thinker means being able to think carefully and thoroughly about something, and to evaluate the evidence and arguments for and against it. It means being able to ask good questions and to seek out information from a variety of sources. It also means being open-minded and willing to consider different viewpoints, even if you don't agree with them. 
It's important to be a good critical thinker because it helps you to make better decisions and to understand complex issues more deeply. It can also help you to be more creative and to solve problems more effectively. 
Some people find it easier to be a good critical thinker than others, but it is a skill that anyone can learn and improve with practice. It's important for students to learn how to be good critical thinkers because it will help them to succeed in school and in their future careers.
