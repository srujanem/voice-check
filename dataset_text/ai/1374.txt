Printing color on packaging is not necessarily cheap, but it can be done more efficiently and cost-effectively than printing color on smaller items like brochures or flyers. This is because printing companies can print large quantities of packaging at once, which allows them to use specialized printing equipment that can produce high-quality color prints quickly and efficiently. 
One way that companies can save money on printing color packaging is by using a printing process called "4-color process printing." This process involves printing with just four colors (cyan, magenta, yellow, and black) but using different combinations and shades of these colors to create a wide range of colors. This can be more cost-effective than printing with a larger number of specialized inks, because it requires fewer printing plates and set-up costs. 
Another way that companies can save money on printing color packaging is by using pre-printed packaging that has been printed with a basic design or pattern. This can be used as a base for additional printing, such as printing a product name or label on top of the pre-printed packaging. This can be more cost-effective than printing a completely new design from scratch, because it reduces the amount of printing that needs to be done.
