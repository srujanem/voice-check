The Queen's husband, Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, is referred to as a prince because he was born into the royal family of Greece and Denmark. His mother was a princess and his father was a prince, so he inherited the title of prince. He is also the Duke of Edinburgh, which is a title he was given when he married Queen Elizabeth II. The title of duke is a high noble rank that is above a prince, but below a king or queen. So, while Prince Philip is technically a prince, he is also a duke and has a higher noble rank.
