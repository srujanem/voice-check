Buffets stay in business because, while some people might eat more than $5 worth of food, other people might eat less. The buffet charges a flat price for all-you-can-eat food, so some people might not eat very much and the buffet will make a profit on those customers. Additionally, buffets often have a high turnover rate, which means that a lot of people come and go throughout the day, so the buffet is able to sell food to a large number of customers. Finally, buffets often offer a variety of foods at a lower price than if you were to order those same items à la carte (one at a time) at a restaurant, which can make them more appealing to customers.
