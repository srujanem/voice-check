Here's what happened, according to the Big Bang Theory: A long, long time ago, the universe was just a tiny dot. It was so small that you couldn't even see it. It was also very, very hot. Suddenly, for some reason, the dot exploded and started to grow. As it grew, it cooled down and things started to form inside of it, like stars, planets, and galaxies. Today, the universe is still expanding and getting bigger and bigger. Scientists use special tools to study the universe and learn more about how it works.
