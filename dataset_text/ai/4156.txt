Some medicines can cause stomach upset if taken on an empty stomach. Taking the medicine with food can help to prevent this side effect. Different foods can affect the way that the body absorbs and uses medicine, so it's important to follow the instructions on the medicine label. For example, some medicines may work better if taken with a high-fat meal, while others may be less effective if taken with certain types of food. It's always best to follow the instructions on the medicine label and to ask a healthcare professional if you have any questions about how to take your medicine.
