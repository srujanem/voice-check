In the 1980s and 1990s, beige was a popular color for computers and other electronic devices because it was thought to look professional and neutral. It was also easier to match with other beige office equipment, like printers and fax machines. 
There are a few reasons why beige was a popular choice for computers. One reason is that beige is a relatively neutral color, so it can go well with a variety of other colors and decor styles. Additionally, beige is a softer and less vibrant color, which may have made the computer look more professional and business-like. 
Another reason that beige was popular for computers is that it was easier to manufacture. Beige plastic was readily available and could be molded into the various shapes and sizes needed for computer cases. This made it a more cost-effective choice for manufacturers. 
White computers were not as common in the 1980s and 1990s because white plastic was more difficult to mold and not as readily available as beige. It was also more prone to yellowing over time, which could make the computer look old and outdated. However, white computers have become more popular in recent years, and you can now find white laptops, desktops, and other devices.
