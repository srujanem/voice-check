Passports are documents that allow people to travel to other countries. They have expiration dates because governments want to make sure that the information in the passport is up to date. For example, if someone has changed their name, address, or appearance since getting their passport, the government wants to make sure that their passport reflects these changes. This is important for security reasons, because it helps to make sure that the person who is traveling is who they say they are. Additionally, some countries require that your passport be valid for a certain amount of time before you can enter or travel through them. For example, some countries may require that your passport be valid for six months after you enter their country. So, in order to make sure that people can travel as they need to, governments set expiration dates for passports.
