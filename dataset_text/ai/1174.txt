At EDM festivals like Tomorrowland and Ultra, the production team works closely with the DJs to plan out the lighting and visual effects for their performances. This usually involves creating a "script" or a detailed plan for how the lights and effects should be timed to the music. 
The DJ and the production team will often communicate and make adjustments to the script as needed, but they try to stick as closely to the original plan as possible in order to ensure that the effects are in sync with the music. 
If the DJ decides to make last-minute changes to their set, the production team may need to quickly adjust the lighting and visual effects to match the new music. They do this using special software and equipment that allows them to control the lights and effects in real-time. 
Overall, the goal is to create a seamless and immersive experience for the audience by syncing the music and effects as closely as possible.
