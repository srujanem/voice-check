Sure! A musical key is a group of musical notes that sound good together. A mode is a specific pattern of whole and half steps (the distances between notes) that makes up a musical scale. There are different modes, such as major, minor, and others. A scale is just a series of notes played in a specific order, usually starting with a specific note and going up or down. For example, let's say we have a piece of music in the key of C major. This means that the notes in the music are mostly C, D, E, F, G, A, and B, and they sound good together because they belong to the key of C major. The mode of this piece of music would be major, because it follows a specific pattern of whole and half steps. And the scale of this piece of music would be the series of notes C, D, E, F, G, A, B, played in that order. I hope this helps! Let me know if you have any more questions.
