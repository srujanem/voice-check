Mah (milliampere-hours) is a measure of the amount of energy a battery can hold. It tells you how long a device can run on a single charge. A higher mAh number means the battery can hold more energy and run for a longer time before it needs to be recharged.\n\nHowever, mAh is not the only factor that determines how many times a device can be charged. The device's power consumption and the battery's output also play a role.\n\nFor example, a smartphone with a 3500mAh battery may use less power than a laptop with a 4640mAh battery. As a result, the external battery with 20000mAh of capacity may be able to fully charge the smartphone five times, but it may not have enough capacity to fully charge the laptop even once.\n\nIt's also important to note that not all batteries are created equal. Some batteries may be more efficient at storing and releasing energy, while others may be less efficient.
