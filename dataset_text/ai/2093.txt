Pain under the rib cage during physical activity could be caused by a number of things. Here are a few possibilities: Muscles: Your ribs are attached to muscles that help you breathe and move your upper body. When you do vigorous physical activity, these muscles can become strained or tired, causing pain. Organs: Your rib cage protects a number of important organs, including your heart, lungs, and liver. If you have an injury or illness affecting one of these organs, it could cause pain under your rib cage. Gas: When you exercise, you might swallow more air than usual, which can lead to bloating and gas. This can cause pain under your rib cage. Other factors: There are many other factors that could cause pain under your rib cage during physical activity, such as acid reflux, a hernia, or even a broken rib. It's important to pay attention to your body and listen to any pain or discomfort you're feeling. If the pain persists or is severe, you should see a doctor for a proper diagnosis and treatment.
