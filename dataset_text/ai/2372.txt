Darknet is a type of network that is not accessible through normal means. It is often used for illegal activities, such as buying and selling drugs or weapons, or for sharing stolen information. To access the darknet, you need to use special software called a "darknet browser." These browsers allow you to visit websites that are not indexed by search engines like Google, and that are only accessible through the darknet. The regular internet, also known as the "clearweb," is what most people use every day to access websites, send emails, and do other online activities. It is called the clearweb because it is easy to access and is not hidden. The darknet is different from the clearweb because it is not as easy to access, and because the activities that take place on it are often illegal or secretive. It is called the "dark" net because it is hidden and not easily accessible to most people.
