The hole in the pen cap is called an "air vent." It's there to allow air to flow into the pen when you replace the cap, which helps to prevent the ink from drying out. When you remove the cap from a pen, the airtight seal that it creates around the pen's ink chamber is broken, which allows air to flow in and out of the chamber. Without the air vent, the ink in the pen might not flow as smoothly or evenly when you use it, because there would be too much pressure inside the ink chamber. The hole in the cap helps to balance the pressure and keep the ink flowing smoothly. So, even though the hole might seem like it would cause the ink to dry out, it actually helps to keep the ink fresh and ready to use.
