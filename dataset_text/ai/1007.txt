Advertisers pay to show commercials in different locations for a variety of reasons. One reason is that they want to reach a large audience, and they may not be able to do that if they only advertise in areas where they offer their products or services. For example, if The General Auto Insurance only advertised in states where they offer their services, they would be missing out on the opportunity to reach people in other states who may be interested in their products. Additionally, even if someone does not live in a state where The General Auto Insurance offers its services, they may still be interested in learning about the company and its products. By advertising in a variety of locations, The General Auto Insurance is able to reach a wider audience and potentially attract new customers.
