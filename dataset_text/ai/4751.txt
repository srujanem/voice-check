Deep fried ice cream is a dessert that involves coating a ball of ice cream in a layer of batter or breadcrumbs, and then deep frying it until the outside is crispy and golden brown. The ice cream is usually frozen solid before it is coated and fried, which helps it to hold its shape while it is being cooked.\n\nWhen the ice cream is put into the hot cooking oil, the heat from the oil causes the outside of the ice cream to melt and become creamy again. However, the batter or breadcrumbs that are coating the ice cream help to keep the ice cream from melting completely. The coating acts as a barrier that helps to keep the heat from the oil away from the ice cream.\n\nThe ice cream is only in the oil for a short period of time, so it doesn't have time to melt completely. When it is taken out of the oil and served, the outside is crispy and the inside is still cold and creamy.\n\nOverall, deep frying ice cream is a way to create a fun and tasty dessert that combines the textures of crispy and creamy in a single bite!
