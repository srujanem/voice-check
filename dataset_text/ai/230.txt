Sleeping pills are designed to help people fall asleep, so it is expected that they may cause drowsiness. The warning label is there to remind people that taking sleeping pills can make them feel sleepy or less alert, and to be careful when doing activities that require attention and alertness, such as driving or operating heavy machinery. 
It's important to follow the instructions on the label when taking sleeping pills, because taking too many or mixing them with other medications or alcohol can increase the risk of drowsiness and other side effects. The warning label is there to help people use the medication safely and effectively.
