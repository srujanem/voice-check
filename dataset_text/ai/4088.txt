Obamacare is a healthcare law passed by the government that requires everyone to have health insurance. Some people, like the Republicans, do not like this law because they believe that it is too expensive and that the government should not require people to have health insurance. They also believe that the law is too complex and that it is not working as well as it was intended to. Because of this, they want to repeal the law and come up with a different solution for healthcare in the United States.
