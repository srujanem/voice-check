In simple terms, mono sound means that there is only one channel of audio, while stereo sound means that there are two channels of audio. This means that with mono sound, all of the audio is coming from a single source, while with stereo sound, the audio is coming from two separate sources, one on the left and one on the right. Now, when it comes to records released in the 60s, many of them were released in mono, meaning that they only had one channel of audio. This was because at the time, most people were listening to music on mono systems, such as radios or record players, which could only play one channel of audio. However, some records were also released in stereo, which meant that they had two channels of audio. These records were meant to be played on stereo systems, which could play both the left and right channels of audio, creating a more immersive and lifelike listening experience.
