Friction works differently on wheels because of the way that they move and the way that they make contact with the ground. When a wheel is rolling, it only makes contact with the ground at a small point, called the point of contact. This means that the force of friction is concentrated at this small point, and it is easier for the wheel to overcome the friction and keep moving.\n\nOn the other hand, when an object is sliding across a surface, the entire surface of the object is in contact with the ground, so the force of friction is spread out over a larger area. This makes it harder for the object to overcome the friction and keep moving.\n\nRace cars and dragsters use wider wheels to take advantage of this difference in the way that friction works. The wider wheels have more surface area in contact with the ground, which means that there is more force of friction pushing against them. This can help the car to grip the ground more effectively and go faster.
