The metric system is a system of measurement that is based on the International System of Units, or SI, which is used all around the world. The base units of the metric system include the meter, which is the unit of length, and the liter, which is the unit of volume. One meter is defined as the distance traveled by light in a vacuum in 1/299,792,458 of a second. This definition was chosen because it is an extremely precise and consistent measurement that is not affected by factors such as temperature or pressure. One liter is defined as the volume of a cube that is 10 centimeters on each side. This definition was chosen because it is easy to measure and is based on the metric unit of length, the centimeter. The metric system is used to measure things like weight, distance, and volume, and it is based on units that are easy to understand and use. It is important because it helps people communicate and compare measurements in a consistent and accurate way, no matter where they are in the world.
