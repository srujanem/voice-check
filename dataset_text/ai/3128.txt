Universities and colleges are both places where people can go to learn and get a degree, but they are slightly different. A university is a school that offers a wide range of academic programs and typically has a larger student body than a college. Universities usually offer undergraduate and graduate degree programs, such as bachelor's degrees, master's degrees, and doctorates. They also often have a number of research facilities and may have a medical school or law school. A college, on the other hand, is usually smaller than a university and often focuses on a specific area of study, such as liberal arts or technology. Colleges may offer undergraduate degree programs, but they usually do not have graduate programs. There is some overlap between universities and colleges, as some colleges may be part of a larger university system and may offer both undergraduate and graduate degree programs. However, in general, universities are larger and offer a wider range of academic programs than colleges.
