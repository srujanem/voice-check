If you coated yourself in a very hydrophobic (water-repelling) gel and then jumped into a pool of water, the water would likely bead up and roll off of your skin and the gel. This is because the gel would create a barrier on your skin that prevents the water from being able to fully wet or stick to your skin. Instead, the water would sit on top of the gel and roll off of you, much like how water beads up and rolls off of a freshly waxed car. 
In other words, the gel would help keep you dry while you're in the water. However, it's important to note that the gel would not make you completely waterproof or able to breathe underwater. You would still need to come up for air if you were submerged in the water for an extended period of time.
