Speed reading is a technique that allows a person to read text more quickly than they would normally. It is not done by injecting anything into the brain. Instead, speed reading involves using various strategies to help a person process text more efficiently. For example, a person might use their finger or a pen to guide their eyes across the page, or they might use special software that helps them read faster by displaying the text in a way that is easier to process. Some people also use techniques like skimming, which involves quickly scanning the text to get a general idea of what it is about, or chunking, which involves grouping words together into larger chunks to make it easier to process the information. Overall, speed reading is about finding ways to read more efficiently and effectively, so that you can get through the text faster without sacrificing comprehension.
