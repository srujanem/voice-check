Domain names are essentially addresses for websites on the internet. When you type a domain name into your web browser, it takes you to the website associated with that domain name. GoDaddy and other companies that sell domain names are called "domain name registrars." They have contracts with organizations called "registries" that oversee the assignment of domain names within specific top-level domains (TLDs), such as .com or .org. Registries allow registrars to sell domain names to individuals and organizations who want to use them for their own websites. Registrars like GoDaddy act as intermediaries between the registries and the end users, handling the technical and administrative aspects of registering and maintaining a domain name. Anyone can become a domain name registrar by applying for accreditation with a registry and meeting the necessary requirements. However, it can be a competitive business, and there are many established registrars with significant market share. If you're interested in getting into the business, you'll need to do some research and be prepared to invest time and resources into building your registrar company.
