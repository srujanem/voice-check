The ocean covers more than seventy percent of Earth's surface and contains an incredible variety of life. Tiny plankton, colorful coral reefs, massive whales, and deep-sea creatures all play important roles in marine ecosystems. Oceans also regulate the planet's climate by absorbing heat and carbon dioxide. Unfortunately, pollution, overfishing, and climate change threaten marine habitats. Protecting the oceans through conservation efforts, sustainable fishing, and reducing plastic waste is essential for preserving biodiversity and maintaining a healthy environment for future generations.
