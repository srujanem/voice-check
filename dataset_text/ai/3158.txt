One of the main ways that processors have gotten faster is by shrinking the size of the transistors that make up the processor. Transistors are tiny electronic switches that can be turned on and off to represent the 1s and 0s of computer code. The smaller the transistors, the more of them you can fit onto a chip, and the more calculations the processor can do in a given amount of time. Ten years ago, processors were being made using transistors that were around 22 nanometers (nm) in size. Today, processors are being made using transistors that are only a few nanometers in size. This allows for more transistors to be packed into a smaller area, which means the processor can do more calculations in a given amount of time.
