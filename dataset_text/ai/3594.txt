When you move a file to the recycle bin, the file is not actually deleted from your computer. Instead, it is moved to a special folder on your computer called the recycle bin. You can still access the file from the recycle bin and restore it to its original location if you need to. When you clear the recycle bin, the files in the recycle bin are permanently deleted from your computer. This means that the files are no longer accessible and cannot be restored. The space on your hard drive that was being used by the deleted files is then made available for new files to be saved. To explain it in a way a five year old might understand: Imagine that you have a toy box in your room. When you want to get rid of a toy, you put it in the toy box. The toy is not really gone - it's still in the toy box and you can still play with it if you want. But if you decide that you don't want the toy anymore, you can throw it away and it will be gone forever. That's kind of like what happens when you move a file to the recycle bin and then clear it. The file is like the toy in the toy box, and when you clear the recycle bin it's like throwing the toy away.
