Animated movies can cost a lot of money for several reasons. One reason is that creating an animated movie is a very labor-intensive process. It can take many people a long time to draw and animate all of the characters and scenes in a movie. This can be especially true if the movie has a lot of complex or detailed animation. 
Another reason is that creating an animated movie requires a lot of expensive equipment and software. This includes computers, specialized animation software, and other tools that are needed to create the movie. 
Finally, there are many other costs that go into making an animated movie, such as paying for the voice actors, the music, and any special effects. All of these things can add up and make the movie more expensive. 
So, while it may seem like making an animated movie is mostly just about drawing and animating on a computer, there are actually many other factors that go into the cost of creating a movie.
