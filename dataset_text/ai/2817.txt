Converting a 2D movie into a 3D movie involves adding a second perspective to the film, which gives the illusion of depth and makes it seem like the objects on the screen are coming towards you or receding into the distance. To do this, special software is used to analyze the 2D movie and create a digital model of the objects and characters in the film. This model is then used to generate a second perspective, or "view," of the film from a slightly different angle. When the two perspectives are combined and displayed on a 3D screen, it creates the illusion of depth and makes it feel like you are watching a 3D movie. It's a bit like how we see in real life - our two eyes see slightly different perspectives of the world, and our brain combines them to create a sense of depth. The process of converting a 2D movie into a 3D movie is just a way to mimic this natural process and create a more immersive viewing experience.
