Dogs like to be pet by humans because it makes them feel loved and reassured. When a dog is petted, their brain releases a chemical called oxytocin, which is associated with feelings of love, bonding, and trust. Dogs are social animals, and they have a natural desire to be close to their human caregivers. Petting can help to strengthen the bond between a dog and a person, and it can also provide a sense of comfort and security for the dog. Dogs may also enjoy being touched because it stimulates certain pleasure centers in their brain, similar to how people enjoy being hugged or massaged. Ultimately, dogs like to be pet by humans because it helps them feel happy and relaxed.
