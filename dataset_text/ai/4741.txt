Lagrange points are points in space where the gravitational forces of two large bodies, such as Earth and the moon, balance the centrifugal force felt by a smaller object, such as a satellite, which is orbiting around one of the larger bodies. This allows the smaller object to remain in a stable position relative to the larger bodies.\n\nThere are five Lagrange points in any two-body system, labeled L1 through L5. These points are located along the line connecting the two large bodies. L1 is located between the two bodies, L2 is located on the side of the larger body opposite the smaller body, L3 is located on the opposite side of the line connecting the two bodies from the larger body, L4 is located in the same orbit as the smaller body, but 60 degrees ahead of it, and L5 is located in the same orbit as the smaller body, but 60 degrees behind it.\n\nLagrange points can be useful for placing satellites or other spacecraft in specific locations relative to the larger bodies. For example, satellites placed at L1 or L2 can be used to observe the sun, while satellites placed at L4 and L5 can be used to study the Earth-moon system.
