Tilt-shift photography is a technique that is used to make real-life objects or scenes look like miniature models. It is done by using a special type of lens called a tilt-shift lens, or by using photo editing software to simulate the effect. To create the effect, the photographer tilts or shifts the lens to change the plane of focus in the photograph. This means that only a narrow area of the photograph is in focus, while the rest of the image is blurry. The result is that the objects in the photograph appear much smaller than they actually are, giving the impression that they are miniature models. The tilt-shift effect is often used to make photographs of real-life scenes look like toy models, which is why the objects in the photograph may look like toys. It can be a fun and creative way to take pictures, and it can be used to make all sorts of scenes and objects look interesting and different.
