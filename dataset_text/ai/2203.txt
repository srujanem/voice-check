The Nazis targeted Jews because they believed in the idea of racial purity and saw the Jewish people as inferior to what they considered to be the superior Aryan race. They also blamed the Jews for Germany's problems and used them as a scapegoat for their own failures. The Nazis used propaganda to spread these hateful ideas and eventually implemented a plan to systematically kill as many Jews as possible, which is known as the Holocaust. It's important to note that the Holocaust was a horrific and tragic event in history that resulted in the deaths of millions of innocent people, and there is no justification for the atrocities that were committed.
