The Cold War was a period of tension between the United States and the Soviet Union that lasted from the end of World War II in 1945 until the collapse of the Soviet Union in 1991. During this time, both sides had a lot of nuclear weapons, which are extremely powerful bombs that can cause a lot of destruction. If the Cold War had turned into a hot war, or a real war with fighting and bombing, it is likely that many people would have been killed and injured, and there would have been a lot of damage to buildings and infrastructure. It is difficult to say exactly how many people would have been killed in a nuclear war, but it is likely that it would have been in the millions, possibly even tens of millions. The long-term effects of a nuclear war would have been devastating as well. The radiation from the bombs could have caused health problems for people and animals, and the damage to the environment could have been severe. It could have taken many years, if not decades, for the affected areas to recover. Overall, it is very lucky that the Cold War did not turn into a hot war, as the consequences could have been catastrophic for humanity.
