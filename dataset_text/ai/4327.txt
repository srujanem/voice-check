Credit card scanners ask "credit or debit" because the machine needs to know how you want to pay for your purchase. Credit cards and debit cards look similar and they both have magnetic strips on the back that can be read by the credit card scanner, but they work in different ways.\n\nWhen you use a credit card, you are borrowing money from the credit card company to pay for your purchase. You can choose to pay back the credit card company in full each month or make smaller payments over time, but you will be charged interest on the money you borrowed if you don't pay it back in full.\n\nWhen you use a debit card, you are using money that is already in your checking or savings account to pay for your purchase. The money is transferred from your account to the merchant's account when you make a purchase.\n\nSome credit card scanners ask you "credit or debit" because they need to know how you want to pay for your purchase and whether you need to enter a PIN (personal identification number) or sign for the purchase. Other machines may not ask you because they are programmed to process credit card transactions automatically or because they are only set up to accept one type of payment (either credit or debit).\n\nI hope this helps!
