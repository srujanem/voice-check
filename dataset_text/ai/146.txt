Catnip is a plant that has a chemical in it called nepetalactone that can affect cats in a way that is similar to how humans might feel after taking drugs or alcohol. When a cat smells or ingests catnip, it can cause them to become more playful, energetic, and sometimes even a little bit frisky. Some cats may become more relaxed or calm after interacting with catnip. 
The effects of catnip usually only last for a short amount of time, usually around 10-15 minutes. After that, the cat will likely lose interest in the catnip and may even become indifferent to it for a while. This is because their body becomes temporarily "immune" to the effects of the catnip. 
It's important to note that not all cats are affected by catnip. Some cats may show no interest in it at all, while others may be very sensitive to it. It's also important to keep in mind that catnip is generally considered safe for cats, although it's a good idea to use it in moderation.
