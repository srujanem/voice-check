There are a few reasons why companies might hire foreign people to do technical or customer support. One reason is that it can be less expensive to hire workers in other countries, especially if the cost of living is lower there. For example, if a company is based in a country with a high cost of living, it might be able to save money by hiring workers in a country with a lower cost of living. 
Another reason is that there may be a shortage of qualified candidates in the company's home country, or the company may have difficulty finding people with the necessary skills or experience. In these cases, the company may look to other countries to find the talent it needs. 
While it's true that language barriers can sometimes be a challenge when working with foreign technical or customer support staff, many companies provide language training or hire staff who are fluent in the language used by their customers. Additionally, advances in technology, such as translation software, can also help to bridge language gaps. 
Overall, companies hire foreign workers for technical or customer support for a variety of reasons, including cost savings and access to a larger pool of qualified candidates. While there can be challenges, many companies have found ways to successfully work with foreign staff and provide high-quality support to their customers.
