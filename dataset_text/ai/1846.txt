The United Kingdom has a National Health Service (NHS) that provides free healthcare to all UK citizens and some people who live in the UK. This means that anyone in the UK can go to a doctor or a hospital and get medical treatment without having to pay for it. However, while the NHS provides a wide range of healthcare services, it does not cover everything. For example, it does not cover most dental care, such as routine check-ups, cleanings, or fillings. Some dental care is available for free on the NHS, but only for certain groups of people, such as children, pregnant women, and people with certain medical conditions. As a result, many people in the UK have to pay for their own dental care. This can be expensive, and some people may not be able to afford it. As a result, they may not go to the dentist as often as they should, which can lead to problems with their teeth. So, while the UK does have free healthcare, it does not cover all types of medical treatment, including dental care. This can sometimes lead to people in the UK having poor dental health.
