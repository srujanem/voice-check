Inflation is when prices for goods and services go up over time. When the cost of making a product goes up, a company has a few options to deal with it. One option is to raise the price of the product to cover the higher costs. However, if the company has a fixed price, like Arizona Iced Tea, they might not be able to raise the price. In this case, the company might have to find ways to cut costs in other areas or find more efficient ways to produce the product. This can help the company keep the price the same even though the cost of making the product has gone up.
