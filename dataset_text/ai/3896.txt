There are a few potential advantages to a company owning many competing and similar businesses: Diversification: By owning multiple businesses in the same industry, a company can spread out its risk. If one of the businesses encounters difficulties, it may be balanced out by the performance of the others. This can make the company as a whole more stable and less vulnerable to downturns in a specific market. Synergies: By owning multiple businesses in the same industry, a company may be able to achieve cost savings through economies of scale. For example, it might be able to purchase raw materials at a lower cost because it is buying in larger quantities. It may also be able to share resources and expertise between the different businesses. Market power: A company that owns a large share of the market in a particular industry may have more bargaining power with suppliers, more ability to set prices, and more ability to withstand competition. Growth opportunities: Owning multiple businesses in the same industry may allow a company to expand its operations more easily, either through acquiring additional businesses or by leveraging its existing assets and expertise to enter new markets.
