The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is a video game that was released in 1998 for the Nintendo 64 console. It is considered by many people to be one of the greatest video games of all time because of its innovative gameplay, beautiful graphics, and engaging story. 
One reason that Ocarina of Time is so highly regarded is because it introduced many new gameplay elements that were innovative at the time and have since become common in video games. For example, the game introduced the concept of a "Z-targeting" system, which allowed players to lock onto enemies and objects in the game world. This made it easier to fight enemies and interact with the environment. 
Ocarina of Time also had a vast, open world for players to explore, which was filled with interesting characters, puzzles, and secrets to discover. The game's story was also very well-written, with a compelling plot and memorable characters. 
Overall, Ocarina of Time is considered one of the greatest games of all time because it combined innovative gameplay, beautiful graphics, and a great story into a single, immersive experience that has stood the test of time.
