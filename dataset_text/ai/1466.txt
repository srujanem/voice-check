Popcorn is a popular snack to eat while watching movies because it is a tasty and convenient food to munch on while sitting in a dark theater. It is also relatively inexpensive and easy to find at movie theaters. The idea of eating popcorn while watching movies probably started when movie theaters began offering popcorn as a snack option. Over time, it became associated with the movie-going experience and is now a staple snack that many people enjoy while watching movies.
