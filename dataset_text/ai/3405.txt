Sloths are not extinct because they have some natural defenses that help them survive in the wild. Even though they are slow, they have sharp claws that they use to climb trees and defend themselves from predators. They also have a thick layer of fur that helps to protect them from the elements and from being bitten or scratched by other animals. Additionally, sloths have a very good sense of hearing and vision, which helps them to detect predators and escape to safety. Finally, sloths are experts at hiding, and they are very good at blending in with their surroundings, which makes them difficult for predators to spot. All of these things combined help sloths to survive in the wild, even though they are not the fastest or strongest animals.
