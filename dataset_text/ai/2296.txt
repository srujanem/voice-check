Crude oil is a complex mixture of various hydrocarbons, which are chemicals made up of hydrogen and carbon atoms. These hydrocarbons can be processed and separated into different types of molecules that can be used to make a wide variety of products, including plastics. To turn crude oil into plastic, it is first refined to separate out the different hydrocarbons. Then, these hydrocarbons are chemically processed to create new molecules called polymers, which are long chains of repeating units of smaller molecules. These polymers can be made into different types of plastics by adding different chemicals and processing them in different ways. Once the plastic has been made, it can be molded into different shapes and forms. There are many different methods for molding plastic, but one common method is to heat the plastic until it becomes soft and pliable, and then to use pressure to force it into a mold. The mold is a shape or form that the plastic takes on as it cools and hardens. Once the plastic has hardened, it can be removed from the mold and used to make a wide variety of products, such as cell phone cases or storage bins.
