WikiLeaks is an organization that publishes secret or confidential information, news leaks, and classified media from anonymous sources. They are known for releasing large amounts of sensitive information, such as government documents and emails, to the public in order to expose wrongdoing or corruption. In this particular case, it sounds like WikiLeaks may be upset because 250,000 unredacted cables (documents containing sensitive information) have been made available online without their permission. Normally, WikiLeaks carefully redacts (removes or censors) sensitive information from the documents they release to protect the identities of the sources and individuals mentioned in the documents. By making the unredacted cables available online, it may be possible for people to identify the sources and individuals mentioned in the documents, which could potentially put them in danger. So, in short, WikiLeaks is upset because the unredacted cables being available online could potentially put people in danger by revealing their identities, and this is not something that WikiLeaks normally does when they release sensitive information to the public.
