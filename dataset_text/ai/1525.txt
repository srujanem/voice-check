Rent-to-own stores like Rent-A-Center exist to provide people with an alternative way to buy household items like appliances, electronics, and furniture. Instead of paying for these items all at once, customers can pay for them in monthly installments. This can be a good option for people who don't have the money to pay for the items upfront, or who don't want to use credit to make the purchase. 
One reason someone might want to rent an appliance, electronic, or piece of furniture is that they may not have the money to buy it outright. Renting these items allows them to pay for them over time, which can make it more affordable. 
Another reason someone might choose to rent these items is that they may not need the item long-term. For example, they might be moving to a new home in a few months and don't want to invest in a lot of new furniture that they'll only use for a short period of time. In this case, it might make more sense to rent the items instead of buying them. 
Overall, rent-to-own stores like Rent-A-Center can be a good option for people who need to buy household items but don't have the money upfront or don't want to use credit to make the purchase.
