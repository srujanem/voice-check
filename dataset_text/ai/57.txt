Artificial intelligence relies heavily on machine learning algorithms that allow systems to analyze massive amounts of data, identify patterns, and improve their performance over time without explicit programming. Through deep learning neural networks inspired by human brain architecture, AI applications can now recognize complex images, process natural language speech, and drive autonomous vehicles. As these systems grow more sophisticated, they are reshaping industries ranging from healthcare diagnostics to financial fraud detection, offering incredible efficiency gains while raising important ethical questions regarding privacy, bias, and automated decision-making.
