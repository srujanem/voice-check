Computers use tiny electronic circuits to store and process information. These circuits can be in the form of transistors or other types of components, and they are the building blocks of computer hardware. When a computer is processing information, it uses a series of electrical signals to represent the data. These signals can be either on or off, and we use the digits 1 and 0 to represent these two states. So a 1 represents an "on" signal, and a 0 represents an "off" signal. For example, when you type a letter on your keyboard, the computer converts the letter into a series of 1's and 0's that represent the letter's digital code. The computer then uses these 1's and 0's to perform various tasks, such as storing the letter in a file, displaying it on the screen, or sending it over the internet.
