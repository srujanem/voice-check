The Star Wars prequels, which include The Phantom Menace, Attack of the Clones, and Revenge of the Sith, were considered by many fans and critics to be disappointing for a number of reasons. 
One reason is that the prequels were released many years after the original Star Wars trilogy, which had gained a cult following and was seen as a classic. As a result, there were high expectations for the prequels, and many fans felt that they did not live up to the quality of the original trilogy. 
Another reason is that the prequels received criticism for their heavy use of CGI (computer-generated imagery) and less reliance on practical effects. Some fans felt that the overuse of CGI took away from the "realism" and immersion of the Star Wars universe. 
Additionally, the prequels were criticized for their writing and character development. Some people felt that the dialogue was wooden and that the characters were not as well-developed as those in the original trilogy. 
Overall, the prequels were seen as a disappointment by many fans because they did not capture the same magic and excitement as the original trilogy.
