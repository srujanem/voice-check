This is a multifaceted topic that requires careful examination from multiple perspectives. India's independence on August 15, 1947, marked the end of nearly 200 years of British colonial rule. The independence movement was led by figures such as Mahatma Gandhi, who championed nonviolent civil disobedience, and Jawaharlal Nehru, who became India's first Prime Minister. The partition of the subcontinent into India and Pakistan resulted in one of the largest mass migrations in history and significant communal violence. India's constitution, adopted in 1950, established it as a sovereign, democratic republic. Understanding these principles is foundational to making progress in this important area.
