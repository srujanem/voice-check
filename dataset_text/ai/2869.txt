When it's cold outside, the oil in your car's engine gets thicker, which makes it harder for it to flow and lubricate all the moving parts. If you try to drive your car right away without warming it up first, the oil won't flow as easily and could cause the engine to wear out more quickly. Warming up your car for a few minutes allows the oil to thin out and flow more easily, which can help protect your engine and make your car run more smoothly. So, it's a good idea to take a few minutes to warm up your car before driving it, especially on very cold days.
