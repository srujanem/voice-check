Honorary degrees are degrees that are given by a university to people who have made significant contributions in a particular field or to society in general. These degrees are not earned in the same way as a traditional degree, which typically requires completing a set of coursework and passing exams. Instead, they are given as a way to recognize and honor the achievements of the recipient. Celebrities, like other individuals, can be nominated or recommended to receive an honorary degree by a university. The university will then consider the nomination and decide whether or not to award the degree. If the celebrity is awarded an honorary degree, they will receive a diploma or certificate recognizing their achievement. If a celebrity with an honorary degree were to apply for a job, the degree would not be considered a valid degree in the same way as a traditional degree earned through coursework and exams. However, it could still be listed on their resume or mentioned in an interview as a way to highlight their accomplishments and contributions to their field or to society.
