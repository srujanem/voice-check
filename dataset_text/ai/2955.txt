It's normal to feel hungry after eating fruits because fruits are generally lower in calories and fat than fatty foods. This means that they don't provide as much energy or stay in your stomach as long, so you may feel hungry again sooner. Fatty foods, on the other hand, are higher in calories and fat, which can help you feel fuller for longer. However, it's important to remember that while fatty foods can help you feel full, they are not always the healthiest choice. It's important to eat a balanced diet that includes a variety of different types of foods, including fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and healthy sources of protein. This can help you feel full and satisfied without overloading on unhealthy fatty foods.
