In the United States, every child is required to attend school until they reach the age of 18 or graduate from high school. However, not every child is able to learn to read and write successfully. Some children may have learning disabilities that make it difficult for them to learn to read and write, while others may face challenges such as poverty, lack of access to quality education, or unstable home environments that make it difficult for them to learn. As a result, some adults in the United States may not be able to read or write even though they have gone to school. It is important for adults who are illiterate to seek out resources such as adult literacy programs that can help them improve their reading and writing skills.
