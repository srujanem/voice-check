Doctor Who is a long-running British science fiction television show about a time-traveling alien called the Doctor. The Doctor is a Time Lord, which means that they can travel through time and space using a spaceship called the TARDIS (Time And Relative Dimension In Space). The Doctor has the ability to regenerate, which means that they can change their appearance and personality when they are close to death, so there have been many actors who have played the role of the Doctor over the years. The Doctor travels through time and space with human companions, visiting different planets and encountering all sorts of alien creatures and villains. Along the way, they help people and try to protect the universe from danger. The show is known for its clever storylines, interesting characters, and exciting action scenes. It is also known for its humor and for its ability to be both serious and lighthearted at the same time. Many people enjoy watching "Doctor Who" because it is a fun and engaging show that can be enjoyed by people of all ages.
