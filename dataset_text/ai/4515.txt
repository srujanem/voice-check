Zyrtec, Claritin, and Benadryl are all medications that can be used to treat allergy symptoms, but they work in different ways and have some differences in terms of how they are used.\n\nZyrtec and Claritin are both antihistamines. They can be taken once a day to help reduce symptoms such as sneezing, runny nose, and itchy eyes.\n\nBenadryl is also an antihistamine, but it works differently from Zyrtec and Claritin. It is usually taken as needed to relieve allergy symptoms, and it may make you feel drowsy.\n\nIn general, Zyrtec and Claritin are considered to be less likely to cause drowsiness than Benadryl.
