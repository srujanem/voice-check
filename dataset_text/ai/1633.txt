Sure! Osmosis is the movement of water from an area where it is more concentrated to an area where it is less concentrated. Imagine you have a cup with sugar dissolved in the water. If you put a straw in the cup, the water in the straw would be less sweet than the water in the cup because there is less sugar in the straw. The water in the straw would want to move to the cup to balance out the concentration of sugar. That movement of water is osmosis. Another way to think about it is that osmosis is like a game of "follow the leader." The water molecules are the players, and they want to be where other water molecules are. If there are more water molecules on one side of a membrane (like the straw), then the water molecules on the other side will try to move over to that side to be with the other water molecules.
