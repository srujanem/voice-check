Cell phones have batteries that are built into the phone and cannot be easily removed or replaced by the user. There are a few reasons for this: 
1. Convenience: If the battery is built into the phone, you don't have to carry around extra batteries or worry about running out of power. You can just charge your phone when the battery runs low. 
2. Size: If cell phones had interchangeable batteries, they would need to be designed with a compartment to hold the battery, which would make them larger and less portable. 
3. Safety: Removable batteries can be a safety hazard if they are not handled correctly. For example, if a battery is damaged or leaking, it could catch fire or cause other problems. By building the battery into the phone, manufacturers can ensure that it is properly protected and safe to use. 
4. Cost: Removable batteries require additional parts and manufacturing processes, which can increase the cost of the phone. By using a built-in battery, manufacturers can save money and pass those savings on to the consumer.
