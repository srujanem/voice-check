A tail wheel on an airplane is usually small because it doesn't have to support much weight. The main wheels, which are located near the front of the airplane, carry most of the weight of the airplane when it is on the ground. The tail wheel is only used to help the airplane turn and to keep it stable when it is taxiing (moving on the ground). It doesn't have to bear much of the weight of the airplane, so it doesn't need to be very big. Does that make sense?
