When you put your cell phone close to your speakers, you might be able to hear a high-pitched sound. This is called a "cell phone interference." Here's how it works: Your cell phone sends and receives signals using radio waves. These signals are a type of energy that can be picked up by other electronic devices, like your speakers. When the signals from your cell phone get too close to your speakers, they can interfere with the sound coming from the speakers, and you might hear a weird noise. It's kind of like when you're trying to watch a movie on your TV, but someone nearby is talking on the phone and it's hard to hear the movie. The phone call is like the cell phone signals, and the movie is like the sound coming from the speakers. The phone call is interfering with the movie, just like the cell phone signals are interfering with the sound from the speakers. Does that make sense?
