Breathing and heartbeat are both controlled by your body's automatic nervous system, which means they happen automatically without you having to think about them. The automatic nervous system is responsible for controlling things like your heart rate, blood pressure, and digestion.\n\nYour heart is a very important organ that pumps blood to all parts of your body. It needs to beat at a certain rate in order to keep your body healthy and functioning properly. If your heart rate gets too fast or too slow, it can cause problems.\n\nBreathing, on the other hand, is something that we can control to some extent. We can take deep breaths, hold our breath, or even control our breathing when we are singing or playing a musical instrument.\n\nOverall, it is better for your body's automatic nervous system to control your heart rate because it helps to keep your body functioning properly and keeps you healthy. If you tried to control your heart rate, you might accidentally make it beat too fast or too slow, which could cause problems.
