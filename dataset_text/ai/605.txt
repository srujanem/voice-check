Human beings are social animals and we have developed certain cultural norms and behaviors that help us interact with one another. One of these norms is the idea of modesty, or the idea that certain parts of the body should be covered up or kept private. This idea of modesty can vary from culture to culture, but it is generally based on the belief that it is important to maintain a sense of decorum and respect in social situations. 
In many cultures, the private parts of the body, such as the genitalia and buttocks, are considered private and are therefore typically covered up in public. This is often done for reasons of hygiene and to prevent the spread of disease, as well as to maintain a sense of dignity and respect. Covering up these parts of the body can also help to prevent unwanted attention or advances from others. 
Overall, the idea of modesty and covering up private parts is a social norm that has developed over time and is meant to help us interact with one another in a respectful and appropriate way.
