Quantum levitation is a phenomenon that occurs when a superconducting material is placed in a magnetic field. Superconducting materials are materials that have zero electrical resistance, which means that they can conduct electricity without losing any energy. When a superconducting material is placed in a magnetic field, it will create its own magnetic field to counteract the external field. This can cause the superconducting material to "float" or levitate above the surface it is placed on, as the opposing magnetic fields can cancel out the gravitational force that would normally pull the material down. The reason the person in the video says that the levitation is "locked" is because the levitation only occurs when the superconducting material is in a specific position relative to the magnetic field. If the material moves too far from this position, the levitation will be lost and the material will fall back down to the surface.
