Companies can make money off of free software in a few different ways. One way is through advertising. For example, Google may show ads to users of its free software, like Chrome, in order to generate revenue. Another way companies can make money from free software is by offering additional services or features for a fee. For example, Google may offer a premium version of Chrome with extra features that users can pay for. Finally, companies can also make money from free software by using it to gather data about users and selling that data to third parties. For example, Google may collect data about how users use Chrome in order to improve the browser and also to sell that data to advertisers. Overall, while the software itself may be free, companies can still generate revenue through advertising, additional features, and data collection.
