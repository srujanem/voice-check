Sure! Declension in German refers to the way that nouns, pronouns, and adjectives change their form to reflect their role in the sentence. For example, the word for "book" in German is "das Buch." But if "das Buch" is the subject of the sentence, it stays "das Buch." If it's the object of the sentence, it changes to "das Buch" (which sounds the same, but is spelled differently). Here are some examples to illustrate: Subject: Das Buch ist interessant. (The book is interesting.) Object: Ich lese das Buch. (I am reading the book.) In the first sentence, "das Buch" is the subject of the sentence, so it stays "das Buch." In the second sentence, "das Buch" is the object of the sentence, so it changes to "das Buch."
