Sure! Keynesian economics is a way of thinking about the economy and how to try and make it work better. It's named after a man named John Maynard Keynes, who was an economist in the early 1900s. One of the main ideas in Keynesian economics is that the government should try to help the economy when it's not doing well. For example, if a lot of people are out of work and there isn't a lot of money being spent, the government might try to spend more money on things like building roads and schools to try and create jobs and get people spending again. Another idea in Keynesian economics is that prices and wages might not always go down when the economy is not doing well, like some other economists believed. Instead, Keynes thought that sometimes prices and wages can stay the same or even go up, which can make it harder for the economy to recover. So, overall, Keynesian economics is about trying to use the power of the government to help the economy when it's not doing well, and to try and make sure that prices and wages don't get in the way of the economy getting better.
