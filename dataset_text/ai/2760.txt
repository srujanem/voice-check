The Renaissance was a time period in European history that lasted from the 14th to the 17th century. It is called the "Renaissance" because it was a time of great cultural and intellectual "rebirth" after the Middle Ages, which was a time of cultural and intellectual decline. During the Renaissance, people in Europe became interested in exploring and learning about the world again. They started to rediscover the ideas and art of the ancient Greeks and Romans, and they began to think about things in new and different ways. Many famous artists, writers, and scientists lived during the Renaissance, and their work has had a lasting influence on the world. The Renaissance was not really a "movement," like a march or a protest. It was more like a period of time when people were doing a lot of interesting and important things. It was a time of great change and growth in Europe, and it paved the way for the modern world we live in today.
