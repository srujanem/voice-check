Netflix acquires content in a variety of ways. One way is by licensing shows and movies from other studios and production companies. This means that Netflix pays a fee to the owners of the content in order to show it on their platform. The amount of the fee can vary depending on a number of factors, including the popularity of the content and how exclusive the license is (for example, if Netflix is the only place that the content can be streamed). Watching a show does not directly cause that show to get paid more. However, if a show is popular and has a lot of viewers on Netflix, it could potentially lead to the show being renewed for additional seasons or to the creators of the show negotiating higher fees for future licenses. As for how Netflix is able to pay for all of its content with just a monthly subscription fee of around $8, it's important to remember that Netflix has millions of subscribers around the world. When you pay your monthly subscription fee, you are contributing to the overall pool of money that Netflix has available to pay for content. Netflix also generates revenue from other sources, such as selling advertising and producing its own content. When you add all of this together, it allows Netflix to pay for the content that it licenses and produces.
