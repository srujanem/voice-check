Old cartoons might look jittery because they were made using a technique called "hand-drawn animation." This means that each frame of the cartoon was drawn by hand, one at a time, by an artist. Because it was done by hand, there might be slight variations in each frame, which can make the overall animation look a bit jittery. It's also possible that the jitteriness you're seeing is due to the way the cartoon was transferred from its original film or video format to the format you're watching it in now. Some older films and videos can suffer from deterioration over time, which can cause the image to become distorted or jittery.
