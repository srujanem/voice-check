In outer space, there is no air or other matter to slow down objects as they move. This means that objects can continue moving at very high speeds without any resistance. On Earth, when an object is moving through air or other matter, it encounters a force called drag that slows it down. This is why it is much harder to achieve very high speeds when moving through air or other matter on Earth.\n\nImagine throwing a baseball. If you throw it straight up into the air, it will eventually slow down and fall back to the ground. But if you throw it in the vacuum of space, it would continue moving at the same speed until it was stopped by some other force, like gravity.
