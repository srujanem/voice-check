There is a connection between our brains and our stomachs called the gut-brain connection. When we feel strong emotions, our brains can send signals to our stomachs that can cause physical feelings. For example, if we feel sad or anxious, we might feel a "knot" in our stomach. Similarly, if we feel embarrassed or regretful, we might feel a "sinking" feeling in our stomach. These physical feelings are caused by changes in our bodies that happen when we experience strong emotions. It's all part of how our bodies respond to the world around us.
