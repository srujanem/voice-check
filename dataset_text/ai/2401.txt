Pigeons are very intelligent birds and are able to be trained to do a variety of tasks, including carrying messages from one place to another. In the past, people used pigeons as a way to send messages over long distances because they are able to fly very fast and can navigate their way back to a specific location. To train a pigeon to carry messages, people would first teach the pigeon to fly back and forth between two locations. They would do this by releasing the pigeon from one location and then rewarding the pigeon with food when it returned to the other location. Over time, the pigeon would learn to fly back and forth between the two locations. Next, people would attach a small container called a "pigeon post" to the pigeon's leg. Inside the pigeon post, they would place a message that they wanted to send to someone at the other location. The pigeon would then fly back and forth between the two locations, carrying the message in the pigeon post. While it is possible for pigeons to fly away and not return, they have a strong homing instinct and are usually able to find their way back to their "home" location. This is why they were used as a reliable way to send messages in the past.
