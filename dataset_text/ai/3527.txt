Gravity is the force that pulls objects towards each other. It's what makes things fall down to the ground and what keeps the Earth, the moon, and all the planets in orbit around the sun. Imagine you have a ball and you drop it. What happens? It falls towards the ground. That's because the Earth is pulling on the ball with its gravity. The Earth is so big and heavy that it has a really strong gravitational pull. That's why everything falls towards the ground when we drop it. But why does the Earth have a gravitational pull in the first place? Well, it's because of all the mass (or stuff) that the Earth is made of. You see, everything in the universe that has mass also has gravity. The more mass something has, the stronger its gravity is. And since the Earth has a lot of mass, it has a strong gravitational pull. So that's how gravity works! It's the force that pulls objects towards each other because of their mass.
