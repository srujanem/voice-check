As a result, they may not be able to understand or respond appropriately to being awakened, and they may become agitated or combative. 
It is also possible that the sleepwalker could accidentally hurt themselves or someone else while sleepwalking, either by tripping or falling, or by accidentally bumping into or touching something that could cause injury. For these reasons, it is generally best to let a sleepwalker continue their sleepwalking episode and to try to guide them back to bed if necessary, rather than trying to wake them up. If the sleepwalker is in a potentially dangerous situation, such as if they are trying to leave the house or if they are near a hazard, it may be necessary to gently guide them away from the danger and back to bed.
