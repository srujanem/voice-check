It's not necessarily a crime for a news organization to interview someone who is wanted by the authorities, as long as the journalists are not participating in any illegal activities or assisting the person in evading capture. However, it can be risky for journalists to interview wanted individuals, as they could potentially be putting themselves in danger. It's also important for journalists to follow ethical guidelines, such as ensuring that their reporting is fair and accurate, and not causing harm to the people they are interviewing or to others. In some cases, journalists may need to take special precautions to protect their own safety and the safety of those they are interviewing.
