Sure! Music theory is a set of rules that helps us understand how music works. A key is a group of notes that sound good together. For example, the key of C has the notes C, D, E, F, G, A, and B. A scale is a series of notes played in a specific order. There are different types of scales, like major scales and minor scales. Major scales have a happy sound, and minor scales have a sad sound. One important thing in music theory is the concept of chords. Chords are groups of notes played together, and they give music its harmony. There are many different chords, and they can be played in different ways to create different sounds. That's just a very basic introduction to music theory, but I hope it helps!
