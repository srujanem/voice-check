Falsetto is a type of singing in which a person sings with a very high and light voice. It is different from normal singing because it is produced using a different technique. Instead of using the chest voice, which is the voice that most people use for normal singing, falsetto is produced using the head voice. The head voice is a higher and lighter type of singing that is produced by using the muscles in the throat and mouth in a different way.\n\nThere are no specific rules about when high-pitched singing becomes falsetto. It is a matter of personal preference and style. Some people may use falsetto to sing high notes that they cannot reach with their chest voice, while others may use it to create a specific effect or style in their music.
