Texting and driving is dangerous because it can cause you to take your attention off the road and focus on your phone instead. This can make it hard for you to see what's happening around you, and it can make it hard for you to react quickly if something unexpected happens. That's why it is illegal in many places. However, it's not illegal everywhere because it can be hard for the police to know if someone is texting and driving. They can't see what's happening inside a car, so they have to wait until they see someone doing something dangerous before they can pull them over. Even then, it can be hard to prove that someone was texting and driving. It's important to remember that even though it may not be illegal in all places, it's still very dangerous and it's not worth the risk. If you need to text or use your phone while you're driving, it's much safer to pull over and stop the car first. That way, you can give your full attention to your phone and make sure you're not putting yourself or anyone else in danger.
