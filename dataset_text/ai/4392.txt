Zoos and amusement parks offer membership programs to allow visitors to pay a single upfront fee that gives them unlimited access to the park for a certain period of time, such as a year. This can be a more convenient and cost-effective option for people who plan on visiting the park multiple times in a year.\n\nWhile it may seem like the park would lose money by allowing someone to visit multiple times for the same price as a single visit, the membership fees are usually set high enough to cover the cost of providing the additional visits. In addition, the park may make money from other sources, such as food and merchandise sales, which can help offset the cost of providing the membership.\n\nOverall, the goal of a membership program is to encourage people to visit the park more often, which can ultimately lead to increased profits for the park.
