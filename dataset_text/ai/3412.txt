Edward Snowden leaked classified information about the United States government's surveillance programs to the media. This is considered a violation of the law because the information he leaked was classified, which means that it was considered secret and was not supposed to be shared with the general public. The government believed that by leaking this information, Snowden had jeopardized national security and put the lives of people in danger. In other words, Snowden broke the rules by sharing information that was meant to be kept secret, and the government believes that this caused harm to the country and its people. Because of this, Snowden has been charged with crimes and is considered a fugitive by the US government. Some people believe that Snowden is a hero who exposed important information that the public had a right to know, while others think that he broke the law and should be punished.
