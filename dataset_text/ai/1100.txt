Flies are able to move very quickly and are able to change direction quickly because they have a lot of tiny muscles in their wings that allow them to make rapid movements. They also have very good vision and are able to quickly perceive threats and avoid them. In addition, flies are very agile and are able to quickly shift their weight to change direction. 
When you try to swat a fly, it is able to sense the movement and can quickly fly away to avoid being hit. This is why it might seem like the fly is dodging your attacks. 
Overall, flies have evolved to be very good at avoiding threats and staying safe, which is why they are able to avoid your attacks even when you think you have them cornered.
