Windows ME (short for "Windows Millennium Edition") was a version of the Windows operating system that was released in 2000. It was known for being buggy and unstable, which means it would frequently crash or stop working properly. It also had a number of other problems, such as poor support for certain hardware and software, and difficulty connecting to the internet. These problems made it frustrating to use and many people didn't like it. In fact, it is generally considered to be one of the worst versions of Windows ever released.
