High schools are generally allowed to use the logos of professional sports teams as long as they are using them in a non-commercial way and are not trying to profit from the use of the logo. In other words, the high school is not selling merchandise with the team's logo on it or using the logo to promote a product or service. 
Additionally, the high schools may have obtained permission from the team or the National Football League (NFL) to use the logo. It is not uncommon for high schools to reach out to professional sports teams and ask for permission to use their logo or mascot as a way to show support for the team and create a sense of school spirit. 
Overall, it is important to remember that professional sports teams own the rights to their logos and can take legal action if they feel that someone is using their logo without permission or in a way that could damage the team's reputation. However, as long as the high school is using the logo in a respectful and non-commercial way, it is generally allowed.
