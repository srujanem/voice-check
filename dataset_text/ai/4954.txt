Derealization is a feeling of detachment or disconnection from the world around you. It can make things feel like they're not real, or like you're observing them from a distance. It's not uncommon to experience derealization when you're under a lot of stress or going through a difficult time in your life. It can also be a symptom of certain medical conditions or a side effect of certain medications.\n\nIt's not fully understood why derealization happens, but it may be related to changes in brain chemistry or the way the brain processes information. Some people may be more prone to derealization than others due to genetics or other factors.\n\nIf you're experiencing derealization and it's causing you distress, it's important to talk to a doctor or mental health professional. They can help you determine the cause of your derealization and provide treatment to help you feel more connected to the world around you.
