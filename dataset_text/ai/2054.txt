Steam is a platform that allows you to play video games on your computer. It works by installing a program on your computer that you can use to browse and purchase games, and then download and play them. You can also use Steam to communicate with other people who are playing the same games as you, and join groups and forums to discuss different games. Basically, Steam makes it easy for you to find and play a wide variety of video games on your computer, and also helps you connect with other gamers from all around the world. It's kind of like a big virtual store for video games, where you can browse and buy games, and then play them on your computer.
