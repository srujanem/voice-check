Vegetarians are people who do not eat meat, including poultry, pork, and beef. Some vegetarians also choose not to eat fish or other seafood. These vegetarians are called "strict vegetarians" or "vegans." They believe that all animals, including fish, should not be used for food or any other purpose. On the other hand, some vegetarians do choose to eat fish or other seafood, even though they do not eat other types of meat. These vegetarians are sometimes called "semi-vegetarians" or "pescatarians." They may choose to eat fish or seafood for a variety of reasons, such as for the health benefits, because they enjoy the taste, or because they believe that fish and seafood are not the same as other animals and therefore do not consider it to be "meat." In short, vegetarians are people who do not eat meat, but some vegetarians choose to eat fish or seafood while others do not.
