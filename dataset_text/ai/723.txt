There are many different organizations and websites that review and rate video games, and they often give out awards for the "best" games of the year. These awards can be called things like "Game of the Year," "Top Game," or "Best Game." These organizations and websites have experts who play and review a lot of different games, and then they decide which ones they think are the best based on various criteria, such as graphics, gameplay, story, and innovation. 
It's important to note that these awards are just opinions, and different people might have different opinions about which games are the best. Also, the titles "Game of the Year" or "Top Game" are just marketing terms that are meant to highlight the fact that a particular game has received a lot of positive reviews or has won an award. They are not necessarily official titles or certifications. 
In general, it's always a good idea to do your own research and read reviews from multiple sources before deciding to buy a game. This can help you get a better sense of what other people think about the game and whether it might be something you'll enjoy.
