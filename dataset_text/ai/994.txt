Dentists are their own separate profession because they focus on the health of the teeth and mouth, which is a different part of the body than the rest of the medical system. Dental insurance is separate from regular medical insurance because the care that dentists provide is different and may not be covered by regular medical insurance. 
Dentists are trained specifically to diagnose and treat problems with the teeth, gums, and other parts of the mouth. They use different tools and techniques than doctors who focus on other parts of the body, and they may have different ways of treating patients. 
Just like how you go to different doctors for different health issues, you may go to a dentist for issues with your teeth and mouth, and you may go to a different doctor for issues with your heart, lungs, or other parts of your body.
