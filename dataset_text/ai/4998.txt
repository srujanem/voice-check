\nLeaving a laptop or mobile phone plugged in for an extended period of time can be detrimental to the battery because it can cause the battery to become overcharged. Overcharging a battery can cause it to degrade over time, which can lead to a decrease in the battery's overall capacity and performance.\n\nImagine if you were to fill up a water bottle and then leave the cap off for a long time. The water would eventually evaporate and the bottle would no longer be full. In the same way, a battery can become overcharged and lose some of its capacity if it is left plugged in for too long.\n\nIt's important to unplug your laptop or mobile phone once it is fully charged so that the battery does not become overcharged. You should also try to avoid leaving your device plugged in all the time when it is not in use. This can help to prolong the life of the battery and keep it performing at its best.
