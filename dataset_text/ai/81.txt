Humpback whales are famous for their complex underwater vocalizations, often referred to as whale songs. These sequences of moans, grunts, and whistles can last for hours and travel vast distances across ocean basins. Remarkably, all males within a specific ocean population tend to sing the same evolving song pattern, which changes gradually from year to year. While scientists believe these elaborate vocalizations play a major role in mating rituals and social navigation, the precise mechanics behind how humpback whales compose and teach these ocean melodies remain a fascinating marine biology mystery.
