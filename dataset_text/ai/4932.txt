Accuracy is a measure of how close a device's measurements are to the true or correct value. To determine the accuracy of a device, we need to compare its measurements to a known, accurate reference. For example, to determine the accuracy of a heart rate sensor, we could compare its readings to the heart rate measured by a medical-grade ECG machine.\n\nTo compare the accuracy of two different devices, we can use the same reference and see which one is closer to the true value. For example, if we compare a heart rate sensor to an ECG machine, we can see which one gives readings that are closer to the ECG machine's readings. The device that is closer to the reference is more accurate.\n\nIt's important to note that accuracy can vary depending on the specific device and the conditions in which it is used.
