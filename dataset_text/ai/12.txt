The event horizon of a black hole represents the ultimate boundary of spacetime beyond which information cannot escape. As matter falls toward the singularity, intense gravitational forces generate immense heat, causing accretion disks to emit high-energy X-ray radiation. Modern astrophysics relies on space telescopes and radio interferometer arrays to observe these energetic phenomena indirecty. In conclusion, the study of celestial singularities continues to provide key insights into general relativity and theoretical physics.
