Critical thinking is a way of thinking about problems or ideas in a careful and organized way. It helps us to think about things more deeply, to ask questions, and to consider different possibilities before making a decision or forming an opinion. Critical thinking is useful for humans because it helps us to make better decisions and solve problems more effectively. For example, if you are trying to decide what to do about a problem at school, critical thinking can help you to think about all the different options available to you and choose the best one. It can also help you to think about the potential consequences of each option, so that you can make a decision that is well-informed and well-reasoned. Overall, critical thinking is an important skill because it helps us to think more clearly and logically, and to make decisions that are based on evidence and sound reasoning, rather than just following our emotions or making decisions based on limited information.
