When we think, our brains are processing and organizing information. Our brains are made up of billions of tiny cells called neurons, which communicate with each other through electrical and chemical signals. When we think about something, our neurons send messages back and forth to each other very quickly, forming patterns and connections that help us understand and remember things.\n\nAs for why we "feel" our thoughts in our head, it's because that's where our brain is located. Our brain is the organ that controls all of our thoughts and actions, so it makes sense that we would feel those things happening in our head. Our feet and other parts of our body don't play a direct role in thinking and processing information, so we don't typically feel those things happening there.
