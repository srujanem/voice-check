The tag "ELI5" stands for "Explain Like I'm 5." It is used to let other people know that the person posting the question or comment wants a simple explanation of something. Some topics can be complex and hard to understand, especially for people who are not familiar with them. By using the ELI5 tag, people are asking for an explanation in a way that is easy to understand, using simple words and concepts. This can be helpful for people who are new to a subject or who have difficulty understanding more technical explanations.
