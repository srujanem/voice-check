The internet is a network of computers that are connected to each other and can communicate with each other. When you use the internet, you are using a device, like a computer or a phone, to send and receive information from other computers. Websites are just files that are stored on computers called servers. When you want to visit a website, your device sends a request over the internet to the server where the website is stored. The server then sends the website back to your device, and your web browser displays it on your screen. So, all websites are stored on servers that are connected to the internet, and when you want to visit a website, your device communicates with the server to get the website and display it for you.
