A metal allergy is a type of allergic reaction that occurs when your body's immune system mistakes a certain metal as a harmful substance and tries to protect you from it. When you come into contact with the metal that you are allergic to, your immune system releases chemicals called histamines. These histamines cause symptoms such as itching, redness, and swelling on your skin. In the case of a nickel allergy, the rash that forms on your skin is a result of the histamines that your body released in response to the presence of nickel. The histamines then cause the blood vessels in your skin to become inflamed, leading to redness, swelling, and itching. It's important to note that not everyone who comes into contact with nickel will develop an allergy. Some people may be more sensitive to nickel than others and may be more likely to develop an allergy.
