The stock exchange is a place where people can buy and sell stocks (a type of investment). Stocks represent a share in the ownership of a company and can be bought and sold just like other products. The stock exchange has a physical location, called the trading floor, where people called stock traders can buy and sell stocks. These stock traders work for different companies, such as banks or brokerage firms, and they use hand signals and electronic systems to buy and sell stocks for their clients (people who have hired them to manage their money). The different color jackets worn by the stock traders have different meanings. For example, a trader wearing a red jacket might represent a company that buys stocks, while a trader wearing a green jacket might represent a company that sells stocks. Overall, the stock exchange is a place where people can buy and sell stocks in an organized and regulated way. It helps facilitate the process of buying and selling stocks and allows people to invest in companies they believe in.
