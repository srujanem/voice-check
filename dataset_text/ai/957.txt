The foods that we eat for breakfast are different in different cultures, and what is considered a "typical" breakfast food can vary greatly from place to place. In some cultures, breakfast may include savory dishes like rice or noodles, while in others it may be more common to eat sweet foods like pastries or fruit. 
The reason that certain foods, like pancakes, eggs, bacon, and cereal, are often associated with breakfast in certain cultures is because they are convenient and easy to prepare in the morning, and they can provide a quick and filling start to the day. These foods also tend to be relatively neutral in flavor, so they can be paired with a variety of other ingredients to suit different tastes. 
It's important to remember that there are no hard and fast rules about what we should or should not eat for breakfast, and that people are free to choose whatever foods they prefer to start their day.
