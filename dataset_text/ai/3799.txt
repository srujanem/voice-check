Contestants on Jeopardy typically choose the lowest amount of money questions first because those questions are usually easier than the ones worth more money. The idea is to get as many points as possible by answering as many questions correctly as possible, so starting with the easier questions can help build up a lead. Additionally, if a contestant gets a question wrong, they lose the amount of money that the question was worth, so starting with the lower-value questions means there is less risk of losing a lot of money if they get a question wrong.
