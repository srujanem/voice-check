Smartphones use a device called a gyroscope to know how to rotate correctly when "Auto-Rotate" is enabled. A gyroscope is a small device that can sense the angle and orientation of a phone or other device. It works by using a spinning wheel inside of it that is able to detect even small changes in the orientation of the device.\n\nWhen "Auto-Rotate" is enabled on a smartphone, the gyroscope is constantly sensing the orientation of the phone. If the phone is turned from a portrait orientation (vertical) to a landscape orientation (horizontal), the gyroscope will detect this change and send a signal to the phone's software to rotate the screen accordingly. The phone's software will then rotate the screen to match the new orientation of the phone.\n\nThis is why when you turn your phone from a portrait to a landscape orientation, the screen will automatically rotate to match the new orientation. It's all thanks to the gyroscope and the phone's software working together to make sure the screen is always displayed correctly.
