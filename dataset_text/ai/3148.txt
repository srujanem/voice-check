Some pool tables have sensors that can detect when a ball goes into a pocket or touches a cushion. These sensors can also detect if a ball has left the table entirely. When the cue ball is returned to the player after a scratch, it's because the sensors on the table detected that the cue ball left the table or went into a pocket. The other balls may not be returned to the player because they are still on the table and can be used for the next shot. It's not a size thing, it's just the way the sensors on the table are programmed to work.
