A burrito and a soft taco are both made with a tortilla, which is a thin, round, and flat bread that is made from corn or wheat flour. The main difference between a burrito and a soft taco is the way they are filled and wrapped. 
A burrito is typically larger than a soft taco and is made by wrapping a tortilla around a filling of beans, meat, cheese, and other ingredients. The tortilla is then folded over the filling to create a cylindrical shape. 
A soft taco, on the other hand, is made by placing the filling inside a tortilla and then folding it over. The tortilla is not wrapped around the filling in the same way as it is in a burrito, so the filling is more visible and the taco is usually smaller than a burrito. 
So, while both a burrito and a soft taco are made with a tortilla, they are different types of dishes because of the way they are filled and wrapped.
