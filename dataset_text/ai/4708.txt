Tea bags often have a warning label that says "Do Not Microwave" because microwaves can cause the tea bag to overheat and potentially catch fire. The warning is a precautionary measure to prevent accidents.\n\nThe metal staples used to hold the tea bag together can also cause problems when microwaved. The staples can create sparks and potentially start a fire in the microwave.\n\nIt is generally safer to heat water in a microwave-safe container and then steep the tea bag in the hot water, rather than microwaving the tea bag directly. This will allow you to enjoy your tea without any risks.
