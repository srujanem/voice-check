When you plug your phone into a USB charger, the charger sends a small amount of electricity to your phone. Your phone's battery needs a certain amount of electricity to charge, and it can only take in a certain amount of electricity at a time.\n\nYour phone has a little computer inside it that helps it talk to the charger and figure out how much electricity it needs. When you plug in your phone, the charger sends a signal to the phone saying, "Hey, I'm here and I can give you electricity." Then the phone sends a signal back to the charger saying, "Okay, I need this much electricity." The charger sends the right amount of electricity to the phone to charge the battery, and the phone's computer helps make sure the battery doesn't get too much or too little electricity.\n\nDoes that make sense?
