The saying "one in the hand is worth two in the bush" means that it is better to have something that is certain and available to you now, rather than taking a risk and trying to get more in the future. The saying is usually used to advise someone to be content with what they have and not to try to get more, especially if it involves taking a risk. For example, if you have one dollar in your hand, it is better to keep that dollar and use it for something you need, rather than trying to get two dollars in the future by gambling or taking on a risky business venture. This is because the dollar you have in your hand is guaranteed, while the two dollars in the future are not certain. So, the saying is meant to encourage people to be satisfied with what they have and not to take unnecessary risks in an attempt to get more.
