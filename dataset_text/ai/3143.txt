Cellphones are generally not prohibited at gas stations, but it is generally recommended to avoid using them while fueling your vehicle. The main reason for this is that cellphones (and other electronic devices) can potentially create a spark, which could ignite any flammable fumes that might be present. Petrol (also known as gasoline) is a type of fuel that is used to power vehicles. It is made up of a mixture of chemicals, and when it is pumped into your car, it is stored in a tank under the car. Sometimes, when petrol is pumped, it can create fumes that can float in the air around the petrol pump. These fumes can be flammable, which means that they can catch on fire easily. If you use a cellphone or any other electronic device while you are fueling your car, there is a small chance that the device could create a spark. This spark could then ignite the flammable petrol fumes, causing a fire. To be safe, it is best to avoid using electronic devices, including cellphones, while you are fueling your vehicle.
