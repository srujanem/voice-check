Water on Mars is thought to have existed in the past, but it is not present on the surface of the planet in large quantities today. Scientists believe that liquid water once flowed on the surface of Mars and that there may still be water ice at the poles and underground. It is possible that Mars lost its water over time due to a number of factors, including solar wind and radiation, which can cause water to evaporate into the atmosphere. It is also possible that Mars' water was absorbed into the ground or froze underground. It is still not fully understood where all of the water on Mars went, but scientists are continuing to study the planet and learn more about its water history.
