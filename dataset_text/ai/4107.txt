MTV and VH1 stopped playing music videos because they realized that people were more interested in watching other types of shows, such as reality TV shows. As a result, they started to focus more on these types of programs and less on music videos. Music videos are still a good way to promote music, but there are now many other ways for people to discover and listen to new music, such as streaming services like Spotify and Apple Music. So while music videos are still important, they are not as important as they used to be in helping artists sell their music.
