Near-sightedness, also known as myopia, is a condition in which a person has difficulty seeing objects that are far away. This is because the light entering their eye is not focused properly, and the image appears blurry. Far-sightedness, also known as hyperopia, is a condition in which a person has difficulty seeing objects that are close up. This is because the light entering their eye is focused behind the retina, and the image appears blurry.\n\nIt is possible for a person to have both near-sightedness and far-sightedness at the same time. This is called mixed vision or mixed astigmatism. In this case, the person may have difficulty seeing both far and near objects clearly. The two conditions do not cancel each other out, but rather both need to be corrected in order to improve the person's vision. This can be done through glasses, contact lenses, or surgery.
