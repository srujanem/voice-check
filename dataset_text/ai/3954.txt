In professional mixed martial arts (MMA) fights, the fight is not over until the referee stops the fight. This means that even if a fighter is knocked out, the other fighter is not allowed to stop fighting until the referee intervenes. The reason for this rule is to make sure that the fighter who has been knocked out is not hit any more times than necessary, and to give the referee time to step in and stop the fight. It's important to remember that the goal of the fight is for one fighter to defeat the other, but not to cause serious injury. So even though a fighter may have been knocked out, the other fighter is still required to follow the rules and not hit them any more after they are unconscious.
