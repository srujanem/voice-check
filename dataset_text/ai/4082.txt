Military strength is typically measured by the size and capabilities of a country's military forces, as well as its defense budget and access to resources such as weapons and technology. The United States is considered to be a superpower because it has a very large and advanced military, with a defense budget that is larger than the budgets of most other countries. In addition to its military strength, the United States also has a large and influential economy, and is a leader in many areas of technology and innovation. These factors all contribute to its status as a superpower.
