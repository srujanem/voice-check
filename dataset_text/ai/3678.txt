The way that maps are drawn with certain continents on top and others on the bottom is mostly a matter of tradition and convention, rather than any inherent characteristic of the continents themselves. In other words, there is no "correct" way to draw a map, and different maps can be oriented in different ways depending on what the mapmaker wants to show. One reason that maps are often drawn with Europe and Asia on top is because these are the two largest continents, and they are often the focus of many maps. However, there is no reason that a map couldn't be drawn with Australia on top instead. In fact, there are many maps that are drawn this way, especially maps that are focused on the Pacific region. So, to answer your question, there is no reason that global maps couldn't be reversed, with Australia on top and Europe on the bottom. It's just a matter of how the mapmaker wants to draw the map.
