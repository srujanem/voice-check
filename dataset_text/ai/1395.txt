Mercenary businesses, also known as private military contractors or private security companies, provide military or security services to governments, organizations, or individuals in exchange for financial compensation. These services can include training, logistics, and security operations, among others. 
Private military contractors are not considered to be part of a country's military forces, and they are not subject to the same laws and regulations as military personnel. Instead, they operate under the laws of the countries in which they are based and the laws of the countries in which they operate. 
It is important to note that the use of private military contractors is a controversial topic, and there have been instances where contractors have been accused of misconduct or violations of international law. Some people believe that the use of private military contractors can blur the lines between military and civilian roles, and that these companies should be subject to greater oversight and regulation.
