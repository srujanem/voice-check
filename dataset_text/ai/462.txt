Irish music and pirate music share some similarities, but they are ultimately quite different. Irish music is a traditional genre of music that originated in Ireland and is characterized by its use of instruments like the fiddle, tin whistle, and bodhran (a type of drum). It is often fast-paced and upbeat, and often includes dancing. 
Pirate music, on the other hand, is a fictional genre of music that is often associated with pirates in popular culture (such as in movies and books). Pirate music is usually fast-paced and energetic, and often incorporates elements of folk music and sea shanties. 
It's possible that the reason you associate Irish music with pirate music is because they both have fast tempos and use similar instruments. However, they are distinct genres of music with their own unique histories and cultural roots.
