MRI, or magnetic resonance imaging, is a medical imaging technique that uses a strong magnetic field and radio waves to create detailed images of the inside of the body. It is a very useful tool for doctors to diagnose and treat a variety of medical conditions.\n\nSteel can affect an MRI machine because it is magnetic. When an MRI machine is in use, it creates a very strong magnetic field that can pull on magnetic objects, such as steel. This can be dangerous if a person is near the MRI machine and has something made of steel on their body, such as jewelry or a metallic object. The strong magnetic field can pull on the steel object and cause it to move towards the MRI machine, which can be dangerous or even deadly if the object hits the person or gets stuck in the machine.\n\nIt is important for people to remove all metal objects before undergoing an MRI scan to ensure their safety. If a person has a metal implant or other metal object inside their body, they should inform the MRI technician before the scan so that they can take the necessary precautions to ensure their safety.
