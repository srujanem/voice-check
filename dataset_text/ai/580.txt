It is not necessarily true that it is harder for women to lose weight compared to men. Weight loss depends on a variety of factors, including a person's diet, exercise habits, and overall lifestyle. 
Both men and women can experience weight gain or weight loss depending on the balance between the number of calories they consume and the number of calories they burn through physical activity. When a person consumes more calories than they burn, they may gain weight, and when they burn more calories than they consume, they may lose weight. 
It is important to note that each person is different and may have unique challenges or factors that affect their weight. It is not accurate or fair to make generalizations about the weight loss experiences of one gender versus another. What is most important is for individuals to find a healthy and sustainable approach to weight management that works for them.
