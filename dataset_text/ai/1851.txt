Sure! A fractal is a type of shape that is made up of smaller copies of itself, kind of like how a puzzle piece is made up of smaller puzzle pieces. Fractals are special because no matter how much you zoom in or out, the shape always looks the same. They can be found in nature, like in the patterns of leaves on a tree or the shape of a snowflake. They can also be created using math. Some people find fractals really interesting because they are so unique and can be very beautiful. Does that help explain what a fractal is?
