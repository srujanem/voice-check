A cable company like Comcast charges for basic cable because they are providing a service to you. They are responsible for setting up and maintaining the infrastructure (e.g., cables, satellites, etc.) that allows you to access TV channels and other programming. They also negotiate with the broadcasters and content providers to secure the rights to transmit their programming to you. All of these costs are factored into the price of the service that they offer. 
Using an antenna to access free, over-the-air TV programming is a different way of accessing TV channels. With an antenna, you are receiving the signal directly from the broadcaster, rather than through a cable or satellite service. The signal may not be as reliable, and you may not have access to as many channels as you would with a paid cable or satellite service. However, an antenna can be a good option for those who want to access a limited number of channels for free.
