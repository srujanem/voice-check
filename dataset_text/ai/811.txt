The penis is not a muscle in the same way that the muscles in your arms or legs are muscles. The penis is actually made up of two types of tissue: vascular tissue, which is responsible for erections, and spongy tissue, which fills with blood during an erection. 
Exercise can improve blood flow to the penis and may help with erectile dysfunction, but it will not make the penis itself grow in size. The size of the penis is determined by genetics and can vary significantly from one person to another. It is not possible to increase the size of the penis through exercise or any other type of physical intervention.
