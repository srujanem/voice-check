The Salem witch trials were a series of trials and executions in Salem, Massachusetts in the late 17th century. At the time, many people believed in witches and thought that some people were witches who could use magic to harm others. In Salem, some young girls accused several women of being witches and said that they had seen the women using magic to harm people. These accusations led to the trials of many people, and some of them were found guilty and sentenced to death. It was later discovered that the young girls had lied about seeing the women use magic, and the trials were seen as a terrible mistake.
