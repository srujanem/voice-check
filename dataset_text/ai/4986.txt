Sails are used to help a ship move, and they can make the ship go faster by catching the wind. However, sails are not always the best way to make a ship move. For example, if the wind is not strong enough or is coming from the wrong direction, the sails might not be able to help the ship move at all.\n\nSails also need to be raised and lowered using special ropes called "halyards." Raising and lowering the sails takes time and effort, so sailors only do it when they need to use the sails to move the ship.\n\nSo, to sum it up, sails are not always unfurled (opened) because they are not always the best way to make the ship move and because it takes time and effort to raise and lower them.
