Sugar substitutes are substances that are used to sweeten food and drinks without adding calories or increasing blood sugar levels. They are used as a way to reduce the amount of sugar and calories in a person's diet, especially for people with diabetes or those trying to lose weight. Some sugar substitutes, like saccharin, are very sweet but don't have any calories because they are not broken down or absorbed by the body in the same way that sugar is. When you eat sugar, your body digests it and turns it into energy. But when you eat a sugar substitute, it passes through your body without being digested or absorbed, so it doesn't provide any calories or energy. So, sugar substitutes can taste sweet just like sugar, but they don't have the same effect on your body because they are not broken down and absorbed in the same way. This is why they are a good option for people who want to reduce their sugar intake or avoid adding extra calories to their diet.
