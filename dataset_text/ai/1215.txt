It is generally illegal to wear headphones or earbuds while driving because it can be dangerous to be unable to hear your surroundings while operating a vehicle. When you are driving, it is important to be able to hear what is happening around you, such as sirens from emergency vehicles, honks from other cars, or even pedestrians trying to get your attention. Wearing headphones or earbuds can make it difficult to hear these sounds, which could put you and others in danger. 
However, deaf people are still able to drive safely because they have learned how to compensate for their hearing loss in other ways. For example, they may use special mirrors or sensors to help them detect what is happening around their car. They may also have a designated passenger who can communicate with them and help them stay aware of their surroundings. 
In summary, the reason it is illegal to wear headphones or earbuds while driving is because it can be dangerous to not be able to hear your surroundings, but deaf people are still able to drive safely because they have found ways to compensate for their hearing loss.
