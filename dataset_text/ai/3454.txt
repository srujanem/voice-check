A corporation is a type of business entity that is legally separate from its owners. This means that the corporation itself can enter into contracts, incur debts, and be sued, just like a person. The owners of a corporation are called shareholders, and they own shares of the corporation. They are not personally responsible for the debts of the corporation. A company is a business organization that sells goods or services. There are many different types of companies, such as sole proprietorships, partnerships, and corporations. A business is an organization or enterprise that is involved in the trade of goods, services, or both to consumers. In fiction, corporations are often depicted as being greedy and selfish, putting profits above the well-being of people and the environment. This may be because corporations, being legally separate from their owners, may be seen as less accountable for their actions and may be more focused on maximizing profits. However, not all corporations are portrayed negatively in fiction, and there are many examples of companies and businesses of other types being depicted as villains as well.
