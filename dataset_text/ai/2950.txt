Many Christian fundamentalists believe that the Bible, which is the holy book for Christians, contains prophecies about the land of Israel and the Jewish people. They believe that the state of Israel, which was established in 1948, is an important part of God's plan for the world and that the Jewish people have a special role to play in God's plan. They also believe that the land of Israel was given to the Jewish people by God and that they have a right to live there. Therefore, they often support the state of Israel and its policies. It's important to note that not all Christians feel this way and that there are many different beliefs within Christianity about the relationship between Christians, Jews, and the state of Israel.
