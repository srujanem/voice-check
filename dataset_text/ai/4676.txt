Cultural mores, also known as social norms, are the unwritten rules that govern how people behave in a particular society. These rules can be very different from one place to another because different societies have different histories, values, and beliefs.\n\nFor example, in some cultures it is considered polite to remove your shoes when entering someone's home, while in other cultures it is not. In some cultures it is customary to greet others with a hug or a kiss on the cheek, while in other cultures it is more common to bow or shake hands.\n\nAmericans have their own set of cultural mores that may be different from those of other countries. For example, in American culture it is often considered impolite to stare at someone or to speak loudly in public, while in other cultures these behaviors may be more acceptable.\n\nOverall, cultural mores are shaped by the values and beliefs of the society in which they exist, and they can be quite different from one place to another.
