Link karma is a measure of the popularity of the links that you submit to Reddit. It is calculated by adding up the number of upvotes and downvotes that your links receive from other users. The number of points you get for a submitted link is not directly related to your link karma. Your link karma may be lower than the number of points you received for a particular link because link karma is calculated based on the total number of upvotes and downvotes that you receive across all of the links that you submit, not just on one particular link. So, even if you received a lot of upvotes on one particular link, if you have submitted other links that received downvotes, it could bring your overall link karma down.
