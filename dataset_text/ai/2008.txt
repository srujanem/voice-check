Bugs, like many animals, have different ways of surviving the winter. Some bugs, like wasps and hornets, die off in the colder months and their colonies do not survive the winter. The queens of some species, however, will seek shelter in a warm place and go into a state of dormancy called "diapause" until the weather warms up again. Other bugs, like ladybugs and monarch butterflies, migrate to warmer climates in the winter. They fly to places where it is still warm and there is food available. Some bugs, like mosquitoes, can survive the winter by finding a place to hide where it is warm and damp, like in the hollow of a tree or in a pile of leaves. They will go into a state of dormancy called "diapause" until the weather warms up again. Finally, some bugs are able to survive the winter by adapting to the cold weather.
