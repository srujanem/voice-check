The idea that life could originate from non-living matter is called abiogenesis. While we don't know exactly how life originated on Earth, scientists have several hypotheses about how it might have happened. One possibility is that the building blocks of life, such as amino acids and nucleotides, formed spontaneously from non-living matter in the early Earth's environment. These building blocks could then have assembled into more complex structures, eventually leading to the first living cells. Another possibility is that life on Earth was brought here by comets or meteorites from other parts of the universe. These celestial objects could have contained the building blocks of life, which then assembled into living cells once they arrived on Earth. Overall, while we don't have a complete explanation for how life originated on Earth, scientists are continuing to study and learn more about this question through research and experimentation.
