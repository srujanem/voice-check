Boiling water is water that has reached a temperature of 212 degrees Fahrenheit (100 degrees Celsius) at sea level, at which point it begins to turn into steam. At this temperature, the water has a lot of energy, and the molecules are moving around very quickly. When you add something to boiling water, it can cook quickly because the hot water will transfer heat to the food or drink. 
Very hot water that is not boiling is water that is hot, but has not reached the boiling point. The temperature of this water will depend on how hot it is, but it will not be as hot as boiling water. When you add something to very hot, but not boiling water, it will not cook as quickly as it would in boiling water because the water does not have as much energy and the molecules are not moving around as quickly. This is why it is usually recommended to add things like tea bags or pasta to boiling water rather than very hot, but not boiling water.
