Detroit was once a very prosperous city because it was home to many big industries, like car manufacturing. These industries provided a lot of jobs for people and helped the city grow and thrive. However, over time, these industries started to decline. This happened for a few reasons. One reason is that other countries started to make cars and other products more cheaply, so people started to buy them instead of the ones made in Detroit. This meant that the industries in Detroit had less business and had to lay off workers or close down completely. Another reason is that the city itself went through some changes that made it a less attractive place to live and work. For example, there was a lot of crime and violence, and the city's infrastructure, like its roads and public transportation, started to fall apart. All of these things made it harder for people to want to live and work in Detroit. As a result of these changes, many people left the city to live and work somewhere else. This led to a decrease in the city's population and made it harder for the city to support itself. The city also had financial problems, which made it hard for it to fix its problems and attract new businesses. All of these things contributed to the decline of Detroit and made it a less prosperous place to live and work.
