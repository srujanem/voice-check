The dusty wooden chest in the attic had sat undisturbed for over half a century. Leo carefully dusted off the heavy brass latch, his heart beating fast as the lock finally yielded with a soft click. Inside, tucked beneath faded wool blankets, lay a leather-bound journal surrounded by hand-drawn maps of the surrounding hills. On the first page, written in dark blue ink, was a single sentence: "If you are reading this, the location of the lost silver mine is no longer a secret."
