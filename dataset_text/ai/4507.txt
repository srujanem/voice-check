A manual option on an automatic vehicle is a feature that allows the driver to take control of the gears and shift them manually, instead of relying on the car's automatic transmission to do it for them. This can be useful for a number of reasons. For example, some drivers enjoy having more control over their car and may prefer the feeling of shifting gears themselves. Others may find it helpful to have the ability to manually shift gears in certain driving situations, such as when driving on steep hills or in heavy traffic. Finally, some people may simply enjoy the feeling of driving a manual transmission car and may prefer the manual option on an automatic vehicle as a way to get a similar experience without the need to learn how to drive a traditional manual transmission car.
