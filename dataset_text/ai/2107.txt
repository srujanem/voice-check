Good question! The speed of the internet is measured in megabits per second (Mbps) because it refers to the rate at which data is transmitted over the internet. A bit is a unit of digital information that can either be a 1 or a 0. A megabit is equal to one million bits. So, if you have a internet connection with a speed of 50 Mbps, that means that your internet connection can transmit 50 million bits of information per second. On the other hand, file sizes are measured in megabytes (MB). A megabyte is a unit of digital information that is equal to 8 million bits. So, if you have a file that is 1 MB in size, it is made up of 8 million bits of information. I hope this helps to explain the difference between megabits and megabytes!
