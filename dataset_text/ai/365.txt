If you are unable to pay a medical bill in the United States, it is important to communicate with the healthcare provider or hospital to try to come to an arrangement. This could involve setting up a payment plan or negotiating a lower amount. If you are unable to pay the bill, the healthcare provider or hospital may turn the bill over to a collection agency, which may try to collect the unpaid amount from you. If the bill remains unpaid, it could also have a negative impact on your credit score. 
It is important to note that not being able to pay a medical bill does not necessarily mean that you will be stuck in the United States. However, if you are unable to pay the bill and are planning to leave the country, it is a good idea to communicate with the healthcare provider or hospital to try to come to an arrangement before you go. 
If you are having difficulty paying a medical bill and are not sure what to do, it may be helpful to seek advice from a healthcare advocate or a financial counselor. They can help you understand your options and assist you in finding a solution.
