A flame is a type of light that is produced when something is burning. When something burns, it means that it is reacting with oxygen in the air to produce heat, light, and other products. A flame is the visible part of this process. It is a bright, hot, and flickering light that is usually yellow or orange in color. Flames can be small, like a candle flame, or they can be large, like a bonfire. Flames are often associated with heat, but they can also be very dangerous if they are not properly controlled.
