This is because the One Ring was created by a very powerful and evil being called Sauron, who wants to use it to conquer and enslave all the other beings in the world. Frodo sets off on a long journey, accompanied by some of his friends and other creatures, to take the One Ring to Mount Doom. Along the way, they face many dangers and challenges, such as fierce orcs, giant spiders, and treacherous mountains. They also meet new friends and allies, including humans, elves, and dwarves, who help them on their quest. In the end, Frodo and his friends are able to destroy the One Ring and defeat Sauron, saving the world from his evil plans. It is a story about friendship, bravery, and the power of good to triumph over evil.
