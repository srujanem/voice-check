Aircraft carriers are large ships that have a long, flat deck where planes can take off and land. They are called "carriers" because they carry planes. These planes can be used to attack other ships, submarines, and land targets, or to defend against enemy planes. One reason aircraft carriers are important is because they give a military the ability to attack targets that are far away from their own country. This means that a country with a strong aircraft carrier fleet can defend itself and its allies, even if the enemy is very far away. Another reason aircraft carriers are important is because they can carry a lot of planes. This means that a military can have a lot of firepower all in one place, which makes it harder for the enemy to attack or defend against. Finally, aircraft carriers are very hard to sink or damage. They have strong armor and many weapons to defend themselves, so it is difficult for an enemy to attack and destroy them. This means that a country with a strong aircraft carrier fleet can feel more secure, knowing that they have a powerful weapon that is hard to defeat.
