Ear wax, also called cerumen, is a natural substance produced by the body to protect the ear. It helps to keep the ear clean and free of dirt, dust, and other foreign particles. Ear wax also has antimicrobial properties, which means it helps to prevent infections in the ear. 
The ear canal is a tube that runs from the outer ear to the middle ear. Ear wax is produced by glands in the skin of the ear canal. As the wax moves from the inner ear to the outer ear, it helps to protect the ear by trapping dirt and other foreign particles. 
So, ear wax serves an important purpose by helping to keep our ears clean and healthy. It's not an annoying byproduct of having an ear canal, but rather a natural and important part of the ear's anatomy.
