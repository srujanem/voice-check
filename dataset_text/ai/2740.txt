The credits on the back of DVD cases are often written in a tall, stretched font to make them easier to read. This is because the DVD case is often placed on a shelf or other flat surface, and the credits are written on the back of the case. When the case is on a flat surface, the credits may be difficult to read if they are written in a normal font. The tall, stretched font makes the letters longer and easier to read from a distance or at a low angle. This is especially helpful when the DVD case is on a shelf below eye level. So, in short, the tall, stretched font is used to make it easier to read the credits on the back of the DVD case, no matter where it is placed.
