Online multiplayer games like Fortnite get initial players through marketing and advertising efforts. They may also offer the game for free or at a discounted price to encourage people to try it out.\n\nThe chicken and the egg problem in this context refers to the fact that a multiplayer game needs a certain number of players to be enjoyable, but people may not want to play the game if there are not already a lot of players.\n\nTo solve this problem, game developers use marketing and advertising to promote the game and attract initial players. They may also offer incentives, such as special in-game items or rewards, to encourage people to try out the game. As more people start playing the game, it becomes more popular, which attracts even more players. This creates a positive feedback loop that helps the game grow and become more successful.\n
