Anonymous is a loosely-organized international group of hackers who are known for participating in high-profile cyber attacks against governments, corporations, and other organizations. While it is difficult to generalize about the methods that Anonymous might use to hack into servers, there are a few common techniques that they and other hackers might use: Brute force attacks: This involves trying every possible combination of characters until the correct password is found. This can be time-consuming, but it is possible to use special software and powerful computers to try many combinations very quickly. Dictionary attacks: This involves trying common words and phrases as passwords. Hackers might use a pre-made list of common passwords, or they might use a list of words from the dictionary. Social engineering: This involves tricking people into revealing their passwords or other sensitive information. For example, a hacker might pretend to be a trusted authority figure or a coworker and ask for someone's password. Exploiting vulnerabilities: Sometimes, servers have weaknesses or "holes" in their security that can be exploited by hackers. Hackers might use these vulnerabilities to gain access to a server without needing a password.
