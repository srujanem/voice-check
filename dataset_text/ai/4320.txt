Drones and remote control aircraft are similar in that they are both able to be controlled remotely, but they are generally classified differently because they have different uses and capabilities.\n\nDrones, also known as unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), are often used for a variety of purposes, including aerial photography, surveying land, delivering packages, and even military operations. They are often equipped with advanced sensors and cameras, and can fly for long periods of time without a human pilot on board.\n\nRemote control aircraft, on the other hand, are typically smaller and simpler devices that are used for recreational purposes, such as flying for fun or racing. They may not have the same advanced capabilities or long flight times as drones, and are usually flown closer to the ground.\n\nBecause drones and remote control aircraft have different uses and capabilities, they are often regulated differently by governments and other organizations. For example, drones may be required to follow stricter rules and regulations regarding where they can fly and how they can be used, while remote control aircraft may have fewer restrictions.
