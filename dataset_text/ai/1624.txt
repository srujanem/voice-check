Mormons are members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, a Christian denomination that was founded in the 19th century in the United States. Some people may consider Mormons to be "crazy" because their beliefs and practices differ from those of other Christian denominations. For example, Mormons believe that the president of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is a prophet who receives revelation from God. They also believe that the Bible is the word of God, but they also believe in other scriptures, such as the Book of Mormon, which they believe is another testament of Jesus Christ. Mormons also have specific practices and rituals, such as baptisms, temple ordinances, and missionary work. These practices may seem strange or unfamiliar to people who are not members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It's important to remember that all religious denominations have beliefs and practices that may be different from those of other denominations.
