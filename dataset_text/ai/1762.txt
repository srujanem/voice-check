The French Revolution, which began in 1789, was a period of radical political and societal transformation in France. Driven by Enlightenment ideals of liberty, equality, and fraternity, the revolution dismantled the monarchy and aristocracy. Key events included the storming of the Bastille, the Declaration of the Rights of Man, and the Reign of Terror. The revolution profoundly reshaped European politics and laid the groundwork for modern democratic governance and nationalism. It is evident that this topic will continue to shape our world in significant ways going forward.
