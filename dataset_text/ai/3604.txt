Artificial nuclear transmutation is a process that involves changing the nucleus of an atom from one element to another through nuclear reactions. This process could potentially be used to reduce the hazard of radioactive waste by converting the radioisotopes present in the waste into stable, non-radioactive elements. In terms of science, it is possible to achieve artificial nuclear transmutation through various methods, such as using particle accelerators or nuclear reactors to bombard the atoms with high-energy particles. However, this process is currently not practical or economically viable on a large scale due to the high cost and technical challenges involved.
