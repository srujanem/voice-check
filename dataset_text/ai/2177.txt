Existentialism is a philosophical movement that emphasizes individual freedom and choice, and the inherent meaninglessness of life. It suggests that we must create our own meaning and purpose in life, rather than rely on external sources or societal norms. Nihilism, on the other hand, is the belief that life has no inherent meaning or value. It is the idea that there is no ultimate purpose or goal to life, and that all values and beliefs are subjective and have no objective truth. Nihilism can be seen as a more extreme form of pessimism, as it denies the existence of any ultimate meaning or purpose in the universe. So, in summary, the main difference between these two philosophies is that existentialism suggests that we have the ability to create meaning in our lives, while nihilism asserts that life has no inherent meaning and that all values are subjective.
