American cheese is a type of processed cheese that is made from a blend of milk, milk fats, and other ingredients. It is usually yellow or orange in color and has a smooth, creamy texture. It is often used as a sandwich cheese or for melting on top of burgers, grilled cheese sandwiches, and other dishes. It is called "American cheese" because it was first made in the United States. Unlike many other types of cheese, American cheese is not aged, which means it does not have the same strong flavor as other types of cheese. It is also very easy to slice and melt, which makes it popular in many different types of dishes.
