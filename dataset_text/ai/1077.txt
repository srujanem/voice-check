Surveyors are professionals who measure and map the land, as well as structures on it. They use specialized equipment, like telescopes and GPS devices, to take precise measurements of the ground and the structures on it. This information is used to create detailed maps and plans for things like building roads, bridges, and buildings. Surveyors also help to determine property boundaries, which is important for things like buying and selling land. In short, surveyors help to create a detailed picture of the land and structures on it, which is useful for a variety of purposes.
