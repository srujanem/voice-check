There are a few reasons why books aren't usually remade in the same way that movies and TV shows often are. One reason is that books are typically longer and more complex than movies or TV shows, so it can be more challenging and time-consuming to adapt a book into another format. Additionally, books often have a strong following of dedicated readers who may not want to see their favorite stories and characters reimagined in a different way. Finally, copyright laws can make it difficult or impossible to remake a book without permission from the original author or their estate.
