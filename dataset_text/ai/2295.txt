The saying "Red sky at night, sailor's delight. Red sky at morning, sailors take warning" is a saying that is used to predict the weather based on the appearance of the sky. The saying is based on the idea that the color of the sky can be used to forecast what the weather will be like. Here's how it works: Red sky at night, sailor's delight: If the sky is red in the evening, it means that the sun is setting behind a clear sky. This usually means that the weather will be good the next day. Red sky at morning, sailors take warning: If the sky is red in the morning, it means that the sun is rising behind clouds. This usually means that there will be bad weather, like rain or storms, later in the day. So, the saying helps sailors (and other people) know what to expect from the weather based on the color of the sky.
