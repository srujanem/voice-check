When you pull a fire alarm, it sends a signal to the fire department to let them know that there is a fire in the building. The fire department then sends firefighters to the building to put out the fire and make sure everyone is safe. The firefighters will also help people evacuate the building if there is a fire, so they can get out safely. When you evacuate a building, it means you leave the building and go to a safe place outside. It is important to follow the instructions of the firefighters and leave the building as quickly and safely as possible.
