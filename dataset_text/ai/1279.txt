Before modern medicine, many women died while giving birth due to a variety of reasons. One reason was that they did not have access to trained medical professionals who could help them through the birthing process and provide necessary medical care. Another reason was that there was a lack of understanding about how to properly care for women during childbirth, which led to many preventable deaths. 
Modern medicine has greatly improved the safety of childbirth for women by providing trained medical professionals, such as doctors and nurses, who are trained to care for women during childbirth. They are able to monitor the mother's and baby's health and provide necessary medical interventions, such as medications to manage pain or surgery to address complications. Modern medicine has also improved the cleanliness of childbirth facilities and has developed new technologies, such as ultrasound, which can help to identify potential problems during pregnancy. 
Other mammals, such as dogs and cats, do not have access to modern medical care and are more likely to die during childbirth. However, they have evolved to give birth in a way that is generally safe for both the mother and the baby. For example, they have strong instincts that help them to take care of their young and protect them from harm. They also have a shorter gestational period, which means that their babies are born at a more developed stage than human babies. This can make childbirth safer for them.
