In fantasy literature, there are often many different types of characters who have the ability to use magic. These characters may be called wizards, mages, sorcerers, enchanters, spellcasters, druids, or shamans, depending on the specific story and the characteristics of the character. Wizards are often depicted as wise and scholarly characters who study magic and are skilled in its use. They may use their knowledge of magic to cast spells and perform feats of magical ability. Mages are similar to wizards, but they may be more focused on the practical application of magic, rather than the study of it. They may use their magical abilities to achieve specific goals or to solve problems. Sorcerers are often depicted as characters who have innate magical abilities, rather than ones they have learned through study. They may be born with magical powers or may have acquired them through some other means, such as a deal with a supernatural being. Enchanters are characters who use magic to charm or bewitch others. They may use their magic to influence the thoughts and actions of others, or to protect themselves from harm. Spellcasters are characters who use magic by casting spells. They may use spells to achieve a variety of effects, such as summoning objects, controlling the elements, or altering reality. Druids are characters who have a deep connection to nature and the natural world. They may use their magic to communicate with animals, control the weather, or manipulate the natural world in some way. Shamans are characters who are believed to have a special connection to the spiritual world and who use magic to communicate with spirits and deities. They may use their magic to heal the sick, divinate the future, or perform other supernatural feats. Overall, the differences between these types of magic users often depend on the specific story and the characteristics of the individual character. Some may be wise and scholarly, while others may have innate magical abilities or a connection to the natural world.
