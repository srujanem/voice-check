Sure! Here's a simple explanation: A condo is a type of home that you can buy. When you buy a condo, you own the unit that you live in and you are responsible for taking care of it. You may have to pay fees to the association that manages the building, but you don't have to worry about a landlord. An apartment is a type of home that you can rent. When you rent an apartment, you don't own it. You pay money to live in the unit, and someone else (the landlord) is responsible for taking care of the building. You don't have to worry about paying for repairs or maintenance, but you also don't have as much control over the space. So, the main difference between a condo and an apartment is that a condo is a type of home that you own, and an apartment is a type of home that you rent. Does that make sense?
