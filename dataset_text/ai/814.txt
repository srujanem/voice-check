Cats have a natural instinct to hunt and play. Pushing things off tables and shelves can be a way for them to practice these instincts. It may also be a way for them to get your attention or to play with you. Some cats may also do this if they are feeling anxious or stressed, as it can be a way for them to release energy. It's important to make sure that your cat has plenty of toys and opportunities to play and exercise, as this can help them to fulfill their natural instincts in a safe and appropriate way.
