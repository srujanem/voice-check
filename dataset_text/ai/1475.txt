ISIL stands for "Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant," while ISIS stands for "Islamic State of Iraq and Syria." Both acronyms refer to the same extremist group that is also known as the Islamic State or Daesh. 
The group's full name is "The Islamic State of Iraq and al-Sham," where "al-Sham" refers to a region that includes Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, and Palestine. This name was shortened to "Islamic State of Iraq and Syria" (ISIS) in English-language media. 
However, the term "Levant" is also used to refer to this same region, so the acronym "ISIL" was sometimes used instead of "ISIS" to avoid any confusion. It's possible that President Obama and others used the term "ISIL" because they believed it was a more accurate translation of the group's full name.
