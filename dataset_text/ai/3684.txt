The ozone layer in the stratosphere absorbs most of the sun's harmful ultraviolet radiation, protecting life on Earth. In the 1970s, scientists discovered that chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) and other industrial chemicals were destroying ozone molecules. The Montreal Protocol, signed in 1987, successfully phased out the production of ozone-depleting substances. The ozone layer is slowly recovering, demonstrating that international environmental cooperation can effectively address global environmental problems.
