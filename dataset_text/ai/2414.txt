The insanity defense is a legal defense that is used in some countries when a person is accused of a crime. It is based on the idea that the person was not responsible for their actions because they were suffering from a mental illness at the time of the crime. In order to use the insanity defense, the person must be able to prove that they were unable to understand the nature of their actions or that they were unable to control their actions because of their mental illness. If the person is successful in using the insanity defense, they may not be found guilty of the crime or they may be found not guilty by reason of insanity. The insanity defense is used very rarely and is often controversial because it can be difficult to prove that a person was not responsible for their actions due to a mental illness. It is also difficult to determine what level of mental illness is required for the defense to be successful.
