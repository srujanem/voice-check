When a comment is "redacted" or "blacked out" on Reddit, it means that the content of the comment has been hidden or obscured. This is often done by a moderator of the subreddit (a person who has been given special powers to manage the community) in order to remove inappropriate or offensive content, or to hide sensitive information that may violate the subreddit's rules or policies. Redacting a comment is different from deleting a comment because the comment still exists on the site, but the content has been hidden. This allows moderators to preserve the context of the conversation and ensure that the conversation can continue without violating any rules or causing harm to anyone involved. So to explain it like you're five: when a comment is redacted on Reddit, it's like someone put a big black marker over the words so you can't see them anymore. A moderator did this because the words were not nice or were breaking the rules of the subreddit.
