When you squint your eyes, you are decreasing the size of your pupils, which allows less light to enter your eyes. This can make it easier to see small details because it reduces the amount of glare and background distractions that you see. It's a little bit like when you use a magnifying glass to look at something small - it helps you focus in on the details. However, it's important to note that squinting your eyes is not a substitute for good vision and you should still make sure to wear glasses or contacts if you need them to see clearly.
