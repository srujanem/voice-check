Vomiting can be a common side effect of taking vitamins on an empty stomach, especially if the vitamins are in pill or capsule form. This can happen because the vitamins can irritate the lining of the stomach, causing it to feel uncomfortable or upset. 
When you eat food, it helps to coat the lining of the stomach and can make it easier for your body to tolerate the vitamins. When you take vitamins on an empty stomach, there is nothing there to help protect the stomach, so the vitamins can irritate it more easily. 
If you are experiencing this issue, you may want to try taking your vitamins with food or with a small snack to see if that helps. You can also try taking liquid vitamins or chewable vitamins, as these may be easier for your body to tolerate. It's always a good idea to talk to your doctor or a healthcare professional if you have any concerns about taking vitamins or if you experience any side effects.
