Domain registrars are companies that allow you to register a domain name, which is the website address that people type into their web browser to visit your website. When you register a domain name, you are essentially reserving that name so that no one else can use it. To register a domain name, you have to pay a fee to the domain registrar. This fee covers the cost of keeping track of all of the domain names that are registered, as well as the cost of providing customer support and other services. You do not need a domain registrar to host your website. You can host your website on a server that you own or rent from a hosting company. However, you do need a domain name in order for people to be able to access your website, and this is where the domain registrar comes in. In short, domain registrars provide the service of allowing you to register a domain name, which is necessary for people to be able to find your website on the internet.
