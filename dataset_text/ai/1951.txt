A scientific theory, on the other hand, is a more detailed explanation for why a scientific law is true. For example, let's say we have a scientific law that says "objects fall towards the ground when we drop them." This is a pattern that we observe every time we drop something, so it's a scientific law. But why do objects fall towards the ground? Well, that's where a scientific theory comes in. One scientific theory to explain why objects fall towards the ground is the theory of gravity. It says that objects are attracted to each other by a force called gravity, and this is why objects fall towards the ground when we drop them. So, to sum it up: a scientific law is a pattern that we observe in nature, and a scientific theory is an explanation for why that pattern exists. Does that make sense?
