When you zoom in or out on an image of a computer screen, you might see weird patterns because the image is made up of tiny dots called pixels. Each pixel is a tiny square that is a specific color, and together, all of the pixels create the image you see on your screen.\n\nWhen you zoom in on an image, you are making the pixels bigger, so you can see each individual pixel more clearly. This is why you might see weird patterns when you zoom in - because you are seeing the individual pixels that make up the image, rather than the image itself.\n\nSimilarly, when you zoom out on an image, you are making the pixels smaller, so the image becomes less detailed.
