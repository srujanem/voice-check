Zeitgeist is a German word that means the "spirit of the times." It refers to the dominant ideas, beliefs, and values that are shared by a particular group of people or a society at a particular point in time. Think of it like the "mood" or "vibe" that everyone in a particular place is feeling at a particular moment. It's the way that people are thinking, feeling, and acting based on what is happening in the world around them. For example, if everyone in a society is really worried about the environment, the Zeitgeist might be described as being "green" or focused on protecting the planet. If everyone is focused on technology and innovation, the Zeitgeist might be described as being "technological." Zeitgeist can change over time as people's values and beliefs evolve. It's a way of understanding the shared experiences and attitudes of a group of people in a specific place and time.
