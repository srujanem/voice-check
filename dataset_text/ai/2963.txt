Some of the things that might be going on include: The nurse might be entering your information into the computer: In many medical offices and hospitals, the nurse will enter your vital signs and other information into a computer system. This helps the doctor to see your medical history and current health status. The nurse might be communicating with the doctor: The nurse might be talking to the doctor about your case and providing them with updates on your condition. This might include discussing your vital signs, any symptoms you're experiencing, and any other relevant information. The doctor might be seeing other patients: Doctors usually have a schedule of patients they need to see, and they might be seeing other patients before they come to see you. The doctor might be reviewing your medical records: Before coming to see you, the doctor might be reviewing your medical records and other information to get a better understanding of your case.
