Turning your computer off at the power point, rather than shutting it down properly, is bad because it can lead to problems with your computer. When you shut down your computer properly, it closes all of the programs and saves any open files that you have. This helps to ensure that your computer is in a stable state and that there won't be any problems when you turn it back on. On the other hand, if you turn off your computer at the power point, it doesn't have a chance to close all of the programs and save your open files. This can lead to problems when you turn your computer back on, such as it not starting up properly or certain programs not working correctly. In general, it's always a good idea to shut down your computer properly, rather than just turning it off at the power point. This will help to ensure that your computer is running smoothly and that you don't run into any problems.
