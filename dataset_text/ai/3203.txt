The "Silent Hill" series is a collection of horror video games that take place in the fictional town of Silent Hill. In the games, the town is often depicted as a mysterious and unsettling place where strange and supernatural events occur. Each game in the series follows a different protagonist who finds themselves drawn to Silent Hill for various reasons. As they explore the town and its surroundings, they uncover secrets and mysteries that are linked to their own pasts, and they must confront their own inner demons and fears as they try to survive and escape the town. The games often involve puzzle-solving and combat, and they often feature disturbing and surreal imagery, as well as supernatural creatures and otherworldly beings that the protagonist must face and defeat. Overall, the "Silent Hill" series is known for its horror atmosphere and storytelling, and it has gained a large and dedicated fan base for its unique blend of horror, mystery, and psychological themes.
