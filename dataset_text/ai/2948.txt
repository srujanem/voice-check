Communism is a type of government and economic system where everyone in society shares everything equally. This means that all the wealth, property, and resources are owned by the community as a whole, rather than by individual people or businesses. Under communism, there is no private ownership, and the government controls the economy. The idea behind communism is that everyone works and contributes to the community, and in return, the community takes care of their basic needs, such as food, shelter, and healthcare. The goal of communism is to create a classless society where everyone is equal and no one is oppressed or exploited.
