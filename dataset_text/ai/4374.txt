Electric cars like Tesla work by using electricity from batteries to power an electric motor. The motor is connected to the wheels of the car, so when it spins, it makes the car move. The driver can control how much electricity is sent to the motor by using the pedals, just like in a regular gasoline car.\n\nThe electric motor in an electric car is very similar to a servo motor. A servo motor is a type of electric motor that is used to control the movement of something, like the steering in a car. In an electric car, the electric motor is used to turn the wheels and make the car go forward, backward, or stop.\n\nThe batteries in an electric car store electricity that is used to power the motor. The batteries are charged by plugging the car into an electric outlet or by using a special charging station. The electricity from the batteries is sent to the motor through a system of wires and controllers, which control the amount of electricity that is used to power the motor.\n\nSo, to sum it up, electric cars work by using electricity from batteries to power an electric motor, which is controlled by the driver using the pedals. The motor is connected to the wheels of the car, so when it spins, it makes the car move. There are many other parts and systems that work together to make an electric car run smoothly and efficiently, but this is the basic idea.
