Tea leaves are generally not eaten because they are not very tasty on their own and can be quite bitter. Instead, tea leaves are usually dried and then brewed to make a hot beverage called tea. To make tea, the dried tea leaves are steeped in hot water to extract their flavor and aromas. The resulting liquid is then strained and served. 
Eating tea leaves may not be harmful to your health, but they are not usually considered a food and are not a good source of nutrients. Additionally, some types of tea leaves may contain compounds that can cause digestive issues if consumed in large amounts. 
So while it is technically possible to eat tea leaves, it is not a common practice and is not generally recommended. It is much more enjoyable to enjoy tea leaves as a hot, flavorful beverage.
