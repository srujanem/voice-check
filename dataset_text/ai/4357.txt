Ayn Rand's philosophy is called Objectivism. It is a way of thinking about the world and our place in it.\n\nObjectivism is based on the idea that reality exists independent of our thoughts and feelings about it. This means that things like mountains, trees, and animals really exist, even if we're not thinking about them or if we don't believe in them.\n\nObjectivism also says that reason, or the ability to think and understand, is the most important human faculty. According to Rand, we should use reason to understand the world and make our own decisions, rather than relying on tradition, emotions, or the opinions of others.\n\nRand also believed that individual rights, or the freedoms and protections that we have as individuals, are very important. She believed that each person has the right to live their own life and pursue their own happiness, as long as they don't harm others.\n\nFinally, Objectivism says that capitalism, or an economic system where people can own and run their own businesses, is the best way to organize society. Rand believed that capitalism allows people to use their own abilities and talents to create and trade value, which leads to prosperity and progress.\n\nSo, in short, Objectivism is a philosophy that says we should use reason to understand the world, respect individual rights, and support capitalism.
