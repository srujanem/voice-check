That long piece of paper that is printed out at every airport gate is called a "flight manifest." It is a list of all the passengers and crew members who are on a particular flight. 
The flight manifest is used by the airline and airport staff to keep track of who is on the plane and to make sure that everyone who is supposed to be on the flight is actually on the plane before it takes off. It also includes important information about the flight, such as the flight number, the departure and arrival times, and the route the plane will be taking. 
In the past, flight manifests were primarily used for safety and security purposes. They allowed airport staff to quickly and easily check who was on the plane in case of an emergency or if there was a problem with the flight. 
Even though we now have digital systems and technologies that can keep track of this information, the flight manifest is still used because it is a reliable and easy-to-use backup system. It ensures that there is always a physical record of who is on the flight, even if the digital systems fail or there is an issue with the electronic records.
