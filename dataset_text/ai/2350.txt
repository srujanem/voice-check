A haiku is a type of poem that originated in Japan. It is a very short poem that has three lines. The first line has five syllables, the second line has seven syllables, and the third line has five syllables again. Haiku poems are usually about nature and the changing seasons. They often include imagery and try to capture a moment or feeling in just a few words. To read a haiku, you start with the first line, then the second line, and then the third line. You can read it out loud or in your head. Some people like haiku poems because they are short and easy to understand, but also because they can be very beautiful and poetic. They can help you appreciate the small moments in life and the beauty of nature.
