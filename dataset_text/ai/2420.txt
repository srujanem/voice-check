Podcasts are like radio shows that you can listen to or watch on your phone, tablet, or computer. They're usually about a specific topic, like sports or news, and they're usually free to listen to. To listen to a podcast, you'll need a device like a phone, tablet, or computer, and a way to access the internet. Then, you'll need to find a podcast app or website where you can find and listen to podcasts. Some popular apps for listening to podcasts are Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Google Podcasts. To listen to a podcast, you just need to search for it in the app or website, and then click on it to start listening. It's that easy!
