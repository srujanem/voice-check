Cold water tastes better than room temperature or warm water because it is more refreshing and can help to quench your thirst. When you are thirsty, your body craves fluids that can help to hydrate your cells and tissues. Cold water is more effective at doing this because it is colder than your body temperature, so it helps to cool you down when you drink it. In addition, cold water can help to stimulate the taste buds in your mouth, which can make it taste more refreshing and enjoyable. So overall, cold water is more desirable because it is more refreshing and helps to hydrate your body more effectively.
