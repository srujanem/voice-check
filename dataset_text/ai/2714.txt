Rubbing alcohol and drinkable alcohol are both types of alcohol, but they are used for different purposes and have different properties. Rubbing alcohol is a type of alcohol that is used as a disinfectant to kill germs on the skin. It is usually made from isopropyl alcohol or ethanol, which are types of alcohol that can be toxic if ingested (eaten or drunk). This means that rubbing alcohol should not be consumed, or drunk, because it can make you very sick or even cause death. Drinkable alcohol, on the other hand, is a type of alcohol that is safe to consume in moderation. Examples of drinkable alcohol include beer, wine, and spirits like whiskey and vodka. These types of alcohol are typically made from grains, fruits, or vegetables, and they are fermented and distilled to produce a beverage that can be enjoyed socially. In summary, rubbing alcohol is a disinfectant that should not be consumed, while drinkable alcohol is a beverage that can be enjoyed in moderation.
