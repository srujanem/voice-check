An endgame is the part of an MMO (massively multiplayer online game) that comes after you have completed all the main objectives or "quests" in the game. Some people enjoy the endgame because it usually offers more challenging content that requires teamwork and strategy to complete. It can also be a way for players to show off their skills and accomplishments by completing difficult endgame content. Additionally, endgame often offers rewards such as powerful equipment or exclusive in-game items that can give players an advantage over others. Some people find the endgame to be the most enjoyable part of an MMO because it provides a sense of accomplishment and allows them to continue playing with their friends.
