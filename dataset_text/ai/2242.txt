Shorting a stock means selling a stock that you do not own. The person you spoke to probably borrowed shares of Netflix from someone else, sold them, and is hoping to buy them back at a lower price in the future. Here's an example of how shorting a stock works: Bob thinks that the price of Netflix stock is going to go down. Bob borrows 100 shares of Netflix stock from his broker. Bob sells the borrowed shares of Netflix for $285 each. The price of Netflix stock goes down, as Bob expected. Bob buys 100 shares of Netflix stock for $250 each. Bob returns the borrowed shares of Netflix to the broker and keeps the difference in price as profit. In this example, Bob made a profit of $35 per share ($285 - $250) by shorting Netflix. However, if the price of Netflix had gone up instead of down, Bob would have lost money. I hope this helps! Let me know if you have any other questions.
