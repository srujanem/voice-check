What people find attractive can be influenced by both culture and instinct.\n\nCulture refers to the beliefs, behaviors, objects, and other characteristics that define a group or society. This includes the standards of beauty that are common in a particular culture. For example, in some cultures, it may be considered attractive to have dark skin, while in other cultures, lighter skin may be preferred.\n\nInstinct, on the other hand, refers to behaviors that are natural and automatic, and that are not learned through experience. Some research suggests that people may have certain instincts when it comes to finding certain physical features attractive, such as a symmetrical face or certain body proportions.\n\nSo, in short, what we find attractive can be influenced by both the culture we were raised in and our natural instincts.
