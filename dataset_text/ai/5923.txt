One of the fundamental concepts in biology is that The digestive system breaks down food into simple nutrients such as carbohydrates, fats, and proteins that can be absorbed into the bloodstream. Mechanical digestion begins in the mouth with chewing, while chemical digestion involves enzymes like amylase and pepsin. The stomach uses gastric acid to further break down proteins. The small intestine is the primary site for nutrient absorption, utilizing villi and microvilli to maximize surface area. The large intestine primarily absorbs water and forms solid waste for excretion. Continued research in this area promises to yield further insights into the fundamental mechanisms of life.
