When people say that someone has died of a broken heart, they usually mean that the person died shortly after experiencing a very stressful or emotional event, such as the death of a loved one. The technical term for this is "stress-induced cardiomyopathy," also known as "broken heart syndrome."\n\nIn broken heart syndrome, the heart becomes weak and doesn't pump blood as well as it should. This can cause symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, and fainting. In some cases, broken heart syndrome can be serious and even lead to death.\n\nIt's important to note that while emotional stress can contribute to broken heart syndrome, it is not the only cause. Other factors, such as physical stress (such as a surgery or severe illness) and certain medications, can also increase the risk of developing the condition.\n\nSo to sum it up, broken heart syndrome is a medical condition that can be caused by emotional or physical stress.
