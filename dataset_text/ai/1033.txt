The red balls on power lines are called "marker balls." They are used to help people see where the power lines are, especially when it is foggy or there is snow on the ground. They are usually placed on the highest part of the power line, so they are easy to see from a distance. 
Power lines carry electricity from one place to another. Electricity is a very powerful force that can be dangerous, so it is important to know where the power lines are so that people and vehicles do not accidentally touch them. The marker balls help people stay away from the power lines and stay safe.
