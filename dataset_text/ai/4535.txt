Calculators are able to solve complex math problems quickly because they use a type of computer called a microprocessor. Microprocessors are tiny chips that are found in many electronic devices, including calculators, computers, and smartphones. They are able to perform a lot of calculations very quickly because they are designed to do one thing really well: perform math operations.\n\nWhen you enter a math problem into a calculator, the microprocessor inside the calculator reads the problem and follows a set of instructions, called a program, to solve the problem. The program tells the microprocessor what to do step by step, such as performing addition or multiplication. The microprocessor is able to carry out these instructions very quickly because it is designed to do math operations very efficiently.\n\nSo, when you use a calculator to solve a math problem, the microprocessor inside the calculator is able to do all of the work for you very quickly, giving you the answer almost instantly.
