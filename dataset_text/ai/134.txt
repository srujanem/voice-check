It's not that your stereo is psychic. It's likely that you have a phone or other device in your room that is connected to the same wireless network as your stereo, and the stereo is picking up a notification from the phone or device. 
When you get a call or text message, your phone sends a notification to the wireless network to let you know. The notification is a signal that is broadcast over the network and can be picked up by any device that is connected to the same network. 
In this case, it sounds like your stereo is picking up the notification and playing the beeping sound as a result. It's not necessarily intentional or designed to happen this way, but it's just a side effect of how the devices are connected and how they communicate with each other.
