Ants are able to survive falls that would be very dangerous for humans because they have a number of adaptations that help them to protect themselves. First of all, ants are very small, which means that they have a smaller surface area and less mass, so they experience less force when they fall. This means that they don't hit the ground as hard as a larger animal like a human would. Secondly, ants have a hard exoskeleton, which is a hard outer shell that covers their body. This exoskeleton helps to protect them from injury when they fall, because it absorbs some of the force of the impact. Finally, ants are able to move their legs and antennae very quickly when they fall, which helps them to orient themselves in mid-air and land on their feet. This helps them to absorb the impact of the fall and reduce the chance of injury. So, when an ant falls from a high place, it is able to survive because it is small, has a hard exoskeleton, and can move its legs and antennae quickly to help it land safely.
