There are many factors that can contribute to why some musicians become successful and others do not. Talent is certainly one factor, but it is not the only one. Some other factors that can influence a musician's success include their level of dedication and hard work, their ability to market themselves and their music, and the amount of resources and support they have access to. Additionally, luck and timing can also play a role in a musician's success. For example, a musician may be in the right place at the right time and get discovered by a record label, or they may have access to resources and connections that can help them get their music out there. Ultimately, the music industry is complex and there are no guarantees of success, even for talented musicians.
