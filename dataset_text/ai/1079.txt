When you legally change your name, you can update your name on any state-issued documents, such as your driver's license or passport, by providing the proper documentation to the issuing agency. This usually involves filling out a form, providing proof of your identity and the name change (such as a marriage certificate or court order), and paying a fee. 
As for your college degree, you can also update your name on this document by contacting your college or university's registrar's office. They will typically ask you to provide proof of your name change, such as a marriage certificate or court order, and may charge a fee for updating your degree. Once your name is updated on your degree, it will show your new name instead of your old name. 
It's important to note that it can take some time to update all of your documents and records with your new name, so you may need to temporarily carry around proof of your name change, such as a copy of your marriage certificate or court order, until everything is updated.
