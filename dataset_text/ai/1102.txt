The fifth of November is a date in the calendar that marks the anniversary of an event that happened in England many years ago. On this day in 1605, a man named Guy Fawkes was caught trying to blow up the Houses of Parliament in London. This was a very bad thing to do, and Guy Fawkes was punished for it. Every year on the fifth of November, people in England remember this event by lighting bonfires and setting off fireworks. Some people also wear masks and have parties to celebrate the day.
