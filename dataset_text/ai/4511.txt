It is generally recommended to change the front tires first if you can only change two tires because the front tires play a larger role in steering and braking. This is especially important in front-wheel drive vehicles, where the front tires are responsible for both steering and propulsion. In rear-wheel drive vehicles, the rear tires also play a role in steering and braking, but to a lesser extent than the front tires. In four-wheel drive vehicles, all four tires play a role in steering and braking, but it is still generally recommended to change the front tires first if you can only change two.\n\nImagine that you are driving a car and you suddenly need to stop. The front tires are the ones that will help you stop the car safely. If the front tires are worn out or damaged, it will be harder for the car to stop quickly and safely. Similarly, when you turn the steering wheel, the front tires are the ones that help the car change direction. If the front tires are in poor condition, it will be harder for the car to turn smoothly and accurately.\n\nIn short, changing the front tires first is generally recommended because they play a larger role in steering and braking, which are important safety functions of a car.
