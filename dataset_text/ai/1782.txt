Cars need gasoline (also called petrol) to function because it is a good source of energy for the engine. The engine in a car works by burning a mixture of gasoline and air in a small space called a cylinder. When this mixture is ignited, it causes a small explosion that pushes a piston down, which turns the car's wheels. Gasoline is a good fuel for cars because it is easy to store and transport, and it has a lot of energy packed into a small space. It is also flammable, which means it can burn easily, which is necessary for it to be used as fuel in the car's engine. Water mixed with alcohol (or any other liquid) is not a good fuel for a car's engine because it does not have enough energy to power the engine.
