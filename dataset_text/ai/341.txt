It's also important to note that the war and Japan's actions during that time are still a very sensitive and emotional topic for many people in China and Korea, and these issues can continue to be a source of tension between the countries. In contrast, the Western countries that were involved in World War II, such as the United States and the United Kingdom, are not directly impacted by Japan's actions during the war in the same way that China and Korea are, and so the issues surrounding Japan's behavior during the war may not be as pressing for these countries.
