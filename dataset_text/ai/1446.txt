Cameras often have square hoods to help reduce the amount of glare and lens flare that can be caused by bright light sources. Glare and lens flare can interfere with the image being captured by the camera and make it appear washed out or distorted. The hood helps to block out these stray light rays, which can help to improve the overall quality of the image. The hood is usually made from a material that is black on the inside to help absorb light, and it is often shaped like a square or rectangle to help block out light coming from all angles.
