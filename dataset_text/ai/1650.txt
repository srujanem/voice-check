Hacking is when someone tries to gain access to a computer, network, or website without the owner's permission. There are many different ways that hackers can try to do this, but some common methods include: Guessing passwords: Hackers might try to guess someone's password by trying common words or combinations of letters and numbers. Using malware: Malware is a type of software that can be installed on a computer without the owner's knowledge. Once it's installed, the hacker can use it to control the computer or steal sensitive information. Exploiting vulnerabilities: All computer systems have vulnerabilities, or weaknesses that can be exploited by hackers. Hackers might try to find and exploit these vulnerabilities to gain access to a system. Phishing: Hackers might send fake emails or create fake websites that look legitimate, in an attempt to trick people into giving away their login credentials or other sensitive information. In general, hackers try to gain access to someone's emails or documents by finding a way to get into their computer or account. Once they have access, they can then read, copy, or delete the files they find. It's important to be careful and protect your computer and online accounts with strong passwords and security measures to help prevent hacking.
