A fixed value of money over time can be harmful because it can lead to deflation, which is when the value of money increases over time. This can make it difficult for people and businesses to make purchases and investments, because the prices of goods and services will seem too high. For example, if you had $100 today and the value of money remained constant, that $100 would be worth more in the future because it could buy more goods and services. This could discourage people from spending money, which can lead to a slowdown in economic activity. An inflation rate of zero is also bad because it can lead to deflation, as described above. Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services is rising, and subsequently, purchasing power is falling. Central banks aim to maintain a low, stable inflation rate in order to support healthy economic growth. If the inflation rate is too low, it can lead to deflation, which can be harmful to the economy.
