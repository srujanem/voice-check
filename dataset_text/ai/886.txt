Your brain doesn't actually have a "voice" like you might think of it. When you hear a voice in your head, it's actually just your own thoughts. The reason you experience your thoughts as coming from inside your head is because that's where your brain is located. Your brain is the organ that controls all of your thoughts, movements, and sensations, so it makes sense that you would feel like your thoughts are coming from inside your head. 
As for why you can't "move" the location of your thoughts, it's because your thoughts are just electrical and chemical activity happening in your brain. You don't have any direct control over this activity, so you can't really control where your thoughts seem to be coming from. 
The frontal lobe is a part of the brain that is involved in things like thinking, planning, and decision-making, but it's not the only part of the brain that's involved in these processes. Your thoughts and perceptions come from activity in many different parts of your brain, not just the frontal lobe. 
It's also not about discipline. You don't have any control over where your thoughts seem to come from, so it's not a matter of discipline or lack thereof.
