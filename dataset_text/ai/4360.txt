Car alarms are designed to attract attention and alert people that there may be a problem with the car. The sound that car alarms make is meant to be distinct and noticeable, so that people nearby can hear it and know that there is an issue. The specific sound that car alarms make is not meant to symbolize or mean anything in particular. It is simply a combination of sounds that is easy for the alarm system to produce and that is likely to grab people's attention.
