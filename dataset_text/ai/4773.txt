Forensic scientists can sometimes tie a bullet to a specific firearm by examining the markings that the gun leaves on the bullet as it is fired. These markings, called "toolmarks," are unique to each firearm and can be used to identify the gun that fired the bullet.\n\nTo do this, the forensic scientist will use a microscope to examine the bullet and compare the toolmarks to those made by known firearms. If the toolmarks on the bullet match those made by a particular firearm, the forensic scientist can conclude that the bullet was likely fired from that gun.\n\nIt's important to note that this process is not always foolproof, and there are limits to the precision with which a bullet can be tied to a specific firearm. However, in some cases, it can be a valuable piece of evidence in a criminal investigation.
