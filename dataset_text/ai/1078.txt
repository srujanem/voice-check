Musicians often memorize pages upon pages of music through a combination of practice and natural ability. Some musicians are naturally gifted with good memories and are able to easily memorize large amounts of information, including music. However, even for musicians with naturally good memories, it still takes a lot of practice and repetition to be able to memorize and perform music accurately. 
One way musicians practice memorizing music is by repeatedly playing and singing the music until it becomes familiar and they can remember it without looking at the score. They may also use techniques like breaking the music down into smaller sections and focusing on memorizing one section at a time, or creating mental images or associations to help them remember the music. 
In general, the more time a musician spends practicing and performing a piece of music, the more they will be able to memorize it and the easier it will become. With enough practice, most musicians are able to memorize and perform large amounts of music accurately.
