When you buy a gift card from a company or store, that company or store is receiving the money from you upfront. They are essentially selling you the right to redeem the value of the gift card at a later time. So, the company or store is not losing any money when you buy the gift card. In fact, they are making a profit because they are receiving the money from you now, even though you may not use the gift card until later. Does that make sense?
