Sure! A false positive means that something was identified as being a certain thing, but it was actually not that thing. For example, if a medical test is supposed to identify whether a person has a certain disease, a false positive would be when the test says the person has the disease, but they actually don't. 
On the other hand, a false negative means that something was not identified as being a certain thing, but it was actually that thing. Using the same example, a false negative would be when the test says the person does not have the disease, but they actually do.
