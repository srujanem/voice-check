When you watch a video on YouTube, the video is actually being streamed from YouTube's servers to your device over the internet. When you go full screen, your device has to request more data from the server to display the video in a larger resolution. This requires the video to be re-loaded, or re-buffered, so that it can play smoothly at the higher resolution. Other video players may use a different method to deliver the video to your device, such as downloading the entire video file before you start playing it. This means that the video is already stored on your device, so it doesn't have to be re-loaded when you go full screen. However, this method takes up more storage space on your device and may not be as efficient for streaming videos over the internet. I hope this helps to explain the difference!
