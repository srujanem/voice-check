It's normal for your nose to get clogged or congested from time to time, and it's common for one side to feel more congested than the other. This can be caused by a variety of things, such as allergies, a cold or the flu, or irritants in the air. When you lay down at night, the position of your head and body can cause the mucus in your nose to shift, which can make one side feel more congested. It's also possible that you have a structural issue with your nasal passages that is causing one side to feel more congested. If you're concerned about your nasal congestion, it might be a good idea to see a doctor to help determine the cause and find a solution.
