Muscles can sometimes pulse or twitch because they are controlled by electrical signals from the brain. These signals tell the muscles when to contract or relax, and this is what allows us to move our body. Sometimes, the brain sends extra signals to the muscles by mistake, which can cause the muscles to pulse or twitch. This is usually not a cause for concern, and it is a normal part of how the body works. It is important to drink enough water and get enough sleep, as being dehydrated or tired can sometimes cause muscles to pulse or twitch. If you are concerned about muscle twitching or if it is happening frequently, you should talk to a doctor.
