It's like turning on the faucet and letting the water flow into the bucket. The more water (or electricity) that the bucket (or battery) can hold, the longer it will take to fill it up.\n\nNow, even though electricity can move very quickly, it still takes some time for it to flow into the phone's battery and fill it up. That's why it can take a while for your phone to fully charge, especially if the battery is very big or if you're using your phone while it's charging.\n\nI hope that helps to explain why it takes some time for a smartphone to fully charge!
