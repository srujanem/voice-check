Wildfires are fires that burn out of control in natural areas such as forests, grasslands, and prairies. They can be caused by lightning, but most are started by people. Wildfires can be very dangerous and can cause people to die in several ways.\n\nFirst, the fire itself can burn people. If someone is caught in the middle of a wildfire, the flames and heat can burn their skin and clothes, causing serious injuries or even death.\n\nSecond, the smoke from a wildfire can be very dangerous to breathe in. Wildfires produce a lot of smoke, which can make it hard to see and can make it difficult to breathe. People who are caught in a wildfire and try to escape may inhale too much smoke, which can cause them to have trouble breathing and can even cause them to lose consciousness.\n\nFinally, people can also die in a wildfire if they are unable to escape the fire or find a safe place to shelter. Wildfires can spread very quickly and can be very hard to predict. If someone is in an area that is being burned by a wildfire and cannot find a way to escape, they may be in danger.\n\nIt is important for people to be prepared for wildfires and to know what to do if one occurs. This includes having an evacuation plan and knowing how to stay safe during a wildfire.\n
