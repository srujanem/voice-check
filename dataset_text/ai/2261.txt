In mathematics, the symbol "..." means that the pattern or sequence of numbers continues indefinitely. For example, the fraction 0.999... is a way of writing the number 1. This is because 0.999... is equal to 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 + ..., and if you add up an infinite number of 1/3s, you will get the number 1. So, to answer your question, 1 = 0.999..., but 1 is not equal to 0.999. The three dots in 0.999... indicate that the pattern of 9s continues indefinitely, while the three dots are not present in 0.999, so the sequence of 9s ends after the first three digits.
