When you double up two plastic grocery bags, you are adding an extra layer of support to the bags. This extra layer helps distribute the weight more evenly across the bags, which makes them less likely to break under heavy weight.\n\nImagine if you had a single bag and you put a heavy object inside of it. The bag might start to stretch and strain under the weight of the object, which could cause it to break. But if you have two bags, the weight of the object is divided between the two bags, which makes it easier for them to support the weight without breaking.\n\nThink of it like a game of "hot potato." If you have a group of people and you try to pass a heavy potato back and forth, each person might have a hard time holding onto the potato for very long before they need to pass it on. But if you have two potatoes, the weight is divided between the two potatoes, and each person can hold onto their potato for a longer time before they need to pass it on. It's the same idea with the plastic grocery bags - the weight is divided between the two bags, which makes them stronger and less likely to break.
