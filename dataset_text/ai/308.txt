The Vikings were a group of people who lived in the Scandinavian countries of Norway, Denmark, and Sweden from about the 8th century to the 11th century. They are known for their naval raids and for being skilled warriors and traders. 
At the height of their power, the Vikings controlled large parts of Europe and even parts of North America. However, as time went on, the Vikings gradually lost their power and influence. This was due to a number of factors, including changes in political and economic systems, and the rise of new civilizations and empires. 
Today, the Vikings are not a separate group of people, but rather are considered part of the modern cultures of Scandinavia. However, their legacy lives on in the history and culture of these countries, and in the many stories and legends that have been told about them.
