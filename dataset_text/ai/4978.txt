When you feel the urge to use the bathroom, your body is sending a signal that it's time to empty your bladder. The muscles in your bladder contract and push the urine out. When you're close to a toilet, your body knows that it's a good place to go, so it might make you feel the urge to go a little more strongly. This is so you'll be more likely to make it to the toilet in time. So, it's not really that your body "knows" you've reached the toilet, but rather that it's just trying to make sure you have the chance to go when you need to. Does that make sense?
