Great question! When we sleep, our bodies naturally get a little bit cooler because we are resting and not moving around as much. Wearing fewer clothes or using a blanket helps to keep us comfortable and at the right temperature. If we wear too many clothes, we might get too hot and sweaty, which can make it hard to fall asleep or stay asleep. It's also easier to move around and get comfortable when we have less clothing on. So, wearing fewer clothes and using a blanket is a good way to stay just the right temperature while we sleep.
