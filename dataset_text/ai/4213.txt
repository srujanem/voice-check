In mathematics, the equal sign (=) is used to indicate that two things are equal. For example, if we say "k = 42," it means that the value of k is 42.\n\nThe triple bar symbol (≡) is also used to indicate equality, but it is used in a different way. It is often used to indicate that two statements or equations are equivalent, which means that they are true in the same way.\n\nFor example, if we say "k ≡ 42," it means that k has the same meaning as 42, or that k and 42 can be used interchangeably in a mathematical expression.\n\nSo in short, the equal sign (=) is used to show that two things have the same value, while the triple bar symbol (≡) is used to show that two things have the same meaning or can be used interchangeably.
