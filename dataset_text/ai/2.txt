The Roman Empire began as a small city-state in Italy and eventually became one of the largest empires in history. At its height, it stretched across Europe, North Africa, and parts of Asia. The Romans built impressive roads, aqueducts, and public buildings that influenced future civilizations. Latin, their language, became the foundation of many modern languages. Although the Western Roman Empire fell in 476 AD, its legal systems, architecture, and cultural achievements continue to shape the modern world.
