High-speed maglev trains operate without traditional wheels or engine components, relying instead on powerful magnetic levitation. Superconducting electromagnets create magnetic forces that lift the train a few inches above the guideway, eliminating physical friction entirely. Another set of magnets creates a traveling magnetic field along the track, propelling the train forward at speeds exceeding 300 miles per hour. Because maglev vehicles make no physical contact with the rails, they offer exceptionally smooth rides, reduced maintenance costs, and unprecedented efficiency for high-speed intercity passenger rail transport.
