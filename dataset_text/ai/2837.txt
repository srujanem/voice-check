Smoking marijuana can stimulate the appetite, which is why some people may feel hungry after using it. This is because marijuana contains a chemical called THC, which can interact with certain receptors in the brain that play a role in appetite and hunger. When THC activates these receptors, it can cause an increase in hunger and lead to what is commonly known as "the munchies." So, if your friend ate a big meal and then smoked pot, they may be feeling hungry again because the marijuana is causing them to feel more hungry than they normally would.
