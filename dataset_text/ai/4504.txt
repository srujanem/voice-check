Venmo and online banking apps are both ways to send and receive money electronically, but they have some differences.\n\nOne difference is that Venmo is a social payments app, which means that it's designed to make it easy to send and receive money with friends and family. When you use Venmo, you can see a feed of your friends' transactions, and you can add comments and emojis to your own transactions. This can make it feel more like a social activity, rather than just a financial transaction.\n\nOn the other hand, online banking apps are usually focused more on managing your overall financial accounts, such as checking your balances, paying bills, and transferring money between accounts. These apps might not have the same social features as Venmo.\n\nAnother difference is that Venmo is a standalone app, while online banking apps are usually just one part of a larger financial service offered by a bank or other financial institution. This means that you might need to have a separate account with Venmo, while you can usually access your online banking features through the same account you use to manage your other financial accounts.\n\nOverall, the choice between using Venmo or an online banking app to transfer money will depend on your specific needs and preferences. If you want a social payments app that's easy to use with friends and family, Venmo might be a good choice. If you're more interested in managing your overall financial accounts, an online banking app might be a better fit.
