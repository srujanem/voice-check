Psoriasis is a skin condition that causes red, scaly patches to appear on the skin. It is a chronic condition, which means it lasts for a long time and can come and go. It is not contagious, which means you cannot catch it from someone else. The exact cause of psoriasis is unknown, but it is thought to be related to the immune system. In people with psoriasis, the skin cells grow faster than normal. As a result, new skin cells are produced too quickly and build up on the surface of the skin, forming the red, scaly patches. These patches can be itchy and sometimes painful. They can appear anywhere on the body, but are most common on the elbows, knees, scalp, and lower back. There is no cure for psoriasis, but there are treatments that can help to reduce the symptoms and prevent flare-ups. These may include medications, light therapy, and lifestyle changes. It's important to work with a doctor or dermatologist to find the best treatment plan for you.
