Thinking requires energy because it is a type of work that your brain does. Just like how your muscles need energy to move your body, your brain needs energy to do things like solve problems, remember things, and make decisions. When you engage in activities that require a lot of mental effort, like studying or solving a puzzle, your brain uses a lot of energy. This can make you feel tired, just like how using your muscles a lot can make you feel physically tired. It's important to take breaks and give your brain a chance to rest so that you can continue to think and learn effectively.
