Ships are shaped like a V, or a pointed shape, because it helps them move through water more efficiently. When a ship moves through the water, it pushes water out of the way. If the bottom of the ship is flat, like a square, it creates a lot of resistance and it is harder for the ship to move forward. A V-shaped bottom, on the other hand, helps the ship slice through the water with less resistance, making it easier to move forward.\n\nIn addition to helping the ship move more efficiently, a V-shaped bottom also helps the ship stay stable in the water. If the bottom of the ship is flat, it is more likely to tip over or roll from side to side. A V-shaped bottom helps the ship stay upright and stable, even in rough waters.\n\nWhile a square bottom might displace more water and allow the ship to carry more cargo, it would also make the ship less efficient and stable. That's why ships are typically shaped like a V instead of a square.\n
