Bottled water is not a scam, but some companies do sell tap water as bottled water. These companies usually filter the tap water to remove impurities and improve the taste before bottling it. Some companies also sell mineral water, which is water that comes from a natural spring and contains minerals like calcium, magnesium, and potassium. It's important to read the label on the bottled water you are considering buying to make sure you know what you are getting. Some bottled water is just tap water that has been treated and purified, while other bottled water comes from natural sources like springs. Overall, bottled water can be a convenient way to stay hydrated when you are on the go, but it's also important to consider the environmental impact of bottled water and to recycle the bottles when you are finished with them.
