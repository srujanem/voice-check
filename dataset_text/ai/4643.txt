Hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) is a chemical that is made up of two hydrogen atoms and two oxygen atoms. It is different from water (H2O) because it has an extra oxygen atom.\n\nHydrogen peroxide can be harmful if it is ingested or comes into contact with the skin or eyes. This is because the extra oxygen atom in hydrogen peroxide can interact with the cells in your body in a way that can damage them.\n\nIn small amounts, hydrogen peroxide is not usually harmful. However, if you swallow a lot of it or if it comes into contact with your skin or eyes for a long time, it can cause irritation or even burns.\n\nIt's important to be careful when handling hydrogen peroxide and to follow the instructions on the label. If you accidentally swallow some or get some in your eyes, it's important to seek medical help right away.
