Honey bees are among the world's most important pollinators. As they collect nectar from flowers, they unintentionally transfer pollen between plants, helping many crops produce fruits and seeds. Without bees, the production of foods such as apples, almonds, and strawberries would decrease significantly. Bee populations face challenges from habitat loss, pesticides, and disease. Planting flowers, avoiding harmful chemicals, and supporting local beekeepers are simple ways individuals can help protect these valuable insects.
