Teeth whitening strips contain a small amount of hydrogen peroxide, which is a bleaching agent that can help to remove stains and discoloration from the surface of your teeth. When you apply the strips to your teeth, the hydrogen peroxide begins to work by breaking down the molecules that cause the stains on your teeth. This process is called oxidation, and it can help to lighten the color of your teeth. 
In addition to hydrogen peroxide, some teeth whitening products may also contain other ingredients that can help to enhance the whitening process. For example, some products may contain abrasive agents that can help to remove surface stains from your teeth, or they may contain enzymes that can help to break down the molecules that cause stains. 
To use teeth whitening strips, you simply need to peel them off of the backing paper and apply them to your teeth according to the instructions on the package. You will typically need to wear the strips for a certain amount of time each day, and the length of time will depend on the specific product you are using. After the recommended treatment time has passed, you should remove the strips and rinse your mouth with water to remove any residue. 
Overall, the goal of teeth whitening is to help your teeth look brighter and more attractive. By using hydrogen peroxide and other ingredients, teeth whitening products can help to remove stains and discoloration from your teeth and give you a brighter, more confident smile.
