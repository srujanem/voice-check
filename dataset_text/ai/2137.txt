Ireland is a country located on an island in the North Atlantic Ocean. It is divided into two main political entities: the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland. The Republic of Ireland is an independent country that is a member of the European Union. It covers about five-sixths of the island of Ireland and is a parliamentary democracy with a president and a prime minister. The capital of the Republic of Ireland is Dublin. Northern Ireland is a part of the United Kingdom, which is a country that also includes England, Scotland, and Wales. It is located in the northeastern part of the island of Ireland and is a parliamentary democracy with a first minister and a deputy first minister. The capital of Northern Ireland is Belfast. The division of Ireland into the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland is a result of a long and complicated history that dates back hundreds of years. In the early 20th century, the majority of people in Ireland wanted to be independent from the United Kingdom. This led to a war of independence, which ended with the creation of the Irish Free State in 1922. However, six counties in the north of the island, including Belfast and Londonderry, decided to remain part of the United Kingdom. This led to the creation of Northern Ireland.
