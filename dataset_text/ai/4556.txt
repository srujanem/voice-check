Silica packets are included in shoe boxes to help absorb moisture and keep the shoes inside them fresh and dry. Moisture, or water, can cause shoes to become moldy or smelly, and it can also cause the materials of the shoes to break down and become damaged. Silica packets are made of a special type of material called silica gel, which is very good at absorbing moisture and keeping it locked inside the packet. By placing silica packets inside the shoe box, the shoes will stay dry and in good condition for longer periods of time.
