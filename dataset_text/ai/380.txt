Having more lawyers on your team can potentially give you more resources and support in a legal case, but it does not necessarily mean that you will have more power in court. The outcome of a legal case is determined by the facts of the case and the application of the law to those facts, not by the number of lawyers involved. 
Having a larger team of lawyers can be beneficial because it allows for more people to work on different aspects of the case, such as researching the law, gathering evidence, and preparing legal arguments. It can also allow for more flexibility if some lawyers need to focus on other cases or if unexpected developments arise. 
However, it's important to note that having a larger team of lawyers does not guarantee a favorable outcome in court. The number of lawyers involved in a case is just one factor among many that can affect the outcome.
