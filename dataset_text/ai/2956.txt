Grigori Rasputin was a Russian man who lived many years ago, from about 1869 to 1916. He was a very religious man and many people believed that he had special powers, such as the ability to heal people who were sick. Rasputin became very famous in Russia and many people, including the royal family, went to him for help with their problems. He became very influential and had a lot of power and influence over the royal family, especially the Tsarina, who was the wife of the Tsar (the leader of Russia). Some people did not like Rasputin and thought that he was a bad influence on the royal family. They believed that he was using his powers to control the Tsarina and the Tsar, and that he was making bad decisions for Russia. Eventually, some people became so angry with Rasputin that they decided to kill him. In 1916, a group of men went to his house and shot him, killing him. His death caused a lot of controversy and people still talk about him today.
