The amount of sleep that a person needs can vary from person to person. Some people are able to function well with less sleep, while others need more sleep in order to feel rested and alert. The amount of sleep that a person needs can also change throughout their life, with most adults needing 7-9 hours of sleep per night. 
There are several factors that can affect a person's sleep needs, including their age, lifestyle, and overall health. For example, older adults tend to need less sleep than younger adults, and people who are physically active or have busy schedules may need more sleep to help their bodies recover from the extra activity. 
It's also important to note that everyone is different, and what works for one person may not work for another. If you're feeling tired or having trouble functioning during the day, it's a good idea to talk to your doctor or a sleep specialist to see if there might be an underlying problem that needs to be addressed.
