Taste in music, films, and other forms of art is subjective, which means that what one person likes might not be the same as what someone else likes. People's tastes can be influenced by many different factors, including their personal experiences, cultural background, and the time and place in which they live. Even though taste is subjective, people often talk about good and bad taste as if it were objective because they have strong feelings about the things they like or dislike. It's normal for people to want to share their opinions and discuss their favorite artists, films, or other works of art. Sometimes, people might also use words like "good" or "bad" taste to describe the things they like or dislike, even though these words might not be accurate because they are based on personal opinions. It's important to remember that everyone's tastes are different, and it's okay to like or dislike something that someone else might not. It's also okay to have strong opinions about the things you like or dislike, as long as you respect other people's opinions and preferences.
