It can be difficult to identify the ideal human diet because different people have different nutritional needs based on factors such as their age, sex, weight, height, level of physical activity, and overall health. Some people may require more or fewer calories, or more or less of certain nutrients, depending on their individual needs and goals. Additionally, research on nutrition is ongoing and new information is constantly being discovered, which can sometimes lead to conflicting recommendations. Finally, there can be a lot of misinformation or conflicting claims about nutrition, which can make it difficult for people to know what to believe.
