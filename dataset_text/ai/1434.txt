Living organisms are made up of cells, which are the basic unit of life. These cells are made up of molecules, which are made up of atoms. Atoms are the smallest units of matter that can exist on their own and are made up of even smaller particles called protons, neutrons, and electrons. 
In order for an organism to survive, its cells need to be able to carry out a variety of functions such as obtaining energy, reproducing, and responding to their environment. These functions require a certain level of organization and structure within the cells. Gas particles, on the other hand, are very spread out and lack the organization and structure needed for life. 
It is possible for an organism to be made up of gas, but it would not be able to carry out the functions needed to sustain life. For example, gas particles do not have the ability to carry out chemical reactions, which are necessary for obtaining energy and reproducing. Additionally, gas particles do not have the ability to sense their environment and respond to stimuli, which are also important for an organism to survive. 
So while it is possible for an organism to be made up of gas, it is not able to sustain life because it lacks the necessary organization and structure.
