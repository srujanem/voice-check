Getting haircuts can help your hair grow better because it can remove split ends and other damaged areas from the hair. When the hair is damaged, it can break off and stop growing. By removing the damaged parts of the hair, you allow the healthy hair to continue growing and keep it looking healthy and strong. 
Think of it like pruning a plant. When you prune a plant, you remove any dead or damaged branches. This helps the plant focus its energy on growing healthy new branches and leaves, rather than trying to keep the damaged ones alive. It's the same with hair. By getting regular haircuts, you can remove the damaged parts and allow the healthy hair to continue growing and looking its best.
