Muslim and Islam are two words that are often used together, but they do not mean the same thing. Muslim is a word that refers to a person who follows the religion of Islam. A Muslim is someone who believes in one God (called Allah) and follows the teachings of the prophet Muhammad. Islam is the name of the religion that Muslims follow. It is a monotheistic religion, which means that Muslims believe in one God. Islam teaches that God revealed his teachings to Muhammad, and that these teachings are recorded in the Quran, a holy book for Muslims. So, to sum it up: A Muslim is a person who follows the religion of Islam. Islam is the name of the religion that Muslims follow.
