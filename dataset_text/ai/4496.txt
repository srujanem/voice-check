Light travels through the air and can bounce off of objects and surfaces. When light bounces off of an object, it can then travel to our eyes, allowing us to see the object. This is why you can see a lit candle from 20 feet away, even though it only casts light 2 feet around it. The light from the candle is bouncing off of the air and other objects in the room, and some of that light is reaching your eyes.\n\nIt's a bit like when you throw a ball and it bounces off the ground. Even though the ball doesn't travel very far on its own, it can still reach you if it bounces off the ground and flies through the air. Light works in a similar way.
