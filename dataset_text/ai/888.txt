FM radio is more popular than AM radio because it is able to transmit sound with better quality. FM stands for "frequency modulation," which refers to the way that the radio station sends its signal over the airwaves. FM radio uses higher frequency waves to transmit the signal, and these waves are able to carry more information and provide better sound quality. AM radio, on the other hand, stands for "amplitude modulation" and uses lower frequency waves that are not able to transmit as much information or provide as good of sound quality. Because FM radio is able to provide better sound quality, it has become more popular than AM radio for music and other types of programming.
