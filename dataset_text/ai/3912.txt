The Earth rotates because it is a sphere, and it was formed by a swirling cloud of gas and dust around 4.5 billion years ago. When this cloud collapsed to form the Earth, it kept spinning, and it has been spinning ever since. It's kind of like when you spin a ball on your finger - the ball will keep spinning for a while, and it will only stop if something slows it down or makes it change direction. The Earth is still spinning today because there is nothing to slow it down or change its direction. Does that make sense?
