Sure! Occam's Razor is a principle that says when we are trying to solve a problem, we should try to find the simplest explanation for it. This means we should look for the explanation that requires the fewest number of steps or the fewest number of assumptions. For example, let's say you are trying to figure out why your toy car won't move. One explanation could be that the battery is dead. Another explanation could be that the toy car is broken and needs to be fixed. According to Occam's Razor, the simplest explanation is probably the right one. In this case, it is more likely that the battery is dead, because that explanation requires fewer steps to test (just changing the battery) and fewer assumptions (that the toy car is not broken).
