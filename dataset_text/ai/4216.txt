Water itself doesn't go bad, but the bottle it's stored in can. The expiration date on a bottle of water is actually the date that the bottle itself is no longer considered safe to use. Plastic bottles can break down over time and may release chemicals into the water. This is especially true if the bottle is exposed to heat or sunlight, which can cause the plastic to break down faster. It's also possible for bacteria or other contaminants to get into the bottle if it's not sealed properly, which could make the water taste or smell bad. That's why it's a good idea to replace the bottle once it reaches its expiration date, just to be safe.
