Centrifugal force is the force that appears to push objects away from the center of a circular motion. For example, if you are in a car and take a turn, you may feel like you are being pushed to the side. This is because your body is trying to move in a straight line, but the car is turning, so you feel a force pushing you outward.\n\nCentripetal force is the force that actually causes an object to move in a circular path. It is the force that is pulling or pushing an object towards the center of a circular path. For example, the force of gravity is a centripetal force that keeps the moon orbiting around the Earth.\n\nSo, to sum it up: centrifugal force is the force that you feel when you are moving in a circular path, while centripetal force is the force that is causing you to move in a circular path.
