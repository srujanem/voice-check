Fast food meals often include fries and soft drinks because they are popular and convenient items to include in the meal. Fries are a type of food that is made by cutting potatoes into thin, long pieces and then frying them until they are crispy and golden brown. They are a tasty and satisfying side dish that can be eaten with a variety of different meals. Soft drinks are beverages that are made with carbonated water, sweeteners, and flavorings, and they are often served cold. They can help to quench your thirst and provide a refreshing break from the salty and savory flavors of the main meal. 
It's not exactly clear when the practice of including fries and soft drinks in fast food meals began, but it has likely been around for a long time. Fast food restaurants have been around for many decades, and it's likely that fries and soft drinks have been popular menu items for much of that time. The combination of a main meal, fries, and a soft drink is often referred to as a "combo" or a "value meal," and it is a convenient and affordable way for people to enjoy a complete meal at a fast food restaurant.
