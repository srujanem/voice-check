If the government just prints more pie and gives it out, it doesn't actually create any more houses or cars. There are still the same number of things to go around, but now there are more slices of pie trying to buy them. This means that the pie slices are worth less because there are more of them trying to buy the same things. For example, if you had one slice of pie and wanted to buy a house that cost 100 slices of pie, you would have to give up your entire slice to buy the house. But if the government printed an extra 100 slices of pie and gave them to everyone, suddenly there are 200 slices of pie trying to buy the same house. This means that the value of each slice of pie goes down, so it might take two slices of pie to buy the house instead of just one. So, while printing more money might seem like a good way to solve economic problems, it can actually make things worse by reducing the value of money and making it harder for people to buy things.
