Facebook is a free website that anyone can use, but it is not actually free for the company to run. The company needs to pay for servers, staff, and other expenses. So, how does Facebook make money?, One way is through advertising. Companies pay Facebook to show their ads to people who use the site. When someone clicks on an ad or sees it and buys something as a result, Facebook makes money. Another way is through selling products. Facebook has a marketplace where people can buy and sell things to each other. Facebook also has a feature called Facebook Pay, which lets people send money to each other or make purchases. Overall, Facebook makes money by offering a service that a lot of people use, and then finding ways to make money from those users, such as by showing them ads or selling them products.
