Water is not commonly sold in cans because cans are usually made of metal, and metal can interact with the water and potentially change its taste. Water is also relatively heavy, so it would be inconvenient to carry around a can of water compared to a bottle of water. Additionally, water is generally less expensive than other drinks, so it doesn't make as much sense to package it in a more expensive container like a can. Finally, bottles of water are easier to store and transport than cans, since they take up less space and are lighter.
