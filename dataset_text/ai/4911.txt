CC stands for "carbon copy" and BCC stands for "blind carbon copy." These are options that you can use when sending an email to multiple recipients.\n\nCC: When you CC someone on an email, it means that you are sending a copy of the email to them. They will be able to see the email and all of the other recipients who were included in the email.\n\nBCC: When you BCC someone on an email, it means that you are sending a copy of the email to them, but they will not be able to see the other recipients who were included in the email.\n\nBoth CC and BCC recipients can reply to or forward the email just like any other recipient. The difference is just in how the email is displayed to them and to the other recipients.\n\nI hope this helps! Let me know if you have any other questions.
