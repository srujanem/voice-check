Intelligence quotient, or IQ, is a measure of cognitive abilities, including things like problem-solving, critical thinking, and the ability to learn new things. While it's true that you can improve certain specific skills by training and practice, it's generally thought that your overall IQ is largely determined by genetics and is relatively stable throughout your life. This means that while you can certainly become better at certain tasks or subjects, it's difficult to significantly change your overall level of intelligence.\n\nIt's also important to note that IQ tests are designed to be challenging, so it's normal to not get every question right. Your IQ score is based on how you compare to other people your age, so it's possible to score higher or lower depending on the group you're being compared to. Finally, it's worth remembering that intelligence is just one aspect of a person and that there are many different ways to be smart and successful.
