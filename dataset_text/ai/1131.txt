It's normal to have difficulty making a tight fist with your hands straight after waking up because your muscles are relaxed when you sleep. When you sleep, your body naturally relaxes, including your muscles. This is why it can be difficult to make a tight fist or do other activities that require muscle control or strength when you first wake up. It's nothing to worry about and should get better as you move around and become more awake. You can try gently stretching your hands and fingers to help loosen them up, and you should be able to make a tight fist more easily as your muscles warm up and become more awake.
