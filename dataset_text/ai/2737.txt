Different types of salt have different characteristics, such as taste, texture, and size of the crystals, which can affect how they are used in cooking. Here are some common types of salt and their differences: Table salt is a very fine, white salt that is often used in cooking because it dissolves easily and is easy to measure. It is also very affordable. Sea salt is made from the saltwater of the ocean. It is coarser than table salt and has a more distinct taste because it contains other minerals from the ocean. Some people prefer the taste of sea salt because they find it to be less salty and more flavorful than table salt. Kosher salt is a type of coarse salt that is used in cooking, especially in kosher cooking. It is called "kosher" because it is used in the process of koshering meat, which means preparing the meat according to Jewish dietary laws. Kosher salt has a milder taste than table salt and is often used to season meats because it sticks to the surface of the food better. In general, the main difference between these types of salt is their taste and texture. Table salt is the most common type of salt used in cooking, but sea salt and kosher salt can add different flavors and textures to dishes. It's a matter of personal preference which type of salt you use in your cooking.
