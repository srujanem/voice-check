Legal pads are typically yellow because the color is easy to see and is not easily confused with other colors. Blueprints are blue because blue ink is used to print them and blue light is used to make them. The blue color helps to reduce glare and makes the lines and text on the blueprint easier to see. The color of a legal pad does not affect its ability to be copied, as legal pads are usually used for writing and taking notes, rather than for printing documents.
