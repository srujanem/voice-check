Conservatives in the United States often argue that the Department of Education is unnecessary because it is an unnecessary layer of bureaucracy that adds costs to the education system without improving student outcomes. They believe that states and local school districts should have more control over education policy and funding, rather than the federal government. Some conservatives also argue that the Department of Education has overstepped its bounds by imposing federal regulations on schools and interfering with local decision-making. Additionally, some conservatives may believe that the Department of Education is not effective at achieving its goals, such as improving student achievement and closing the achievement gap between different groups of students.
