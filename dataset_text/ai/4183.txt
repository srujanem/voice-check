The 16:9 aspect ratio (the ratio of the width to the height of an image or screen) was chosen as the standard for many types of visual media because it is a good compromise between showing a lot of the image and having a more cinematic or widescreen appearance. This aspect ratio is also similar to the aspect ratio of traditional movie film, which is why it is often used for movies and television shows.\n\nOne reason the 16:9 aspect ratio is so popular is that it is close to the aspect ratio of the human field of vision, which is about 16:10. This means that when you watch something in 16:9, it feels more natural and immersive because the image takes up a larger portion of your field of view.\n\nAnother reason the 16:9 aspect ratio is used so widely is that it works well on a variety of different screen sizes, from small phone screens to large television screens. This makes it easier for content creators to produce media that can be watched on a variety of devices without having to worry about the aspect ratio looking wrong on any of them.\n\nOverall, the 16:9 aspect ratio was chosen as the standard for many types of visual media because it looks good on a wide range of screen sizes and offers a nice balance between showing a lot of the image and having a more cinematic appearance.
