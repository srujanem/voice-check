When you spin a basketball on your finger, it stays in place because of something called "centrifugal force." This is a force that acts on an object that is spinning around a center point. When you spin the basketball on your finger, the ball wants to fly off in a straight line away from your finger, but because it is attached to your finger, it can't fly off. Instead, it stays in place and spins around your finger. Centrifugal force is the force that makes the ball feel "heavy" when you are spinning it.
