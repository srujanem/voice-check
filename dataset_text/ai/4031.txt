When you close your eyes and visualize something, your brain is creating a mental picture using information from your memory. Your memory is like a library of all the things you have seen, heard, and experienced. When you want to visualize something, your brain goes to the library and pulls out the information it needs to create a mental image. It's kind of like how you use your eyes to see things when they are in front of you, except you are using your mind instead of your eyes to see the images.
