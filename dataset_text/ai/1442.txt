J.R.R. Tolkien, the author of The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings, was a linguist as well as a writer. He was very interested in languages and enjoyed creating his own languages for the different races of creatures that he invented in his stories. 
To invent the Elvish language, Tolkien started by coming up with a set of sounds and symbols that he thought would be suitable for the Elvish language. He then used these sounds and symbols to create words and sentences in the Elvish language. 
Tolkien also drew inspiration from real-life languages for the Elvish language. For example, he used elements of Finnish, Welsh, and Latin to help create the grammar and structure of the Elvish language. 
Overall, inventing the Elvish language was a long and careful process for Tolkien, as he wanted it to sound and feel authentic and believable within the world of Middle-earth.
