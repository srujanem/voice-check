The human digestive system is responsible for breaking down food into nutrients that the body can absorb. The process begins in the mouth, where salivary amylase starts breaking down carbohydrates. Food travels down the esophagus to the stomach, where it is mixed with gastric acid and enzymes. The small intestine is the primary site of nutrient absorption, while the large intestine absorbs water and prepares waste for elimination. Understanding these principles is foundational to making progress in this important area. Furthermore, it is worth noting that the implications of human digestive system extend beyond the immediate context.
