When you're sick, your body is working hard to fight off an illness or infection. One way it does this is by releasing certain chemicals that can change the way things taste. These chemicals can make healthy foods taste bad, even though they might be good for you. It's kind of like when you have a cold and everything tastes kind of bland or weird. This is because your body is focusing on getting better, and it might not be as interested in food as it usually is. That's why it's important to try to eat a balanced diet and get enough nutrients, even when you're not feeling well, to help your body heal and recover.
