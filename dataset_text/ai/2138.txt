Soda can become flat because carbon dioxide, which is what gives soda its fizz, can escape from the can even though it is sealed. This can happen because the carbon dioxide is dissolved in the liquid, and it will tend to come out of the solution and into the air if given the chance. When you open a can of soda, the pressure inside the can decreases, which allows some of the dissolved carbon dioxide to come out of the solution and form bubbles. If you leave the can open for a long time, more and more of the carbon dioxide will come out of the solution and escape into the air, which is why the soda becomes flat.
