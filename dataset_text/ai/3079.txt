Light from the sun appears yellow to us because it is made up of a mixture of different colors of light, and the color yellow is the most dominant. However, light from stars appears white to us because it is made up of a similar mixture of colors, but the colors are more evenly distributed. The colors of light are determined by the wavelengths of the light. Different wavelengths correspond to different colors of light. For example, red light has longer wavelengths, while blue light has shorter wavelengths. When light is made up of a mixture of many different wavelengths, it appears white to our eyes. So, to sum it up: the sun appears yellow to us because it has more yellow light in it than other colors, while stars appear white because they have a more even mixture of all the colors of the rainbow.
