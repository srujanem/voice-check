Before the internet, music videos were primarily used as a way to promote an artist's music and increase album sales. Music videos were often played on television music channels, such as MTV, VH1, and BET, and could also be rented or purchased on VHS or DVD.\n\nRecord companies made money from music videos by selling the rights to air the videos on television or by selling copies of the video to consumers. Record companies would also often include music videos as part of an album package, where the cost of the video would be included in the price of the album.\n\nOverall, music videos were seen as a way to help increase the popularity and success of an artist, and the profits from the sales of music videos and increased album sales would help offset the costs of producing the music video.
