Activated charcoal is a fine, black powder that is made from things like coconut shells, wood, and peat. It is called "activated" because it is treated with oxygen to make it very porous, which means that it has lots of tiny little holes in it. This makes it good at trapping things, like impurities in the skin.\n\nPeople use activated charcoal in a number of different ways. One common use is in skin care products, where it is used to help absorb excess oil and impurities from the skin. It is also sometimes used in toothpaste and mouthwash to help whiten teeth and freshen breath.\n\nThere are also some people who take activated charcoal as a supplement, either in capsule form or mixed into a drink. Some people believe that it can help with things like bloating, gas, and diarrhea, although there is not much scientific evidence to support these claims.\n\nOverall, activated charcoal is a safe and natural substance that can be used in a variety of ways to improve the health and appearance of the skin and teeth. It is important to follow the instructions on any product that contains activated charcoal, as using too much can cause unwanted side effects.
