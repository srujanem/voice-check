Here's a simple way to understand how it works: Imagine that you have a tray of marbles and you shake the tray back and forth. The marbles will start to roll around and move all over the place. But if you shake the tray in just the right way, the marbles will start to arrange themselves in a pattern. That's kind of like what's happening with the salt on the vibrating plate.
