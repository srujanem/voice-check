Radiation is energy that travels through the air or through space. It can be created by different things, such as the sun, radio waves, and even the food we eat. There are different types of radiation, including light, heat, and radio waves. Some types of radiation are harmless, like the light we see every day. But other types of radiation, like X-rays and nuclear radiation, can be harmful if we are exposed to too much of it. Radiation can also be used to help people, like when doctors use X-rays to see inside our bodies and check for broken bones or other injuries. But it is important to be careful with radiation and not be exposed to too much of it, because it can cause harm to our bodies.
