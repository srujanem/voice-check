A non-disclosure agreement (NDA) is a legal contract that is used to protect sensitive information from being shared with others. The purpose of an NDA is to prevent someone from disclosing or sharing confidential information, such as trade secrets, business plans, or other proprietary information, without the permission of the person or company who owns the information.\n\nIf you witness someone in your company doing something against the law, you should report the activity to your supervisor or to the appropriate authorities. The NDA may still apply, but it is important to follow the laws and regulations that apply to your company. If you are unsure what to do, you can speak with a lawyer or seek guidance from someone you trust. It is always better to be safe and follow the law, rather than risk getting in trouble or breaking your agreement.
