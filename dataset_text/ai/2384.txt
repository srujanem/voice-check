Mental fatigue is a feeling of tiredness or exhaustion that is related to mental or cognitive activity, rather than physical activity. It can occur when you have been thinking hard, concentrating for a long time, or working on a task that requires a lot of mental effort. Your brain is a complex organ that is responsible for many functions, including thinking, learning, and problem-solving. Just like your muscles, your brain can get tired when it is working hard. When your brain is tired, you may feel like you don't have the energy or motivation to think clearly or make decisions. One way that mental fatigue can affect you is by making it more difficult to pay attention or concentrate. When you are mentally fatigued, your brain may struggle to focus on a task, which can make it harder to learn new information or remember things. Another way that mental fatigue can affect you is by making you feel more emotional or reactive. When you are mentally tired, you may be more likely to feel irritable, frustrated, or overwhelmed. So, to sum it up, mental fatigue is a feeling of tiredness that occurs when your brain has been working hard and needs a break. Just like your muscles, your brain needs rest and recovery in order to function at its best.
