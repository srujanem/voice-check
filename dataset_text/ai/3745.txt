Electricity is a type of energy that can be used to power machines and appliances. When you flip a switch to turn on a fan, for example, you are allowing electricity to flow from the outlet through a wire and into the fan. The electricity flows through the wire and into the fan motor, which is a small electric machine. The motor converts the electrical energy into mechanical energy, which is the energy of motion. The motor uses this energy to spin the blades of the fan. So, in short, when you flip a switch to turn on a fan, you are allowing electricity to flow through a wire and into the fan motor. The motor converts this electricity into motion, which is used to spin the blades of the fan.
