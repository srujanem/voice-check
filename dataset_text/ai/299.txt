There are a few reasons why you might have difficulty with speed reading. One reason could be that you are not familiar with the material you are reading. If you are trying to speed read something that you don't understand, it can be hard to keep up with the words and make sense of what you are reading. 
Another reason could be that you are not used to reading at a fast pace. Speed reading takes practice and it can be hard to keep up with the words if you are not used to reading quickly. 
Finally, it could be that you have a reading disability, such as dyslexia, which makes it difficult for you to process written words quickly and accurately. If you think this might be the case, it is important to speak with a teacher or a reading specialist to get help with your reading skills.
