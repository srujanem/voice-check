The names of chip companies are often plural because they are named after the type of chips that they make. For example, Fritos is a brand of corn chips, Doritos is a brand of flavored tortilla chips, Ruffles is a brand of ridged potato chips, Lays is a brand of potato chips, and Cheetos is a brand of cheese-flavored snacks. The names of these brands are all plural because they refer to multiple chips, rather than just one. In other words, when you buy a bag of Fritos, you are getting a bag of multiple Fritos chips, not just one.
