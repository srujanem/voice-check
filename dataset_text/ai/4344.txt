Silencers, also known as suppressors, are devices that are attached to the barrel of a gun and are designed to reduce the amount of noise and muzzle flash that is produced when the gun is fired.\n\nWhen a gun is fired, the bullet is propelled out of the barrel by a rapidly expanding gas. This gas is what causes the loud noise and bright flash that is associated with gunfire.\n\nSilencers work by allowing the gas to expand slowly and cool off as it passes through a series of tubes or chambers inside the suppressor. This helps to reduce the noise and flash that is produced when the gun is fired.\n\nAs for the second part of your question, the silencers in movies often do not sound like real ones.
