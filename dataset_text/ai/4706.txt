The stars always seem to be in the same spot in the sky because they are so far away from the Earth. Even though the Earth is always spinning and rotating, the stars are so far away that they appear to be in the same place in the sky all the time. Imagine that you are standing on a playground and you see a big tree in the distance. If you spin around and around on the playground, the tree might seem to move a little bit, but it will always be in roughly the same place because it is so far away. The same is true for the stars. They are so far away from the Earth that they appear to stay in the same place even though the Earth is spinning.
