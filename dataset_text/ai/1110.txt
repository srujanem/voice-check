British and American humor can be quite different, although there is also a lot of overlap. Here are a few key ways that British and American humor might differ:

3. Tone and delivery: The way that jokes are told and received can vary between British and American humor. British humor is often known for being more dry, understated, and subtle, while American humor is often more boisterous and in-your-face. 
4. Subject matter: Different cultures may find different things funny, and this can affect the types of jokes that are told. For example, British humor is often known for being more satirical and politically charged, while American humor is often more focused on pop culture and everyday life.
