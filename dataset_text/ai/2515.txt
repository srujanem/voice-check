Comic Sans is a font that was designed to look like handwriting, with a playful and casual style. Some people find it fun and appropriate for certain types of documents, such as invitations or flyers for events. However, it has also become widely criticized and is often considered to be inappropriate for more formal or professional documents because it can look childish or unprofessional. Some people find it difficult to read, and it may not be suitable for documents that need to convey information clearly and seriously. It's important to choose a font that is appropriate for the purpose of the document and the audience it is intended for.
