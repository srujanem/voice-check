Life imitating art means that real life events or situations are similar to or inspired by something that has been depicted in a work of art, such as a book, movie, television show, or painting. Here are some examples of life imitating art: A character in a book or movie becomes popular and people start dressing or acting like them in real life. A scientist reads a science fiction novel and is inspired to invent something similar to the technology described in the book. A politician gives a speech that is similar to a speech given by a character in a movie or TV show. A fashion designer creates a new style of clothing that is inspired by a work of art, such as a painting. In these examples, life is imitating art because real life events or situations are being influenced or inspired by something that has been depicted in a work of art.
