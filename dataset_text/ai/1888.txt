The Big Bang is the name scientists give to the event that happened about 13.8 billion years ago, which is thought to be the beginning of the universe. It's called the Big Bang because it was an explosion that sent matter and energy flying out in all directions. The cause of the Big Bang is not fully understood, but scientists have a few theories about what might have caused it. One theory is that the Big Bang was caused by a singularity, which is a point in space where the laws of physics break down. Another theory is that the Big Bang was caused by the collapse of a previous universe. It's important to note that the Big Bang is still a theory, and scientists are constantly working to better understand it and how the universe began. However, it is the most widely accepted theory for the origin of the universe, and it is supported by a great deal of evidence.
