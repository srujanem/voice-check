Hotels often offer different amenities at different price points to appeal to different types of travelers. Some people are willing to pay more for a higher level of luxury and convenience, while others may be more price-sensitive and looking for a good value. 
The cheapest hotels in a brand may offer more amenities for free because they are trying to attract budget-conscious travelers who are looking for a good deal. These hotels may not have as many amenities or as high-end finishes as the more expensive hotels in the same brand, but they can still offer a good level of comfort and convenience at a lower price point. 
On the other hand, upper echelon hotels that are more expensive may not need to offer as many amenities for free because they are targeting travelers who are willing to pay more for a higher level of luxury and convenience. These hotels may also have more amenities and services available for an additional fee, such as spa treatments or room service, which can help to increase their revenue. 
Overall, it comes down to the different target markets and business models of different hotels, and the amenities and services that they choose to offer reflect their target audience and the type of experience they are trying to provide.
