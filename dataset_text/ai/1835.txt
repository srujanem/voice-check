The Hubble Space Telescope is a very powerful telescope that is able to capture images of objects in space that are very far away. It can take detailed images of galaxies that are millions of light-years away because it is able to capture a lot of light and it is able to see very faint objects. On the other hand, taking clear images of Pluto is more difficult because it is much closer to us than the galaxies that the Hubble can see. Pluto is also a lot smaller than most galaxies, so it is harder to see clearly from such a distance. Additionally, Pluto is very far away from Earth, so it is difficult for telescopes on Earth to get a clear view of it. Overall, the reason that the Hubble can take such clear images of distant galaxies but not of Pluto is because of the differences in distance, size, and the amount of light that each object gives off.
