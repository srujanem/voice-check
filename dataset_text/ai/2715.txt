An umlaut is a diacritical mark that is placed above a vowel in some languages, such as German. It changes the sound of the vowel. Here's how to pronounce the letters with umlauts: Ä: This is pronounced like the "ey" sound in the English word "hey". Ö: This is pronounced like the "ur" sound in the English word "burr". Ü: This is pronounced like the "ee" sound in the English word "see", but with rounded lips. Here's an example of how to use umlauts in a word: Mädchen: This means "girl" in German.
