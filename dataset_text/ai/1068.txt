Electric cars and gas cars work a little bit differently, so they need different kinds of gears. 
Electric cars have motors that can generate a lot of power very quickly. This means that they don't need as many gears as gas cars, because they can go from a standstill to high speeds without as much help. Gas cars, on the other hand, need gears to help them get up to speed. They have engines that can't generate as much power as quickly as electric motors, so they need to use gears to help them go faster. 
Think of it like this: Imagine you're trying to climb a really steep hill. You could try to walk up the hill using just your own strength, but it would be really hard and take a long time. But if you had a bike with gears, you could use the gears to make it easier to pedal and go up the hill faster. That's kind of how gears work in a car - they help the engine (or motor) to do its job better and go faster.
