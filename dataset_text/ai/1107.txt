Sleeping too much can actually make you feel tired because it can disrupt your body's natural sleep-wake cycle. When you sleep, your body goes through different stages of sleep, including deep sleep and REM (rapid eye movement) sleep. These stages are important for your physical and mental health, and they help you feel rested and refreshed when you wake up. When you oversleep, you may disrupt these stages and throw off your body's natural sleep-wake cycle, which can lead to feelings of fatigue. It's also possible that oversleeping may be a sign of an underlying health issue, such as a sleep disorder or depression, which can also contribute to feelings of fatigue. It's generally recommended to aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night for adults to feel rested and refreshed.
