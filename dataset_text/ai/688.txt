Giving money as a gift is often considered impersonal because it can feel like the giver did not put much thought or effort into choosing a gift. A gift card, on the other hand, shows that the giver has at least considered the recipient's interests or needs and has chosen a gift card for a specific store or type of merchandise that the recipient might enjoy. 
Gift cards also have the added advantage of being more flexible than cash, as the recipient can choose to use the gift card at a later date or save it for a special occasion. With cash, the recipient may feel pressure to spend it right away or may not know how to best use the money. 
Overall, the main difference between giving money and giving a gift card is the perceived level of thought and effort that goes into the gift. While both options can be practical and appreciated, a gift card can feel more personal and considerate because it shows that the giver took the time to select a gift specifically for the recipient.
