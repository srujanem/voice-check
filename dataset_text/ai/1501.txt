The synchronizer is a device that connects the machine gun to the plane's engine, and it monitors the position of the propeller blades. When the synchronizer senses that a propeller blade is about to pass in front of the gun, it temporarily stops the gun from firing. As the blade moves out of the way, the synchronizer allows the gun to start firing again.
