Dark circles under the eyes are usually caused by shadows being cast by the lower eyelids. The skin under the eyes is thin and delicate, and the blood vessels that are located just under the skin can sometimes be seen through the skin, especially if the skin is pale. When the skin is thin, the blood vessels can cast a shadow, making the area under the eyes look dark. Sometimes, dark circles can also be caused by allergies, lack of sleep, or certain medical conditions. If you have dark circles and you're not sure why, it might be a good idea to talk to a doctor to see if there's anything that can be done to help.
