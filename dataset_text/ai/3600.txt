Secession is when a group of people or a place decides to stop being part of a larger group or union that they were previously a part of. For example, in the United States, each state is part of the larger union known as the United States of America. If a state wanted to secede, it would mean that they would no longer be a part of the United States and would go back to being an independent country. This would be a very big and complicated process, and it would involve many different steps. It would likely involve the state's government passing laws and making decisions about how to separate from the United States. It would also involve negotiations and discussions with the federal government and other states about how to divide up things like money, resources, and responsibilities. Secession is not something that happens very often, and it is generally not an easy or simple process. It usually requires a lot of discussion and negotiation, and it can sometimes lead to conflicts or even wars.
