There are a few reasons why YouTube comments might seem worse than comments on other social media platforms: YouTube is a very large platform, with billions of users. This means that there are a lot of people posting comments, and it can be difficult to moderate all of them. YouTube allows anonymous commenting, which means that people can post comments without revealing their real identity. This can lead to more hateful or offensive comments, as people may feel more free to say things that they wouldn't normally say if they were using their real name. YouTube has a feature called "upvoting" which allows users to "like" a comment by giving it a thumbs up. This can lead to certain comments, especially inflammatory or controversial ones, getting more visibility and attention than they would otherwise. YouTube is a platform that attracts a wide range of users, including young people and adults. This can lead to a mix of comments that are not always appropriate for all audiences. Overall, it's important to remember that not all comments on YouTube (or any other platform) are representative of the entire community. It's always a good idea to be mindful of what you read and take online comments with a grain of salt.
