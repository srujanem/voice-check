Your skin's cells produce a pigment called melanin in response to UV rays, which helps to protect your skin from the sun's harmful effects. When your skin produces more melanin, it becomes darker, and this is what we call a tan. Sunscreen and tanning lotion are products that you can use to protect your skin from the sun's UV rays. Sunscreen works by creating a physical barrier on your skin that blocks the UV rays from reaching your skin cells. Tanning lotion, on the other hand, is designed to help your skin produce more melanin, so that you can get a tan more quickly. There is no limit on how tanned someone can be, but it is important to remember that getting too much sun exposure can be harmful to your skin. Sunburns and excessive tanning can increase your risk of developing skin cancer, so it is important to use sunscreen and tanning lotion responsibly and to avoid spending too much time in the sun.
