Sure! A server is a special computer that is used to store and manage websites, or to store and manage data for other computers. Imagine that you have a bunch of notebooks at school, and you want to keep them organized and safe. You might put them all in a big cabinet or locker. That's kind of like what a server does - it stores and organizes information, just like the cabinet or locker stores and organizes your notebooks. But servers are a little bit different from regular computers, because they're usually more powerful and they can handle lots of different requests at the same time. So, if lots of people want to access a website or use a program at the same time, the server can help make sure that everything runs smoothly and everyone gets what they need. Does that make sense?
