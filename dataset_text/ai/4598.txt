Identity theft is a crime where someone pretends to be someone else in order to steal money or other benefits. For example, someone might steal someone else's name, birth date, and social security number, and use that information to open a credit card account or get a loan in someone else's name. This can cause a lot of problems for the person whose identity was stolen, because they might not even know it happened until they get a bill or notice that they have to pay for something they didn't buy.\n\nTo become someone else, a person might try to get someone else's personal information, such as by stealing their mail or hacking into their computer. They might also try to trick someone into giving them their information, like by pretending to be a bank or a government agency and asking for someone's social security number or password. It's important to be careful with your personal information and to make sure you're only giving it to trusted sources.
