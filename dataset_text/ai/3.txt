Baking bread is both a science and an art. Start by mixing flour, water, yeast, salt, and a small amount of sugar. Knead the dough until it becomes smooth and elastic, then let it rise in a warm place until it doubles in size. Shape the dough into a loaf and allow it to rise again before baking in a preheated oven. Freshly baked bread has a crisp crust, a soft interior, and a comforting aroma that makes it a favorite in many households.
