There are a few reasons why it took a while for cell phones to use a universal charger. First, different companies made cell phones, and each company wanted to use its own charging technology and connectors. This meant that if you had a phone from one company, you would need a different charger than someone with a phone from another company. Second, technology was constantly changing and improving, which meant that newer phones often used different charging technology than older phones. This made it difficult to create a universal charger that would work with all phones. Finally, it took some time for the industry to agree on a standard for universal chargers. The micro USB charger, which is now widely used, was not developed until the late 2000s. Overall, it took a while for cell phones to use a universal charger because different companies used different charging technology, technology was constantly changing, and it took time to agree on a standard.
