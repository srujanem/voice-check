When a woman is pregnant, the baby grows inside her uterus (womb). The baby is formed when a man's sperm fertilizes a woman's egg. The fertilized egg is called a zygote. The zygote divides into smaller cells and grows into a ball of cells called a blastocyst. The blastocyst implants itself into the wall of the uterus, where it starts to grow into a baby.\n\nThe baby gets its mass from the mother's body. The mother's body provides the baby with the nutrients and energy it needs to grow and develop. The baby also gets mass from the placenta, which is an organ that grows in the uterus during pregnancy. The placenta provides the baby with oxygen and nutrients from the mother's blood.\n\nSo, to sum it up, the mass for the baby comes from the mother's body and the nutrients and energy provided by the placenta.
