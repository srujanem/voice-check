The hair on different parts of your body grows at different rates and for different lengths of time because of the different types of hair follicles in those areas. Hair follicles are tiny structures in your skin that grow and produce hair. The hair on your head and face is called terminal hair, and it grows for a longer period of time than the hair on other parts of your body, like your arms and legs. Terminal hair also grows much thicker and longer than the hair on other parts of your body. On the other hand, the hair on your arms, legs, and eyebrows is called vellus hair, and it is much finer and shorter than terminal hair. Vellus hair grows for a shorter period of time and stops growing at a certain length. So, the reason the hair on your head and face continues to grow until it is cut is because those hairs have special follicles that allow them to grow for a longer period of time.
