Eating 7000 calories in one sitting would be a very large amount of food for most people and could lead to weight gain. However, it's important to understand that the process of gaining or losing weight is not as simple as just "storing" or "burning" calories. When you eat food, your body breaks down the nutrients in the food and uses them for energy. Any excess energy that your body doesn't use right away can be stored in your body as fat. So if you eat more calories than your body needs for energy, you might gain weight. However, it's important to note that the process of gaining or losing weight is complex and can be influenced by many different factors, including your age, gender, body size, and level of physical activity. It's also important to note that weight can fluctuate for many reasons, and it's not always a direct result of how many calories you eat in a single sitting.
