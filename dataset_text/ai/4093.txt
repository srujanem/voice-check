The universe is expanding, which means that all of the galaxies in the universe are getting farther apart from each other. But the universe doesn't have an edge like a ball or a balloon. Instead, it's more like a piece of fabric that can stretch and grow without ending. Imagine you have a piece of paper, and you draw a bunch of dots on it. If you hold the paper flat, the dots are close together. But if you stretch the paper, the dots get farther apart. The paper is expanding just like the universe, and the dots are like the galaxies. We can't see to the edge of the universe because there is no edge. If we could see forever, we would still never reach the end of the universe because it goes on and on.
