When you get a "knot" in your muscle, it's called a muscle spasm or muscle cramp. It happens when the muscle becomes tight and doesn't relax. Sometimes, you can see the knot because it feels like a bump under your skin. Massage can help to relax the muscle and make the knot go away. Other things that might help are stretching, taking a warm bath or shower, and using a heating pad or warm cloth on the muscle. Drinking plenty of water and getting enough rest can also help prevent muscle knots from happening.
