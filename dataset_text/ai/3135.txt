Ambient temperature feels hot when it is close to your body temperature because your body is used to being a certain temperature and it has to work harder to maintain that temperature when the air around you is hot. When the ambient temperature is hot, your body sweats in an attempt to cool down and this can make you feel uncomfortable. On a hot day, your body sweats to try and cool itself down. When the sweat evaporates off of your skin, it helps to lower your body temperature. However, on a very hot day, the air around you may be so hot that the sweat does not evaporate as quickly, which means that your body is not able to cool down as effectively. This is why you might feel especially hot and uncomfortable on a day when the temperature is high. So, even though the ambient temperature on a hot day might be close to your body temperature, it can still feel much hotter because your body is working harder to maintain its normal temperature.
