Fast charging in mobile phones is a feature that allows you to charge your phone's battery more quickly than with a normal charger. It works by using a higher voltage and/or more current to charge the battery, which means that more energy can be transferred to the battery in a shorter amount of time.\n\nThere are a few different ways that fast charging can be implemented in a mobile phone. One way is to have a special fast charger that uses a higher voltage and/or more current than a normal charger. This fast charger can then be used to charge the phone's battery more quickly.\n\nAnother way to implement fast charging is to have a fast charging feature built into the phone itself. This can be done by using a special chip or other hardware that is designed to accept a higher voltage and/or more current from the charger. This allows the phone to charge more quickly, even if you are using a normal charger.\n\nIn short, fast charging is a feature that can be implemented either in the charger or in the phone, and it allows you to charge your phone's battery more quickly than with a normal charger.
