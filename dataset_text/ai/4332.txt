When you throw your plastic bottle in the recycling bin, it is collected by a truck and taken to a recycling facility. At the recycling facility, the plastic bottles are sorted by type and cleaned. Then, they are chopped into small pieces called "plastic flakes."\n\nThe plastic flakes are melted and formed into small pellets. These pellets are then used to make new plastic products, such as bottles, bags, and other household items.\n\nThe whole process from collecting the plastic bottles to making new plastic products can take a few weeks to a few months, depending on the type of plastic and the equipment being used. It's important to recycle plastic bottles because it helps to reduce the amount of waste in the environment and saves resources, such as oil, that are used to make new plastic.
