Mitosis is the process of cell division that results in two genetically identical daughter cells. It is divided into four phases: prophase, metaphase, anaphase, and telophase. During prophase, chromosomes condense and become visible. In metaphase, chromosomes align at the cell's equator. Anaphase sees sister chromatids pulled to opposite poles, and in telophase, two new nuclei form. Mitosis is essential for growth, repair, and asexual reproduction.
