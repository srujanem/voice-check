People from ancient cultures are often depicted wearing light summer clothes and sandals because that is what they wore in the warmer months of the year. Just like how we wear different clothes depending on the weather, people in ancient times also wore clothing that was appropriate for the climate and season.\n\nFor example, in the hotter parts of the year, people might wear lightweight clothing made of natural materials like cotton or linen to help keep them cool. They might also wear sandals or open-toed shoes to allow air to circulate around their feet and help keep them comfortable.\n\nIn the colder months of the year, people might wear heavier clothing made of materials like wool to help keep them warm. They might also wear boots or closed-toe shoes to protect their feet from the cold.\n\nIn the northernmost parts of the Roman Empire, it was probably colder than in the parts of the empire that were closer to the Mediterranean Sea. So, people in those parts of the Roman Empire might have worn heavier clothing and closed-toe shoes during the colder months to stay warm.
