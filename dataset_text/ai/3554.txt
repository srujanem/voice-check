Here's a simple explanation of how radio waves can be used to transmit music: Radio waves are a type of electromagnetic radiation, which means they are a type of energy that travels through the air at the speed of light. Radio waves can be used to transmit all kinds of information, including music. To transmit music using radio waves, the music is first recorded onto a device, like a CD or a computer. The recording is then converted into a series of electronic signals, which can be transmitted over the air using a radio transmitter. The radio transmitter sends the electronic signals through the air as radio waves. The radio waves travel through the air until they reach a radio receiver, which can be a radio, a car stereo, or a device like a smartphone or a tablet. The radio receiver has an antenna that captures the radio waves and converts them back into electronic signals. The electronic signals are then sent to a device called a speaker, which converts the signals back into sound waves that we can hear. And that's how radio waves can be used to transmit music!
