Neoliberalism is a political and economic ideology that believes in free markets, deregulation of industries, and reduced government intervention in the economy. It emphasizes the use of private enterprise and individual responsibility to drive economic growth and prosperity. In practical terms, this often means lower taxes and fewer regulations on businesses, as well as privatization of public services such as education and healthcare. Proponents of neoliberalism argue that these policies lead to increased efficiency and innovation, while critics argue that they can lead to inequality and harm to the environment. Overall, neoliberalism is a way of thinking about how to organize an economy and society that values individual freedom and free markets over the role of the state in guiding and regulating economic activity.
