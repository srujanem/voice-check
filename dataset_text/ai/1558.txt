In the United States, when you own property, such as a piece of land or a house, you are required to pay property tax to the government. Property tax is a tax that is based on the value of the property that you own. The government uses the money from property tax to pay for things like schools, roads, and other public services. 
Even though you own the land and the house that you built on it, the government still has the right to collect property tax because they are responsible for providing the public services that benefit the community. For example, if you live in a city or town with good schools, clean streets, and other amenities, it is likely that these things were paid for, at least in part, by property tax. 
Think of property tax as a way to contribute to the community and help pay for the things that make it a good place to live. It is similar to how you might pay for a membership at a gym or a subscription to a magazine - you are paying for a service that you are using and that benefits you.
