A "deep cut" is a song that is not one of the more popular or well-known songs by a particular artist or band. It is usually found on an album, rather than being a single that is released to the radio. The phrase "deep cut" comes from the idea of digging deep into an album to find a song that may not be as well-known or widely heard as other songs. People might describe a song as a "deep cut" if they think it is particularly good, or if they want to share a song with others that they think is not as well-known as it should be.
