Accents are the way that people speak in a particular region or country. Accents are formed because people learn to speak by listening to the people around them, and the way that they speak becomes part of their own way of speaking. This is why people from different places often have different accents. 
We can't just stop speaking in our accent when we want to because our accent is a part of who we are. It is something that we have learned and become accustomed to over time, and it is difficult to change. However, some people may be able to change their accent to some degree with practice and training. For example, actors may learn to speak with a different accent for a role, or someone who is bilingual may be able to switch between accents when speaking different languages.
