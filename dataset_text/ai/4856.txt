When you use your muscles, you are putting them through a type of stress or strain. This can be through activities like lifting weights, running, or even just using your muscles to perform everyday tasks like carrying groceries or typing on a computer. When your muscles are put under this type of stress, they actually break down and become damaged, at least on a very small scale.\n\nHowever, your body is very good at repairing and rebuilding itself, and it will work to repair the damaged muscle fibers. In the process of repairing the muscle fibers, the body actually makes the muscles stronger and more efficient. This is why you might feel sore after exercising, but over time, your muscles will get stronger and better able to handle the stress you put on them.\n\nSo, to summarize, when you use your muscles, they break down and become damaged. But your body works to repair the damage and in the process, makes the muscles stronger and more efficient. This is why muscles get better the more you use them.
