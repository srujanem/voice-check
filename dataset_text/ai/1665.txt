Cancer is a disease that occurs when cells in the body start growing out of control. Normally, our bodies are made up of many different types of cells, and they all have specific jobs to do. But when a group of cells starts growing and multiplying too quickly, it can form a tumor. Tumors can be cancerous or noncancerous. Cancerous tumors, also called malignant tumors, can spread to other parts of the body and cause serious problems. Noncancerous tumors, also called benign tumors, usually don't spread and aren't usually life-threatening. As for why we haven't cured cancer yet, it's because it's a very complex disease and there are many different types of cancer. Each type of cancer behaves differently and requires different treatments, so it's difficult to develop a one-size-fits-all cure. Scientists and doctors are constantly working on new treatments and ways to improve survival rates for people with cancer, but it's a challenging problem to solve. As for a person's chances of survival, it really depends on the type of cancer they have and how advanced it is. Some types of cancer can be cured if they are caught early, while others are more difficult to treat. In general, the earlier cancer is found and treated, the better the chances of survival. However, it's important to note that every person and every case is different, so it's hard to predict someone's chances of survival with any certainty.
