An offshore bank account is a bank account that is located in a country other than the one where the person who owns the account lives. Offshore bank accounts can be used for many legitimate purposes, such as investing money, managing financial assets, or saving for retirement. However, some people may use offshore bank accounts for illegal or unethical purposes. For example, they may use the account to hide money that they have obtained through illegal activities, such as drug trafficking or embezzlement. They may also use offshore accounts to avoid paying taxes on the money they have earned. It is important to note that it is not necessarily illegal to have an offshore bank account, but it is illegal to use an offshore account to engage in criminal activities or to intentionally avoid paying taxes.
