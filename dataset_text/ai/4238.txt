Powerline networking is a way to use your home's electrical wiring as a network. It allows you to connect devices to the internet or to each other by plugging them into outlets, rather than using cables or wireless signals.\n\nHere's how it works:\n\nYou plug a special adapter into an electrical outlet near your internet router. This adapter is connected to the router with a cable, and it sends data through your home's electrical wiring.\n\nYou plug a second adapter into an outlet in another room, where you want to connect a device like a computer or TV.\n\nThe two adapters communicate with each other using the electrical wiring in your home. They send data back and forth, just like how two computers can communicate over a network.\n\nThe device you plugged into the second adapter can now access the internet or connect to other devices on the network.\n\nPowerline networking is a convenient way to get internet or network access in rooms that are far from your router or don't have a good wireless signal. It's also a good option if you don't want to run cables through your walls or floors.
