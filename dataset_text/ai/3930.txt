During a woman's menstrual cycle, her body goes through a lot of hormonal changes. These hormonal changes can sometimes cause a woman to have intense food cravings, especially for sweet or salty foods. It's kind of like a "heads up" from your body that it needs more energy and nutrients to get through the next few days. Just like when you're hungry and need to eat, your body is telling you that it needs something to help it function properly. It's totally normal to have these cravings and it's important to listen to your body and give it what it needs.
