The process of domesticating wolves and turning them into the dogs we know today likely happened over a very long period of time, and it's not clear exactly what prompted early humans to begin this process. However, it's possible that early humans may have noticed that wolves were helpful to them in certain ways, such as helping to hunt for food or providing protection. They may have also simply been attracted to wolves because they found them to be interesting or friendly. 
Over time, early humans may have started to breed wolves that were particularly good at performing certain tasks, or that had desirable traits such as being friendly or obedient. They may have also provided food and shelter for the wolves in exchange for their help. Through this process, the wolves began to evolve and become more adapted to living with humans, eventually becoming the domesticated dogs we know today.
