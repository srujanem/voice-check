Bacon is a type of meat that is made from the fatty cuts of pork. It is typically cured with salt and then smoked, which gives it its distinct flavor and aroma. Many people enjoy eating bacon because it tastes delicious and adds a lot of flavor to dishes. There is no one specific reason for the obsession with bacon, but it could be because it is widely available and popular in many different countries and cultures. Additionally, bacon has become something of a cultural phenomenon and has been featured in many memes and jokes online. It has also been used as a flavor in a variety of products, such as bacon-flavored candy and even bacon-scented candles. Overall, bacon has become a widely recognized and beloved food item, which may contribute to its popularity and the obsession with it.
