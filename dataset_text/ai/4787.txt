A hard drive is a type of storage device that is used to store data on a computer. It stores things like files, documents, and pictures. A graphics card is a type of computer hardware that is used to process and display images on a computer. It is often used for tasks that require a lot of visual processing, like playing games or editing photos.\n\nThe main difference between the two is what they are used for. A hard drive is used for storing data, while a graphics card is used for processing and displaying images.\n\nThe price difference between the two is due to the different functions they serve and the technology used to create them. Hard drives are typically less expensive because they are simpler and do not require as much advanced technology as graphics cards. Graphics cards, on the other hand, are more expensive because they have more complex functions and require more advanced technology.
