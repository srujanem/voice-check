Liberal arts is a type of college degree that focuses on a broad range of subjects, rather than just one specific area of study. This can include subjects like literature, history, philosophy, math, and science. The idea behind a liberal arts education is to give students a well-rounded knowledge base and critical thinking skills that can be applied to any career. It's not accurate to say that people "fail at life" after getting a degree in liberal arts. While it's true that some people who study liberal arts may face challenges finding a job or building a career after graduation, this is not unique to liberal arts majors. In fact, many people who study liberal arts go on to have successful careers in a wide variety of fields, such as education, business, law, and more. It's important to keep in mind that no one type of college degree guarantees success in life. What matters most is not the specific degree you earn, but rather the effort you put into your studies, your work ethic, and your ability to adapt to changing circumstances. Whether you study liberal arts or any other field, if you work hard and are determined to succeed, you can achieve your goals.
