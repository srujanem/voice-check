An engine oil change is a straightforward maintenance task that extends the life of your vehicle. Begin by elevating the car safely on jack stands and positioning a drain pan beneath the oil pan plug. Unscrew the plug to let the dirty oil drain completely, then replace the washer and tighten the plug back into place. Next, unscrew the old oil filter, apply a thin coat of fresh oil to the new filter's rubber seal, and screw it on hand-tight. Refill the engine with the recommended oil viscosity and check the dipstick level.
