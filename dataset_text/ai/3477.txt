Dreams happen while we are sleeping and our brains are in a different state than when we are awake. While we are sleeping, our brains are less active and not processing information in the same way as when we are awake. That's why it can be harder to remember things that happen in our dreams. It's kind of like when you watch a movie or read a book - it can be hard to remember all the details later because your brain wasn't fully focused on it at the time. When we wake up, our brains switch back to the awake state and all the details from our dreams can get jumbled up or fade away. So, it's normal to forget most of our dreams. Does that make sense?
