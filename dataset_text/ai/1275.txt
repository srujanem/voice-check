The reason why windshield wipers don't wipe the whole windshield is because they are designed to follow the curve of the windshield. This is done so that the wipers can effectively remove rain and other debris from the surface of the windshield without leaving any streaks or smudges. 
The small triangle that is left unwiped is usually located at the very top of the windshield, near the driver's side. This area is often difficult for the wipers to reach because it is close to the edge of the windshield and the wiper blades may not be long enough to reach it. 
In some cases, it may be possible to adjust the wipers or replace them with longer blades to improve their coverage. However, it is important to keep in mind that the wipers are an important safety feature and making any changes to them could potentially affect their performance.
