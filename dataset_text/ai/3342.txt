Torque is a measure of how much force a car's engine can produce to turn the wheels. It's what makes a car able to accelerate and climb hills. Imagine you have a wrench and you're trying to loosen a bolt. If you push really hard on the handle of the wrench, you'll be able to turn the bolt more easily. That's because you're applying a lot of force, or torque, to the bolt. Now imagine you have a car with a really powerful engine. When you step on the gas pedal, the engine starts turning faster and producing more force, or torque. This torque is used to turn the wheels of the car, which makes the car go faster. So, in general, a car with a lot of torque will be able to accelerate quickly and climb hills more easily, while a car with less torque might struggle a bit in those situations.
