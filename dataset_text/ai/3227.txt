Antibiotic resistance is one of the most serious global public health threats. When bacteria are exposed to antibiotics, susceptible bacteria die while resistant ones survive and reproduce. The overuse and misuse of antibiotics in medicine and agriculture has accelerated this process. Resistant infections are more difficult and expensive to treat, and some pathogens are becoming resistant to all available antibiotics. Addressing this crisis requires strict prescribing practices, reduced agricultural antibiotic use, and investment in new drug development.
