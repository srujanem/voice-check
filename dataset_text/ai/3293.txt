Autism is a developmental disorder that affects how a person communicates with others and how they interact with the world around them. People with autism may have difficulty understanding and expressing their own emotions, and may have trouble communicating with others or understanding social norms. They may also have repetitive behaviors or interests, and may be sensitive to sensory input such as light, sound, or touch. Asperger syndrome is a type of autism that is often referred to as "high-functioning" autism. People with Asperger syndrome may have similar social and communication difficulties as those with other types of autism, but they may have fewer delays in language development and cognitive abilities. It is not known exactly what causes autism, but it is believed to be a combination of genetic and environmental factors. Some studies have suggested that certain medical conditions or exposures during pregnancy may increase the risk of a child developing autism, but more research is needed to understand the causes of this disorder. It's important to remember that every person with autism is unique, and the symptoms and severity of the disorder can vary widely from person to person.
