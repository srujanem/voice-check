All of the stars and planets in our solar system appear white to us when we see them from Earth because they reflect sunlight. When sunlight hits an object, it can bounce off of it in all different directions. If an object is white, it reflects all of the colors of the spectrum equally, so when the sunlight reflects off of it, it looks white to us. Think about it like this: if you have a white piece of paper and you shine a flashlight on it, the light will bounce off of the paper in all directions. The paper will look white because it is reflecting all of the colors of the flashlight's light back to your eyes. The same thing happens with the stars and planets.
