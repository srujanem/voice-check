It is not possible for every single person in the United States to be financially stable and lead prosperous lives at the same time. This is because there are limited resources, such as money and job opportunities, available in the economy. 
Imagine that there is a pie, and each slice of the pie represents a certain amount of resources. If everyone in the United States were to work extremely hard and try to get a slice of the pie, there would not be enough slices to go around for everyone to have an equal amount. Some people would have more slices and be more financially stable, while others would have less and struggle to make ends meet. 
However, it is possible for a large number of people to be financially stable and lead prosperous lives if the economy is strong and there are enough resources to go around. This can happen when there are plenty of good jobs available and people are able to earn enough money to meet their basic needs and save for the future. So, it is important for the government and businesses to work together to create a healthy and thriving economy.
