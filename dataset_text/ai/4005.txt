Sure! When you heat up iron-based metals, the molecules inside start to move around more and the metal becomes softer. This is because the heat is giving the molecules more energy, so they can move around more easily. When you then quickly cool the metal, it can't move around as much anymore because the molecules don't have as much energy. This makes the metal harder. It's kind of like when you're running around outside on a hot day and you're all sweaty and can't stop moving. But if you suddenly jump into a pool of cold water, you'll feel a lot more stiff and not want to move around as much because the cold water takes away some of your energy. The same thing happens to the molecules inside the metal when it's cooled quickly. They don't have as much energy to move around, so the metal becomes harder.
