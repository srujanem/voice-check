r/circlejerk is a subreddit, or a forum, on the website Reddit where users can post and discuss content that is meant to be humorous or satirical. The term "circlejerk" refers to a situation where a group of people engages in self-congratulatory or self-referential behavior, often to the exclusion of outsiders or those who disagree with them. In the context of r/circlejerk, the term is often used to describe posts or discussions that are overly self-indulgent or insular, or that rely on inside jokes or memes that might not be understood by someone who is not familiar with the subreddit.
