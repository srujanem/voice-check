The European settlers didn't die off from native diseases for a few reasons. First, many of the European settlers had already been exposed to similar diseases in Europe, so their bodies were somewhat prepared to fight off the diseases that they encountered in the Americas. Additionally, the European settlers often brought their own diseases with them, such as smallpox, which devastated Native American populations who had never been exposed to these diseases before and had no immunity to them. Finally, the European settlers had access to better medical care and treatments than the Native Americans, which helped them survive illnesses that might have otherwise been fatal.
