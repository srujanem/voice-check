When you look directly at the sun, it can be very bright and can cause your eyes to water. When your eyes water, they produce more mucus, which can irritate the lining of your nose. This irritation can cause your nose to tickle and make you want to sneeze. Sneezing is a way for your body to try to get rid of the irritant and clear your nose. So, in short, you sneeze when you look at the sun because it can cause your eyes to water and your nose to tickle, which can lead to a sneeze.
