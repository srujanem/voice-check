When we talk about the size of an engine in terms of liters, we are referring to the volume of the cylinders inside the engine. Cylinders are the tubes inside the engine where the fuel is burned to create power. The more cylinders an engine has, or the larger the cylinders are, the more fuel it can burn and the more power it can produce. For example, a 3.7L engine has 3.7 liters of volume inside its cylinders, while a 5.0L engine has 5.0 liters of volume inside its cylinders. This means that the 5.0L engine can burn more fuel and produce more power than the 3.7L engine. However, it's important to note that there are many other factors that can affect the performance of an engine, such as the type of fuel it uses and the design of the engine.
