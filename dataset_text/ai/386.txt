When you're gaming, you don't look at your keyboard because you have practiced using the keyboard and have learned where the keys are located through muscle memory. This means that your fingers know where to go without you having to look at the keyboard. When the lights are off or it's dark, it can be more difficult to see the keys, so your fingers might have a harder time finding the right keys without looking. It's a bit like trying to walk through a familiar room in the dark - you might bump into things or have a harder time navigating because you can't see where you're going.
