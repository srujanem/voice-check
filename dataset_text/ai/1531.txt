Sneezing is a reflex that helps to clear the nose of irritants such as dust, dirt, or pollen. When the nose is exposed to one of these irritants, it sends a signal to the brain, which then triggers the sneezing reflex.

For example, some people might have a stronger sneezing reflex than others, which could cause them to sneeze more times in a row. Others might have a more sensitive nose, which could cause them to sneeze more often in general.
