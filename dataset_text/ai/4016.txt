Babies cry because it's their way of communicating that they need something, like food, a diaper change, or to be held. Sometimes babies cry because they're in pain or feeling discomfort, or because they're just really tired and need to sleep. Babies also cry when they're feeling overwhelmed or upset, or when they're trying to get your attention. Babies and toddlers make noise when they're upset because it's their way of telling you that something is wrong and they need your help. They might cry, wail, or scream because they're feeling really strong emotions, like anger, frustration, or sadness. Babies and toddlers will stop making so much noise when they feel better and their needs are met. As they get older, they'll learn how to use their words to communicate what they need, and they'll cry less. But it's important to remember that crying is a normal and healthy way for babies and toddlers to express their feelings, and it's okay to let them cry sometimes if it's not hurting them.
