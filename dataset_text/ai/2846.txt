Hybrid cars are better for the environment because they use less fuel than traditional gasoline-only cars. This is because they have two sources of power: an electric motor and a gasoline engine. The electric motor is powered by a battery that can be recharged by plugging the car into an electrical outlet. When the car is running on electricity, it produces zero emissions, which means it doesn't release any harmful gases into the air. This makes hybrid cars much cleaner to drive than gasoline-only cars. Even when the hybrid car is using its gasoline engine, it is more efficient than a traditional gasoline-only car because the electric motor helps the gasoline engine work more efficiently. This means the hybrid car uses less fuel and produces fewer emissions overall. So, while hybrid cars do still use some gasoline, they use less of it than traditional gasoline-only cars, which is better for the environment.
