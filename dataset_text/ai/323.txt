Color blindness is caused by a genetic variation that affects the way the brain processes certain colors. It is more common in males than females because the genetic variation responsible for color blindness is located on the X chromosome, which males only have one of (females have two X chromosomes). Because males only have one X chromosome, they are more likely to be affected by color blindness if they inherit the genetic variation from their mother. 
So, to explain it like you're five: Color blindness is a special way that some people's brains see colors that is different from most people. Boys are more likely to be colorblind than girls because it is something that is passed down from their mother.
