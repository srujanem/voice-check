Oil is a natural substance that is found in the ground, but it can cause pollution when it is not handled or used properly. When oil is extracted from the ground and transported to be refined or used, it can sometimes spill or leak from pipelines, tanks, or vessels. These spills can be caused by accidents or equipment failure, and they can have serious environmental consequences. Oil spills can pollute the air, land, and water. They can kill plants and animals and harm the habitats that they live in. Oil spills can also be harmful to people, especially if they come into contact with the spilled oil or breathe in the fumes that it gives off. To prevent oil from polluting the environment, it is important to handle and use it safely and responsibly. This includes proper maintenance of equipment, careful transportation, and emergency planning to respond to spills quickly and effectively.
