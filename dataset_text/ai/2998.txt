Noam Chomsky is a linguist, philosopher, and political activist. He is known for his work on the nature of language and how it is used and acquired by humans. Chomsky has also been involved in activism, and has written and spoken about political issues such as democracy, freedom of speech, and human rights. In simple terms, Chomsky is a person who studies language and how it works. He is also someone who cares about social and political issues and works to make the world a better place.
