The internet is a vast network of interconnected computers and servers that allows people to communicate and access information from all over the world. It is made up of many interconnected networks that use a common set of communication protocols, or rules, to exchange data. 
Websites are hosted on servers, which are computers that store and provide access to the content of a website. When you visit a website, your computer connects to the server where the website is hosted and retrieves the information that you requested. 
Different browsers are software programs that allow you to access and view websites on the internet. They all use the same internet, but they might display the information in different ways or have different features. 
Internet providers are companies that offer internet access to customers. They provide the connection to the internet through various technologies, such as cable, fiber, or satellite. Different internet providers may offer different levels of service or different prices, but they all provide access to the same internet. 
The internet is not owned by any one person or organization. It is a global network that is maintained by many different individuals and organizations working together. There is no specific location for the internet, as it is made up of many interconnected networks that span the entire globe.
