The "night lights" map of the United States shows how bright different parts of the country are at night. The map is divided in the middle because it is showing the United States from coast to coast, and the country is very big. The west side of the map is generally darker than the east side because there are fewer people and fewer cities on the west coast. In general, cities are brighter at night because they have more streetlights, buildings, and other sources of light. So the map shows that the east coast, which has more cities, is brighter at night than the west coast.
