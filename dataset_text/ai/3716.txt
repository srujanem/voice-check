In a passive PA system, the speakers and amplifiers are separate components that are connected by wires. The amplifier boosts the audio signal coming from the mixer or audio source, and sends it to the speakers, which then produce sound waves that we can hear. In an active PA system, the speakers and amplifiers are combined into one unit. This means that the amplifiers are built into the speakers, and there is no need for separate amplifiers. The audio signal from the mixer or audio source is sent directly to the active speakers, which amplify the signal and produce sound waves. In general, passive PA systems are more complex and require more setup, but they offer more flexibility and control over the sound. Active PA systems are simpler and more convenient, but they may not offer as much control over the sound.
