In accounting, a bank statement is a record of all the transactions that have occurred in a bank account during a specific period of time, usually a month. When you receive a salary, it is an inflow of money into your bank account. In accounting, an inflow of money is recorded as a credit. This means that when your salary is credited to your bank account, it is recorded as a credit in your bank statement.\n\nOn the other hand, when you spend money, it is an outflow of money from your bank account. In accounting, an outflow of money is recorded as a debit. This means that when you make a purchase or pay a bill using your bank account, it is recorded as a debit in your bank statement.\n\nTo sum it up, a credit in a bank statement represents an inflow of money, while a debit represents an outflow of money.\n
