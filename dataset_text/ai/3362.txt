Submarines do have cameras mounted on the outside to aid in the use of sonar! These cameras are called periscopes, and they help the submarine's crew see what is happening above the water. Sonar works by sending out sound waves and listening for the echoes that bounce back when they hit something. By measuring the time it takes for the echoes to return, the submarine can figure out how far away things are and what they're made of. However, sonar is not very good at seeing things in fine detail, so the periscopes are used to give the crew a better look at what's happening outside the submarine. The periscopes have special lenses and mirrors that allow the crew to see a wide area around the submarine, even if the water is very deep. So, while sonar is very important for helping submarines navigate and find things in the water, the periscopes are also very useful for giving the crew a clearer picture of what's going on outside the submarine.
