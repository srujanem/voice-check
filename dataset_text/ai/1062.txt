When you adjust the volume on your TV, you are actually adjusting the strength of the sound coming out of the speakers. The volume control on your TV allows you to increase or decrease the amount of electricity that is sent to the speakers, which in turn makes the sound louder or softer. 
When you press the "up" button on your TV's remote control or on the TV itself, you are telling the TV to increase the amount of electricity being sent to the speakers. This makes the sound louder. Similarly, when you press the "down" button, you are telling the TV to decrease the amount of electricity being sent to the speakers, which makes the sound softer. 
The TV has a range of volume levels that it can produce, and it knows what level to increase or decrease based on the signal it receives from the remote control or the buttons on the TV. It can increase or decrease the volume in small increments, so you can fine-tune the volume to your liking.
