Headaches can be caused by a variety of things, including tension in the muscles of the head and neck, sinus issues, eyestrain, and even changes in the weather. To avoid headaches, it's important to get enough sleep, stay hydrated, and try to reduce stress. It can also help to take regular breaks if you spend a lot of time working on a computer or doing other activities that involve focusing on a screen. Some people find that eating a healthy diet, exercising regularly, and getting enough rest can also help prevent headaches. If you do get a headache, it can sometimes help to take a pain reliever like acetaminophen or ibuprofen, rest in a quiet place, and apply a cold pack or warm cloth to your forehead. It's also a good idea to try to figure out what might be causing your headache and try to avoid those triggers in the future.
