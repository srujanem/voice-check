If the backboard is shattered during a basketball game, the game would be stopped immediately. The players and officials would leave the court, and the broken backboard would be replaced with a new one. This process can take some time, so the game may be postponed or temporarily paused until the new backboard is in place. 
A backboard is an important part of a basketball court because it is the surface that the basket is attached to. It is made of a strong, transparent material like glass or acrylic, and it is mounted on a metal or wooden frame. The backboard is used to reflect the ball back into play, and it also helps to protect the basket and keep it in place. If the backboard is damaged or broken, it can be dangerous for the players and officials, so the game must be stopped until the problem can be fixed.
