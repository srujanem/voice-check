Music leaks happen when someone gets ahold of a copy of a music album before it is officially released and shares it with others. This can happen in a few different ways. One way is if someone working on the album, such as a record label employee or a music producer, shares it with someone else. Another way is if the album is being stored digitally, such as on a computer or online, and someone is able to hack into the system and obtain a copy. Leaks can also occur if physical copies of the album, such as CDs or vinyl records, are stolen or lost and then found by someone else who shares the music. It is important to remember that it is illegal to share music before it is officially released, as it can hurt the sales and profits of the artist and their record label.
