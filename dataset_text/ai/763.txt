The Mars One mission was a proposed plan to send humans to live on Mars permanently. The idea was to send a group of people to the planet and establish a permanent human settlement there. The mission was planned to be funded through a combination of private investment and media rights.

Some experts also raised concerns about the psychological and physical effects of living on Mars permanently, and whether it would be safe for humans to do so.
