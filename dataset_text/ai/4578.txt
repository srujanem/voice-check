In the wild, pandas have to constantly search for new sources of bamboo to eat, which can be hard work.\n\nIn zoos and other places like that, pandas are given all the bamboo they need to eat, so they don't have to worry about finding it themselves. This makes it easier for them to survive and stay healthy.
