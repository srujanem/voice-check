YouTube "partnership" is a program that allows YouTube creators to earn money from their videos through advertising. Here's how it works: YouTube creators make videos and upload them to the platform. YouTube places ads on the creators' videos. When people watch the videos, they see the ads and YouTube earns money from the advertisers. YouTube gives a portion of the money they earn from the ads to the creators, as a way of thanking them for making and sharing the videos. So, YouTube could advertise without paying creators anything, but they choose to do it this way because it helps them get more content for the platform. When creators know they can earn money from their videos, they are more likely to make and share more of them, which keeps people coming back to YouTube to watch more videos. It's a way for YouTube to incentivize creators to keep making content and for advertisers to reach a wider audience through the platform.
