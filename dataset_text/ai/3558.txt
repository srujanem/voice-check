It's illegal to switch lanes in an intersection because it can be very dangerous. Imagine you are driving through an intersection and you see a car in the lane next to you suddenly move over into your lane. You might not have time to react and avoid a collision, especially if you are turning or stopped at a red light. Switching lanes in an intersection can also cause confusion for other drivers. They might not know which lane you are in, or they might not expect you to be there. This can lead to accidents as well. It's important to follow the rules of the road and stay in your lane while driving through an intersection. This helps to keep everyone safe and prevents accidents from happening.
