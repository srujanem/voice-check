The sea is made out of salt water because it contains a high amount of dissolved salts, like sodium and chloride. These salts come from rocks on land that have been worn down over time and carried into the ocean by rivers. When the water from rivers flows into the ocean, it mixes with the saltwater of the sea, but it doesn't change the overall salt content of the ocean very much because there is so much more water in the ocean compared to the amount of water that flows into it from rivers. So the sea stays salty even though it is constantly being replenished with fresh water from rivers.
