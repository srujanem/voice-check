There are a few reasons why your laptop might be running slower now than when it was brand new: Over time, you might have installed new software, saved new files, and made other changes to your laptop, which can take up space and slow down your system. Your laptop's hardware (such as the processor and memory) may have become outdated, which can also contribute to slower performance. Your laptop's hard drive may be almost full, which can also slow down the system. There may be software or system errors that are causing your laptop to run slower. Finally, it's possible that your laptop's battery may not be holding a charge as well as it used to, which can cause the laptop to slow down. Reformatting your laptop can help speed it up in some cases, but it may not solve all of these issues. If your laptop is still running slowly after a reformat, you may need to consider upgrading your hardware or troubleshooting other issues.
