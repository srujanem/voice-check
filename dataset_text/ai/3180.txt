The world's population has increased a lot over the past 100 years because people are living longer and having more children. In the past, many people died at a young age from diseases and other causes. But with advances in medicine and healthcare, more people are surviving to old age and having children of their own. Another reason for the population increase is that there have been improvements in agriculture, which has allowed people to grow more food and feed more people. This has helped to reduce hunger and malnutrition, which can lead to more people surviving and having children. Finally, the population has also increased because of immigration, which is when people move from one place to another. This can happen for many reasons, such as people moving to a new country to find work or to escape war or persecution. When people move to a new place, they can have children and contribute to the population growth.
