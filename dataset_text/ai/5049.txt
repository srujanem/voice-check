From a scientific perspective, Ecology is the scientific study of the interactions among organisms and their biophysical environment. An ecosystem consists of all the living organisms (biotic factors) in a given area interacting with the non-living physical environment (abiotic factors). Energy flows through ecosystems unilaterally, entering primarily as sunlight and dissipating as heat, while nutrients are continuously cycled. Population dynamics, community interactions such as predation and symbiosis, and the impacts of human activities on biodiversity are central themes in ecological research. This topic remains a vibrant area of study with significant implications for medicine and biotechnology.
