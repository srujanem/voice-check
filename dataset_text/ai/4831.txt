Dinosaurs are a group of reptiles that lived millions of years ago and are now extinct. There are many things that make dinosaurs different from other reptiles, such as their size, shape, and the way they moved.\n\nOne of the things that makes dinosaurs different is their size. Many dinosaurs were very large, much larger than most other reptiles that have ever lived. For example, the T. rex was one of the largest dinosaurs and could be as long as 40 feet from head to tail!\n\nAnother thing that makes dinosaurs different is the way they moved. Most dinosaurs walked on two legs, while other reptiles, like lizards and snakes, usually crawl on four legs or slither on their bellies.\n\nFinally, dinosaurs are different from other reptiles because they have a certain group of bones in their skeleton that are not found in other reptiles. These bones help scientists tell if a fossil is a dinosaur or not.\n\nKomodo dragons, armadillos, and iguanas are not considered dinosaurs because they are not closely related to dinosaurs. They are reptiles, but they are not part of the same group as dinosaurs. These animals are still alive today, while dinosaurs are extinct.
