If air is injected into your bloodstream, it can be very dangerous and potentially life-threatening. This is because air bubbles in your bloodstream can cause blockages in your blood vessels, which can prevent oxygen and nutrients from reaching your organs and tissues. This can cause serious problems, such as a heart attack or stroke, and it can even be fatal. It's important to only receive medications and fluids through properly sterile and sealed equipment to avoid this type of accidental injection.
