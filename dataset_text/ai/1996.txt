A large gap between rich and poor can be bad for a country's prosperity because it can lead to social and economic problems. When there is a large gap between the rich and the poor, it can be harder for people who are poor to get the resources and opportunities they need to improve their circumstances. This can create a cycle of poverty that is difficult to break. On the other hand, when there is a smaller gap between the rich and the poor, it can be easier for everyone to access the resources and opportunities they need to succeed. This can create a more stable and prosperous society, where everyone has a better chance to reach their full potential. In addition, a large gap between the rich and the poor can also lead to social unrest and conflict, as people may feel that they are not being treated fairly and that the system is stacked against them. This can make it harder for a country to achieve peace and harmony, and can lead to problems such as crime and violence. Overall, it is important for a country to have a more balanced distribution of wealth and resources, so that everyone has a fair chance to succeed and contribute to the prosperity of the nation.
