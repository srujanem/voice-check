Movies are rated by organizations like the Motion Picture Association of America (MPAA) or the British Board of Film Classification (BBFC). These organizations watch the movie and decide what age group it is suitable for based on the content of the movie.\n\nSometimes, a movie may not have been finished being made yet, so it hasn't been shown to the organizations that rate movies. That's why it might say "not yet rated."\n\nIt can also take a while for a movie to be rated because the organization has to watch the entire movie, which can take some time. They also have to discuss the movie with other people and make a decision together about what rating to give it.\n\nSo, "not yet rated" just means that the movie hasn't been rated by the organization yet, but it will be rated before it is released in theaters or made available for people to watch at home.
