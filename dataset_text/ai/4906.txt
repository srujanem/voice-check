Movie theaters offer discounted tickets for seniors and children because they generally have less disposable income than adults. This means that they may not be able to afford to pay the full price for a movie ticket, so the theater offers a discount to make it more affordable for them. It's also a way for the theater to attract more business from these groups of people. Even though seniors and children take up the same amount of space as adults, the theater is still making money from their ticket sales, just at a lower price.
