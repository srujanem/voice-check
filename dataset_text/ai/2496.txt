An itch is a feeling that makes you want to scratch your skin. It happens when something touches your skin and sends a message to your brain. Your brain then sends a message back to your skin to scratch. There are many different things that can cause an itch. For example, if you have dry skin, it can itch. If you have an insect bite or an allergic reaction to something, it can also make your skin itch. Sometimes, even just wearing certain clothes or having certain fabrics touch your skin can cause an itch. It's important to try to figure out what is causing your itch so you can try to stop it. For example, if your skin is dry, you can try using a lotion to moisturize it. If you have an insect bite, you can try using a cream to help reduce the itch. Scratching an itch can sometimes make it feel better temporarily, but it's important to try not to scratch too much because it can damage your skin.
