The endocrine system regulates bodily functions through hormones secreted by glands directly into the bloodstream. Key glands include the pituitary, thyroid, adrenal glands, and pancreas. The pituitary gland, often called the master gland, controls other endocrine glands. Insulin, produced by the pancreas, regulates blood glucose levels. Hormonal imbalances can lead to disorders such as diabetes, thyroid disease, and growth abnormalities.
