Exclusives can be financially beneficial for game developers because they may receive financial incentives from the company whose platform they are releasing the game on exclusively. For example, if a game developer releases a game exclusively on the PlayStation, they may receive a larger advance or a higher percentage of the sales from Sony than they would if they released the game on multiple platforms. This is because exclusives can be used as a selling point for the platform, and the platform owner (in this case, Sony) wants to make sure the game is only available on their platform to encourage people to buy their platform over others. On the other hand, releasing a game on multiple platforms allows the game developer to reach a larger audience, which can potentially lead to more sales. However, the game developer may not receive as much financial support from the platform owners and may have to split the profits from sales with each platform. Ultimately, it's up to the game developer to weigh the pros and cons of each approach and decide which option is the most financially beneficial for them.
