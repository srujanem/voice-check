When a police officer wants to pull over more than one car at once, they will usually use a technique called a "felony stop." This is a type of high-risk traffic stop that is used when the police suspect that the driver of the vehicle may be dangerous or armed. To perform a felony stop, the police officer will turn on their flashing lights and sirens, and then quickly approach the vehicle from behind. They will also position their own vehicle in a way that blocks the road, making it difficult for the other vehicle to continue driving. If the driver of the other vehicle does not immediately pull over, the police officer may use additional tactics to try to get them to stop, such as flashing their lights or sounding their siren more aggressively. They may also use hand signals or shout commands through a loudspeaker to try to get the driver's attention. It's important to remember that if you see a police officer trying to pull you over, you should pull over as soon as it is safe to do so. If you are unsure about what to do, it is usually best to just follow the officer's instructions.
