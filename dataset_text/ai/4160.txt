Contact lenses are thin, clear disks that you put on your eyes. They help you see better by bending light in a way that helps your eyes focus on things more clearly.\n\nThere are different types of contact lenses, and they last for different amounts of time. Monthly contact lenses are meant to be worn for about a month, and then you throw them away and get a new pair. Bi-weekly contact lenses are meant to be worn for about two weeks, and then you throw them away and get a new pair. Daily contact lenses are meant to be worn for one day, and then you throw them away and get a new pair the next day.\n\nToric contact lenses are a special type of contact lens that are shaped differently than regular contact lenses. They are made to correct vision problems that are caused by a condition called astigmatism. Astigmatism is when your eye is shaped more like a football than a sphere, which can make it hard for you to see clearly. Toric contact lenses are designed to correct this problem by bending light in a special way that helps your eye focus better.\n\nI hope that helps! Let me know if you have any other questions.
