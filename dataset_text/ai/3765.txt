Corking a baseball bat involves drilling a hole into the barrel of the bat and filling it with cork, or another lighter material. The idea behind corking a bat is that it makes the bat lighter, which in theory should allow the player to swing the bat faster and generate more power. However, corking a bat is illegal in professional baseball because it can give players an unfair advantage. In addition, corking a bat can also weaken the structure of the bat and make it more prone to breaking. It's important to note that there is no scientific evidence to support the claim that corking a bat actually improves a player's performance. In fact, many players and coaches believe that corking a bat can actually have a negative effect on a player's performance because it changes the balance and feel of the bat.
