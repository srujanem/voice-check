Anonymous is a loosely organized international group of hackers who are known for participating in high-profile cyber attacks against governments, corporations, and other organizations. They often use the internet to publicize their actions and to promote their causes, which can include things like free speech, government transparency, and social justice. Some examples of things that Anonymous has done include: Launching cyber attacks against websites belonging to governments, organizations, and individuals that the group disagrees with or perceives as being corrupt. These attacks, known as "denial of service" (DoS) attacks, involve overwhelming a website with traffic in order to make it unavailable to users. Exposing sensitive information, such as government documents or private corporate data, that the group believes should be made public in the interest of transparency or accountability. Participating in protests and other forms of activism, both online and in person, in support of various social and political causes. It's important to note that Anonymous is not a single entity, but rather a loosely organized group of individuals who may or may not be affiliated with one another. As such, it can be difficult to accurately assess the group's overall impact or effectiveness.
