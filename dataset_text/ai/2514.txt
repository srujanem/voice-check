Reality shows blur out brand name images on film for a couple of reasons. One reason is to avoid any potential copyright issues. Another reason is that reality shows often get paid by companies to feature their products or brands on the show. If a brand is shown without permission, the producers of the show might not get paid for it. So, by blurring out the brand names, the producers can still show the products without breaking any rules or laws.
