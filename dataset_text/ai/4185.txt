Bruxism is the medical term for grinding your teeth. It can happen when you're awake or asleep, and it's usually a habit that people do without realizing it. It's more common in adults, but it can also happen to children.\n\nThere are a few reasons why people might grind their teeth. Stress, anxiety, and tension can all cause people to grind their teeth, especially at night while they're sleeping. Some people grind their teeth because they have a misalignment in their jaw, or because they have a sleep disorder like sleep apnea.\n\nIf your friend is grinding their teeth at night, it might be a good idea for them to see a dentist or a doctor. They can help figure out what's causing the grinding and come up with a plan to stop it. In the meantime, you can try wearing earplugs or using a white noise machine to block out the sound of the grinding.
