A router firewall is a security system that is built into a router and is designed to protect the network that the router is connected to. It works by blocking certain types of traffic that are known to be malicious or potentially harmful.\n\nA personal firewall is a security system that is installed on a single computer and is designed to protect that specific computer from malicious or potentially harmful traffic.\n\nThe two types of firewalls can interact with each other, depending on how they are configured. For example, if a rule is set up on a personal firewall to block certain types of traffic, and the same type of traffic is also blocked by the router firewall, then the traffic will be blocked by both firewalls.\n\nWhen you are connected to a public network, such as a public Wi-Fi hotspot, your personal firewall can still help to protect your computer by blocking certain types of traffic that are known to be malicious. However, you may also want to be careful about the types of websites you visit and the information you share while connected to a public network, as the security of these networks can vary.
