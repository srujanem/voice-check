Ambulances are typically owned and operated by hospitals, as you suspected. However, sometimes hospitals contract with private ambulance companies to provide additional ambulance services. These private companies may operate under their own brand name, which is why you might see ambulances with different company names on them. 
It's important for hospitals to have enough ambulances to meet the needs of the community, and contracting with private companies can be a way to make sure there are enough ambulances available. Additionally, private ambulance companies may be able to offer specialized services or equipment that hospitals don't have in-house. 
Overall, the main goal of ambulances is to provide quick and effective medical care to people who are sick or injured and need to be transported to a hospital or other medical facility for treatment. Whether an ambulance is owned by a hospital or a private company, the important thing is that it is staffed with trained medical professionals who can provide the necessary care.
