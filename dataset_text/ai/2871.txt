Computers generate heat because they have a lot of small parts inside them that are constantly working and moving. These parts, like the processor and the graphics card, use electricity to function, and as a byproduct of this process, they give off heat. The more work a computer does, the more heat it generates. Cooling systems, like fans or heat sinks, are used to keep a computer from getting too hot. Some computers, like smartphones, are designed to be small and portable, so they don't have room for large cooling systems. As a result, they may not generate as much heat, or they may have special materials and designs that help them dissipate heat more efficiently. In general, the materials used in computers are not naturally warm. It's the process of using electricity to power the computer's parts that causes them to heat up. That's why it's important to use a cooling system to keep a computer from getting too hot, especially if it's being used for tasks that require a lot of processing power.
