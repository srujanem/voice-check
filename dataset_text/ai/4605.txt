Babies cry and make a fuss before they go to sleep because they are communicating their needs and feelings to their caregivers. Babies have a lot of different needs, and they rely on adults to help them with things like getting fed, getting their diapers changed, and feeling safe and comfortable.\n\nSometimes, babies cry because they are hungry or in need of a diaper change. Other times, they might cry because they are tired or overwhelmed and need a break from stimulation. Some babies might cry because they are feeling anxious or scared and need some extra comfort.\n\nIt's important to try to figure out what your baby is trying to tell you when they cry, so you can help them feel better and get the things they need. With time and patience, you'll learn to understand your baby's cries and be able to comfort them when they are upset.
