The human immune system is a complex defense network composed of cells, tissues, and organs working in harmony to protect the body against pathogens. The innate immune system acts as an immediate, general barrier, dispatching white blood cells to neutralize invading bacteria or viruses. Meanwhile, the adaptive immune system creates specialized antibodies tailored to specific antigens, establishing a biological memory that provides long-term protection against future infections. Maintaining a balanced diet, regular physical exercise, and adequate sleep provides essential support to keep this defensive system functioning efficiently.
