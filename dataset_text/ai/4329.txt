An address is usually assigned to a property based on the street that the property faces. For example, if a property's main entrance is on one street, then the address will be assigned to that street. If the property faces more than one street and there is no clear main entrance, then the address may be assigned to the street that is most commonly used to access the property. It's also possible that the address may be assigned to the street that the property is most closely associated with, or the one that is most visually prominent from the property. In any case, the street that an address is assigned to is typically determined by the local government or postal service, and it can sometimes be changed if there is confusion or if the property is divided into multiple parcels with different addresses.
