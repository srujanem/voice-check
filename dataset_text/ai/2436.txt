Sure! A router is a device that helps connect your devices, like computers, phones, and tablets, to the internet. It does this by sending data back and forth between your devices and the internet. A modem is a device that connects your home or office to the internet. It does this by converting the data that comes through your internet service provider (ISP) into a format that your devices can understand. So, a router and a modem both help you connect to the internet, but they do it in different ways. A modem connects you to the internet, and a router helps distribute that connection to all of your devices. Think of it like this: a modem is like a pipe that brings water into your house, and a router is like the faucet that helps distribute the water to different rooms in your house.
