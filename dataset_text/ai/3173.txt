A CPU heatsink tower is a device that is used to keep a computer's CPU (central processing unit) cool. The CPU is a very important part of a computer because it is responsible for carrying out all of the instructions that a computer needs to follow. When the CPU is working hard, it can get very hot, and if it gets too hot, it can start to malfunction. The heatsink tower is designed to help dissipate this heat and keep the CPU running at a safe temperature. The heatsink tower is made up of several parts, including a base, fins, and a fan. The base is a flat, metal plate that sits on top of the CPU. The fins are thin, metal strips that stick up from the base. The fan is a small, electric motor with blades that spin around. The way that the heatsink tower works is by using the fan to blow cool air over the fins. As the air flows over the fins, it absorbs heat from the metal. The heat is then carried away from the CPU and out of the computer. The hotter the CPU gets, the faster the fan will spin, and the more cool air it will blow over the fins.
