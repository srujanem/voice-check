There are a few reasons why AAA games can be sold for cheap on Steam or other game platforms: Sales and discounts: It is common for game developers and publishers to offer sales and discounts on their games, especially during holidays or special events. This can allow them to sell more copies of the game at a lower price, which can help to increase their overall revenue even though they are making less money per sale. Volume: As you mentioned, it is possible that game developers and publishers can make up for lower prices by selling a large volume of copies. This is especially true for popular games that have a wide audience. Digital distribution: One of the main benefits of digital distribution is that it is much cheaper for game developers and publishers to sell games online compared to physical copies. This means they can afford to sell their games at a lower price and still make a profit. Competition: There are many different game platforms and stores available, and competition among them can lead to lower prices for games. For example, if one platform is selling a game for a lower price than another, it may encourage more people to buy the game from the cheaper platform, which can help to drive down the overall price of the game. Older games: Sometimes, game developers and publishers may lower the price of their older games to make them more attractive to potential buyers. This can help to generate more revenue from these older games and also help to promote their newer releases.
