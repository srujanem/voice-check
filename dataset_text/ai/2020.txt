When your skin gets wet, it can wrinkle because the outer layers of skin absorb water. As the water is absorbed, the skin cells in the outer layers of skin will start to swell. This can cause the skin to wrinkle, much like how a sponge can wrinkle when it absorbs water. After your skin has been wrinkled from being wet, the outer layers of skin may start to peel off. This is because the outer layers of skin are constantly being replaced by new skin cells. When the skin is wet and swollen, the outer layers of skin may become loose and start to peel off. So, in short, when your skin gets wet, it can wrinkle because the outer layers of skin absorb water and swell. As the outer layers of skin start to peel off, the wrinkles will go away.
