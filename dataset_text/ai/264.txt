In general, when you receive money or property as a gift or inheritance, it is not considered taxable income. However, there are some exceptions to this rule. For example, if the money or property you received was part of an estate that was being distributed to you, you may be required to pay taxes on the value of the property or money that you received. This is because the estate may have to pay taxes on the property or money before it is distributed to the beneficiaries. 
Additionally, if you receive a large sum of money as a gift or inheritance and you invest it or use it to generate income, you may be required to pay taxes on any earnings or profits that you make from the money. 
It's important to note that each country has its own tax laws, so it's a good idea to consult with a tax professional or review the tax laws in your country to determine whether you are required to pay taxes on your inheritance or gift.
