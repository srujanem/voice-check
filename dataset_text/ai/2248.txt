Flash is a software platform that was used to create interactive content on the web, such as animation, games, and video players. It was developed by a company called Adobe, and it was very popular in the early days of the internet. However, Flash had several issues that made it difficult to use on mobile devices like iPhones and iPads. For example, it used a lot of battery power, and it could slow down the device. Additionally, Flash was not very secure, and it was often used to spread viruses and other malicious software. As a result, Apple decided not to support Flash on its iOS devices. Instead, Apple developed its own technology called HTML5, which is a more modern and efficient way to create interactive content on the web. HTML5 is now used by most websites and mobile apps, and it is supported by all modern browsers and devices, including iPhones and iPads.
