Sure! A computer search engine is a tool that helps you find information on the internet. Here's how it works: First, you type a question or a few keywords into the search engine's website or app. The search engine then looks through all the websites it knows about and tries to find ones that might have the answer to your question or information about your keywords. The search engine uses special software called an "index" to quickly find websites that might be helpful. The index is like a big list of all the words that appear on all the websites the search engine knows about, and it helps the search engine find websites faster. Once the search engine has found a list of websites that might have the answer to your question, it shows you the list in the form of search results. These results might include links to websites, pictures, videos, or other information.
