Being a carbon-based life form means that the structure of the molecules that make up the organism's body are based on carbon. Carbon is a chemical element that is found in all living things and is essential for life. It is a very special element because it can form a large number of chemical bonds with other elements, including other carbon atoms, which allows it to be a building block for the complex molecules that are necessary for life. 
In the human body, carbon is found in many different molecules, including proteins, carbohydrates, and fats. It is also found in the nucleic acids DNA and RNA, which carry the genetic information that is passed from one generation to the next. 
Overall, the human body is made up of about 18% carbon by weight. While this may not seem like a lot, it is a critical element for the functioning of the body. Without carbon, life as we know it would not be possible.
