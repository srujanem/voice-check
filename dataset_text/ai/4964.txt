The ".com", ".net", and ".org" at the end of a website's address, called a "top-level domain" or TLD, are different types of domain names.\n\nA domain name is like the address of a website. Just like how you need an address to find a house, you need a domain name to find a website.\n\n".com" stands for "commercial", and is typically used by businesses.\n\n".net" stands for "network", and is often used by internet service providers, companies that provide online services, or other organizations involved in networking.\n\n".org" stands for "organization", and is often used by non-profit organizations or groups that are not businesses.\n\nSo, having a ".com" or ".net" at the end of a website's address can give you an idea of what type of organization or company the website belongs to. However, anyone can use any of these TLDs for their website, so it's not always a perfect indicator.\n\nFor example, a non-profit organization might choose to use a ".com" TLD for their website, or a business might use a ".org" TLD. It's up to the owner of the website to decide which TLD they want to use.
