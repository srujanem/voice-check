There are many reasons why some people might not "believe" in vaccines or modern medicine. Some people may not understand how vaccines work or may be misinformed about their safety and effectiveness. Others may be swayed by misinformation or conspiracy theories that are spread online or through social media. Some people may also have personal or religious beliefs that lead them to reject certain medical treatments. It's important to note that not believing in vaccines or modern medicine is not unique to any one country or culture. There are people in every country who may have doubts or reservations about vaccines or medical treatments. However, it is true that vaccine hesitancy and the spread of misinformation about vaccines and medicine is a global problem that can have serious consequences for public health.
