A hangover is a group of unpleasant signs and symptoms that can develop after drinking too much alcohol. When you drink alcohol, it is absorbed into your bloodstream and can affect your body in many ways. It can make you feel happy and relaxed, but it can also make you feel sick and tired. The more alcohol you drink, the more likely you are to have a hangover the next day. Some of the signs and symptoms of a hangover may include: A headache, Nausea or vomiting, Fatigue or feeling very tired, Thirst, Dry mouth, Stomach pain, Light sensitivity, Dizziness or feeling lightheaded, Trouble concentrating or thinking clearly, Mood changes, such as feeling irritable or anxious, Hangovers can last for several hours or even a full day. To help feel better, it's important to drink plenty of water and get some rest. Avoiding alcohol or drinking in moderation can help prevent hangovers.
