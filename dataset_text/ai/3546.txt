Ubuntu Edge was a proposed smartphone that was intended to be able to function as both a high-end smartphone and a desktop computer when connected to a monitor. It was being developed by a company called Canonical, which is the company behind the popular Ubuntu operating system. The idea was that the Ubuntu Edge would be able to run both mobile apps and desktop apps, allowing users to use the same device for both their phone and computer needs. One of the reasons that the Ubuntu Edge was considered so "awesome" was because of its innovative design and high-end specifications. It was intended to have a powerful processor, a lot of memory and storage, and a high-resolution display. It was also going to be made of high-quality materials, such as a ceramic case and a sapphire crystal display. Unfortunately, the Ubuntu Edge project was never fully realized. The company behind it launched a crowdfunding campaign to try to raise the money needed to develop and produce the device, but they were unable to reach their fundraising goal. As a result, the Ubuntu Edge was never produced and is now considered a failed project.
