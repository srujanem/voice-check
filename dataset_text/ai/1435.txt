There are a few different ways that restaurants can play music. Some restaurants might use a music streaming service like Pandora or Spotify, which allows them to choose specific songs or playlists to play on a loop. Other restaurants might use a CD player or a portable music device like an iPod to play CDs or digital music files. Some restaurants might even have a special radio frequency that they use to play music, although this is less common. In general, the method that a restaurant uses to play music will depend on the size of the restaurant and its budget, as well as the preferences of the owners and employees.
