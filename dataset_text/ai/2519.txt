DNA replication is the process by which a cell copies its genetic information before cell division. The double helix unwinds as helicase breaks the hydrogen bonds between base pairs. DNA polymerase then adds complementary nucleotides to each template strand in the 5' to 3' direction. This semi-conservative process ensures that each daughter cell receives an identical copy of the genome, with one original strand and one newly synthesized strand.
