When a country needs to mobilize its military, it typically does so by calling up reserve soldiers and activating National Guard units. These are groups of soldiers who are trained and ready to serve, but are not on active duty full-time.\n\nIn some cases, a country may also conscript, or draft, soldiers to serve in the military. This means that they may require all able-bodied citizens between certain ages (such as 18-40) to serve in the military, either full-time or in a reserve capacity. Conscription is more common in some countries than others, and is often only used during times of war or other national emergencies.\n\nIt's important to note that each country has its own laws and procedures for mobilizing its military, and these can vary widely. Some countries may have a larger standing army, while others rely more on reserves and conscription.
