Corn syrup is cheaper than sugar in the United States because the government provides financial support to corn farmers through a program called subsidies. Subsidies are payments or other forms of financial assistance that the government gives to certain industries or businesses to help them stay afloat or to encourage them to produce certain products. 
In the case of corn, the government provides subsidies to corn farmers to help them cover their costs and to keep the price of corn low. This makes it cheaper for companies to produce corn syrup, which is made from corn, and therefore also makes it cheaper for consumers to buy. 
So, in short, corn syrup is cheaper than sugar in the United States because the government helps to keep the price of corn low by providing subsidies to corn farmers. This makes it cheaper for companies to produce corn syrup, which is then also cheaper for consumers to buy.
