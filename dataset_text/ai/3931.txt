Okay, let's start with the basics. When you buy stocks, you are buying a tiny piece of ownership in a company. For example, if you buy stock in Nike, you are buying a tiny piece of ownership in Nike. To buy stocks, you need to have a special account called a brokerage account. You can think of this like a special bank account, but instead of keeping money in it, you keep stocks. To buy stocks, you need to tell your brokerage account which stocks you want to buy, and how many you want to buy. You can do this by using a website or an app. You can also ask a person called a broker to help you buy stocks. Now, let's talk about making money off stocks. When you own stocks, you can make money in two ways. The first way is if the company does well and the value of the stock goes up. For example, let's say you buy a stock for $10 and the value of the stock goes up to $15. If you sell the stock at $15, you will make $5. The second way to make money off stocks is if the company pays you a tiny piece of its profits in the form of a dividend. This is like a present the company gives you for owning a tiny piece of it. So, when is the best time to sell stocks? That's a difficult question and there is no one answer that works for everyone. Some people like to hold onto their stocks for a long time, hoping that the value will go up. Other people like to sell their stocks as soon as they make a profit. It really depends on your goals and how much risk you are willing to take.
