Glasses and contacts both have their own advantages and disadvantages. It really depends on what your needs and preferences are. Here are some things to consider: Glasses sit on your nose and rest on your ears. They can be comfortable to wear and easy to put on and take off. They also allow you to see clearly without having to touch your eyes. However, some people find that glasses can be heavy or bulky, and they can be prone to getting scratched or fogging up. Contacts sit directly on your eye and are almost invisible. They can be more comfortable to wear for some people, especially if they are active or play sports. Contacts also allow you to see clearly without any frame blocking your view. However, contacts require more care and maintenance than glasses. You have to clean them regularly and replace them every few weeks or months. You also have to be more careful when handling them, as it is easy to damage them or get them dirty. Overall, both glasses and contacts can be good options for correcting vision problems. The best choice for you will depend on your personal needs and preferences. It is always a good idea to talk to an eye doctor to determine the best option for you.
