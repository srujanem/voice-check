Sure! The factorial of a number is a way of multiplying that number by all the numbers that come before it. For example, the factorial of 4 is 4 x 3 x 2 x 1, which is equal to 24. The factorial of 0 is a little bit different. If we try to find the factorial of 0 by multiplying all the numbers that come before it, we don't have any numbers to multiply! There are no numbers that come before 0. So, to make the math work out, we say that the factorial of 0 is equal to 1.
