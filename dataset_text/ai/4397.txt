This cone is called the "diaphragm."\n\nWhen electricity flows through the wire, it creates a magnetic field around the wire.\n\nThe magnetic field around the wire interacts with the magnet in the speaker, causing the wire (and the cone attached to it) to move.\n\nAs the cone moves back and forth, it pushes and pulls the air around it, creating sound waves that we can hear.\n\nThe sound waves created by the movement of the cone travel through the air and into our ears, where our brains interpret them as sound.\n\nSo in short, the magnet, wire, and cone work together to create movement that produces sound waves, which we hear as sound.
