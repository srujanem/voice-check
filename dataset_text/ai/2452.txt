Domain names are like addresses for websites on the internet. Just like you need a street address to receive mail or visit a house, you need a domain name to access a website. There are companies called domain name registrars that sell domain names to people and businesses. These registrars have to be approved by a group called the Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers (ICANN). ICANN is responsible for making sure that all the domain names on the internet are unique and organized. Some popular domain name registrars include GoDaddy, Network Solutions, and Namecheap. These companies have been around for a long time and have built up a good reputation, so many people trust them to buy their domain names from. Anyone can sign up for a domain name, but they have to go through a registrar to do it.
