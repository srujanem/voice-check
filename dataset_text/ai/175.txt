Heart attacks are usually caused by something, but they can happen randomly in some cases. A heart attack occurs when the blood flow to a part of the heart is blocked. This can be caused by a blockage in one of the blood vessels that supplies blood to the heart. The blockage is usually caused by a buildup of plaque, a substance made up of fat, cholesterol, and other substances in the blood. Plaque can build up in the blood vessels over time and can eventually rupture, causing a blood clot to form. If the blood clot is large enough, it can block the blood flow to the heart, causing a heart attack. 
In some cases, heart attacks can be brought on by other factors, such as high blood pressure, smoking, or stress. However, they can also happen randomly, even in people who do not have any known risk factors for heart disease. 
It is also possible for people to have more than one heart attack. After a heart attack, it is important to work with a healthcare provider to make lifestyle changes and take medications as prescribed to lower the risk of having another heart attack.
