Greenhouse gases are gases that are able to trap heat in the Earth's atmosphere. They do this because they are able to absorb certain types of energy, specifically the energy that is given off by the Earth as it cools. This energy is called infrared radiation.\n\nThe atoms in greenhouse gases have electrons that are arranged in such a way that they can absorb infrared radiation. When the infrared radiation hits the gas molecules, it causes the electrons to vibrate. This vibration causes the atoms in the molecule to heat up, which in turn heats up the surrounding air.\n\nThe most common greenhouse gases are water vapor, carbon dioxide, methane, and ozone. These gases are found naturally in the Earth's atmosphere, but human activities such as burning fossil fuels and agriculture can also increase the levels of these gases in the atmosphere.\n\nOverall, the ability of greenhouse gases to absorb and trap heat is important for keeping the Earth warm enough for us to live on. However, if there are too many greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, it can cause the Earth to become too warm, which is known as global warming.
