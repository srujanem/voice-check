There could be a few reasons why the text looks different on your TV compared to your computer monitor. One reason could be that the display quality of the TV is lower than that of the computer monitor. TVs and monitors can have different technologies and features that affect how they display text, such as the type of screen (e.g. LCD, OLED, etc.), the pixel density, and the refresh rate. Additionally, the text on your computer might be set to a higher resolution than the text on your TV, which can make it appear clearer and more defined. Finally, the distance between the TV and your eyes can also affect how the text appears, as text on a larger screen might look more pixelated if you are too close to it.
