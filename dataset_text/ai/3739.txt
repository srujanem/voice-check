Gambling is considered immoral by some people because it can be addictive and lead people to make poor decisions. It's easy to lose a lot of money quickly when gambling, especially if you get caught up in the excitement and keep betting even when you're losing. Some people also believe that gambling is wrong because it can take advantage of people who are struggling financially and who might be more likely to gamble in an attempt to make money. Additionally, some people think that gambling is a waste of time and resources because it doesn't contribute anything positive to society.
