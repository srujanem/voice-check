Rats are a common choice for laboratory research because they are relatively easy to care for, reproduce quickly, and have a lifespan that is long enough to allow for long-term studies. They are also similar to humans in many ways, including their anatomy and physiology, which makes them useful for studying a wide range of health conditions and diseases. Additionally, rats are very intelligent and can be trained to perform various tasks, which makes them useful for studying learning and behavior. Overall, rats are a valuable tool for researchers because they can provide insights into many different areas of science and medicine.
