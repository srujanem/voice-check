Gandalf the Grey was a powerful wizard, but he didn't have the ability to fly or to transport people instantly from one place to another. The giant birds that Gandalf could summon, known as Eagles, were intelligent beings with their own will and could not simply be ordered around like a mode of transportation. 
In the Lord of the Rings series, the Eagles did play a helpful role at times, such as rescuing Gandalf and the others from the top of Orthanc and helping to defeat the dragon Smaug. However, the Eagles were not able to carry people long distances or to fly them to their exact destination. 
Additionally, the journey of the characters in the Lord of the Rings serves as a metaphor for the journey of life and the challenges and obstacles that we face. The characters had to walk and travel by various means, such as horses and boats, in order to reach their destination. This added to the sense of adventure and the sense that the characters were truly undertaking a long and difficult journey.
