Inception is a movie about a group of people who are experts at entering people's dreams and manipulating them. The main character, Cobb, is hired by a businessman named Saito to plant an idea into the mind of another businessman, Robert Fischer. To do this, Cobb and his team create a series of dreams within dreams, each layer deeper than the last. They do this by using a machine called a "totem," which helps them keep track of which level of the dream they are in. Cobb has been trying to plant this idea in Fischer's mind because he wants to be reunited with his children, who he has not seen since his wife, Mal, died. However, Cobb is haunted by memories of his wife, who tried to convince him that their life together was just a dream and that he needs to wake up. At the end of the movie, Cobb returns home and sees his children waiting for him. He spins his totem, a spinning top, to see if he is still in a dream or if he is finally awake. The movie ends before the top falls, leaving it up to the viewer to decide if Cobb is still dreaming or if he has finally returned to reality.
