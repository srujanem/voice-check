The situation in Ukraine is a complex one, and there are a number of factors that have contributed to the way it has been addressed by the international community.

Another factor is that the international community has sought to address the situation in Ukraine through diplomatic and economic means, rather than military intervention. This has included imposing sanctions on Russia and supporting Ukraine through economic and military assistance. 
It's also important to note that the situation in Ukraine is ongoing, and the international community continues to monitor developments and consider its options for addressing the conflict.
