In most places, people are allowed to carry handguns because they are considered a reasonable means of self-defense. On the other hand, swords are generally considered to be more dangerous than handguns because they are larger and can cause more serious injuries. For these reasons, most places have laws that restrict the carrying of swords in public places. It's important to note that laws regarding the carrying of weapons vary from place to place, so it's always a good idea to check the laws in your specific location before carrying any type of weapon.
