If a lawyer lets slip that their client is guilty, either intentionally or not, it could have serious consequences. This is because a lawyer's primary duty is to defend their client's interests and protect their rights. If a lawyer reveals that their client is guilty, they may be violating their duty of confidentiality, which is a legal obligation to keep information shared by a client private. This could lead to the lawyer facing disciplinary action, such as being disbarred or losing their license to practice law. Additionally, the lawyer's revelation could be used as evidence against their client in court and could potentially harm their case. It is important for lawyers to maintain confidentiality and protect their client's interests at all times, even if their client is guilty.
