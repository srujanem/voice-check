Traffic lights have three lights because they are used to control the flow of traffic at intersections. Red, yellow, and green are the three colors that are used in traffic lights because they are easy for people to recognize and understand. Red means "stop," yellow means "caution," and green means "go." 
Having three lights allows traffic to be controlled in all directions. If there was just one light that changed color, it would be more confusing for people to know when it was safe to cross the intersection. For example, if the light was red, people would not know if they should stop because the light is red in all directions, or if they should go because the light is green in the opposite direction. 
By using three lights, traffic can be controlled more efficiently and safely. When the red light is on, all traffic must stop. When the yellow light is on, traffic should slow down and be prepared to stop. When the green light is on, traffic can proceed through the intersection. This helps to prevent accidents and keeps traffic moving smoothly.
