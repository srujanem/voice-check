An unincorporated area is a place that is not part of a city or town and is not governed by a local government. This means that it is not within the boundaries of a city or town, and it does not have its own local government or council to make decisions for the people who live there.\n\nUnincorporated areas are often rural areas, or they may be suburbs or neighborhoods that are located outside of a city or town. They may also be called "unincorporated communities" or "unincorporated towns."\n\nIn an unincorporated area, the county government is responsible for providing certain services, such as police protection, fire protection, and road maintenance. However, because an unincorporated area is not part of a city or town, it may not have the same level of services or amenities as an incorporated area, such as libraries, schools, or parks.\n\nIn some cases, people who live in an unincorporated area may choose to incorporate their community into a city or town in order to have more control over their local government and to access more services and amenities.
