Hot water is more effective at removing dirt, grime, and germs from dishes than cold water because it helps to loosen and dissolve these substances. When you use hot water to wash your dishes, the heat helps to break down the food particles and other contaminants that may be stuck on the dishes. Hot water also helps to kill germs and bacteria on the dishes, which is especially important for maintaining good hygiene. 
Overall, using hot water to wash your dishes helps to make sure that they are thoroughly cleaned and sanitized, which is why it is a common practice in many households. So, if you want to make sure that your dishes are really clean, using hot water is a good idea.
