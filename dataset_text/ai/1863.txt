Scientology is a religion that was founded by L. Ron Hubbard in the 1950s. It is based on the idea that people have an immortal spirit called a "thetan" that has been reincarnated many times and can be freed from the cycle of reincarnation through a process called "auditing." Auditing involves a person sitting with a trained auditor and discussing their thoughts and experiences in order to identify and resolve any spiritual difficulties they may be experiencing. One of the main appeals of Scientology is the belief that people have the ability to improve their lives and achieve higher levels of spiritual awareness through auditing and other Scientology practices. Some people may find this belief system appealing because it offers a sense of hope and the possibility for personal growth and improvement.
