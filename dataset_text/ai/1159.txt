The climate is the way the weather is and the way it changes over time. Right now, the climate is changing in a way that is not good for people or animals. The Earth is getting warmer because people are using a lot of energy that comes from burning things like oil and coal. This makes gases that trap heat in the Earth's atmosphere, like a blanket. These gases are called greenhouse gases. 
The Earth has always had greenhouse gases, and they help to keep the planet warm enough for life. But when there are too many of them, it can make the Earth too hot. This can cause problems like heatwaves, storms, and floods. It can also make it hard for plants and animals to live. 
We're not completely sure what will happen in the future, but many scientists think that if we don't start using less energy and burning fewer things, the Earth will keep getting warmer. This could make life difficult for people and animals. Some people are working hard to find ways to use less energy and burn fewer things, so that we can help the Earth stay healthy.
