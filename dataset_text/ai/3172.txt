Malnutrition encompasses both undernutrition and overnutrition and affects people in all countries. Undernutrition, including stunting and wasting in children, results from inadequate intake of calories and essential nutrients. Overnutrition, leading to overweight and obesity, results from excessive caloric intake and poor diet quality. Both forms impair health and cognitive development. Addressing malnutrition requires improving food systems, nutrition education, and access to diverse, nutrient-rich foods.
