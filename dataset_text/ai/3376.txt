The Socratic Method is a way of asking questions to help people think about something more deeply and come to a better understanding of it. It's named after Socrates, who was a philosopher in ancient Greece. Here's how it works: Someone has a question or problem they want to solve. The person using the Socratic Method asks a series of questions about the problem to help the other person think more deeply about it. The person answering the questions tries to explain their thoughts and ideas. The person using the Socratic Method asks more questions to help the other person clarify their thinking and ideas. The goal of the Socratic Method is to help people come to a better understanding of something by thinking about it more deeply and critically. It's often used in classrooms or other educational settings to help students learn and think more critically about a topic.
