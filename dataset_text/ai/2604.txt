Artificial livers and kidneys are a type of medical technology that scientists and engineers are working on. They are trying to create devices that can do the same job as a real liver or kidney, which are two important organs in our bodies that help us stay healthy. Creating artificial organs is very difficult because they have to be able to do a lot of different things, just like real organs do. For example, a liver helps filter toxins and waste products out of our blood, and a kidney helps control the amount of fluid in our body. Creating a device that can do all of these things is a complex challenge that requires a lot of scientific knowledge and technical expertise. Scientists and engineers are making progress on developing artificial livers and kidneys, but it is still a work in progress. It may take some time before we have fully functional artificial livers and kidneys that can be used in people. However, there are already some medical technologies that can help people whose livers or kidneys are not working properly, such as dialysis machines for kidneys and liver transplant surgery.
