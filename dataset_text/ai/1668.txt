Human rights are the fundamental rights and freedoms that belong to every person, regardless of nationality, gender, ethnicity, or religion. They are enshrined in documents such as the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, adopted by the United Nations in 1948. These rights include the right to life, freedom from torture, the right to education, and freedom of expression. The international community works through treaties, courts, and advocacy organizations to hold states accountable for protecting these rights. Continued research and public awareness are crucial to addressing the challenges in this field.
