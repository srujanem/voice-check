Cuban cigars are considered to be some of the best cigars in the world because they are made from high-quality tobacco leaves that are grown in the Vuelta Abajo region of Cuba. The tobacco leaves are fermented and aged for several years before they are made into cigars. The process of making Cuban cigars is also very traditional, with many of the steps being done by hand. This attention to detail and the use of high-quality tobacco is what makes Cuban cigars so special and coveted by cigar enthusiasts. Additionally, because of the US trade embargo with Cuba, Cuban cigars are not widely available in the US, which has added to their mystique and made them even more sought after.
