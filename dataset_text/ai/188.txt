During the Cold War, the border between East and West Germany was heavily guarded and patrolled by both East German and Soviet forces. It was very difficult for people living in East Berlin to cross the border illegally, especially at places where the border was heavily guarded. If someone was caught trying to cross the border illegally, they could face severe punishment, including imprisonment or even death. 
Additionally, the border between East and West Germany was not just a physical barrier, but also a political and ideological divide. The East German government did not want people to leave the country, as it saw this as a loss of human capital and a blow to the regime's legitimacy. As a result, the East German government took steps to make it very difficult for people to leave the country, including building the Berlin Wall and other barriers along the border. 
So while it might have been possible for some people to try to cross the border at less heavily guarded areas, it was still a very risky and dangerous endeavor.
