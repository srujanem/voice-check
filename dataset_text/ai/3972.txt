Acne is caused by a combination of factors, including genetics, hormones, and the environment. When your body produces too much oil, or sebum, it can clog your pores and lead to acne. Genetics plays a role in how much oil your body produces and how sensitive your skin is to certain hormones. Stress and certain medications can also increase the production of hormones, which can lead to acne. However, it is important to note that acne is not caused by dirt or poor hygiene. In fact, over-cleansing or scrubbing your skin too hard can actually make acne worse.
