Streaming movies is generally considered to be safer than downloading them, because when you stream a movie, you are not actually downloading the file onto your device. Instead, you are watching the movie while it is being transmitted to your device over the internet. This means that you are not creating a copy of the movie on your device, which reduces the risk of downloading potentially malicious software or malware along with the movie. However, streaming movies does come with some potential risks. For example, if you are streaming movies from an unsecured or untrustworthy website, you could be exposing your device to malware or other forms of cyberattacks. Additionally, if you are streaming movies over an unsecured internet connection, your data could be intercepted by third parties. To minimize these risks, it is important to only stream movies from reputable websites and to use a secure, encrypted internet connection whenever possible.
