In most languages, the first letter of a sentence is capitalized because it helps us to understand that a new sentence is starting. Think of it like a stop sign for your eyes: when you see a capital letter at the beginning of a sentence, it tells you to pause and pay attention because a new idea is coming. This helps us to read and understand written language more easily. For example, if you see a bunch of words all jammed together with no capital letters, it can be hard to know where one sentence ends and the next begins. But if you see a capital letter at the beginning of each sentence, it's easier to know where to pause and take a breath when you're reading out loud. So, in short, we capitalize the first letter of a sentence to make it easier to read and understand written language. Does that make sense?
