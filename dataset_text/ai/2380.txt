A tax write-off is a way for a person or business to reduce their taxable income, which results in paying less in taxes. It's a way to save money on taxes by subtracting certain expenses from your income before you calculate how much tax you owe. Here's an example: Let's say you own a small business and you make $50,000 in profit in a year. You also had $10,000 in business expenses, such as buying supplies, paying rent, and hiring employees. If you were to write off these expenses, you would only have to pay taxes on $50,000 - $10,000 = $40,000 of income. This means you would save money on taxes because you're only paying taxes on a lower amount of income. There are many different types of expenses that you can write off, depending on the type of business you have and the tax laws in your country.
