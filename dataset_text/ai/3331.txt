Diamonds are expensive because they are rare and because people are willing to pay a lot of money for them. Diamonds are made of carbon, which is a very common element, but diamonds are not very common in nature because they are formed under very specific conditions that do not happen very often. It takes a lot of time, pressure, and heat for a diamond to form, and most diamonds are found deep underground, which makes them difficult to extract. The process of mining diamonds and bringing them to market is also expensive. In addition, diamonds have been given a lot of value in society because of their beauty and their use in jewelry, which has helped to make them even more expensive. So, even though diamonds are made of a common element, they are not very common in nature and are considered valuable because of their rarity and the work that goes into mining and selling them.
