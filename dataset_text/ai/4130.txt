It can be challenging for NCAA athletes to balance traveling for their sport and college work, especially during busy times like March Madness. However, there are a few things that can help them manage their time and responsibilities more effectively:\n\nPlanning ahead: NCAA athletes can try to plan their schedules as far in advance as possible so they know what to expect and can make arrangements for things like missing classes or completing assignments while they are on the road.\n\nCommunicating with professors: It is important for NCAA athletes to communicate with their professors about their travel schedules and any conflicts that may arise. Professors may be able to provide accommodations such as allowing the athlete to make up missed assignments or exams, or allowing them to complete coursework online while they are away.\n\nStaying organized: NCAA athletes should try to stay organized and keep track of their assignments, deadlines, and other responsibilities. This can help them stay on top of their work and avoid falling behind.\n\nSeeking support: NCAA athletes may also find it helpful to seek support from their coaches, academic advisors, and other resources on campus, such as tutoring or study groups, to help them manage their responsibilities.\n\nOverall, it takes a lot of discipline and time management skills to balance college work and traveling for a sport. However, with the right strategies and support, it is possible to successfully manage both.
