In the early days of the World Wide Web, people used to type "www" before the domain name of a website in order to access it. For example, to visit the website "example.com", you might have typed "www.example.com" into your web browser. The "www" stands for "World Wide Web", and it was used as a way to distinguish websites from other types of resources that were also accessible through the internet. However, as the internet has grown and evolved, the use of "www" has become less necessary, and many websites can be accessed simply by typing the domain name without the "www".
