Expensive cameras are typically made with higher quality materials and components, which can make them more durable and reliable. They may also have more advanced features and capabilities, such as a higher resolution sensor, faster autofocus, and better low light performance. These features can make a big difference for professional photographers who rely on their equipment to capture high quality images in a variety of conditions. Additionally, expensive cameras may also have more robust build quality, which can make them more comfortable to use and handle over long periods of time. Finally, expensive cameras often come with better warranties and support, which can be valuable for professionals who need to rely on their equipment for their livelihood.
