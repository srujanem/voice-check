It's normal for people to talk to themselves out loud from time to time, especially when they are thinking through a problem or trying to remember something. This is called "private speech" and it can be helpful because it allows us to think out loud and organize our thoughts. 
However, if you find that you are talking to yourself out loud too often and it is becoming annoying or disruptive, there are a few things you can try to help stop the behavior: 
1. Try to write down your thoughts instead of speaking them out loud. This can help you organize your thoughts and ideas without making noise. 
2. When you catch yourself talking to yourself out loud, try to stop and take a deep breath. This can help you become more aware of the behavior and give you a chance to pause and think before you speak. 
3. If you are feeling stressed or overwhelmed, try to find ways to relax and de-stress. This can help reduce the urge to talk to yourself out loud. 
4. Consider seeking help from a mental health professional if the problem persists. They can help you understand why you are talking to yourself out loud and provide strategies to help you stop the behavior. 
Remember, it's normal to talk to yourself out loud every once in a while, but if it is becoming a problem, it's important to take steps to address it.
