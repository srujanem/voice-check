Green olives are sometimes stuffed with ingredients like pimentos, garlic, or cheese to add flavor to the olives. People stuff olives in this way because it can make them taste more interesting and add a different texture to the olive. 
There are a few different ways that olives can be stuffed. Some olives are stuffed by hand, while others are stuffed using machines in a factory. In either case, the process usually involves removing the pit or seed from the center of the olive and then filling the hole with the desired stuffing. The stuffed olive is then usually placed back into a brine solution to preserve it. 
So, while there might not be any elves involved in the process, there are people (or machines) working to stuff the olives and make them ready for you to enjoy.
