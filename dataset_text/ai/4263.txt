Bolt action rifles are more suitable for shooting powerful ammunition because they are generally more reliable and accurate than semi-automatic or fully automatic rifles. This is because bolt action rifles have a simpler design and fewer moving parts, which means there is less chance of a malfunction occurring.\n\nIn a bolt action rifle, the shooter must manually operate the bolt to chamber a new round after each shot. This process is slower than firing a semi-automatic or fully automatic rifle, but it allows the shooter to carefully control each shot and make sure that the rifle is properly loaded and ready to fire.\n\nBecause of their simplicity and reliability, bolt action rifles are often preferred for shooting large calibers, which produce a lot of recoil and require more precise handling. Bolt action rifles are also often used for long range shooting, where accuracy is critical.\n\nOverall, bolt action rifles are a good choice for shooting powerful ammunition because they are reliable, accurate, and allow the shooter to carefully control each shot.
