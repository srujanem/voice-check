Opposites can attract because they can balance each other out and bring new perspectives and ways of thinking to a relationship. For example, if one person is very organized and structured, they may be attracted to someone who is more spontaneous and flexible. This can help create a well-rounded partnership where both people can learn and grow from each other. 
It's also possible that people are drawn to those who are different from them because it can be exciting and refreshing to be with someone who has different interests and experiences. 
However, it's important to note that opposites do not always attract, and it's not necessary for two people to be completely different in order to have a successful relationship. What's most important is that two people are compatible and can communicate effectively with each other.
