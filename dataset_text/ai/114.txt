Certain sounds can be irritating to some people because they have a high frequency or because they are loud. High-pitched sounds, like the sound of metal scraping or a squeaking on a whiteboard, can be especially annoying because they are at a frequency that is particularly noticeable to the human ear. Some people may also find loud noises to be irritating, especially if they are sudden or unexpected. It's also possible that some people simply have a lower tolerance for certain sounds, whether they are high-pitched or loud, and find them more irritating than others do.
