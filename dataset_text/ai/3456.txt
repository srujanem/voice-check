Education: Democrats generally believe that the government should invest more in public education, while Republicans generally believe that parents and local communities should have more control over education decisions. Gun control: Democrats generally support stricter gun control laws, while Republicans generally support the right to bear arms as protected by the Second Amendment to the U.S. Constitution.
