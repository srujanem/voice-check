Aluminum foil is made by rolling sheets of aluminum metal until they are very thin. When the sheets are rolled, they go through a machine that polishes the metal and gives it a shiny finish on one side. The other side of the foil is left with a dull finish because it is the back side that was not polished by the machine. So, the shiny side of aluminum foil is the side that has been polished and the dull side is the side that has not been polished.
