When a police officer pulls you over for speeding, they will usually ask you for your driver's license, registration, and proof of insurance. They may also ask you for your name and address. The officer will then go back to their car to run a check on your information to make sure you are licensed to drive and that your vehicle is registered. 
While the officer is in their car, they may be checking to see if you have any outstanding warrants or if there are any other issues with your driving record. They may also be communicating with their dispatch center or other officers to get more information. 
It's important to stay in your vehicle and follow the officer's instructions while they are running the check. The officer may return to your car to ask you more questions or to issue a citation (ticket) if they believe you have broken a traffic law. 
It's also a good idea to remain calm and respectful while interacting with the officer. It can be stressful to be pulled over, but it's important to remember that the officer is just doing their job and trying to keep the roads safe for everyone.
