Protesters are people who take time out of their regular schedules to attend protests and speak out about something they feel strongly about. Some protesters may have jobs or other responsibilities, but they are able to make time to attend the protest by taking time off work or rearranging their schedules. Other protesters may not have jobs or may have more flexibility in their schedules, which allows them to attend the protest for longer periods of time. It's important to note that not all protesters are able to attend protests for days on end, and the length of time that people are able to protest can vary based on their individual circumstances.
