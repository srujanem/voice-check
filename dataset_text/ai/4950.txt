A whole house generator is a device that produces electricity to power your home in the event of a power outage. It runs on a fuel source, such as propane, to generate electricity. The amount of power the generator consumes will depend on how much electricity you are using at a given time. If you are using a lot of electricity, the generator will need to consume more power to keep up with the demand. If you are using less electricity, the generator will consume less power. The generator will always be working to produce the amount of electricity that you need, and the amount of power it consumes will vary based on how much electricity you are using.
