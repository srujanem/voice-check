Radio waves are a type of electromagnetic radiation, just like light waves, X-rays, and microwaves. Electromagnetic radiation is a type of energy that travels through the air at the speed of light. Radio waves are a type of electromagnetic radiation that is invisible to the human eye. Radio waves are used to transmit information through the air, such as music, news, and other types of programming on the radio. They are also used to transmit signals for cell phones, television, and other types of communication. Although we cannot see radio waves with our eyes, we can use special equipment, such as radios and televisions, to detect and receive them. When a radio wave hits the antenna of a radio or TV, the energy in the wave is converted into an electrical current that can be used to transmit information. So, while we cannot see radio waves directly, we can use technology to detect and use them to transmit information.
