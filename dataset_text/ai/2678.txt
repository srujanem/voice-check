Cancer can kill people in several ways. Here are a few examples: A cancerous tumor can grow and press against other organs or tissues in the body, which can cause them to stop working properly. For example, if a cancerous tumor grows in the lungs, it can make it difficult for a person to breathe. Cancer cells can spread (metastasize) to other parts of the body, which can cause problems in those areas. For example, if cancer cells spread to the bones, they can weaken the bones and cause them to break more easily. Cancer can affect the body's immune system, which is the part of the body that helps fight off infections and diseases. If a person's immune system is weakened by cancer, they may be more likely to get sick or have infections that are more difficult to treat. Some cancer treatments, such as chemotherapy and radiation, can be very hard on the body and can cause side effects that make a person feel very sick. Overall, cancer can kill people by causing problems with the way the body functions and by making it harder for the body to fight off other health problems.
