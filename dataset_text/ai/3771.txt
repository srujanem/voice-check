Social Darwinism is a belief that some groups of people are biologically superior to others, and that they should therefore have more power and privileges. This belief was used to justify discrimination, racism, and other harmful practices. It is named after Charles Darwin, a scientist who proposed the theory of evolution, which explains how species change and adapt over time. However, Social Darwinism twisted his ideas to justify treating certain groups of people unfairly. This belief is not supported by scientific evidence and is not accepted by most people today, as it goes against the values of fairness and equality.
