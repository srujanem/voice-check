Last names, also called "surnames," are names that are passed down from parents to their children. They are usually given to people to identify their family or their ancestors. Last names can come from many different sources. Some last names are based on a person's job or profession. For example, if a person's job is to make shoes, their last name might be "Shoemaker." Other last names are based on a person's place of origin. For example, if a person comes from a place called "Greenfield," their last name might be "Green." Last names can also be based on a person's physical characteristics or personal qualities. For example, if a person has red hair, their last name might be "Redhead." So, last names are like special names that tell people a little bit about who you are and where you come from.
