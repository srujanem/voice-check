Scratching your skin can cause it to turn red because scratching can irritate the skin and cause blood vessels in the skin to widen. When the blood vessels widen, more blood flows to the area and this can make the skin look red. It's kind of like when you get a sunburn and your skin turns red because your blood vessels have widened due to the sun's rays. Scratching your skin can also cause it to become itchy, which is your body's way of telling you that something is irritating your skin. If you scratch your skin too much or too hard, you might accidentally scratch the skin until it bleeds. This is not good for your skin because it can cause infections or scarring. It's important to be gentle with your skin and try not to scratch it too much.
