The air we exhale is warm because it has been in our body, where it picks up heat from our body as it passes through our respiratory system. When we exhale, we are releasing this warm air out of our mouth. 
On the other hand, the air we blow out cold is because it has not yet been in our body and has not had a chance to pick up heat. When we blow out air, we are just forcing air out of our mouth that was already at the temperature of the environment around us. This is why the air we blow out is usually the same temperature as the air in the room.
