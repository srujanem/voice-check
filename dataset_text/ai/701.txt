Human beings have become more successful than other animals on Earth for a variety of reasons. One reason is that we have developed very advanced brains that allow us to think, communicate, and problem-solve in complex ways. This has allowed us to create tools, build shelter, and develop agriculture, which has allowed us to survive and thrive in many different environments. 
Another reason is that human beings are highly adaptable and able to learn and adapt to new situations quickly. We have also been able to develop and use technology, such as fire and the wheel, which has helped us to overcome many challenges and become more efficient at meeting our basic needs. 
Finally, human beings have also been able to form complex societies and work together in groups, which has allowed us to share knowledge and resources and work together to achieve common goals. 
While chimpanzees and gorillas do share some traits with humans, they have not been as successful because they do not have the same advanced brains or the ability to use and create technology in the same way that humans do.
