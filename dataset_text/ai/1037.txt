The length of the day, or the amount of time between sunrise and sunset, changes throughout the year because of the tilt of the Earth's axis. On December 21, the North Pole is tilted farthest away from the sun, which is why it's the shortest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere. 
After December 21, the days will start to get longer again. However, the weather doesn't change immediately because the Earth's atmosphere and oceans take time to warm up or cool down. It takes some time for the warmer weather to reach the surface of the Earth, so even though the days are getting longer, it may still be cold for a while.
