Steps that have a flat area or "landing" between sets of stairs are called "staircases with a landing." These types of stairs are often found in buildings and are designed to make it easier and safer to climb the stairs.\n\nWhen you walk up stairs, you use your leg muscles to lift your body weight. If you have to walk up a lot of stairs in a row without any breaks, your leg muscles can start to feel tired and it can be harder to keep climbing.\n\nHaving a landing between sets of stairs allows you to take a short break and catch your breath. It also gives you a place to rest your legs for a moment before continuing to climb the stairs.\n\nIn addition to making it easier to climb the stairs, staircases with landings also help to make the stairs safer. If you trip or stumble while climbing stairs, having a landing can give you a place to catch yourself before you fall.\n\nSo, in short, the reason why many buildings have steps with landings is to make it easier and safer to climb the stairs, especially if you have to go up a lot of them.
