High functioning depression is a type of depression that is characterized by the ability to maintain outward appearances of functioning normally while experiencing the symptoms of depression. It is a real illness that is recognized by the medical community.\n\nDepression is a serious medical condition that affects how a person feels, thinks, and behaves. It can cause a wide range of symptoms, including feelings of sadness, hopelessness, and a lack of energy or interest in activities. People with depression may also have difficulty sleeping, changes in appetite, and difficulty concentrating.\n\nHigh functioning depression can be difficult to recognize because the person with the condition may be able to continue to go to work or school, and may not appear to have any obvious problems. However, they may still be struggling with the internal effects of their depression, such as feeling constantly tired, having a hard time concentrating, or not enjoying activities that they used to find pleasurable.\n\nIt is important to recognize that high functioning depression is a genuine illness and that people who have it need support and treatment to manage their symptoms. If you or someone you know may be experiencing high functioning depression, it is important to speak with a medical professional or mental health provider for a proper diagnosis and treatment plan.
