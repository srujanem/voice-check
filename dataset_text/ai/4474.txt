Dinosaurs were either carnivorous or herbivorous based on the types of teeth and jaws they had. Carnivorous dinosaurs, like the Tyrannosaurus rex, had sharp teeth and strong jaws that were designed to tear and chew meat. Herbivorous dinosaurs, like the Diplodocus, had flat teeth and jaws that were good for grinding and crushing plants.\n\nThe type of diet a dinosaur had was not necessarily determined by the food that was available to them. Instead, it was determined by the way their bodies were built and the tools they had to find and eat food. For example, a carnivorous dinosaur like the T. rex would not have been able to eat plants very well because it had sharp teeth and a powerful jaw that was better suited for tearing meat. On the other hand, a herbivorous dinosaur like the Diplodocus had flat teeth and a weaker jaw that was better suited for grinding and chewing plants.\n\nSo, in short, the type of diet a dinosaur had was determined by the way its body was built and the tools it had to find and eat food.
