Even if a phone doesn't have a SIM card or reception, it may still be able to make emergency calls. This is because emergency calls can be made using a technology called "Enhanced 911" (E911). To make an emergency call without a SIM card or reception, a phone can use a technology called "Wi-Fi Calling". Wi-Fi Calling allows a phone to use a Wi-Fi network to make and receive calls and texts, even if the phone doesn't have a SIM card or cellular reception. So, if you need to make an emergency call and your phone doesn't have a SIM card or cellular reception, you can try to connect to a Wi-Fi network and use Wi-Fi Calling to make the call.
