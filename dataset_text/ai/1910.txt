These cards represent different creatures, spells, and other powers that you can use to defeat your opponent. To start the game, each player shuffles their deck and draws a certain number of cards to start with. Each player takes turns playing cards from their hand and using them to attack the other player or defend themselves. The goal of the game is to reduce your opponent's "life total" to zero before they can do the same to you. You do this by attacking them with your creatures and using spells to cause damage. When you run out of cards in your deck, you have to shuffle your discard pile (the pile of used cards) and use it as your new deck.
