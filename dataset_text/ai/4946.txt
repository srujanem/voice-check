The electrons in an atom do not lose charge as they spin around the nucleus. In fact, they have a negative electric charge, and this charge is not lost or gained as the electrons move.\n\nThe energy that is required for an electron to move around the nucleus comes from the energy needed to change the electron's position or speed. This energy is usually supplied in the form of light or heat, and it can cause the electron to jump to a higher energy level.\n\nWhen an electron is in a higher energy level, it is further away from the nucleus and requires more energy to move. This is why it is said that the energy of an electron is quantized, or limited to certain specific values.\n\nI hope this helps to explain the behavior of electrons in an atom.
