A semicolon is a punctuation mark that you can use in a sentence to connect two independent clauses, or groups of words that can stand on their own as complete sentences. Here's an example of how you can use a semicolon: I'm going to the store; do you want to come with me?, In this sentence, the first clause is "I'm going to the store" and the second clause is "do you want to come with me?" Both of these clauses could be written as separate sentences, but by using a semicolon, you're connecting them and showing that they are closely related. So, to use a semicolon correctly, you need to have two independent clauses in your sentence that you want to connect. Just make sure that you use a semicolon and not a comma, because a comma is not strong enough to connect independent clauses and could create a comma splice. I hope this helps!
