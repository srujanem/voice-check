Preheating the oven is important because it helps the oven reach the desired cooking temperature before you put your food in. Imagine you are making cookies and the recipe says to bake them at 350 degrees for 10 minutes. If you just turn the oven on and then put the cookies in right away, the oven might not be at the right temperature yet. The cookies would have to cook for longer than 10 minutes to be done, because the oven wasn't hot enough to start with. But if you preheat the oven, that means you turn it on and let it heat up to the right temperature before you put the cookies in. That way, the oven is already at the right temperature when you put the cookies in, so they can cook for the right amount of time and come out perfectly. So, preheating the oven helps your food cook more evenly and helps it turn out the way it's supposed to.
