Wisdom teeth are molars that grow in at the back of your mouth, usually in your late teenage years or early twenties. Some people have room in their mouths for wisdom teeth and they come in just fine, but other people don't have enough room. When this happens, the wisdom teeth can become trapped in the gums and cause pain, infection, and other problems. That's why some people have to have their wisdom teeth removed. But even if you do have to get your wisdom teeth removed, they once had an important job to do!, A long time ago, people had very different diets than we do today. They ate a lot more tough, chewy foods that were hard to break down. Wisdom teeth were extra molars that helped them chew their food better so they could get all the nutrients they needed to stay healthy. But as our diet has changed and we don't need to chew our food as much, we don't need wisdom teeth as much as we used to. That's why not everyone has room for them and why some people have to get them removed.
