Sometimes, the magnetic strip on the back of an ATM card can become damaged or demagnetized. This can make it difficult for the card reader to accurately read the information stored on the magnetic strip. 
Placing a plastic bag over the card can help in these situations because the plastic bag acts as a protective layer that can help to shield the card from interference. It's possible that the plastic bag helps to block out external sources of magnetic interference that might be disrupting the card reader's ability to read the card's magnetic strip. 
It's also possible that the plastic bag helps to keep the card clean, which can improve the card reader's ability to read the magnetic strip. Dust and dirt can sometimes accumulate on the surface of the card, and this can interfere with the card reader's ability to accurately read the card. Covering the card with a plastic bag can help to protect it from these types of contaminants, which can make it easier for the card reader to read the card. 
It's important to note that this solution is not guaranteed to work in every situation. If the card is severely damaged or demagnetized, it may not be possible for the card reader to read it, even with the help of a plastic bag. In these cases, it may be necessary to replace the card.
