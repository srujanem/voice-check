Lobotomy is a surgical procedure in which the connections to and from the prefrontal cortex, the part of the brain involved in personality, behavior, and decision-making, are cut or damaged. This procedure was once used as a treatment for certain mental illnesses, but it is no longer performed because it has been found to be ineffective and to have significant negative side effects. 
Lobotomy is also sometimes used colloquically to refer to someone who is behaving in a way that seems dull, emotionless, or lacking in intelligence, as if they had undergone a lobotomy. However, this use of the term is not accurate, as a person who has undergone a lobotomy would likely experience significant cognitive and emotional changes, and would not necessarily behave in a specific way.
