Some people believe that this idea takes away from our sense of responsibility and accountability for our actions. If we don't really have the ability to choose differently, then it doesn't make sense to hold people responsible for their choices. On the other hand, others argue that this idea can help us understand why people do the things they do, and can help us make better choices in the future.
