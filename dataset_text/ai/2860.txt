There are several reasons why modern elections are not decided solely by internet voting. First, there are concerns about the security and integrity of internet voting. It is possible for hackers to try to manipulate the results of an internet election, or for someone to try to vote multiple times. This could lead to the wrong person being elected, or to widespread mistrust in the election process. Second, not everyone has access to the internet or a computer. While libraries and schools might have computers available, not everyone lives near one of these facilities, or might not have the time or transportation to get there. This could mean that some people are unable to participate in the election, which would not be fair. Finally, there are also logistical challenges to organizing and administering an internet election. It would be difficult to ensure that all eligible voters have the opportunity to vote, and to count and verify the votes in a timely and accurate manner. Overall, while internet voting might have some benefits, it also has many challenges that make it difficult to implement in modern elections.
