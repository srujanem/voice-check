Strategy and tactics are both ways of achieving a goal, but they operate at different levels. Strategy is the overall plan for achieving a goal, while tactics are the specific actions taken to carry out that plan. For example, a military might have a strategic goal of defeating an enemy force. To achieve this goal, they might develop a number of different tactics, such as using air strikes, ground assaults, and naval blockades. In terms of naming weapons, "strategic" often refers to weapons or systems that are used to achieve long-term goals or objectives, such as a strategic missile, which is a missile that is used to attack an enemy's military or political infrastructure. "Tactical" often refers to weapons or systems that are used to achieve more immediate goals or objectives, such as a tactical flashlight, which is a flashlight that is used by military or law enforcement personnel in close-quarters combat or other tactical situations.
