Multiple sclerosis, or MS, is a disease that affects the brain and spinal cord. These are important parts of your body because they help send messages between your brain and the rest of your body. In people with MS, the protective covering (called myelin) around the nerves in the brain and spinal cord gets damaged. When this happens, it's harder for the messages to get through and the body doesn't work as well as it should. This can cause a person with MS to have problems with things like walking, seeing, and thinking clearly. MS is a chronic disease, which means it lasts a long time and there is no cure for it. But there are treatments that can help people with MS manage their symptoms and live full lives.
