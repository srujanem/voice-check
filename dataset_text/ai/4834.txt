Yes, it is possible to feel tired just from thinking, even if you are not physically active. When we use our brains to think, learn, or solve problems, it takes energy and can tire us out. It is similar to how our muscles can feel tired after we have used them a lot, like after a workout.\n\nJust like our muscles need rest and time to recover after we have used them a lot, our brains also need rest and time to recover after we have used them a lot. That's why it's important to take breaks and get enough sleep, so that our brains can rest and be ready to work again the next day.
