Dementia is a term used to describe a group of symptoms that can occur when the brain is damaged or diseased. These symptoms can include memory loss, difficulty with thinking and problem-solving, changes in personality, and difficulty with everyday tasks. 
Having dementia can be very difficult for the person experiencing it, as well as for their family and friends. It can be hard to remember things and to communicate with others, and people with dementia may have trouble understanding what is happening around them. 
Despite these challenges, people with dementia are still themselves. They may have some changes in their abilities and personality, but they are still the same person they have always been. It is important to remember that people with dementia should be treated with kindness, respect, and patience, just like anyone else.
