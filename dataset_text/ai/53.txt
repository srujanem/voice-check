To make a delicious homemade vinaigrette, start with a classic ratio of three parts oil to one part acid. Combine extra virgin olive oil with your choice of acid, such as fresh lemon juice or red wine vinegar, in a small bowl or mason jar. Add a teaspoon of Dijon mustard to act as an emulsifier, binding the liquids smoothly together. Season with finely minced garlic, kosher salt, black pepper, and a touch of honey for balance. Whisk vigorously or shake the jar until the mixture becomes creamy and fully integrated.
