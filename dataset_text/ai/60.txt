The Grand Canyon, carved over millions of years by the relentless flow of the Colorado River, is one of the world's most impressive geological wonders. Reaching depths of over a mile and stretching 277 miles long, the canyon exposes distinct bands of colorful sedimentary rock layers that reveal billions of years of Earth's geological history. Visitors from around the world flock to its rims to hike steep trails, experience whitewater rafting, and witness dramatic light shifts across the canyon walls at sunrise and sunset, making it an iconic natural treasure.
