Sauron was a very powerful being in Middle-earth, the fictional world in which the Lord of the Rings takes place. He was a Maia, which were a race of powerful spirits created by the godlike being known as Eru. Sauron was once a servant of the godlike Valar, but he became corrupted and turned to evil. Sauron was able to exert a great deal of control over others through the use of the One Ring, a powerful artifact that he created. The One Ring had the power to enslave the wills of those who wore it, and Sauron used it to dominate the other races of Middle-earth. Sauron was defeated the first time around when the Ring was cut off his finger by Isildur, a human warrior. However, Sauron was not killed, and he remained a formidable enemy even without the Ring. He was able to rebuild his power over time and became a great threat once again, which is why everyone was afraid of him.
