If the Earth did not have a moon, the oceans would be very different from what they are now. The moon has a strong gravitational pull on the Earth, which causes the tides. The tides are the rise and fall of the water level in the oceans. They happen twice a day and are caused by the moon's gravity pulling on the Earth's water. Without the moon, there would be no tides. 
The tides are important because they help to mix the oceans and keep them from getting too stagnant. Without the tides, the oceans would be much less dynamic and there might be less movement of water, which could affect the temperature and currents in the oceans. 
It's difficult to say exactly how the oceans would be without the moon, but they would likely be very different from what we know today.
