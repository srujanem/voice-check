Browsers are software programs that you can use to access and view websites on the internet. Most browsers are free to download and use, so they don't directly make money by charging people to use them. Instead, many browsers, like Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox, make money through advertising and partnerships. For example, Google might pay a company to show an advertisement in Chrome, or Mozilla might partner with a company to make their browser the default choice for that company's products. In addition, some browsers, like Chrome, offer additional services that users can pay for, such as cloud storage or a virtual private network (VPN). Overall, browsers make money by showing advertisements, partnering with companies, and offering paid services to users.
