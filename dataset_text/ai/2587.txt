It's not safe to refreeze meats because when meat is thawed, bacteria that may have been present before freezing can multiply. If the meat is then refrozen, the bacteria will still be present and could cause food poisoning if the meat is eaten. When vegetables are thawed, they may become soft, but they do not contain the bacteria that meat does, so it is generally safe to refreeze vegetables. It's important to handle meat safely to prevent the risk of food poisoning. This means keeping raw meat separate from other foods, cooking it to the right temperature, and storing it properly in the fridge or freezer.
