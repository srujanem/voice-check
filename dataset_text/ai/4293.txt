Large corporate stores often have security measures in place to prevent theft, such as surveillance cameras and loss prevention staff. These measures can help them to identify when someone takes an item without paying for it. For example, if someone takes a packet of gum from a supermarket, the security cameras may have recorded the person taking the gum, and loss prevention staff may be able to review the footage to identify who did it.\n\nIt's also possible that the store has electronic scanners at the checkout registers that can track what items are being purchased. If the scanner registers show that someone didn't pay for an item, the store may be able to determine that it was stolen.\n\nIt's important to note that stealing, even small items like a packet of gum, is against the law and can result in consequences such as being banned from the store or facing criminal charges. It's always best to pay for items that you take from a store, rather than trying to steal them.
