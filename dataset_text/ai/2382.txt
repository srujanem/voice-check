Dreaming is a natural part of the sleep process. It's a time when your brain is very active, but your body is relaxed and still. During a dream, your brain creates images and stories in your mind, kind of like a movie. Some people remember their dreams more easily than others. Some people might remember their dreams every day, while others might only remember them once in a while. It's completely normal to have trouble remembering your dreams, and it doesn't mean there's anything wrong with you. Sometimes, people just have a harder time remembering their dreams because they don't pay attention to them when they wake up, or because they don't get enough deep sleep.
