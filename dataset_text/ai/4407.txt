Quenching refers to the process of rapidly cooling hot steel to harden it. Hot oil is better for quenching steel than cold oil because it can absorb more heat from the hot steel.\n\nImagine that you are trying to cool down a hot cup of tea by blowing on it. It will take a long time to cool down because the air can only take away a small amount of heat at a time. Now imagine that you are trying to cool down the hot tea by pouring cold water into the cup. The cold water can absorb a lot more heat from the hot tea, so the tea will cool down much faster.\n\nIt's the same with quenching steel. Hot oil can absorb more heat from the hot steel, so it can cool the steel down faster than cold oil. This is important because the faster the steel is cooled, the harder it will become.
