In films and TV shows, cutting all the wires on a bomb is often portrayed as a way to defuse the bomb and prevent it from exploding. However, this is not always the case in real life. In reality, bombs can be designed in many different ways and can be rigged with various types of triggers and fail-safes. Therefore, it is not always possible to simply cut all the wires and disable the bomb. Additionally, cutting the wrong wire or wires on a bomb could potentially cause it to explode. Bomb technicians, or people trained to disarm bombs, use specialized equipment and techniques to carefully and safely disarm bombs. They follow specific procedures and protocols to ensure their safety and the safety of others. So in short, cutting all the wires on a bomb is not always the correct or safe way to disarm it, and in real life, bomb technicians follow specific procedures and protocols to safely disarm bombs.
