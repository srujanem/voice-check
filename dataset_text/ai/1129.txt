It is generally best to try to keep a severely wounded person awake. This is because if someone becomes unconscious, it can be difficult to know if they are okay or if they need medical help. When someone is awake, they can tell you if they are in pain or feeling sick. They can also tell you if they are having trouble breathing or if something doesn't feel right. Keeping a severely wounded person awake can also help them stay alert and aware of their surroundings, which can be important in case they need to move or be transported to a hospital. It's important to be careful when trying to keep a severely wounded person awake, however. You should not try to shake or startle them, as this can cause further harm. Instead, try gently talking to them and asking them questions to keep them alert. If you are unable to keep the person awake or if they show signs of worsening injury, it is important to call for emergency medical assistance right away.
