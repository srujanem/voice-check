Soda can make you feel dehydrated because it contains a lot of sugar and caffeine, which can increase your need to urinate. When you urinate more, you can lose fluids more quickly, which can make you feel dehydrated. Carbonated water, which is what soda is typically made with, is just water that has been infused with carbon dioxide gas to give it a fizzy or bubbly texture. The carbonation itself does not cause dehydration. However, some people may feel more thirsty after drinking carbonated beverages because the gas can make your mouth and throat feel dry. So, even though soda contains water, the sugar and caffeine in it can cause you to lose more fluids and make you feel dehydrated.
