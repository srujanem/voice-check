A paradox is a statement or situation that seems to be contradictory or absurd but may actually be true. Here are a few examples: This statement is a lie. If the statement is true, then it is actually not a lie. But if the statement is not a lie, then it must be true. This creates a paradox because we don't know which is true. I am lying. If the person saying this is actually lying, then they are telling the truth. But if they are telling the truth, then they are not lying. Again, this creates a paradox because we don't know which is true. The Barber of Seville is a famous paradox about a barber who only shaves the men in a town who do not shave themselves. The question is: does the barber shave himself? If he does, then he doesn't shave himself because he only shaves the men who don't shave themselves. If he doesn't shave himself, then he does shave himself because he is a barber and shaves men. Paradoxes can be confusing and difficult to understand, but they can also be interesting and thought-provoking.
