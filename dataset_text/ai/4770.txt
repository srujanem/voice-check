In the United States, social collar colors are typically associated with different types of employment or social status. Here is a brief explanation of some common social collar colors:\n\nWhite collar: White collar workers are typically office workers or professionals who do not typically work with their hands. They may work in factories, construction, or other types of manual labor. They often wear uniforms or protective gear while working.\n\nGreen collar: Green collar workers are typically involved in environmentally-focused industries, such as renewable energy or conservation.\n\nThere are no strict rules about what colors represent different types of employment or social status, and the meanings of different collar colors can vary. Additionally, some people may use different collar colors to refer to different things altogether.
