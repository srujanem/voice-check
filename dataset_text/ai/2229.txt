Butterflies in your stomach is a feeling that people often describe when they are excited or nervous about something. It's a feeling that you might get when you start falling in love with someone, or when you're about to do something that makes you a little bit nervous, like giving a speech or going on a roller coaster. Imagine that there are little butterflies fluttering around inside your stomach. That's what it feels like when you have "butterflies in your stomach." It's a feeling of excitement and nervousness all mixed together. Some people might feel their heart beating faster, or their palms getting sweaty when they have this feeling. It's a normal and common feeling to have when you're excited about something or when you're a little bit nervous.
