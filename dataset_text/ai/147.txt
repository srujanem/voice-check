Soap operas are instantly identifiable for a few reasons. One reason is that they often have a specific visual style. Soap operas tend to use close-up shots of actors' faces to show emotions and reactions, and they often have dramatic or romantic music playing in the background. This helps create a sense of drama and tension. 
Another reason is that soap operas tend to have a specific type of storytelling. Soap operas are known for having long, ongoing storylines that unfold over the course of many episodes or even years. They often feature a large cast of characters who are interconnected and have complicated relationships with each other. This can make it easy to recognize a soap opera even if you haven't seen it before. 
Finally, soap operas are often identified by their genre. Soap operas are a type of drama that focuses on the personal and professional lives of characters who are usually in their 20s to 40s and are often upper-middle class or wealthy. This can make them stand out from other types of television shows, which might focus on different characters or topics.
