It is a problem that exists in many countries, not just the United States. The commission is responsible for reviewing the boundaries of electoral districts and making recommendations for changes based on factors such as population changes and community interests.\n\nIn Europe, many countries also have independent or nonpartisan bodies that are responsible for drawing electoral district boundaries.
