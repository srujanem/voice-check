There are a few reasons why you might want to consider replacing your box spring when you replace your mattress. 
First, the box spring provides support for your mattress. It helps to evenly distribute the weight of your body and the mattress across the bed frame. If the box spring is worn out or damaged, it may not be able to provide the necessary support for your new mattress. This could cause the mattress to wear out more quickly or be less comfortable to sleep on. 
Second, a new box spring can help to extend the life of your new mattress. When you sleep on a mattress, it naturally compresses and expands. Over time, this can cause the mattress to become uneven or sag in certain areas. A new box spring can help to prevent this by providing a more stable surface for the mattress to rest on. This can help the mattress to maintain its shape and support for longer. 
Finally, if you are upgrading to a new mattress with different features or a different level of firmness, you may want to consider a new box spring to match. Different types of box springs may be better suited to certain types of mattresses. For example, a firmer box spring may be more suitable for a softer mattress, while a softer box spring may be better for a firmer mattress. 
In summary, replacing your box spring when you replace your mattress can help to provide the necessary support and stability for the mattress, extend its lifespan, and ensure that it is as comfortable as possible.
