Banks typically do not decide how much money is dispensed in each denomination when customers make withdrawals. This is because the amount and denominations of bills dispensed is determined by the cash management system of the bank, which is designed to optimize the balance of cash on hand and reduce the cost of managing that cash. 
The cash management system takes into account various factors, such as the amount of money being withdrawn, the availability of different denominations, and the expected demand for cash in the future. Based on this information, the system determines the optimal mix of denominations to dispense. 
In some cases, the system may decide to dispense a higher number of $20 bills because it has determined that this will be the most efficient way to meet the customer's request while also maintaining an optimal balance of cash on hand. 
It is also worth noting that the availability of different denominations may be limited at any given time due to factors such as the amount of cash being held in the bank's vault or the amount of cash being transported to or from the bank. This could also affect the denominations of bills dispensed.
