When you move your cursor to the right or left of the screen, it disappears because your computer is programmed to keep the cursor within the boundaries of the screen. If the cursor were allowed to move beyond the edges of the screen, it would be difficult to use and control. 
Think of the screen as a big picture frame. The cursor is like a toy car that you're driving around inside the picture frame. If you try to drive the toy car off the edge of the picture frame, it will disappear from view. But if you keep the toy car inside the picture frame, you can see it and control where it goes. That's why the cursor disappears when you move it to the right or left of the screen, but stops at the edge when you move it up or down.
