Chemotherapy is a type of treatment that uses powerful medications to kill cancer cells. One side effect of chemotherapy is hair loss, because the medications used can also harm healthy cells in the body, including cells in the hair follicles. The hair on your head, eyebrows, and eyelashes are all made up of cells that are constantly growing and dividing. Chemotherapy can cause these cells to stop growing and dividing, which can lead to hair loss. Eyebrows and eyelashes may not be affected as much because they are made up of smaller, finer hairs that may be less sensitive to the chemotherapy. I hope that helps!
