When you're driving a car and you hit the brakes, the brakes apply a force to the wheels, which slows the car down. When the car stops, it has a certain amount of energy, or momentum, because it was moving. When you let go of the brakes, the car's momentum can cause it to continue moving forward for a short time, even though you're not pressing on the gas pedal. It's kind of like when you push a swing and it keeps swinging back and forth for a while after you stop pushing it. The swing has momentum because you gave it energy by pushing it, and it continues moving because of that energy.
