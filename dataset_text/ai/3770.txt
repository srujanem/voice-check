Sure! DNA is a special code inside the cells of living things that tells the cells what to do. It's kind of like a recipe book for building and running your body. Every living thing has its own special DNA code, which makes it different from other living things. DNA is made up of really tiny parts called "nucleotides." There are four different types of nucleotides in DNA, and they are usually abbreviated A, C, G, and T. The order of these nucleotides in DNA is like a secret code, and it tells the cells what to do and how to grow. DNA is found in almost every cell in your body, and it's what makes you unique. It's kind of like your own special blueprint that tells your body how to be you!
