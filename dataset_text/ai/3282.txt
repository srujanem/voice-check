The internet is a global network of computers that are connected to each other. It allows computers to communicate with each other and exchange information. The World Wide Web (also known as the "web") is a way of accessing information on the internet. It is a collection of documents, images, and other resources that are stored on servers (computers that store information) around the world, and are connected to each other using hyperlinks. Think of the internet as a giant network of roads, and the World Wide Web as a way of traveling on those roads to get to different places. The internet is the infrastructure that allows the web to exist, but the web is the way that we access and interact with the information that is stored on the internet. I hope this helps!
