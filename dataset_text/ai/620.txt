The Charlie Hebdo shooting was a tragic event that happened in Paris, France on January 7, 2015. On that day, two men armed with guns entered the offices of a magazine called Charlie Hebdo, which is known for publishing cartoons and articles that are sometimes critical of certain groups or religions. The men started shooting, and in the end, 12 people were killed and 11 others were injured. 
Many people were very upset and shocked by this event, and there were protests and demonstrations in cities around the world to show support for the victims and to stand up for the right to freedom of speech. The men who carried out the shooting were later caught by the police and punished for their crimes. 
This event was a very sad and terrible thing that happened, and it reminds us of the importance of trying to understand and respect each other, even if we have different opinions or beliefs.
