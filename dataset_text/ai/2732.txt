Dead voter fraud occurs when someone casts a vote in the name of a deceased person. This can happen in a few different ways. One way is if someone fraudulently uses the personal information of a deceased person to register to vote and then casts a vote in that person's name. Another way is if someone who is responsible for handling the voting process, such as an election official, intentionally allows a vote to be cast in the name of a deceased person. It's important to note that this type of fraud is rare and is generally not a significant problem in elections. Elections are typically run by officials who are trained to follow procedures to ensure the integrity of the voting process and prevent fraud.
