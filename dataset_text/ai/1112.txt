Google Maps is free to use because it is supported by advertisements. When you use Google Maps, you might see ads for local businesses or other services. These ads help Google generate revenue, which they can use to pay for the expenses of running and maintaining Google Maps. 
Google is a large company that makes money in many different ways. In addition to showing ads in its products like Google Maps, Google also generates revenue from other sources, such as the sale of products and services, the sale of advertising on its search engine and other websites, and from the sale of hardware products like smartphones and laptops. 
By using the revenue it generates from these various sources, Google is able to offer its products and services, like Google Maps, for free to users. This is a way for Google to attract more users to its products and services, which can then help it generate more revenue through advertising and other means.
