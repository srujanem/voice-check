Your car's engine is designed to run on a certain type of fuel, and using a different type of fuel can cause problems. If your car requires premium fuel, it means that it is designed to run on a higher-octane fuel that can better withstand the heat and pressure inside the engine. When you use regular gasoline in a car that requires premium fuel, the engine may not be able to burn the fuel properly and can cause it to shut off or malfunction. Think of it like this: different types of fuel are like different types of food. Just like how some people need to eat a special type of food because they have allergies or sensitivities, your car's engine needs a certain type of fuel to run properly. If you give it the wrong type of fuel, it won't work as well and could even cause problems.
