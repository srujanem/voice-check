Atlanta is not actually in the middle of Georgia, it is located in the north of the state. It is not on the coast because it is not near the ocean. Instead, it is inland, which means that it is not near the coast. This is why it is not like other major Eastern US cities that are located on the coast, like Philadelphia, Baltimore, and Washington D.C. However, even though Atlanta is not on the coast, it is still a very important city and is the capital of Georgia.
