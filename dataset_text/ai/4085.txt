Your ability to blur your vision on demand is due to the muscles in your eyes, called the ciliary muscles. These muscles allow you to change the shape of your lens, which helps you focus on objects that are near or far away. When you want to blur your vision, you can try relaxing these muscles, which will cause the lens to become less curved and your vision to become more blurry. This is similar to when you look at something far away and your lens becomes less curved, which helps you see things that are farther away more clearly.
