Time dilation is a phenomenon that occurs when two objects are in relative motion to each other. It means that time appears to pass at different rates for different objects, depending on their relative speeds. To understand this, let's imagine that you are standing on a platform and your friend is standing on a train that is moving very fast past the platform. If you look at your friend, it will seem to you that they are moving very quickly, and that time is passing more slowly for them than it is for you. This is because, from your perspective, your friend's movements are slowed down, and so it appears to you that time is passing more slowly for them. But from your friend's perspective, it is you who are moving, and it is your movements that are slowed down. From their perspective, it is you who are aging more slowly, because time appears to be passing more quickly for them. So, to sum up, time dilation is all about how time appears to pass at different rates for different objects, depending on their relative speeds. And this is due to the fact that, from different perspectives, different objects will appear to be moving, and it is the movement of these objects that affects how quickly time appears to pass.
