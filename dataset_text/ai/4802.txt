Barcodes and QR codes are simply a way of storing and communicating information. They are made up of a series of lines and dots that can be read by a scanner or a camera. The information that is stored in a barcode or QR code is just a series of numbers or letters, similar to the words and numbers in a book.\n\nA virus is a piece of software that is designed to cause harm to a computer or device. It can do things like delete files, steal information, or take control of the device. In order to run a virus, it needs to be loaded onto the computer or device and then executed by the operating system.\n\nBarcodes and QR codes do not have the ability to execute software or run a virus. They can only store and communicate information. So, even if a virus was somehow encoded into a barcode or QR code, it would not be able to run or cause any harm unless it was first loaded onto a computer or device and then run by the operating system.
