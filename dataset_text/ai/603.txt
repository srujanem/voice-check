In general, it is not considered animal cruelty to kill insects because insects are not considered to have the same capacity for suffering as animals like mammals and birds. This is because insects do not have the same level of brain development and consciousness as these animals. However, it is still important to be mindful of and respectful towards all living creatures, including insects, and to avoid causing unnecessary suffering. Some people also choose to avoid killing insects whenever possible, and instead try to find ways to peacefully coexist with them or to remove them from a space without harming them.
