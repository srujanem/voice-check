Shipping Ramen Noodles can be profitable for a company because they can sell the noodles at a higher price than what it costs them to produce and ship them. The cost of the driver and gas is just one part of the expenses that the company has to pay in order to get the noodles to customers. There are also costs associated with producing the noodles, packaging them, and advertising the product. When a company is able to sell the noodles for more than it costs to produce and ship them, it can make a profit. 
To understand this better, let's use an example. Imagine a company makes a batch of 100 Ramen Noodles and it costs them $1 to produce each noodle. This means it costs the company $100 to make the batch of noodles. Now, let's say it costs the company $5 to ship a batch of noodles to a customer. This means it will cost the company a total of $105 to produce and ship the batch of noodles. If the company can sell the noodles for $110, they will make a profit of $5. 
So, even though the cost of the driver and gas may seem high, if the company is able to sell the noodles for a higher price, they can still make a profit. This is how businesses work - they produce a product or service, and sell it for a price that is higher than the cost of producing and delivering it, in order to make a profit.
