As a general rule, you are not able to choose where your tax dollars go because the government uses tax money to fund a wide variety of programs and services that benefit the entire population. These programs and services are decided by elected officials who are chosen by the people to represent their interests and make decisions on their behalf. While you may have specific ideas about how you would like your tax dollars to be used, it is ultimately up to the government to determine the best way to use the money for the benefit of the country as a whole.
