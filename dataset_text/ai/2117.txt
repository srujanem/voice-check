Graduate school is a type of school that people go to after they have finished college. It is usually for people who want to get a higher degree, like a Master's degree or a Doctorate. To get into graduate school, people usually need to have a bachelor's degree and good grades. They also need to take a test called the GRE (Graduate Record Exam) and write an essay called a personal statement. In graduate school, people take classes and do research on a specific subject they are interested in. They also might work as a teaching assistant or a research assistant to help pay for their education. Graduate school is different from being an undergraduate because it is more advanced and the classes are usually more difficult. Graduate students also have more freedom to choose what they want to study and do more independent research.
