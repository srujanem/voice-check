In math, division is the process of dividing one number (the dividend) by another number (the divisor) to find out how many times the divisor goes into the dividend. For example, when we divide 10 by 2, we are finding out how many times 2 goes into 10. The answer is 5, which means that 2 goes into 10 five times.\n\nIn the expression "5/5," the dividend is 5 and the divisor is also 5. When we divide 5 by 5, we are finding out how many times 5 goes into 5. The answer is 1, which means that 5 goes into 5 one time. That's why 5/5 is equal to 1.\n\nOn the other hand, the expression "0/0" is a little bit different. In this case, both the dividend and the divisor are 0, which means that we are trying to find out how many times 0 goes into 0. However, it doesn't make sense to talk about how many times one number goes into another when both numbers are 0, because any number divided by 0 is undefined.\n\nSo, in math, we say that 0/0 is undefined. This is different from saying that 0/0 is equal to 1, or any other number for that matter.
