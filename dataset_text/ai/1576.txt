Traffic jams in big cities can start for a variety of reasons. Here are a few common causes: 
1. Too many cars on the road: If there are more cars on the road than the road can handle, it can lead to a traffic jam. 
2. Accidents or other incidents: If there is an accident or some other incident on the road, it can cause a traffic jam as cars slow down or come to a stop to avoid the incident. 
3. Road construction or lane closures: If a road is under construction or a lane is closed, it can cause a traffic jam as cars are forced to merge into fewer lanes. 
4. Sudden stops: If a car in front of you suddenly slows down or stops, it can cause a traffic jam as other cars also slow down or stop to avoid a collision. 
5. Congestion at intersections: If there are a lot of cars trying to turn at the same time, it can cause a traffic jam as cars have to wait their turn. 
Traffic jams can happen at the same time every day because many people have predictable schedules, such as commuting to and from work at the same times every day. This can lead to more cars on the road at the same time, which can cause a traffic jam.
