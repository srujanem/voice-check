AC current is created when an electrical current is alternating back and forth, changing direction periodically. In an AC circuit, the current flows in one direction for a short time, then reverses and flows in the opposite direction. This happens over and over again, many times per second.\n\nEven though the electrons in the current are constantly changing direction, they still end up moving from one place to another. Imagine a group of people standing in a circle, holding hands and passing a ball back and forth. Even though the ball is constantly changing hands and moving in different directions, it is still traveling around the circle. Similarly, even though the electrons in an AC current are constantly changing direction, they are still moving through the circuit and transferring energy from one place to another.\n\nI hope this helps to clarify things!
