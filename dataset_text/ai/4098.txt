Ice works well to remove blood stains because it helps to solidify the blood, making it easier to remove. When blood is in liquid form, it can be more difficult to clean up because it can spread and get absorbed into fabrics. But when you apply ice to a blood stain, the cold temperature causes the blood to become solid, or "freeze," making it easier to scrape off or wipe away. Once the frozen blood is removed, you can then treat the stain with a stain remover or laundry detergent to help remove any remaining residue. Does that make sense?
