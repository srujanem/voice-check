Circlejerking on Reddit refers to a situation where a group of users engage in repetitive, self-congratulatory behavior, often in the form of posting and upvoting content that aligns with their own views or beliefs. This can create a " echo chamber " effect, where the same ideas are reinforced and discussed among the group, rather than engaging with differing viewpoints or having a meaningful exchange of ideas. It can also be used to mock or ridicule a group of users who engage in this behavior. The term "circlejerk" is often used as a pejorative to criticize this type of behavior.
