Dreams are like stories that our brain makes up while we sleep. Sometimes these stories can be really strange or confusing, and other times they can be about things that have happened to us in real life. When we wake up, our brain is no longer in the same state it was in when we were dreaming, and so it can be hard to remember the details of the dream. Just like it can be hard to remember a movie or a book that we saw or read a long time ago, it can be hard to remember our dreams if we don't write them down or try to remember them right after we wake up. So, it's normal to forget our dreams a few minutes after we wake up.
