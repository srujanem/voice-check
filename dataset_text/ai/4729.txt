Military camouflage is a type of clothing or other material that is designed to help soldiers blend in with their surroundings. This can be especially important for snipers, who need to hide in order to get a good shot. Camouflage works by using patterns and colors that help to break up the outline of a person's body, making them harder to see against the background.\n\nFor example, if a soldier is wearing camouflage in a forest, the clothing might have green and brown patterns on it to match the trees and leaves. This can help the soldier blend in with the environment and be less visible to the enemy.\n\nHunters can also use camouflage to help them blend in with their surroundings while hunting. For example, a hunter might wear camouflage that has patterns of leaves and branches on it to help them blend in with the trees and bushes when hunting deer.\n\nOverall, camouflage works by using patterns and colors that help to blend in with the environment, making it harder for people or animals to spot someone who is wearing it.
