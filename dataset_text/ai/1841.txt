DotA, which stands for Defense of the Ancients, is a custom map or game mode for the real-time strategy game Warcraft 3. In Warcraft 3, players can choose from a variety of different maps and game modes to play, and DotA is one of those options. In DotA, two teams of players face off against each other with the goal of destroying the other team's "Ancient," a large building located at the heart of their base. Each player controls a single hero character with unique abilities, and the objective is to work together with your team to defeat the enemy heroes and destroy their Ancient. Warcraft 3 is the overall game that DotA is played within, while DotA is a specific type of game mode within Warcraft 3.
