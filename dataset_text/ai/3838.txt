Bottled water doesn't necessarily go "bad" in the same way that food does, but it can still go stale or develop an off taste or smell over time. This is because even though water itself doesn't spoil, it can pick up odors and flavors from its surroundings or from the materials that the bottle is made of. For example, if the water is stored in a place with strong odors, it can absorb those odors and taste or smell unpleasant. The expiration date on bottled water is generally set as a precaution to ensure that the water is of the best quality when you drink it. It's just a way for the manufacturer to guarantee that the water is fresh and has not been sitting around too long.
