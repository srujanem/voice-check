Radio stations play the same songs over and over again for a few reasons. One reason is that they want to appeal to a certain audience. For example, a radio station that plays mostly pop music is likely trying to appeal to people who like that type of music. By playing the same popular songs over and over again, they can attract more listeners who will tune in to hear their favorite songs. 
Another reason that radio stations play the same songs is that it helps them make money. When a song is played on the radio, the artist and record label that produced the song get paid. So, the more a song is played, the more money the artist and record label make. Radio stations also make money by selling advertisements, and they want to attract as many listeners as possible so that they can sell more ads. By playing the same popular songs, they can attract more listeners and make more money. 
Finally, radio stations play the same songs because it's easier for them to plan their programming. If they played a different set of songs every hour, it would be more difficult for them to schedule their programming and make sure that they have enough music to fill their airtime. By playing the same songs over and over again, they can have a more predictable schedule and know exactly what songs they will be playing at any given time.
