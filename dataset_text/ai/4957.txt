Painkillers, also called analgesics, work by blocking the signals that tell your brain that you're in pain. Different painkillers work in different ways, and some are better at relieving certain types of pain than others. For example, acetaminophen (such as Tylenol) can help with mild to moderate pain, but it might not be as effective at relieving severe or chronic pain. Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), such as ibuprofen and naproxen, can help with inflammation and pain.
