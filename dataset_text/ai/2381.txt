Imgur is a popular image hosting service that is often used by people on Reddit because it is easy to use and provides a lot of features for free. When you want to share an image on Reddit, you can upload it to Imgur and then post the link to the image on Reddit. Imgur will automatically resize the image so that it fits in the subreddit's post, and it also provides a direct link to the image that you can use to share it on other websites. Additionally, Imgur has a large community of users who can comment on and upvote the images that they like, which makes it a good place to discover new content.
