LSD and psilocybin (found in "shrooms" or magic mushrooms) are both drugs that can cause people to see and experience things that aren't real. When people take these drugs, they can see bright colors and patterns, hear strange noises, and feel things that aren't really there. They may also have a hard time telling what is real and what is not. In the brain, these drugs work by changing the way certain chemicals called neurotransmitters work. Neurotransmitters are chemicals that help brain cells communicate with each other. LSD and psilocybin can change the way these chemicals work, which can cause the brain to send strange signals to the rest of the body. This is what causes the strange sensations and perceptions that people experience when they take these drugs. It's important to remember that these drugs are not safe for everyone to take and can have serious side effects. It's always a good idea to talk to a doctor or other trusted adult before taking any kind of drug.
