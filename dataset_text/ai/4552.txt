A time signature is a symbol that tells you how many beats are in each measure of music and what kind of note gets one beat. It looks like a fraction, with two numbers one on top of the other. The number on the top tells you how many beats are in each measure, and the number on the bottom tells you what kind of note gets one beat. For example, a time signature of 4/4 means that there are four beats in each measure and a quarter note gets one beat.
