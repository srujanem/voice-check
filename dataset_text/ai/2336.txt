Cables tangle so quickly and easily because they are made of thin, flexible material that can easily twist and turn around each other. When you move a cable around or put it in a bag or drawer, it can get tangled up with other cables or objects. To prevent cables from tangling, you can try using cable ties or clips to keep them organized and in place. You can also try using a cable organizer or storage case to keep your cables separate and untangled. It can also help to make sure you wrap the cables neatly and carefully when you're not using them. Think of cables like spaghetti noodles – if you throw them all in a pile, they'll get tangled up, but if you put them in a bowl and keep them separate, they won't tangle as easily.
