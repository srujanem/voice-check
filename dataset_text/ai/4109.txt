There are a few reasons why computer processors (also called CPUs) haven't been able to keep growing in power at the same rate as other components. One reason is that CPUs are getting very small and it is becoming more difficult to make them smaller and more powerful. Another reason is that as CPUs get more powerful, they also produce more heat, which can be a problem. Finally, there are physical limits to how fast a processor can go. Even though we might be able to make a processor that runs at, say, 5 GHz, it might not actually be any faster than a processor that runs at 3.8 GHz because of the way that CPUs work.
