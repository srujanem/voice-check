The warning light for a malfunctioning headlight or brake light is actually a required safety feature on all modern vehicles. If one of these lights is not functioning properly, the vehicle's computer system will detect the issue and turn on a warning light on the dashboard to alert the driver.\n\nThe reason you may not have noticed this warning light before is because it is often easy to overlook, especially if you are not paying close attention to your dashboard while driving. Additionally, some vehicles may have multiple warning lights on the dashboard, so it is possible that the warning light for a malfunctioning headlight or brake light could be hidden among other warning lights.\n\nIt is important to pay attention to all warning lights on your dashboard, as they are designed to alert you to potential issues with your vehicle. If you see a warning light, it is a good idea to have your vehicle checked by a mechanic as soon as possible to ensure that it is safe to drive.
