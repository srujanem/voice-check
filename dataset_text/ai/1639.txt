Sure! Wind is the movement of air across the Earth's surface. It happens because the Earth is heated by the sun, which causes the air near the surface to warm up and rise. As the warm air rises, it leaves a space behind it, which is then filled by cooler air from other parts of the Earth. This movement of air from one place to another is what we call wind. To make blood, your body needs to have a special type of cell called red blood cells. Red blood cells are made in your bone marrow, which is the spongy tissue inside your bones. Your bone marrow contains special cells called stem cells, which can turn into different types of cells, including red blood cells. When your body needs more red blood cells, it will tell the stem cells to turn into red blood cells and enter your bloodstream. Red blood cells are important because they carry oxygen from your lungs to the rest of your body.
