The Internet is a vast network of interconnected computers and servers that can communicate with each other. When you send an email, it travels from your computer to a server that is connected to the Internet. From there, the email is sent through a series of interconnected servers, kind of like a relay race. Each server passes the email along to the next server until it reaches its destination. So if you send an email from the United States to Japan, the email would first travel to a server in the US that is connected to the Internet. Then it would be passed along to servers in other countries, such as Canada or Russia, until it finally reaches a server in Japan. The servers that the email travels through are connected to each other using a variety of methods, including wires, cables, and even satellites. These connections allow the email to travel from one place to another very quickly, even if the two places are on opposite sides of the world. I hope that helps to explain it!
