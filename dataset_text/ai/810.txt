Other people hear your voice how it sounds to them. When you hear your own voice, you are hearing the sound waves that your vocal cords produce traveling through your skull and into your inner ear. This is different from how other people hear your voice, which is the sound waves that your vocal cords produce traveling through the air and into their ears. 
When you listen to a recording of your voice, it may sound different from how you hear it in your head because the sound waves are being captured and played back through a device, rather than traveling directly through your skull and into your inner ear. 
So, in summary, other people hear your voice how it sounds to them, and a recording of your voice may sound different from how you hear it in your head.
