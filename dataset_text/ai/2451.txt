Copper is a good conductor because it has a lot of free electrons, which are particles that are able to move freely through the material. When electricity flows through a conductor, it is actually the movement of these free electrons that allows the electricity to flow. Copper has more free electrons than many other materials, which makes it a better conductor. This is why it is often used in electrical wires and other applications where good conductivity is important.
