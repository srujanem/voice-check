Hotel California is a popular song by the band The Eagles. It was released in 1976 and has since become one of their most well-known and beloved songs. The lyrics describe a person's stay at a hotel called "Hotel California," which is known for being a place where people can check in, but never leave. The song uses figurative language to describe the hotel as a place where people go to escape from their problems and live in luxury, but eventually become trapped and unable to leave. The lyrics suggest that the hotel is a metaphor for the glamour and excess of the music industry, and the dangers of getting caught up in that lifestyle.
