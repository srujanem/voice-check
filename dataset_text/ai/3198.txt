Sure! Fire needs three things to burn: fuel, oxygen, and heat. When you pour water on a fire, the water absorbs a lot of heat. This causes the water to turn into steam, which takes up a lot of space and pushes the air out of the way. When the fire doesn't have enough oxygen, it can't burn. So when you pour water on a fire, you're taking away one of the things it needs to keep burning. This is why water is such a good way to put out a fire. It's also why you should never pour water on a grease fire - water can't mix with oil or grease, so it just spreads the fire instead of putting it out. Does that make sense?
