Planets orbit on a horizontal plane because of the way they were formed. When the solar system was first forming, a disk of gas and dust surrounded the young sun. As this disk cooled, bits of dust and ice stuck together to form bigger and bigger clumps. These clumps eventually became the planets and other bodies we see today. Because the disk was flat and horizontal, the planets all formed in a plane around the sun. As a result, the planets all orbit on a horizontal plane around the sun, rather than going every which way. This is why, when we look up at the sky, we see the planets moving across the sky in a relatively straight line, rather than zigzagging all over the place. I hope that helps!
