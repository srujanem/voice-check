Copyright law exists to encourage creativity and innovation by giving creators exclusive control over their works for a limited time. This means that creators can benefit financially from their creations and have the incentive to keep creating new and exciting works. If copyrights never expired, it would be difficult for new creators to enter the market and for society to benefit from new creations. Additionally, once a copyright expires, the work enters the public domain and can be freely used by anyone. This allows new creators to build upon the work of those who came before them, leading to even more creativity and innovation. So, in short, copyrights expire because it helps encourage new creations and promotes the progress of society.
