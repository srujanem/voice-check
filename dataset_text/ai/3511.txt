Horses need shoes because they are often used for activities that can be hard on their hooves. For example, horses used for riding or for pulling carriages or plows may be required to travel long distances or perform tasks that put a lot of strain on their hooves. Shoes help protect their hooves from wear and tear and can help prevent injuries. Cows, on the other hand, do not typically need shoes because they are not typically used for activities that are hard on their hooves. Cows spend most of their time grazing in pastures or barns, which does not put a lot of strain on their hooves. In addition, cows' hooves are naturally tougher and more resistant to wear and tear than those of horses. So, in short, horses need shoes because they do activities that can be hard on their hooves, while cows do not typically need shoes because they do not do activities that are hard on their hooves.
