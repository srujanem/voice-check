Diesel engines do tend to be more fuel efficient than gasoline engines, and they can offer better mileage per gallon (MPG). However, there are a few reasons why diesel engines are not commonly used in hybrid vehicles like the Toyota Prius. One reason is that diesel fuel is not as widely available as gasoline, especially in some countries. This means that it might be more inconvenient for drivers to find diesel fuel, which could be a drawback for hybrid vehicles that are designed to be as convenient and easy to use as possible. Another reason is that diesel engines produce more emissions, particularly nitrogen oxides (NOx), which can be harmful to the environment and contribute to air pollution. Hybrid vehicles like the Prius are designed to be as environmentally friendly as possible, so using a diesel engine might not align with this goal. Finally, diesel engines tend to be more expensive and complex to manufacture than gasoline engines, which could make hybrid vehicles that use diesel engines more expensive for consumers. Overall, while diesel engines can be more fuel efficient than gasoline engines, there are other considerations such as availability, emissions, and cost that have led Toyota and other car manufacturers to choose gasoline engines for hybrid vehicles like the Prius.
