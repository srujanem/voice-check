Fire is made up of three things: fuel, oxygen, and heat. When you light a fire, you're providing the heat. The fuel is something that can burn, like wood or paper. And oxygen is the air that we breathe. The visible part of the fire, the flames, are made up of hot gases. These hot gases are made up of tiny particles of the fuel that are burning, as well as other substances that are created when the fuel burns. The heat of the fire makes these gases rise up and mix with the air, creating the bright flames that we see. So, when you look at a fire, you're seeing the heat and light that are being produced by the burning fuel. The fuel is being turned into hot gases, and those gases are what you see as the flames.
