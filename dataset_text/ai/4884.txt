The blood stream delivers nutrients to the body through a process called circulation. The heart pumps blood throughout the body, and the blood vessels (arteries, veins, and capillaries) carry the blood to every part of the body.\n\nThe body needs nutrients like oxygen, glucose (sugar), and vitamins to function properly. When you eat food, your body breaks down the nutrients and puts them into the blood stream so they can be delivered to the cells that need them.\n\nThe blood vessels have special cells called endothelial cells that line the inside of the vessel. These cells have tiny gates called valves that can open and close to control the flow of blood. When a cell needs a nutrient, it sends a signal to the endothelial cells to open the valves and allow more blood to flow to that area.\n\nSo, in a way, the cells "tell" the blood stream where to deliver the nutrients by sending signals to the endothelial cells. The brain also plays a role in this process by controlling the heart rate and blood pressure, which helps to regulate the flow of blood throughout the body.\n\nI hope that helps to explain it!
