Cars do have an anti-rust coating! It's called "rustproofing," and it's a process that's designed to protect the metal parts of a car from corrosion, which is what happens when metal is exposed to water and oxygen and starts to break down and form rust. Rust can be a big problem for cars, especially in areas where there's a lot of snow, ice, and salt on the roads. These things can all contribute to the formation of rust, which can weaken the metal parts of a car and make them more prone to failure. 
There are a few different ways that car manufacturers and aftermarket companies can rustproof a car. One method is to apply a special coating to the metal parts of the car that helps to protect them from rust. Another method is to apply a special type of wax or oil to the metal parts that helps to seal out moisture and oxygen. Both of these methods can help to extend the life of a car and keep it running smoothly for longer, especially in areas where there's a lot of salt and other corrosive substances on the roads.
