When scientists write complicated math on a chalkboard, they are usually trying to solve a specific problem or understand a particular phenomenon. The math they use is a tool to help them do this. It's kind of like how a carpenter uses a hammer and nails to build a house. 
Sometimes, the scientists already know the answer to the problem they are working on, but they still need to use math to explain how they arrived at that answer. Other times, they may not know the answer yet and are using math to try to figure it out. In either case, the math is an important part of the scientific process and helps scientists make sense of the world around us.
