Magic Johnson is a retired professional basketball player who announced in 1991 that he was HIV positive. HIV stands for human immunodeficiency virus, and it is a virus that can weaken the immune system and make it harder for the body to fight off infections and diseases. However, thanks to advances in medical treatment, it is now possible for people with HIV to live long and healthy lives. 
There are medications called antiretroviral therapy (ART) that can help to keep the virus under control and prevent it from damaging the immune system. These medications are very effective at preventing the virus from replicating and spreading, and they can help people with HIV to maintain good health for many years. 
Magic Johnson has been taking ART medications since he was diagnosed with HIV, and these medications have likely played a major role in helping him to maintain good health. It is also important for people with HIV to take care of their overall health by eating a healthy diet, getting regular exercise, and getting medical care when needed. 
Overall, while HIV can be a serious and life-threatening condition, with proper treatment and care, it is possible for people with HIV to live long and healthy lives, just like anyone else.
