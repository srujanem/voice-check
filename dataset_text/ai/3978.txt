Software patents are a type of patent that cover new and innovative software programs. The issue with patent trolls is that they are individuals or companies who hold patents on software and other technologies, but do not actually create or sell any products. Instead, they make money by suing other companies who they claim are using their patented technology without permission. This can be a problem because it can be very expensive for companies to defend themselves against these lawsuits, and the patent trolls may not have a legitimate claim to the technology in the first place. In other words, patent trolls can use their patents to extort money from other companies, even if they are not actually entitled to it.
