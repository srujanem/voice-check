Time is a measure of how long events take to happen. It's what we use to order events and to compare how long things take. For example, we say that one event happened before or after another event. We also say that something takes a long time or a short time to happen. Time has always existed in some sense, but the way we understand and measure it has changed over time. Scientists believe that time began with the Big Bang, which is the event that marked the beginning of the universe as we know it. Before the Big Bang, it's not clear what happened or how time worked, if it even existed at all. Some scientists have proposed that time may have had a different meaning or may not have existed at all in the very early universe. So, to summarize, time is a way of measuring how long events take to happen and it has always existed in some sense, but the way we understand and measure it may have changed over time.
