On September 11, 2001, terrorists hijacked four planes and crashed them into the World Trade Center in New York City, the Pentagon in Washington, D.C., and a field in Pennsylvania. The attacks killed nearly 3,000 people and were a major tragedy for the United States. Some people think that 9/11 was an "inside job," meaning that they believe that the attacks were not carried out by terrorists, but rather were planned and carried out by people within the United States government or other powerful organizations. These people might believe this for a variety of reasons, such as because they think that certain events or actions taken by the government before or after the attacks seem suspicious or because they believe that the official explanation of what happened doesn't make sense. It's important to note that there is no credible evidence to support the idea that 9/11 was an inside job.
