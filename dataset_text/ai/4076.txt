The steel of the Titanic was strong, but it was not able to withstand the force of the iceberg. When the iceberg hit the ship, the steel was not able to bend enough to absorb the impact and instead, the force of the collision caused the steel to break. The welded joints of the ship were especially vulnerable to breaking because the welds were not as strong as the rest of the steel. So, when the iceberg hit the ship, the force of the collision caused the welded joints to break and the ship began to sink.
