Donnie Darko is a science fiction movie about a teenager named Donnie who experiences strange events and has visions of a giant rabbit named Frank. One night, Frank tells Donnie that the world will end in 28 days, 6 hours, 42 minutes, and 12 seconds. As the days pass, Donnie starts to see and do things that he doesn't understand, like sleepwalking and causing destruction. He also meets a girl named Gretchen, who helps him try to figure out what is happening to him. As the end of the 28 days approaches, Donnie learns that Frank is actually from a different universe, and that he has been sent to prevent a catastrophic event from happening in Donnie's world. In the end, Donnie sacrifices himself to stop the event and save the world. Overall, the movie is about the consequences of time travel and the power of love and self-sacrifice.
