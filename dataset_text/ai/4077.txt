Curly hair stays curly because of the shape of the hair follicles that produce it. The follicles are shaped like a spiral, which causes the hair to grow out in a curling pattern. When you use a straight iron or pull your hair straight, you are temporarily altering the shape of the hair strands. However, once you stop applying heat or take out the ponytail, the hair will return to its natural curling pattern because the shape of the follicles has not changed. Does that make sense?
