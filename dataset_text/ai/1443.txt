Collectors toys are often worth less once the box is opened because the toy is no longer in its original packaging. Many collectors value the condition of the packaging as much as the condition of the toy itself. They may want to keep the toy in its original packaging in order to preserve its value. 
Think of it like a rare book. If a rare book is in excellent condition and has its original cover, it will be worth more than the same book that has been well-loved and has a torn cover. The packaging of a toy, or the cover of a book, helps to protect the item and keep it in good condition. 
In the case of a toy, the packaging can also be part of the overall collectibility of the toy. Some collectors are interested in the design and artwork on the packaging, and may want to keep the toy in its original packaging in order to display that packaging. 
So, in summary, collectors toys are often worth less once the box is opened because the toy is no longer in its original packaging, and the condition of the packaging is important to many collectors.
