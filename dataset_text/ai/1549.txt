When you use incognito mode, your web browser doesn't save your browsing history, search history, or cookies. This means that websites you visit in incognito mode won't be able to track your activity using these methods. 
However, there are other ways that websites can track your activity, even in incognito mode. For example, if you are signed in to your Google account while using incognito mode, Google may still be able to track your activity and use it to personalize your search results or show you targeted ads. 
Similarly, if you watch a video on YouTube while you are signed in to your YouTube account, that video may be added to your watch history, even if you are using incognito mode. This is because your watch history is tied to your YouTube account, rather than being stored in your web browser. 
So, even if you use incognito mode, it's possible for websites to track your activity if you are signed in to an account or if you have given them permission to track you in some other way.
