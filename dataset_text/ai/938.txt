Books don't have ratings like movies, video games, and music because books are generally considered to be for a more general audience than those other forms of media. While movies, video games, and music are often created for specific age groups or audiences, books are often written for a wider range of people and are not necessarily intended for children, teenagers, or adults exclusively. 
There have been efforts to create rating systems for books in the past, but they have not been widely adopted. One reason for this is that books are often more complex than other forms of media, and it can be difficult to assign a simple rating that accurately reflects the content of a book. Additionally, books are often read by people of all ages, and a rating system that is appropriate for one age group might not be appropriate for another. 
Overall, the lack of a rating system for books is due to the fact that books are typically considered to be for a more general audience and are not necessarily targeted at specific age groups like other forms of media.
