Energy companies want to sell as much energy as they can, but they also want to make sure that they are able to meet the energy needs of all of their customers. If everyone used a lot of energy all at the same time, the energy company might not have enough power to go around. This could cause problems, like blackouts. To avoid this, energy companies try to encourage their customers to use less energy, especially during times when lots of people are using a lot of energy, like on hot summer days when everyone has their air conditioners turned on. By using less energy, people can help to make sure that there is enough energy for everyone. In addition, using less energy can also save people money on their energy bills. So, by helping people use less energy, energy companies can actually make their customers happier, even if they are using less of the company's product.
