Neon Genesis Evangelion is a complex and psychological anime series that explores themes of identity, relationships, and the human condition. As Shinji struggles to come to terms with his role as a pilot and his relationships with other characters, he must also grapple with deep personal issues, such as feelings of inadequacy and loneliness.
