Selecting proper footwear for long-distance hiking is vital for preventing injuries and ensuring trail comfort. Trail running shoes offer lightweight flexibility and fast drying times, making them popular for fast-paced hikers carrying lighter packs. Conversely, traditional leather boots provide stiffer ankle support and durable protection when navigating rugged, rocky terrain under heavy loads. Always test new footwear on shorter walks with your planned hiking socks before tackling long trails. Ensuring adequate toe room prevents painful bruising on steep downhill descents, keeping your feet healthy over miles of terrain.
