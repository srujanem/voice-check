Personality is the combination of thoughts, emotions, and behaviors that make a person unique. It is thought that both genetics and environment play a role in shaping a person's personality. Genetics are the instructions that are passed down from our parents that tell our bodies how to grow and function. Some of these instructions might include things like what color our hair and eyes will be, or how tall we will be. Environment includes things like how we were raised, the people we spend time with, and the experiences we have. All of these things can influence our personality. So, to answer your question, it is thought that both genetics and environment play a role in shaping our personality.
