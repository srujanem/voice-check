Ice cream is made with cream, which is a liquid, and other ingredients like sugar, flavoring, and sometimes eggs. When these ingredients are combined and frozen, they form a softer, smoother texture than pure water when it is frozen.\n\nWater expands when it freezes and becomes a solid, taking up more space than it did when it was a liquid. This is why ice cubes are larger than the water that was used to make them. However, the ingredients in ice cream don't expand as much when they freeze, so the ice cream remains softer and more scoopable than pure ice.\n\nAdditionally, ice cream is usually churned while it is freezing, which helps to incorporate air into the mixture and create a smoother, creamier texture. This is why ice cream is soft and scoopable, while ice is hard and more difficult to scoop.
