The electricity is used to create a strong magnetic field, which can make the particles move faster and faster as they go around the LHC. As the particles move around the LHC, they sometimes collide with each other. When this happens, it creates a burst of energy that can be used to create new particles. Scientists use the LHC to study these particles and learn more about the universe.
