Antibiotics are medications that are used to treat infections caused by bacteria. They work by either killing the bacteria or preventing them from multiplying. Viruses are very different from bacteria, and antibiotics are not effective against them because they work in a different way.\n\nWhen you get a cold or the flu, it is caused by a virus, not bacteria. Antibiotics do not work against viruses because they are not designed to attack them. Instead, your body's immune system fights the virus and helps you get better.\n\nIf you have a cold or the flu, it is important to get plenty of rest, drink lots of fluids, and take over-the-counter medications to help with your symptoms. If you have a bacterial infection, your doctor may prescribe an antibiotic to help you get better. But if you have a viral infection, antibiotics will not help and may even be harmful if you take them when you don't need them.
