Malware, viruses, and trojans are types of harmful software that are often created and spread by hackers. Hackers are people who use computers and the internet to try to access other people's computers or networks without permission, often with the intention of causing harm or stealing information. Hackers can create and spread malware in various ways, such as by sending it through email attachments or by planting it on websites that people might visit. Once a person's computer is infected with malware, the hacker can use it to gain access to the person's files, steal personal information, or cause other problems. McAfee is a company that makes software to help protect computers from malware and other threats. While McAfee and other security companies work to protect people from malware and hackers, they are not the ones creating and spreading the malware in the first place.
