Silencers, also known as suppressors, are devices that are attached to the end of a gun barrel to reduce the amount of noise and flash that is produced when the gun is fired. They work by trapping the gases that are produced when the gun is fired and allowing them to cool before they are released into the air. 
Although silencers do not completely eliminate the noise of a gun being fired, they can significantly reduce the volume of the sound. This can be especially useful in certain situations where loud noises could be disruptive or dangerous, such as shooting at a range or hunting in an area with sensitive wildlife. 
Silencers are also useful for protecting the hearing of the person shooting the gun, as the loud noises produced by guns can be damaging to hearing over time. 
Overall, silencers can be useful in a variety of situations where reducing the noise produced by a gun is important. However, it's important to note that they are still loud, and it is important to follow all safety precautions when using a gun, with or without a silencer.
