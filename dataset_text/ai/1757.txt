Sure! Net neutrality is the idea that all websites and online services should be treated equally by internet service providers (ISPs). This means that an ISP should not be able to block or slow down access to any particular website or service, or charge more for access to certain websites or services. Think of the internet like a highway. Net neutrality is like a rule that says all the cars on the highway (websites and online services) should be able to go the same speed and have the same rules, no matter what kind of car it is (whether it's a big company or a small website). Without net neutrality, an ISP (like the people who built and own the highway) might be able to give some cars special treatment (like making them go faster) or charge more for certain cars to use the highway (like charging more for a big company's website to be accessed). This would be unfair to the other cars on the highway (like smaller websites) and could make it harder for people to access the information and services they want online.
