Stock markets exist because they provide a place where people can buy and sell shares of ownership in companies. These shares represent a small piece of ownership in a company, and when you buy them, you become a shareholder in that company. The stock market is an important part of the economy because it allows people to invest in companies and potentially earn money from their investments. Without a stock market, it would be much harder for companies to raise the money they need to grow and expand. This could make it more difficult for new businesses to get off the ground and for existing businesses to innovate and improve. Overall, the stock market is an important way for people to save and invest their money, and it plays a key role in helping businesses grow and thrive. So while it may not be necessary in the strictest sense, it serves a valuable purpose in the economy and is an important part of the financial system.
