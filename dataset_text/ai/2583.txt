Chimpanzees and gorillas are intelligent animals and are capable of learning and using sign language to communicate. However, they are still animals and have different needs and behaviors than humans. They are not able to live in human society in the same way that humans do and may not understand or be able to follow the rules and expectations of human society. Additionally, chimpanzees and gorillas are not able to fully understand or participate in the complex social and cultural activities of human society. They do not have the same level of cognitive abilities and consciousness as humans, and therefore are not considered to be sentient in the same way that humans are. It is important to recognize and respect the differences between humans and other animals and to treat all living beings with kindness and compassion. However, it is not appropriate to extend the same rights and responsibilities to animals as we do to humans, as they have different abilities and needs.
