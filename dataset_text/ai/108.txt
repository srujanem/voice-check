Some animals are popular as mascots because they are seen as strong, powerful, and fierce. Lions, for example, are often used as mascots for sports teams because they are known for being brave and confident. Tigers are also seen as strong and fierce, so they are sometimes used as mascots as well. Bears are often associated with strength and courage, so they can also make good mascots. 
Mascots are used to represent a group or organization, and the choice of mascot can often reflect the values or characteristics that the group wants to portray. For example, a sports team might choose a lion as their mascot because they want to convey a sense of strength and determination to their opponents. 
In general, mascots are chosen because they are seen as positive, inspiring, or representative of the group they are representing. They can help to build a sense of unity and pride among members of the group, and can also be used to help promote the group or organization to the wider community.
