Perpetual motion is the idea of a machine that can run indefinitely without any external energy input. However, this is not possible because of the laws of thermodynamics. The first law of thermodynamics states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only converted from one form to another. This means that a machine cannot produce energy out of thin air - it must get the energy from somewhere. The second law of thermodynamics states that whenever energy is converted from one form to another, some of it is always lost as heat. This means that over time, a machine will inevitably lose energy and eventually stop working. So, while it might be tempting to think that a machine could produce free energy and run forever, the laws of thermodynamics tell us that this is not possible.
