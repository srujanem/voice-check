Guns are not just accessories like cars or fancy hats in the United States. Many Americans have a strong cultural tradition of gun ownership, and for some people, owning a gun is an important part of their identity. Guns are also often seen as a means of self-defense and protection. Some people may also own guns for hunting, target shooting, or other sports. While guns are not necessary for survival in the same way that food and water are, some people may feel more secure knowing that they have a way to protect themselves and their families if necessary.
