There are many different classes of ships and submarines in the world, and the names of these classes can vary depending on the country that builds them and the role that they are intended to play.\n\nIn general, ships are named after their function, the place where they were built, or a person who was important to the country or navy. Similarly, a destroyer is a type of ship that is used for protecting other ships and for attacking enemy ships.\n\nThere are also different categories of ships, such as aircraft carriers, cruisers, and battleships, which are named based on their size and the types of weapons and equipment they carry.\n\nOverall, the names of ships and submarines can give us an idea of what they are used for and where they come from, and they can help us to understand more about the history and capabilities of different navies around the world.
