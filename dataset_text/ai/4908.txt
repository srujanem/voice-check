In a car with a manual transmission, the engine keeps running when the car is stationary because the clutch is engaged (pressed down). The clutch connects the engine to the transmission, which is what allows the car to move. When the clutch is engaged and the transmission is in gear, the engine's power can be transmitted to the wheels and the car can move.\n\nIf you want to stop the car but keep the engine running, you can put the transmission in neutral (not in any gear) and keep the clutch engaged. This disconnects the engine from the transmission and the wheels, so the car won't move, but the engine can continue to run.\n\nIn a car with an automatic transmission, there is no clutch pedal. The transmission automatically engages and disengages the engine as needed, so you don't have to worry about it. You can stop the car and keep the engine running by putting the transmission in "park." This locks the transmission and the wheels, so the car won't move, but the engine can continue to run.
