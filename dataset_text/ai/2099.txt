Time is a way that we measure how long things take to happen. For example, we might say that it takes an hour for the sun to go across the sky or that it takes a week for a plant to grow. Space is the area around us that we can move through. It's what separates one place from another. Time dilation is a strange effect that happens when things move very fast. It means that time seems to go by slower for the thing that is moving fast than it does for things that are not moving as fast. Time is relative because it can seem to go by at different speeds for different people or things. For example, if you were in a spaceship traveling very fast through space, time would seem to go by slower for you than it would for someone who was standing on Earth. This is because of the time dilation effect. The time/space continuum is a way of thinking about how time and space are connected. It helps us understand how things in the universe change and move over time.
