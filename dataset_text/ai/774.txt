"Forrest Gump" is a movie about a man named Forrest who has a low IQ but is a very kind and good person. He goes on many adventures and meets lots of different people throughout his life. The movie is about how he learns from and helps these people, and how he overcomes challenges and becomes successful despite facing many difficulties. 
The main message of the movie is about the importance of being kind and compassionate towards others, and about how one person's actions can have a big impact on the world around them. It also suggests that no matter what challenges we face in life, we can still achieve great things if we work hard and stay positive.
