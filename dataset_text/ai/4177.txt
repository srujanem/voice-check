Solder is a metal alloy that is used to join two pieces of metal together. Lead is one of the elements that can be used in solder, but it is not used in all types of solder. Some types of solder do not contain lead at all.\n\nIn general, it is not dangerous to touch a solder joint. However, if the solder contains lead, it is possible that touching the joint could cause some lead to be transferred to your skin. Lead can be harmful if it is ingested or inhaled, so it is important to wash your hands after handling solder, particularly if the solder contains lead.\n\nIf you are working with computer parts and are concerned about the possibility of lead in the solder, it is a good idea to wear gloves and to wash your hands thoroughly after handling the parts. It is also a good idea to follow proper safety guidelines when working with any type of electronics, such as wearing safety glasses and following proper handling procedures.
