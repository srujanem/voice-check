Allergies occur when the body's immune system mistakenly identifies a normally harmless substance as a harmful invader. When this happens, the body mounts an immune response to the substance, which can cause symptoms such as sneezing, itching, and difficulty breathing.\n\nPeople can develop allergies at any age, and it is not uncommon for people to develop allergies to substances they were not previously allergic to. This can happen because the body's immune system changes over time, and a person may become more sensitive to certain substances as they age.\n\nIt is also possible for a person to develop an allergy to a substance they have been exposed to for the first time. For example, if someone has never eaten peanuts before, and they eat peanuts for the first time as an adult, they may develop an allergy to peanuts.\n\nIn summary, allergies can develop at any age, and they can be caused by either a change in the body's immune system or by exposure to a new substance.
