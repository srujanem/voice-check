Movies often use replicas or stunt cars that are made to look like the real thing, but are not actually worth as much money. These cars are specially designed to be able to withstand the stresses of being involved in stunts and high-speed chases, so they can be used over and over again. In some cases, the movie studio may also rent or borrow real cars from car manufacturers, and then return them once filming is complete. The cost of any damage to the cars is usually factored into the budget for the movie. So, while it may look like the movies are destroying expensive cars, they are usually using special cars that are built to be able to handle the rough treatment.
