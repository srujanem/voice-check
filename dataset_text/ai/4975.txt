An old computer screen may flicker when you're not looking at it directly because it may be experiencing a problem with its refresh rate. The refresh rate is the number of times per second that the image on the screen is updated, or "refreshed." If the refresh rate is too low, the image on the screen may appear to flicker or "strobe," especially if you're not looking at it directly.
