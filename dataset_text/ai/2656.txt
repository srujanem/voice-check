Medicare is a government-run health insurance program in the United States that provides coverage to people who are 65 years of age or older, as well as to some younger people with disabilities. Additionally, Medicare is designed specifically for people who are 65 or older, and it may not have the capacity to cover everyone in the country. Another reason is that many people in the United States already have private health insurance through their employer or on their own. Some people may not want to switch to Medicare, even if it were available as an option. Finally, making Medicare available to everyone would be a significant change to the healthcare system in the United States, and there are likely to be different opinions on whether this is a good idea or not.
