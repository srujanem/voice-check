The heavy iron door swung open with a rusty groan, revealing a long spiral staircase descending into absolute darkness. Clara paused on the threshold, raising her brass lantern to illuminate the damp stone steps ahead. Whispers of cold air draft drifted upward from the depths, carrying the faint metallic scent of ancient bronze and forgotten dust. She tightened her grip on the old map, knowing that somewhere below lay the lost archive she had spent a decade hunting. With a steady breath, she took her first step down.
