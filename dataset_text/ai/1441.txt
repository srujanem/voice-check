Water itself does not go "old" or expire. However, if you leave water sitting in a glass or container for an extended period of time, it can become contaminated by microorganisms or other substances that may be present in the air or on the surface of the container. This is especially true if the water is not covered or if the container is not clean. 
If you're concerned about the quality of your tap water, you can always boil it to kill any potential contaminants or use a water filter to remove impurities. It's also a good idea to clean your water containers regularly to help prevent contamination. 
Overall, it's generally safe to drink tap water as long as it comes from a reliable source and has been treated properly. However, it's always a good idea to use caution and practice good hygiene to help ensure that the water you're drinking is clean and safe.
