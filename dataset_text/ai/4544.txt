Horsepower and torque are both measurements of how much power an engine can produce, but they are slightly different.\n\nThink of horsepower as the speed at which an engine can do work. It's like how fast you can run. If you want to run a race as fast as possible, you would want to have a lot of horsepower.\n\nTorque is like how much weight you can lift. It's the force that the engine can apply to make something move. Imagine trying to open a jar of pickles. If the lid is really tight, you might need to use a lot of force (or torque) to twist it open. If you have a lot of torque, you would be able to open the jar easily.\n\nIn a high performance car, you would generally want both horsepower and torque. Horsepower helps the car go fast, while torque helps the car accelerate quickly and feel powerful when you press the gas pedal.
