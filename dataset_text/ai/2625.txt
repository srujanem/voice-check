Conservatives are people who generally believe in preserving traditional values and institutions. They tend to be in favor of limited government, individual responsibility, and free enterprise. They also tend to be more skeptical of change and more supportive of social and cultural traditions. Republicans are a political party in the United States. Many members of the Republican Party identify as conservatives, although there are some members who do not consider themselves to be conservative. In general, Republicans and conservatives tend to be more supportive of free market capitalism, lower taxes, and a strong national defense. They also tend to be more supportive of traditional values and social issues such as opposition to abortion and same-sex marriage. It's important to note that not all conservatives are Republicans, and not all Republicans are conservatives. There are also conservatives who belong to other political parties or who do not align with any particular party.
