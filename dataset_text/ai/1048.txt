It's not easy to get close to the center of a tornado and take pictures or videos because tornadoes are very dangerous and can cause a lot of damage. They are formed when warm, moist air rises and cool, dry air sinks, creating a whirlwind that can reach speeds of over 300 miles per hour. When a tornado touches the ground, it can pick up debris, such as trees and buildings, and throw them through the air. This can be very dangerous for anyone who is close by. 
Therefore, it is important to stay away from tornadoes and not try to get too close to them. If you see a tornado, you should take shelter immediately and wait until it has passed before coming back out. It is also important to follow the advice of local authorities, such as emergency services and meteorologists, who can give you information about how to stay safe during a tornado.
