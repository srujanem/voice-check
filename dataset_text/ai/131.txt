When you don't get enough sleep, your body doesn't have enough time to repair and refresh itself. As a result, you may feel tired and your eyes may look puffy and dark. This is because the skin under your eyes is thin and delicate, and it can become swollen or bruised when you don't get enough rest. 
The dark circles under your eyes are caused by blood vessels that are visible through the skin. When you're tired, your body is working harder to keep you awake and alert, which can cause the blood vessels under your eyes to become more dilated (larger). This makes them more visible, which can create the appearance of dark circles. 
So, in short, dark circles under your eyes are a sign that your body needs more rest and that you might be feeling tired. It's important to get enough sleep to help your body stay healthy and strong.
