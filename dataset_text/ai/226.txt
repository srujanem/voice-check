People with liver disease may have a distinct body odor that can be described as sweet or musty. This is because their liver is not functioning properly and is unable to remove certain substances from the body as it should. These substances, such as certain amino acids, can be broken down by bacteria on the skin and produce an unpleasant smell. It is not uncommon for people with liver disease to have a change in body odor, and it can be a sign of liver problems. However, it is important to note that not all people with liver disease will have a change in body odor, and other conditions can also cause changes in body odor. It is always important to speak with a medical professional if you are concerned about changes in your body odor or other symptoms that may be related to liver disease.
