Pain is your body's way of telling you that something is wrong or that you are in danger. Imagine that you are playing outside and you accidentally touch a hot stove. You would feel a sharp, burning sensation and your body would automatically pull your hand away from the stove. This is because the heat from the stove is causing damage to your skin, and your body is trying to protect you from further injury. Pain is also a way for your body to alert you to other types of injuries or illnesses. For example, if you have a headache, it is your body's way of telling you that something is not right with your head. Or if you have a stomachache, it is your body's way of telling you that something is not right with your stomach. In short, pain is a way for your body to communicate that something needs attention or care. It is an important part of your body's defense system and helps keep you safe.
