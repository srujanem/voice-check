It's easier to aimlessly browse Reddit for hours because it's more immediately gratifying. When you're browsing Reddit, you can quickly scroll through many different posts and see a variety of different things. This can be satisfying because you're constantly being presented with new information and stimuli. 
On the other hand, reading a book requires more focus and concentration. It can be more challenging to stick with a book for an extended period of time, especially if the material is difficult or dense. It can also be less immediately gratifying because you may not see the rewards of your effort until later on. 
However, reading a good book on a topic you're really interested in can be very rewarding in the long run. It can help you learn new things, expand your knowledge and understanding of a subject, and even improve your critical thinking skills. So it's important to find ways to make reading more enjoyable and rewarding, such as setting aside dedicated time for reading, finding a comfortable and quiet place to read, or finding a book that matches your interests and reading level.
