Connecting to the Internet is a way for your device, such as a computer, phone, or tablet, to send and receive information with other devices over a network. To connect to the Internet, you typically need to use a device called a modem, which is connected to a network service provider (such as an Internet service provider, or ISP). The modem allows you to access the Internet by converting the digital signals that your device sends and receives into a format that can be transmitted over a phone line, cable, or other type of connection. It is generally not possible to build something that would give you Internet access without paying for a service from an ISP or using a mobile data plan. ISPs and mobile carriers use infrastructure such as cables, towers, and satellite systems to provide Internet access to their customers, and they charge fees to cover the costs of maintaining and expanding this infrastructure. However, it is possible to access the Internet for free in some public places, such as libraries, cafes, and airports, by connecting to a Wi-Fi network that is provided by the establishment. These types of Wi-Fi networks usually require you to agree to terms of service before you can use them.
