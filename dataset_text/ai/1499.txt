Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being, affecting how people think, feel, and behave in daily life. Conditions such as depression, anxiety, and schizophrenia are among the most common and debilitating illnesses worldwide. Effective treatments include psychotherapy, medication, lifestyle changes, and social support. Despite growing awareness, significant stigma surrounding mental illness prevents many people from seeking help. Integrating mental health services into primary healthcare systems is considered a critical step toward addressing this global health challenge. It is evident that this topic will continue to shape our world in significant ways going forward.
