When a person dies, their muscles relax and their eyelids may open. Closing the eyelids can make the person look more peaceful and can be a way to show respect for the person who has passed away. It is a common practice in many cultures and is often done as part of preparing the body for burial or cremation. There is also a medical reason for closing the eyelids. The eyes may dry out if they are left open, which can cause discomfort for the person who has passed away. Closing the eyelids can help to keep the eyes moist and comfortable.
