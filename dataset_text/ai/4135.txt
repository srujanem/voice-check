Alcohol poisoning is when a person has consumed so much alcohol that it becomes toxic to their body. When you drink alcohol, your liver works to break it down and remove it from your body. However, the liver can only process a certain amount of alcohol at a time, and if you drink too much too quickly, the excess alcohol can build up in your bloodstream and cause serious problems.\n\nSymptoms of alcohol poisoning can include vomiting, difficulty breathing, confusion, seizures, and even coma or death. This can happen because alcohol can affect the way your brain and body functions, including your heart rate and breathing.\n\nIt's important to be careful when drinking alcohol and to never drink too much. It's also a good idea to have a designated driver or another safe way to get home if you do choose to drink. If you think someone might have alcohol poisoning, it's important to get help right away by calling 911 or taking them to the hospital.
