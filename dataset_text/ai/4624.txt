When a plane is flying through clouds, it is not necessarily louder than when it is flying on a clear day. However, it can seem louder to someone on the ground if the clouds are low and the plane is flying above them. The sound waves from the plane's engines can bounce off the clouds and be directed back down towards the ground, making it seem louder than it would if the clouds weren't there.\n\nOn a clear day, the sound waves from the plane's engines would travel straight through the air and not be reflected back towards the ground in the same way. So it might seem quieter to someone on the ground.\n\nIt's also important to note that the distance from the plane to the ground can affect how loud it seems. If a plane is flying very high in the sky, it may be farther away from the ground and therefore seem quieter, even if it's flying through clouds.
