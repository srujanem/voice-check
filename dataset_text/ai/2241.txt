A Roth IRA is a type of retirement account that you can use to save money for your future. It's called a Roth IRA because it's named after a Senator named William Roth, who helped create the account. Here's how it works: You put money into the account. This is called making a "contribution." You can only contribute a certain amount of money each year, and you have to follow some other rules too. The money in the account grows over time. You can put your money into different investments, like mutual funds, which can help the money grow faster. When you're ready to retire, you can take the money out of the account. You won't have to pay taxes on the money you take out, as long as you follow the rules. The idea behind a Roth IRA is that you save money for your retirement now, and then you don't have to pay taxes on the money when you take it out later.
