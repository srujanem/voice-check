Patents expire because they are legal agreements that give the holder of the patent the right to stop other people from making, using, or selling the patented invention for a certain number of years. The reason patents have an expiration date is to encourage innovation. When a patent expires, other people can start making and using the invention without asking for permission from the person who owned the patent. This means that more people can start using and improving upon the invention, which can lead to new and better products being developed. 
Think of it like this: if you invent a new kind of toy, you might get a patent to protect your idea. This means that other people can't make toys that are exactly the same as yours without your permission. But if you only have the patent for a certain number of years, after that time other people can start making toys like yours. This helps to encourage new ideas and inventions, because people know they will eventually be able to use the ideas of others to make new and improved products.
