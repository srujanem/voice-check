Free shipping means that a company will send you the items you ordered without charging you any extra money for the cost of shipping the items to you. This can be a good deal for you because you don't have to pay any extra money to get the items delivered to you. Sometimes, companies offer free shipping as a way to get more people to buy from them. They might make up for the cost of free shipping by charging a little bit more for the items themselves, or by making money in other ways. For example, a company might make money from advertising or by selling other products or services. Other times, companies might offer free shipping because they have worked out deals with shipping companies to get a lower rate on shipping, or because they have a lot of items to ship and can get a bulk discount. Overall, there are many different ways that companies can afford to offer free shipping, and it can be a good deal for customers if they don't have to pay extra to have their items delivered.
