Racketeering is a type of crime that involves using a business or organization as a way to commit other crimes. It is often associated with organized crime groups, such as the mafia, because these groups will use legitimate businesses as a cover for their illegal activities. For example, a mafia group might own a restaurant, but they might also use the restaurant to sell drugs or to launder money (which means to make it look like the money came from a legal source when it actually came from illegal activities). Racketeering can also involve extortion, which is when someone threatens to harm someone or do something unpleasant to them unless they give the person money or do what they want. So, to explain it like you're five: racketeering is when people use a business to do bad things and/or to make people do what they want. It's a way for them to make money from breaking the law.
