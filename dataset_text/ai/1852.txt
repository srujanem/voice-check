Sure! Computer hacking is when someone uses their computer skills to gain unauthorized access to someone else's computer or network. This can be done in a number of ways, such as using special software to guess a person's password, or finding and exploiting a weakness in a computer's security system. Imagine that you have a lock on your bedroom door to keep out intruders. A hacker is like a person who is trying to pick the lock or find another way to get into your room without your permission. They might try different techniques or tools to try to open the lock and get inside. In the same way, a hacker might try different techniques or tools to try to get into a computer or network. They might try guessing passwords, or they might use a special program to find a weakness in the security system and exploit it to gain access. It's important to protect your computer and your personal information from hackers by using strong passwords and keeping your security software up to date. This can help prevent hackers from being able to gain access to your computer and steal your personal information.
