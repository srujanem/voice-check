There are several reasons why online games, including those made by companies like Blizzard, might experience server issues. 
One reason is that online games are very complex and require a lot of resources to run smoothly. They often have millions of players who are all trying to use the servers at the same time, and this can cause the servers to become overloaded and experience issues. 
Another reason is that online games are constantly being updated and changed, and these updates can sometimes cause unexpected problems. For example, a new feature might be added to the game that ends up causing server issues, or a bug might be introduced that causes problems for players. 
Finally, it's worth noting that even with the best planning and testing, it can be difficult to predict exactly how an online game will perform once it is released to the public. There are many variables that can affect the performance of the game and its servers, and it can be difficult to anticipate and prepare for all of them. 
Overall, while companies like Blizzard try their best to prevent server issues, they can still occur due to the complex nature of online games and the many variables that can affect their performance.
