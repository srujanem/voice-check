Reading is better than watching TV because it helps your brain work harder. When you read, your brain has to think about what the words on the page mean and how they fit together to tell a story. This helps your brain to grow and learn new things. Watching TV, on the other hand, is more of a passive activity. You are just sitting and letting the story unfold in front of you without really having to think about it. While TV can be entertaining, it doesn't challenge your brain in the same way that reading does. It's also important to balance both activities. Reading and watching TV can both be enjoyable and relaxing, but it's important to find a balance and make sure you are doing other things that are good for your body and mind as well, like going for a walk or playing a sport.
