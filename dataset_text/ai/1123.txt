Great white sharks and other dangerous sharks can be unpredictable and their behavior can vary greatly. In some cases, swimmers are able to swim with sharks without any harm because the sharks are not interested in attacking or biting them. This can happen if the sharks are well-fed and not feeling threatened by the presence of the swimmers. 
However, in other cases, sharks can be more aggressive and may attack or bite objects, including cages that are meant to protect swimmers. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the sharks are hungry or feel threatened by the presence of the swimmers. It's important to remember that sharks are wild animals and their behavior can be difficult to predict, so it's always best to be cautious and respect their boundaries when interacting with them.
