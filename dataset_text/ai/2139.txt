The Star Wars prequel trilogy, which includes the movies "The Phantom Menace," "Attack of the Clones," and "Revenge of the Sith," is set in a galaxy far, far away and a long time ago. In this galaxy, there is a government called the Galactic Republic, which is made up of many different planets and species. The Republic is run by a group of politicians called the Senate, and there is also a council of wise leaders called the Jedi Council. The Trade Federation is a group of merchants who have a lot of power and influence in the Republic. They often disagree with the Jedi and the politicians in the Senate about how to run the government and handle conflicts. Throughout the prequel trilogy, there is a lot of political maneuvering and conflict as different groups try to gain more power and control over the Republic. Eventually, this leads to the downfall of the Republic and the rise of the Galactic Empire, which is a dictatorship ruled by the evil Emperor Palpatine.
