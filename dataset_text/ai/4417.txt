Bruises happen when an injury causes small blood vessels under your skin to break and leak blood. This blood collects under the skin and can cause a black and blue mark. When you press on a bruise, you are pressing on the blood that has collected under the skin. This can cause the area to feel sore or painful because the blood is being squeezed and moved around. Bruises usually go away on their own after a few days or weeks, but until then, they may feel tender or painful when you touch them or put pressure on them.
