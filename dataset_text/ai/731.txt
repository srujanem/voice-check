A headache is a pain that you feel in your head. It can be caused by many things, such as tension, sinus problems, or eyestrain. When you have a headache, you might feel pressure or a throbbing sensation in your head. 
Paracetamol and acetaminophen are medications that can help relieve headaches. They work by blocking the production of certain chemicals in the body that cause pain and inflammation. When you take paracetamol or acetaminophen, it can help reduce the pain and discomfort you feel from a headache. 
It's important to remember that it's always a good idea to talk to a doctor or pharmacist before taking any medication, and to follow the instructions on the label carefully.
