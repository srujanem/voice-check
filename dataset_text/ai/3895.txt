It's not uncommon for dogs to vomit if they are hungry, especially if they have not eaten in a while. When a dog hasn't eaten in a while, their stomach may produce extra acid, which can lead to nausea and vomiting. This is the body's way of trying to get rid of the excess acid. It's important to make sure that your dog is eating regularly and has access to fresh water at all times to help prevent this from happening.
