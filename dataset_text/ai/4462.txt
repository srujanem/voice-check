At a restaurant, when you pay with a debit or credit card, the server will usually bring a card reader to your table and process the payment before you leave. If you want to add a tip, you can tell the server the amount you want to add and they will enter it into the card reader. The tip will be added to the total amount that is charged to your card. So, the tip and the total cost of your meal will be charged to your card at the same time.\n\nFor example, let's say you have a meal that costs $20 and you want to leave a 15% tip, which is $3. When you pay with your card, the card reader will charge a total of $23 to your card, which includes the cost of your meal and the tip. The tip and the total cost of the meal will be charged to your card at the same time. Does that make sense?
