It's not accurate to say that liberal arts majors are considered worthless. In fact, many people believe that a liberal arts education is valuable because it helps students develop important skills such as critical thinking, communication, and problem-solving, which are useful in a variety of careers. 
However, it is true that some people may view liberal arts majors as not being as valuable as other types of degrees, such as those in a technical or vocational field, because they may not lead to specific job titles or careers. This perception may be based on the belief that a liberal arts education does not provide students with the specific knowledge and skills they need to be successful in a particular field. 
It's important to note that there is no one-size-fits-all answer to the question of which type of degree is the most valuable. Different people have different goals and interests, and what is valuable to one person may not be valuable to another. Ultimately, the value of a degree depends on what you want to do with it and how you use the skills and knowledge you gained from your education to achieve your goals.
