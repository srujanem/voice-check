Planets and stars are round because of the force of gravity. Gravity is a force that pulls everything towards the center of an object. When an object has a lot of mass, like a planet or a star, the force of gravity is very strong. This causes the object to become round, like a ball. Imagine you have a ball of clay. If you try to squash it or stretch it, the clay will try to resist and keep its shape. But if you let the clay sit on a flat surface, it will slowly sink down and become round. This is because the force of gravity is pulling the clay towards the ground. Planets and stars are made of gas, dust, and other materials. These materials are held together by their own gravity. When the materials are swirling around and moving fast, they can sometimes collide and create shapes that are not round. But over time, the force of gravity will cause the materials to settle down and become round. So that's why planets and stars are round: because of the force of gravity pulling everything towards the center.
