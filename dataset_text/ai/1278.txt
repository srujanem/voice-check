Political parties in the United States have different ideas about how the government should be run and what policies should be implemented. When one party is in power, they may try to pass laws or make decisions that align with their beliefs, while the other party may disagree and try to block those efforts. This can lead to conflict and disagreement between the parties. 
Compromise is often necessary in order for the government to function, but it can be difficult for politicians to find common ground and agree on solutions. This is especially true when the parties have very different ideologies or priorities. 
There are also other factors that can contribute to hostility in American politics, such as media coverage that focuses on conflicts and divides, and social media platforms that can amplify and spread divisive messages. 
Overall, it's important for people to try to understand each other's perspectives and work towards finding solutions that benefit the country as a whole, rather than just one side or the other.
