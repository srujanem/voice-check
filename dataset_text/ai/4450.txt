Debt can be good or bad depending on the circumstances. Here's a simplified explanation:\n\nWhen a person or a business takes out a loan, they are borrowing money from someone else. This can be good if the person or business plans to use the money to make more money in the future. For example, if a person takes out a loan to go to college, they may be able to get a higher paying job after they graduate, which would allow them to pay off the loan and make a profit.\n\nOn the other hand, taking on too much debt can be risky. If a person or business is unable to pay back the loan, they may end up defaulting on the loan, which can have serious consequences such as losing their home or business.\n\nIn general, it is important to be careful and thoughtful about taking on debt. It can be a useful tool to help you achieve your goals, but it is important to make sure that you are able to manage your debt and pay it back on time.
