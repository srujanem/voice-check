Clouds are made up of tiny water droplets or ice crystals that are suspended in the air. They form when moist air rises and cools, and the water vapor in the air condenses into tiny drops or crystals.\n\nAlthough clouds may seem heavy, they are actually very light because they are made up of such small particles. In fact, a cloud that weighs a million pounds would be so large that it would stretch from the ground all the way up to the top of the atmosphere!\n\nThe reason clouds can stay up in the sky is because the air that they are made of is lighter than the air around them. This is because the air in the cloud is warmer and less dense than the air around it, so it is able to float on top of the cooler, denser air.\n\nSo, even though clouds may seem heavy, they are actually able to float in the air because they are made of small, light particles and are surrounded by air that is denser and heavier than they are.
