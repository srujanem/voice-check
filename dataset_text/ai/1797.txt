When you put music on your iPod Classic, iTunes organizes the files into folders to make it easier for the iPod to find and play the songs. The folders are labeled with numbers, like "F00" or "F01," because they are easier for computers to understand than words or names. Each folder usually contains several songs, and each song is given a four-letter name to make it easier for the iPod to find and play it. The name is usually a combination of letters and numbers that represent the artist, album, and song title. For example, a song by an artist named "John Smith" from an album called "My Music" might be named "JSMM.mp3," where "JS" stands for "John Smith," "MM" stands for "My Music," and "mp3" is the file format. So when you listen to music on your iPod Classic, the iPod goes through the folders and finds the songs with the four-letter names to play them for you.
