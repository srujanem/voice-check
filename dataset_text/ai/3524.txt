Human beings tend to create routines because they can be comforting and help us feel more in control of our lives. When we do the same things in the same way every day, we don't have to think as much about what we're doing, which can be helpful if we have a lot going on in our lives. For example, if you always sit in the same seat on the train every day, you don't have to worry about finding a seat or deciding where to sit. This can save you time and energy, and it can make your commute feel more predictable and familiar. Similarly, if you walk the same path to work every day, you might find that it helps you feel more focused and energized. This is because you know what to expect and can prepare yourself mentally and physically for the day ahead. Overall, routines can help us feel more comfortable and secure, which can be especially important if we're facing challenges or stress in other areas of our lives.
