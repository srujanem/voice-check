Radiation can make you sick or kill you by damaging the cells in your body. When cells are damaged, they may not be able to function properly or may die. This can cause a wide range of symptoms, depending on which cells are damaged and how severely they are damaged. For example, if the cells in your skin are damaged by radiation, you may develop a sunburn-like rash. If the cells in your digestive system are damaged, you may experience nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea. If the cells in your blood are damaged, you may have a low white blood cell count, which can make it harder for your body to fight off infections. Radiation can also damage the cells in your body's tissues and organs, such as the brain, heart, and liver. This can cause more serious symptoms, such as difficulty thinking, trouble with movement, and organ failure. In severe cases, radiation can cause death by damaging the cells in the body so severely that the body's systems can no longer function properly.
