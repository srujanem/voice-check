Deja vu is the feeling that you have experienced something before, even though you know that you haven't. It's like a sense of familiarity with something that should be new to you. Scientists aren't entirely sure why deja vu happens, but they have a few theories. One theory is that deja vu is caused by a glitch in the brain that causes it to mix up memories. Another theory is that deja vu is caused by the brain trying to make sense of new experiences by comparing them to old memories. It's important to note that deja vu is not a sign of anything wrong with your brain. It's a normal, common experience that many people have had at some point in their lives. It usually only lasts for a short time and isn't harmful. So if you ever experience deja vu, don't worry! It's just your brain doing its thing.
