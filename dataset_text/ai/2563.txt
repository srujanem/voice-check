High fructose corn syrup (HFCS) is a type of sugar that is made from corn. It is often used in processed foods, such as soft drinks, candy, and baked goods, because it is cheap and easy to use. Some people believe that HFCS is less healthy than natural sugar because it may be linked to certain health problems, such as weight gain, diabetes, and heart disease. However, it's important to note that HFCS is not necessarily less healthy than natural sugar - both types of sugar contain the same number of calories and can contribute to these health problems if consumed in large amounts. The key to maintaining a healthy diet is to consume all types of sugar in moderation and to balance them with a variety of healthy foods.
