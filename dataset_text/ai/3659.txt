Hospitals don't necessarily advertise to attract patients who need emergency care, like someone with a broken arm. Instead, they might advertise to let people know about the other types of care they offer, such as regular check-ups, vaccines, and surgeries. They might also advertise to let people know about the specialties and expertise of their doctors and nurses. Advertising can also help hospitals attract new patients who are looking for a place to receive care. For example, if someone is moving to a new city and needs to find a new doctor, they might see an advertisement for a hospital and decide to go there for their medical needs. Overall, advertising can help hospitals get the word out about the services they offer and make it easier for people to find the care they need.
