It's not uncommon for mixed martial arts (MMA) fighters to try to cut a significant amount of weight before a fight, especially if they are trying to compete in a lower weight class. Cutting weight can be a challenging and difficult process, and it's important to do it safely and responsibly. One way that fighters might try to cut weight is by losing water weight. This can be done by reducing the amount of water and other fluids that they consume, and also by sweating more through activities like saunas or intense exercise. However, it's not healthy or safe to try to lose more than a few pounds of water weight in a short period of time, and losing 20 pounds in a day is not recommended. It's also important to note that cutting weight too quickly can be dangerous and can negatively impact a fighter's performance in the ring. It's important for fighters to work with a trained professional, like a nutritionist or sports doctor, to help them safely and effectively cut weight.
