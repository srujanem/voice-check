Mass is a measure of how much matter is in an object. It is the amount of stuff that makes up something. The more mass something has, the more it weighs.\n\nParticles that have no mass are called massless particles. These particles can still have other properties, such as energy and momentum, but they do not have mass. Examples of massless particles include photons, which are particles of light, and gluons, which are particles that hold quarks together to form protons and neutrons.\n\nHope that helps!
