Dreams are a product of the brain and they don't follow the same rules as the real world. In dreams, anything can happen and time can pass differently than it does in the real world. So, it's not surprising that dreams might not always end at what we consider to be "appropriate" times. 
Sometimes, our brains will end a dream because it's no longer interesting or because the dreamer is about to wake up. Other times, the dream might end in a way that feels like it makes sense in the context of the dream. For example, if you're dreaming about falling from a building, your brain might end the dream just before you hit the ground because that's a natural stopping point for that particular dream. 
It's important to remember that dreams are just a normal part of the sleep cycle and they don't necessarily have any deeper meaning. They're just a way for our brains to process information and sort through our thoughts and experiences while we sleep.
