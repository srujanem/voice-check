Grad school is a type of school where people go to study a specific subject in more depth after they have already completed college. It is usually for people who want to become experts in their field or get a higher degree, such as a Master's or a Doctorate. Elementary school is a type of school for children in the early stages of their education, usually ages 5 to 11 or 12. It is also called grade school or primary school. Junior high school is a type of school for children in their middle school years, usually ages 11 or 12 to 14 or 15. It is also called middle school. A community college is a type of school that provides two-year associate's degree programs and shorter certificate programs. It is a less expensive option for people who want to go to college, but may not have the money or grades to go to a four-year university right away. A university is a type of school where people can go to study a wide range of subjects at a more advanced level. It usually takes four years to complete a bachelor's degree at a university. Universities often have a lot of different departments that offer a variety of majors, or subjects of study.
