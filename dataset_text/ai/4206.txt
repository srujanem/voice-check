Human beings generally like familiarity and routine because it makes them feel safe and comfortable. When things are familiar, we know what to expect and we can predict how things will go. This can be especially important when it comes to things like our home and our bed, which are places where we feel safe and relaxed.\n\nOn the other hand, humans can also get bored when they do the same thing over and over again because our brains are designed to constantly learn and explore new things. When we do the same thing repeatedly, our brain doesn't have the opportunity to learn and grow, and this can lead to feelings of boredom.\n\nSo, in short, humans like familiarity because it makes us feel safe, but we also need new experiences and challenges to keep our brains active and engaged.
