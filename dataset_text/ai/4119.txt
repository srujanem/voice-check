Richard Nixon was the President of the United States from 1969 to 1974. He is not well liked by many people because he did some things that were dishonest and illegal while he was in office. One of the main reasons people hate him is because of a scandal called Watergate. Watergate was a break-in at the Democratic National Committee headquarters in 1972, and it was later discovered that Nixon's administration was involved in the crime. Nixon was also involved in trying to cover up the break-in and lied about it to the American people. Another reason people hate Nixon is because he was President during the Vietnam War, which was a very controversial and unpopular war. Many people believe that Nixon could have ended the war sooner, but he chose to continue fighting it instead. Overall, Nixon is remembered as a President who was dishonest and who did not always act in the best interests of the American people.
