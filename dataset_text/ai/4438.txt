The bird will use its beak and feet to collect the materials and then arrange them into a structure that provides support and protection for the eggs and chicks.\n\nIt's true that the first stick or twig that a bird places in its nest might blow away in the wind, but birds are skilled at finding and using materials that are sturdy enough to withstand the elements.
