Noise-cancelling headphones use a special microphone to pick up the sound of the noise around you. Then, they use a computer inside the headphones to create a special sound that is the exact opposite of the noise. This special sound is called an "anti-noise" sound. When the anti-noise sound is played through the headphones at the same time as the noise, it cancels out the noise. So, when you wear noise-cancelling headphones, you only hear the music or audio that you want to listen to, and not the noise around you. Does that make sense?
