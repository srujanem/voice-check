Sure! Here's a simple explanation of how WiFi works: A device, like a computer or phone, has a WiFi antenna that sends and receives signals. The device sends a signal to a WiFi router, which is connected to the internet. The router sends the signal through the internet to the website or service that the device is trying to access. The website or service sends a signal back through the internet to the router. The router sends the signal to the device through the air, using radio waves. The device's WiFi antenna receives the signal and translates it into a form that the device can understand, like a webpage or a video. So, WiFi works by sending and receiving signals through the air using radio waves. This allows devices to communicate with each other and access the internet without needing to be physically connected with wires.
