Cement and concrete are often used interchangeably, but they are actually two different things.\n\nCement is a powdery substance made from a mixture of substances such as limestone and clay. It is heated to a high temperature to create a strong, binding substance.\n\nConcrete is a mixture of cement, water, and aggregate (such as sand and gravel). When the cement is mixed with water, it creates a paste that binds the other ingredients together to form a solid mass.\n\nSo, cement is one ingredient that is used to make concrete. Concrete is a finished product that is used in construction to create strong, durable structures like buildings, roads, and bridges.\n\nThink of it this way: cement is like the flour that is used to make bread, and concrete is like the bread itself. The flour is an important ingredient, but it's not the final product.
