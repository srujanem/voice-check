Coaxial cables are able to transmit a lot of data using a single pin because they use a technique called pulse code modulation (PCM) to encode and transmit the data. PCM involves taking a continuous analog signal (like the video signal from a camera) and converting it into a series of digital pulses. These pulses are then sent down the coaxial cable using the single pin. 
At the other end of the cable, the digital pulses are converted back into an analog signal, which can be displayed on a TV or other device. Because the digital pulses can represent a very wide range of analog values, coaxial cables are able to transmit a lot of data using just a single pin.
