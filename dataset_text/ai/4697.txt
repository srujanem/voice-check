During low tide, the water does not disappear. It just goes out to sea, away from the shore. The water is still there, it's just that the tide is low and the water level is lower than usual. The water moves in and out with the tides because of the gravitational pull of the moon and the sun on the Earth's oceans. When the gravitational pull of the moon is stronger, the water is pulled towards the shore, causing high tide. When the gravitational pull of the sun is stronger, the water is pulled away from the shore, causing low tide. So the water does not disappear or concentrate elsewhere, it just moves back and forth with the tides.
