A Bachelor of Arts (BA) degree is a type of undergraduate degree that is usually awarded by a college or university. It typically requires the completion of a set of general education courses in subjects such as English, history, and social sciences, in addition to major courses in a specific subject area.\n\nIn the case of a BA in Mathematics, the degree program will include a mix of general education courses and math courses. The math courses may include topics such as calculus, algebra, and geometry, as well as more advanced courses in areas such as abstract algebra, real analysis, and topology.\n\nWhile math is often thought of as a scientific subject, it is also considered a liberal art because it involves the study of abstract concepts and ideas. This means that a BA in Mathematics is classified as a Bachelor of Arts degree rather than a Bachelor of Science (BS) degree.\n\nThere are other examples of subject areas that are classified as liberal arts even though they may be thought of as scientific or technical in nature. For example, a degree in computer science might be classified as a BA rather than a BS, depending on the specific program and the emphasis on liberal arts courses.
