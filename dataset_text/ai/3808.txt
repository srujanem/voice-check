In a bobsled, the two people in the middle are called the "pushers." They are responsible for helping the bobsled get a good start at the beginning of the run. When the bobsled is at the starting line, the pushers will push the bobsled as hard as they can to get it moving. Once the bobsled is moving, the pushers will jump into the back of the bobsled and hold on tight. The pushers are very important because they help the bobsled get a fast start, which can make a big difference in the overall time for the run. They also help to balance the bobsled and keep it stable as it goes down the track.
