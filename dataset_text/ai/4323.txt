Free radicals are molecules that have an uneven number of electrons. This makes them unstable and reactive, which means they can cause damage to cells in your body.\n\nYour body produces some free radicals as a normal part of metabolism, but you can also be exposed to them from things like pollution, tobacco smoke, and radiation. When free radicals build up in your body, they can cause damage to your cells and contribute to the development of diseases such as cancer, heart disease, and Alzheimer's disease.\n\nIt's important to take care of your body and reduce your exposure to free radicals, but it's also important to remember that free radicals are a natural part of life and some level of exposure is normal and necessary. The key is to find a balance and protect your body as much as you can. You can do this by eating a healthy diet with plenty of fruits and vegetables, exercising regularly, and not smoking.
