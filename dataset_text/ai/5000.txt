\nNoise-cancelling headphones work by using tiny microphones to pick up the noise around you. Then, the headphones use special technology to create a sound wave that is the exact opposite of the noise. When the opposite sound wave meets the noise, it cancels it out, making it much harder for you to hear the noise.\n\nHere's an example to help explain it better: imagine that you are standing in a room with a loud fan. The fan is making a lot of noise, and it's hard for you to hear anything else. Now, imagine that you have a special device that can create a sound wave that is the exact opposite of the noise the fan is making. When you turn on the device, the sound wave it creates cancels out the noise from the fan, making it much easier for you to hear other things in the room. That's kind of how noise-cancelling headphones work, except they use tiny microphones and special technology to create the opposite sound wave instead of a special device.
