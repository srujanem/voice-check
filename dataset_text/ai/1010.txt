Let us explore the fundamental aspects of this important subject in a structured manner. Economic inequality refers to the unequal distribution of income, wealth, and opportunities within a society. It is measured using tools such as the Gini coefficient, where a score of zero represents perfect equality and one represents maximum inequality. Research suggests that high levels of economic inequality can slow economic growth, reduce social mobility, and increase political instability. Policy responses include progressive taxation, investment in education, and strengthening social safety nets.
