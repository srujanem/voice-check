When you use a handheld sprayer on a hose, the water flows through the hose and into the sprayer. The sprayer has a small opening that the water flows through, and this opening is covered by a small piece of metal called a valve. When you squeeze the trigger on the sprayer, the valve opens and allows the water to flow out of the sprayer and create a spray. When you stop squeezing the trigger, the valve closes and the water flow is stopped. This prevents the water from flowing out of the sprayer and the pressure in the hose from building up too much. So, as long as the valve is closed, the water in the hose won't explode.
