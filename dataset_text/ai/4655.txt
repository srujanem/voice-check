Ladders are designed to allow people to safely reach high places, such as the roof of a house or the top of a tree. The steps towards the top that you are not supposed to climb are called "warning steps," and they are there to remind people to be careful when using the ladder.\n\nSometimes, people might be tempted to stand on the warning steps because they want to reach just a little bit higher. However, these steps are not as sturdy as the other steps, and they are not meant to support the weight of a person. If you stand on them, you could lose your balance and fall, which could be very dangerous.\n\nThe warning steps are included on ladders to help prevent accidents and keep people safe. Even though they might not seem very useful, they serve an important purpose in reminding people to be cautious when using a ladder.
