Saving receipts from your transactions can be helpful for a few different reasons: Tracking expenses: Receipts can help you keep track of how much money you're spending and where you're spending it. This can be useful for budgeting or just understanding where your money is going. Proof of purchase: If you need to return something or dispute a charge on your credit card, a receipt can serve as proof that you made the purchase. Taxes: Receipts can also be useful when it comes to preparing your tax return. If you have receipts for items that you can claim as tax deductions (like charitable donations or business expenses), you'll need to have those receipts on hand to be able to claim the deduction. Overall, saving receipts can be a good habit to get into because they can help you keep track of your finances and provide important documentation if you need it.
