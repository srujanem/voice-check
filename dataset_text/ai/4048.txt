It would be very difficult for one individual to build a new internet from scratch. The internet is a vast network of interconnected computers that are able to communicate with each other. Building such a network would require a lot of resources, including computers, servers, and networking equipment, as well as expertise in areas like computer programming, networking, and cybersecurity. Additionally, building a new internet would require creating new protocols and standards for how the network operates and how data is transmitted between computers. Overall, building a new internet would be a huge undertaking that would require the contributions of many people with different skills and resources.
