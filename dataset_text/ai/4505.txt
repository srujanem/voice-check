Gas prices change every day because the cost of producing and distributing gasoline changes on a daily basis. There are many factors that can affect the cost of gasoline, such as the cost of crude oil, the cost of transportation, and taxes.\n\nCrude oil is one of the main ingredients in gasoline, and the cost of crude oil can change due to a variety of factors, including political events, natural disasters, and changes in the global economy.\n\nThe cost of transportation also plays a role in the price of gasoline. It costs money to transport gasoline from the places where it is made to the gas stations where it is sold. If the cost of transportation goes up, that can make gasoline more expensive.\n\nFinally, taxes can also affect the price of gasoline. Governments often impose taxes on gasoline to help pay for things like road construction and maintenance. When these taxes go up, it can make gasoline more expensive.\n\nSo, as you can see, there are many different factors that can cause the price of gasoline to change from day to day. That's why gas prices tend to be more variable than the prices of other goods.
