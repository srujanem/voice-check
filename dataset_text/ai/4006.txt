Even though Syria has not signed the Chemical Weapons Convention, it is still considered illegal for any country to use chemical weapons under international law. The use of chemical weapons is prohibited by the 1925 Geneva Protocol, which Syria has signed. The Chemical Weapons Convention is just a more recent agreement that builds upon the Geneva Protocol and sets out additional rules for the destruction of chemical weapons and the punishment of countries that use them. Just because a country is not a member of the Chemical Weapons Convention does not mean that it can use chemical weapons without any consequences. Countries that are members of the United Nations, like Syria, are expected to follow international laws and agreements, even if they have not formally signed them. If a country breaks these rules, it can be held accountable through measures such as sanctions, international pressure, and even military action.
