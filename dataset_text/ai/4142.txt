Existentialism is a philosophical movement that emphasizes individual freedom and choice, and the inherent meaninglessness of life. It suggests that people must create their own meaning in life, rather than relying on external sources such as religion or tradition.\n\nAbsurdism, on the other hand, is a philosophical belief that the world is fundamentally irrational and meaningless. Absurdists believe that there is no inherent meaning to life, and that any attempt to find meaning is ultimately futile. This can lead to feelings of despair and hopelessness.\n\nBoth existentialism and absurdism deal with the idea of meaning in life, but they have different approaches to it. Existentialists believe that people can create their own meaning, while absurdists believe that meaning is impossible to find.
