Diamonds are naturally occurring materials that are found in the earth. They are made of a type of carbon and are very hard, which makes them difficult to cut and shape. The first diamond cutters used very simple tools, such as chisels and hammers, to shape diamonds. They would hit the diamond with the chisel or hammer to chip away at it, slowly shaping it into the desired shape.\n\nAs time went on and people learned more about diamonds and how to work with them, they developed more advanced tools and techniques for cutting and shaping diamonds. Today, diamond cutters use special saws and lasers to cut diamonds with precision, and they use a variety of techniques to create different shapes and styles of diamonds.\n
