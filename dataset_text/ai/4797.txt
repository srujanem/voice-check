Asbestos is a group of minerals that are made up of long, thin fibers. These fibers can be breathed in and can get stuck in the lining of the lungs, where they can cause scar tissue to form. This scar tissue can make it hard for the lungs to work properly and can cause breathing problems.\n\nAsbestos fibers can also cause changes in the cells of the body. Over time, these changes can lead to the development of cancer. When the body's cells divide and grow, they usually do so in a controlled way. But when there are changes in the cells, they can start to grow and divide in an uncontrolled way, leading to the development of a tumor. This uncontrolled cell growth is what we call cancer.\n\nAsbestos has been linked to several types of cancer, including mesothelioma (a cancer of the lining of the chest and abdomen), lung cancer, and ovarian cancer. It is important to try to avoid exposure to asbestos, because the risk of developing cancer from asbestos exposure increases the longer and more often a person is exposed.
