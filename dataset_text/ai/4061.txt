The terms "white" and "black" to describe race are not based on the actual colors of people's skin. Instead, they are social constructs that have been used to categorize people based on their race. The use of these terms to describe race is a relatively recent development in human history and has varied over time and place. In general, the terms "white" and "black" have been used to differentiate between people of European ancestry and people of African ancestry, respectively. However, these terms are not accurate or precise, as there is a wide range of skin tones and physical characteristics within each racial group. It is important to note that race is a complex and multifaceted concept that is not solely determined by physical characteristics. It is often used to categorize people based on social, cultural, and historical factors as well.
