Traceroute (also known as tracert on Windows systems) is a tool that you can use to see the route that a packet of data takes from your computer to a specific destination on the internet. It works by sending a small packet of data to the destination, and then measuring how long it takes for that packet to get there. Along the way, the packet will pass through several different servers and routers. Traceroute can show you the name and IP address of each of these servers and routers, as well as how long it took for the packet to get to each one. Traceroute is useful for a few different reasons. For example, if you are having trouble connecting to a website or other online service, you can use traceroute to see if there is a problem with one of the servers or routers along the way. It can also be helpful if you want to see how fast your connection is to a specific destination, or if you want to see which servers and routers your data is passing through.
