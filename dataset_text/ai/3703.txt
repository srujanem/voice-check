Sure! The subprime crisis was a financial crisis that happened around 2007 and 2008. It was caused by a lot of people borrowing money to buy houses, even though they couldn't afford to pay back the loans. These loans are called subprime mortgages. CitiGroup was one of the banks that was most affected by the subprime crisis because it had a lot of these subprime mortgages on its books. When people started defaulting on their loans (meaning they couldn't pay them back), CitiGroup lost a lot of money. This made it very difficult for the bank to keep operating and it had to ask for help from the government. So, to sum it up, the subprime crisis happened because a lot of people took out loans to buy houses, but they couldn't afford to pay them back. CitiGroup was affected because it had a lot of these risky loans and lost a lot of money when people defaulted on them.
