Sure! Night vision cameras use special technology that allows them to capture light that is too dim for the human eye to see. This technology works by amplifying the small amount of light that is present, so that we can see things in the dark. When light hits an object, it bounces off of the object and creates a shadow behind it. So, even in the dark, objects will still create shadows.
