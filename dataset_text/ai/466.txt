In the United States, pickles are often served with sandwiches as a condiment or garnish. They add a crunchy, tangy flavor to the sandwich and can help to balance out the flavors of the other ingredients. Some people like the way that pickles add a refreshing burst of flavor to a sandwich, while others enjoy the way that they help to cut through the richness of certain meats or spreads. There are many different types of pickles that can be used in sandwiches, including dill pickles, bread and butter pickles, and sweet pickles. Overall, pickles are a popular addition to sandwiches because they add an extra layer of flavor and texture that can help to make the sandwich more interesting and satisfying to eat.
