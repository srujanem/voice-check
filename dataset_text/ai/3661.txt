Spiders are attracted to light and movement, just like many other animals. When the spider sees the red laser dot moving around, it perceives it as prey or something to investigate. Spiders have a natural instinct to hunt and capture prey in order to survive, so the spider will often chase after the laser dot in an attempt to catch it. It's similar to how a cat might chase a toy on a string, because they think it's something they can catch and play with. So in this case, the spider is simply following its natural instincts and responding to the stimulus of the moving laser dot.
