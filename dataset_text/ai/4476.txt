When you wake up, your eyes might feel "cloudy" because they are dry and your tear film is not evenly distributed across your eyes. The tear film is a thin layer of moisture that helps to keep your eyes lubricated and healthy. When you sleep, your eyes do not blink as much as they do when you are awake, which can cause the tear film to become more concentrated in certain areas of your eye. This can create a "cloudy" appearance when you open your eyes, especially if you have not had enough sleep or if the air in your bedroom is dry. The "cloud" you see is just the tear film that is not evenly spread across your eye. To help alleviate this feeling of dryness, you can try blinking more frequently or using some artificial tears to help re-moisturize your eyes.
