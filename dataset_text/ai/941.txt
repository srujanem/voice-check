A 3D printer is considered a printer because it is a machine that is used to create physical objects from digital designs, just like a traditional printer is a machine that is used to create physical documents from digital documents. 
The way a 3D printer works is by laying down thin layers of material, one on top of the other, until the desired shape is achieved. This process is similar to the way that a traditional printer works by laying down ink or toner onto a piece of paper to create an image or text. 
So, a 3D printer is essentially a machine that is used to "print" three-dimensional objects, which is why it is called a 3D printer. It is not just an advanced manufacturing machine because it is specifically designed to create objects using a printing process.
