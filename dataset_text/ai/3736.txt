The Cold War was a period of geopolitical tension between the United States and the Soviet Union and their respective allies from approximately 1947 to 1991. Rather than direct military confrontation, it was characterized by proxy wars, an arms race, the space race, and ideological competition between capitalism and communism. Key events included the Berlin Blockade, the Cuban Missile Crisis, and the Korean and Vietnam Wars. The dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991 marked its end.
