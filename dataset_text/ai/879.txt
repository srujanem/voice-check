Self-harm is a way that some people cope with difficult emotions, such as depression, anxiety, or stress. It is not usually a way to seek attention, but rather a way to try to deal with overwhelming emotions. People who self-harm may do so because they feel numb or disconnected from their emotions, and self-harm can help them feel something, even if it is physical pain. Self-harm can also be a way to express emotions that are difficult to put into words. 
It is important to understand that self-harm is a sign of emotional distress and is not a healthy way to cope with problems. If you or someone you know is self-harming, it is important to seek help from a mental health professional, such as a therapist or counselor. They can help you find healthy ways to cope with your emotions and address the underlying issues that may be causing you to self-harm.
