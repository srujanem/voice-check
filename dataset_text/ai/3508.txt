Being addicted to cigarettes means that you have a strong desire to smoke them, even if you know they are not good for your health. You may feel an urge to smoke a cigarette when you are feeling stressed, anxious, or bored. You may also feel a sense of relaxation or enjoyment when you smoke a cigarette, which is called a "buzz." However, smoking cigarettes can also have negative effects on your health, such as increasing your risk of developing lung cancer, heart disease, and other serious health problems. It is important to try to avoid becoming addicted to cigarettes and to seek help if you are already struggling with an addiction to them.
