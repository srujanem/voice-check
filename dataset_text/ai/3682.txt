The United States has had an embargo on Cuba, which means that it has placed certain restrictions on trade and other activities with Cuba, for many years. The main reason for the embargo is that the U.S. government does not agree with the way that the Cuban government is run. The U.S. government has concerns about human rights in Cuba and about the way that the Cuban government treats its citizens. The U.S. also has concerns about the Cuban government's support for certain other governments and groups that the U.S. considers to be a threat to its own security. The U.S. government believes that the embargo is necessary in order to put pressure on the Cuban government to change its policies and practices.
