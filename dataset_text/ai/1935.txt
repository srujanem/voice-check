Carrier pigeons, also known as homing pigeons, are special types of pigeons that have been trained to fly back to a specific location, such as their home loft or the location of their owner. They are often used to carry messages or small items over long distances. Here's how they work: Someone writes a message and places it in a small container, called a "pigeon mail" or "pigeon post." The pigeon is then released from a specific location, such as a battlefield or a disaster area, and flies through the air using its natural instincts to navigate. The pigeon uses its sense of smell, sight, and the earth's magnetic field to find its way back to its home loft or the location of its owner. When the pigeon arrives at its destination, it is often rewarded with food and water as a reward for completing its task. Overall, carrier pigeons are able to fly long distances and deliver messages quickly and accurately thanks to their natural abilities and training. They have been used for centuries to communicate over long distances and continue to be used in some parts of the world today.
