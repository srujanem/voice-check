Standard deviation is a measure of how spread out a group of numbers is. It helps you understand how much the numbers in a group differ from the average (or mean) of the group.\n\nImagine you have a group of 5 students and their heights are: 4 feet, 5 feet, 6 feet, 6 feet, and 7 feet. The average height is 5.6 feet. This means that the students are, on average, 5.6 feet tall.\n\nHowever, some students are taller than the average and some are shorter. The standard deviation helps us understand how much taller or shorter each student is compared to the average height.\n\nFor example, the student who is 4 feet tall is 1.6 feet shorter than the average height, while the student who is 7 feet tall is 1.4 feet taller than the average height. The standard deviation tells us how much these differences vary.\n\nIn general, a low standard deviation means that most of the numbers are close to the average, while a high standard deviation means that the numbers are more spread out and further away from the average.\n\nI hope this helps!
