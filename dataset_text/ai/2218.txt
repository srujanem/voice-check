Licking a 9V battery can give you a small shock because your tongue is a good conductor of electricity. When you lick the battery, the electrical current from the battery flows through your tongue and into your body. This can cause a small shock or tingling sensation. On the other hand, when you touch a 9V battery to your skin, the electrical current does not flow as easily because your skin is not as conductive as your tongue. Therefore, you do not feel a shock or tingling sensation. It is important to note that while a small shock from a 9V battery is not likely to cause any serious harm, it is not a good idea to lick batteries or touch them to your skin on purpose. It is always best to handle batteries with care and follow proper safety guidelines.
