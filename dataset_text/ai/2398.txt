The Curiosity rover is a robot that was built by scientists here on Earth and sent to Mars to explore the planet and send back information and pictures to us. To control the rover, the scientists send it instructions from Earth using a special computer. The instructions tell the rover what to do, such as driving to a certain spot or using its camera to take a picture. To send the instructions and receive the pictures and other information that the rover sends back, we use special antennas on Earth that can send and receive radio signals. These radio signals travel through space and can reach the rover on Mars. The rover has a special antenna that it uses to send and receive the radio signals, too. So, the way it works is that the scientists send the rover instructions using a computer and an antenna on Earth, and the rover receives those instructions and carries them out using its own computer and antenna. Then, the rover sends back pictures and other information using its antenna, and we receive that information using an antenna on Earth.
