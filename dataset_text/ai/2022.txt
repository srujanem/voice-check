After ejaculating, it is normal for the tip of the penis to be sensitive and possibly feel sore. This is because during ejaculation, the muscles in the prostate and the seminal vesicles contract and push fluid into the urethra (the tube that carries urine and semen out of the body). This process can sometimes cause a feeling of discomfort or burning when you urinate, but it should go away on its own after a short period of time. It is important to drink plenty of water and stay hydrated, which can help to flush out any irritants that may be causing the burning sensation. If the discomfort persists or becomes severe, it is a good idea to talk to a healthcare provider for further evaluation and treatment.
