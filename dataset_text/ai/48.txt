Electric vehicles (EVs) operate using an electric motor powered by rechargeable battery packs, eliminating the need for traditional internal combustion engines. Because they produce zero tailpipe emissions, EVs significantly reduce urban air pollution and overall greenhouse gas emissions when charged via renewable energy sources. Modern advancements in lithium-ion battery technology have steadily increased driving range while dramatically reducing charging times. As public charging infrastructure expands globally and production costs drop, electric transportation is transitioning from a niche alternative to the mainstream standard for personal mobility.
