Carbon credits are a way for companies to offset their carbon dioxide emissions, which contribute to climate change. Here's how it works: Governments or organizations set limits on how much carbon dioxide (a type of greenhouse gas) certain industries or companies can emit. If a company emits more carbon dioxide than the limit allows, they must either reduce their emissions or buy carbon credits to offset the excess. Carbon credits can be bought and sold on a carbon market, similar to how stocks are bought and sold on a stock market. Companies that are able to reduce their emissions below the limit can sell their extra credits to other companies that need them. By buying carbon credits, companies can "offset" their excess emissions and show that they are trying to be environmentally friendly.
