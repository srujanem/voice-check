Spicy foods contain a compound called capsaicin, which is what gives them their heat. Capsaicin is an oily substance, so it doesn't mix well with water. This is why drinking water doesn't immediately rinse the heat away when you eat something spicy. Instead, the capsaicin sticks to your mouth and tongue, which is why you continue to feel the heat for a while. 
One way to help rinse the heat away is to drink a beverage that contains a fat or oil, such as milk or yogurt, because capsaicin is soluble in fats and oils. These types of beverages can help to coat your mouth and tongue, and help to rinse away the capsaicin. You can also try eating something that is cool or cold, such as ice cream or a popsicle, which can help to soothe the heat on your tongue.
