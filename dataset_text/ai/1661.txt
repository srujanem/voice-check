The stock market is like a big auction. When someone wants to sell a stock, they put it up for auction and other people can bid on it. The highest bidder gets to buy the stock. When a lot of people want to buy a particular stock, the price goes up because people are willing to pay more for it. When fewer people want to buy a stock, the price goes down because there are fewer people willing to pay for it. There are many factors that can cause the stock market to go up or down.
