Space probes are designed to fly long distances through space, and they use a variety of technologies to do so. One of the main ways that they are able to travel such far distances is through the use of propulsion systems, which are devices that produce a force that can push the spacecraft in a particular direction. 
There are many different types of propulsion systems that space probes can use, depending on their mission and the distances they need to travel. Some of these systems use chemical reactions to produce a force, while others use electricity or radiation. 
One type of propulsion system that is commonly used on space probes is called a rocket engine. Rocket engines work by burning a fuel, such as hydrogen or kerosene, and using the hot gases produced by this reaction to push against a nozzle. The nozzle converts the gases into a high-speed stream of gas that propels the spacecraft forward. 
Space probes also have other systems on board that help them to survive the harsh conditions of space. These systems include things like solar panels, which provide the spacecraft with power, and heaters and insulation, which help to keep the spacecraft warm and protect it from the extreme temperatures of space. 
Finally, space probes are carefully planned and designed to make sure that they can survive the long journey through space. This includes designing the spacecraft to be able to withstand the stresses of launch and the harsh conditions of space, as well as making sure that the spacecraft has enough fuel and other resources to complete its mission.
