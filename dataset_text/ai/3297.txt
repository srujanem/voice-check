Electricity is sent over long distances through power lines, which are wires that are strung between towers. The electricity flows through these wires and is carried to the places where it is needed, like homes and businesses. One of the ways that electricity companies can send electricity over long distances without losing power is by increasing the voltage of the electricity as it travels through the power lines. This is because the higher the voltage, the less current is needed to transmit a given amount of power. And since current is what causes energy losses in the power lines, by using a higher voltage and lower current, the electricity can be sent more efficiently and with less loss of power.
