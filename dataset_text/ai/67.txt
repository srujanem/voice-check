A cool sea breeze rattled the loose metal shutters of Detective Vance's waterfront office as midnight approached. Across the desk, a nervous informant placed a heavy brass key alongside a waterlogged leather ledger. "Everything you need to expose the harbor ring is inside that dockhouse," the man whispered, glancing anxiously toward the dark alleyway below. Vance picked up the key, noting its unusual anchor engraving. The dockyard elite thought they controlled the city's shipping lines, but this single piece of metal was about to shatter their entire operation.
