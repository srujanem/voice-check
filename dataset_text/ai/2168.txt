Here's a simple explanation: Carbohydrates: Carbohydrates are a type of energy-providing nutrient found in foods like bread, rice, and potatoes. There are two main types of carbohydrates: simple carbohydrates and complex carbohydrates. Simple carbohydrates are found in foods like candy, fruit, and sugary drinks. They are quickly broken down by the body and used for energy. Complex carbohydrates are found in foods like whole grains, legumes, and vegetables. They take longer for the body to break down and provide a slow, sustained release of energy. Fats: Fats are another type of nutrient that our bodies need for energy and to help with other important functions. Fats can be found in foods like oils, nuts, and avocados. They help to keep our skin healthy and protect our organs. Fats also help our bodies absorb certain vitamins and minerals. Proteins: Proteins are nutrients that our bodies need to build and repair tissues. They can be found in foods like meat, dairy products, and beans.
