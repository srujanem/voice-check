Gold is naturally a yellow color, but the shade of yellow can vary depending on what other metals are mixed with it. For example, if gold is mixed with copper, it can create a red or rose color. If gold is mixed with silver, nickel, or palladium, it can create a white color. The color of gold can also vary based on how it is polished and finished. A high polish can make the gold appear brighter, while a matte finish can make it appear more muted. So the color of gold can change because of the other metals that are mixed with it, or because of the way it is finished and polished.
