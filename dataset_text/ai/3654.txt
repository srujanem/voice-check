Sure! Imagine that there is a big, giant computer inside of your head. This computer is what helps you think and feel and see and hear and all of that fun stuff. Now, imagine that there are a bunch of other computers just like it, but they are inside of other people's heads. These computers are all connected to each other, kind of like how your phone can connect to the internet and talk to other computers all around the world. And when all of these computers are connected, they can share information and ideas and thoughts with each other. So, even though we are all different people with our own bodies and brains, we are all connected in a way that lets us share our thoughts and experiences. Some people call this "consciousness" or "the universe" or "God." It's like we are all part of one big, giant computer network that helps us all think and feel and experience the world together. Does that make sense?
