Representation in media, including movies, is important because it helps to shape people's understanding of the world and the people around them. When a movie like "Exodus: Gods and Kings" or "21" features an all-white cast, it reinforces the idea that only white people can be main characters or heroes. This can be harmful because it ignores the diversity of the real world and the contributions and experiences of people who are not white. It can also make it harder for people who are not white to see themselves reflected in the media they consume, which can be isolating and damaging to their sense of identity and self-worth. 
In general, it's important to strive for diversity and representation in media so that people of all backgrounds can see themselves and their experiences reflected in the stories being told. This can help to create a more inclusive and understanding society.
