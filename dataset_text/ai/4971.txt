Banks are worth a lot of money because they do more than just hold onto people's money for them. Banks also lend out money to people and businesses who need it, and they charge interest on those loans. This is how banks make most of their money. For example, if a bank has $100 in deposits from customers and they lend out $80 of that money to someone who needs a loan, the bank can charge the borrower interest on that loan. If the borrower pays back the loan plus the interest, the bank will have made a profit.\n\nIn addition to lending out money, banks also make money from fees. For example, they might charge a fee for people to use their ATMs or for businesses to process credit card transactions.\n\nSo, while banks do hold onto people's money for them, they also do a lot of other things to make money. That's why they are worth a lot of money.
