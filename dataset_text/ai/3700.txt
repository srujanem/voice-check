Shakespeare is taught in English class because he is one of the most famous and influential writers in the English language. He was a playwright and poet who lived in the 16th and 17th centuries, and his works are still widely read and studied today. One reason Shakespeare is so important is that he helped to shape the English language itself. He introduced many new words and phrases that are still commonly used today, and his writing style has influenced generations of writers. Another reason Shakespeare is taught in English class is that his plays and poems deal with timeless themes and ideas that are still relevant today. For example, his works explore issues of love, jealousy, power, and betrayal, and they often provide insight into the human condition. Finally, Shakespeare's works are also considered to be great works of literature, and they are often included in English literature curricula because they are thought to be valuable and important works that students should study.
