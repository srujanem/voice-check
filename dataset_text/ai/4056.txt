Lobbyists are people who work to try and influence decisions made by politicians. They might do this by talking to politicians and trying to persuade them to support certain ideas or laws. Lobbyists often work for companies, organizations, or special interest groups, and they try to convince politicians to support things that would be good for those groups. Some people think that lobbyists have too much power because they might spend a lot of money to try and influence politicians. They might do things like buy advertisements, fund campaign events, or offer other kinds of support to politicians. In some cases, lobbyists might also offer gifts or favors to politicians in order to try and win their support. Because of this, some people believe that lobbyists can have too much influence on the decisions made by politicians.
