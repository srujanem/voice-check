PRISM and other NSA programs were kept secret by the US government, and only certain people with high level security clearance were allowed to know about them. Hacker groups like Anonymous are not able to just go and access this information because they don't have the necessary clearance and it is also illegal for them to try to hack into government systems to get this information. The information about PRISM and other NSA programs was eventually made public through leaks by people who did have access to the information and wanted to share it with the world.
