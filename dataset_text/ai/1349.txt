During an eye exam, doctors will often ask you to read characters off a chart or a screen that is placed at a distance from you. They may also ask you to read characters off a mirror, which is placed close to your face. The reason for this is that the doctor wants to test your near vision, or your ability to see objects that are close to you. 
When you read characters off a chart or a screen, the doctor is able to see how well you can see distant objects. When you read characters off a mirror, the doctor is able to see how well you can see objects that are close to you. 
By testing both your distant and near vision, the doctor can get a complete picture of your overall eye health and determine if you need glasses or other vision correction. Reading characters off a mirror is just one way that the doctor can test your near vision. So, it is an important part of the eye exam.
