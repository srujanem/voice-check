Touch screens on mobile phones use a technology called capacitive touch, which means they can detect the electrical conductivity of your skin. When you touch the screen with your finger, your body conducts electricity, which the phone can detect and use to determine where on the screen you touched. Pens and other objects do not conduct electricity in the same way as your skin, so the phone is not able to detect their touch. Some touch screens are designed to work with special styluses that have a tip made of a material that can conduct electricity, but these are not as common as screens that only work with your finger.
