The term "liberal media" is often used by people to describe news organizations or journalists who are perceived as having a left-leaning or progressive political bias. This means that these media outlets or journalists are thought to favor liberal or progressive viewpoints, policies, and candidates over conservative ones. 
There is no single definition of what constitutes a "liberal" or "progressive" viewpoint, and different people might have different ideas about what these terms mean. However, generally speaking, "liberal" or "progressive" viewpoints tend to favor social justice, equal rights, and support for marginalized groups, while "conservative" viewpoints tend to favor tradition, personal responsibility, and a smaller role for government. 
It's important to note that the idea of a "liberal media" is often used as a way to criticize or dismiss news and information that is perceived as being biased or inaccurate. However, it is important to approach any news source, whether it is considered "liberal" or "conservative," with a critical eye and to seek out multiple sources of information in order to get a well-rounded understanding of an issue.
