Men and women have different amounts of certain hormones in their bodies, which can affect muscle growth. Testosterone is a hormone that helps build muscle. Men have more testosterone than women, which is why men generally have more muscle mass. Even if a man and a woman do the same amount of exercise and follow the same diet, the man may still have more muscle mass because of the difference in testosterone levels.\n\nIt's also important to note that genetics plays a role in muscle growth. Some people may have a natural tendency to build muscle more easily than others, regardless of their gender. So while hormones and diet can play a role in muscle growth, they are not the only factors.
