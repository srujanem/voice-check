Toll roads are roads that charge a fee for vehicles to use them. When you pay a toll to get on a toll road, you are paying for the privilege of using the road for a certain distance. When you get off the toll road, you have finished using the road and you may be required to pay an additional fee to cover the cost of maintaining the road and providing the service of letting you use it. 
Think of it like paying for a ride on a bus or a train. When you get on the bus or train, you pay a fee to ride a certain distance. When you get off, you have finished using the service and you may be required to pay an additional fee to cover the cost of the ride. In the same way, when you pay a toll to get on a toll road, you are paying for the service of using the road for a certain distance. When you get off the toll road, you have finished using the service and you may be required to pay an additional fee to cover the cost of using the road.
