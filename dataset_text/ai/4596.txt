A rocket goes up because it shoots hot gas out of the back of it. The hot gas pushes the rocket in the opposite direction, and that's what makes the rocket go up. The hot gas is made by burning fuel in a special part of the rocket called the engine.\n\nWhen the fuel burns, it creates a lot of heat and pressure. The heat and pressure force the gas to shoot out of the back of the rocket through a small opening called the nozzle. The gas comes out really fast, and that's what pushes the rocket.\n\nSo, to make a rocket go, you need fuel, an engine to burn the fuel, and a nozzle to shoot the hot gas out the back. When you light the fuel, it creates a controlled explosion inside the engine that makes the hot gas, and the hot gas pushes the rocket up into the sky.
