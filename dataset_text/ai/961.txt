Photos of other planets often look different from photos of Earth because they are taken by spacecraft that are far away from the planets and are using special cameras to capture images. These cameras are designed to work in the harsh conditions of space, where there is no air, and can take pictures with a very high level of detail. The images are then sent back to Earth, where they are processed and enhanced to bring out even more detail. This can make the images look more like 3D renderings, because they are so detailed and clear. However, they are still real photographs taken by spacecraft.
