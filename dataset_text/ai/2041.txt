A comprehensive understanding of this topic requires us to examine both theoretical and practical dimensions. Nationalism is the ideology that a group of people sharing a common identity, culture, or language should form or maintain their own state. It has been a powerful force in shaping modern history, driving independence movements and the unification of nations. However, extreme nationalism has also fueled conflicts, wars, and ethnic persecution. In the contemporary world, nationalism is in tension with internationalism and global cooperation, as nations navigate issues of sovereignty, immigration, and shared challenges like climate change. It is evident that this topic will continue to shape our world in significant ways going forward.
