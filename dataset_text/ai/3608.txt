Sure! Fiber optics are a way to transmit information using light. They are made of very thin strands of glass or plastic, and they work by sending pulses of light through the strands. The light is used to transmit information in the form of signals, just like how your television or phone sends and receives information. Imagine you have a flashlight and you want to send a message to your friend across the room. You could turn the flashlight on and off really fast to spell out each letter of the message, and your friend could watch the light and decode the message. This is similar to how fiber optics work, except that the light is transmitted through the fiber optic strands instead of through the air. Fiber optics are used in many different types of communication systems, such as the internet, phone lines, and even in medical equipment.
