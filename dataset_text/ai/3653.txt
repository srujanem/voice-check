Sure! When you borrow money to pay for college, you might have to pay back that money with interest. Interest is a fee that you have to pay for borrowing the money. So, if Congress decides to cut student loan interest rates, it means that the fee you have to pay for borrowing money to pay for college might be lower. This might make it easier for you to pay back the money you borrow, because the interest will be lower. It's kind of like when you go to the store and buy something. If the store has a sale and lowers the price of something you want to buy, it might be easier for you to afford it because it costs less money. Cutting student loan interest rates is a little bit like having a sale on the money you borrow to pay for college. Does that make sense?
