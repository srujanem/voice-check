When a person dies "on impact," it means that they died instantly upon being struck by a force or object. This can happen in a car accident, for example, if a person's body is subjected to a sudden and extremely powerful force. There are several ways that a doctor can determine that a person died on impact. One way is by examining the injuries that the person sustained. If the injuries are severe and there is no sign of struggle or defensive wounds, it may be evidence that the person died instantly. Another way is by looking at the scene of the accident. If the force of the impact was strong enough to cause significant damage to the vehicle or other objects, it may suggest that the person died instantly. Finally, a doctor may also consider the person's medical history and any underlying health conditions that they may have had. If the person had a medical condition that made them more vulnerable to serious injury or death, it may be more likely that they died on impact. It's important to note that determining the exact cause of death can be complex, and a doctor will consider all of the available evidence when making a determination.
