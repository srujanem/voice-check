When people smoke cigarettes, they inhale the smoke from the burning tobacco into their lungs. This smoke contains many harmful chemicals that can damage the body. Over time, the body becomes used to the smoke and starts to build up a tolerance to it. As a result, smokers may not cough as much when they inhale the smoke because their bodies are no longer as sensitive to it.\n\nHowever, just because someone has a tolerance to the smoke doesn't mean that it is safe to smoke. Smoking is still very harmful to the body and can cause serious health problems, such as lung cancer, heart disease, and stroke. It is always best to avoid smoking to protect your health.
