Laws in war, also known as international humanitarian law, are in place to protect civilians and limit the harm caused during armed conflicts. These laws are meant to ensure that people are treated humanely, even during times of war. Imagine two kids having a fight in a park. They might push and shove each other, but they probably wouldn't use weapons or try to seriously hurt each other. That's because they know that there are rules they have to follow, like not using weapons or trying to seriously hurt each other. Laws in war work in a similar way. They set out rules for how countries can fight each other, and they try to make sure that the fighting is limited and that civilians are protected as much as possible. This helps to reduce the suffering and destruction caused by war.
