Those crumbs on the bottom of your pizza are called "cornmeal." They are made from ground up corn and are often used to prevent the pizza from sticking to the surface it is cooked on. Some pizza makers sprinkle a small amount of cornmeal on the surface of the pizza pan or baking sheet before placing the dough on it. This helps to keep the pizza from sticking and makes it easier to slide the finished pizza off the pan and onto a cutting board. The cornmeal also adds a slightly crunchy texture to the bottom of the pizza crust.
