Heroin is a drug that can be very harmful to the body. When people use heroin, it causes their body to release chemicals called endorphins. These chemicals make people feel good and can help reduce pain. However, the body can become used to having these chemicals released all the time, and it can start to depend on the drug to feel good.\n\nAs a result, people who use heroin may start to experience negative side effects when they don't have the drug in their body. For example, they may feel sick, anxious, or depressed. They may also have trouble sleeping, and they may experience physical pain.\n\nOne of the most serious side effects of heroin is that it can cause people to neglect their health. When people are addicted to heroin, they may not take care of themselves as well as they should. They may not brush their teeth, wash their hair, or take care of their skin. This can lead to problems like rotten teeth, sores on the skin, and hair falling out.\n\nIt's important to remember that drugs like heroin can be very dangerous and should be avoided.
