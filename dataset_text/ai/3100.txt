In physics, space and time are considered to be part of a single, unified concept called spacetime. According to the theory of relativity, the presence of mass or energy can cause spacetime to curve. This means that the straight path of a planet or spaceship, for example, may appear to bend as it travels through the curved spacetime around a massive object like a star or black hole. Imagine that spacetime is like a rubber sheet. If you place a heavy object on the sheet, it will cause the sheet to bend and curve. If you then roll a small ball on the sheet, it will follow the curve of the sheet rather than rolling in a straight line. This is similar to how the path of a planet or spaceship might appear to bend as it moves through the curved spacetime around a massive object. It's important to note that this is just an analogy and that the actual nature of spacetime is much more complex. However, it can help to provide a rough understanding of how mass and energy can affect the shape and structure of spacetime.
