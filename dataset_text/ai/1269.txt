In a standard deck of playing cards, the Jack is a face card (also known as a court card) that is typically depicted with a picture of a young man or boy. The Jack is not a knight or a prince, but rather a lower ranking face card that is ranked below the Queen and above the Ten in most card games. Each suit in a standard deck of playing cards has four face cards: the King, Queen, Jack, and Ace. The King is the highest ranking face card, followed by the Queen, then the Jack, and finally the Ace. The Jack is often called a "knave" in older card games, and is sometimes depicted holding a sword or other weapon. In modern playing cards, the Jack is usually depicted in a pose or with an expression that reflects the suit it represents. For example, the Jack of Spades is often shown holding a sword, while the Jack of Hearts is often shown with a sad or wistful expression.
