Dogs sleep a lot because their bodies need rest just like ours do. When we sleep, our bodies and minds get a chance to rest and repair themselves, and the same is true for dogs. Dogs also sleep more than humans do, and this is because they are more active when they are awake. They use a lot of energy running, playing, and exploring, so they need more sleep to help them recover and get ready for the next day. So if you see your dogs sleeping a lot, it's because their bodies are working hard and need the rest to keep going!
