The ringing noise that you hear when there is silence is called tinnitus. It is a condition that causes a person to hear a sound in their ears or head that is not actually there. The sound can be a buzzing, ringing, or whistling noise, and it can be constant or intermittent. Tinnitus can be caused by a number of things, including damage to the ears or the auditory nerve, exposure to loud noises, certain medications, and other health conditions. Sometimes, the cause of tinnitus is unknown. Tinnitus is a common condition, and it can be annoying or even disruptive to a person's daily life. However, there are things that can be done to help manage tinnitus, such as wearing earplugs to protect the ears from loud noises, avoiding certain medications that may cause tinnitus, and managing stress and anxiety. Some people may also find relief from tinnitus by using white noise machines or other sound therapy techniques.
