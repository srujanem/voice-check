Marijuana is a drug that is often used for recreational purposes. It can be smoked or eaten in foods called edibles. When a person uses marijuana, it can affect their brain and body in a number of ways. One of the effects of marijuana is that it can make a person feel relaxed or "high." However, marijuana can also have negative effects on a person's ability to drive safely. When a person is under the influence of marijuana, their judgment, coordination, and reaction time can be impaired. This can make it more difficult for them to drive safely and increases the risk of car accidents. It's important to remember that it is never safe to drive while under the influence of marijuana or any other drug. If you have used marijuana and need to drive, it's important to wait until the effects of the drug have worn off before getting behind the wheel.
