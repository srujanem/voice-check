Dark matter is a type of matter that scientists believe exists because they can see its effects on the way other things move. For example, when scientists look at how fast stars are moving around in the center of a galaxy, they can tell that there must be something else in the galaxy that is pulling on the stars and making them move faster. This "something else" is called dark matter. Scientists can't see dark matter directly, but they know it's there because they can see its effects. Dark energy is a type of energy that scientists believe exists because they can see its effects on the way the universe is expanding. The universe is getting bigger all the time, and scientists can see this because they can measure how far away certain objects are from us. They have found that the universe is expanding faster than they thought it should be, based on the amount of matter (like stars and planets) that we can see. They think that dark energy might be causing the universe to expand faster. Like dark matter, scientists can't see dark energy directly, but they can see its effects. I hope this helps!
