Antarctica is a very cold and isolated continent that is located at the South Pole. It is mostly covered in ice and snow, and the temperature can drop to well below freezing. There are no permanent residents in Antarctica because it is a very difficult place to live. It is too cold and isolated for most people to want to stay there for a long time. 
However, Antarctica is not completely uninhabitable. Scientists and researchers from around the world go to Antarctica to study the environment, wildlife, and geology of the continent. They live and work in research stations, which are special buildings that provide a place for them to sleep, eat, and do their work. 
Although some people do live in Antarctica for short periods of time, it is not a place that is suitable for permanent settlement. There are no cities or towns in Antarctica, and it is not possible to grow food or raise animals there. It is also a very expensive place to visit, so not many people go there. 
Overall, Antarctica is an important place for scientific research and exploration, but it is not a place where people can live permanently.
