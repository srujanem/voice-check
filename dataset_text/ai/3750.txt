NASA is interested in building a base on Mars because it is a planet that we believe may have had water on its surface in the past, and it is possible that there may be signs of past life there. Mars is also a more challenging place to explore than the Moon because it is farther away and has a much harsher environment, with very thin air, extreme temperature variations, and no protective magnetic field like Earth has. By building a base on Mars, NASA and other space agencies can learn more about the planet and its history, and test out new technologies and techniques for living and working on another world. As for why NASA isn't building a base on the Moon instead, it's because we already have a lot of experience with the Moon from the Apollo missions and other lunar exploration programs. While the Moon is still an important destination for scientific research, it is not as much of a mystery as Mars, and it doesn't offer as many opportunities for learning new things. However, it is worth noting that NASA and other space agencies are also interested in exploring the Moon again and building a base there eventually. In fact, NASA has a program called Artemis that aims to send humans back to the Moon in the next few years, with the goal of establishing a sustainable presence there and using it as a stepping stone to eventually reach Mars. So while we are currently focusing on building a base on Mars, we are also planning to build a base on the Moon in the future.
