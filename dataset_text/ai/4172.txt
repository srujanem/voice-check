Student debt is a type of debt that people take on when they go to college or university. In the United States, many people have to borrow money to pay for their education because the cost of tuition, books, and other expenses can be very high. The debt is called "student debt" because it is money that students borrow to pay for their education.\n\nThe reason that student debt is such a big thing in the United States is because the cost of education has been rising faster than inflation for many years. This means that each year, it becomes more and more expensive to go to college or university. As a result, more and more people are taking on student debt in order to afford their education.\n\nIn addition, many people in the United States have to borrow a lot of money to pay for their education, and this can be a burden for them after they graduate. It can be difficult for them to find a job that pays enough to cover their student debt and other expenses, so they may have to put off buying a home or starting a family until they can pay off their debt. This is one reason why student debt has become such a big issue in the United States.
