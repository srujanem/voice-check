It's natural to feel a sense of excitement or thrill when looking at a high place, like a cliff or a tall building. This excitement can sometimes be accompanied by the urge to jump. This is because our brains are wired to respond to potential threats or dangers in our environment, and standing on the edge of a high place can trigger this response. 
However, it's important to remember that jumping from a high place can be very dangerous and can cause serious injury or even death. It's important to resist the urge to jump and to keep yourself safe. 
It's also helpful to remember that the feeling of wanting to jump is just a feeling and does not mean that you actually want to do it. It's okay to feel these kinds of sensations, but it's important to make safe choices and to seek help if you are feeling overwhelmed or unsure of how to cope with these feelings.
