Sure! Live vaccines are made from a weakened form of a virus or bacteria. When you get a live vaccine, your body is exposed to a tiny amount of the virus or bacteria, which is just enough to trigger an immune response. Your immune system then fights off the weakened virus or bacteria, and it "remembers" how to do this in the future. That way, if you are ever exposed to the real, full-strength virus or bacteria, your immune system will be ready to fight it off and keep you from getting sick. Does that make sense?
