Copyright is a legal concept that gives the creator of a original work, like a song or a movie, the exclusive right to use and distribute that work. Fair use is a legal doctrine that allows you to use someone else's copyrighted work without their permission for certain purposes, like criticism, commentary, news reporting, teaching, scholarship, or research. Making a collage of videos from YouTube, music videos, and movies and posting it on YouTube would likely not qualify as fair use because you would be using someone else's copyrighted works for your own commercial gain. If you want to use someone else's work in your own video, you will need to get permission from the copyright owner or license the work. It's important to respect copyright laws and not use other people's work without permission. If you do, you could get into legal trouble and be sued for copyright infringement.
