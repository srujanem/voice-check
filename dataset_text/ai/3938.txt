An alley-oop is a play where one player throws the ball to another player, who then catches the ball and immediately dunks it or lays it in. It can be a very exciting play because it involves one player throwing the ball up high and another player jumping up to catch the ball and score. However, alley-oops are generally more difficult to execute in a half-court setting because there is less space to move and fewer players to create distractions. There is also a higher risk of turning the ball over if the pass is not successful. That's why you generally see alley-oops more in fast break situations, where there is more space to operate and fewer defenders.
