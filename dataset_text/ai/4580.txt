Light and sound are both types of energy that can travel through space and matter. They are made up of tiny particles called photons and sound waves, respectively.\n\nLight is a type of electromagnetic radiation, which means that it is made up of tiny particles called photons that are constantly moving and vibrating. These photons can travel through empty space and through different materials, such as air, water, and glass.\n\nSound is a type of mechanical wave that travels through a medium, such as air or water. Sound waves are made up of compressions and rarefactions of the medium through which they are traveling. When an object vibrates, it causes the air or other medium around it to vibrate as well. These vibrations travel through the medium and can be heard when they reach our ears.\n\nEverything is made up of atoms, which are the basic building blocks of matter. Atoms are made up of even smaller particles, such as protons, neutrons, and electrons. These particles can move and vibrate in different ways, and this movement and vibration is what gives rise to different forms of energy, like light and sound.
