Acne is a common skin condition that causes pimples, blackheads, and other blemishes. It is caused by the overproduction of oil by the sebaceous glands in the skin, which can clog pores and lead to inflammation. While there is no "cure" for acne, there are many treatments that can help to clear up the skin and prevent new breakouts from occurring. These treatments include over-the-counter and prescription medications, as well as lifestyle changes such as washing the face regularly and avoiding picking at pimples. With the right treatment and a good skincare routine, it is possible to effectively manage acne and keep the skin looking clear and healthy.
