The Prestige is a mystery thriller film about two rival magicians, Alfred Borden and Robert Angier, who are obsessed with creating the best stage magic tricks. They become bitter enemies after a tragic accident occurs during a performance, and they spend the rest of the movie trying to one-up each other with increasingly elaborate and dangerous tricks. The main plot revolves around the "transported man" trick, in which a magician appears to instantly transport themselves from one location to another. Borden and Angier both try to figure out how the other is pulling off this trick, and they resort to espionage, sabotage, and even murder in their quest to outdo each other. In the end, it is revealed that Borden has a twin brother, and the two of them have been switching places during the "transported man" trick. Angier, desperate to discover the secret, creates a machine that can make copies of living beings, but the copies are not quite right and he is unable to continue using them. He eventually drowns himself in a water tank as part of a new magic trick, and Borden is arrested for his murder. So in summary, The Prestige is a story about two magicians who become enemies and go to great lengths to outdo each other, ultimately leading to tragedy and the downfall of one of them.
