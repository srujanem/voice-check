The person who creates a song is generally the owner of the copyright in that song. This means that they have the exclusive right to do certain things with the song, such as reproduce it, distribute it, and perform it publicly. A record company may also have certain rights in a song if they have signed a contract with the songwriter or composer to produce and distribute recordings of the song. In this case, the record company will typically have the right to manufacture and sell copies of the song, but the songwriter or composer will still retain their copyright and other rights in the song. If you download an mp3 of a song without permission from the copyright owner, you may be infringing on their exclusive rights. This means that you could be sued for copyright infringement. It's generally a good idea to obtain permission from the copyright owner before downloading a song or to use a legally authorized service to download music. In general, it's important to respect the rights of creators and to support them by purchasing music and other creative works rather than downloading them illegally. This helps to ensure that they can continue to create and share their work with the world.
