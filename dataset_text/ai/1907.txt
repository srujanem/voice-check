Batteries have something inside them called a "charge controller" that helps them keep track of how much charge they have left. When you use a device that is powered by a battery, the charge controller helps keep track of how much charge is going out of the battery. When the charge gets low, the controller sends a signal to the device to let it know that the battery is running out of power. Device-specific batteries, like the ones used in phones, work in a similar way to standard AA batteries. However, they may have different types of charge controllers and may be designed to work with specific devices. For example, the charge controller in a phone battery may be designed to work with the specific charging and power needs of a phone, while the charge controller in an AA battery may be designed to work with a wider range of devices.
