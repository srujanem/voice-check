Chefs at restaurants have many responsibilities. They are in charge of creating and preparing the menu, which involves developing and testing recipes, selecting ingredients, and determining portion sizes. They also oversee the kitchen staff and ensure that food is prepared safely and to the desired quality standards. 
In addition to these tasks, chefs may also be responsible for managing the budget and ordering supplies, training new kitchen staff, and ensuring that the kitchen is clean and organized. They may also work closely with the front-of-house staff to coordinate service and make sure that the dining experience is enjoyable for customers. 
While it may seem like chefs simply stand around and give orders, they are actually very busy and have many important tasks to manage in order to run a successful restaurant.
