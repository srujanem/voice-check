Yes, cars have sensors that can measure their own speed. These sensors are called speedometers. Speedometers use a device called a speedometer cable, which is connected to the car's transmission. As the car moves, the transmission causes the speedometer cable to rotate. The speedometer cable is connected to the speedometer, which is a gauge inside the car that measures the speed of the car. The speedometer has a needle that moves around a dial to show the car's speed. Some cars also have sensors called GPS sensors, which can use satellite signals to measure the speed of the car.
