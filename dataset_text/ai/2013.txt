When you're stressed, your body goes into "fight or flight" mode. This is a natural response that helps you deal with stressful situations by preparing your body to either fight or run away. Part of this response involves the release of certain hormones, such as adrenaline, that can affect your body in various ways. One way stress can affect your body is by causing digestive problems, such as an upset stomach. Stress can cause your muscles to tense up, including the muscles in your digestive system. This can lead to symptoms like stomach pain, nausea, and diarrhea. Stress can also affect the way your body processes food, leading to digestion problems. So, when you're stressed, your body is reacting to a perceived threat by preparing to either fight or run away. This can sometimes cause digestive problems like an upset stomach.
