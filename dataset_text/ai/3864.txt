Politicians make promises to get elected because they want to show voters what they will do if they are elected. However, sometimes it is not possible or practical for them to follow through on all of their promises. This can be because of a variety of reasons, such as a change in circumstances, a lack of support from other politicians, or a lack of resources. It is not always enforced that politicians must complete their campaign pledges because there is no legal requirement for them to do so. Additionally, it can be difficult to determine whether or not a promise has been fulfilled, as it may depend on how the promise is interpreted or the specific circumstances surrounding it. It's important to remember that politicians are human, and they may not always be able to follow through on everything they promise. However, voters can hold them accountable by paying attention to their actions and holding them to their promises.
