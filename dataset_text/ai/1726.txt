There are several reasons why housing costs so much. One reason is that there is a limited supply of land, especially in urban areas where demand for housing is high. This means that the price of land can be very expensive, which can make it costly to build homes on it. Another reason is that it costs money to build homes. There are labor costs, materials costs, and other expenses that go into building a house. These costs can add up, especially if the home is large or has lots of features and amenities. Finally, the cost of housing is often affected by demand. If a lot of people want to live in a particular area, the demand for housing will be high, and this can drive up the price of homes. Similarly, if there are not many people looking to buy homes in an area, the demand will be low and the price of homes will be lower.
