Great question! To understand how we know the speed of light, it's important to understand what light is. Light is a type of energy that travels through the air as waves. These waves move very fast, and that's what allows us to see things. Now, to measure the speed of light, we use a special tool called a "light clock." Imagine a light bulb hanging from the ceiling, and a mirror on the floor beneath it. When the light bulb turns on, it sends a beam of light down to the mirror. The light reflects off the mirror and travels back up to the light bulb. We can use this setup to measure the speed of light because we know how far the light travels (the distance between the light bulb and the mirror) and how much time it takes to get there (the time it takes for the light to turn on and off). By dividing the distance by the time, we can calculate how fast the light is moving. So, to answer your question, we know the speed of light because we can measure it using a light clock. It's a bit more complicated than that, but that's the basic idea!
