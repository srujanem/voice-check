Expiry dates are put on medications for a number of reasons. One of the main reasons is that after a certain amount of time, medications may start to break down or become less effective. This is because they may become contaminated with bacteria or other substances, or because the active ingredients may start to break down or change in some way. 
It is generally not recommended to use medications past their expiry date, because there is no way to know for sure how they will work or if they will be safe to use. In some cases, medications that are past their expiry date may be less effective or may not work at all. In other cases, they may be more harmful or toxic than they would be if they were used before the expiry date. 
If you are in need of medication and the only option you have is a medication that has expired, it is generally safer to get a new prescription from a healthcare provider than to use the expired medication. This is because your healthcare provider can help you determine if the medication is still safe to use, and can also help you find a suitable alternative if needed.
