Gold is a metal that is shiny and yellow in color. People have liked gold for a long time because it doesn't rust or tarnish and it is very pretty to look at. Gold is also very rare, which means that not a lot of it is found in the world. This makes it special and valuable. People have used gold to make jewelry and other decorative items for thousands of years. Gold is also used in some electronic devices because it is a good conductor of electricity. In the past, people would use gold as money because it was rare and valuable and could be easily traded for other things. Even today, many people still see gold as a symbol of wealth and success.
