Gorillaz is a virtual band that was created by Damon Albarn and Jamie Hewlett. The band consists of four animated members: 2D, Murdoc, Noodle, and Russel. These characters are portrayed in music videos and other media as cartoons. However, when Gorillaz performs live, they usually have real people dressed up in costumes to represent the cartoon characters on stage. The real people play instruments and sing, while the cartoon characters are projected on screens or shown on stage in some other way.
