Sure! The Higgs boson is a particle that was discovered by scientists at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), which is a giant machine that smashes particles together to see what happens. The Higgs boson is special because it helps explain why other particles have mass. You see, particles are constantly moving around and bumping into each other, but some particles are very heavy and some are very light. The Higgs boson is like a sticky goo that particles can bump into, and when they do, it slows them down and gives them mass. So the Higgs boson helps us understand how some things in the universe are very heavy and some things are very light. It's a really important discovery because it helps us understand how the universe works!
