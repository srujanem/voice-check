When we mix colors of paint, we are combining different pigments together to create a new color. Pigments are particles of color that are suspended in a liquid medium, such as water or oil. When you mix two pigments together, the particles of color combine to create a new color. For example, when you mix blue paint with yellow paint, the blue and yellow pigments combine to create a green color. 
On the other hand, when we mix colors of light, we are combining different wavelengths of light together. Light is made up of many different wavelengths, and each wavelength corresponds to a different color. When you mix two colors of light together, the wavelengths of light combine to create a new color. For example, when you mix blue light with yellow light, the wavelengths of light combine to create a white light. 
So, the reason that mixing colors of paint and mixing colors of light work differently is because they are two different things: pigments and wavelengths of light. When you mix pigments together, you are combining particles of color. When you mix wavelengths of light together, you are combining energy waves.
