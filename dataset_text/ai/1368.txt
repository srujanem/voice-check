Railroad tracks are usually built on top of a bed of crushed stone, which is known as ballast. The ballast serves several purposes: 
1. It helps to hold the tracks in place and keep them from shifting or moving out of alignment. 
2. It helps to distribute the weight of the trains evenly across the tracks, which helps to prevent the tracks from bending or breaking under the weight of the trains. 
3. It helps to drain water away from the tracks, which can help to prevent rust and other types of damage to the tracks. 
4. It helps to reduce the noise and vibration caused by trains passing over the tracks. 
So, in short, the rocks underneath the tracks help to keep the tracks stable, strong, and in good condition, which makes it safer and more efficient for trains to travel on them.
