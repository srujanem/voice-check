When you pee, the water in the toilet bowl moves around and some of it goes down the drain, which is a hole at the bottom of the toilet. The water goes through a pipe and into the sewer, which is a big underground system of pipes that carries water and waste away from houses and buildings. The water in the sewer is cleaned and treated before it is released into rivers, lakes, or the ocean. So, even though you might be adding more water to the toilet bowl when you pee, some of it is also going down the drain and into the sewer. That's why the water level in the toilet bowl doesn't go up very much, or at all. It's also why the toilet doesn't overflow and make a big mess. The drain and the pipes help to keep everything moving and flowing properly.
