Snowflakes are made of ice crystals that form in the sky when it is cold and there is moisture in the air. As the ice crystals fall from the sky and get closer to the ground, they might encounter different temperatures and humidity levels. This can cause the snowflakes to take on different shapes and patterns. Since there are so many different factors that can affect the way a snowflake forms, it is very unlikely that two snowflakes would be exactly the same. Scientists have studied snowflakes for many years and they have never found two that are exactly the same. So, while it is not impossible that two snowflakes could be exactly the same, it is very, very unlikely. That's why we say that snowflakes are unique and each one is different from all the others.
