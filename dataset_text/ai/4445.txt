When you drink alcohol, it can cause your body to lose more fluids than you take in. This can lead to dehydration, which can contribute to the feeling of being hungover. Even if you are drinking alcoholic beverages that contain mixers, like cocktails, you may still become dehydrated because alcohol can interfere with the body's ability to regulate fluid balance.\n\nTo help prevent dehydration and alleviate the symptoms of a hangover, it's important to drink plenty of water while you are drinking alcohol and also after you finish drinking. This can help to replenish the fluids that your body has lost and help you feel better the next day. It's also a good idea to avoid drinking too much alcohol in one sitting, as this can increase the risk of dehydration and the severity of a hangover.
