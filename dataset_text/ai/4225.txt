On spacecraft, electronics are grounded by connecting them to a conductor that is in contact with the surface of the planet or other celestial body the spacecraft is orbiting. This conductor acts as a reference point for the electrical current in the spacecraft's electronics and helps to dissipate any excess charge or electrical noise.\n\nWhen two spacecraft dock, they are typically isolated from each other electrically to prevent any electrical interference or damage to the electronics on either spacecraft. This is usually achieved through the use of specialized connectors that have built-in insulation to prevent electrical contact between the two spacecraft. The spacecraft may also be connected to each other through a cable that is used to transfer data and power between the two, but this cable is typically also insulated to prevent any electrical interference.\n\nIn simple terms, grounding helps to keep the electrical systems on a spacecraft running smoothly and safely, and isolating the electrical systems of two docked spacecraft helps to protect them from any potential problems.
