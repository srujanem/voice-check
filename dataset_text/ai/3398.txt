Having insurance on a priceless artwork can help cover the cost of repairing or replacing the artwork if it is damaged or destroyed. For example, if a Picasso painting gets destroyed in a fire, the insurance company may pay for the cost of repairing the painting or replacing it with a new one. This can be especially helpful if the artwork is very valuable and it would be very expensive to repair or replace it without insurance. So, in this case, having insurance on the Picasso painting would help to protect the owner's investment in the artwork and ensure that they don't have to pay a large amount of money out of their own pocket to repair or replace it.
