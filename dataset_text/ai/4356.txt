Gears are used to change the speed or torque (turning force) of a device. On a bicycle, the pedals are connected to a chain, which is connected to gears. When you pedal the bike, you are turning the chain, which in turn turns the gears. The gears are connected to the wheels of the bike, so when the gears turn, they make the wheels turn.\n\nIf the gears are bigger (have more teeth), they will be able to turn the wheels more slowly but with more force. This is useful for going up steep hills, because it allows you to pedal more easily.\n\nIf the gears are smaller, they will be able to turn the wheels more quickly, but with less force. This is useful for going faster, because it allows you to pedal faster without having to put in as much effort.\n\nBy shifting the gears on a bike, you can change the size of the gears that the chain is connected to, which changes the speed and torque of the bike. This allows you to pedal more easily or go faster, depending on what you need.
