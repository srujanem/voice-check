Photosynthesis is the fundamental biological process through which green plants, algae, and certain bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy. Using light absorbed by chlorophyll pigments, plants take in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil to synthesize glucose, a simple sugar that fuels their growth. Oxygen is released into the atmosphere as a crucial byproduct of this reaction. By forming the base of nearly every terrestrial food chain, photosynthesis sustains life on Earth while actively helping regulate global atmospheric carbon levels.
