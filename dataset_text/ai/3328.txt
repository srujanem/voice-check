A college and a university are both places where people can go to learn and get a degree. However, there are some differences between the two. A college is a smaller institution where people can go to study a specific subject or get a degree in a certain area, such as business or liberal arts. Colleges are usually part of a larger university and offer undergraduate degrees. A university is a larger institution that includes several colleges. Universities offer undergraduate and graduate degrees, and sometimes even doctoral degrees. Universities are usually more research-oriented and have a wider range of subjects and programs available for students to study. So, to sum it up, a college is a smaller place where you can get a degree in a specific subject, and a university is a larger place where you can get a degree in a wide range of subjects, including graduate and doctoral degrees.
