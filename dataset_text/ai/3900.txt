It's not uncommon for people to be afraid of arachnids, especially spiders, because they are often associated with danger or venom. Spiders, in particular, have eight legs and move quickly, which can be unsettling to some people. Additionally, some spiders can bite and inject venom, which can be painful or even harmful to humans. It's also possible that the fear of arachnids is a learned or cultural fear, meaning that people may have been taught to be afraid of them or may have grown up in a culture where spiders are feared. Other insects or creatures may not be as frightening to some people because they do not have the same associations with danger or venom.
