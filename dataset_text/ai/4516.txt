Micellar water is a type of gentle, oil-based cleansing water that is used to remove makeup and impurities from the skin. It is made up of tiny particles called "micelles" that are suspended in water. Micelles are made of surfactants, which are molecules that have a water-loving (hydrophilic) end and an oil-loving (lipophilic) end.\n\nWhen you apply micellar water to your skin, the micelles work to lift away dirt, oil, and makeup from your pores. The oil-loving end of the micelles attracts and grabs onto the oil and dirt, while the water-loving end helps to rinse it away. This makes it an effective and gentle way to cleanse the skin, especially for people with sensitive skin or who are prone to breakouts.\n\nMicellar water is typically used by applying a small amount of the liquid onto a cotton pad and gently wiping it over the skin. It can be used on its own or as part of a multi-step skincare routine.\n
