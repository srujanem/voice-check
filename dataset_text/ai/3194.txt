Transparent tape is made of a clear, thin plastic material that is sticky on both sides. When you apply the tape to frosted glass, the tape sticks to the glass and covers up the frosted surface. The frosted surface is made up of tiny scratches or patterns that scatter light in many different directions, making the glass appear hazy or blurry. When you apply the tape, it covers up these scratches and patterns, so the light can pass through the glass more easily, making it appear clearer. It's kind of like if you had a bunch of tiny scratches on a mirror, it would be hard to see your reflection because the light would get scattered in many different directions. But if you cleaned the scratches off the mirror, the light would pass through the mirror more easily and you would be able to see your reflection more clearly. That's what happens when you apply the transparent tape to the frosted glass - it helps the light pass through more easily, making the glass appear clearer.
