On Reddit, users can upvote or downvote a post to show their approval or disapproval of it. The number of upvotes a post gets is called its "score." The score is displayed next to the post, and the post is ranked on the subreddit (a subreddit is a community on Reddit centered around a specific topic) and on the website as a whole based on its score. The reason you don't see many posts with very high scores (like 200,000 or 500,000) is because it's relatively rare for a post to get that many upvotes. This is because there are a lot of factors that can influence how many upvotes a post gets, such as the popularity of the subreddit it's posted in, the quality of the content, and how interesting or relevant it is to the community. Additionally, Reddit has a feature called "score decay" which means that as a post gets older, its score will gradually decrease over time. This is to prevent old posts from dominating the front page of the website and to give newer posts a chance to be seen. So, in summary, while there are a lot of people using Reddit and viewing posts, it's still relatively rare for a post to get a very high score because there are many factors that can influence how many upvotes it gets and because score decay causes the score to decrease over time.
