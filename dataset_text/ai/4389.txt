When you think hard, your brain uses more energy and blood flow than it does when you're not thinking as hard. This increased activity can cause your blood vessels to constrict, which can lead to a headache.\n\nHeadaches can also be caused by muscle tension in the neck and shoulders, which can be a result of sitting in one position for a long time or being stressed or anxious.\n\nSometimes, a headache can be a sign of a more serious problem, like a concussion or infection. If you have a headache that lasts for a long time or is severe, you should talk to a doctor to make sure there isn't something more serious going on.
