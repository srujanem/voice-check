Older houses may have a traditional wooden door with a glass or plastic secondary door because the secondary door is used to protect the wooden door from the elements. The wooden door is typically on the inside of the house and is used as the main entrance for people to go in and out. The secondary door, which is usually on the outside of the house, is made of glass or plastic and is used to keep the wind, rain, and snow from damaging the wooden door. It also helps to insulate the house by providing an extra layer of protection from the cold and heat. So, the secondary door is kind of like a "protector" for the wooden door, and it helps to keep the wooden door in good condition for a longer period of time.
