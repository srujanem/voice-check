Income tax is a type of tax that the government collects from people and businesses based on the money they earn. In the United States, the government uses the money it collects from income tax to pay for things that are important for the country, such as schools, roads, and the military. Paying income tax is a way for people to contribute to the cost of running the country and to help pay for the things that we all need and use. The government sets rules about how much income tax people and businesses have to pay based on how much money they earn. These rules are called tax laws, and they are written down in a document called the tax code. Everyone who earns a certain amount of money is required to pay income tax. This is because the government needs a way to pay for the things it does to make our country a better place to live.
