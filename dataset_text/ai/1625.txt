Computers run faster when they are cold because heat can cause the computer's components to slow down or stop working properly. The computer has many tiny parts inside it that work together to perform tasks, and these parts can become damaged or stop working if they get too hot. When the computer is cold, the parts can work more efficiently and faster because there is less heat to interfere with their performance. On the other hand, when the computer is hot, the heat can cause the parts to slow down or stop working properly, which can make the computer run slower. It's like when you're running around outside on a hot day - you might start to feel tired and slow down because of the heat, but if you're running around in the cold, you might feel more energetic and be able to run faster.
