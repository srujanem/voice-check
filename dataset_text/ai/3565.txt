When you look up at the sky from the ground, you can see a lot of stars because there is very little light pollution on the ground to block them out. However, when you are on an airplane, you are much higher up in the atmosphere and there is more air between you and the stars. This extra air makes it harder to see the stars because the light from the stars has to pass through more of it before it reaches your eyes. Additionally, airplanes are usually brightly lit inside, which can make it harder to see the stars outside. Finally, the windows on airplanes are designed to keep the inside of the airplane pressurized and to block out some of the noise, but this also makes it harder to see outside clearly.
