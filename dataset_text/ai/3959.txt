Your brain is trying to keep you safe. When it's dark and you're alone, your brain doesn't have as much information about what's going on around you, so it starts to imagine things that might be there that could be dangerous. It's kind of like a alarm system, to make sure you're aware of your surroundings and ready to react if something bad happens. But sometimes, your brain can imagine things that aren't really there, or that are much scarier than they really are, like the monsters in a horror movie. It's okay, though - your brain is just trying to keep you safe. Just try to remind yourself that it's just your imagination, and there's nothing to be afraid of.
