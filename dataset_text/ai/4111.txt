Primate refers to a group of mammals that includes humans, apes, monkeys, and lemurs. There are no primates native to North America, because millions of years ago, the continents were in different locations than they are today. South America and Africa used to be joined together, and primates evolved in Africa. When the two continents separated, some primates ended up on South America. Similarly, Asia and North America used to be joined together, but when they separated, no primates were on the North American continent. Does that make sense?
