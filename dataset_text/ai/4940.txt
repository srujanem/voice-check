A balloon can float very high up in the sky, but it will not be able to reach space. The reason is that the atmosphere, or the air around the Earth, gets thinner and thinner the higher you go. Eventually, there is not enough air to keep the balloon floating, and it will stop rising and begin to fall back down to the ground.\n\nTo give you an idea of how high a balloon can go, hot air balloons can reach an altitude of about 10,000 to 15,000 feet, which is about 3 to 4.5 kilometers. This is much higher than most airplanes fly, but it is still a long way from space, which begins at an altitude of about 62 miles (100 kilometers) above the Earth's surface.\n\nSo, while a balloon can float very high up in the sky, it will not be able to escape the Earth's atmosphere and float away into space.
