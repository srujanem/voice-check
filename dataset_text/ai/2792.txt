Bitrate refers to the amount of data used to represent a second of audio or video. In general, a higher bitrate means that more data is used to represent the audio or video, which can result in a higher quality recording. A lower bitrate means that less data is used, which can result in a lower quality recording. Imagine that you are trying to recreate a picture using only a small number of colored dots. If you have a lot of dots, you can create a detailed and accurate picture. But if you only have a few dots, the picture will be much less detailed and may not look very good. The same idea applies to audio and video. A higher bitrate allows for more detail and a higher quality recording, while a lower bitrate results in a lower quality recording with less detail.
