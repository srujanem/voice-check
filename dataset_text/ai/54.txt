The human brain is an extraordinarily complex organ composed of roughly 86 billion neurons communicating through trillions of synaptic connections. Divided into specialized regions like the cerebral cortex, cerebellum, and brainstem, it processes sensory input, controls physical movement, and regulates vital involuntary body functions. Through neural plasticity, the brain continuously reshapes its structural pathways in response to new experiences, learning, and environmental changes. This dynamic adaptability enables us to acquire complex skills, form lasting memories, and navigate unpredictable challenges throughout our entire lives.
