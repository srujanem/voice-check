Ironic means something that is the opposite of what you expected or what you thought would happen. It is often used to describe situations that are strange or amusing because they are not what you would expect. For example, if it is raining outside and you forget your umbrella, that could be considered ironic because you needed your umbrella to protect you from the rain, but you forgot it. Another example is if you are trying to be quiet, but you accidentally make a loud noise. That could be considered ironic because you were trying to be quiet, but you ended up being loud. So, to use the word "ironic" correctly, you would describe a situation where something happens that is the opposite of what you expected or what you thought would happen. I hope that helps!
