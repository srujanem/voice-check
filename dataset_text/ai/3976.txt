There are a few reasons why solar power isn't the most widely used source of electricity yet. One reason is that it can be expensive to set up a solar power system, especially if you need to buy a lot of solar panels and other equipment. Another reason is that solar power only works when the sun is shining, so it can be difficult to use solar power at night or on cloudy days. Finally, some places just don't get very much sunshine, which makes it hard to use solar power. Despite these challenges, many people and companies are still interested in using solar power because it is a clean, renewable energy source.
