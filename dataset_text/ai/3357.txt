When a person dies from starvation, their body essentially runs out of energy. The body needs food and nutrients to function properly, and when it doesn't get enough, it starts to break down its own tissues in order to survive. This can lead to organ failure, as the body's organs aren't getting the nutrients they need to function properly. Eventually, the person's body becomes too weak to continue functioning, and they die. It's important to remember that starvation can be prevented by getting enough food and nutrients to keep the body healthy.
