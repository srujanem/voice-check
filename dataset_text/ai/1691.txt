Inflation is when the prices of things go up over time. Imagine you have a dollar and you go to the store to buy a cheeseburger. If the cheeseburger costs $0.50 today and $0.75 next month, that means the price of the cheeseburger has gone up and there has been some inflation. Inflation is considered normal because it happens naturally as an economy grows and changes. When more people are working and making money, they have more money to spend, which can lead to higher demand for goods and services. This can cause prices to go up. If inflation goes too far, it can be a problem because it can make it harder for people to afford things. When prices go up too much, it's called high inflation. To reverse high inflation, governments and central banks can use tools like raising interest rates or printing less money. So, if you can't buy a $0.25 cheeseburger anymore, it's probably because of inflation. As the economy changes and grows, prices of goods and services can go up.
