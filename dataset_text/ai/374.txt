When you are reading something while thinking about something else, it means that your brain is not fully focused on the task of reading and comprehending the text. This can make it difficult for you to retain and understand the information that you are reading. 
Your brain is able to process and understand information better when it is focused and paying attention to the task at hand. When you are distracted by other thoughts, your brain is not able to fully process and understand the information in the text, which can make it difficult for you to remember what you have read. 
It's a bit like trying to listen to someone speak while also trying to do a puzzle at the same time. It's hard to do both things well because your brain can only focus on one thing at a time. So, if you want to remember what you have read, it's important to try to clear your mind and focus on the text as you read it.
