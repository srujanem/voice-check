Websites like PayPal build a customer base by offering a service that is useful and convenient to people. In the case of PayPal, the service is a way to send and receive money online. In order to get people to trust them with their personal information, PayPal has taken several steps to ensure that their website is secure and that their customers' data is protected. 
One way that PayPal protects its customers' data is by using secure servers to store information. This means that the data is stored in a way that makes it difficult for anyone to access or steal it. PayPal also uses encryption to protect the data when it is being transmitted between their servers and the computers of their customers. This makes it harder for anyone to intercept the data and use it for nefarious purposes. 
Another way that PayPal has built trust with its customers is by offering good customer service. If a customer has a problem or a question, they can contact PayPal and receive prompt and helpful assistance. This helps to build trust and confidence in the company. 
Finally, PayPal has a reputation for being a reliable and trustworthy company. This reputation is built over time as the company consistently provides good service to its customers and handles their data responsibly. As a result, more and more people are willing to use PayPal and trust them with their personal information.
