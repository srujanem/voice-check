Diseases are caused by germs, which are tiny organisms that can make people sick. Some germs, like bacteria and viruses, are found all over the world, but others are more common in certain parts of the world.\n\nIn tropical regions, the weather is hot and humid most of the time. This type of climate is perfect for some types of germs to grow and spread. Many of the diseases that are more common in tropical regions are caused by germs that are transmitted by insects, like mosquitoes and ticks. These insects thrive in hot, humid environments, and they can easily spread the germs they carry from one person to another.\n\nIn temperate regions, the climate is not as hot and humid, so there are fewer insects that can carry and spread diseases. This is one reason why some diseases are more common in tropical regions than in temperate regions.\n\nAnother reason is that some tropical regions have less access to clean water, proper sanitation, and healthcare than temperate regions. This can make it easier for germs to spread and for people to get sick.\n\nOverall, the combination of a warm, humid climate and less access to clean water, proper sanitation, and healthcare can make it easier for diseases to spread and for people to get sick in tropical regions compared to temperate regions.
