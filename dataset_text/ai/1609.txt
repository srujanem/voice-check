World War 2 was a big war that happened a long time ago. A lot of countries were fighting against each other. Some of the countries that were fighting were called the Allies and some were called the Axis. The Allies were made up of countries like the United States, the United Kingdom, and Russia, and the Axis were made up of countries like Germany, Italy, and Japan. The war started because the Axis countries were trying to take over other countries and the Allies were trying to stop them. The war was fought in many different places, like Europe, Africa, and Asia. It was a very sad time because a lot of people lost their lives and families were separated. The war ended in 1945 when the Allies won and the Axis countries surrendered. It was a very important moment in history because it helped bring peace to the world again.
