During a fire, it is generally not safe to use the elevator because the power to the elevator may be cut off or the elevator shaft may become filled with smoke. Instead, people in skyscrapers are usually instructed to use the stairs to evacuate the building.\n\nThe stairs are typically located on the edges of the building, and people can use them to go down to the ground floor or to a designated safe area. It is important to stay calm and follow the evacuation procedures that are in place for the building. This might include staying in a specific area until it is safe to evacuate or following the instructions of a fire warden or other designated person.\n\nIt is also important to remember not to panic and to try to stay as low to the ground as possible to avoid breathing in smoke. If you are unable to use the stairs, you should try to find a safe place to wait for help, such as a room with a closed door or a window that can be opened.\n\nI hope this helps!
