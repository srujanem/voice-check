Commercial toilets are often designed to be more powerful when flushing because they are used by many people in a short period of time, and therefore need to be able to clear waste more efficiently. The plumbing system in a commercial building is also often larger and more powerful than the plumbing in a typical home, which helps to create a stronger flush. Toilet design also plays a role in how powerful the flush is. Some commercial toilets have a larger opening in the bottom of the bowl, which allows more water to flow through and helps to clear waste more effectively. Additionally, commercial toilets may use a different type of flushing mechanism, such as a pressure-assisted flush, which uses pressurized air to help push waste out of the bowl.
