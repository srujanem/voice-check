Water turns white when it is frozen because when water freezes, it forms crystals of ice. These crystals are made up of water molecules that are arranged in a specific pattern, and when light hits them, it reflects off of the surface in all directions. This is what gives ice its white appearance. Another reason that ice appears white is that it is made up of small air bubbles. When water freezes, it expands, which can cause small air bubbles to become trapped inside the ice. These air bubbles also contribute to the white color of ice. Overall, the white color of ice is due to the way that light reflects off of the surface of the ice crystals and the presence of small air bubbles inside the ice.
