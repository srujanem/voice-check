Cars are often shaped aerodynamically to help them move more efficiently through the air. This can make them faster and use less fuel. Buses, on the other hand, are not usually designed to be as fast as cars and are not typically used for long distance travel. Buses are also generally larger and heavier than cars, so they may not benefit as much from an aerodynamic design. Additionally, buses often have other design considerations, such as the need to carry a large number of passengers and the need to be easily accessible to people with disabilities.
