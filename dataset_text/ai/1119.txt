In the 1980s, many TV sitcoms featured "flashback episodes" where characters would reminisce about past events. These episodes were often used as a way to revisit memorable moments from earlier episodes, to fill time in the show's schedule, or to add an element of nostalgia for the audience. 
One reason these episodes may have been common is that they allowed the show's writers to recycle old ideas and jokes, rather than coming up with entirely new material. This could have been especially useful for shows that had been on the air for a long time, as it allowed them to revisit popular elements from earlier seasons. 
Another reason for the popularity of flashback episodes might have been the desire to appeal to viewers who had been following the show from the beginning. By revisiting past events, the show could remind viewers of why they liked the show in the first place and encourage them to continue watching. 
Overall, the use of flashback episodes was a common storytelling device in many TV shows during the 1980s, and it allowed writers and producers to reuse old ideas and appeal to the show's dedicated audience.
