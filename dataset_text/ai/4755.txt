A government bond is a type of loan that a government takes out from people or organizations. The government promises to pay back the loan with interest after a certain amount of time.\n\nFor example, let's say the government wants to borrow $100 from you. They might offer to pay you back $110 after 5 years. The $10 that they pay you back is called interest.\n\nWhen you buy a government bond, you are giving the government money to borrow. The government uses this money to pay for things like roads, schools, and hospitals. In exchange for lending them the money, the government agrees to pay you back with interest.\n\nGovernment bonds are considered to be a very safe investment because they are backed by the government.
