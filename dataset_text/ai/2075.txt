Electricity is the flow of tiny particles called electrons through materials that allow them to move freely, called conductors. Electricity is generated at a power plant, usually using heat or the movement of magnets to create it. The electricity is then sent through power lines to homes and businesses. Inside our homes and buildings, the electricity flows through wires to power lights, appliances, and other devices.
