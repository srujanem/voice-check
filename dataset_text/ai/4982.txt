When you have diabetes, your body is unable to use and store sugar (glucose) properly. When you have too much sugar in your blood, your body tries to get rid of it by excreting it in your urine. This process of getting rid of excess sugar in your urine is called "glycosuria."\n\nWhen your body is getting rid of sugar in your urine, it's also getting rid of water. This can lead to dehydration, which is when your body doesn't have enough water. When you're dehydrated, you may feel thirsty because your body is trying to tell you that it needs more water.\n\nIn addition to feeling thirsty, you may also feel tired or have dry mouth, dry skin, and dark yellow urine. These are all signs that your body is not getting enough water. It's important to drink plenty of fluids and follow your doctor's recommendations for managing your diabetes to help prevent dehydration and other complications.
