Shooter games, like Battlefield 3 and Modern Warfare 3, are not very realistic when it comes to weapons and attachments. In shooter games, however, players often have access to large amounts of ammunition and can continuously fire their weapons without needing to stop and reload. Additionally, the attachments that are available in these games, such as silencers and scopes, do not always function in the same way that they do in real life. In some cases, the attachments may not have any effect on the weapon at all, or may give the weapon abilities that it does not have in real life. Overall, while shooter games can be fun to play, it is important to remember that they are not meant to be realistic and should not be used as a source of information about real-world weapons and tactics.
