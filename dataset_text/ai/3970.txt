Protesting can be a way for people to try to make a change in their workplace or in society more generally. Sometimes, people who protest do risk losing their jobs, but they may feel that the issue they are protesting is important enough to take that risk. It's also possible that the company they work for might not want to or be able to hire more people to replace the ones who are protesting, especially if the protest is successful and leads to changes that the company agrees to. It's also important to remember that in many places, it is illegal for an employer to fire someone just for protesting, although it can still happen.
