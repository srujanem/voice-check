In a hotel with central heating and cooling (HVAC), each room typically has its own thermostat that allows the guests to adjust the temperature to their liking. The thermostat sends a signal to the central HVAC system, which then adjusts the temperature in that particular room. The central HVAC system is designed to be able to control the temperature in each room independently, so that guests in one room can set their thermostat to a different temperature than guests in another room. This is done by using a series of ducts and vents that are connected to the central HVAC system and run throughout the hotel, allowing the system to deliver hot or cold air to the various rooms as needed. The central HVAC system is also able to adjust the temperature in each room individually based on the time of day and the occupancy of the room.
