A car's maximum speed is the highest speed that it is designed to be able to reach. This maximum speed is often much higher than the speed limit on most roads because it is not safe or legal to drive at such high speeds in most places. The speed limit is the maximum speed that is allowed on a particular road or highway. It is set by the government and is meant to ensure the safety of everyone who is driving on the road. So, even though a car may be able to go faster than the speed limit, it is important to follow the speed limit and drive at a safe and legal speed. This helps to prevent accidents and keep everyone on the road safe.
