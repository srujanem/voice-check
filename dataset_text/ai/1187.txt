When you pack a cigarette, you are making the tobacco inside the cigarette more compact. This can make the cigarette burn more evenly and slowly, which can enhance the flavor and smoothness of the smoke. Packing a cigarette can also increase the intensity of the "throat hit," which is the sensation you feel in the back of your throat when you inhale smoke. This is because the packed tobacco is more densely packed, so there is more tobacco in each puff, and this can create a stronger throat hit.
