Marijuana, also known as weed, is a plant that contains chemicals called cannabinoids. Some people use marijuana for medical purposes, while others use it for recreational purposes. In many parts of the world, including the Western world, marijuana is illegal because it is considered a drug that can be harmful to people's health and well-being. There are several reasons why marijuana became illegal in the Western world. One reason is that some people believe that marijuana can be addictive and can lead to other harmful behaviors, such as driving while under the influence of drugs. Another reason is that marijuana can have negative effects on a person's mental health, such as causing anxiety, paranoia, and hallucinations. In the United States, marijuana was made illegal in the early 20th century as part of a larger movement to regulate and control the use of drugs. This movement was driven by concerns about the negative effects of drugs on society and the belief that drugs should be regulated in order to protect public health and safety. In summary, marijuana is illegal in many parts of the Western world because it is considered a drug that can be harmful to people's health and well-being.
