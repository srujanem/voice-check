Fish and sea mammals are two different types of animals. Fish use their tails, also called fins, to swim through the water. Sea mammals, like dolphins and whales, also use their tails to swim, but they move them in a different way. 
Fish have long, thin tails that they move from side to side to swim through the water. This helps them move quickly through the water and change direction easily. 
Sea mammals have much larger tails that are shaped like a paddle. They move their tails up and down to swim through the water. This helps them swim faster and stronger, especially when they need to swim long distances or dive deep into the water. 
Each of these methods of tail movement has evolved to suit the needs of the particular animal. Fish need to be able to move quickly and change direction easily in order to catch prey and avoid predators, so their tails are designed for agility. Sea mammals need to be able to swim long distances and dive deep, so their tails are designed for strength and power.
