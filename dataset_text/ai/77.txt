A dense emerald canopy shielded the forest floor from the afternoon heat, filtering sunlight into soft golden rays. Maya carefully stepped over mossy roots, trailing the faint footprints left along the muddy riverbank. Nearby, the rushing water splashed against smooth river rocks, drowning out the gentle rustle of leaves overhead. She paused, pulling out her compass to verify her direction before heading deeper into the uncharted valley. Somewhere ahead, hidden beyond the rising mist, lay the ancient stone ruins depicted in her grandfather's old field journal.
