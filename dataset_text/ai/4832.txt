Vector art is a type of digital artwork that is created using mathematical equations and lines, rather than pixels. These lines and shapes are called "vectors," and they can be easily resized and scaled without losing any quality.\n\nIn a regular picture, the color values for each pixel are stored in a grid, and when you zoom in or resize the image, you can see the individual pixels and the image becomes pixelated. With vector art, the lines and shapes are not made up of pixels, so they don't become pixelated when you zoom in or resize the image.\n\nImagine you have a picture of a house. In a regular picture, the house is made up of tiny squares called pixels, and each pixel has a specific color value. If you zoom in on the house, you can see the individual pixels and the house starts to look blocky and pixelated.\n\nWith vector art, the house is not made up of pixels. Instead, it's made up of lines and shapes that are created using math. When you zoom in on the vector art house, the lines and shapes stay smooth and clean, because they are not made up of pixels. This is why vector art looks good when it's scaled up or down – it's not made up of pixels, so it doesn't become pixelated.
