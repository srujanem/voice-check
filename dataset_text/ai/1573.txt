Bottled water does not technically expire in the same way that other food products do. However, it is still important to check the expiration date on bottled water because it can become contaminated over time. 
Water can pick up impurities from the environment, such as bacteria and minerals, as it sits in the bottle. These impurities can change the taste and quality of the water. Additionally, plastic bottles can break down over time, which can cause chemicals from the plastic to leach into the water. 
The expiration date on bottled water is usually set by the manufacturer as a way to ensure that the water is of the highest quality when it is consumed. It is not a hard and fast rule, but it is generally recommended to consume bottled water within a year or two of the expiration date to ensure that it is as fresh and clean as possible.
