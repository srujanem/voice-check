In the Christian tradition, Jesus is believed to be the son of God and the fulfillment of ancient Jewish prophecies. One of these prophecies, found in the Hebrew Bible, states that the Messiah, or savior, would be descended from the line of King David. Because Mary was engaged to be married to Joseph at the time of Jesus' conception and birth, it was traditionally believed that Joseph was Jesus' legal father and that Jesus inherited his royal lineage through Joseph. 
This belief is based on the idea that in ancient times, a person's family and ancestry were traced through the father's side. So even though Jesus and Joseph were not biologically related, Jesus was considered to be part of the family of Joseph and therefore part of the royal line of King David. 
It's important to note that this belief about Jesus' ancestry is based on tradition and faith, and it is not a scientific or historical fact.
