An electric well is a type of well that uses an electric pump to bring water up from underground and into your home. When you use water from an electric well, the pump turns on and sends water through a pipe and into your home. If you try to use water from an electric well when there is no power, the pump will not be able to turn on and the water will not be able to flow. This means that you will not be able to use any water from the well until the power comes back on. 
If you try to use water from an electric well when there is no power, it could cause damage to the pressure tank. The pressure tank is a large container that holds water under pressure. When you turn on a faucet or use water in any way, the pressure in the tank decreases. When the pressure in the tank gets low enough, the pump turns on and adds more water to the tank to bring the pressure back up. If you try to use water from an electric well when there is no power, the pump will not be able to turn on and the pressure in the tank will not be able to stay high enough. This can cause the tank to become damaged or even burst, which could cause water to leak out and cause damage to your home. 
In summary, if you try to use water from an electric well when there is no power, it will not be able to flow and it could cause damage to the pressure tank. It is important to make sure that the power is on before you try to use water from an electric well.
