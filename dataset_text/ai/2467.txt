Duracell and Energizer batteries are well-known brands that produce high-quality batteries that are designed to last longer than some off-brand batteries. These batteries are made with higher-quality materials and are subjected to more rigorous testing to ensure that they perform well and last for a long time. One reason that Duracell and Energizer batteries cost more is because the materials and processes used to make them are more expensive. These brands also invest a lot in research and development to continuously improve their products, which also contributes to the higher price. In general, it's usually a good idea to invest in high-quality batteries from trusted brands because they tend to last longer and provide more consistent performance. While they may cost more upfront, they can save you money in the long run by not having to constantly replace batteries that don't last as long.
