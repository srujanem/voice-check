Laughter is often a social response. When you are with your friends, you might feel more comfortable laughing and having fun because you are in a safe and supportive environment. When you are alone, you might not feel as inclined to laugh because there is no one there to share the experience with you. It's also possible that you might feel self-conscious about laughing when you are alone, which could make it harder to feel amused. Finally, it's worth noting that laughter is often contagious, so being around people who are laughing can make it easier to laugh yourself.
