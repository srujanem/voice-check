Sure! Water is a clear substance that lets light pass through it. That's why you can see fish and other things at the bottom of a pond or a pool. But when water is moving, like in a stream or a fountain, it can create shadows because the water is blocking the light.\n\nThink about it like this: imagine you are standing in a room with a flashlight. If you shine the flashlight on a wall, you will see a bright spot on the wall where the light is hitting it. But if you hold your hand in front of the flashlight, your hand will block the light and create a shadow on the wall. The same thing happens with a stream of water. The water is blocking the light and creating a shadow.\n\nI hope that helps! Do you have any other questions?
