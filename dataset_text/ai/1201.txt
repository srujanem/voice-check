In today's rapidly evolving world, this subject has become increasingly relevant. Plants convert sunlight into chemical energy through photosynthesis. This process occurs in the chloroplasts and involves two stages: the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle. During the light-dependent reactions, water molecules are split, releasing oxygen as a byproduct. The Calvin cycle uses carbon dioxide from the atmosphere to produce glucose, which serves as the primary energy source for the plant. Continued research and public awareness are crucial to addressing the challenges in this field.
