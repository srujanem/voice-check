Casinos are businesses, and they make money by offering games that have a built-in advantage for the house, also known as the "house edge." This means that, on average, players will lose money over time, while the casino will make a profit. In card games like blackjack, the house edge comes from the fact that the dealer has a slight advantage over the player. This is because the dealer plays last, so they have the opportunity to see what the player has and adjust their strategy accordingly. Additionally, if the player busts (goes over 21), the dealer automatically wins, even if the dealer would have otherwise lost the hand. It's also important to note that casinos often offer games with different levels of house edge, so it's possible for a player to choose a game that has a lower house edge and improve their chances of winning. However, it's still important to remember that no matter what game you're playing, the house will always have an advantage in the long run.
