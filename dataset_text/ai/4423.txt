Regulators can check if a system stores sensitive data by asking the company or individual that owns the system to provide information about what kinds of data the system handles and where it is stored. They may also inspect the system directly, or ask for documentation or evidence that the system is properly secured and follows relevant regulations.\n\nIf a system does not provide a means to read sensitive data, it may still be okay, as long as the data is properly encrypted and stored in a secure location. However, it is important for the company or individual responsible for the system to have a way to access and manage the data, in case it is needed for legal or compliance purposes.\n\nIf a system is not storing sensitive data properly, regulators may find out through inspections, audits, or reports from other sources. It is important for companies and individuals to follow relevant regulations and take appropriate steps to protect sensitive data to avoid legal consequences.
