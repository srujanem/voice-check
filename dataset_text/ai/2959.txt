Some people are able to eat a lot of food without gaining weight because their bodies are able to burn off the calories they consume. This can be due to genetics, metabolism, or other factors. On the other hand, if someone eats a lot of food and doesn't exercise, their body will likely store some of those calories as fat, which can lead to weight gain. Exercise helps to burn off calories and can help keep a person's weight in check. So, it's important to think about both what we eat and how much we move our bodies in order to maintain a healthy weight.
