The third prong on an electrical plug is called the ground prong. It's there to help protect you from getting shocked by electricity. Here's how it works: When you plug something in, electricity flows through the wires to power the device. Sometimes, there can be a problem with the device or the wires that can cause the electricity to flow in a way that it's not supposed to. If this happens, the excess electricity can escape and you can get shocked if you touch it. The ground prong is there to help prevent this from happening. It's connected to the ground, which is a special place in the earth that's safe for electricity to flow. If there's a problem and the electricity tries to escape, it can go through the ground prong instead of through you. That way, you're less likely to get shocked. So it's a good idea to have a ground prong on your electrical plugs because it helps to keep you safe.
