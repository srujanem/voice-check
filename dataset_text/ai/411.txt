Happy hour is called happy hour because it is a time when people can go to a restaurant or bar and get special deals on drinks and sometimes food. It is usually a time when people can relax and have a good time with friends after work or school. 
The reason it is not just one single hour is because it is typically a longer period of time, often lasting a few hours, when the special deals are available. This gives people more time to stop by and enjoy the deals. Some places might have happy hour every day, while others might only have it on certain days of the week or at specific times. 
So while it is called happy hour, it is not necessarily just one hour long. It is just a name for a time when people can go out and have a good time while also getting some great deals on drinks and food.
