The FBI and other law enforcement agencies work to combat child pornography and other illegal activities on the internet, including on the "deep web," which is a part of the internet that is not indexed by search engines and is not easily accessible to the general public. However, shutting down websites that distribute child pornography is a complex and resource-intensive process that requires following legal procedures and working with other countries and agencies. It's also important to understand that the internet is a global network, and websites can be hosted in any country. This means that even if the FBI is able to identify and shut down a website in the United States, it might be hosted in a country with different laws and regulations, making it more difficult to take down. Additionally, the people who operate these websites often use various techniques to try to hide their identities and locations, making it harder to track them down and prosecute them. Ultimately, it's a difficult and ongoing battle to try to eliminate illegal activities on the internet, but law enforcement agencies are working hard to try to make it as difficult as possible for these activities to take place and to hold those responsible accountable for their actions.
