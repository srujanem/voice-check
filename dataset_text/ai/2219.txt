When you hear your stomach growling, it's actually the sound of your digestive system at work. Your digestive system is responsible for breaking down the food you eat and turning it into energy for your body. As the muscles in your digestive system contract and relax, they push food through your intestines. When there's no food in your intestines, the muscles can contract more easily and make more noise. This can cause the growling sound you hear. So, when you hear your stomach growling, it means your body is getting ready to digest some food!
