All living things require some sort of energy source to survive and reproduce. For animals, one way to get energy is to breathe in oxygen and use it to break down food molecules and turn them into energy. This process, called cellular respiration, is how animals are able to move and function. As for why evolution favored animals that require oxygen and food, it's because these energy sources are very efficient and plentiful. Oxygen is abundant in the Earth's atmosphere, and there are many different types of food available for animals to eat. Using oxygen and food as energy sources allowed animals to thrive and evolve into the diverse array of species we see today. It's possible that there may have been other energy sources that could have supported life on Earth, but we don't know for sure because evolution is driven by what works best in a given environment. The animals that were able to thrive and reproduce the most successfully were the ones that passed on their traits to the next generation, leading to the development of the diverse array of species we see today.
