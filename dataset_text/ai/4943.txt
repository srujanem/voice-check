Frying food in oil makes it crispy because the heat from the oil causes the water inside the food to evaporate. As the water evaporates, it creates steam, which helps to puff up the food and make it crispy.\n\nBoiling food in water, on the other hand, causes the water to be absorbed by the food, which can make the food softer. This is because the water mixes with the food's cells, breaking down the cell walls and softening the food.\n\nSo, in short, frying in oil makes food crispy because it causes the water inside the food to evaporate and puff up the food. Boiling in water makes food softer because it causes the water to be absorbed by the food, breaking down the cell walls and softening the food.
