Gentrification is seen as a bad thing by some people because it can lead to the displacement of lower-income residents and communities of color from neighborhoods that they have lived in for generations. This is often because as gentrification occurs, property values and rents in these neighborhoods tend to increase, making it more difficult for low-income residents to afford to live there. As a result, they may be forced to move to less desirable areas or even become homeless. 
Additionally, gentrification can also lead to the loss of cultural diversity in a neighborhood as the new, more affluent residents bring in their own cultural practices and businesses, potentially leading to the displacement of long-time residents and the businesses they patronize. 
Gentrification can also lead to social tension and conflict as the new, more affluent residents may not always have the same values or priorities as the long-time residents, leading to misunderstandings and conflicts. 
Overall, gentrification can have a number of negative consequences for the people and communities that are affected by it, which is why it is often seen as a negative process.
