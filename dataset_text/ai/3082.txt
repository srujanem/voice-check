Flooding the engine refers to the act of filling the engine with too much fuel, which can cause problems and damage. When an engine is flooded, it means that there is too much fuel in the cylinders, and the engine is not able to start or run properly. This can be caused by a variety of things, such as a malfunctioning fuel pump or carburetor, a stuck open fuel injector, or a problem with the ignition system. To fix a flooded engine, you will need to remove the excess fuel and try starting the engine again. This can be done by opening the throttle (also called the "gas pedal") and cranking the engine over a few times to allow the excess fuel to burn off. You may also need to check and fix any underlying problems that caused the flooding in the first place. It's important to be careful when working on an engine, as it can be dangerous and should only be done by someone who is trained and experienced in auto mechanics. If you're not comfortable working on your own engine, it's always a good idea to seek help from a professional mechanic.
