All simple carbs, including sugar and wheat, are broken down into glucose (a type of sugar) in the body. However, the processes by which they are digested and absorbed into the body can differ.\n\nSugar is a simple carbohydrate made up of just one or two sugar molecules. It is quickly and easily broken down by the body, which is why it provides a rapid source of energy. When you eat sugar, it is absorbed into the bloodstream and used by the body for energy.\n\nWheat, on the other hand, is a complex carbohydrate made up of many sugar molecules linked together. It takes longer for the body to break down wheat and release the glucose it contains. As a result, the body gets a slower, sustained source of energy from wheat rather than a quick burst of energy like it does from sugar.\n\nBoth sugar and wheat are broken down in the digestive system and absorbed into the body through the small intestine. However, the specific process by which they are broken down and absorbed can differ. For example, the body uses different enzymes to break down sugar and wheat, and the way in which these substances are absorbed into the bloodstream can also differ.\n\nI hope that helps to explain the difference between how sugar and wheat are digested in the body!
