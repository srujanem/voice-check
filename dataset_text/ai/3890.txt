Radiation is the emission of energy as electromagnetic waves or as moving subatomic particles, especially high-energy particles that cause ionization. Radiation is made up of tiny particles called photons or particles such as electrons, protons, and neutrons. These particles can be harmful to living things, including humans, if they are exposed to high levels of radiation. When radiation is absorbed by the human body, it can cause damage to cells and tissues. This can lead to health problems, such as cancer, skin burns, and other illnesses. It's important to be careful around sources of radiation, such as nuclear power plants and medical equipment that use X-rays, and to protect ourselves from excessive exposure to radiation.
