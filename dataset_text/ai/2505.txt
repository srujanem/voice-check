Rats are often used in scientific research because they are small, easy to care for, and have a short lifespan compared to humans. They are also less expensive to use in experiments than other animals, like monkeys. In addition, rats have a physiology that is similar to humans in many ways, so they can be used to study diseases and other health conditions that affect humans. However, it's important to note that scientists also use other animals, including monkeys, in research when it is necessary to study a particular condition or disease that cannot be effectively studied in rats or other animal models. Ultimately, the choice of which animal to use in research depends on the specific research question being asked and what animal can best help answer it.
