A bubble is a situation in which the price of an asset (like a stock or a house) becomes very high compared to its normal value. This usually happens because people are willing to pay a lot of money for the asset, and so the price keeps going up and up. But eventually, the price of the asset gets so high that it becomes too expensive for most people to buy. And when that happens, fewer and fewer people want to buy the asset, and the price starts to go down. Sometimes, the price can go down very quickly, and a lot of people who bought the asset at the high price can lose a lot of money. This is what happened with the dot-com bubble in the late 1990s, when the price of tech stocks went up very quickly, but then suddenly went down just as fast. The same thing happened with the housing bubble a few years ago, when the price of houses in some areas became very high, but then suddenly started to go down. So a bubble is a situation where the price of something goes up very quickly, but then suddenly drops down again.
