There are a few reasons why business establishments might keep their lights on after business hours. One reason is for security. Having the lights on can make the store or building look occupied and deter burglars or vandals. Another reason is for convenience. If the store or building is in a busy area, the lights can help people see where they are going and make it easier to find the entrance. Finally, some businesses keep their lights on as a way to advertise. If the store or building is brightly lit, it can catch the attention of people passing by and make them more likely to come in and shop. While it is true that keeping the lights on does use energy and costs money, businesses often weigh the cost of the electricity against the benefits of having the lights on. They may decide that the benefits of having the lights on outweigh the cost of the electricity.
