In most places, it is not illegal for car manufacturers to sell directly to consumers, but it is typically not done because it would be inefficient and costly for the manufacturer to set up a network of retail locations and sales staff to sell cars directly to consumers. Instead, car manufacturers typically sell their cars to dealerships, which are independent businesses that are responsible for selling the cars to consumers. This allows the manufacturer to focus on making the cars, while the dealerships focus on selling the cars and providing customer service. It is also common for car manufacturers to have their own branded dealerships that sell only their brand of cars. One reason that some people might find car salespeople to be "assholeish" is because they are under a lot of pressure to make sales. They are paid based on how many cars they sell, so they may try to be pushy or aggressive in order to close a sale. However, not all car salespeople are like this, and it is possible to find a dealership and salesperson who are helpful and respectful.
