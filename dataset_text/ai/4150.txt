It contains a chemical called delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), which is responsible for its psychoactive effects. When THC is consumed, it can affect the way that the brain processes information and can cause changes in mood, perception, and behavior.\n\nOne of the potential side effects of using cannabis is paranoia, which is a feeling of fear or anxiety that is often accompanied by the belief that others are trying to harm or deceive you. THC can alter the way that the brain processes information and can change the levels of certain chemicals in the brain, such as dopamine and serotonin, which can affect mood and behavior. In addition, THC can interfere with the way that the brain processes and remembers events, which can lead to confusion and anxiety.\n\nIt is important to note that not everyone who uses cannabis will experience paranoia or other negative side effects. The effects of cannabis can vary from person to person, and they depend on a variety of factors, including the strain of cannabis that is being used, the method of consumption, and the individual's age, gender, and overall health.
