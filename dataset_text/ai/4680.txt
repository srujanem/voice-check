Asphalt is a type of paving material that is used on roads, parking lots, and other surfaces. It is made from a mixture of small stones, sand, and a type of oil called bitumen, which helps to bind the mixture together. Bitumen is a thick, sticky substance that is black in color, which is why asphalt is usually black as well.\n\nConcrete is another type of paving material that is made from a mixture of water, cement, and small stones. It is a very hard and durable material, but it can be more expensive to use than asphalt. Concrete is also not as flexible as asphalt, which means it can crack more easily when the ground beneath it moves or settles.\n\nAs far as the color of asphalt, black is not the only option. Asphalt can be made in a variety of colors, including red, green, and even pink. However, black is the most common color because it absorbs heat from the sun, which can help to melt snow and ice on the road more quickly in colder climates.\n\nAs for the effect of the color of asphalt on the surrounding area, it is true that dark-colored surfaces tend to absorb more heat from the sun than lighter-colored surfaces. This can make the area around an asphalt road or parking lot feel warmer, especially on hot days. However, the heat absorbed by the asphalt is typically not enough to significantly impact the temperature of the surrounding area.
