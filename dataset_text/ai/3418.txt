Sure! Radioactivity is a natural process that occurs when certain types of atoms, called radioactive isotopes, break down and release energy. This energy can be in the form of particles or waves, and it can be harmful to living things if they are exposed to too much of it. Atoms are made up of tiny particles called protons, neutrons, and electrons. Some atoms have more protons and neutrons than others, and these heavier atoms are more likely to be radioactive. When these atoms break down, they release energy in the form of particles or waves. The particles and waves released during radioactivity can be harmful to living things, including humans, because they can damage or change the cells in our bodies. When cells are damaged or changed, they may not work properly or they may grow out of control, which can lead to health problems or even cancer.
