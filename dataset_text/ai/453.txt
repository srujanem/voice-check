It is generally not recommended to try to wake someone up while they are sleepwalking, as it can be disorienting and may cause confusion or agitation. It is better to gently guide the person back to bed and try to help them return to sleep. 
Sleepwalking, also known as somnambulism, is a type of sleep disorder in which a person gets up and walks around while they are still asleep. It is more common in children, but it can also occur in adults. During sleepwalking, the person's brain is not fully awake, so they may not be able to respond normally to stimuli or understand what is happening around them. They may also act out their dreams or perform activities that are not safe, such as attempting to drive a car or leaving the house. 
If you are concerned about someone who is sleepwalking, it is important to try to keep them safe by removing any hazards from their environment and making sure that they do not leave the house. If the sleepwalking occurs frequently or causes problems, it may be helpful to speak to a healthcare professional for advice.
