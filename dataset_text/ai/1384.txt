Comets are made up of a mixture of dust, ice, and other materials, and they have a lot of this material packed into a small space. When a comet gets close to the Sun, the heat and light from the Sun cause the surface of the comet to become very hot and the ice to start to vaporize. As the ice vaporizes, it releases dust and other materials into space, creating the bright, glowing trail that we see when we look at a comet. 
Even though comets lose some of their material each time they pass by the Sun, they are so large and have so much material packed into them that it takes a very long time for them to run out of stuff to leave behind. Some comets have been orbiting the Sun for billions of years and are still going strong, leaving behind bright, glowing trails each time they pass by. 
So, even though comets do lose some of their material over time, they have enough material packed into them to last for millions or even billions of years.
