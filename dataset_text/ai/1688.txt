Martha Stewart is a famous American businesswoman, author, and television personality. In 2004, she was charged with and later convicted of certain crimes related to her involvement in a stock sale. Here's a simple explanation of what happened: Martha Stewart owned some shares of a company called ImClone Systems. One of her friends, who also owned shares of ImClone, told Martha that the company was about to announce some bad news that would likely cause the stock price to go down. Martha then decided to sell her shares of ImClone before the bad news was announced, which saved her from losing a lot of money. However, it is illegal for people who have inside information about a company (like Martha did) to use that information to make financial decisions. This is called insider trading, and it is against the law because it gives some people an unfair advantage over others in the stock market. So, Martha was charged with and later convicted of insider trading and other related crimes. She was sentenced to serve time in prison and pay a fine.
