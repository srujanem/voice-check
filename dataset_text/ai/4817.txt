A pier is a structure that is built out from the shore into a body of water, like a lake or ocean. Piers are often used for fishing, swimming, or as a place to tie up boats.\n\nTo build a pier, workers first dig a hole in the ground to create a foundation for the pier. Then they pour concrete into the hole to create a solid base for the pier. Next, they build the pier by adding support beams and decking. Finally, they attach the pier to the shore with cables or rods to keep it in place.\n\nA bridge is a structure that is built over a river, road, or other obstacle to allow people and vehicles to cross from one side to the other. There are many different types of bridges, but they all have some basic parts in common.\n\nFirst, workers build the foundation of the bridge by digging into the ground or using large concrete blocks to create a solid base. Then they add support beams and girders to create the main structure of the bridge. Finally, they add the decking, which is the surface that people and vehicles will drive or walk on.\n\nTo make sure that a bridge or a pier is strong enough to hold the weight of the people and vehicles that will use it, engineers use math and physics to calculate the amount of weight that the structure can bear. They also test the materials that are used to build the bridge or pier to make sure they are strong enough.\n\nSometimes, when a bridge or pier is being built, workers need to drain the water out of the area where they are working. This is usually done with pumps or by building temporary dams. Draining the water makes it easier for workers to access the area and build the structure.
