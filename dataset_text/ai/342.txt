Teachers are allowed to raise their voices in order to get the attention of their students, especially in a classroom setting where there may be a lot of noise or distractions. It is not generally considered appropriate for teachers to yell at students in an aggressive or abusive manner. However, it is important for teachers to have some level of authority and control in the classroom in order to create a safe and orderly environment for learning. Yelling may be used as a last resort in certain situations, but it is not meant to be a regular part of a teacher's behavior. It is important for teachers to use appropriate methods of discipline and to treat their students with respect.
