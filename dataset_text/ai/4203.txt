Solar panels are made up of photovoltaic cells, which are made of layers of materials that produce electricity when they are exposed to sunlight. Over time, the efficiency of these cells can decrease for a few reasons:\n\nDust, dirt, and other debris can accumulate on the surface of the panel, which can block sunlight from reaching the cells and reduce their efficiency.\n\nThe cells can degrade over time due to exposure to the sun's UV radiation, wind, and other elements, which can weaken their ability to produce electricity.\n\nThe connections between the cells and the rest of the solar panel system can become loose or damaged, which can reduce the amount of electricity that is produced.\n\nThe inverter, which converts the electricity produced by the solar panel into a form that can be used in your home, can also lose efficiency over time.\n\nOverall, solar panels will lose some of their efficiency over time, but this decline is usually slow and the panels can still produce electricity for many years. Regular cleaning and maintenance can help to extend the life and efficiency of your solar panels.
