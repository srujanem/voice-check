Scratching an itch feels good because it sends a message to the brain that something is being done to relieve the sensation of an itch. When we scratch, the brain releases chemicals called endorphins, which are chemicals that help to reduce the sensation of pain and can also produce a feeling of pleasure. However, scratching too much can cause the skin to become damaged, which can lead to infection or other problems. It is important to scratch an itch only enough to relieve the sensation and not so much that it causes harm to the skin.
