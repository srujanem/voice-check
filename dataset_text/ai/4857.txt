Traction control and ABS (anti-lock braking system) are two different safety features that can help you when driving in the snow.\n\nTraction control helps to prevent your wheels from slipping on the road surface by detecting when one or more of your wheels is starting to spin. When this happens, the traction control system can automatically apply the brakes to that wheel or reduce the power being sent to the wheels to help you regain traction and keep your car moving in the direction you want to go.\n\nABS, on the other hand, helps to prevent your wheels from locking up when you are braking. When you brake hard, your wheels can stop turning and start sliding on the road, which can make it difficult to steer and can cause you to lose control of your car. ABS helps to prevent this by automatically pumping the brakes for you, which helps to keep your wheels turning and allows you to maintain control of your car.\n\nOverall, traction control and ABS can both be helpful when driving in the snow because they can help you to maintain control of your car and avoid slipping and sliding on the road.
