There are many reasons why people forget things that they have read. Some possible reasons include: Lack of attention: If you are not paying attention while you are reading, your brain may not fully process the information, which can make it harder to remember. Distractions: If you are reading while being distracted by other things, it can be harder to remember what you read. Lack of understanding: If you do not fully understand the material that you are reading, it can be harder to remember it. Lack of interest: If you are not interested in the material that you are reading, your brain may not be as motivated to remember it. Lack of repetition: If you do not review the material that you have read, your brain may have a harder time remembering it. By focusing on the material that you are reading and reviewing it regularly, you can help your brain remember it better.
