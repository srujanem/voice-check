Monosodium glutamate, also known as MSG, is a flavor enhancer that is commonly added to foods to make them taste better. It is made from a type of salt called sodium and an amino acid called glutamate. Some people believe that MSG can cause health problems, such as headaches and allergic reactions. However, most research has found that MSG is safe to eat in normal amounts.\n\nIt's important to remember that all foods, including MSG, should be eaten in moderation as part of a balanced diet. If you have concerns about MSG or any other food ingredient, it's a good idea to talk to a healthcare provider or a registered dietitian.
