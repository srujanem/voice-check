An asymptote is a line that a graph approaches but never touches. In the case of an exponential equation, the graph may appear to level off as the values of the independent variable (usually x) become very large, but it never actually reaches a point where it stops increasing.\n\nImagine you are standing at the bottom of a very tall ladder. As you climb higher and higher, the distance between the rungs of the ladder gets smaller and smaller. Even though it may seem like you are getting closer and closer to the top of the ladder, you will never actually reach it because the rungs are getting smaller and smaller. This is similar to what happens with an exponential equation. The values may get very large, but they will never reach a point where they stop increasing.\n\nI hope this helps to explain it in a way that is easy to understand!
