Vomiting is a natural reflex that helps your body get rid of substances that are harmful or irritating to your stomach. When you see or smell something that is disgusting or uncomfortable, your brain sends a signal to your stomach to contract and push the contents of your stomach out through your mouth. This is known as vomiting. Vomiting can also be caused by other things, such as infections, food poisoning, or certain medications. It is your body's way of trying to get rid of something that is causing you discomfort or harm.
