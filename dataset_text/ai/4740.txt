The flu is a virus that infects the respiratory system, which includes the nose, throat, and lungs. Flu viruses can spread from person to person through the air when an infected person talks, coughs, or sneezes. They can also spread when someone touches a surface or object that has the virus on it and then touches their mouth, nose, or eyes.\n\nFlu viruses can survive for a short time on surfaces, so it's possible to get the flu if you touch something that has the virus on it and then touch your face.\n\nFlu viruses can disappear for a while and then come back, especially during the fall and winter months when people spend more time indoors and are in closer contact with each other. This is why we often see more cases of the flu during these times of year.\n\nFlu viruses can also change over time, which is why it's important to get a new flu vaccine each year. The flu vaccine helps protect against the most common strains of the virus that are expected to circulate during the flu season.
