Cooling towers for nuclear power plants are tall because they need to be able to cool large amounts of water that is used to cool the nuclear reactor. The water absorbs heat from the reactor as it flows through pipes and then it is sent to the cooling tower to be cooled down. The cooling tower works by using fans to blow air over the water, which causes the water to evaporate and cool down.\n\nCooling towers are often built in pairs because they can be used to cool the water in a nuclear power plant more efficiently. One cooling tower can be used while the other is being maintained or repaired, so that the plant can continue to operate without any interruptions. However, it is also possible for a nuclear power plant to have only one cooling tower, or even more than two. The number of cooling towers needed depends on the size of the plant and the amount of heat that needs to be removed.
