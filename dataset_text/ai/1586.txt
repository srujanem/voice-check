Intelligent design is the idea that certain features of the universe and living things show evidence of having been designed by an intelligent being, rather than having arisen through natural processes such as evolution. Creationism is the belief that God or a higher power created the universe and all living things, as described in the Bible or other religious texts. 
Intelligent design is often considered a form of creationism, because it suggests that certain aspects of the natural world were created by a higher power. However, proponents of intelligent design do not necessarily believe in the same specific religious accounts of creation as traditional creationists. Instead, they argue that the complexity and design of the natural world is best explained by an intelligent being, without necessarily specifying which being or religion is responsible. 
So, the main difference between intelligent design and creationism is that intelligent design is a more general idea that focuses on the idea of design in the natural world, while creationism is a specific religious belief about how the universe and living things were created.
