Wearing someone else's glasses will not ruin your eyes. However, it is important to use glasses that are prescribed specifically for your eyes. This is because every person's eyes are different and require a specific prescription to correct their vision. If you wear someone else's glasses, they may not have the correct prescription for your eyes, which can cause your eyes to work harder to see clearly and may cause discomfort or strain. In addition, wearing glasses that are not properly fitted for your face can also cause discomfort and may even cause damage to the glasses themselves. It is important to always wear the glasses that are prescribed for you and to have your eyes regularly checked by an eye doctor to ensure that you have the correct prescription.
