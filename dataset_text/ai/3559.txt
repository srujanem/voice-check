Elephants are not farmed in the same way that other animals, such as cows or pigs, are farmed because they are large and intelligent animals that require a lot of space and resources to care for. They also have a long lifespan, living for up to 70 years, so it would be a long-term investment to farm them. In addition, elephants have complex social and emotional lives and need to be with other elephants in order to be happy and healthy. This would make it difficult to keep them on a farm, as they would need a lot of space and social interaction with other elephants. It is also important to note that hunting elephants for their tusks is illegal in most countries, as elephants are protected under international law. Instead of hunting elephants, people are encouraged to support conservation efforts that help protect elephants in the wild and prevent them from becoming endangered.
