Velcro works by using tiny hooks and loops to hold two pieces of material together. The hooks are made of a hard, stiff material that can catch onto the loops, which are made of a softer, more flexible material.\n\nWhen you use Velcro, you might notice that it becomes a little less sticky over time. This is because the hooks and loops can start to get worn down or matted down with use. However, Velcro is designed to be very durable, so it can still hold its strength even after being used many times.\n\nOne way that Velcro stays strong is by using high-quality materials that are made to last. The hooks and loops are also usually made in a way that they can bend and flex without breaking, which helps them hold up better over time.\n\nSo, even though Velcro might not be as sticky as it was when it was brand new, it's still able to hold two things together very well, even after being used many times.
