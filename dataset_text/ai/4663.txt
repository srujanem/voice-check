When you stir a drink, you use a spoon or a stirrer to mix all the ingredients together. Stirring is a gentler way to mix ingredients and is often used for drinks that are served over ice.\n\nOne reason people might choose to shake a drink instead of stir it is because shaking can help to fully incorporate and emulsify (mix together) ingredients that might not mix well together, like citrus juice and simple syrup (a sweet syrup made with sugar and water). Shaking also adds a little bit of air to the drink, which can give it a slightly lighter and bubbly texture.\n\nOn the other hand, stirring is often used for drinks that are served neat (without ice) because it doesn't add any air to the drink and helps to keep the ingredients well mixed without diluting the drink too much.\n\nSo, the main difference between a shaken drink and a stirred one is the way they are mixed and the effect that has on the texture and flavor of the drink.
