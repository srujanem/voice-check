It's normal to feel more awake and alert when you're doing something that you enjoy, like playing video games. On the other hand, if you have to do a task that you don't find very interesting or engaging, it can be easier to feel tired or sleepy. This is because our brains release chemicals called neurotransmitters that help us feel awake and focused when we're doing something that we find enjoyable, but these neurotransmitters may not be released as much when we're doing something that we don't find as enjoyable. So, if you find your work to be less engaging than playing video games, it can be easier to feel tired while doing your work.
