It's natural for people to project their own emotions and feelings onto inanimate objects, especially when those objects have personal meaning or significance to them. For example, a person might feel attached to a stuffed animal because it reminds them of a happy time in their life, or they might feel sad when they see a commercial about a lamp being replaced because they have a sentimental attachment to the lamp. This is a common way that people cope with their emotions and try to make sense of the world around them. It's also a way for people to express their creativity and imagination by giving human-like qualities to inanimate objects.
