Being aware that people are different from each other is not a bad thing. In fact, it's an important part of being human and helps us understand and appreciate the world around us. However, it becomes a problem when we start to judge or treat people differently based on these differences. This is called racism, and it can cause a lot of harm and hurt to people. It can make people feel bad about themselves, and it can even lead to violence and conflict. So, while it's important to be aware of our differences, it's also important to respect and value everyone as equals.
