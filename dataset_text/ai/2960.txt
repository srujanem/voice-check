Waves are a type of disturbance that travels through space and matter. They can be found all around us and are often associated with the movement of energy. There are many different types of waves, including sound waves, light waves, and radio waves. Each type of wave has its own unique properties and behavior. Sound waves are waves that travel through the air (or through any other medium, such as water or metal). They are caused by vibrations, like when you speak or play music. The vibrations create waves of pressure that travel through the air and can be heard by your ears. Light waves are a type of electromagnetic wave that travels through empty space. They are made up of electric and magnetic fields that vibrate at the same time. Light waves are what allow us to see things, because they bounce off of objects and enter our eyes. Radio waves are a type of electromagnetic wave that is used for communication. They are used to transmit information, such as music or talking, through the air or through space. Radio waves are used in many different ways, including in radios, TVs, and cellphones. In general, waves are able to travel through different materials and are often used to carry information or energy from one place to another.
