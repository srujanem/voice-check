Netflix pays movie and TV companies to license their movies and shows, which means they are paying for the right to stream the content on their platform. When a company licenses content from another company, they usually agree to pay a certain amount of money for a certain period of time. For example, Netflix might pay a TV network or movie studio a certain amount of money to be able to stream a popular TV show for a year. The amount of money that Netflix pays for a license depends on a number of factors, including the popularity of the content, the length of the license, and the number of people who will be able to watch the content. Netflix also pays more for content that they produce themselves, because they have to pay for the cost of making the show or movie. So, to sum it up, Netflix pays movie and TV companies to be able to show their content to its subscribers. This is how Netflix is able to offer such a wide range of movies and TV shows to its users.
