The side view mirrors in cars are designed to make objects appear closer because it helps the driver see things that are behind and to the side of the car better. When you look in the mirror, you can see more of what's behind you, and it seems like the objects are closer than they really are. This is because the mirror is curved, and it reflects the objects in a way that makes them appear closer than they actually are.\n\nHere's a simple way to understand it: Imagine you are standing in a room and you want to see what's happening outside. You could just open the door and look outside, but that might not give you a very good view. Instead, you could use a mirror to see what's happening outside. If you hold the mirror up to the window, you can see more of what's happening outside because the mirror reflects the view from the window. Just like the mirror in the room, the side view mirrors on a car help the driver see more of what's happening around the car.
