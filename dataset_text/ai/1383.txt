Game shows get the money to give away prizes from a few different sources. One way is through sponsorships, where companies pay to have their products or services advertised on the show. This money helps to cover the cost of the prizes and other expenses related to producing the show. 
Another way game shows can generate money is through ticket sales or other forms of revenue from the live audience. For example, if a game show tapes in front of a live studio audience, the show may charge for tickets to the taping. 
Finally, game shows can also generate revenue through the sale of broadcasting rights to networks or streaming platforms. The networks or platforms that air the show will typically pay a fee to the producers of the show in exchange for the right to air it. 
Overall, game shows are able to give away money and prizes because they generate revenue from a variety of sources, including sponsorships, ticket sales, and the sale of broadcasting rights.
