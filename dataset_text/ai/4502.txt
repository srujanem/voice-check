Companies often set goals to phase out certain products or practices by a certain date because it can take time to make the necessary changes. For example, if a company like Starbucks wants to get rid of plastic straws, they need to find a suitable replacement that meets the needs of their customers and is also environmentally friendly. They also need to make sure that all of their stores are able to switch to the new product, which may require training employees and updating processes and systems. Making these changes can take time, so the company sets a goal to have the change completed by a certain date.
