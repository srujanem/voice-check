When you drive up to a drive-thru window, there are usually sensors or cameras that detect when a car has stopped at the window. The person working at the drive-thru window will see a notification on their computer or device that tells them that there is a car waiting at the window, and they will begin the process of taking your order. Some drive-thru windows may also have a speaker system that allows the person working at the window to communicate with you through a microphone and speakers, so you can place your order and pay for your food.
