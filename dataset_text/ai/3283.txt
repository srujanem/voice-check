Wipeout is a game show where people compete in an obstacle course that includes a lot of challenging and physically demanding activities. While the course is designed to be challenging and physically demanding, it is also designed with safety in mind. The people who participate in the show are trained and prepared for the challenges they will face, and they wear protective gear, such as helmets and padding, to help protect them from injury. In addition, there are medical professionals on hand to provide any necessary medical attention if someone does get hurt. Even though the show can be physically demanding and people sometimes take hard falls, the participants are generally able to walk away without serious injury because of the precautions that are in place to keep them safe.
