Oversleeping is when you sleep more than your body needs. Everyone's sleep needs are different, but most adults need about 7-9 hours of sleep per night. If you sleep more than this, you might feel groggy or tired because your body has already gotten the rest it needs. Sleeping is important for our bodies to rest and repair themselves. When we sleep, our bodies release chemicals that help us feel rested and alert when we wake up. But if we sleep for too long, our bodies might not have a chance to fully complete this process, which can lead to feeling tired or groggy. So, it's important to get enough sleep, but not too much. If you're feeling tired or groggy after oversleeping, try setting a consistent bedtime and wake-up time, and aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night. This can help you feel more rested and alert during the day.
