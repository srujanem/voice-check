Restarting a computer can often help solve problems because it gives the computer a fresh start. When you use your computer, it stores lots of information in its memory and uses that information to help it run more quickly. However, sometimes that information can get "cluttered" or become outdated, which can cause the computer to slow down or have other problems. Restarting the computer clears all of that information out of the memory, which can help the computer run more smoothly. It's kind of like starting a race with a clean slate - you're starting fresh and don't have any old, unnecessary information weighing you down.
