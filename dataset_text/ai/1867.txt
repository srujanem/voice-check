The front page of Reddit, also known as the "homepage," is a list of the most popular and active discussions on the site. These discussions are organized into communities called "subreddits," and users can subscribe to and unsubscribe from different subreddits to customize their homepage. The algorithm that determines which discussions appear on the front page takes into account a number of factors, including the number of upvotes (likes) and downvotes (dislikes) a discussion has received, the amount of activity it has received (such as comments or new posts), and the age of the discussion. For logged-in users, the front page will include discussions from all of the subreddits that the user is subscribed to. The algorithm works the same way for all users, whether they are logged in or not. It's just that logged-in users will see a customized version of the front page that only includes the subreddits they are interested in, while logged-out users will see the front page for the entire site.
