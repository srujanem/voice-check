Zeitgeist is a German word that means "the spirit of the times" or "the general cultural, intellectual, ethical, spiritual, or political climate within a nation or even specific groups, along with the general ambiance, morals, sociocultural direction, and mood associated with an era." In other words, it refers to the dominant ideas, values, and beliefs of a particular time and place. For example, the zeitgeist of the 1960s in the United States might be described as one of social and political upheaval, with the civil rights movement and the anti-war movement being two major forces shaping the culture of the time. So, in a sense, the zeitgeist is like the "mood" or "vibe" of a particular time and place, and it can have a big influence on the way people think and act.
