Let us explore the fundamental aspects of this important subject in a structured manner. Gravity is the fundamental force of attraction between objects with mass. Described by Einstein's general theory of relativity as the curvature of spacetime, it governs the motion of planets, stars, and galaxies. On Earth, gravity gives objects weight and causes them to fall at approximately 9.8 meters per second squared. The escape velocity — the speed needed to break free from Earth's gravitational field — is approximately 11.2 kilometers per second. It is evident that this topic will continue to shape our world in significant ways going forward.
