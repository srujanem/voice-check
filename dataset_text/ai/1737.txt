Sure! Evolution is the process by which living things change over time. It happens because of natural selection, which is a process that occurs because certain traits (or characteristics) are more helpful for survival in a particular environment. These traits are passed down from one generation to the next, and over many generations, they can become more common in a population. Monkeys are evolving right now, just like all other living things. In fact, all living things are constantly evolving, because natural selection is always happening. However, the process of evolution is slow, and it can take many generations for changes to become noticeable.
