Billionaires typically do not keep their money in cash form, because it would be difficult to store and transport such a large amount of money. Instead, they keep their money in financial instruments such as stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and real estate. These financial instruments can be easily bought and sold, and they can also generate additional income through dividends or rent. 
The value of a billionaire's net worth is determined by the value of all of the financial instruments and assets that they own. This value can fluctuate based on changes in the market value of these assets. For example, if the value of a billionaire's stocks goes up, their net worth will also go up. On the other hand, if the value of their stocks goes down, their net worth will go down as well. 
Billionaires may also keep some of their money in bank accounts or other types of financial institutions, such as investment firms, to manage their wealth and make it easier to access when they need it.
