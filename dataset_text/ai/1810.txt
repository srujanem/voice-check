Sure! A cousin is a relative who is the child of your aunt or uncle. So, if your mom's sister has a child, that child is your cousin. Second cousins are the children of your cousins. So if your cousin has a child, that child is your second cousin. Removed cousins are a little more complicated. When people are "removed," it just means that they are not as closely related as other cousins. For example, if you and your cousin are first cousins (meaning you have the same aunt or uncle), and your cousin has a child, that child is your first cousin once removed. This is because you are one generation apart from each other. If your cousin's child has a child, that child is your second cousin once removed. This is because you are two generations apart.
