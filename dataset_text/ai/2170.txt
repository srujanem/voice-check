Sure! Recursion is a way of solving a problem by breaking it down into smaller and smaller pieces, until you reach a point where the problem is simple enough to be solved directly. Imagine you have a big pile of toys, and you want to count how many toys you have. One way to do this is to count the toys one by one. But if you have a lot of toys, this could be very time consuming. Instead, you could try using recursion. You could divide the pile of toys into two smaller piles, and count the toys in each pile separately. Then you could add the two numbers together to get the total number of toys. This process of dividing a problem into smaller pieces and solving each piece separately is called recursion.
