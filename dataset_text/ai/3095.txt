Rent seeking is a term used in economics and political science to describe the use of resources to try to obtain economic gain without creating any new wealth. It is often associated with attempts to use political influence or other non-productive activities to shape government policy or regulations in a way that benefits a particular group or individual. In Nigeria, rent seeking may take many forms, such as lobbying for government contracts, using connections to obtain favorable treatment or regulation, or using political power to shape policies that benefit a particular group or individual. Imagine a group of people who want to build a new amusement park in a certain area. They might try to use their political connections or financial resources to influence the government to give them special treatment, such as tax breaks or exclusive access to land, in order to make it easier for them to build the park. This would be an example of rent seeking, because the group is trying to use their resources to obtain economic gain without creating any new wealth. Overall, rent seeking is generally considered to be harmful to society, because it can lead to policies that are not in the best interests of the general public and can create inequality.
