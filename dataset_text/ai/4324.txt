Light is made up of tiny particles called photons that travel through the air and are perceived by our eyes as different colors. The colors we see are determined by the wavelengths of the light, with shorter wavelengths appearing as blue or violet, and longer wavelengths appearing as red or orange. Black, on the other hand, is the absence of light. So, in a sense, you could say that black is the absence of color.\n\nTo answer your question, light itself cannot be black because it is a source of illumination and is made up of photons, which are particles of energy that are responsible for the perception of light. However, an object can appear black if it absorbs all of the light that hits it and doesn't reflect any of it back to our eyes. This is why some objects, like black clothing or a black car, can appear very dark and absorb light instead of reflecting it.\n\nI hope this helps to explain the concept!
