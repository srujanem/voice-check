Our eyes are designed to focus on one thing at a time, which helps us see things clearly. If our eyes could move independently and look in different directions at the same time, it would be difficult for our brains to process the information and make sense of it. Instead, our eyes are able to move together in a coordinated way, allowing us to focus on one thing and see it clearly. This helps us see the world around us more effectively and makes it easier for our brains to understand what we're looking at.
