FM stands for "Frequency Modulation" and AM stands for "Amplitude Modulation." These are two different ways that radio stations can transmit their signals through the air. 
In FM radio, the frequency of the radio wave is changed to transmit the sound. This results in a clearer and higher-quality sound. FM is often used for music because it can transmit the full range of sound, from the lowest bass notes to the highest treble notes. 
In AM radio, the amplitude (or strength) of the radio wave is changed to transmit the sound. This results in a lower-quality sound, but it can transmit the signal over longer distances. AM is often used for talk radio because the human voice can be easily understood even with a lower-quality sound. 
FM is generally more popular than AM because it offers a higher-quality listening experience. However, AM radio is still used and can be a good choice for certain types of programming, such as news and talk radio.
