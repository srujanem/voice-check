Pilot cabins in planes have so many buttons and switches because planes are very complex machines that need to be able to perform a wide variety of tasks. These buttons and switches allow the pilots to control all of the different systems and functions of the plane, such as the engines, the navigation systems, and the communication systems.\n\nTouchscreens can be used to control some of these systems, but they can't replace all of the buttons and switches. This is because some tasks, like turning on the engines or changing the altitude of the plane, need to be done quickly and accurately. It's easier for pilots to do this by pressing a physical button or switch than it is to try to tap a screen while they are flying the plane.\n\nAdditionally, touchscreens can be difficult to use when a plane is shaking or moving around, which can happen during turbulence or when the plane is taking off or landing. Physical buttons and switches are easier to use in these situations because they don't require precise finger movements.\n\nOverall, the buttons and switches in the pilot cabin are there to help the pilots control the plane safely and effectively. While touchscreens can be useful in some cases, they can't replace all of the buttons and switches that are needed to fly a plane.
