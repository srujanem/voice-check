When a breeze blows on your skin, it can help to evaporate sweat from your body. This process of evaporation cools your skin, which is why a breeze can feel cool, even if the air itself is hot. Humidity can affect how cool a breeze feels because high humidity means that there is a lot of moisture in the air already. When the air is already very moist, it is harder for sweat to evaporate from your skin, which means that the breeze will not be as effective at cooling you down. On the other hand, if the air is dry, it is easier for sweat to evaporate, so the breeze will feel cooler.
