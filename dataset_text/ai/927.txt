The National School Lunch Program is a government program that helps make sure that kids in the United States have healthy meals to eat at school. The program provides money to schools so that they can serve lunches that meet certain nutritional standards. These lunches are usually less expensive than lunches that kids can buy at a restaurant or that they can bring from home. This means that families who might not be able to afford to buy their kids lunch every day can still send their kids to school with a healthy meal. The program is important because it helps make sure that all kids have enough to eat, which is important for their health and learning.
