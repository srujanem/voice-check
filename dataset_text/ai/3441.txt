Itchy skin, or pruritus, is a common condition that can be caused by a variety of things. When your skin feels itchy, it's because your body is trying to tell you that something is not right. One common cause of itchy skin is dryness. When your skin doesn't have enough moisture, it can feel dry, tight, and itchy. This is especially common during the winter months when the air is dry, or if you take long hot showers or baths. Another common cause of itchy skin is an allergic reaction. Your body may react to something you touched or ate by causing your skin to become itchy, red, and inflamed. This can be caused by things like poison ivy, certain foods, or even some types of makeup or laundry detergent. Itchy skin can also be caused by certain medical conditions, such as eczema, psoriasis, or dermatitis. These are all skin conditions that can cause your skin to become itchy, red, and inflamed. So, to sum it up, there are many things that can cause your skin to be itchy. It could be because your skin is dry, you have an allergic reaction, or you have a medical condition that is causing your skin to become itchy.
