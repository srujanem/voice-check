Orange juice is often kept in the refrigerated case near the milk at the supermarket because it is a perishable product that can spoil if it is not kept cold. This is especially true for orange juice that is not made with preservatives, as it can go bad more quickly at room temperature. On the other hand, apple, grape, cranberry, tomato, and other types of juice are often made with preservatives that help to extend their shelf life. As a result, these types of juice can be kept on a shelf at room temperature without spoiling as quickly. 
It's also worth noting that orange juice is a popular breakfast drink and is often consumed chilled, so it makes sense to store it in a place where it can be easily accessed and kept cold. Other types of juice, on the other hand, may be consumed at any time of day and may not need to be kept as cold, so they can be stored on a shelf in the middle of the store.
