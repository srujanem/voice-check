Mixing and mastering are two important processes that are used to improve the sound quality of a piece of music. Mixing is the process of combining different audio tracks together to create a final version of a song. Each track in a song is usually a separate recording of a different instrument or vocal, and mixing is the process of adjusting the volume, panning, and effects of each track to make them all fit together seamlessly. Mastering is the process of taking the final mixed version of a song and making it sound as good as possible for distribution. This can involve adjusting the overall volume and balance of the song, as well as adding certain audio effects to give the song a polished, professional sound. In short, mixing is about making all the different parts of a song work together, and mastering is about making the final version of the song sound as good as possible. Both processes are important for creating a high-quality finished product.
