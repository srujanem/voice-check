To bake a classic French quiche with a silky custard filling, start by blind-baking a flaky pastry crust until golden. In a bowl, whisk together four large eggs, one cup of heavy cream, and half a cup of whole milk until thoroughly blended. Season the mixture with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of ground nutmeg. Layer cooked bacon pieces, sautéed leeks, and shredded Gruyère cheese inside the baked crust, then pour the custard carefully over the top. Bake at 375°F until the center is set and slightly golden.
