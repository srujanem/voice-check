On Twitter, hashtags are words or phrases preceded by a "#" symbol. They are used to categorize tweets and make it easier for other users to find tweets about a particular topic. For example, if you tweet about your love of pizza, you might include the hashtag #pizza. This would allow other users who are interested in pizza to easily find your tweet by searching for the hashtag #pizza. Hashtags are also often used to participate in "Twitter chats," which are online conversations that take place on Twitter around a particular topic. For example, there might be a Twitter chat about education, and participants in the chat might use the hashtag #edchat to participate in the conversation. So when you see a tweet with a bunch of hashtags, it's usually because the person tweeting is trying to categorize their tweet and make it easier for other users to find it, or they are participating in a Twitter chat.
