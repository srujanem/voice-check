The pyramids of Egypt have survived for thousands of years because they were built to last. They are made of large blocks of stone, which are much less prone to weathering and erosion than softer materials like sand or dirt. The stones were also cut and fitted together very carefully, which has helped them to stay in place over time. 
The pyramids are also located in a desert region, where there is not a lot of rain or other types of weather that can cause erosion. The dry climate has also helped to preserve the pyramids, as there is no moisture in the air to wear away the stones. 
In addition, the pyramids are located in a relatively isolated area, which means they are not exposed to a lot of foot traffic or other types of wear and tear that can damage them. All of these factors have contributed to the pyramids' longevity, and that's why they are still standing after all these years.
