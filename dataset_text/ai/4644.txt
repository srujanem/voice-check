A drug overdose occurs when someone takes too much of a medication or illegal drug. This can be accidental, but it can also be intentional if someone is trying to harm themselves.\n\nWhen someone takes too much of a drug, it can cause their body to have a harmful reaction. The drug can affect the way their organs work, and this can lead to serious health problems or even death.\n\nFor example, if someone overdoses on a drug that affects their breathing, it can make it hard for them to get enough air. This can cause their brain to not get enough oxygen, which can lead to brain damage or death.\n\nSimilarly, if someone overdoses on a drug that affects their heart, it can cause their heart to beat too fast or too slow, which can also be dangerous.\n\nIt's important to always follow the instructions on a medication label and to only take the amount of a drug that your doctor or pharmacist recommends. If you or someone you know has taken too much of a drug and is experiencing serious health problems, it's important to get medical help right away.
