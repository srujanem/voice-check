Your body has a natural clock called a circadian rhythm that helps regulate when you feel awake and when you feel tired. This clock is set based on the pattern of light and dark in the environment, so when it's dark at night, your body gets ready for sleep, and when it's light in the morning, your body gets ready to wake up. Even if you've had a full night of sleep, your body might still be in sleep mode when you wake up at 6 am, which is why you might feel tired. It's important to try to go to bed and wake up at the same time every day to help regulate your body's clock and make it easier to wake up feeling rested.
