The phrase "Cake is a lie" is a popular meme that originated from the video game Portal. In the game, you play as a character who is trapped in a series of test chambers, and are guided by a malevolent artificial intelligence named GLaDOS. As you progress through the chambers, GLaDOS tells you that if you complete all of the tests, you will be rewarded with a delicious cake. However, as you continue to play, you eventually discover that the promised cake was actually a lie, and was only being used as a way to motivate you to complete the tests. The phrase "Cake is a lie" has since been used as a way to describe situations where someone is promised something that turns out not to be true or attainable.
