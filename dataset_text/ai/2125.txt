It's completely normal to feel scared or nervous in situations that are unfamiliar or feel potentially dangerous, like going up a dark staircase. When we're in a dark or unfamiliar place, our brains naturally try to protect us by looking out for potential threats. Our brains are very good at noticing changes in our environment, and it's possible that when we're going up the stairs in the dark, our brains might be trying to figure out if there's anything behind us that could be a threat. It's also possible that our imaginations might play a role in how we feel when we're in the dark. When we can't see what's around us, it's easy for our minds to fill in the blanks with things that might be scary or dangerous. It's important to remember that it's okay to feel scared in certain situations, and it doesn't make you weak or less brave. It's just your brain and body's way of trying to keep you safe. If you're feeling scared, it can be helpful to try to find a way to make the situation feel safer, like turning on a light or having someone with you.
