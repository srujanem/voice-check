In the original Doom game, the player character's gun was fixed in place and couldn't be aimed up or down. This was primarily because the game was designed to be played on a flat 2D plane, and the designers wanted to keep the gameplay simple and straightforward.

Additionally, the game's graphics and engine were not designed to accommodate player characters aiming up or down, so it would have been difficult or impossible to implement this feature without significant changes to the game. 
Overall, the decision to not allow players to aim up or down in Doom was likely a combination of design and technical limitations, and was made to keep the gameplay focused on fast-paced action and simple controls.
