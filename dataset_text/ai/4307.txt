Immunotherapy is a type of medical treatment that helps your body's immune system fight diseases, like cancer or infections. Your immune system is a natural defense system that helps protect your body from harm. It is made up of cells, tissues, and organs that work together to defend your body from infections and other harmful substances.\n\nImmunotherapy works by boosting your immune system or by helping it work better. For example, some immunotherapies help your immune system recognize and attack cancer cells, while others help it to better fight infections.\n\nThere are different types of immunotherapy, and they work in different ways. Some immunotherapies help your body's immune system to work better by giving it extra help, like special proteins called antibodies. Other immunotherapies help your immune system to work harder by stimulating it to attack cancer cells or infections more aggressively.\n\nImmunotherapy can be used to treat many different diseases, including cancer, infections, and autoimmune disorders. It is often used in combination with other treatments, like chemotherapy or radiation, to help improve the chances of recovery.
