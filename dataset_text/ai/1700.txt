Choose your investments: Once you have a brokerage account, you'll need to decide what to invest in. It's generally a good idea to diversify your portfolio by investing in a mix of different types of investments. Start small: When you're just starting out, it's a good idea to start small and gradually build up your investment portfolio over time.
