When you breathe in, your body takes in oxygen from the air and delivers it to your cells. Your cells use the oxygen to break down glucose (a type of sugar) and other nutrients in a process called cellular respiration. During cellular respiration, your cells produce energy and also produce carbon dioxide as a byproduct. The carbon dioxide is then exhaled out of your body when you breathe out.\n\nSo, to sum it up, the carbon dioxide that you exhale comes from the process of your body breaking down food and producing energy. It is not something that you directly inhale, but is produced by your cells as a byproduct of cellular respiration.
