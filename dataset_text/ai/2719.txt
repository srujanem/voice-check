Sure! Copyright infringement and piracy are both related to the unauthorized use of someone else's creative work. Here's how they differ: Copyright infringement occurs when someone uses someone else's copyrighted work without permission. This might include reproducing, distributing, or publicly displaying the work. For example, if you make copies of a book and sell them without the author's permission, you would be committing copyright infringement. Piracy, on the other hand, refers specifically to the unauthorized distribution of copyrighted material, usually in digital form. This might include downloading or sharing music, movies, or other media without paying for it. Piracy is a type of copyright infringement, but it is a more specific kind of unauthorized use. In both cases, the person using the work without permission is breaking the law and could face legal consequences. It's important to respect the rights of creators and only use their work with their permission.
