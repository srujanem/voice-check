When a crime has been committed, the police try to find the person who did it. One way they try to do this is by showing a group of people to the person who witnessed the crime and asking if they recognize anyone. This is called a "police lineup." The people in the lineup are called "fillers." Fillers are usually people who look similar to the suspect and are around the same age and height. They are not paid actors. Sometimes, the police will ask people who work at the police station or people who have been arrested for a different crime to be in the lineup. These people are not suspects in the crime being investigated. They are just helping the police with their investigation.
