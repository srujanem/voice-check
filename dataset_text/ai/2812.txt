Olive oil is a type of fat that is good for you in moderation. It is high in monounsaturated fats, which can help to lower cholesterol and reduce the risk of heart disease. Olive oil is also a good source of antioxidants, which can help to protect your cells from damage. It is generally recommended to consume olive oil in moderation, as it is high in calories and can contribute to weight gain if consumed in large amounts. A good rule of thumb is to use it as a substitute for other types of fat, such as butter or vegetable oil, and to limit your intake to about 2 tablespoons per day. Overall, olive oil can be a healthy addition to your diet when consumed in moderation and used as a replacement for unhealthy fats. Just be sure not to overdo it, as too much of any type of fat can be unhealthy.
