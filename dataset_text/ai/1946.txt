Kbps stands for "kilobits per second." It is a measure of the amount of data that is transferred in a second. In the context of music, kbps is often used to describe the quality of an audio file. Higher kbps means that there is more data in the file, which can result in a higher quality sound. Imagine that you have a bucket of water and you want to fill up a small cup with water. If you pour the water very slowly, it will take a long time to fill up the cup. But if you pour the water quickly, it will fill up the cup faster. The same is true for data. If you have a high kbps, it means that the data is being transferred quickly, which can result in a higher quality sound. So, when people say that they want a song to be 320 kbps, they are saying that they want the highest quality version of the song. They want all of the data to be transferred quickly so that they can hear the best quality sound.
