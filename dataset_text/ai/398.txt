There are many factors that can affect the lifespan of different animals. One of the most important factors is the size of the animal. Generally speaking, larger animals tend to live longer than smaller animals. For example, elephants can live for several decades, while mice typically only live for a few years. 
Another important factor is the type of environment in which the animal lives. Some animals, like sharks, live in environments that are relatively stable and provide them with the resources they need to survive. Other animals, like spiders, may live in environments that are more variable and can be more challenging to navigate. This can affect their lifespan. 
There are also many other factors that can affect an animal's lifespan, including its diet, the presence of predators, and the presence of diseases. Some animals have evolved to have longer lifespans as a way of increasing their chances of survival and reproducing, while others may have shorter lifespans because it is more advantageous for them in their particular environment.
