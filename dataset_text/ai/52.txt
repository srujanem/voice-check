The Renaissance was a transformative cultural movement that originated in fourteenth-century Italy before spreading across Western Europe. Characterized by a revived interest in classical Greek and Roman philosophy, literature, and art, the period placed a newfound emphasis on humanism and individual potential. Masters like Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, and Raphael introduced revolutionary artistic techniques, including linear perspective and realistic anatomical detail. This intellectual flowering laid the foundations for modern science, political theory, and artistic expression, marking the decisive transition from the Middle Ages into the modern era.
