When a helicopter loses power to the main rotor, it will begin to descend rapidly toward the ground. However, the pilot can use a technique called autorotation to safely land the helicopter.\n\nHere's how it works:\n\nThe pilot shuts off the engine and allows the helicopter to descend.\n\nAs the helicopter falls, the main rotor blades continue to spin due to the air rushing past them. This is called autorotation.\n\nThe pilot uses the tail rotor to control the direction of the descent and guides the helicopter towards a suitable landing spot.\n\nJust before landing, the pilot pulls back on the controls to flare the helicopter and slow its descent. This helps to cushion the impact when the helicopter touches down.\n\nOverall, autorotation is a way for the pilot to use the natural movement of the helicopter and the force of gravity to safely land the aircraft when the engine is no longer providing power to the main rotor.
