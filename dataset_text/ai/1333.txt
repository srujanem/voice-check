When you chew and swallow food, your body starts to digest it, which means that it breaks down the food into smaller pieces that your body can use for energy. This process releases chemicals in your brain that make you feel satisfied and happy. 
On the other hand, when you chew food and spit it out, your body doesn't get to experience the full process of digestion, so it doesn't release those happy chemicals. That's why chewing and spitting out food doesn't feel as satisfying as chewing and swallowing it. It's like you're only getting part of the experience.
