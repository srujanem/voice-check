An overbite is a dental condition where the top front teeth protrude out too far over the bottom front teeth. This can cause a number of problems, both medical and cosmetic.\n\nFrom a medical perspective, an overbite can cause difficulty biting and chewing food properly, leading to problems with digestion. It can also cause pain in the jaw joint, known as the temporomandibular joint, or TMJ. An overbite can also cause the teeth to wear down unevenly, leading to problems with tooth decay and gum disease.\n\nFrom a cosmetic perspective, an overbite can affect the appearance of a person's smile, which can have a negative impact on their self-esteem.\n\nOverall, it is important to address an overbite in order to maintain good oral health and avoid potential problems down the road.
