A chancellor is a high-ranking official or leader in a university or a country. A president is also a high-ranking official or leader, but in a different type of organization or country.\n\nIn a university, the chancellor is often the head of the university and is responsible for managing the university's affairs and making important decisions. The president of a university is also a leader, but they might have a different role and be responsible for different things. Some universities have both a chancellor and a president, and in these cases, the chancellor might have a more ceremonial role, while the president is responsible for the day-to-day operations of the university.\n\nIn a country, the chancellor is the head of the government, similar to a prime minister. The president is also a leader, but they might have a different role, such as serving as the head of state or representing the country in foreign affairs. Some countries have both a chancellor and a president, and in these cases, the chancellor might be responsible for leading the government, while the president has a more ceremonial role.\n\nOverall, the main difference between a chancellor and a president is the type of organization or country they lead and the specific responsibilities they have.\n
