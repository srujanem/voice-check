To repave an airport runway, the airport must close the runway to all aircraft. This is because it is not safe for planes to take off or land on a runway that is being worked on. Before the runway can be repaved, the old pavement must be removed. This is usually done by a machine called a milling machine, which grinds off the top layer of the pavement. Next, the surface of the runway is prepared for the new pavement. This may involve smoothing out any rough spots or filling in any holes or cracks. Once the surface is ready, a new layer of pavement is laid down. This is usually done by a machine called a paving machine, which spreads out a layer of asphalt or concrete. After the new pavement has been laid, it must be allowed to cure or harden for a certain amount of time before the runway can be reopened to planes. While the runway is closed, planes may need to be diverted to other airports. This can cause delays and disruptions to flights, so it is important to plan runway repaving carefully.
