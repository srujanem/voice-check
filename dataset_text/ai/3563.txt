Cancerous tumors can often be surgically removed, but it is not always possible to completely remove all of the cancerous cells. This is because cancer cells can sometimes spread to other parts of the body before a tumor is found, and it can be difficult for surgeons to know exactly where all of the cancer cells are located. In addition, some tumors are located in areas of the body that are difficult to reach or operate on, such as deep within the brain or in the lining of the chest or abdomen. Removing these tumors can be risky and may cause damage to other important structures in the body. Finally, some people may not be healthy enough to undergo surgery due to other medical conditions they may have.
