The light from the early universe is still traveling through space today, even though the events that produced the light happened a very long time ago. Think of it like throwing a ball up into the air. The ball goes up into the sky and then comes back down. Even though the ball is no longer in your hand, you can still see it because it is still moving through the air. Similarly, the light from the early universe is still moving through space, so we can see it even though the events that produced the light happened a very long time ago. Does that make sense?
