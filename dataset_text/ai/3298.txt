Babies, puppies, and kittens are often considered cute because they have physical features that are designed to elicit a nurturing response from adults. These features include large eyes, round and chubby faces, and soft, smooth fur. In evolutionary terms, these features may have developed as a way to ensure the survival and reproduction of the species. When an adult feels a strong desire to take care of and protect a baby, puppy, or kitten, they are more likely to invest time and resources in caring for the young animal. This can help the young animal grow and develop into a healthy adult, increasing the chances that they will be able to reproduce and pass on their genes to future generations. So, in short, we think babies, puppies, and kittens are cute because they have features that make us want to take care of them, and this may have evolved as a way to help ensure the survival of the species.
