There are several ways to estimate how many people watched the Super Bowl. One way is to use ratings data from Nielsen, a company that measures television ratings. Nielsen uses a panel of people who agree to have their TV viewing habits tracked, and this panel is representative of the larger population. By measuring how many people in the panel watched the Super Bowl, Nielsen can estimate how many people in the larger population also watched it. 
Another way to estimate how many people watched the Super Bowl is to look at how many people streamed the game online or watched it on their phones or tablets. Many companies, including the NFL, provide streaming options for the Super Bowl, and these options can be used to estimate how many people watched the game online. 
Overall, there are many ways to estimate how many people watched the Super Bowl, and these estimates are often combined to give a more accurate picture of the total number of viewers.
