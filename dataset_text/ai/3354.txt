Suicidal thoughts can be a side effect of some medications because they can change the way your brain works. The brain is responsible for controlling your thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, and certain medications can alter the balance of chemicals in the brain that can affect these things. For example, some antidepressant medications, which are used to treat conditions like depression and anxiety, can sometimes cause people to have suicidal thoughts, especially when they first start taking the medication or when they increase the dose. This is because the medication can take some time to start working and can cause changes in the brain that can lead to these thoughts. It's important to remember that these side effects are usually temporary and can be managed with the help of a doctor or mental health professional.
