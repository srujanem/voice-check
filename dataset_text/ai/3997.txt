When you work out really hard, your body uses up a lot of energy and can get very tired. When this happens, your muscles may start to feel sore or weak, and you might not feel very good. Sometimes, when your body is working really hard, it can also cause you to feel sick to your stomach or even throw up. This is because your body is trying to get rid of extra energy that it doesn't need, and it's a way for your body to say "I need to rest now." It's important to listen to your body and give it the rest it needs after a hard workout or long run.
