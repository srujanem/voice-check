Down syndrome is a condition that is caused by having an extra copy of chromosome 21. Chromosomes are structures inside our cells that contain our genetic material, which is what determines many of our characteristics, such as our eye color, height, and even our risk for certain health conditions.\n\nNormally, we have two copies of each chromosome, one from our mother and one from our father. However, people with Down syndrome have three copies of chromosome 21, which is why it is also sometimes called "trisomy 21."\n\nHaving an extra copy of a chromosome can disrupt the normal development of the body and brain, leading to the physical and intellectual disabilities that are characteristic of Down syndrome. While it is true that certain genes on chromosome 21 can be beneficial or "dominant," having too many copies of these genes can also have negative effects on development.\n\nI hope this helps to explain the cause of Down syndrome in a way that is easy to understand.
