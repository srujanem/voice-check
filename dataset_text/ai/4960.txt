For example, if Team A is expected to score 10 points and Team B is expected to score 20 points, the spread might be "Team A +10." For example, a moneyline of +200 means you can win $200 if you bet $100 and the team wins. A negative number means that team is the favorite (more likely to win), and the number represents how much money you need to bet to win $100.
