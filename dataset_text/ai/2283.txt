Pumpkins are a type of fruit that grow on vines. They have a thick outer layer and a hollow center with seeds inside. People like to carve pumpkins into jack-o'-lanterns for Halloween decorations. They are also used for cooking and baking. Some animals, such as squirrels and birds, might eat pumpkins if they find them in the wild. People also eat pumpkins in many different ways, such as roasting the seeds, making soup, or adding them to pies or other baked goods. Pumpkins are a good source of nutrients, such as vitamin A and potassium. They are also used to make products like pumpkin pie spice and pumpkin seed oil.
