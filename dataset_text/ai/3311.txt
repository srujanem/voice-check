In the past, doctors would often make house calls because it was the most practical way for them to see their patients, especially if the patient was unable to leave their home. However, as medicine has advanced and hospitals and clinics have become more readily available, it has become less common for doctors to make house calls. One reason for this is that doctors now have access to more advanced equipment and resources at hospitals and clinics, which can help them diagnose and treat patients more effectively. Additionally, many insurance plans only cover medical care that takes place at a hospital or clinic, so it may not be financially practical for doctors to make house calls. Overall, the main reason that doctors don't make house calls as often as they used to is because there are now more efficient and effective ways for them to provide medical care to their patients.
