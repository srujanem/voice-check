Cell division is a fundamental biological process that allows organisms to grow, repair, and reproduce. Mitosis produces two genetically identical daughter cells and is responsible for growth and tissue repair. Meiosis, on the other hand, produces four genetically unique gametes through two rounds of division and is essential for sexual reproduction. The regulation of cell division is critical, as errors can lead to cancer. It is evident that this topic will continue to shape our world in significant ways going forward.
