Sure! Kinematics is a branch of physics that studies the motion of objects without considering the forces that cause the motion. It's a way to predict and describe how objects will move based on their mass, size, shape, and other properties.\n\nImagine you're playing with a ball. You can throw the ball, and it will travel through the air and land somewhere. Kinematics helps us understand how the ball will move based on how hard you throw it, how high it goes, and how long it stays in the air.\n\nIn a game engine, kinematics is used to predict and control the movement of objects in a virtual world. For example, if you're making a game with characters that can jump, kinematics would be used to figure out how high the character will jump and how long they'll stay in the air.\n\nI hope that helps!
