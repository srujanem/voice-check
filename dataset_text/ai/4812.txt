Popes are chosen by a group of Catholic officials called cardinals. In the past, it was not uncommon for young men to become popes because they were often chosen as part of political maneuvering or as a way for powerful families to increase their influence within the Catholic Church. However, in recent times, the selection process for popes has become more formalized and focused on choosing the most qualified candidate, regardless of age. As a result, it is less common for young men to be chosen as popes. It is also worth noting that the role of pope is a demanding and demanding position, so it is not uncommon for older men with more experience to be chosen.
