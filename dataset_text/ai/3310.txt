In a car with a manual transmission, the engine is connected directly to the wheels through a clutch and a gearbox. When you push start a manual car, you are essentially using the momentum of the car to spin the engine and get it running. On the other hand, a car with an automatic transmission does not have a clutch. Instead, it uses a device called a torque converter to connect the engine to the transmission. This means that the engine and transmission are not directly connected, and it is not possible to use the momentum of the car to spin the engine. In order to start an automatic car, you need to use the starter motor, which is an electric motor that is used to turn the engine over and get it running.
