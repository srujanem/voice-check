School buses are out and about at different times of the day for a few different reasons. One reason is to transport students to and from school and other activities, like field trips. Another reason is that school buses might need to go to different places for maintenance, such as getting an oil change or getting new tires. Additionally, school bus drivers often need to practice driving their routes, so they might be out driving around even when there are no students on the bus. This helps them make sure they know the best and safest way to get to all of the places they need to go with students on board. Finally, sometimes school buses are used to transport students or groups of people to events or activities outside of regular school hours, like sports games or field trips.
