Smoking cigarettes can be very harmful to your health and can cause many serious health problems, such as lung cancer, heart disease, and stroke. It is also very addictive, which means that once you start smoking, it can be very hard to stop. There is no good side to smoking cigarettes. In fact, there are only negative consequences. Smoking can shorten your lifespan and make you more likely to get sick. It can also be expensive and can harm the people around you, as secondhand smoke can be harmful to others. It's important to remember that you can choose not to start smoking, and if you do smoke, you can work to quit. There are many resources available to help you quit smoking, and it is worth the effort to improve your health and the health of those around you.
