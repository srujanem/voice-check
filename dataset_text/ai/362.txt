Cigarettes contain many harmful chemicals because they are created through a process called combustion, which involves burning tobacco and other materials. When tobacco is burned, it creates thousands of chemicals, many of which are toxic and can cause cancer. These chemicals are what make smoking cigarettes so dangerous. 
One of the chemicals that is found in cigarettes is nicotine, which is a highly addictive substance. This is why people continue to smoke cigarettes even though they know that they are harmful. However, it is possible to make cigarettes with less harmful chemicals, or to create alternative products that deliver nicotine in a safer way, such as nicotine gum or patches. These products can help people to quit smoking or reduce their exposure to harmful chemicals.
