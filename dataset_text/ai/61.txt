Proper sleep hygiene is essential for maintaining physical health, cognitive performance, and emotional resilience. Establishing a consistent sleep schedule by going to bed and waking up at the same time every day helps anchor your body's internal biological clock. To optimize sleep quality, keep your bedroom environment dark, quiet, and comfortably cool, while eliminating screen exposure at least one hour before bedtime. Blue light from phones and televisions suppresses melatonin production, making it harder to fall asleep. Prioritizing restful sleep pays immediate dividends in daily focus, mood, and long-term wellness.
