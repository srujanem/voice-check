Gorillas are strong because they have a lot of muscle mass and they use their muscles to do things like climb trees, move branches, and defend themselves. They are able to eat mostly fruits and vegetables because they are herbivores, which means they only eat plants. Fruits and vegetables contain a lot of nutrients that help gorillas grow and stay healthy. Even though they might not be eating meat, they are still able to get the protein and other nutrients they need from the plants they eat. So even though they might not be eating as much protein as some other animals, they are still able to build strong muscles through their diet and activities.
