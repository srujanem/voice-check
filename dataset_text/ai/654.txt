Buildings often leave their lights on all night for a variety of reasons. One reason is for safety and security. Keeping the lights on can deter burglars or vandals from breaking in, and it can also help people see where they are going if they need to enter the building in the dark. Another reason is for convenience. If people need to use the building at night, they will want the lights on so they can see what they are doing. Finally, some buildings are used for events or activities that take place at night, so the lights need to be on for those events.
