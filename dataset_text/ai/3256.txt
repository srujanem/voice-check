When you mix yellow and blue paint, you get green because of the way that colors work. Each color is made up of different wavelengths of light. When you mix two colors together, the wavelengths of light combine to create a new color. Yellow paint reflects mostly yellow light and absorbs other colors. Blue paint reflects mostly blue light and absorbs other colors. When you mix yellow and blue paint together, the yellow paint absorbs the blue light and reflects the yellow light, while the blue paint absorbs the yellow light and reflects the blue light. When these two reflected lights mix together, they create a new color: green. On a molecular level, the paint pigments are made up of tiny particles called molecules. When you mix the yellow and blue paint, the molecules of the two pigments combine and interact with each other. This interaction causes the pigments to absorb and reflect different wavelengths of light, which creates the green color that you see.
