It's completely normal to have different reactions to a song the first time you hear it compared to after you've listened to it more. Our preferences and opinions can change over time, and sometimes it takes a few listenings for us to fully appreciate a song or to understand its lyrics and message. It's also possible that your initial negative reaction to the song was influenced by other factors, such as your mood or the context in which you heard it. It's worth giving a song a few more chances to see if your feelings towards it change. After all, music is a very personal and subjective experience, and what one person loves, another person may not.
