There are a few reasons why some authoritarian governments in East Asia may be able to remain in power for a long time. One reason is that they often have strong control over the military and the police, which they can use to suppress any attempts at dissent or resistance. Another reason is that they often have a lot of resources, such as money and access to technology, which they can use to buy the loyalty of key groups within society, such as the business community or certain ethnic groups. Additionally, these governments may also use propaganda and censorship to control the information that people have access to, making it harder for people to organize and mobilize against them. Finally, these governments may also have strong networks of allies and supporters both within the country and internationally, which can help to protect them from external pressure to change.
