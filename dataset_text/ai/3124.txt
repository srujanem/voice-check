Caesar was assassinated because many people believed that he had become too powerful and was a threat to the Roman Republic. They thought that he was trying to become a tyrant and take over the government. Augustus, on the other hand, was able to avoid being assassinated because he was careful to present himself as a leader who was working for the good of the Roman people. He was able to convince the Roman people that he was not trying to become a tyrant and that he was working to restore order and stability to the Roman Republic. In addition, Augustus was able to win the support of the military, which was a very powerful force in Rome at the time. He was able to use the military to help him defeat his enemies and maintain control of the government. Overall, Augustus was able to avoid being seen as a tyrant because he was able to present himself as a strong and capable leader who was working to benefit the Roman people. He was also able to win the support of the military, which helped him to maintain his power and avoid being assassinated like Caesar.
