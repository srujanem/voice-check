The phrase "the 1%" refers to a small group of people who are very wealthy and have a lot of influence over society. The phrase "the 99%" refers to everyone else who is not part of the 1%. This phrase is often used to talk about the way that wealth and power are distributed in society, and how the 1% often have more influence and resources than the rest of the population. It's a way of highlighting the differences between the very rich and everyone else, and the ways in which the rich might have more advantages and opportunities than others.
