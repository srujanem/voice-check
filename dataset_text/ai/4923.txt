Acids and bases are fundamental chemical concepts. Acids donate protons and have a pH below 7, while bases accept protons and have a pH above 7. The pH scale ranges from 0 to 14, with 7 being neutral. Strong acids like hydrochloric acid fully dissociate in water, whereas weak acids like acetic acid partially dissociate. Neutralization reactions between acids and bases produce water and salt, and are widely used in industrial and biological processes.
