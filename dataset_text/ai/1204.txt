First, when you use your credit card, the merchant you are purchasing from pays a fee to the bank for processing the transaction. This fee is called the "interchange fee" and it helps to cover the cost of providing the credit card service to you.

If you always pay your balance in full and on time, then the bank will not make any money from interest charges. However, if you carry a balance and pay interest, the bank will make money from the interest charges.

Overall, if you are good at using your credit card and pay your balance in full and on time, you can actually be a valuable customer for the bank because you help them earn money from interchange fees and other sources.
