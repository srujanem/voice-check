When you stretch your muscles, you are increasing the range of motion in your joints and increasing blood flow to the muscles. This can help to improve flexibility and decrease muscle stiffness, which can make your muscles feel good. 
When you sit for a long time, your muscles can get tight and stiff because they are not being used as much. Stretching helps to loosen up the muscles and can feel good because it helps to relieve tension in the muscles. 
Think of it like this: when you stretch, you are giving your muscles a good stretch and a massage, which can feel really nice. Just like when you give a big hug to someone you love, it can make you feel good and relaxed. Stretching your muscles can have a similar effect.
