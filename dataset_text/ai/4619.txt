A gas planet is a type of planet that is made mostly of gas, rather than solid matter like the Earth. Jupiter and Saturn are both gas planets.\n\nThe Earth is a rocky planet, which means it is made mostly of solid materials like rock and metal. It has a solid surface that we can walk on, and it has an atmosphere (a layer of gases around the planet).\n\nJupiter and Saturn, on the other hand, are much larger than the Earth and are made mostly of gases like hydrogen and helium. They do not have a solid surface that we can walk on, and their atmospheres are much thicker than the Earth's atmosphere. They also have many moons and rings around them.\n\nOverall, the main difference between the Earth and gas planets like Jupiter and Saturn is that the Earth is a rocky planet with a solid surface, while Jupiter and Saturn are made mostly of gas and do not have a solid surface.
