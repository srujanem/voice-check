Sure! A pencil eraser is a block of rubber that you can use to erase mistakes you make when you're writing or drawing with a pencil. When you rub the eraser over the mistake, the rubber removes the graphite (a kind of soft black material) from the paper.\n\nThe reason the eraser works is because rubber is a very good material for picking up and removing other materials. When you rub the eraser over the paper, the tiny particles of graphite stick to the rubber and are lifted off the paper. The eraser doesn't actually "erase" the graphite, it just moves it somewhere else.\n\nI hope that helps! Let me know if you have any other questions.
