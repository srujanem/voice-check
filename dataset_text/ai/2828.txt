It is often set in the past, usually during the 19th century, and often has a Victorian or Wild West feel to it. The people in steampunk stories might use steam-powered machines to do things like fly airships or fight battles. They might also have steam-powered gadgets and gizmos that do everyday tasks. Steampunk stories often have elements of fantasy, like magic or mythical creatures, mixed in with the technology.
