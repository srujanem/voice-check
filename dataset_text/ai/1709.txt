Sure! Cancer is a type of disease that happens when cells in your body grow out of control. Normally, your body makes new cells when they are needed, and old cells die off. But with cancer, new cells keep being made, even when they're not needed. These extra cells can form a mass called a tumor. Tumors can be cancerous, which means they can grow and spread to other parts of the body, or they can be benign, which means they aren't cancer and don't spread. Other animals, including pets like dogs and cats, can also get cancer. In fact, cancer is actually quite common in many different types of animals. We don't know for sure why some people and animals get cancer and others don't. It can be caused by a combination of things, including genetics (meaning it runs in the family), certain infections, and things in the environment, like tobacco smoke and sunlight. Cancer has been around for a very long time. People have written about it and found evidence of it in human remains for thousands of years. However, we have only recently been able to understand more about cancer and how to treat it.
