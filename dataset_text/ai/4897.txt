Instead, the universe is made up of billions of galaxies, and all of these galaxies are moving away from each other.\n\nIf you trace the movements of the galaxies backwards in time, you would not find a single point where they all originated from, because the universe has no center. According to this theory, the universe started as a very hot and dense point, and it has been expanding and cooling ever since.
