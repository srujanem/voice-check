There are a few different factors that can contribute to differences between the internet speed you're paying for and the speeds you're actually seeing. One possibility is that your home network is not set up optimally, which can slow down your internet speeds. Another possibility is that the websites or online services you're using are running slowly, which can also impact your speeds. It's also possible that there are temporary issues with your internet service provider that are causing slower than usual speeds. In general, it's not uncommon for actual internet speeds to be somewhat slower than the speeds you're paying for due to a variety of factors, so it's always a good idea to keep an eye on your speeds and make sure you're getting the performance you expect.
