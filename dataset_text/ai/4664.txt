Artificial neural networks are a type of computer program that are designed to mimic the way that the human brain works. The human brain is made up of billions of tiny cells called neurons, which are connected together and communicate with each other through a network of connections.\n\nIn a similar way, an artificial neural network is made up of a series of interconnected "neurons" that can process and transmit information. These neurons are organized into layers, with the input layer receiving information, the hidden layers processing the information, and the output layer producing a result.\n\nWhen we input data into an artificial neural network, it processes the information and makes a prediction or decision based on what it has learned. For example, a neural network might be trained to recognize images of animals, and when it sees a new image, it can predict which animal is in the image based on what it has learned from previous examples.\n\nNeural networks can be used for a wide variety of tasks, including image and speech recognition, language translation, and even playing games like chess. They are a powerful tool for solving problems that are too complex for humans to solve on their own.
