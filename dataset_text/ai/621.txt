Babies aren't wrinkly when they're born because the amniotic fluid that surrounds them in the womb keeps their skin hydrated and smooth. The amniotic fluid also helps to protect the baby's skin from being damaged or infected while they are developing in the womb. When a baby is born, they are usually covered in a thin layer of a substance called vernix, which helps to protect their skin and keep it moisturized. After birth, the baby's skin will start to dry out a bit, but it should still remain smooth and soft. So, even though a baby has been in the womb for nine months, they are still well-protected and well-hydrated, which helps to keep their skin smooth and wrinkle-free.
