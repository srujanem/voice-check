Methamphetamines can also cause a person's teeth to rot or fall out, a condition known as "meth mouth." In addition, both drugs can cause a person to have mood swings and erratic behavior, which can lead to a lack of personal hygiene and poor grooming. These changes in appearance can occur relatively quickly after a person starts using these drugs. It's important to note that using methamphetamines and crack cocaine can have serious negative consequences on a person's health and well-being, and it is not recommended to use these drugs.
