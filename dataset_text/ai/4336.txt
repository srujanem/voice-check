Banks and pyramid schemes are very different things. Here's how they differ:\n\nBanks:\n\nA bank is a financial institution that provides a range of services, including accepting deposits, making loans, and allowing customers to open checking and savings accounts.\nBanks make money by charging interest on loans and by earning interest on the money they hold in deposits.\nBanks are regulated by government agencies to ensure that they operate safely and fairly.\nPyramid schemes:\n\nA pyramid scheme is a fraudulent investment scheme that relies on constantly recruiting new members to bring in money.\nIn a pyramid scheme, each person involved recruits others to join. Money made by the new members funnels up to the higher members.\nThe scheme relies on an endless supply of new members to bring in money. Eventually, the scheme collapses because there aren't enough new members to support the pyramid.\nPyramid schemes are illegal in many countries because they are inherently fraudulent and almost always result in losses for the majority of participants.\nI hope that helps clarify the difference between banks and pyramid schemes.
