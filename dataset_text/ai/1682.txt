A comprehensive understanding of this topic requires us to examine both theoretical and practical dimensions. Urbanization is the process by which rural populations migrate to cities, leading to the growth of urban areas. This phenomenon is driven by the search for better economic opportunities, access to services, and improved living standards. While urbanization fuels economic development and innovation, it also presents challenges such as housing shortages, traffic congestion, environmental pollution, and the strain on public infrastructure. Sustainable urban planning is essential to manage these challenges effectively. Continued research and public awareness are crucial to addressing the challenges in this field.
