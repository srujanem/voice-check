Bison and buffalo are two different animals that belong to the same family of mammals called Bovidae. There are several differences between bison and buffalo, but here are the main ones: Appearance: Bison and buffalo have different physical characteristics. Bison are larger animals with shaggy, dark brown fur and a distinctive hump on their shoulders. They have long, curved horns and a beard-like mane on their chest. Buffalo are generally smaller and have shorter, darker fur that is usually black or dark brown. They have horns that are wider and more curved at the base and a mane that is shorter and less prominent. Habitat: Bison and buffalo live in different parts of the world. Bison are native to North America and Europe, while buffalo are native to Africa and Asia. Diet: Bison and buffalo have slightly different diets. Bison are herbivores and mostly eat grass, while buffalo are omnivores and eat a wider variety of plants and small animals. Behavior: Bison and buffalo have different social and behavioral patterns. Bison are social animals that live in herds, while buffalo are more solitary and tend to live in smaller groups. In summary, bison and buffalo are two different animals that belong to the same family of mammals. They have different physical characteristics, live in different parts of the world, have different diets, and have different social and behavioral patterns.
