It's thought that humans are drawn to baby animals (and human babies too) because they are small and vulnerable, and we have a natural instinct to protect and care for them. We also tend to find things with big eyes, round faces, and soft features to be cute and endearing, which many baby animals have. This can make us feel warm and fuzzy inside, and it's often a very positive and rewarding experience to be around baby animals. So it's not necessarily that the animals themselves are "cute," but rather that we have a natural inclination to find them cute because of their size, appearance, and need for care.
