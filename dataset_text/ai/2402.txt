Splitting bills for tables at restaurants can be a time-consuming and potentially error-prone task for servers, especially if the restaurant is busy and there are multiple tables to attend to. It can also be inconvenient for the customers, as they may have to wait for the server to come back with their separate checks and pay for them separately. In some cases, splitting the bill can also cause confusion or misunderstandings if different people at the table ordered different items at different prices. Additionally, many point-of-sale (POS) systems are not designed to easily split bills for tables. This can require manual calculations and additional steps to ensure that each person is charged correctly for their individual items. Overall, while some restaurants may be willing to split bills for tables upon request, it is generally not a common practice because it can be inconvenient and time-consuming for both the restaurant staff and the customers.
