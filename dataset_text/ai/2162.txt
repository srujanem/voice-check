The Taliban and al-Qaeda are two separate groups, although they have had some connections in the past. The Taliban is a militant group that originated in Afghanistan and ruled the country for a time. They follow a strict interpretation of Islam and have been known for their harsh treatment of women and their opposition to education. The Taliban are primarily based in Afghanistan and Pakistan. Al-Qaeda is a global terrorist network that was founded by Osama bin Laden. Al-Qaeda has carried out a number of attacks around the world, including the September 11th attacks on the United States in 2001. Al-Qaeda's main goal is to establish a global Islamic caliphate and to engage in violent jihad against non-Muslims. In the past, the Taliban provided support and shelter to al-Qaeda in Afghanistan. However, the two groups are distinct and have different goals and ideologies.
