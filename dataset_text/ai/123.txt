Bugs like fruit flies are attracted to sweet and ripe fruits, and they are also attracted to the smells of humans. They are also attracted to warm, moist environments. When you are on vacation, you may be eating sweet fruits or other foods that attract fruit flies, and they may be drawn to the smells of your body. If you want to avoid attracting fruit flies, you can try to keep your surroundings clean and free of sweet or moist substances that might attract them. You can also try using insect repellents or fly swatters to help keep them away.
