A Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) attack is a type of cyber attack where a malicious actor intercepts communication between two parties and pretends to be one of the parties to the other party.\n\nImagine you are trying to send a message to your friend using a walkie-talkie. The MITM attack would be like someone else intercepting your message and pretending to be your friend to you, while also pretending to be you to your friend. This person could then change the message you sent or even send a completely different message to your friend.\n\nIn the case of a WiFi network, the MITM attacker could set up a fake WiFi network and try to trick devices into connecting to it instead of the legitimate WiFi network. The attacker could then intercept and modify any data that is sent or received over the fake WiFi network.\n\nTo prevent MITM attacks, it is important to use secure communication methods and to verify the identity of the other party before sending sensitive information.\n
