Windows updates are designed to improve the performance and security of your computer. These updates often include fixes for bugs and vulnerabilities that have been discovered in the operating system, as well as new features and improvements. 
Sometimes, these updates may not seem to make any visible changes to your computer, but they may be fixing important issues in the background that you are not aware of. It is important to keep your computer up to date with the latest updates in order to ensure that it is running smoothly and securely. 
If you feel like Windows is updating too frequently, you can check your update settings and see if there is a way to adjust the frequency or schedule of updates. You can also choose to manually install updates rather than having them installed automatically. However, it is generally recommended to keep your computer up to date with the latest updates to ensure that it is running at its best.
