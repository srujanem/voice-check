Sure! Refrigeration is a way to keep things cold by using a special machine called a refrigerator. A refrigerator works by using a gas called a refrigerant, which is able to change from a gas to a liquid and back again very easily. Inside the refrigerator, there is a part called the compressor. The compressor pumps the refrigerant gas around a closed system of tubes. When the gas is pumped through the tubes, it gets very hot. Next, the hot gas is sent through a part called the condenser. The condenser is made of lots of thin tubes with a lot of space around them. As the hot gas goes through the tubes, it cools down and turns into a liquid. The cooled liquid is then sent through a part called the evaporator. The evaporator is also made of thin tubes, but this time they are inside a box or compartment. The box or compartment is the part of the refrigerator where you keep your food. As the cooled liquid passes through the tubes in the evaporator, it absorbs heat from the air inside the refrigerator. This makes the air inside the refrigerator get colder. Finally, the cooled liquid goes back to the compressor to start the cycle all over again. It gets pumped through the tubes and gets hot again, and the process repeats. So, to answer your question, the electronic power in a refrigerator helps to pump the refrigerant gas around the system of tubes and through the compressor, condenser, and evaporator. This process of moving the gas and changing it from a gas to a liquid and back again is what helps to effectively reduce the temperature inside the refrigerator.
