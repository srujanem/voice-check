In most Western cultures, it is traditional to use the suffix "Jr." or "III" after a man's name to indicate that he is the son of a person with the same name. For example, if a man's name is John Smith Sr., and his son's name is John Smith Jr., it indicates that the son is named after his father. This tradition is not typically used for women's names, and you will not often see women with the suffix "Jr." or "III" after their name. Instead, it is more common for women to use their maiden name or a different surname after marriage. It is completely acceptable to name your daughter after your wife if you would like to do so. There is no rule that says a woman's name cannot be passed down in this way. You can also choose to give your daughter a unique name if you prefer.
