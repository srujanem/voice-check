Benign tumors are growths that are not cancerous, meaning they do not have the ability to spread to other parts of the body and invade normal tissue. They are usually not life-threatening and can often be removed easily through surgery.\n\nThere are many different types of benign tumors, and they can occur in different parts of the body. They can be caused by a variety of factors, such as genetics, environmental factors, and certain infections.\n\nBenign tumors grow because the cells in the tumor are dividing and reproducing more rapidly than normal cells. This can cause the tumor to grow in size. However, benign tumors do not have the ability to spread to other parts of the body like cancerous tumors do.\n\nIt's important to note that while benign tumors are not cancerous, they can still cause problems if they grow too large or press on nearby organs or tissues. In these cases, a doctor may recommend removing the benign tumor to prevent any potential issues.
