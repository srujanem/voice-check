For example, if a teacher asks a student to turn in their homework and the student does so, the student is complying with the teacher's request.\n\nGroup think is a phenomenon that can occur when people are part of a group and they start to think and act in similar ways, even if those ways might not be the best or most logical. This can happen because people in the group want to fit in and be liked by the other group members, so they go along with the group's decisions or ways of thinking.\n\nConformity is similar to compliance, but it refers to when someone goes along with the beliefs, behaviors, or actions of a group, even if they don't agree with them. For example, if a group of friends all think that a certain type of music is the best, and someone in the group doesn't really like that type of music but still goes along with the group and pretends to like it, they are conforming to the group's belief.\n\nIn the context of social psychology, these three concepts can be studied to understand how people's behavior and beliefs are influenced by others and by the groups they belong to.
