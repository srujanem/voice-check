Fever is a self-regulatory mechanism that occurs when the body's temperature rises above its normal range. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as an infection or inflammation. When the body has a fever, it is trying to kill off the bacteria or virus that is causing the illness. The higher body temperature makes it harder for the germs to survive and multiply.\n\nSweating is one way that the body cools down and brings the fever down. However, if the fever is very high or lasts for a long time, it can be harmful and it may be a good idea to see a doctor. The doctor can determine the cause of the fever and prescribe medications to help bring the fever down and make you feel more comfortable. It's important to drink plenty of fluids and get rest while you have a fever, as well.
