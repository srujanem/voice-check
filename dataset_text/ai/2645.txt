Darkness is the absence of light. Light is a type of energy that travels through the air in the form of waves. We can see light when it reflects off of objects and enters our eyes. When there is no light present, or when an object absorbs all of the light that hits it, we see darkness. Imagine that you are in a room with a light on. The light is shining on the walls, the floor, and the objects in the room. When you close your eyes, you can't see anything because the light is not entering your eyes. This is similar to what happens when an object absorbs all of the light that hits it. The object absorbs the light and reflects none of it, so we see darkness instead of the object. So, to sum it up, darkness is the absence of light. It is not a thing itself, but rather the absence or lack of something.
