Comic book writers and artists use bold words to show that a character is speaking loudly or emphasizing a particular word or phrase. It's a way to help the reader understand how the character is feeling or what they might be trying to say. Think of it like when you talk to your friends and you say something really loudly or with a lot of emphasis because you want to make sure they understand what you're saying. That's kind of what bold words do in comics. They help the reader understand the characters and the story better.
