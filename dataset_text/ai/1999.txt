Tropes are a common pattern, theme, or motif that appears in creative works, such as literature, films, television shows, and video games. They are often used to add depth, complexity, and interest to a story, but they can also be overused or cliched. TV Tropes is a website that catalogs and explains a wide range of tropes that appear in television shows and other forms of media. It is a reference resource for writers, filmmakers, and other creators, as well as for fans who are interested in the way that different tropes are used in different works. The website is organized by category and subcategory, so it is easy to find information about specific types of tropes. For example, if you are looking for tropes that relate to love or romance, you can find a list of tropes in the "Love Tropes" category.
