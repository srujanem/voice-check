Wind energy harnesses the natural kinetic force of moving air to generate clean, renewable electricity. Modern wind turbines feature massive aerodynamic blades mounted on high towers, designed to catch wind gusts and turn a rotor connected to an internal electric generator. Offshore wind farms, positioned in open coastal waters, capture stronger and more consistent winds than land-based installations. Because wind power produces no atmospheric emissions during operation and relies on an inexhaustible natural resource, it plays an increasingly central role in transitioning the global energy grid away from fossil fuels.
