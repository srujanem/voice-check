Let us explore the fundamental aspects of this important subject in a structured manner. Plants convert sunlight into chemical energy through photosynthesis. This process occurs in the chloroplasts and involves two stages: the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle. During the light-dependent reactions, water molecules are split, releasing oxygen as a byproduct. The Calvin cycle uses carbon dioxide from the atmosphere to produce glucose, which serves as the primary energy source for the plant. Understanding these principles is foundational to making progress in this important area. Recent studies have further confirmed the significance of these factors in relation to photosynthesis.
