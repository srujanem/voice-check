When you do something without "thinking" about it, it means that you are doing it automatically or habitually. This can make it easier because your brain doesn't have to work as hard to figure out what to do. It's like when you ride a bike or drive a car. At first, you have to think about every step, but once you get the hang of it, you can do it without really thinking about it. Your brain has learned how to do it automatically. So when you shoot a basketball, your brain already knows how to aim and how hard to throw the ball, because you have practiced it before. When you write down your SSN, your brain knows the numbers by heart because you have written them down many times before. And when you remember someone's birthday, it's because you have celebrated it before and your brain has stored that information. It's like a shortcut in your brain that helps you do things more easily.
