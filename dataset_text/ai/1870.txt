Homestuck is a webcomic (a comic that is published on the internet) that follows the story of a group of four kids who play a video game called Sburb. Sburb is a game that allows the kids to enter a fantasy world and build their own kingdoms. In the beginning of the story, one of the kids, John, receives a copy of the game in the mail and starts playing it. As he plays, he meets three other kids online: Rose, Dave, and Jade. They all start playing Sburb together and help each other build their kingdoms. As they play, strange things start happening. For example, John's house starts to shake and he finds out that Sburb is actually a real game that can affect the real world. The kids also meet a group of creatures called trolls who are trying to stop them from playing the game. The story gets really complicated from there, but the main idea is that the kids are trying to save their world and the troll's world from being destroyed by playing Sburb and defeating the main villain, Lord English.
