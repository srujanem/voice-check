An S-bend in plumbing is also known as a P-trap or trap. It is a curved pipe that is located under sinks, showers, and toilets. The purpose of the P-trap is to prevent sewer gases from entering the home. Sewer gases are smelly and can be harmful to people's health. They are produced by the breakdown of waste in the sewer system. The P-trap works by holding a small amount of water in the curve of the pipe. The water creates a barrier that blocks the sewer gases from entering the home through the plumbing fixtures. The P-trap is an important part of the plumbing system because it helps to keep your home safe and healthy. It is shaped like an S or a P because that shape helps the water to stay in the curve of the pipe and form a barrier against the sewer gases.
