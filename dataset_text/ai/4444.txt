Prescription pill bottles are often made of a yellowish or greenish plastic because those colors are used to indicate that the bottles contain medications. This helps to prevent people from accidentally consuming the wrong pills or taking the wrong dosage, as different medications may have different colors, shapes, and markings on the pills themselves.\n\nThe use of specific colors for prescription pill bottles helps to create a standard system that is easy to understand and follow, especially for people who may have multiple medications that they need to take regularly. It is important to always follow the instructions provided by your healthcare provider and to store your medications safely to ensure that you are taking the correct pills at the correct time and in the correct dosage.
