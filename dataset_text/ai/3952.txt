The moon doesn't fall to Earth because it is actually falling towards Earth all the time. But because the moon is also moving sideways really fast, it never gets close enough to the surface to crash. It's kind of like how a baseball can be hit really hard and fly up into the air, but it will eventually fall back down to the ground because of Earth's gravity. The moon is like a ball that has been hit really hard and keeps on flying, but never quite makes it all the way to the ground. Does that make sense?
