Water pipes in cities are usually made of materials that are resistant to freezing, such as copper or PVC (polyvinyl chloride). They are also buried underground, which helps to protect them from the cold temperatures of the air. Additionally, the water in the pipes is moving, which helps to keep it from freezing. When water is stationary, it is more likely to freeze, which is why the pipes in your house can freeze if the furnace is not running and the water is not moving. 
To prevent your pipes from freezing, it is important to keep the temperature in your home above freezing (32°F or 0°C) and to insulate any pipes that are located in unheated areas, such as basements or attics. You can also let the faucets in your home drip a little bit, as this will help to keep the water moving and prevent it from freezing.
