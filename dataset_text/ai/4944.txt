Snow itself is not dirty, but it can pick up dirt and other particles as it falls through the air or as it melts and runs off of surfaces. When snow sticks to your car, it can leave behind any dirt or other substances that it picked up on its journey. This is why your car may look dirty after a snowfall, even though the snow itself is white and clean.\n\nAnother reason your car may look dirty after a snowfall is because the snow can cause salt, sand, or other substances that are used to melt ice on the roads to stick to your car. These substances can be abrasive and can cause scratches or other damage to your car's paint.\n\nIn summary, snow itself is not dirty, but it can pick up and leave behind dirt and other substances that can make your car look dirty.
