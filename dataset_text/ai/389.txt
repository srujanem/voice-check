Microtransactions are a way for companies to make money by selling small items or features in video games. Even though some people might not like microtransactions, companies still use them because they can be a successful way to make money. For example, if a company sells a game for $60, but also offers microtransactions that allow players to buy special weapons or costumes for their characters, they might be able to make more money overall by selling those items to players who want them. While some people might be unhappy with microtransactions, others might be willing to spend a little extra money to get items that they think are worth it. That's why microtransactions continue to exist in some video games.
