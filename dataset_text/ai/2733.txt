The md5sum is a way of checking to see if two files or pieces of data are the same. It does this by using a special formula, called a "hash function," to turn the data into a string of letters and numbers. This string is called a "hash," and it is unique to that particular piece of data. If two pieces of data are exactly the same, their hashes will also be the same. If there is even a small difference between the two pieces of data, their hashes will be different. For example, if you have two text files that are almost the same, but one of them has an extra period at the end of a sentence, the hashes for the two files will be different because the data is not exactly the same. So, the md5sum can be used to check if two pieces of data are the same by comparing their hashes. If the hashes are the same, the data is the same. If the hashes are different, the data is different.
