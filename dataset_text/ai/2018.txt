Irony is when something is said or done that is different from what is expected. There are different types of irony, but they all involve a contrast between what is expected and what actually happens. Here are some examples of irony: Verbal irony: This is when someone says something, but they mean the opposite. For example, if someone says "Oh, that's just great" in a sarcastic tone, they are being ironic because they don't actually think it's great. Dramatic irony: This is when the audience knows something that the characters in a story do not. For example, in a mystery movie, the audience might know who the killer is, but the characters don't. Situational irony: This is when something happens that is the opposite of what was expected.
