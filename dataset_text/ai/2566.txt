There are rules for war because war can be very destructive and harmful, and people want to try to minimize the damage and suffering that it causes. The rules of war, also known as "international humanitarian law," are a set of guidelines that try to protect innocent civilians and limit the ways that countries can fight each other. For example, the rule that says you are not supposed to attack reporters is in place because reporters are not directly involved in the fighting and should not be targeted. Similarly, there are rules against targeting hospitals, schools, and other places that are not being used for military purposes. These rules are meant to help reduce the impact of war on people who are not involved in the fighting and to make sure that the fighting is as fair as possible.
