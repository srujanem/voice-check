Overhead electrical wires carry a very high voltage of electricity, which can be dangerous and potentially deadly if you come into contact with them. The wires are often coated with a rubber or plastic material to help insulate them and prevent accidents, but this coating can wear down over time or be damaged, so it's important to stay away from overhead electrical wires.\n\nIf you touch an overhead electrical wire, you will not get electrocuted immediately, but the electricity can pass through your body and cause serious injury or death. The electricity can cause your muscles to contract, which can make it difficult to let go of the wire. It can also damage your internal organs and cause burns.\n\nIt's important to stay away from overhead electrical wires and to use caution around any electrical sources. If you see an overhead wire that is down or damaged, do not touch it and call the proper authorities for help.
