When you get a shot, a needle is used to inject a medication into your body. The needle can sometimes cause a little bit of pain, but it usually goes away quickly. After the shot, your arm may feel sore because the needle can cause a small amount of damage to your muscle or skin. This is completely normal and should go away after a few days. 
Sometimes, the medication that is injected can also cause soreness or tenderness at the injection site. This is also normal and should go away on its own. If the soreness or tenderness lasts for a long time or is severe, you should speak to your doctor. 
In the meantime, you can try using a cold pack on your arm to help reduce the soreness and swelling. You can also try gently massaging the area to help reduce any muscle stiffness. If your arm is still sore after a few days, you can take over-the-counter pain medication like acetaminophen or ibuprofen to help reduce the soreness.
