Nosebleeds usually occur when the blood vessels in the lining of your nose become damaged or swollen. This can be due to a variety of reasons, such as dry air, allergies, colds, or even picking your nose. The blood vessels in your nose are located in the front part of your nose, near the nostrils. These blood vessels are more prone to bleeding because they are close to the surface of your skin and have a lot of small branches. When you have a nosebleed, the blood usually comes from one of the blood vessels in the front part of your nose. It is possible that the blood vessels on one side of your nose are more prone to bleeding than the blood vessels on the other side, which is why you might experience nosebleeds only on one side. It is also possible that you are more prone to nosebleeds on one side due to other factors, such as the shape of your nose or the way you sleep. If you are concerned about your nosebleeds, you should speak with a healthcare professional for further evaluation and treatment.
