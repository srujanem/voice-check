The Big Bang Theory is a scientific explanation for how the universe began. It suggests that the universe started as a very small, hot, and dense point, and then expanded rapidly. This expansion caused the universe to cool and form the stars, galaxies, and everything else that we see today. Scientists believe that the Big Bang happened about 13.8 billion years ago. They have evidence for this, such as the fact that we can see the light from very distant galaxies, which must have taken a long time to reach us. As for where the things that caused the Big Bang came from, that is still a mystery. Some scientists believe that the universe has always existed and that the Big Bang was just the beginning of the current phase of the universe. Others believe that the universe was created by some kind of divine force or other external cause. There is still much that we don't understand about the universe, and scientists are constantly working to learn more.
