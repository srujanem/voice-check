When you wear 3D glasses, each lens is polarized, meaning that it only allows light waves of a certain orientation to pass through. This is what allows the 3D effect to be created when you watch a 3D movie or play a 3D video game. 
When you wink at a mirror while wearing 3D glasses, the movement of your eyelid blocks the light that is being reflected off the mirror and passing through one of the lenses.
