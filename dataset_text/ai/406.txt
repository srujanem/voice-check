When you update a program on your computer, the update usually includes new features or changes to the program. In order to apply these changes, the program needs to be closed and then reopened. Restarting your computer is one way to close all programs and then start them again, so that the changes made by the update can take effect. 
Sometimes, an update may also include changes to the way the program interacts with other parts of the computer, such as the operating system or other programs. In order to apply these changes, it may be necessary to restart the computer so that the operating system can reload and recognize the updates. 
Overall, restarting your computer after installing an update helps to ensure that the program is running correctly and that all of the changes made by the update are applied properly.
