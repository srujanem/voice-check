Hawking radiation is not like light, it is actually particles that are produced by the black hole. These particles are produced in pairs, and one particle falls into the black hole while the other particle escapes. This process is called "quantum tunneling." Imagine you are a ball and you want to roll over a hill. If the hill is very steep, you might not be able to roll over it. But if the hill is not too steep, you might be able to roll over it. The same thing happens with particles trying to escape a black hole. If the black hole is very massive, it is like a very steep hill and it is very difficult for the particles to escape. But if the black hole is not too massive, it is like a hill that is not too steep and it is easier for the particles to escape. Does that help?
