Jar Jar Binks was a character in the Star Wars prequel trilogy, which includes the movies "The Phantom Menace," "Attack of the Clones," and "Revenge of the Sith." Some people did not like Jar Jar because they thought he was annoying and not as interesting as other characters in the Star Wars universe. Some people also thought that his comedic relief was not funny and that it took away from the more serious and action-packed parts of the movies. Overall, it seems that Jar Jar was not a very popular character among some fans of the original Star Wars movies, but opinions about him vary widely.
