/r/circlejerk is a subreddit, or a forum on the website Reddit, that is intended to be a place for people to post humorous or absurd content and make fun of the subject matter. The term "circlejerk" refers to the idea of a group of people agreeing with and reinforcing each other's views or behaviors without critical thinking or consideration of alternative perspectives. The subreddit is meant to be a humorous and satirical take on this concept, and the posts and comments are often meant to be ironic or facetious. It is not a subreddit that is meant to be taken seriously, and it is not a place for respectful or serious discussions.
