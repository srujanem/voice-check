Pigeons were trained to deliver messages by being taken to the location where the message needed to be delivered and then being given food. The pigeon would then associate that location with the reward of food, and would fly back to that location whenever it was released. This is known as "homing" behavior, and it is a natural instinct for pigeons. To make sure the pigeon knew where to go, people would often attach a small tag with the destination written on it to the pigeon's leg. The pigeon would then fly to the location indicated on the tag and deliver the message.
