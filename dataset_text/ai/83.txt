Quantum cryptography leverages the fundamental principles of quantum mechanics to establish unprecedented levels of data transmission security. By employing quantum key distribution (QKD) protocols, information is encoded onto single photons. Any unauthorized attempt to eavesdrop or intercept the quantum state inevitably disrupts the delicate superposition, alerting communicating parties instantly. As quantum computing advances threaten traditional public-key encryption schemes, quantum cryptography provides a future-proof foundation for global cybersecurity.
