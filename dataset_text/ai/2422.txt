World of Warcraft, or WoW, is a very popular video game that people have been playing for a long time, since 2004. It's called an "MMORPG," which stands for "massively multiplayer online role-playing game." That means that lots of people from all around the world can play it at the same time, and they get to create their own character and go on adventures in a fantasy world. There are lots of different things to do in WoW, like fighting monsters, completing quests, and learning new skills. You can also join a group of other players to work together and tackle bigger challenges. One of the reasons why WoW is still popular after so many years is because the game's developers keep releasing new updates and expansions. This means that there's always new content to explore and new things to do in the game. Another reason is that people really enjoy playing with their friends, or even making new friends while playing the game. And since there are so many different ways to play and so much to do, it can be really fun to keep playing for a long time. Finally, some people might find WoW to be addictive because it's really engaging and immersive. It can be easy to lose track of time while playing, especially if you're really into the game and having a good time.
