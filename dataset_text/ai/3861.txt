It's normal for people to feel more alert and awake when they are trying to go to sleep, especially if they are in a quiet, dark room and their body is expecting to sleep. When you watch TV or a movie, your brain is actively engaged in processing what you are seeing and hearing, which can make you feel more awake. When you are trying to go to sleep, your brain may be less stimulated, which can make it harder to fall asleep. There are also other factors that can make it difficult to fall asleep, such as stress, anxiety, or an irregular sleep schedule. If you are having trouble falling asleep consistently, it might be helpful to try to relax before bedtime, establish a regular sleep schedule, and create a sleep-friendly environment in your bedroom. If you are still having trouble falling asleep despite trying these strategies, it might be a good idea to speak with a healthcare professional. They can help determine if there is an underlying medical condition or sleep disorder that is causing your difficulty falling asleep.
