The cost of a PC component, such as a processor, is determined by a variety of factors, including the materials and technology used to make it, the cost of research and development, and the overall demand for the product. More expensive processors often use more advanced technology and materials, which can make them more expensive to produce. In addition, more expensive processors may be targeted at a higher-end market, where consumers are willing to pay more for a product with better performance. 
It's also important to keep in mind that the price of a processor is just one factor to consider when building a PC. Other components, such as the motherboard, memory, and graphics card, can also have a significant impact on a PC's performance and overall cost. So, it's important to carefully consider all of the components you need and choose the ones that will best meet your needs and budget.
