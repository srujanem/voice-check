Bioré pore strips are adhesive strips that are applied to the nose (or other areas of the face) and are designed to remove blackheads. Blackheads are clogged pores that are filled with a combination of oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria. They appear as small, dark spots on the skin and can be difficult to remove without the right products or techniques. 
When you apply a Bioré pore strip to your nose (or other area of your face), it sticks to your skin and begins to dry. As it dries, it will start to harden and become more rigid. As the strip hardens, it will also start to pull on your skin. This pulling action helps to loosen and remove the blackheads from your pores. 
The strip is made of a material that is specifically designed to grab onto the blackheads and other impurities that are clogging your pores. As it pulls on your skin, it will also pull out the blackheads and other impurities that are stuck in your pores. These impurities are what you see when you remove the strip. 
So, to sum it up, Bioré pore strips work by sticking to your skin, hardening, and pulling on your skin to remove blackheads and other impurities from your pores.
