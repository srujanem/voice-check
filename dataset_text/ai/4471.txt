Electronic signatures and digital signatures are both ways of signing a document electronically, but they work in slightly different ways.\n\nAn electronic signature is any electronic symbol, sound, or process attached to or logically associated with a document and executed or adopted by a person with the intent to sign the document. This can include a typed name, a scanned image of a handwritten signature, or even just a checkmark to indicate agreement.\n\nA digital signature, on the other hand, uses encryption to securely sign a document. It requires the use of a digital certificate, which is issued by a third party and verifies the identity of the person signing the document. A digital signature is more secure than an electronic signature because it is much harder to forge.\n\nIn summary, an electronic signature is a simple way to sign a document electronically, while a digital signature is a more secure way to do so. Both can be used to become paperless and sign documents electronically, but a digital signature is generally considered more reliable and secure.
