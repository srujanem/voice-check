Antibiotics work by killing or stopping the growth of bacteria that can cause infections. Some medications, including antibiotics, can be affected by the foods you eat or the other medications you take. It's important to follow the instructions for taking your medication to make sure it works as well as it can. When you take an antibiotic on an empty stomach, it can be absorbed into your bloodstream more quickly, which means it can start working faster. If you take it with food, the food can interfere with the absorption of the medication, which means it might not work as well. That's why it's important to follow the instructions and take the antibiotic before food or on an empty stomach, as directed. It's also important to finish the entire course of antibiotics, even if you start feeling better. This helps make sure that all of the bacteria are killed, which can help prevent the infection from coming back.
