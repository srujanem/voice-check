Athlete's foot is a fungal infection that affects the skin on the feet, especially between the toes. Fungi thrive in warm, moist environments, which is why they are more commonly found on the feet. The skin on the feet is also more prone to fungal infections because it is constantly rubbing against shoes and socks, which can create the perfect conditions for fungi to grow. Fungi can also grow on other parts of the body, such as the webbing between the fingers or behind the ears, but these areas are not as conducive to fungal growth because they are not as warm and moist as the skin on the feet.
