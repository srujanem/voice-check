Civil law and criminal law are two different types of law that deal with different types of cases.\n\nCivil law deals with disputes between individuals or organizations. These disputes can be about things like money, property, or contracts. In civil cases, one person or organization is usually suing another person or organization to get some kind of relief, like money or damages.\n\nCriminal law, on the other hand, deals with crimes that are committed against society as a whole. Crimes like murder, theft, and robbery are examples of crimes that would be dealt with under criminal law. In criminal cases, the government (usually through a prosecutor) brings charges against a person accused of committing a crime. If the person is found guilty, they may face punishment like jail time or fines.\n\nSo, in short, civil law is about disputes between people, and criminal law is about crimes against society.
