The concept of the "edge" of the universe is a bit tricky, because the universe is constantly expanding. This means that there is no fixed boundary or edge to the universe. Instead, the universe is thought to be infinite, with no end or edge in sight. It's also worth noting that the concept of "beyond" doesn't really make sense when it comes to the universe, because there is no outside of the universe. The universe is everything that exists, and so there is nowhere else for anything to be beyond it.
