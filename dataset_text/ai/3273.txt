Hydraulic systems are made up of a few different parts that work together to move and control fluid (usually oil) in order to do a specific job. These parts include: Pumps: These are used to move the fluid from one place to another. Valves: These control the flow of fluid in the system. Cylinders: These are used to move or lift heavy objects. They work by using the pressure of the fluid to push a piston, which moves a rod that is attached to the object being lifted. Hoses: These are used to connect the different parts of the system together. Reservoirs: These are used to hold the fluid that is used in the system. Hydraulic systems are usually made from metal, such as steel or aluminum. They may also have some parts made from rubber, such as the hoses. There are different types of hydraulic systems, depending on the specific job they are designed to do. For example, some hydraulic systems are used to lift heavy objects, while others are used to control the movement of machine parts or to power vehicles.
