Computers and tablets have processors that work very hard to perform a lot of tasks quickly. This makes them produce a lot of heat, which can be harmful to the processor and other parts of the computer if it gets too hot. That's why computers have cooling systems to help keep them from getting too hot. 
Smartphones, on the other hand, don't usually have as many tasks to do as a computer, so they don't produce as much heat. This means they don't need as much cooling. However, some smartphones do have cooling systems to help keep them from getting too hot when they are doing a lot of tasks, just like a computer. 
Overall, the main reason computers need cooling is because they have more powerful processors that produce more heat, while smartphones and tablets can often get by without cooling because they have less powerful processors that produce less heat.
