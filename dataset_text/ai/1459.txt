Let us explore the fundamental aspects of this important subject in a structured manner. Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think, learn, and problem-solve. Modern AI systems rely on machine learning, particularly deep learning, where neural networks with multiple layers are trained on vast datasets to recognize patterns. Applications of AI span numerous fields, including natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and medical diagnostics. As AI capabilities grow, important ethical questions arise around bias, accountability, privacy, and the future of work. Continued research and public awareness are crucial to addressing the challenges in this field. It is also important to consider the broader societal and environmental implications associated with artificial intelligence.
