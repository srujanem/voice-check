Existentialism is a philosophical concept that deals with the meaning and purpose of life. It suggests that each person is responsible for creating their own meaning in life, and that this meaning is not predetermined or given by any external force or authority. Existentialism emphasizes individual freedom and choice, and the idea that people must take ownership of their own lives and make their own decisions. It also suggests that life is inherently meaningless, and that it is up to each person to give their own life meaning through their actions and choices. Existentialism can be a difficult concept to understand, but a simple way to think about it is to consider that you are in control of your own life, and it is up to you to decide what you want to do with it. It is important to think about what is meaningful and important to you, and to make choices that reflect those values.
