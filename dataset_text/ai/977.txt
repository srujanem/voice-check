Microwaves are a type of electromagnetic radiation that can heat up the water, fat, and sugar molecules in food. When microwaves hit metal, they can cause the electrons in the metal to move around, which can create sparks and potentially start a fire. This is why it is not safe to put metal in a microwave. 
However, the metal stands in some microwaves are designed to be safe to use because they are coated with a material that does not allow microwaves to pass through it. This means that the microwaves cannot heat up the metal stand and cause any sparks or fires. It is important to check the instructions for your microwave to see if the metal stand is safe to use.
