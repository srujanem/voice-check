Bees can't actually "smell your fear," but they can sense when you are scared or agitated. They can do this because they are sensitive to movement and changes in body posture. If you are waving your arms around or running away, they may perceive this as a threat and become more defensive. On the other hand, if you stay calm and move slowly, they are less likely to get upset. So, even though bees can't smell your fear, they can sense when you are behaving in a way that might be a threat to them.
