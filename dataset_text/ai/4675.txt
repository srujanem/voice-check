Which one do you think will hit the ground first?\n\nBoth balls will fall at the same speed! In fact, if you were to drop an elephant and a feather from the same height, they would fall at the same speed!\n\nSo, to sum it up: when gravity is the only force acting on an object, the object's mass doesn't really matter.
