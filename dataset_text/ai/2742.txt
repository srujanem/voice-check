It's normal to have dreams about waking up and starting your day, especially if you're feeling stressed or anxious about your daily routine. When we sleep, our brains are still active and we can have all kinds of dreams, including ones where we're going about our normal daily activities. Sometimes these dreams can feel very real, and it can be confusing or disorienting when you wake up and realize you were just dreaming. It's also possible that you may be having these kinds of dreams because you're having trouble falling asleep or staying asleep, which can be caused by a variety of factors such as stress, anxiety, or sleep disorders. If you're concerned about your sleep patterns or if you're having difficulty sleeping, it might be helpful to talk to a doctor or a mental health professional. They can help you figure out what might be causing your sleep problems and suggest ways to improve your sleep.
