Singapore is a country in Southeast Asia that is known for being a clean and modern city. It is often described as "the only shopping mall with a seat in the UN" because it has many shopping malls and is also a member of the United Nations. The phrase "Disneyland with the death penalty" refers to the fact that Singapore is a very safe and orderly place, similar to Disneyland, but it also has strict laws that can result in severe punishment, including the death penalty. This phrase is often used to highlight the contrast between Singapore's modern and developed appearance and its strict legal system.
