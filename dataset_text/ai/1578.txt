Black boxes are not actually indestructible, but they are designed to be very durable and able to withstand extreme conditions. They are often used on airplanes and other vehicles to help investigators figure out what happened in the event of an accident or incident. 
Black boxes are made of a strong, heat-resistant material such as titanium or stainless steel. They are also coated with a special fire-resistant material that helps protect them from heat and flames. This makes them very tough and able to withstand extreme conditions. 
In addition to being physically strong, black boxes are also designed to be able to survive underwater. They are equipped with a beacon that can be detected by search and rescue teams even if the box is submerged in water. 
While black boxes are not indestructible, they are designed to be very durable and able to withstand a lot of wear and tear. This helps ensure that they can be used to help investigators understand what happened in the event of an accident or incident, even if the rest of the vehicle or aircraft was severely damaged.
