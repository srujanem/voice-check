Microwave presets like "popcorn" or "plate of food" are programmed with specific cooking times and power levels based on the type of food being cooked and the desired outcome. For example, the "popcorn" preset is programmed to cook the popcorn for a specific amount of time at a specific power level to achieve the optimal level of popped kernels. Similarly, the "plate of food" preset is programmed to cook a general type of dish (such as a frozen dinner) for a certain amount of time at a certain power level to heat it to the appropriate temperature. In general, the cooking times and power levels for microwave presets are determined by the manufacturer based on testing and experimentation. The manufacturer will try different cooking times and power levels to find the combination that results in the best outcome for a particular type of food.
