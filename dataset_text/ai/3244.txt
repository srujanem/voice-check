This means that the horns can be trimmed or filed down and will grow back over time. Second, rhinos are endangered species, which means that there are very few of them left in the world. It is important to protect and conserve these animals, rather than exploiting them for commercial purposes. Third, rhino horns are often used in traditional medicine, but there is no scientific evidence to show that they have any medicinal value. In fact, using rhino horns for medicinal purposes can actually do more harm than good, as it encourages illegal poaching and the killing of these animals.
