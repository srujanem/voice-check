Clear ice is usually formed when the water is frozen slowly, allowing time for impurities to be removed. When water freezes quickly, it doesn't have time to get rid of the impurities and air bubbles, which can make the ice appear white and cloudy. You can try to control the freezing process to produce clear ice by freezing the water slowly. One way to do this is to use a container with a small opening at the top, such as a plastic bottle with the top cut off. Fill the container with purified water and put it in the freezer. As the water starts to freeze, the impurities and air bubbles will rise to the top and escape through the small opening. If you freeze the water slowly enough, you should be able to produce clear ice. Keep in mind that even if you freeze the water slowly, you may still end up with some clouds or impurities in the ice. This is because it is difficult to completely remove all impurities from the water, and some air bubbles may become trapped in the ice as it freezes. However, by freezing the water slowly, you can significantly reduce the amount of clouds and impurities in the ice.
