Eventually, the air molecules will become too spread out to be considered a "group," and they will just be part of the general background of gas and dust that exists throughout the universe.\n\nSo to sum it up: if air is released into space, it will just float away until it is pulled back by the gravity of another object, or until it becomes too spread out to be considered a group.
