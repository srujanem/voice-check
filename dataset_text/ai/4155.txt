Electric buses are a type of bus that runs on electricity instead of gasoline or diesel fuel. There are a few reasons why every bus at Schiphol airport is electric, but not all buses in general:\n\nCost: Electric buses are typically more expensive to purchase than traditional buses. This means that not all cities or bus companies can afford to buy electric buses.\n\nInfrastructure: Electric buses require charging stations, which can be expensive to install. Not all cities or bus companies have the infrastructure in place to support electric buses.\n\nRange: Some electric buses have limited range, which means they can only travel a certain distance before they need to be recharged. This can be a problem for long-distance routes.\n\nAvailability: There are not as many electric buses available as there are traditional buses, so it can be difficult for some cities or bus companies to find enough electric buses to replace their entire fleet.\n\nOverall, there are many factors that go into whether or not a city or bus company can use electric buses, and it's not always possible for every bus to be electric. However, more and more cities and bus companies are starting to switch to electric buses because they are better for the environment and can save money on fuel costs in the long run.
