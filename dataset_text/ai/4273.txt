No, you are not doing anything wrong. When you look through someone else's glasses, you are not seeing the world as they would see it with their glasses off. Glasses are designed to correct vision problems, such as nearsightedness or farsightedness, by bending light in a specific way so that it is focused correctly on the retina at the back of the eye. When you look through someone else's glasses, the lenses are still bending light in the same way as they would for the person who is wearing them, so you will not see things the same way as if you were not wearing any glasses at all.
