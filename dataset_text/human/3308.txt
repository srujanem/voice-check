Prussia was it 's own state and associated with the Holy Roman Empire for a long time . The HRE was a collection of Germanic states that got traded among many dukes , kings , emperors , et cetera for a long time . It became Germany when a bunch of Germanic states united in the 19th century . If you want more information about the when and how of Prussia becoming part of Germany ( or the creation of Germany in general ) check out Otto von Bismarck . He was Prussian and is credited with uniting Germany as it is today ( kinda ) . Regarding the name 's similarity to Russia , it 's important to note that Russia was known as Rus ' for a very long time before it became Russia . So , the etymology seems to be pretty separate from Prussia . They just share the same suffix / morpheme -ia and the roots share a terminal s. It 's really just coincidence that the names are so similar .
