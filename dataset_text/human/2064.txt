First , understand that nearly all electric generation is steam moving a turbine on a generator which creates electricity to transmission along lines or storage in ( capacitors and batteries ) so , nuclear power is used to create heat , to make steam to turn a turbine and make electricity . The Nuclear part is a set of radioactive and nonradioactive rods ( in 1970s technology ) which when set in an array get hot through the reaction , to heat water to make steam to turn a turbine . The rods are set up so as to be raised or lowered to intensify or reduce those reactions , along with insulating material to control the rate of those radioactive reactions and to control the heat created .
