Walking Race Athletes: Siri Chand Ram won 20km walk gold at 1982 Delhi Asian Games. Gurmeet Singh set national record (1:22:07). Jared Tallent (Australia) won double Olympic medals in 2008 Beijing 20km/50km walk. Jane Saville (Australia) won 2004 Athens bronze.

Later, the group of soldiers went to Delhi from Meerut. They laid siege on the Red Fort and declared the Moghul king, Bahadur Shah II as the Emperor of India. Protesting soldiers from different parts of India reached Delhi. Consequently, the protest became serious. It spread like wild fire to Kanpur and Jhansi.
