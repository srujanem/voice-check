Diabetes can affect your nerves . And extremities tend to be worst hit . People with diabetes can lose sensation in their feet , or lose the ability to use their feet due to nerve damage . With loss of sensation , a cut or a sore can easily get infected without the person knowing it , and that infection can spread and cause the need for the foot to be amputated ( which , I suppose , also causes inability to use their feet ) Diabetes can also mess up your blood flow to your extremities as well . Combined with the above nerve damage , and your feet are in serious danger .
