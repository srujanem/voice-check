Siwalik Range : These hills are the most recent formations and are located in the southern part of Himalayas. They have lesser height. They are also called ‘the Foothills’ of the Himalayas. These hills have narrow strips of plains or valleys which are called “Duns”. For example, Dehradune, Kota, Patli, Chaukhamba, Udhampur and Kotli.
