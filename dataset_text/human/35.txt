'The Sepoy Mutiny' took place due to maladministration of the British East India Company and anti-Indian acts in 1857. As a result, the administration of India was handed over to the British Queen from the British East India Company. Indian Government Act 1858 brought India under the direct administration of the Queen. The post of Governor General was changed into 'Viceroy'. Lord Canning became the first Viceroy of India. A new post called 'Secretary of State for India' was created in the British government.
