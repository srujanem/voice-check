Type 2 diabetes can be triggered by lifestyle changes such as overweight and inactivity. Treatment: Healthy eating and physical activity are the first steps to getting blood glucose levels and blood pressure under control. Antidiabetic pills and insulin injections can be used at some stage to manage diabetes. Exercise
