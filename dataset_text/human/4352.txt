Moreover , while there have been many possible explanations for a link put forth ( including by other commenters here ) , there is no data - driven explanation . One very important paper that has not been mentioned is from [ JAMA Psychiatry in 2012 ] ( URL_0 ) . This was a meta - analysis , meaning that data were compiled from pre - existing studies and used to increase the ability of the researchers to detect important but uncommon findings ( suicide is generally very uncommon in anti - depressant trials ) . Over 9,000 patients were evaluated . The conclusion contains the following statement : > For youths , no significant effects of [ anti - depressant ] treatment on suicidal thoughts and behavior were found , although depression responded to treatment . No evidence of increased suicide risk was observed in youths receiving active medication . Studies such as this were borne of skepticism in the psychiatric community and , though also not definitive in their conclusions , should encourage reconsideration of this now widely - accepted - as - fact issue .
