Yes absolutely . The fat is an energy reserve and would enable the fat person to survive starvation for far longer , as long as there was access to water . Assuming their diets before the starvation started i.e. none of them had any severe nutritional deficiencies the difference in energy reserve ( fatty tissue ) would make a big difference . You will die from starvation long before you die of any specific nutritional deficiency ( vitamin C , Vitamin B12 , iron for example ) .
