Smuggling can be checked effectively by encouraging import substitutions. Controlling domestic market prices, proper export-import policy (Exim), healthy foreign trade policy, strict coastal vigilance service, punitive measures to economic crimes, international and inter-state trade agreements are the main remedies to check smuggling. Proper awareness 77 should be created among the people that smuggling is an economic crime and anti-national activity. Social boycotts of smuggled goods have a great effect on checking smuggling.
