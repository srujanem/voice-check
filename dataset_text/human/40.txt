Chamarajendra Wodeyar X (1881-1894) assumed the Mysore throne in 1881. The Commissioners rule came to an end and a British Resident was appointed. Along with this, C. Rangacharlu was appointed as dewan. He established the Mysore Representative Assembly. Gold mines in Kolar in 1881, Bangalore - Mysore Railway line in 1882 was also started. Later Krishnaraja Wodeyar IV (1894-1940) initiated reforms in Gold mines, establishment of colleges, construction of Marikanive reservoir, Railways, Hospitals and Hydro electric project across Cauveri at Shivanasamudra. Electricity was supplied to Kolar Gold mines and to Bangalore. The first city to be electrified in India was Bangalore.
