My brother was a Harvard recruiter and explained that there is a point based system assigned to each applicant . The score runs from 1 to 5 . This was back before the SAT 's went to 2400 and were still in the 1600 range . An applicant who scored in the 1510 - 1600 range was given a score of 5 . Grade point average in the school they went to was rated . The amount of sports and how well the student did at those sports were given a set of guidelines for how many points they received . Even the interview was given a score of 1 - 5 . The trouble with the point based system is that frequently you 'll have applicants who are on the verge of making it into the school . That 's where the college essays came in to help those students who are on the verge of cutting it . Of course , if a college essay was complete trash , it will give the recruiters a reason to stamp out an applicants chances and give it to someone more deserving . Essentially , every point of the application process is an obstacle the applicant must overcome and the college essay is no different .
