India Seasons: India has a tropical monsoon climate with four seasons: Summer (March to May), South-West Monsoon (June to September), Retreating Monsoon (October to November), and Winter (December to February). Ganganagar in Rajasthan records 52°C in summer. Convectional pre-monsoon rainfall in April-May is called Kala Baisakhi in West Bengal, Coffee blossoms in Karnataka, and Mango showers in Kerala.
