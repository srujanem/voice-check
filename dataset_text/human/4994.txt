It might make more sense if you remember that pottery predates smelting in every society . Our ancestors would have already been able to create fires hotter than campfires with kilns , and would be familiar with the idea of using fire on raw materials to make useful tools . Also , as dancingwithcats says , certain metals , like copper , can be found in the ground in their native forms and could simply be picked out of the ground and hammered cold . Both of those things likely contributed to humans modifying kilns to extract metal and invent early metallurgy . And also , they did n't start out making 2000 degree furnaces ; they would probably have started out melting copper , then discovered that mixing it with other metals creates bronze , and then eventually made furnaces hot enough to extract iron and many other metals .
