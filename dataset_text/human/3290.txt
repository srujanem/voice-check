TLDR version : it was found on the surface , it was useful , it ran out , and people wanted more . Once upon a time it was very common in the form of a well , and oil would spill out of the ground . In its unrefined , natural form however it is very sticky and nasty . Someone discovered how to refine and use it though , noticing that it is very flammable if cleaned . This revolutionized the lighting and heating industry , let alone what it would do once someone would figure out the internal combustion engine . But the supply on the surface dried out , so people started digging into the wells where it came out of . Oil is in deep pockets in the ground , but long ago the pockets would overlap with the surface , the same way how underground lakes sometimes are the start of rivers .
