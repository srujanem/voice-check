Mica has some qualities; the important among them are insulation and elasity. It is used in the manufacture of telephones, telegraph, wireless service, glass, colour varnish, artificial rubber, dynamos and chemical industries. Mica is also called ‘kage bangara’ in Kannada. Types of Mica : Based on the chemical composition and colour, mica is divided into many types.
