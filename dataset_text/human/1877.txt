The major downside of electric vehicles has always been the energy source . In a train , it 's easy , you 're on tracks , so you can just build an infrastructure around the rails to power the train . With a free - moving vehicle like a truck , you ca n't just have it on rails . Nowadays Tesla is doing a pretty good job at having fully electric vehicles with relatively light and powerful batteries , but trucks incur much harder usage than personal cars , they are almost always on the move and do n't spend a lot of time in a garage where you could plug them up for the night . Tesla is building a small infrastructure of fast - charging stations , but it 's ridiculously small compared to the infrastructure of gas stations . afaik , there exist hybrid trucks , which burn both hydrocarbon fuel and have electric motors , with regenerative breaks et al .
