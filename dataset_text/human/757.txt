Junagadh, Hyderabad & Kashmir: Junagadh merged in 1949 after its Nawab fled. Hyderabad merged in 1948 after Indian forces defeated the Nizam's Razakars. Maharaja Hari Singh signed instrument of accession merging Jammu and Kashmir with India after Pakistani invasion.

Entrepreneurship: Derived from French 'Entreprende' (to undertake). Entrepreneur is an innovator and risk-taker driving capital formation, GDP growth, and employment. District Industrial Centres (DICs 1978) provide single-window support.
