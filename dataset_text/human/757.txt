People mentioned the technical reasons , but I think there is also the aesthetic reason . People have been painting on rectangular canvases before photographs , and we 're accustomed to framing being rectangular . A lot of our aesthetic norms are based on that image format . Can you imagine the " rule of thirds " on a circular image ? I 'm fairly sure we have the technology to make decent circular images , but we choose not to . There are some toy cameras that do make circular images maybe due to an exaggerated fisheye effect , but they are rarely ever used " seriously . "
