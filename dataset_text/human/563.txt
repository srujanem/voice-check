Basketball Legends: S.V. Appayya (first Karnataka player 1951 Asian Games), G. Dileep (1980 Moscow Olympics), B.S. Goutam (Arjuna/Ekalavya awardee), Geethu Anna Jose (first Indian in Australian WNBL), Earvin 'Magic' Johnson (USA, 9 NBA titles Lakers), Michael Jordan ('Air Jordan', 6 NBA titles Bulls, 41694 points), Kareem Abdul Jabbar (Sky Hook technique, 7 ft 2 in).

Partition of Bengal: Bengal was the centre of protests and anti-British sentiments. In order to curb this, Viceroy Lord Curzon proposed a plan to divide Bengal citing administrative problems. East and West Bengal based on Muslim and Hindu population were created in 1905.
