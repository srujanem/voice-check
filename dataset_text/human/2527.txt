In ancient Rome the sounds of U , V , and W , as we currently know them , were not distinguished without context . Context was typically required to get pronunciation " right " . The roman V was pronounced both as a ‘ w ’ in words like VENI ( pronounced “ way - nee ” ) and as the vowel ‘ u ’ in words like IVLIUS ( pronounced as “ Julius ” ) . As a result , many languages _ do _ use UU shape , and many actually pronounce it " double v " . All because the romans could n't create any clarity ;)
