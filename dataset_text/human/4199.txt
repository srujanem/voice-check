Commonly pictures of the Carribean are taken in shallow water . Shallow water always makes water look more clear , as the bottom will reflect light back to the surface . The other main reason is the sand . Sandy substrate will always have clearer water compared to muddier water . Most of the attractive areas we think of in the Carribean have a lot of sand and this keeps the " local water " very clean as sand is heavier than silt and harder to stir up into the water .
