Child Labour & Laws: Article 24 of Constitution prohibits child labour below 14 in hazardous units. Child Labour Prohibition and Control Act 1986, NCLP 1988, Sarvashikshana Abhiyan, and RTE Act 2009 protect child rights.

Yogasana - Standing Postures: Ardha Chakrasana (half wheel, strengthens neck/chest), Garudasana (eagle posture, balances on one leg), Prasaritha Padotthanasana (forward bend with wide legs), Angushthasana (balancing on big toes).
