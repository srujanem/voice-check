Cholera is shed in feces . So if someone has cholera and poops in a water source , the disease microorganisms are now present in that water source , ready to infect the next person that comes along and drinks from it . There is fecal bacteria everywhere you go and on many things you touch daily . You do n't even want to think about it . But unless a person had a communicable disease that can be spread that way , it wo n't harm you .
