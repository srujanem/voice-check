A rumour spread among the soldiers that the cartridges used in these rifles were greased with the fat of cow and pigs. While cows were sacred to the Hindus, pigs were blasphemous for Muslims. This incident became the immediate cause for the revolt.

Tribal Movements: The Santala tribal revolt in Bengal and Orissa hillocks fought against permanent landlords and money-lenders. Munda movement led by Birsa Munda and Halagali Hunters rebellion in Karnataka were prominent tribal uprisings.
