20 years of the Gopalakrishna GokhaleDadabhai Naoroji beginning of Congress are called the ‘Moderate Age’. Among the moderates, M.G.Ranade, Surendranath Bannerjee, Dadabhai Naoroji and Gopalakrishna Gokhale were prominent. The moderates trusted the British administration and their impartiality. They used to place their requests and appeals before the government within the constitutional framework. The moderates taught people about political situations.
