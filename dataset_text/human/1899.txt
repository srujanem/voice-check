The Indian Government established National Highways Authority of India in the year 1989 to construct and maintain National Highways. The No.1- National Highways link major cities of India with capitals of neighbouring countries. Ex.Lahore – Amritsar – Delhi – Agra – Calcutta – Imphal and Mandalay – Bari – Kathmandu Highways. II Grade National Highways link with the capitals of various states and ports.
