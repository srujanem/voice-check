To answer the specific question of the influence of the Communist Party , it is total . There are no legitimate opposition parties with any ability to influence policy . The Party decides everything from the list of candidates , to the winners , to the policies they implement . The Party is the State . Without membership in and compliance with the Party , a citizen has no chance of working in government . However , the Party has allowed some amount of Capitalist activity , raising the question of how much influence monied interests have in the Party . There has been an attempt to crack down on the corruption of Party members . But with so many families of China 's ruling elite living in luxury , it 's impossible to know how much influence money has on what the Party chooses to do , given the opacity of governance in China .
