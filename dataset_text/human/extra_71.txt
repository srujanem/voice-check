First , let 's go through a mental exercise . We start with a point . It has neither length , nor width , nor height , nor volume . It has NO dimension . It is it 's own single termination point . Extend a point along one dimension . You have a line . It has length . It has two terminating points . Extend a line along another dimension . You get a plane with length and width , with four terminating points . Extend a plane along another dimension . You get a cube with length , width , height , and eight terminating points . Extend a cube along another dimension . You get , conceptually , a hyper cube . It has length , width , height , and some name for the fourth physical dimension I do n't know . It has 16 terminating points . You can do this unto infinity . That does n't mean our brains can mentally conceive what such shapes actually look like . What you 're looking at in these gifs are projections . Your hand is 3D , not 2D. But what does your hand look like if it were 2D ? You already know : look at your shadow . Another way to understand it is to pass your hand through a 2D plane , the cross section of your hand on that plane is going to start with a point , and bulge and change as your fingers , wrist , and forearm pass through it . And that is more precisely what you see here . These are cross sections of 4D objects passing through a 3D volume , exactly like the cross sections of a 3D volume passing through a 2D plane .
