The reason for the 1 is that the phone system needs to know when you 're finished dialing based only on the numbers you 've dialed so far . If you start with some number other than 1 , then it knows you 're dialing only seven digits . If it starts with 1 , then it knows that you 're dialing ten more digits , so it does n't start trying to connect the call after the first seven . This is n't an issue with cell phones , where you send the whole number in at once , but older phones it would send each digit to the phone system as you dialed or pressed the digit .
