Arya Samaj: Established by Dayananda Saraswati, who emphasized that the Vedas were the source of truth and knowledge, giving the advice 'Back to the Vedas'. He condemned idol worship and the caste system by birth, advocating that caste be decided based on ability. He encouraged widow marriage, urged the use of swadeshi goods, and propounded his teachings in 'Satyartha Prakasha'. Arya Samaj conducted the 'Purification Ritual' (Shuddhi) to bring back converted individuals to Hinduism, proclaiming 'India should be for Indians'.
