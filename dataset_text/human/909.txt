Vikings are defined by their religion , their way of life and geography . Their place of origin is Scandinavia and while many settled down in the rest of Europe and other parts of the world , most stayed there . I 'm Danish and if I went back far enough , there 's are VERY high chance I have more than one Viking forefather . So the people are the same but they stopped being Vikings and became Danes , Norwegians and Swedes . The reason for this is christianity . The Viking era in Scandinavia lasted from about 750 to 1050 . At this point , christianity was expanding and more and more people converted in the North . Ultimately , the official religion went from the norse gods to christianity . Harald Blåtand ( Bluetooth ) is attributed for doing this , but he probably just took credit for what people were already doing . With the change of religion came a change in lifestyle . They stopped conquering and began focusing on trade ( which they had been doing all along ) . Slowly , they went from Vikings to Scandinavians / Northern Europeans . TL;DR : Christianity .
