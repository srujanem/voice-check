As has already been explained , it was originally " knave " , a servant , but changed to " jack " to avoid confusion with the king . Actually , many people were already using the word " jack " to refer to the knaves , but this was regarded as lower - class . It 's a word that means a simple man , and survives in the word " lumberjack " . If you 're asking about the designs on the traditional Anglo - American pack , they do n't represent anyone in particular . However , they are very similar to the designs on the traditional French pack , and these are said to represent specific people . They are : * Jack of spades = Ogier the Dane , a knight of Charlmagne ; * Jack of hearts = La Hire , real name Étienne de Vignolles , a 15th century military commander ; * Jack of diamonds = Hector , the hero of the Iliad ; * Jack of clubs = Lancelot , one of the Knights of the Round Table ; or alternatively Judas Maccabeus , the leader of a Jewish revolt against the Seleucid Empire in the 2nd century BC .
