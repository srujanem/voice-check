Smuggling : Smuggling is the act of secretly bringing goods from foreign countries and sending then to other countries without paying any import and export duties. The smuggling is dangerous to national interests. It leads to undesirable economic activities. The native industries and markets suffer grossly. Checking smuggling has become very essential.
