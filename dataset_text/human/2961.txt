On the basis of their chemical nature, hormones can be divided into groups : (i)peptide, polypeptide, protein hormones (e.g., insulin, glucagon, pituitary hormones, hypothalamic hormones, etc.) (ii)steroids (e.g., cortisol, testosterone, estradiol and progesterone) (iii)iodothyronines (thyroid hormones) (iv)amino-acid derivatives (e.g., epinephrine).
