Community Health & Waste Management: Proper waste management categorizes waste into Wet waste (organic, sewage) and Solid waste (glass, plastics, metals). Methods: Composting, Burying, Incineration. Sewage treatment purifies wastewater for agricultural reuse. Open wells need concrete aprons and covers to prevent mosquito breeding.

Gaidin Liu: Jadonang of the Naga tribe built a youth's army to fight against the British. Gaidin Liu, a young lady, collected ladies of North-eastern India and joined Jadonang in the Herakka movement. After Jadonang was hanged in 1931, Gaidin Liu led guerrilla warfare against the British until she was imprisoned in 1932 till 1947.
