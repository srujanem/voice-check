It 's simple : Chosen from among all others by the immortal elders , Solomon , Hercules , Atlus , Zeus , Achilles , Mercury ... Billy Batson and his mentor travel the highways and bi - ways of the land on a never ending mission .... to right wrongs , to develop understanding , and to seek justice for all . In time of dire need , young Billy has been granted power by the immortals to summon awesome at the utterance of a single word : * * SHAZAM * * ( Zam , zam , zam ) A word which transforms him in a flash into the mightiest of mortal beings : Captain Marvel !
