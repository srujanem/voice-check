In their earlier period , the Beatles were doing the same kind of rock & roll as other bands of that time , and even did covers of existing material , such as " Roll Over Beethoven " and so forth ; even then , you could tell the difference . Their music was more vibrant , more compelling , crisper , more inspired . A song such as " I Saw Her Standing There " does not have brilliant lyrics , it 's just one of thousands of cliche love songs , yet the melody is powerful and moving . Later Beatles songs were very inventive . They started dealing with topics that other musicians did not deal with , they became poetic , satirical , even surrealistic , combined with even more elaborate and inventive melodies . The later work of the Beatles remains among the very best found in rock & roll , to this day , and it was at the time unprecedented , and became a huge inspiration for everyone who came after the Beatles .
