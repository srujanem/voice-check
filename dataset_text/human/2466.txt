LED bulbs are more efficient than incandescent bulbs , so you need less energy to produce the same amount of light . However , bulbs were never previously described in terms of lumens ( light output ) , but in terms of wattage ( energy use ) . That conversion tells you that the 1.2W LED bulb you are buying will have the same light output as a 10W incandescent bulb . All the light for about 1/8th the electricity .
