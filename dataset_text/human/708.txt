OK . Lets be clear here . There are benefits to the apple connectors that many fans like to talk about . But the reason Apple does not use a standardized connector has more to do with the accessory market then it does any technical ability . Apple owns the patent on those connectors . If a third party wants to make an IPhone accessory they need to pay Apple for the right to use the connector . This means Apple gets a cut of the entire third party accessory market without having to actually make any of the accessories themselves . If apple used a standard connector it would be able to do everything the new connectors can do ( * nearly everything * ) but Apple would not make any money when someone made an IPhone FM transmitter .
