Used to own a chain of cellphone stores : " All coverage is local " . Nobody has coverage everywhere . If it covers where you work , live , and regularly go , you tell your friends it is great ! If it does n't , you tell your friends it stinks . You need to find the best carrier for your specific needs . As to the marketing claims , it is no different than Kellogg 's Raisin Bran and Post Raisin Bran each claiming that they have the most raisins . This kind of a claim is rarely challenged , can not easily be proved , and so just becomes exactly what it is : bullshit marketing .
