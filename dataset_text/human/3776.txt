Think of it like a human running in the cold . Your efficiency , or " gas mileage , " is directly related to how hard it is to run . If you put a scarf over your face to block the cold , it will be harder to breathe and therefore harder to run , so your " gas mileage " goes down . Now , some scarves are easier to breathe through than others . Some are cleaner or dirtier than others . Picking an easy - to - breathe - through scarf that is nice and clean is a great way to improve your gas mileage . In fact , if you want to run your fastest for a short period , you 'll take the scarf off all together . And sometimes car and motorcycle enthusiasts will actually do this : They 'll go without the air filter to reduce resistance and increase efficiency and just accept that it 's bad for their engine in the long run . ( Now , it 's not actually this simple , because the engine would need to be tuned to run properly and to its fullest potential without an air filter . )
