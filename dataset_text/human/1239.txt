The internet + the fact that it uses more system power to have split screen and many games push consoles to their limits without split screen . If you have a solid 30FPS game with single player then adding split screen can cause the game to drop to choppy / unplayable levels . So in order to comfortably add split screen sacrifices have to be made in the graphics department and these days people do n't take kindly to games that do n't look like they are on the cutting edge graphics wise . So i m sure better graphics for single player wins out over worse graphics for split screen in most cases .
