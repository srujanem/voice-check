When a company gets big enough , and starts making a decent amount of money , they typically decide they want to allow individual people to invest money into their company . They start this by having something called an initial public offering . This is when they announce that they are going to divide part of their company into pieces called " shares " , and make these shares available for purchase by anyone . The price per share is the dollar amount that you see for a company'a stock quote . When you buy shares , it 's usually because you think the company is likely to do well . When they do well , their stock quote goes up , and your share is worth more . If you sell your shares after the price goes up , than you make money . If the stock goes down , and you end up selling it , you lose money . You do n't make or lose any money until you actually sell the stock . The up and down movement of stock prices is complicated and I do n't think I can do a concise enough job explaining that , but at a basic level , prices go up when more people want to buy the stock than sell it , and they go down when more people want to sell than buy . The stock market is a general term for the all the markets that facilitate the buying and selling of shares . There are several stock exchanges out there , with the most famous probably being The NY Stock Exchange .
