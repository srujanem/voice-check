Rebellion of Surapura and Koppal: Surapura is located in present Yadgir District. During the reign of Venkatappa Nayaka who came to power in 1853, it raised a flame of rebellion against the British. In 1857, it came to the notice of the government that representatives of Nana Saheb were present in Surapura. Officer Campbell submitted a report that the King was involved in maladministration. Venkatappa Nayaka is presented as the leader of the 1857 revolt in Karnataka by historians. The British army captured Surapura in 1858.
