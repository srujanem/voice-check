Rural Development & PURA: 68.84% of Indians live in villages. Mahatma Gandhi advocated 'Grama Swarajya'. Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam conceptualized PURA (Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas) with physical, knowledge, electronic, and economic links.

Meditation & Ashtanga Yoga: Dhyana is one of the 8 limbs of Patanjali's Ashtanga Yoga. Involves aligning trunk, back, and neck in postures like Padmasana or Swasthikasana, focusing mind continuously on a single object. Benefits include mental clarity, emotional balance, stress reduction, and enhanced concentration.
