Formulae . Basically at higher level math you learn a bunch of generic equations that are fairly simple / you already know how to solve . Now you take a big complex problem and you break it down and reshape it using algebra then you apply the appropriate formula . Think of a coin sorting machine . You have a jumbled bunch of different coins . The first thing the coin sorting machine does is organize the coins by type . Its not counting anything , just putting everything in its proper place . Now each stack of coins is the same type so its pretty easy to count up how much money is in each stack , and finally the counting machine adds those simple answers to give a total . The algebra is where you organize everything ( sort the coins ) the formula is where you solve the known problems ( count the stacks ) and formulating the answer is just recombining those parts ( adding up the totals ) . tl;dr first they break it up into simple parts , solve the simple parts and recombine the answers , each step is fairly simple but time consuming
