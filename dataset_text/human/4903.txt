South West Monsoon separates India into two branches: one is the Arabian Sea branch and the other is the Bay of Bengal branch. Monsoon winds are obstructed by the Western Ghats and as such, maximum rainfall is seen to the West of the Ghats. As these winds proceed to the East, they get depleted of rain.
