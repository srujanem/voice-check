It 's called a [ Shepard tone ] ( URL_0 ) . Basically , a tone fades out as it descends in pitch . As it fades out , another tone fades in , an octave higher . Tones an octave apart tend to overlap in your brain ( they are perfectly consonant ) and you do n't notice the overlap . You perceive it as one continuously descending pitch . Even better , there are a few tones doing this at the same time , which really drives home the illusion .
