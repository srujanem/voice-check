Safety regulations play a big part in it - new regulations are released annually that major manufacturers must conform to . Things like heights of bumpers / hoods , A / B / C pillar stiffness requirements , etc . Changes such as these prevent Ford , Chevy and every other big manufacturer from re - releasing older models with updated technology . That said , custom auto shops have been doing upgrades to old models for decades . The idea behind these cars , commonly called restomods , is to take an old chassis and modify it to accept modern engines , suspensions , wheels , etc .
