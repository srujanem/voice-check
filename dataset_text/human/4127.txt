Good is not necessarily happy or nice in this context . It is morally correct . It is implied that one of the things that protects evil beings ( demons ) is the morals that cause good beings to pull their punches . To the foolish , this will seem like the good are not capable of wiping out the evil until the evil have gone too far , and the good realize that eradicating evil is necessary . At this point the good do not pull their puches . They do not show mercy . They 're finished with trying to rehabilitate the evil . This can be a truly terrifying thing to witness , let alone be subjected to .
