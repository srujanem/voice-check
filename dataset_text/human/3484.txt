A mainframe performs transactional batch processing and has backwards compatibility as far back as the stone age . Cloud computing means you are consuming resources out of a pool . The resources you consume , the work performed , is n't guaranteed to be on any one server , or just one server , and hardware can be subdivided through virtualization . The whole point is that you do n't need to know where in the cloud your work is , but that it is in * this * cloud as opposed to * that * cloud .
