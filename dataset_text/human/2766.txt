My understanding is that we sleep in 90 minute cycles . The first 90 minutes is more focused on muscle refresh with a little REM ( brain refresh ) . Each subsequent cycle spends more and more time in REM . Everyone 's body is different but this refresh work is critical to a long life . If not done , then heart and other issues are a very real threat . 3hrs - 2 cycles , 4.5hrs - 3 cycles , 6hrs - 4 cycles , 7.5 hrs - 5 cycles [ which tends to be what is advised ] Unfortunately can not store sleep but you can every 3 days catch up . I am not a doctor by any stretch but did spend a lot of time in sleep studies with sleep doctors . Found my fatigue and ability to sleep instantly when needed was a result of sleep apnea - essentially was n't sleeping but rather choking / micro wake up every 1 - 2 minutes for 6 - 7 hours for years . Solved apnea 3 years ago and wake up refreshed all the time . Men especially - snoring / choking is a real problem - do n't ignore . Short answer - yes there can be a problem with long term limiting sleep . You should gauge the proper amount on how you feel waking up - each one of us is different .
