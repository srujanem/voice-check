Biologically and structurally there is no difference between hair and fur . Both are a outgrowth of a protein called keratin , which also makes up things like nails and claws . Fur is used to describe animal with thick coats ( e.g. dogs , bears , cats , rabbits ) , hair is usually reserved to describe animals with thin , sparse coats ( e.g. humans , hippos , elephants , manatees ) .
