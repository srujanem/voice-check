He signed the ‘Treaty of Srirangapatna’ in 1792 which had unfavourable conditions. With this, Third Anglo-Mysore War came to an end. The British were successful in inserting unfavourable conditions in order to weaken Tippu Sultan. Tippu was forced to part with half of his kingdom, was forced to pay three crore rupees as war damage fee and had to pledge two of his sons as a guarantee against the payment. He was also forced to release the prisoners of war.
