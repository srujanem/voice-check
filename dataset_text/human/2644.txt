The pars distalis region of pituitary, commonly called anterior pituitary, produces growth hormone (GH), prolactin (PRL), thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH), Portal circulation adrenocorticotrophic hormone (ACTH), luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle Posterior stimulating hormone (FSH). Pars intermedia pituitary secretes only one hormone called melanocyte stimulating hormone (MSH).
