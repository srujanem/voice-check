Eyes come in two configurations for animals . Predator and prey . Prey have their eyes on the sides of their head for a very wide panoramic view of the world . They evolved to have that view so they could see things sneaking up on them as soon as possible and try to get away . The problem with having such a wide view is that it is much more difficult to focus on any particular thing . This brings us to the eyes of a predator . When an animal is hunting , it needs to be able to focus in on its prey and chase it and catch it . Part of catching it is depth perception . With a wide field of vision it is harder to distinguish how far something is . It is very hard to catch something when you do n't know if it 's 5 feet in front of you or 15 feet in front of you . Since humans are wired for predator mode , our eyes and brains evolved to focus on things as opposed to giving us a panoramic view . If you did have a wider field of vision it would be much harder for you to catch prey and you 'd , in an evolutionary sense , die off and the gene that allowed you to see in panorama would die with you .
