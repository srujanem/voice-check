Gravity is kind of special so I 'm going to answer a related question : What is electromagnetism and why does it exist ; why does charge repel or attract charge after all ? The answer is that charge is not a stuff that electrons and protons have . Instead , think about it this way : physical things interact , and there are different kinds of interaction ( electromagnetic , gravitational , weak nuclear , strong nuclear ) . What we know is that physical things can be grouped by how they interact . For example , take all the things that interact by all four interactions . Those things shall be called quarks . Now take all the things that interact by all of them except the strong nuclear interaction . Those things shall be called leptons . We do n't know why some of them interact with some interactions and not others , we only know that they do and so we group them that way . ( In a way , it 's like asking why zebras are n't reptiles . They just are n't , and that 's why they 're lumped with the mammals rather than the reptiles . ) So what is electric charge ? Electric charge is simply this : it 's the tendency to participate in the electromagnetic interaction . If you have a thing , and it 's in one of the classes of things that participates in the electromagnetic interaction , then we say it has electric charge . ( Likewise , if something participates in the strong nuclear interaction , then it has what 's called " color " , which is just a different kind of charge . ) Electric charge is * nothing more * than that . It is not a goop , a dusting , a component , or a kind of paint . So , the naive answer to your question about gravity would be that mass is just a label that is attached to things that attract gravitationally . I say it 's a naive answer , because gravity is actually more complicated , and things that have no mass at all can still be affected by gravity .
