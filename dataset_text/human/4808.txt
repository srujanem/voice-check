Cancer is when extra cells grow in your body , and they are n't the right kind of cells . People often say " cancer " like its some big scary all - encompassing monster . The reality is that when someone tells you that they have " cancer , " they have a specific kind with a fancy medical name based on where it is on their body and what kind of extra cells are growing there . Different kinds of bad cells are worse than others , or are harder to treat because of where they are located . This is why some cancers , like pancreatic cancer , are considered death sentences , and others , like leukemia , are considered very treatable .
