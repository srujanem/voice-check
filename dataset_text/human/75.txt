South-West Monsoon & Rain Distribution: South-West Monsoon brings 75% of India's annual rainfall via Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal branches. Mawsynram in Meghalaya gets the highest rainfall in the country, while Ruyli in Jaisalmer gets the least (8.3 cm). Average annual rainfall is 118 cm. Because monsoon failure causes drought and heavy monsoon causes floods, Indian agriculture is called a 'gamble with the monsoons'.
