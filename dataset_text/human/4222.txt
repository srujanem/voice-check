For some people there are 4 primary colors . See , the 3 primary colors , and the idea of color itself is a result of human biology . Most people have 3 different types of cone cells in their eyes . Each type of cell reacts to a different part of the light spectrum ; red , green , and blue . Red , green and blue are the primary colors of additive colors , your monitor for instance has RGB LEDs . Subtraction colors like paints are the more familiar Red , Yellow , Blue . Some people , mostly women have 4 different cone cells in their eyes . This is called [ tetrachromia ] ( URL_0 ) . Because of this they see a fourth primary color , probably yellow . This means that they can see more distinct shades of the colors we normally see . On a related note , women in general see more distinct shades of colors , particularly reds then men .
