Because people need to borrow money . We pool our money into a bank , and then banks lend it out . Its not just people buying houses who need money , but businesses do too , particularly smaller ones . Your local pizza parlor or chinese food restaurant was probably started by a loan from a bank . Pretty much , if you are n't large enough that people will buy bonds you underwrite , and you are n't willing to sell off ownership , then you get money by going to banks .
