The Japanese thought that the USA would get involved in the war eventually . Since they knew that they US had a lot of ships at Pearl Harbor they thought that they could make the US Navy weak if they destroyed a lot of ships before the US was ready for an attack . This was actually correct , but the US had very large manufacturing industry so they were able to rebuild the Navy faster than the Japanese thought they could . It was like if you think someone is going to fight you , you might sucker punch them so that you have a free hit and they are weaker before the fight actually starts .
