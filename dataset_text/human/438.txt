He 's that one behaviorist known for classical conditioning . His experiment : he rang a bell right before giving his dogs food , and through that , the dogs associated the ringing of the bell with the anticipation of food . The bell ringing in this case would be the knowledge of a bathroom nearby ( for other people like my mom , it 's seeing the toilet ) . Since you know that you 'll relieve yourself in the bathroom ( just like the dogs learned that after hearing the bell , they would get food ) , the conditioned response would be wanting to pee immediately .
