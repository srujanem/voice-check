First Aid Procedures: Drowning rescue requires clearing airways and performing artificial respiration by pressing the victim's chest/diaphragm 12 times per minute while lying face-down. Bandages: Band-aids for small cuts, Elastic Crepe bandages for joint sprains, Sterilized Gauze rolls (2x2, 3x5, 5x9) for bleeding control.
