The Kurds are an ethnicity of people who do not have a country to call their own . They are closer related to Persians than they are Arabs or Turks , and they have a language and history distinct from all three . The nearly 40 million of them live in Turkey , Iraq , Iran , and Syria . The reason why the female fighters are so important is because not only is it a big deal that nearly 50 % of their army is women , but of all regions of the world this sort of social equality is huge . It is also a big story because it shows the Kurds as a people and not an army , all active bodies to protect their brothers and sister .
