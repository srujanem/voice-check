The word they are using is " bearer bond " . A bearer bond is a little bit like a check . When the bond is " mature " ( a certain date has passed ) you can redeem the bond for cash . If you " bear " ( have ) the bond , you 're entitled to redeem it no matter how you obtained it . ( A bond sold as a security is usually registered , meaning it is sold to a certain person , and in order to redeem it , you have to be that person , and there 's a ledger kept whenever the bond is sold to another person so that it 's ownership is clearly defined . The advantage of a bearer bond is that nobody knows you hold the bond until you try to redeem it , so it has some privacy value ( and it 's useful if you 're a criminal , for example , and do n't want your finances to be traceable ) . )
