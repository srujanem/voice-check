Yes it is possible . On average , a license plate is 6 - 8 numerical / letter combination . Short term memory can easily remember that short of a combination . Additionally , at a traffic stop you normally tell dispatch the license plate of the car you are stopping before you even come to a stop and initiate a conversation with the person you just stopped . So if that vehicle flees , all you have to say is the vehicle is fleeing I 'm in pursuit . However if for some reason you do n't get the plate and the vehicle flees you 'll be surprised how much the brain remembers when your adrenaline is pumping .
