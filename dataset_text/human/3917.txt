There are about 1 to 2 million Islets of Langerhans in a normal human pancreas representing only 1 to 2 per cent of the pancreatic tissue. The two main types of cells in the Islet of Langerhans are called ααααα-cells and βββββ-cells. The α-cells secrete a hormone called glucagon, while the βββββ-cells secrete insulin.
