Omaha was originally a center for agriculture trade in the area . Farmers and ranchers from the midwest would take their products to Omaha to trade or ship along the Missouri river . As time went on these businesses grew to expand into other regions but kept their headquarters in Omaha . That is why ConAgra , Union Pacific , Mutual of Omaha , and Berkshire are in Omaha . The same phenomena can be seen in Kansas City and Chicago with their cattle and other livestock trading in the past and becoming modern hubs today .
