# 1 : Isolation . It would take months to travel from the US to England , and this includes lessons on proper pronunciation . Accents are most prominent when areas can be cut off for extensive periods of time . # 2 : Language mixing . The Americans encountered both other European languages ( Spanish , Dutch , and French for the most part ) as well as with the native languages . Moose , Moccasin , Chipmunk , Pecan , Skunk , and Totem are all words just from Algonquin , for example . The Americans adopted certain words , and often pronounced them in ways that English would n't . # 3 : Origins . Yes , most settlers were English , but there were large numbers of Dutch , French , Spanish , Scottish , German , and Irish who arrived as well . Not to mention the natives .
