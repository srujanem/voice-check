I think it has more to do with what 's in the poop rather than the poop itself . I used to think that dogs were just stupid and loved the taste of a turd regardless of state . My dog never ate poop until we gave him a bacon flavored bone . The next day she took an immediate interest in her fresh turd and proceeded to eat it . It was at that moment that I realized that artificial flavoring in pet food and treats tend to survive the trip through the animal and come out the other end as poo flavoring . Yes , dogs are stupid . That does n't mean cat poop is n't delicious . Science should really find an answer to this .
