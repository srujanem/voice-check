SOS did n't start as an abbreviation of " Save Our Souls " , it 's just a really recognisable signal in Morse code ( ... --- ... ) . Save Our Souls then started as a way to remember the code . In fact the distress signal is ... --- ... without the pauses you 'd leave between the letters if actually sending the letters SOS . It 's really a special " letter " meaning distress , which happens to sound like SOS .
