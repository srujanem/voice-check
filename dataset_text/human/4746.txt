Every search engine has their own catalog , similar to the catalogs you see in a library . A very simplified view is to think of every website in their system catalogued as a set of keywords . When a user enters something to search on , the search engine goes to this catalog combines the result from the keywords you entered and returns the result . All of this is made complicated by a number of factors 1 . The very large number of websites 2 . Constantly updating the catalogue as more websites are created and old ones change . 3 . Finding the relevant keywords for a site 4 . A good scoring system that gives high scores for relevant sites 5 . Doing all of the above fast !
