Intelligence is not some kind of prize that every species is evolving towards . It 's a very unusual and complicated evolutionary path with a relatively narrow use . Many species evolve some level of intelligence . Dolphins , crows , parrots , primates they all evolved some pretty significant problem solving and tool using level of intelligence . But there 's more common evolutionary advantages that are much more robust . Simply laying a lot of eggs . Being very fast , very big or very small for instance . The humble cockroach is one of the biggest evolutionary successes this planet has ever seen and it 'll most likely never evolve towards being an intelligent species because it would n't improve upon the success of the roach . That said , dinosaurs were n't a single species . There were a lot of different dinosaurs over time . T - rex lived closer to our age than the days when the stegosaurus wandered around for instance . Towards the end of the era of the dinosaurs , there were some pretty intelligent dino 's going around . If they had n't suffered mass extinction event , who knows what those might have evolved into .
