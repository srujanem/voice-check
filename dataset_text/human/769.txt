Basically , stem cells are undifferentiated cells and have the ability to become any other type of cells ( liver , kidney , muscle etc ) . Stem cells can come from many different sources . One way to get stem cells is from embryonic stem cells . This requires the fertilization of an egg by a sperm and then , before it can become a fetus , harvesting the undifferentiated cells ultimately stopping the creation of a fetus . The controversy comes in because people some believe the start of life is when an egg and sperm fuse and that not allowing that to grow into a fetus is essentially abortion / murder . Unfortunately this has tainted all stem cell research , even the use of stem cells from bone marrow etc , in the eyes of some of the public . Hope this answers it for you .
