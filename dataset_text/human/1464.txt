Tungabhadra project : Irrigation and generation of hydroelectric power are the main objectives of this project. It is established jointly by Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh states. This dam is built across Tungabhadra river at Mallapur near Hospet in Vijayanagara district. It provides hydro-electric power and irrigation facilities to 5.5 lakh hectares in Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka. The reservoir is called ‘Pampasagara ’. 6. Nagarjunasagar Project :
