It can be done . By 3D modelling , this is what is happening . When a 3D model is created , it is a bunch of points in space . By using various lighting techniques , textures and some other files , what they essentially are doing is just that - create a likeness of a human , add motion and speech to it and then just render it - ( make all of it happen at full resolution ) . Many parts of the visuals are coded - such as physics engine that adds collisions , gravity etc ; Lighting engines that can simulate the light and its effects ; motion libraries to easily incorporate motion etc .. All of them are programs that run with each other . If you watched an animated movie ( such as ones by Pixar , Disney and the likes ) , that is what you are watching .
