Creationism : God made everything effectively as it is today . For example , according to creationism , humans existed just days after the Earth was created . Intelligent Design ( ID ): Living things are really complicated and could not have developed through natural processes . Therefore some intelligent force or entity must have played a large role in the development of different species . ID does n't specify who this designer is , but there 's a strong implication that it 's essentially a god . Neither of these ideas is considered to be supported by the scientific community . In general , ID is viewed as a toned - down version of creationism to present creationism in a more scientific - sound and less religious manner .
