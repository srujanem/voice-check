* * Scientific law * * - If X , then Y. A law simply is a way to predict , based on previous observations , how certain things interact . A law can be very limited in its application because you can only predict results if the circumstances are similar to the ones you measured . Laws do not attempt to explain * why * a certain thing occurs , just that it does . * * Scientific theory * * - If X , then Y , because Z. Scientific theories come up with an explanation for why certain things happen in certain circumstances . Theories have been tested , and can predict results which can then be tested . Some scientific theories take several laws and define the underlining principle that explains all these laws .
