There are a couple different ways this works . For instance , during the World Wars before the US sent combat troops , they were selling weapons to the Allied forces . Arms manufacturers got money from the governments of England , France , etc . Much of which was redistributed to its employees through wages , the US government through through taxes , and to their suppliers . During , say , the Iraq War , the US is largely funding itself . So the government is giving money to the troops , contractors , arms manufacturers , etc . In theory , this would create more jobs and kickstart the economy , get people spending more and using more American services , and this did work for a short time , but the costs of the war turned into a giant sinkhole .
