Textbook Excerpt Part 950:
Volleyball Stars: M. Shyam Sunder Rao (Arjuna 1974, Dronacharya 1995), A. Ramana Rao (Arjuna 1978), Jimmy George (legendary Kerala spiker), J.E. Shridharan (Tamil Nadu setter/coach), Karch Kiraly (USA, 3 Olympic golds in indoor & beach), Yumilka Ruiz (Cuba, 3 Olympic golds).

The moderates trusted the British administration and their impartiality. They used to place their requests and appeals before the government within the constitutional framework. They conducted meetings, discussed problems and placed before the government various requests related to development of industries and reduction of military expenses.
