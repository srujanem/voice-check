To put it as simply as possible , Sony considered the lack of investment in Information Security an ' acceptable risk ' . This , plus the high profile nature of the company across a number of different markets makes it an attractive target for anyone looking to send a message , like the recent bout of hacking . I have n't been following this particular incident , so I 'm not sure of the hacker 's actual motivations , but the core of the issue lies in Sony 's decision to not invest in InfoSec or follow a number of basic security guidelines . Side Note - Sony is n't the only company guilty of this . You 'll find most companies who do n't focus specifically in the tech industry have subpar security practices , as its considered an investment in an area of the company that provides no profit . Its an unfortunate reflection of the general ignorance about what an increasingly digital world means .
