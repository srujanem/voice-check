Biologist here , pubic hair honestly does nt do much . It was formed through thousands of years as an evolutionary sign that a woman and male were old enough to mate . The reason why we * do n't * have hair everywhere else is because it is a safe haven for parasites and bugs . There is no evolutionary advantage as well as it would n't be needed to demonstrate fertility as pubic hair is .
