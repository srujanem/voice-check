A Clockwork Orange does n't have a " message " , it 's a dystopian story and character study on Alex . I do n't think the film presents his actions as good , or anything other than " unbridled lust / rage " at all . The film asks questions about how far it is ethical to go to " fix " people . In the film , Alex is forcibly rehabilitated with classical conditioning , forcing him to go through extreme pain for a very long period of time , with the end result of one of his favorite things in the world ( classical music ) being physically painful for him , because it was part of the technique used to torture him and make him " better " .
