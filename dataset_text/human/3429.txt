In order for a company to have exclusive rights to a good , they have to have a patent on it . For most foodstuffs , there either is no patent for it , or the patent expired years ago . When you think about it though , most of the food sold in the average grocery store has existed in one form or another for hundreds of years . Think about Mac ' n Cheese . I 'm fairly certain that Kraft did n't come up with the idea to put cheese on noodles , and even if they patented the box of easy prep Mac ' n Cheese , their patent on it would have expired a long time ago . The grocery store has just as much right to sell their own version of this product as Kraft does , both logically and legally .
