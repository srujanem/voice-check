I think the idea is that the iPhone represents rampant capitalism and materialism . It is the current status symbol of personal and individual wealth and ambition which communism is philosophically opposed to . Thus the perception is that having an iPhone and being a communist is hypocritical , a more apt comparison would be like being a left - winger / liberal but being opposed to gay marriage ; the two are ideologically incompatible . This is just an explanation , not a reflection of my own standpoint .
