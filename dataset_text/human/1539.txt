* Degree * as an English word just means " amount , level , or extent " of something . It comes from the Latin * de * + * gradus * , " step down " or " step from " , i.e. a set of incremented steps to measure some quantity . It 's used for a lot more than just temperature and angles , too . Check out [ Degree ] ( URL_0 ) on Wikipedia . Alcohol concentration , sugar concentration , density , curvature , etc . etc . Very widespread use .
