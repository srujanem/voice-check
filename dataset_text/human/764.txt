In a thunder storm it is believed that warm air creates convection currents ( up drafts ) within the cloud which causes water molecules to " rub " on each other , causing basically static electricity . This eventually builds until there 's enough charge for the cloud to discharge in the form of Lightning . These convection currents are also what are responsible for hail and I think tornadoes . Now in the winter that warm air really is n't there , because its cold out . So the storms do n't have that easy access to the energy to generate lightning . This makes thundersnow rare because the storm has to has to develop convection currents from a very low contrast in low and high altitude temperatures .
