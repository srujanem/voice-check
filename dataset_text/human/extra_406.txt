In addition to other posts , back in the day many industry leaders for stuff like cotton , lumber and tobacco collectively recognized hemp and marijuana as a threat to their businesses . Hemp is actually a very useful plant and grows like a weed so it 's easy to manage large fields of it . If a competitor had started introducing products made with hemp , it would have dug deeply into their profits and would have shaken the monopolies on their well established cash crops / goods . Hemp had been know for it 's uses for centuries , most notably in the use of nautical rope for it 's strength and durability . It can be used to replace wood paper , as an alternative to cotton fabric , etc . Since these industries had money in spades they bought the legislation required to make both the psychoactive and non - psychoactive strains illegal by way of demonizing Cannabis as a whole .
