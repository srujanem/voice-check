Round Table Conferences: The First Round Table Conference was held in 1930 without Congress. In 1931, Gandhi-Irwin Pact was signed and Gandhiji attended the Second Round Table Conference. B.R. Ambedkar demanded separate electorates for depressed classes, leading to the Poona Pact which reserved seats for depressed classes within general electorates.

Non-Conventional Energy: Renewable sources like Solar energy (Baramar Rajasthan, Chikkaballapur Karnataka) and Wind power (Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Karnataka) conserve exhaustible fossil fuels.
