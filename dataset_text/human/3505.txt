Because the half life is the time required for half of the material to decay . So : 1 HL = 50 % of the material remains 2 HL = 25 % of the material remains 3 HL = 12.5 % of the material remains and so on ... You will notice that the percentages will never get down to zero , and so discussing " full life " would be meaningless as a full life is equal to infinity .
