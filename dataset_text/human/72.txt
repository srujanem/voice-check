Northern Great Plain & Peninsular Plateau: The Northern Great Plain (Sutlej-Ganga plain) is completely flat, formed by alluvial soil deposited by Indus, Ganga, and Brahmaputra rivers. The Peninsular Plateau is the largest and most ancient division covering 16 lakh sq. km. It includes Central Plateau and Deccan Plateau (Sahyadri in Western Ghats). Anaimudi peak (2695 m) in Annamalai range is the highest peak in South India.
