Do you pay rent for your housing ? Do you have trouble finding a job ? Do you fear you might not have enough money to retire at a reasonable age ? Do you find your wallet constrained by the prices of everyday goods ? If not , congratulations , you 're already on top . But billions of people do face some or all of these problems . And one of the main reasons they do is as a consequence of the socioeconomic elite manipulating economies and governments in order to augment their own wealth and power ( which in turn is what they use for said manipulation ) . The problem is n't that they 're rich per se , the problem is that they use unfair techniques to * stay * rich ( and get richer ) at the expense of society .
