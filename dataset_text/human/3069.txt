Intelligence has some contra - survival aspects as well - a large brain requires a lot of energy to run , which means a lot more food is required . The trade - off is that an intelligent animal can apply its intelligence to solve problems , which may help it go get more food . Dinosaurs may simply have had a different balance of needs than proto - humans did , and the solution to their particular equation may have favored low intake requirements over the intelligence to increase their intake . Remember , going to the moon and paying taxes are not the goals of evolution - only reproduction is . And evolution does not look for perfect solutions , only good - enough solutions . Dinosaurs had a solution that worked ( until the meteor came ) , so there was no pressure to develop anything else .
