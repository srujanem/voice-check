In 1858, the Queen of England made a declaration. This declaration has been called India's 'Magna Carta'. The agreements made by the East India Company with the kings were accepted. Ambitious expansion plans had to be given up. A stable government had to be provided to Indians. There would be equality before law. The government would exhibit religious tolerance.

Linguistic Reorganisation of States: After Potti Sriramulu died on 58-day fast for Vishalandhra, Justice Fazal Ali Commission (with Panikkar & Kunjru) was set up in 1953. The State Reorganisation Act 1956 formed 14 States and 6 UTs. Mysore State was formed on Nov 1, 1956 and renamed Karnataka in 1973.
