Remember though stocks are a gamble . But you can lessen your chances of losing with some due diligence and luck . I for one have no due diligence and lost a grand some of money quite easily because I was too lazy to do enough research on the companies which I invested in . That , and I got greedy / scared so I held when I should have sold and I sold when I should have held . So ... in the ways of do what I say , not what I do : 1 ) Research companies deeply until you can ensure yourself that it is actually worth more that you think it is selling at . 2 ) Set the price that you think it 's worth as your sell limit , and actually sell at that . If it ends up going higher than that , then oh well , at least you made money . Try to find out why your guess was wrong and learn from that mistake ( though many times it may just be that the market is stupid and is overpricing the stock ) . 3 ) Set another price as a lower limit of how much you 're willing to lose in that investment ( maybe 10 % ? If it drops 10 % , then that 's all you lose . Warning though , it could very well just drop 10 % just to then suddenly skyrocket ; thus you lose on potential gains ) . In any case , I 'm probably not the best person to be getting advice from , but I think if I actually followed through myself , then I 'd be in the positive rather than negative . Good luck !
