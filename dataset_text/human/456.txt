Herbivores have multiple stomachs with different enzymes that allow them to extract nutrition from fiber . Humans do not . Plus herbivores eat for up to half their lives , chomping on uncooked grasses and such . They did a test where some humans were given a diet equivalent to a gorilla , for a week . They spent ~8 hours chomping on raw vegetables , and by the end they were lethargic and malnourished . The human body simply ca n't sustain itself on raw vegetables . We evolved through cooking an omnivorous diet .
