In addition to the binding ability of egg proteins , eggs are great emulsifying agents ... meaning they are able to bring together oily substances and substances that dissolve in water or are water - based . Ever heard the phrase , " * oil and water do n't mix * " ? Well , in the presence of eggs , they do . Also , egg yolks bring richness -- * aka fats ( which carry many flavors ) * -- and moistness to dishes . Basically , eggs are very versatile in cooking and baking and do a lot of things simultaneously .
