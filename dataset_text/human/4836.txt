In the US , the Democrats typically believe in more liberal policies . They tend to be pro - choice ( support abortion rights ) , support the legalization of same - sex marriage , support government benefits like Social Security and Medicare / Medicaid , and tend to lean toward larger government . Republicans typically believe in more conservative policies . They tend to be pro - life ( anti - abortion ) , oppose the legalization of same - sex marriage , mostly oppose what they call " government handouts " , and lean toward smaller government .
