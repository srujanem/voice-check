Microwaves heat water . When you put a steak in the microwave and hit the power button the water inside the steak starts warming , the water nearest the surface of the steak heating fastest . Before the centre of your steak is nice and warm though the edges are very very hot . So hot that the water the microwave is heating can boil turning to steam . The pops you hear are structures in the steak breaking as the steam expands inside them .
