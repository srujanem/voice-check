The Old Testament was written between 2000 and 3000 years ago in the Hebrew language . It is the part of the Bible that both Jews and Christians believe in . It mostly deals with the legendary history of the Jewish people ( talking about founding fathers such as Adam , Noah , Abraham , Moses , and Jacob ) . But it also contains hundreds of commandments . Among these are the famous ten commandments , but also such rules that you may not trim your beard and that you may not eat pork . The New Testament was written in the first and second centuries AD and is considered holy only by Christians . It is written in the Greek language . It describes the life of Jesus in four different versions , which are known as the four gospels . It also contains letters , called epistles , in which Paul and other founding fathers of Christianity explain their religious beliefs . There are some differences in the religious beliefs presented in the Old and New Testament . What they are exactly depends on whom you ask . But these are some obvious examples : - In the OT , there is only God . In the NT , Jesus is also considered a God - like figure because he is God 's son . - In the OT , God is a figure who frequently interferes with people 's lives and talks to them , even though he is invisible . In the NT , God is far away , and he sends Jesus and his disciples to talk to people . - In the OT , there is no life after death . In the NT , people are promised eternal life -- which means life after death . - In the OT , God makes it pretty clear that he only likes Jews -- they are the chosen people . In the NT , God likes anyone who believes in him , whether Jewish or not .
