Food for thought . URL_1 " Most conservative Bible scholars today take a different view , namely , that Luke is recording Mary ’s genealogy and Matthew is recording Joseph ’s . Matthew is following the line of Joseph ( Jesus ’ legal father ) , through David ’s son Solomon , while Luke is following the line of Mary ( Jesus ’ blood relative ) , though David ’s son Nathan . Since there was no Greek word for “ son - in - law , ” Joseph was called the “ son of Heli ” by marriage to Mary , Heli ’s daughter . Through either Mary ’s or Joseph ’s line , Jesus is a descendant of David and therefore eligible to be the Messiah . Tracing a genealogy through the mother ’s side is unusual , but so was the virgin birth . Luke ’s explanation is that Jesus was the son of Joseph , “ so it was thought ” ( Luke 3:23 ) .
