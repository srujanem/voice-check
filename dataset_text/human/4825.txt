If we start a company together you and I both own half of it . Now if I wanted to share my half with my brother I could split my part of the company into halfs again and give one to my brother . This is done with big companies many many times . Each part of the company is called a stock and big companies have many many many stocks . Now when people want to buy or sell their part of the company ( their stock or also called shares ) they do this on a market . That is the stock market . Every day people try to sell and buy parts of companies . If you buy stock in companies that do well , your little part of the company will become worth more ; but if that company looses all its money and has to go bankrupt and close , no one will give you much or any money for the stocks you have .
