Its actually rather simple . There are 2 main components . A Cathode ( - ) and an Anode ( + ) . There is a filament in the Cathode , that heats up ( much like the filament in a toaster ) and burns off electrons . Those electrons , which are negatively charged are attracted to the positive Anode , most commonly made of Tungsten . When those electrons hit the Tungsten plate , boom , X - Rays .
