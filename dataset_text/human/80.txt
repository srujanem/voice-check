Economy and Government & Five Year Plans: Sir M. Visvesvaraya published 'Planned Economy for India' in 1934, regarded as 'Father of Economic Planning in India'. Planning Commission was formed in 1950, executing 12 Five-Year Plans. The Green Revolution in 1967-70, led by Dr. M.S. Swaminathan ('Father of Indian Green Revolution'), introduced high-yielding variety seeds, fertilizers, and pre-harvest technology. NITI Aayog replaced Planning Commission on Jan 1, 2015.
