There 's actually a pretty tight relationship between lifespan , body size , and a bunch of other variables such as rate of metabolism ( how much energy you consume ) . Roughly speaking , the bigger you are , the longer you live . It 's not that surprising - small creatures can eat what they need and grow to maturity faster . A mouse has to eat a handful of peanuts to match its body weight . An elephant has to eat half a forest . So , bigger size = need to eat more = need to grow more in order to reproduce = grow more slowly = longer lifespan .
