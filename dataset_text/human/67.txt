Social Stratification & Untouchability: Social stratification classifies people into upper and lower classes based on income, caste, gender, or education. Untouchability is an inhuman practice called a 'stigma' on Hindu society by Mahatma Gandhi. Article 17 of the Constitution prohibits untouchability, enforced through the Untouchability (Offences) Act 1955, amended as Citizens' Rights Protection Act in 1976, and Regulation of 1989.
