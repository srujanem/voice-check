Many other species get cancer just like humans ; this includes ( but is nt limited to ) cats , dogs , horses , fish , birds and hamsters . The reason why it is so common is most likely due to a couple of things : One , humans live for a long time compared to most other animals . This means that our cells have to make LOTS of copies , which means that there is more chance for there to be a bad copy . Another reason is that humans expose themselves to a lot of bad things , that many animals in the wild do not . This can be radiation from your microwave , eating too much unhealthy food , smoking cigarettes , some special medicines or even the liquid we use to clean our dishes ! Cancer has probably been around for as long as there have been life with more than one cell ( a multicellular organism ) - some ancient Egyptians even had cancer !
