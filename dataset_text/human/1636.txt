Of those three , only murder and manslaughter are actual legal terms ( in the US at least ) . Generally speaking , murder is killing someone intentionally . Whether the murder is premeditated or not specifies the " degree " of the murder ( i.e. murder in the first degree vs second degree ) . Manslaughter is killing someone without intending to . It could be that the person intended to cause them serious harm , but not kill them , or if they killed them on accident . Again , this is in U.S. terms . Assassination is killing someone ( usually prominent ) by surprise attack . It 's not a legally defined term .
