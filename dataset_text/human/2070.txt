As per the policy of NCF-2005 and KCF-2007 not only minimum content and skill each child is supposed to learn is introduced in each lesson, activities and projects to stimulate additional learning are also suggested. Similarly at the end of each practical lesson projects for creating new activities are suggested to stimulate creativity among children.
