In a TV signal ( or at least the North American NTSC signal ) , there is what 's called horizontal sync and vertical sync , which occur as the picture gets to the edge of the screen ( it shoots from left to right to make a line , then goes down and does the next one left to right , etc . ) . Basically , this sync signal is just the voltage in the cable dropping below the level of a black picture as the beam moves back to left or top of the screen . There 's a special circuit in the TV which detects these sync signals and controls an oscillator so that it can move the electron gun in tandem with the signal that 's being broadcasted in case either is not exactly at the proper frequency .
