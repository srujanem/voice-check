A decibel is actually a * logarithmic * unit , meaning that each ten steps up the scale is actually a ten times increase in intensity . 20dB represents a relative intensity of 100 so if you added ten of those together it could reach 1000 intensity which is 30dB But really , there 's a bit more going on than that with stuff like sound waves cancelling eachother out , so it 'll probably end up somewhere between 20 and 30 decibels
