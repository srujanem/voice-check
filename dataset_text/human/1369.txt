The workers enjoy facilities of specialization, training, talent, experience and educational qualifications. These employees’ day to day transactions are recorded in written form. They get fixed wages, allowances, paid leave and pension facilities. In addition, they get medical facility too. They have specific hours of work. The workers in insurance companies, educational institutions, banks armed forces, private companies, government departments are identified as organised workers. Unorganised Workers : People who work in areas which are not governed by specific rules
