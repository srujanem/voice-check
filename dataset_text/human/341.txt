Extremists opposed the partition of Bengal in 1905 as 'Divide and Rule' policy. Protests started mounting throughout the country against the division of Bengal. Swadeshi movement called for a ban on western goods and encouraged Indian goods. In 1911 the British government withdrew the Declaration of Partition of Bengal.
