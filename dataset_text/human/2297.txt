The view in many companies is that customer support / tech support is a money pit . Call centers like these generate very little money , regardless of quality . This makes sense if you think about it . Call center quality is going to have very little effect on the purchasing decisions of the average consumer . So companies have a choice , outsource all call center services to another company or try to build a very expensive call center from scratch . Many companies choose to go the easy route and outsource their call centers entirely . For some companies , this just means hiring another company here in the same country to handle it . For more and more companies , India has the cheapest call centers for hire .
