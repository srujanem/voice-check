Your blood type is based on what proteins are present on the surface of your red blood cells . There are three types of proteins we 're interested in . We will call these proteins A , B and Rh . You can have all of these proteins , none of them , or any combination . The letter part of your blood type is determined by the A and B proteins . If you have both types , you are AB . If you only have A , you are A , only B is B. If you have neither A nor B , you are type O. O means " lacks both A and B. " The + or - on your blood type is based on the Rh protein . If you have the Rh protein , you are Rh positive , which gives you a + on your blood type . If you do not have the Rh protein , you are Rh negative , and get a - on your blood type . A - is similar to type O. It just means your blood does not contain this protein . Your immune system will attack anything it does n't recognize . That includes blood cells . As long as the blood cells in a transfusion do not have anything your body does not recognize , your body will accept it . If your body sees a protein it does not recognize , it will attack the new blood cells . For example , if you are B+ and you get any type of blood with the A protein in it , your body will attack the new blood . As long as the new blood does not contain A , it will work just fine . This means a B+ can accept B- , O+ , etc . , but not A- , AB+ , etc . This is why O- is called a " universal donor " . O- blood can be given to anybody . The immune system of a B+ person will look at the O- blood and say , " I do n't see anything here I do n't recognize " and leave it alone . This makes O- blood more in demand , because if someone in an emergency room needs a blood transfusion , and there 's no chance to find out the blood type , they have to use O- to be safe . This also makes it sort of nice to be AB+ , because an AB+ can receive blood from any donor .
