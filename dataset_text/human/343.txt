The role of Revolutionaries in freedom struggle: The revolutionaries dreamt of a totally free India, believing British could be thrown out only by violent means. 'Anusheelana Samithi' and 'Abhinava Bharata' were important organizations. V.D.Savarkar, Aurobindo Ghosh, Shyam KrishnaVerma, Madame Cama, Khudiram Bose, Ramaprasad Bismil, Bhagat Singh and Chandrashekar Azad were prominent revolutionary leaders.

Collective Behaviour & Mobs: Collective behaviour refers to spontaneous, unstructured group actions. Mobs are temporary assemblies responding to provocation. Uncontrolled mob displays escalate into violent riots causing loss of life and property.
