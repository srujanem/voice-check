India Water Resources: Irrigation is the artificial supply of water to agriculture from canals, wells, and tanks. Well irrigation is most common (59.7% tube wells), followed by Canal irrigation (flood and perennial canals) and Tank irrigation. Multi-purpose river valley projects include Damodar River Valley Project ('Sorrow of Bengal'), Bhakra Nangal Project (Govindasagar reservoir), Hirakud Project ('Sorrow of Odisha'), and Tungabhadra Project (Pampasagara).
