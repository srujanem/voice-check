There are valid reasons not to vaccinate certain individuals , they may be immuno - compromized or allergic to one of the ingredients in the vaccine . These individuals rely on other people to get vaccinated so that they are protected from the disease through [ herd immunity ] ( URL_0 ) . Individuals who are against vaccinations for reasons other than medically valid reasons are called anti - vaccers . These individuals put their children at risk because they are more likely to get a preventable disease , and spread it to other unvaccinated children who have valid reasons for not getting vaccinated . One of the bigger arguments that anti - vaccers like to make is that vaccines cause autism . Only there is no research that links incidence of [ vaccination to autism ] ( URL_2 ) .
