Northern Mountains: The Himalayas extend 2500 km from Pamir Knot to Arunachal Pradesh, divided into three main ranges: 1. Siwalik Range (foothills with narrow valleys called Duns like Dehradun), 2. Himachal (Middle Himalayas with hill stations like Shimla, Mussorie, Nainital), and 3. Greater Himalayas (Himadri, covered in snow with Mount Everest at 8848 m and origin of Ganga at Gangotri). K2 (8611 m) lies in trans-Himalayas.
