I 'll try and make this really simple . A citizen 's arrest is when a regular person ( who is n't a police officer of any kind ) arrests someone who has committed a crime . In the US the rules vary slightly from State to State , but in general anyone can arrest a person they see commit a felony . They then have to hand them over to the police promptly . The difference between this and a police arresting you is that the police can arrest you for crimes someone else saw and then tells the police about , where a citizen can only arrest you for something they see happen . It works the same way a regular arrest would , it could be the citizen simply saying , your under arrest and then waiting for the cops to come , up to full on handcuffing and the whole 9 yards .
