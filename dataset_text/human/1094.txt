The British thought of exploiting this situation in their favour. A long war was waged from 1775 to 1782 between the Marathas and the British. Initially, though the Marathas gained upper hand, finally they lost it. The Maratha federation was unable to sustain the war with the British and finally entered into ‘Salbai Agreement’
