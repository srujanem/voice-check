Computers use standardized protocols to communicate , regardless of language or location of the user . The protocols use a predetermined order and positioning of bits that are universally predetermined , so every computer that knows the protocols are on the same page . Think of this as the ' To ' and ' From ' areas on a letter . The actual contents of the letter do n't matter to the post office . Its up to the programmer to code the client ( user software ) and server to ensure that the letter contents are understood by both parties .
