There 's a lot of different ways to alter difficulty in a game like that , it really just depends on how the AI is constructed . For example , every player has different stats ( some good at shooting three - pointers , some good at long passes , etc . ) . On the easy setting maybe each AI player is not aware of the other players stats and it chooses to pass the ball randomly instead of considering their teammates proficiencies . On a higher difficulty each AI player might have full access to the other AI players ' stats and so it will only pass the ball to the person who can shoot the best from that distance . Additionally the game could just skew the AI stats based on the difficulty . Maybe on easy it drops everyone 's ability to shoot / pass / whatever by an amount , on medium it uses their actual stats , and on hard it increases their stats by an amount so everyone on the team can shoot / pass / whatever really well . In a lot of AI structures the main way to alter behavior like this is to add randomness . You normally have them make a decision based on the data present ( i.e. pass to the guy with the best shooting ability ) but then you set a threshold for randomness , so say 5 % of the time instead of passing to the best player you pass to a random player . To make the game more easy / difficult you could alter the chance of them taking random actions instead of optimal actions . There are a lot of different AI structures that could be used for decision making for things like sports games , but each of them can be skewed to make certain decisions by altering the parameters in the game .
