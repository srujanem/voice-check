The " paradigms " are similar , but since the cloud ( or butt ) is AFAIK typically _ virtualized _ it allows a more flexible strategy for managing the back end . You 'll find that " the cloud " consists of a group of managing applications for virtualized systems , be they filesystems or entire " machines " . In addition , the desktop " paradigm " has changed the public 's attitude towards computers , they are now running the operating systems themselves on their own hardware . The new applications or apps are accessed through e.g. the browser . AFAIK the mainframe provided the home area ( /home/ in UNIX ) if not the entire operating system .
