Keep in mind that watching a movie is an exercise in suspension of disbelief . The entire experience - framing , lighting , acting , sound , effects - is supposed to make you feel you 're watching something real . On the other hand , * making * a movie is pretty much the opposite . You 're surrounded by crew members . Someone is continually touching up your makeup . You have to stop every 5 minutes in order to adjust the lighting . There are no special effects added yet . There is no spooky soundtrack playing . It is obviously fake . If there are things on the set that are particularly scary , or adult ( like nudity ) it will be shot in such a way that the child is n't actually seeing what happens , but is edited in later . So the child is n't exposed to the things that someone watching the movie will see .
