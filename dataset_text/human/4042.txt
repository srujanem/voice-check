My father and I both grind our teeth to a serious degree ( I 've worn my canines flat and cracked a couple of teeth clenching them in the night ) . Dentists have told us that there is a genetic component involved , and that is often commences in response to stress . Unfortunately , even if the stressful situation passes , or is resolved , it is really difficult to stop the habit of grinding . One dentist even described to me a paper he 'd seen presented at a conference which showed how the grinding behaviour is mapped in your brain , and that the neural pathways it uses are very difficult to disrupt or overlay ( apologies for my lack of correct medical terminology , I 'm not in the field and just trying to remember how it was explained to me ) - basically he concluded it would be very unlikely for a ' chronic bruxer ' such as myself , to ever successfully break the habit . Splints for the win !
