It varies from state to state , but for killing a dog , yes it is pretty much legal as long as its done in a humane way with as little possible pain . As for eating dog , it is actually legal in 44 states but the sale of dog meat for human consumption is pretty much illegal for the majority of states . It really comes down to the specific state laws though . Some , like Virginia , are very strict and disallow the unnecessary killing of any animal not expressly used for " farm activities " . Some states , like Pennsylvania , it is actually legal to both butcher and sell dog or cat meat . ( They were actually working on passing a law to ban it , not sure if it went through ) . Check state animal cruelty laws . Those will usually encompass both the legality of killing the dog and the consumption of the dog . Edit : spelling and clarification
