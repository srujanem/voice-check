this : Home Rule Ireland was under the control of Britain. The Irish people began the ‘Home Rule Movement’ in order to obtain the right to carry on the administration of their country themselves. Influenced by this, Annie Besant started the movement in India too. In 1916, two Home Rule Leagues came into existence. One began under the leadership of Tilak and had Pune as its centre. It had its branches in Maharashtra, North Karnataka, Central provinces and Berar areas.
