So you have this clock with a photon bouncing back and forth SUPER fast , so normally if you move the whole clock , nothing happens . At human - comprehensible speeds , there 's no significant effect . But picture the clock moving horizontally at INCREDIBLE speeds . When the photon bounces off one mirror , it ca n't go straight back up , it needs to travel at an angle to hit the top mirror ( since the mirror has moved during the photon 's travel time ) .
