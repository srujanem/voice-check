Its the connection point from the axel assembly to the tire / wheel itself which is part of the tire pressure handling system , it can fill / maintain tire inflation pressure , but can also lower and raise tire pressure in the fly for increased traction ( lowered pressure ) or for high speed travelling ( filling to recommended pressure ) . Should a tire incur a leak , lets say a reasonable sized leak it will also keep / try to keep this tire inflated until the vehicle and its occupants find an appropriate spot safe and secure enough to do a tire change .
