It has to do with muscle relaxation and neurotransmitters during the sleep cycle . In certain stages of sleep , your brain tells all the * * skeletal * * muscles in your body to relax , and become " un - tense " . ( Skeletal muscles are the ones you control ) . Depending on which stage you might wake up in , you could have difficulty clenching your fists ( or balancing yourself if you stand up as soon as you wake ) .
