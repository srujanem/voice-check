Servers are computers all over the world that your computer can talk to . They are set up to respond anytime your computer asks them for something . Different servers are designed to respond to different kinds of requests . For instance , your computer can ask a server to : * send you a file , like an image or a song * search for cake recipes and send you a list of them * store a video for you , so your friends can see it on their computers * send a message to your friend
