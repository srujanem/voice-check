It 's different definitions of GB . Hard drive manufacturers say that a gigabyte is 1000x1000x1000 bytes , while Windows says that it 's 1024x1023x1024 bytes . 750 GB by the first definition is about 700 GB by the second , so that 's what Windows shows . We 're now supposed to use gigabyte and GB only to mean the first definition , and call the second a gibibyte ( GiB ) . Old habits die hard though , since we 'd been using kilobyte to mean 1024 bytes for decades .
