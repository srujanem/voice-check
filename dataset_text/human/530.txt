Cars are worthless without the infrastructure to support them . If there are no hydrogen fuel stations , no one is going to buy one of Toyota 's fuel cell cars , no matter how cool and green they may be . So by making it easier for more companies to build fuel cell cars , Toyota encourages more hydrogen stations to open up which will lead to them selling more of these cars too . Likewise , Tesla will benefit if more people build superchargers that work with their vehicles ( something that 's only possible if you can use Tesla 's patents ) . Also , at this point , I 'm pretty sure Elon Musk is building technology because he thinks it 's a good idea and not because he 's trying to make money .
