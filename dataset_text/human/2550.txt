For some people it is a self - loathing / self - punishment thing . They hate themselves so much that they feel they need to be punished . For some people it is a compulsion . Skin picking can fall in the self - harm category , as can hair plucking . These people do n't necessarily need to hate themselves , but might have other compulsions like OCD that make them feel like they need to hurt themselves . Additionally , self harm can ... feel good . This one is hard to explain , but it mostly has to do with the fact that physical pain is physical . It is something that has an easy form . Emotional pain on the other hand is really vague and can be really overwhelming . Some people use hurting themselves as a way to focus out of the emotional pain because it is easier to focus on a cut than on something that is inside of your skull .
