ISIS = Islamic State in Iraq and Syria and ISIL = Islamic State in Iraq and the Levant . ISIL is actually a more accurate translation of the group 's original name , which refers to Iraq and " al - Sham " , or the Levant . But now the group has actually abandoned the geographic restriction and now simply calls itself the Islamic State ( IS ) , so the media and the administration are currently using the names for political and branding reasons . The US is happy to continue to intervene in Iraq , but does n't want to get involved on the ground in Syria , so they 've chosen the name that emphasizes Iraq and downplays Syria . The media , as far as I can tell , prefers ISIS either because they like the sound , because it was the first name they committed to , or because they know Americans are even more clueless about the word " Levant " than they are about Syria .
