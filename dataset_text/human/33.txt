Pitts India Act 1784 was implemented to outline the powers of the British East India Company and the British Government. Another important aspect of this Act was the establishment of the 'Board of Controllers' consisting of six Commissioners. The 'Board of Controllers' replaced the 'Board of Directors'. Later, the Charter Act of 1813 licensed the British East Company to stay for another twenty years in India. This act authorized all interested persons to carry out trade in India, starting the free trade era in India.
