I think it 's also partly a cultural thing . I 've heard that in some cultures cash is a perfectly acceptable gift . In cultures where it is not , till the advent of gift cards , you had to buy somebody a gift . And also , it probably was n't all too easy to figure out the cost of the gift unless it was something very popular . A gift of cash in these cultures seem like the gifter is lazy and/or the amount may seem on the lower side . Now , with gift cards , in these very same cultures , you have a paradox . It 's kinda like a gift but it really is currency limited to a store . That being said , when lower denominations are involved , a gift card seems a bit more special than hard cold cash . For instance , a $ 20 Starbucks card seems ( even to me ) more special than a $ 20 bill , when both can be used for the exact same purpose .
