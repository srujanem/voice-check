From a technical perspective , lines , whether copper or fiber can only transfer so much data at any given time . Putting aside latency etc and looking at it from a purely mathematical perspective . If you have a 100Mb line and want to offer internet to 10 customers , they can each only get 10Mb if all using it at the exact same time ... So they offer cheaper subscription to the person who only expects to use 5Mb ... which allows them to offer 15Mb to someone else .
