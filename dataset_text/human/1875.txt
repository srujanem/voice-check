China is an Oligarchy , with this group of people belonging to the Politburo Standing Committee of the Communist Party of China more or less controlling the entire country . It 's a 4 - 9 man ( currently 7 ) committee that holds the most important positions in the country . They get chosen during each National Congress of the Communist Party of China ( held once every 5 years ) and they are selected to be in the committee not because of their ability , but because they belong to the right family and have sucked up to the right people who then tell everyone in the party to vote for them . And also because of seniority .
