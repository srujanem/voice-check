Computer programs that people use to edit video and photos have an easier time taking out a solid color than a random pattern . So , some photos and videos are taken in front of a green screen to make it easier to remove the background from the photo or video . After the background is removed , a computer program can then take a * different * background and put it behind the people in the photo / video to make it look like they 're in front of that instead . ---- Note that green is n't the only color that can be used . Any color can be used for this technique ( * chroma - key * -- * chroma * for color , and * key * because it 's one color that can be taken out ) , but whatever color is used should * * not * * be anywhere else in the photo / video because it 'll get cut out like the background . If the people will be wearing green , blue screens are sometimes used . Pure black or pure white are also commonly used .
