There is no way to build up resistance to being knocked out . What boxers do is train to " roll with the punches " . Let 's say , they know a right hook is about to land on their jaw and they ca n't avoid it , they 'll " roll with it " , and by turning their head that way anyway , it 'll slightly absorb some of the shock . As an analogy , picture that I am sat at a red light and I see your car approaching me at 40 mph and I do nothing , I take the impact of the full 40 mph collision . If in another scenario I was able to start driving forward at 20 mph ( assuming I could safely do that ) then I 'd only receive the impact of a 20 mph collision .
