Rowlatt Act 1919 gave judges power to arrest and interrogate people based on mere suspicion. Gandhiji protested against this Act through satyagraha. Thousands of Indians participated in these protests.

Child Marriage Prohibition Act 2006: Child marriage (boy < 21, girl < 18) is a crime punishable with 2 years imprisonment and Rs 1 lakh fine. Toll-free helpline 1098 and 47 designated officers in Karnataka enforce protection.
