The police and military do n't call them " silencers " , but rather " suppressors " . One of the big things they do is eliminate the muzzle flash . In any kind of tactical situation this makes it harder to figure out where the shot came from . They 're therefore essential for snipers . Also , even though they certainly do n't totally silence a gun , they do reduce the noise pretty substantially , which helps tremendously for hearing . An indoor shootout with no suppressors is going to leave everyone temporarily deaf and with permanent hearing damage . With suppressors you 're fare a lot better .
