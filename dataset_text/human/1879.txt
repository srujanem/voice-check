When inflation occurs , prices usually rise and then wages are raised as a reaction to rising prices , so the consumer always gets a little bit screwed as there is a period of time where things are expensive and we 're not making more . Inflation really gets its bad name from stagflation , which is when inflation occurs and the economy stagnates , which results in higher prices and no change or cut wages .
