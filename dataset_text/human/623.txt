Mohammed Ali Jinnah & Partition: Jinnah joined Congress in 1906, defended Tilak, and led Home Rule League. Later he led the Muslim League, demanding Pakistan at the 1940 Lahore Session. Mountbatten Plan led to the Indian Independence Act of 1947, partitioning India and Pakistan on August 15, 1947.

Panchayat Raj Institutions: 73rd Constitutional Amendment 1993 established uniform 3-tier Panchayats: Grama Panchayat, Taluk Panchayat, Zilla Panchayat. Karnataka provides 50% reservation for women in PRIs.
