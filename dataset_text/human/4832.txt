Living organisms evolved to eat food . " Food " is really other living organisms . The more you process food , more you lose of the bits that make it food . Vitamins , minerals , fiber , trace elements and other little things we call " micro - nutrients . " Your tummy does n't know how to make that stuff help the rest of your body . Fast food goes through a lot of processing by people and machines in big factories so a lot of the foodishness is lost . Then other non - food stuff is added to make it last longer and tast better . If you want to eat healthy , look at the ingredients . Do n't eat stuff with more that 5 or so ingredients and do n't eat stuff you ca n't pronounce .
