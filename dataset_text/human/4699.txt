People leveraged debt , and when it was time to pay off the debt , money did n't exist . For example : you go and buy a car . The bank loans you money , and you slowly pay off that car . That money does n't exist , but it will when you finish paying off the car . The bank is now able to say " look at all this money we ( will ) have ! " So their balance sheets read like they have that money already . They do this with a few dozen people , and then they have enough money on their balance sheets to loan to someone buying a house . Someone buys a house , and promises to pay that money back , so now the bank has allll that money on their balance sheets . They can now loan to hundreds of people . Once hundreds of people buy houses , and promise to pay them back , they now have billions of dollars on their balance sheets . Then fuzzy stuff starts to happen . None of that money exists , yet . So people constantly are willing to pay more and more for houses , and the bank keeps giving larger and larger loans . The value of homes go higher , so loan officers are like " do n't worry about paying this back , just pay for a few years , then sell it when houses are worth more " . This works for a while but eventually , someone says " ummm I ca n't pay this back and nobody is willing to buy this house from me . " And then the entire house of cards falls apart , because none of that money anywhere existed . And the banks , which had nothing to start off with , suddenly find themselves utterly worthless ... to the point where they are extremely negative with cash . This means they no longer have the money that people deposited in them . A bank going out of business has huge consequences which would take another half hour to write up
