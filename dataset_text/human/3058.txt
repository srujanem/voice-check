Pipes leak . They always do . A very long pipeline will leak on a lot of currently pretty pristine land . A lot of the intervening land the pipe must cross are native reserves and many people accuse the oil companies of coercion or bribes to get permission for some of the land , and that it 's really not in the best interests of the people that live there who are already having enough trouble with access to fresh , clean water and the like . It 'd fan the flames of the operations on the Alberta tar sands , which are controversial in their own right . Getting oil from the tar sands is obscenely bad for the environment . Continued reliance on oil is also per se a controversial issue . Many people would rather not make oil reliance easier to encourage development in other directions .
