Amps are a measure of current . If you think of a river , it would be how fast the river is flowing . Volts are a measure of charge potential . If you think of a river , it would be how high the hill is at the top of the river . Watts are a measure of power . It 's how hard the river smacks you the face when you stand in it 's way ( ok , the analogy is really starting to break down ) . Mathematically , Watts = Amps * Volts .
