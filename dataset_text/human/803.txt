Hurdles Race Rules: Track sprint variation where runners clear 10 hurdles in lane. Start: crouch start with 8 strides to first hurdle. Clearance: jump at minimum height, trailing leg bent perpendicular. 3 strides between hurdles.

Eruption of the Revolt and its spread: Most of the Indian soldiers in the British army believed that if they fought together, they could drive away the British from India. At that time, the soldiers were being given new rifles called 'Royal Enfield'.
