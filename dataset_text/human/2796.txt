Student loans are a speculative investment that no rational lender would ever undertake without collateral and co - signers . In other words , your parents should have to mortgage their house to afford your college tuition . The government has decided that it wants more college graduates than there are people who can afford to attend , or who could borrow the money to attend . To close that gap , the government engages in lending to people who otherwise would never be able to qualify for those loans . In return the government is gambling that in fact those people will manage to pay off that debt . They are gambling that the value of the education received will enable those borrowers to increase their lifetime earnings by enough to make the necessary repayments . Because there 's so little scrutiny about how much is borrowed and for what purpose , the college system has been able to raise tuitions with the knowledge that the government will continue to make loans available to pay for the tuition . So the risk the government has to take on each individual student has increased over time . Today things appear to be approaching an equilibrium where the difference in lifetime earnings and the cost of the debt + interest is approaching zero . When we pass that threshold , and the cost of the debt + interest becomes demonstrably more than the increase in lifetime earnings , there will be a revolution . A whole lot of people are going to default on their student loans . Then we 'll see what happens next .
