Power Resources: Power resources are essential for the economic development of the country. Conventional power resources are those which can get exhausted. Ex: coal, petroleum, atomic power etc. Non-conventional power sources are those which are not exhaustible. Most important of them are solar power, wind power and hydro-electric power.
