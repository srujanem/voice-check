The area of black soil is also called Deccan Trap. The soil i s formed from the weathering of igneous rock. It contains more clay particles. It is fertile and capable of retaining water. This type of soil is seen in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Western areas of Andhra Pradesh
