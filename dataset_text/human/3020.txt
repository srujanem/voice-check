It depends on which cat allergen you react to . Most likely you are allergic to a protein that cats have in their saliva . As cats lick themselves as they bathe , the protein is trapped in their fur . Cats constantly shed and then the protein is distributed off their bodies and onto furniture and whatever else . Hypoallergenic cats produce low levels of these proteins and commonly shed less or do not have fur at all . So to answer the question , if you have the most common type of cat allergy and you did n't eat anything that came in contact with it 's saliva , you 'll probably be fine .
