They started digging for it because it was an incredibly cheap and efficient way of lighting and heating places , before oil came into use the only way to heat homes and buildings was through fireplaces and if you were well off through whale oil heaters , and to have artificial light was from candles(not very luminescent ) or for the incredibly well off whale oil lanterns . Oil made bright artificial lights available to the common and poor folk . Before it was widely mined for it was used as medicine and had some other applications similar to other tars .
