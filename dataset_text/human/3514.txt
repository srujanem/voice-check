Reddit admins can inflict a " shadowban " against suspected spammers or brigaders . The details are murky , but basically it 's an invisible ban from participating on Reddit . Your profile is deleted to everybody except yourself , and anything you post is marked as spam and must be specifically allowed by subreddit moderators for others to see .
