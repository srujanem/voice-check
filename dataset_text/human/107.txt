Ranjit Singh was born in 1780. Shah Zaman of Kabul made him governor of Lahore with the title 'Raja'. He organized his army on European lines, incorporating Gorkhas, Biharis, Pathans, Sikhs and Muslims. He established a Foundry at Lahore to manufacture Canons. He entered a Treaty of Friendship with the British in 1809 at Amritsar and ruled as an independent king until 1839.
