There are dominant and recessive genes ( example : R for dominant , r for recessive ) . Each person has two copies of a gene , one from each parent . If a gene is dominant , even if there is a recessive gene , the dominant gene is the one that determines physical traits ( example : RR has the same physical traits as Rr ) . If a gene is recessive , you need two copies of the recessive gene , because Rr results in the dominant trait instead ( example : rr for recessive trait ) . Red hair is recessive , so it requires either two parents with red hair or two parents with genes for red hair ( example : Rr paired with Rr , which has a chance to produce rr ) . If his parents do not have red hair but he does , it means that his parents both had Rr as their genotype .
