Eye movement is controlled by the brain but , almost always , the system that has the eyes converge to see the same object of interest is not under conscious control . The brain essentially automates it . It is possible to learn to diverge your eyes , for example , some people can learn to view stereo pairs and see depth effects without any special optics . There are concerns that watching artificial stereoscopic images ( in 3D movies , VR headsets , etc . ) could cause problems with our automated convergence system because some of the cues are conflicting . For example , in 3D movies the focus distance stays the same but the convergence can vary , something impossible in a real - world environment .
