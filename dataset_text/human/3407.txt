Both are radio waves , the difference is in how information is carried . FM stands for Frequency Modulation , and AM for Amplitude Modulation . Frequency modulation means that the wavelength of the signal changes to carry information . Amplitude Modulation means that the relative strength of the signal changes to carry information .
