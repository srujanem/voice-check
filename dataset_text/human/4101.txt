URL_0 > Ecological functions . Maggots are important as decomposers , helping to break down decaying tissues and retaining the nutrients , rather than being lost . The flesh of dead animals are quickly reduced by maggots . Furthermore , maggots are important in food chains , being consumed by a wide variety of invertebrates and vertebrates . The hover fly ( Syrphus ribesii ) , which is an important pollinator in the adult stage , also has a helpful larval stage , as the maggots are active predators of aphids and other plant - sucking insects and thus are natural enemies of plant pests ( Kendall 2007 ) .
