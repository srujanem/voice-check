T ORQUE ON CURRENT LOOP, MAGNETIC DIPOLE 4.9.1 Torque on a rectangular current loop in a uniform magnetic field We now show that a rectangular loop carrying a steady current I and placed in a uniform magnetic field experiences a torque. It does not experience a net force. This behaviour is analogous to that of electric dipole in a uniform electric field (Section 1.11).
