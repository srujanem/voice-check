At least in New Zealand 40 % means that it 's 1 standard a shot . In a lot of places vodka for example must be at least 40 % , and others 40 % is the max , so these are legal reasons . Finally , a lot of spirits are distilled to percentages which are much higher than 40 % so things like casking are cheaper , so it 's diluted for sale and 40 % is generally the most palatable .
