A claim on land is only as valid as your ability to enforce it . If I buy some land here and someone start erecting a structure on it I can , through the governments institutions made available to me , force them off - violently if need be . Nobody , not even and entire nation , not even the whole human race collectively , could be said to have sovereign power over any of the moon or Mars . In addition to that : no reputable organization is selling land on extra - terrestrial bodies , so it 's not even like you could buy land through the American government in the trust that they will later be able to gain sovereign power of the moon or whatever . You ca n't even buy the reasonable assumption you will later have a meaningful claim to the land .
