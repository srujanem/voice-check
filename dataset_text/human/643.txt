The cells that are sloughing off are different to the cells which pigment the skin . The cells which pigment the skin ( melanocytes ) are in the epidermis directly attached to the dermis . They never really move and hardly ever make new ones . This is why scars do n't have pigment . The skin cells people talk about falling off ( keratinocytes ) are constantly produced by other keratinocytes which are also attached to the dermis .
