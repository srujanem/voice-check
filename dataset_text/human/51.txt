Rebellion of Bedas of Halagali: Halagali is a small village of Mudhol taluk of Bagalkote district. In 1857, the British banned the usage of weapons under the Arms Act. The Bedas who always kept guns as part of their customs were good hunters. They rebelled against the British when asked to surrender their firearms. The Bedas of Manturu, Boodni, Alagundi and neighbouring villages joined Halagali Bedas. The British army entered Halagali village and suppressed the Bedas in an inhuman way, hanging the rebels to death.
