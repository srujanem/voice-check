If you have a pot of boiling water on the stove , and turn off the heating element ( our analogue for the shortest day of the year ) , the water does not immediately cool down . It takes time for it to drop from 212 degrees to room temperature . If , a minute later , the water is 150 degrees , and you turn the heat back up to 90 degrees ( our analogue for the day after the shortest day of the year ) , then even though the water is receiving energy again , it will continue to cool , since it is higher than the input .
