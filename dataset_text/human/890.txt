The sun does warm up winter air . Thanks to Earth 's close proximity to the sun ( and its greenhouse - effect atmosphere ) most temperate zones in winter sit around a balmy zero degrees centigrade . Mars , for instance , which has no such luxuries sits usually around sixty below . During winter , temperate zones are exposed to less direct exposure to the sun , which makes it colder . The sun is still acting on the planet , though , else it would be much , much colder .
