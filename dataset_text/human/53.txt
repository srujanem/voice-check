Young Bengal Movement: Started in Calcutta during the 1820s and 1830s by Henry Louis Vivian Derozio (1809-1831), an Anglo-Indian professor at Hindu College. Derozio started the 'Academic Association' in 1828, a free thinking debate association. The young Bengal movement held discussions on nature, humanism, and God. Derozio worked towards spreading the message that rational thinking would liberate people from superstitions and social discrimination. He advocated Women Rights and opposed caste discrimination.
