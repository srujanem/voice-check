Entrepreneurship: Derived from French 'Entreprende' (to undertake). Entrepreneur is an innovator and risk-taker driving capital formation, GDP growth, and employment. District Industrial Centres (DICs 1978) provide single-window support.

National Integration via Sports: Physical education brings together students of diverse backgrounds, languages, castes, and religions into unified teams. Sports foster mutual respect, sportsmanship, and national integration through shared goals and rule-based fair play.
