Small cats ( like domestic house cats ) have a million natural predators . Any coyote around here would love to have your kitty ( or dog for that matter ) for lunch . Even still , house cats will display very dominant traits based on their environment and what they 're hunting . A cat hunting a bird , a mouse , or other rodent has nothing to fear from their prey . But if you introduce a larger predator , like a human , then cats ( just like any other animal ) rely on their survival instincts around unfamiliar humans or large animals . Survival instincts can be displayed in the form of " skittishness . "
