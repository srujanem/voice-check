Before the implementation of the act, there were three presidencies under British rule. They are: Bengal, Madras and Bombay. All these three were independent administrative units. Under the Regulating Act of 1773, the Bengal Presidency gained control over the other two presidencies. The Governor of Bengal became the Governor General of all the three presidencies. The Governor General was authorized to direct, exercise control and to supervise over the other two presidencies. According to this Act, the Supreme Court was established in Calcutta.
