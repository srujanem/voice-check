In order to use torrents you must have a torrent client . I recommend [ Deluge ] ( URL_0 ) . It is generally safe if you download torrents with a high seeder ratio ( the green number ) , but always be sure to scan it first . I recommend * never * downloading a torrent if it has 20 > seeders . Some useful terminology : seeder : someone who distributes the torrent and has all of it . Once you finish downloading a torrent , you become a seeder . leacher : someone who is downloading the torrent . You may download whatever that person has downloaded from seeds , and vice versa . s / l ratio : how many seeders to leachers . you generally want a more seeders than leachers .
