When you short , you do n't sell stock that you own , you sell stock that you 've borrowed from someone else . Imagine the price of apples is 50 cents . and falling . Imagine Bob and Alice and I are apple dealers . The price of borrowing an apple for a day is 2c . I borrow an apple from Bob for a day for 2c . I sell ( short sell ) this apple for 50c the same day . Next day , the price of apples is 45c . I buy a new apple from Alice for 45c , and give it back to Bob to replace the one I borrowed yesterday . The price of apples has fallen , and I 've made 3 cent .
