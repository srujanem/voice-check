If it was a 12 sided die , yes that would make sense . But because you have to ADD two numbers together , the odds are entirely different . First off , it 's impossible to roll a 1 - so even by the 1 in 12 rule , it 's no longer a fit ( it 'd have to be 1 in 11 - but even that 's wrong ) . You have to remember that there 's more than one way to create 6 , for instance . 1 + 5 , 2 + 4 , 3 + 3 , 3 + 3 , 4 + 2 , 5 + 1 . That 's SIX ways that you can make 6 . If you take all the possible outcomes of rolling two dice , you 'll end up with 36 different possibilities ( 1 + 1 , 1 + 2 , ... 1 + 6 , 2 + 1 , 2 + 2 , ... 2 + 6 , ... 6 + 6 ) . So you have to divide the * number of ways * you can come up with an outcome by the total number of possible outcomes . In this case , the chance of getting a 6 is 6 out of 36 .
