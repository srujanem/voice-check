1 . What 's currently being done about climate change ? Not enough . 2 . How can you help ? Small help : recycle , carpool , buy less stuff , take the train instead of flying . Medium help : become a vegan ( vegetarian is almost as good ) , get rid of your car entirely , put solar panels or a windmill on your house , spend time planting trees and flowers and community vegetable gardens . Big help : join the activists blocking pipeline construction , get some friends and go live in trees in a forest that 's about to be clearcut to stop them from cutting it , figure out a way to get 99 % of our state and federal Congresspeople out of office and replaced with non - corrupt people whose heads are n't up their asses . 3 . When will the effects become deadly ? They probably already are . Desertification in Africa is quickly destroying lots of what used to be productive farm land , which is causing higher food prices that have caused starvation and deadly food riots and will most likely cause famines , resulting in starvation . It 's also increasing competition between farming communities and herding communities who have to make do with less and less land , which has historically and is today causing ethnic strife . Many deadly natural disasters would have been less severe or never happened at all if not for climate change , like Hurricane Sandy . The predicted effects are grim . Ten countries in Northeast Africa are dependent on the Nile River , and if it starts to run dry , there will undoubtedly be famine as well as conflict and warfare over scarce water resources . Pacific Island nations are threatened with being inundated and many refugees may have no place to go .
