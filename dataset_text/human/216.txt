Gandhiji made use of indirect protests, non-violence and satyagraha as the main instruments for freedom struggle, writing about his principles in the newspaper 'Young India'. Congress established 'Tilak Swarajya Fund' with an investment of one crore rupees.

Child Labour & Laws: Article 24 of Constitution prohibits child labour below 14 in hazardous units. Child Labour Prohibition and Control Act 1986, NCLP 1988, Sarvashikshana Abhiyan, and RTE Act 2009 protect child rights.
