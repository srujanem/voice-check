Expansion and irritation of your stomach leads to the activation of your enteric nervous system , which is a network of nerves running all throughout your GI tract . This activation leads to peristalsis and movement of the colon to make space for the new " to be digested " food . It takes hours to make stool from your food , it never runs right through you .
