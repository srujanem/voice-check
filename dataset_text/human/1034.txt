One difference between Western animation and Japanese anime is the attitude towards filler . In anime , since the show is usually based on a manga , it 's considered all right for some episodes to not have very much to do with the story . In Dragon Ball Z , for instance , the manga and anime ran at the same time , and when the anime got to the end of the latest manga , material had to be made up . This is what brought us things like Goku and Piccolo racing cars , or episode - long screaming matches that included flashbacks ( exaggeration ) . This kind of thing can be considered a bonus for folks who already read the manga . In the West , filler is considered as just a cost - saving strategy . Western shows are more commonly the establishers of their story , so there 's less of a reason for side material . This means a Western show will usually have fewer episodes per season . That 's definitely not the only reason ( Nickelodeon really screwed over Korra in its later half , among other reasons ) , but it is something to consider .
