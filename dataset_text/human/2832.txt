A lot of microwaveable food comes with some sort of metallic heating shroud in it to help cook better . Then danger is when you put something in that has the right shape and topography to be able to arc . If you put a spoon in the microwave , your microwave will be ok , and the spoon will heat . If you put a fork in , you will get arc between the tines . Those sparks arcing around are what can be harmful to the microwave .
