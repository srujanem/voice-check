The most simple answer to this question is " the Supreme Court of the United States said so . " The more complicated answer is that while a knock and announce is part of the common law , this requirement can be done away with when there 's good reason to . Those reasons generally fall under the category of safety , preventing the destruction of evidence , and preventing escape . Though this goes back to " the supreme court said so . " It can , of course , be done away with by statute , but the answer to that is that the various legislatures have not seen fit to pass a law to make it illegal .
