The cost of space exploration is probably a lot higher than that of ocean exploration for the same " relative " result , hence the increased spending . I seem to recall reading that with NASA 's space shuttle , it cost on average around $ 10k to put 1 kg of payload into orbit . Not to mention the cost of failure is higher ( you destroy your launch vehicle , possibly kill your trained crew , or crash into another object ) . Deep sea exploration requires a large investment too , but probably not as much . A lot of the deep sea exploration crafts are unmanned and it would still be cheaper than $ 10k a kilogram to put things at the bottom of the ocean . To a lesser extent there 's probably more hype ( space is cool ) and known natural resources ( asteroids are extremely plentiful in materials that are very rare on earth ) , whereas it 's unclear what the benefit of deep sea exploration is , other than discovering new species of life .
