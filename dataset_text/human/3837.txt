You might find this interesting . The Latin word for ' right ' is ' dexter . ' That 's where the word ' ambidextrous ' comes from - it basically translates to ' having two right hands . ' If you could write equally well with both hands , it was like having two right hands . The Latin word for ' left ' ? ' Sinister . ' So I think you can see the association that arose that relates to OP 's question . You might ask if there 's such a word as ' ambisinister , ' as a counter to ' ambidextrous . ' There is ! It means to be clumsy and unskilled with both hands . It 's interesting how the connotation of each hand ( righty = good , lefty = bad ) has led to those words and meanings .
