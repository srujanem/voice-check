There 's a difference between what most consumers would consider " fresh " and the legal constraints within which orange juice manufacturers get to call their orange juice " fresh . " The process of extracting and preserving orange juice ( primarily to mitigate the effects of oxidation ) removes the flavor , so flavor and aroma needs to be added back manually at the end of the process . What you 're tasting is a function of the recipe of tastes and smells they inject into the processed " juice " , not a function of what any of the individual oranges that contributed to what you are drinking tasted like when they were plucked from their trees .
