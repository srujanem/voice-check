The seasonal nature of the flu has not been established by research . The temperature is n't likely to have anything to do with it . Being colder does n't have a direct effect on the immune system , and nothing about warm weather will kill the flu virus . In fact there is similar seasonal fluctuation in tropical areas compared with temperate areas [ source ] ( URL_1 ) . Tropical flu epidemics have caused * controversy * over some of the hypotheses for why flu is seasonal including . * Dry air makes flu more transmissible * People are indoors and close together during flu season * Decreased vitamin D production during the winter weakens the immune system * Seasonal decrease in UV radiation may allow for survivability of the virus [ further reading ] ( URL_0 )
