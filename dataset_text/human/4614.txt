Gravity . If they were lopsided , they would simply crumble down until they were pretty much round . On any planet with liquid ( I guess that means earth and venus ? ) , there will be rain , and it will erode mountains . If the planet has parts that move around ( like the plates do on earth ) , they may push up some mountains , but any excessively high or low places would tend to be evened out . Of course the gas giants will be pulled into essentially perfect spheres by gravity . Any planet that rotates will be ever so slightly " squished " by centripetal force ; the equator will be slung out and the poles will be slightly lowered .
