amalam athulam | sujalam sufalam mataram shyamalam saralam sushmitham Bhushithm | dhareem bharaneem maataram vande maataram, ||Vande|| -Bankim Chandra Chatterjee 62 Meaning Mother I pay my obeisance. You are water laden, you are fruit laden, you blow the perfumed breeze from the malaya mountains, you who dazzle in your green costume I pay my obeisance. You shine with pure moonlight, You are adorned with flowers and budding fruits, You give peace through your smiling face
