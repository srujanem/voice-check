If patents never expired it would be possible to build unlimited monopolies - a handful of companies could continue leveraging their patents to stifle competition and innovation ( which happens even with today 's long patent lives , and business / software patents ) . Imagine if the patent for a telephone had been extended indefinitely , and was broadly applied to any and all two - way communication devices as more " improvement " patents continued to be built on top of it , only licensable by one sole company .
