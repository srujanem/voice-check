Act implemented by the British East India Company provoked the tribal revolt. Of the tribal rebellions, the Santala’s revolt and Munda movement are prominent. In this regard, the Halagali Hunters rebellion in Karnataka is noteworthy. The Santala tribal revolt can be termed India’s first movement. These tribals lived in Bengal and Orissa hillocks.
