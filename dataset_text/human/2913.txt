It 's actually only in North America that 8.5 x 11 is considered the ideal paper size . Most other countries use the size called A4 , which is part of the German system called ISO 216 . The underlying principle is that when folded in half length - wise the paper retains its original aspect ratio : 1 to the square root of 2 . The origins of the exact dimensions of " letter " size paper ( 8½ in × 11 in ) are lost in tradition and not well documented . " Government Letter " ( 8 in × 10½ in ) was prescribed by Herbert Hoover when he was Secretary of Commerce to be used for U.S. government forms , apparently to enable discounts from the purchase of paper for schools . In later years , as photocopy machines proliferated , citizens wanted to make photocopies of the forms , but the machines did not generally have this size paper in their bins .
