A deep cleft divides the cerebrum longitudinally into two halves, which are termed as the left and right cerebral hemispheres. The hemispheres are connected by a tract of nerve fibres called corpus callosum . The layer of cells which covers the cerebral hemisphere is called cerebral cortex and is thrown into prominent folds.
