3 - D glasses need to project different images to each eye ( then your brain will interpret the two images as having depth ) . Most 3 - D glasses do this using polarized lenses with [ shifted polarization direction ] ( URL_0 ) . Light that passes through one lens , ca n't pass through the other lens ( so it looks black ) , but since most light ( that is n't projected by a polarized source ) is n't polarized nothing else appears odd . Photographers use a similar trick to photograph oil paintings , it allows them to illuminate the painting without the light source creating highlight reflections due to texture of the paint .
