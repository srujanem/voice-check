In 1817 the Peshwa attacked the British Residency in Poona and burnt it down. Appa Saheb of Nagpur and Malhar Rao Holkar rebelled against the British and were suppressed ruthlessly. In 1818 Peshwa Baji Rao II fought against the British at Koregaon and Ashti and surrendered. The British abolished the Peshwa post and installed Pratapa Simha at Satara.
