India Forest Resources: Natural vegetation covers 21.71% of India's area (7,13,789 sq. km). Forest types include: 1. Evergreen forests (>250 cm rain), 2. Deciduous monsoon forests (75-250 cm rain, 65.5% of total forests), 3. Tropical Grasslands, 4. Mangrove forests (Sunderbans in Ganga delta), 5. Desert forests, and 6. Alpine forests of Himalayas. Madhya Pradesh has largest forest area.
