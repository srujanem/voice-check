Incognito mode only affects your browser . It wo n't keep any history / cookies / other information on your browser but it still goes through all the same channels otherwise . You can see the traffic on your network , sites that use trackers can still track your IP and when you visited what pages , and accounts like youtube can still do all the things they normally do . Incognito does n't do anything but make sure your history does n't save * in the browser * .
