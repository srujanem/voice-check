Our bodies are made of bajillions of tiny parts called cells . Cells get old and die just like all living things , so new cells have to get made all the time . Different cells have different jobs and when one cell makes more cells , the new cells are supposed to do the same job as the old one . Sometimes a bad thing happens instead and the new cells just want to make lots more cells instead of doing their job . Then the new cells they make just want to make new cells too that do n't do any real work . It takes energy to make new cells so now your body is giving all the energy to the cells that keep making new cells instead of giving it to other cells who are doing their jobs . So now there are lots of cells that are n't doing their job and the cells that are still trying to do their job are n't getting energy to make good cells . Things that " cause cancer " are things that make it more likely that that bad thing happens , and less likely that your body can get rid of the bad cells so they do n't make any more .
