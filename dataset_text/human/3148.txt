For every acre of trees vs. hemp , you get 4x more pulp from hemp . It grows almost universally and requires little attention . Hemp fibers are also the strongest fibers on the market that can be mass produced for cheap . Ships worldwide , including the military , still use hemp for their docking lines . It 's non - elastic and strong enough to hold a full sized ship . Elasticity in docking lines is a very bad thing . The lines can snap under tension and cut people clean in half in their recoil . Keep in mind industrial hemp production , and the stuff people smoke are not the same thing .
