There is a term called market segmentation . Basically , the hotel industry has understood that people can be classified people based primarily on income status among other sorts of factors . Research has shown that customers in each segment exhibit different price behaviors . Basically , hotels that charge $ 400 - 1000 / night ( Luxury & Upper Upscale on the STR scale ) know that they usually attract rich guests to their hotels . These guests are less likely to be price - resistant , not caring too much about money piece . Because of the little price - resistance , these nice hotels charge for every little thing and know that their guests will pay for it . Keep in mind that if you do pay for the best , you get the best ( quality , experience , service , etc . ) Conversely , hotels that are on the lower end ( Midscale & Economy on the STR scale ) know that they are likely to attract guests that are on a small budget and are heavily price - resistant . Therefore , a method of attracting guests is to offer everything free !
