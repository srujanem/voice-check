First Phase : Moderates (1885 - 1906): Traditionally, the first 20 years of the beginning of Congress are called the 'Moderate Age'. Among the moderates, M.G.Ranade, Surendranath Bannerjee, Dadabhai Naoroji and Gopalakrishna Gokhale were prominent.

India's Role in World Wars: Over 1.5 million Indian soldiers fought in WWI. Mysore Lancers led by Regimentdar B. Chamaraja Urs and Col J. Desiraja Urs captured Haifa, Gaza, and Damascus. The Teen Murti memorial in Delhi honours Mysore, Jodhpur, and Hyderabad Lancers.
