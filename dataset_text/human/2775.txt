Part of it is the culture of the region . The people of that culture used the foods that were available to them to create their meals . Over time , this became socially acceptable . There is another reason , however : generally , most breakfast foods if you notice are rich in protein , fats , and carbs . Take an american breakfast : eggs , bacon , sausages , maybe grits or toast , etc . Take an italian breakfast : coffee with milk , cornetto ( think of it like an italian croissant ) , butter and jelly . Take an lebanese breakfast : labneh ( hard yougurt ) with olive oil , zaatar ( a soft of lebanese pizza ) , maybe some kibbe ( lebanese meat empanada ) , etc etc . Generally , each culture used the foods that they had available to make a meal that allows them to get started on their day .
