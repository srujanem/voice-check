You 're right that some people buy them . Where you 're wrong is that you 're thinking some substantial minority pay a little bit each , making them profitable . Microtransactions work like this : 99 % of people never pay them , and get annoyed by them . 1 % react to them like an addict , paying thousands upon thousands of dollars to get the next new shiny thing . ( No joke , I have a friend that spent about $ 12k on them before he recognized he was out of control and disabled his google wallet . ) So they annoy almost everyone , and then viciously take advantage of a tiny few . There was a South Park episode about this a few weeks ago , that broke down the psychology and business in microtransactions .
