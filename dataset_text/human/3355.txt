HT 4 . TH Flipping two coins only gives us one way to get two heads ( get heads , then get heads again ) . But two ways to get heads and tails ( H then T , or T then H ) . Dice are just a more complicated version of the same . There 's only one way to roll two dice and get 2 : 1 and 1 . But you can get 3 out of ( 1,2 ) and ( 2,1 ) . And you can get 4 out of ( 1,3 ) ( 2,2 ) or ( 3,1 ) .
