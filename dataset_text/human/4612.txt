Grass can reproduce it 's self through rhizomes and stolons ( below and above grounds roots respectively ) that 's called tillering . The existing plant sustains the new one until it is established and rooted . The growing point of a grass plant ( crown ) is at the base near the soil , so you can cut it and it keps growing back . You probly do n't see the seed if you mow it frequently enough . Seeds are harvested differently depending on the species of grass for commercial sales .
