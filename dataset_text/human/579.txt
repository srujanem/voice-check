We have * lots * of carbon in our bodies , but it 's usually bound up in large molecules , rather than more pure carbon like graphite or diamond . * The proteins that make up most of your structures are made up of amino acids , which are carbon compounds . * The lipids and carbohydrates that build us ( and the ones that we can use for energy - like fats and sugars ) are basically long carbon chains , along with hydrogen and oxygen . * Your DNA , which codes everything your cells make , is made of sugars , phosphates , and nitrogenous bases , all of which contain carbon . These four types of molecules - carbohydrates , lipids , proteins , and nucleic acids , are called * organic molecules * - they 're the things we know are the molecules of life . And every one of them are built around carbon chains .
