Drug tests look for the remnants of drugs is the body . One way or another , when drugs are introduced to the body the go from bloodstream to brain , and are eventually metabolized ( broken down ) into other chemicals . These are the chemicals that drug tests look for . Like investigating an explosion , you do n't look for more explosives , you look for evidence of explosives . Different tests test for different drugs , for different prices . Some test multiple , some do n't . A big issue with this is the time it takes for the metabolized chemicals to leave the body . Some drugs like marijuana can take up to a month , others like cocaine can be gone in a week or less .
