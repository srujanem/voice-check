Ham radio operator here . There is a standard for the amount of space between letters , and between words . It is slightly longer than the space between the dot and dash elements that make up letters and punctuation marks . Someone who sends really beautiful code sends these elements precisely and accurately , making it easier for listeners to pick up where letters and words end . " The time taken to produce the sound equivalent to one ' di ' or ' dit ' ( dot ) is taken as unit time and called a dot unit . A ' dah ' is approximately of three dot units length and the space between two sound elements of a letter is one dot unit , i.e. silence period is one dot unit . The space between two letters or characters is equal to three dot units . The space between two words is equal to five dot units .
