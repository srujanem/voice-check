National Integration via Sports: Physical education brings together students of diverse backgrounds, languages, castes, and religions into unified teams. Sports foster mutual respect, sportsmanship, and national integration through shared goals and rule-based fair play.

Jallianwallah Bagh Massacre: On April 13, 1919, about 20,000 protestors assembled in Jallianwallah Bagh in Amritsar. General Dyer arrived without warning and fired upon the unarmed crowd, killing 379 people. Rabindranath Tagore surrendered his Knighthood in protest, and Udham Singh later killed General Dyer.
