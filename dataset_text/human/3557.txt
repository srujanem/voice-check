Hate to break it to you , but they do . Rigor mortis , depending on conditions ( animal health , size , temperature , etc ) takes 2 - 24 hours typically . In processed meat , it 's already in a package by then . Sometimes , the process is allowed to complete , as it can actually result in more tender meat . When it comes to big animals , like cows , controlled temperature and electrical stimulation ( contracting / relaxing the muscles so they do n't lock up ) can be used to avoid or control it .
