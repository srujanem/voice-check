* * ELI5 * * : Coulomb Counting is the main way to determine state of charge in a modern rechargeable battery . It is like counting how many gallons of water were used to fill your tank . Since evaporation of water out of the tank and gunk that collects in the tank ( reducing its capacity over time ) both affect how much charge the tank can hold , we measure its temperature and how fast it spits water out the spigot to get a more accurate measure .
