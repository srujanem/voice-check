A number like 123 is : 3 ones + 2 tens + 1 hundred The numbers get ten times bigger as you go up . 1 , 10 , 100 , 1000 .... Also , you can only make it out of numbers from 0 - 9 . 19 + 1 is 20 , not 1{10 } . In binary a number like 101 is : 1 one + 0 twos + 1 four The numbers get two times bigger as you go up . 1 , 2 , 4 , 8 , 16 ... Also you can only make it out of numbers from 0 - 1 . 11 + 1 is 100 , not 12 . We chose a number system that goes up by tens because we have ten fingers . If we had two fingers , we might use binary in our everyday lives .
