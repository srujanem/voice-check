Lifestyle Diseases & Prevention: Non-communicable diseases caused by unhealthy diet, lack of exercise, smoking, and stress. Common types: Hypertension (high blood pressure > 140/90, risk factor for heart attack/stroke, managed by DASH diet), Atherosclerosis (plaque build-up in arteries), Coronary Artery Disease (CAD), Type 2 Diabetes (insulin resistance, normal glucose 80-120 mg/dl), Obesity.

Rowlatt Act 1919 gave judges power to arrest and interrogate people based on mere suspicion. Gandhiji protested against this Act through satyagraha. Thousands of Indians participated in these protests.
