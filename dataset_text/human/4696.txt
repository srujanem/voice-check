Inevitably, the British entered an agreement with Hyder Ali through the ‘Madras Treaty’. The first Anglo-Mysore War ended with the signing of the ‘Madras Treaty in 1769’. Second Anglo-Mysore War (1780-1784) : The Madras Treaty had put the political developments in South India on a temporary hold. The British attempted to break the ‘Madras Treaty’.
