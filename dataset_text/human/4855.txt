The debate your asking about is popular vote vs electoral vote . The popular vote would be the 300 million American votes counting for one vote . For example : if 200 million people vote for one candidate and 100 million vote for another , majority would win . The electoral vote system in which America has adopted , gives ' points ' to each state determined on the population . For example , New York has more points than Delaware . Most Americans see this system as flawed because if your residency is in a state , like new York , that is traditionally democrat , your vote as a republican does n't count . Technically , if a state wins a democratic vote , all the republican votes in that state are , in a sense , eliminated . This is also what most believe , deters people from voting . However , in a ' swing ' state like florida ( meaning that traditionally , it has a record of flip - flopping their political party ) some deem their votes more valuable because they have a real shot in changing the majority vote in their state . I hope I made that clear enough . Also : a candidate could win the popular vote and lose the electoral vote . Which is why there is some controversy .
