There are two parallel rails , which carry a very large electrical charge . The projectile forms a conductive bridge between them , so current flows up one rail , through the projectile , and down the other . Because of the induced magnetic fields in the rails and the projectile , there is force acting on the projectile , pushing it along the rails . When it gets to the end , its momentum carries it in the same direction . Essentially , a railgun is an electric motor - except that it 's straight rather than circular . The Navy has been testing one for a while now , and they are talking about putting one on a ship , where they say it will throw projectiles 100 kilometers .
