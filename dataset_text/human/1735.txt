Previously , you could pour molten metal into open faced circular molds . Three dimensional shapes ( such as cubes ) could not as easily be punched out or cast . Meanwhile , anything with sharp corners will tend to have those corners beat up rather quickly . So squares are out , those nice distinct corners would get damaged really quickly . Octagons and other many sided shapes are sometimes used because the less distinct corners tend to get rounded off slower .
