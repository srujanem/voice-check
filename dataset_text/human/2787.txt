One reason is that you have likely seen a bunch of 3D renderings or other artistic representations of planets and have accepted that to be normal , so the truth looks strange by comparison . Another big reason is that a camera may not be capturing light in the same spectrum as the human eye . For example , a camera may capture its 3 channels in Infrared , red , and green instead of red , green , and blue . This will lead to some odd coloration from what you 'd expect . Often a spacecraft will only have a single - color camera but then it places filters ( think colored Saran wrap ) of various colors ( in the visible spectrum and outside of it ) in front of that camera to visualize the planet / moon in different colors , which can be combined to get a full color image .
