So let 's say British Petroleum bribes a politician . We find he received a hundred thousand dollars , but he swears up and down that he took the bribe and never helped BP . So , a year later , congress votes on whether or not to continue the embargo of Iran 's oil . BP would obviously prefer if we did . If that politician votes for the embargo to continue , was it because of the bribe ? How could we ever know ? And on the flip side , should he be obligated to vote against BP 's interests into perpetuity now ? Even if it 's against the interests of his constituents ? The only safe way to proceed is to ban receiving the money altogether .
