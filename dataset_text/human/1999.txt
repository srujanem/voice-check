I 've heard of gift cards often being seen as impersonal alongside cash , other times they were perfectly fine . It 's all about the different grade of how personal a gift is . At the most impersonal , there is cash . You are essentially saying " I did n't know what to get you , so here 's the money to get something " At slightly less impersonal is gift cards " I did n't know exactly what you wanted , so here is a gift card for a store / restaurant you really like " Then it 's stuff like a shirt , or socks , or other generic item " Here is something I think you 'll like " And then there is very personal stuff " Here is something that I had custom ordered / hand made for you " Money is seen as impersonal because you are saying that you do n't know what the person likes , but you feel you need to give a gift . Gift cards are seen as slightly better because it 's showing that you at least know some of what the person likes , whether is a store , a restaurant or that they like to buy stuff off of Xbox Live .
