The idea of doing prescribed burns in australia is to reduce fuel loads in bushland . It is done in a tactical way which will give authorities the best chance at stopping a bushfire , or at least to buy time and slow it down so residents in its path have time to escape . We conduct burns in spring and autumn purely because the fuel moisture is too high in winter to get an effective burn and lower the fuel load . Burns conducted when the fuel moisture is too high do n't get the ground fuels like grasses which significantly increase the rate of spread of a bushfire . And from a resource sense , the amount of manpower and drip torch fuel it would take to get a small burn completed is just not practical . Source - national parks firefighter conducting burns in Australia for 5 years Also might add that of course with Australia 's erratic weather doing burns like this greatly increases the risks of burns escaping . Unfortunately the pressure from annual targets of bushland burnt by state governments can increase the risks of burns being conducted in unfavorable conditions
