Imagine that you are in a room with no windows . There is no experiment that you can do inside that room to tell the difference between being on the surface of the Earth and being accelerated in outer space with a force of 1 gravity . You could jump . You could weigh yourself on a scale . You could drop a ball and measure how long it takes to fall . Everything you try will be * * exactly * * the same . This is the sense in which they are equivalent .
