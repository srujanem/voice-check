Winds at different altitudes are going in different directions . Let 's say the winds at 500 - 700 ft are SW - NE but from 700 - 900 they are W - E. You can adjust how far North and how far East you eventually get by varying your altitude between the two currents of winds . You 're not likely to find completely opposing wind currents though , so it 's not like you can navigate very sharply . All in all , you are * mostly * at the whim of the winds , but with some level of rudimentary control .
