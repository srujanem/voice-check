Hawking 's " A Brief History of Time " explains this very well with spacetime diagrams . Basically , if you were to consider the black hole as a singularity instead of an event horizon , the actual singularities combine their mass into a more massive singularity . As soon as the two black holes cross eachother 's event horizons , they become one black hole , because the event horizon is the " edge " of the black hole . The singularity , being so massive , would move past the event horizon of another black hole and they would combine as well , not just the singularities .
