People were trading long before currency existed . You could n't just give someone your hard earned harvest for free . You expect something in return . So you give him your some of your harvest , he gives you some of his prey . Now it 's winter and you need more meat . You ca n't trade your harvest for it . What if , instead , earlier you traded some of your crop for meat , and some extra for a symbol of previous transactions ? Eventually societies standardize their symbol for debit in the form of currency .
