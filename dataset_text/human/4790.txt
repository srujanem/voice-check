The changes in distance from the sun are absolutely inconsequential from the perspective of energy from the sun . It is n't the distance from the sun that determines winter and summer . If it was , winter would be during the same time period worldwide , which it is n't . What determines whether it is winter or summer is the angle of the earth relative to the sun . The earth is tilted on its axis , which is the imaginary pole on which the earth rotates every day . The axis also spins , but much more slowly . Depending on whether the part of the earth on which you reside is tilted toward or away from the sun determines whether you experience summer or winter .
