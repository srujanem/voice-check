Recreational Games: Unhook the anklets, Tiger-gun-man (pointing signals), Flag war (capturing opponent flag with jail rule), Newsman (whispering message chain), Naughty horse, Hand ball, Whose throne is this, Our bear, Whose seat, Capital tour, Challenge, Punjabi Kabaddi.

Improvements in transport and communication, journalism, establishment of organizations, and changes in administration saw people getting engaged in huge numbers in many provinces. Lord Lytton brought in 'Domestic Papers Control Act' in 1878 to exercise control over Indian publications, and Lord Rippon brought in 'Ilbert Bill' to bring in uniformity in the judicial system.
