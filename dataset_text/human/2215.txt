The difference between " protected speech " and " socially acceptable speech " seems to be lost on everyone ... Free speech protects you from government censorship . As long as you do n't incite violence , you can be as racist , sexist , ageist , etc . as you want . Free speech DOES NOT protect you from the consequences of your speech . People can hate you , fire you , shame you , ostracize you , all without infringing your free speech .
