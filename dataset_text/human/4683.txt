The Goal of american football is to beat your opponent team by scoring more points than them . this is accomplished by 1 ) a touchdown- where any part of the ball crosses the end zone of the 100 yard field . this is worth 6 points , and afterward a team can kick the ball through goalposts for 1 more point , or try another play for 2 points . 2 ) a field goal- where the ball is kicked through the goal posts from the field . worth 3 points . the teams get to the end zone by gaining ground on the field . at the start of each offensive possession , a team has 4 tries to get the ball 10 yards . if they succeed , they get 4 more tries ( called " downs " ) to get 10 more yards , and so on until they fail , where the other team gets the ball , or score ( see above ) . The Purpose of the defense is to try and stop this , though defensive players can score a touchdown on an intercepted pass or fumble ( where a member of the offense drops the ball ) .
