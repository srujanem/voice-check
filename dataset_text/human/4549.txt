I 'll give you the short version . When cars are backed up at an intersection or highway ramp eventually the first car has to move . All the cars behind it do n't move at the same time like a train does . The first car moves , then the second car waits a second and moves , then the third car can only move after the second car moves , and so on . The traffic jam is basically all of these cars not moving while additional cars continue to pile up behind it .
