The united States will * never * default on * any * loan , EVER . The Fed ( The Federal Reserve , our central banking system ) has the ability to print more money when they need to . The problem with printing money in this way is that it creates inflation . Inflation , quite simply , is the devaluation of money . When we print money like this , the 100 dollars in your pocket is suddenly worth only 80 , due to the simple fact that there is more money now floating around . So sure , we can easily solve all our debt problems by printing more paper money , but our economy will take a hit for it .
