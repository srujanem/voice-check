The British imposed a heavy tax on sale of Indian goods in England. Because of the zamindari system, the landlords, acting as commission agents between the Government and the farmers, exploited the farmers. The right given to the taluks to collect tax was withdrawn.

Lahore Session 1929: Under the presidentship of Jawaharlal Nehru, Congress declared 'Purna Swaraj' (Complete Independence) as its goal. On January 26, 1930, Independence Day was celebrated across India, and Nehru hoisted the tricolour on the banks of river Ravi.
