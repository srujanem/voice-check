The problem is twofold . Firstly , in order to enhance the organoleptic(taste / appearance / smell etc ) properties of the food , food companies need to add in ingredients that can be deleterious to your health . These include * high levels of sodium * nitrates * sugar * easily digest starch * Unhealthy fats such as oxidised PUFA , trans fats and to an extent saturated fats * colourings , preservatives etc The other problem is that they * remove * beneficial nutrients from the food in order to make them more appealing . Examples are : * Refining flour to make white bread which removes fibre , b vitamins etc * Using cheaper , lower quality ingredients to cut costs . Using iceberg lettuce instead dark leafy greans for example . * Nutrients degrade as they are exposed to processing / long shelf life such as vitamin c and folate
