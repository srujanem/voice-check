An simple way to think about dimensions is how many length based measurements you can make . If you take a point , how many length based measurements can you make of it ? None . ( 0 ) If you take a line , you can measure its length . ( 1 ) If you take a square , you can measure its height and width . ( 2 ) If you take a cube , you can measure its height , width and depth . ( 3 ) If you take a Tardis , you can measure its height , width , depth and a point in time . ( 4 )
