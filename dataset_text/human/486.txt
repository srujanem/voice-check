Cape Canaveral was chosen because : 1 . It 's geographic location is as close as can be on the US mainland to the equator , which makes launching easier 2 . Florida has a relatively stable and predictable temperature range and weather patterns . 3 . It 's near water in the direction that rockets go , which is helpful for emergency splashdowns 4 . At the time it was built , it was in a relatively unpopulated area . This alleviated noise concerns as well as minimizing collateral damage should accidents occur . Also , in case you are wondering , why is Houston the command center and not Florida ? Because Lyndon Johnson was president at the time all this infrastructure was being built and he was from Houston and was simply seeing to it that his home state and home city benefited .
