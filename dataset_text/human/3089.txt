When I was in the army , we were taught to count a second for every three hundred metres ( meters , for the american audience ) , when estimating how far away a shooter is . If you knew exactly when the shot was fired , you 'd probably need about 600 m to get out the way . Your problem is that you 've no reliable way of knowing when the shot is fired , as with most rifle rounds , the sound of the gun will arrive after the bullet does .
