Second Phase : Extremists (1906 - 1920): One group of Congress members who became unhappy with the mild attitude of the moderates called them 'political beggars'. Aurobindo Ghosh, Lala Lajpat Rai, Bipin Chandra Pal and Balgangadhara Tilak were the prominent extremist leaders.

Terrorism & Global Impact: Terrorism uses systematic violence to create fear for political or ideological goals. Major attacks include 9/11 in USA and 26/11 in Mumbai. India enforces strict anti-terrorist laws and specialized defence forces.
