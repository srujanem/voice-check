I believe that loss of blood flow results in a lack of oxygen to the nerves present . I 'm not sure about the causal link from no oxygen to pins and needles , but it results in the nerves misfiring and discharging electrical signals that are interpreted by the brain . The randomness causes the the spiky sensation ( each stabbing sensation is an individual nerve ) . Also there is no moderation of the signals ( they discharge at any strength ) which is why they are typically unpleasant .
