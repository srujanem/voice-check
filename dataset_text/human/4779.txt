Basically , yes . There 's also a ' survival and adventure ' aspect to it , where you need to collect blocks to craft into tools to collect more blocks . For instance , you can make a wooden pickaxe really easily but it breaks fast and works really slowly . You can use the wooden pickaxe to collect stone to make a stone pickaxe to collect iron to make an iron pickaxe to collect diamond to make a diamond pickaxe . There 's also enemies that spawn in darkness when you explore caves . Take a look at the [ crafting ] ( URL_0 ) wiki page , there 's a lot more to this game than you first realize .
