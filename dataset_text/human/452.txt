Wars are mean and nasty business . However , nations have come together and collectively agreed that there are certain things which are TOO mean and nasty , even for war . Usually these are things which do not have an immediate military necessity , which unfairly target civilians or non - combatants , or which utilize banned biological or chemical weapons . The example you give is slightly mistaken . Shooting paratroopers from the sky is not a war crime , since paratroopers are enemy combatants with armed for paratrooper duty with intent to do you harm . The post you mentioned talks about shooting * ejected pilots * from damaged aircraft . This is a war crime since the pilot has already been shot down , and thus does not pose a significant threat to you From the [ Wiki ] ( URL_0 ) on War Crimes , " Attacking enemy troops while they are being deployed by way of a parachute is not a war crime.[21 ] However , Protocol I , Article 42 of the Geneva Conventions explicitly forbids attacking parachutists who eject from disabled aircraft and surrendering parachutists once landed .
