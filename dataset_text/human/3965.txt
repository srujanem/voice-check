Colored ink is not expensive . Colored ink that has been put into cartridges that will work in the particular inkjet printer you own is stupidly expensive , because the same people make the ink cartridges and the printers and have locked you into their ink cartridge ecosystem . Take your old genuine cartridges to Costco or somewhere that will do refills , or buy a laser printer . Inkjet ink , gallon for gallon , costs more than oil . But it wo n't heat your house .
