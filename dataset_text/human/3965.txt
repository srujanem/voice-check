1922, N police used their force against peaceful protestors. Enraged by this, the people tried to enter the police stations. Police resorted to firing. When their ammunition got exhausted, the police ran into the station. The angry protestors set the police station on fire. About 22 police were burnt alive inside the Non-co-opration movement station. This incident is termed 13 Chowri-Chowra incident in history. The incident made Gandhiji unhappy.
