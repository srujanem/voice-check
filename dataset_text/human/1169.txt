He started the Navaratri (Dasara) festival of Mysore province in Srirangapatna. Chikkadevaraja Wodeyar (1673-1704) : He was an efficient soldier and administrator. He checked the invasion of Shivaji at Madurai, Ikkeri and Bijapur. He captured Magadi, Madhugiri, Koratagere and other places. He purchased Bangalore from the Mughal military general.
