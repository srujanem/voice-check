Temperature is not only controlled by the amount of heat added by the sun , the Earth and the surface ocean also contribute to the temperature of the air . As the air gets colder and Autumn turns to Winter , the amount of solar incidence decreases but the heat lost from land and water mitigates the drop in temperature somewhat . On Dec 21 , solar incidence is at its minimum but there is still heat coming off the earth . Solar incidence begins to increase , but he earth gives off less and less heat ( as it is now cooler ) . Eventually , the earth cools enough so that it is at equilibrium with the atmosphere , but solar incidence is increasing , reversing the process through the summer .
