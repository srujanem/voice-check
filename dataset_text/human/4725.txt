It can be some of both . But a lot will be hardware . Servers are designed so that many things can be replaced without having to take the entire server down . Hard drives and power supplies are a common example . Many servers have redundant power supplies that will let you replace one of them without taking the server down . Likewise , with hard drives in RAID arrays , you can replace a failed hard drive without interrupting the system . There 's a lot of extra cost involved to build a system like that , but it pays off in environments where downtime is very costly .
