Because of a relationship between absorptivity and emissivity described by [ Kirchoff 's Law of thermal radiation ] ( URL_0 ) . In short : The reason why black is more emissive is because it absorbs more radiation in thermal equilibrium and thus must also radiate more to maintain that equilibrium . Why heatsinks are n't black is because plain aluminium is still quite emissive in infra - red ( where it matters ) and because black paint may have lower thermal conductivity than bare metal , reducing the primary method of cooling , convection , as plain radiation is a far less important method of cooling .
