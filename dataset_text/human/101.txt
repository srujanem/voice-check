The Marathas installed Shah Alam-II back on the throne of the Mughal Empire in Delhi. Shah Alam-II gave Koara and Allahabad to Marathas, which he had earlier given to the British. This resulted in enmity between Marathas and British. A long war was waged from 1775 to 1782 between Marathas and British, ending with the 'Salbai Agreement'.
