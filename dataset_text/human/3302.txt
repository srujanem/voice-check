There are many negative effects of starving yourself . For example , your body needs proteins to function and without food it will break down your muscle to get them . Your body needs calcium to function and without calcium in your diet it will break down your bones to get it . Your immune system wo n't have the nutritional support to function at 100 % , so your ability to fight infection / disease decreases . Yes , long - term fasting will make you lose weight , but you will end up weaker than you were before you started . Fat is not the only thing your body uses to maintain itself .
