In the case of things like chips that're easily broken , the air pocket in the bag keeps them from being destroyed in transit . Put some chips inside a baggy and suck all the air out , then seal it . Throw that around a bit , and see how nice those chips look afterwards . Now do the same thing again , but blow the bag full of air . They 'll be in much better shape . Basically , in the case of some products , it prevents you from opening a bag of crumbs .
