United Nations Organization (UNO): Founded on October 24, 1945 at San Francisco Conference with 51 charter members, headquartered in New York. Its 6 principal organs are General Assembly, Security Council (5 permanent members with Veto), ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ (The Hague), and Secretariat.
