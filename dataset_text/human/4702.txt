Her daughter Caylee was reported missing by her grandma . 3 few months later Casey Anthony pled not guilty to 1st degree murder . 2 months after that , Caylee 's remains were found near the family home . The prosecution sought the death penalty , but on July 5th 2011 , she was found not guilty of murder , aggravated child abuse and aggravated manslaughter of a child . She was , however , found guilty of four misdemeanor counts of providing false information to law enforcement . She got sentenced to a year in jail and a $ 4000 fine , but with credit for good behaviour and time served , she got out less than two weeks later .
