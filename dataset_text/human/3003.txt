By your question , I 'm taking it that you mean a regular balloon for like a birthday party filled with helium . The altitude and pop of the balloon depends on several factors . Helium is lighter than air so that 's why the balloon floats ( air pushes it up ) . Eventually , the balloon will reach a point where the air is less dense and equals the same density as the balloon and helium and it will no longer keep going up . Though , along the way , since the air pressure is less the more it goes up , the balloon is going to expand . It will continue to do this until the material of the balloon can no longer handle the pressure and burst . So if the balloon was strong enough to handle the air pressure , it will eventually maintain a steady altitude until the helium slowly escapes or if it was not strong enough to handle the air pressure , it will pop .
