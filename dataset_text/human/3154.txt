c) Greater Himalayas (Himadri) : These are known for the highest peaks of the Himalayas and the earlier formed ranges of the Himalayas. This range is completely covered with snow. Hence it is called ‘Himadri’ (abode of snow). This is about 6000 meters to 8000 meters above sea level. The highest peak, ‘Mount Everest’ (8848 meters) lies between Nepal and Tibet.
