If you are talking about legally , then there is nothing that states that teachers can not yell , at least as far as I 'm aware . ( Again , like other users have said , each state makes their own laws regulating education . ) Of course , it depends on what it said , but if a teacher screams , " SILENCE ! " or something along those lines , then it is not typically seen as scarring the child . Now , as far as educators go , being one myself , I can say that yelling is generally seen as failing to manage your classroom properly . Theoretically , one should never have to raise one 's voice because the rules are clear , the punishments are clear , and the lessons are engaging enough where students do n't want to distract themselves or others . Now , this being said , I can see in some situations where yelling is justified . For instance , let 's say there is a fire and the kids start to panic . A quick yell to be quiet could manage to get the kids focused again so that they can receive directions that will keep them safe .
