There is no single survey or sales list etc from which there could be one single game that is the ' best selling ' , so different games will come in different places on different lists . So if ten gaming sites were to keep a tally of what games their users report buying over the course of a year , those ten websites may end up with ten totally different games as their " best selling game of the year " . Quite often if you look at the small print of something that advertises itself as the best selling or number one product it will usually say something like " according to the * * * rating " . This will tell you which survey or list they have used that lists their game as being the best selling . They are n't necessarily lying or falsley advertising , they are just showing the results of one of many thousands of different lists . Usually the position of ' best game ' is given based on one of two things , either number of sales or customer survey , so a game may not have physically sold as many units as another but a magazine may run a large scale survey and more people report preference for a certain game . So , even if it is lower SELLING , the makers can still say it 's " voted game of the year " but in small print you will find it says something like " according to * * * magazine " . It 's not false advertising but it 's clever use of small print .
