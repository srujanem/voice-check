Farmers and Labours Protests: Farmers in Champaranya revolted against indigo planters. Kisan Sabhas organized protests in Tebhag, Malabar, Telangana, Bengal, and Maharashtra. Labour movement began in Calcutta in 1827, leading to Madras Labour Union and Bombay textile unions.

Major Indian Ports & Airports: Ports: Kandla, Mumbai ('Gateway of India'), Marmagoa, New Mangalore, Kochi, Tuticorin, Chennai, Visakhapatnam, Paradeep, Kolkata ('Tea Port'). Airport Authority of India manages 11 international airports.
