Animated characters are rarely meant to " look " like any specific people . That specific movie is made with an art style that is fairly typical to Japan . Various features are exaggerated , like hair and eyes . Really , making one or two things exaggerated is a great way to easily and quickly differentiate characters . Good character designs do n't need to be realistic , they just need to be relatable . Think of the Simpsons . Those characters are all weirdly colored freaks with bizarre proportions , yet they are all identifiable as people and we relate to them as if they were alive . In short , you 're just seeing a different cultural representation of art than you 're accustomed to .
