Paraphasing what I read in a book . Our torso is where majority of our vital organs are located . Thus it has been an limbic response / instinct to cover your torso when confronted by danger or when placed in an uncomfortable situation . That is known as a " pacifying " action . You " pacify " yourself by covering your torso when feeling discomfort . This is why people tend to cross their arms / hold their stomach / put an object in between a person when they feel uncomfortable . This natural need to feel protected may be why you feel more at ease when under the sheets , as you feel more " pacified " and " protected " by it . FYI , the book I read was " What Everybody is Saying " by Joe Navarro . It 's a great book on reading body language , which looks deep into automatic responses by the body for genuine reading .
