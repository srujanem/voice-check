The soils in the lower areas are called alluvial soil. Lower area soils are most extensively spread over vast areas compared to other types of soil. This type of soil is covered in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Odisha, Punjab, Haryana and Brahmaputra valley of Assam.
