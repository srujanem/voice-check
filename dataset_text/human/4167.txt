For binary to decimal , you start at the right and add a value for every " 1 " , adding nothing for every " 0 " . The pattern to follow is : 2 ^ 0 : 1 2 ^ 1 : 2 2 ^ 2 : 4 2 ^ 3 : 8 And so on . So , " 0010 0111 " can be read as " 1 + 2 + 4 + ( nothing ) + ( nothing ) + 32 + ( nothing ) + ( nothing ) , " or 39 . The important thing is to know the pattern of the powers of 2 . 1 , 2 , 4 , 8 , 16 , 32 , 64 , 128 , 256 , 512 , 1024 .
