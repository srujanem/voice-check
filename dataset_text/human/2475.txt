There are three different methods . A linear mono audio track was the first . Most of the tape was used for the video signal , and a stripe on the edge was used for audio . Then a linear stereo track was developed . Same idea as a casette audio tape . Stereo , but not high quality sound . Then hi - fi audio . This is a much more complicated system with the audio signal imbedded into the video signal . Much better sound quality than the previous systems , comparable to CD , though vulnerable to tracking problems and head wear .
