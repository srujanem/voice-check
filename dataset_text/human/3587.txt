Because that will throw off how long a day is . The issue is n't how many seconds are in a year , it 's how many * days * are in a year . Times units like days , months and years are based on astronomy . Units like seconds , minutes and hours are human constructs , subdivisions of the day . If we made a second longer , then what would happen is that over the course of a year , 10 a.m. would have different meanings -- sometimes it would mean mid - morning , sometimes it would mean dinnertime , sometimes it would be in the dead of night .
