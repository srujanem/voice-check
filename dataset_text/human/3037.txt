The sexual cycle involves the following three steps: (i)Fusion of protoplasms between two motile or non-motile gametes called plasmogamy. (ii)Fusion of two nuclei called karyogamy. (iii)Meiosis in zygote resulting in haploid spores. When a fungus reproduces sexually, two haploid hyphae of compatible mating types come together and fuse.
