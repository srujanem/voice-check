The computer has files of varying sizes . These take up varying amounts of spaces on the hard drive . Say you write to the hard drive 3 different files . One takes up 5 " spaces " the next 2 spaces , and the last 5 spaces . Later , you delete the 2 space file . Now you 've got 5 occupied spaces , 2 free spaces , 5 occupied spaces , and the rest of the drive is free after that . You want to write a file to your hard drive , it takes up 3 spaces . The hard drive sees the 2 free spaces , and begins writing there .. But then it runs out of room , the 3rd space is occupied by another file . So it has to go past that file and write there . You end up with { 5 spaces ( file 1 ) } { 2 spaces ( file 4 ) } { 5 spaces ( file 3 ) } { 1 space ( file 4 ) . } Now , when you tell your computer to retrieve file 4 , it has to look in two different places to get all the pieces , or ' fragments . ' When you ' defragment ' your hard drive , you 're telling it , organize these better so files are arranged more contiguously .
