Seeds are just one way to propagate plants . Many can be spread by cuttings , bulbs , grafts , division , and so on . As an example many hobbiest gardeners may be familiar with , a [ coleus plant ] ( URL_0 ) is a pretty simple plant to propagate by cuttings . You can pretty much simply cut stems off , a few inches from the ends , knock off all but a few leaves , stick the severed end in soil , and add water . Pretty soon , you 'll have as many coleus plants as you made cuttings . Bananas have actually encountered difficulty due to this . Because they have to be propagated essentially entirely this way , there tend to be widespread genetically identical groups . They 're all basically clones of the original . Thus , if a disease evolves that can take advantage of and kill one member of that group , it can kill all of the members . Entire cultivars have been lost .
