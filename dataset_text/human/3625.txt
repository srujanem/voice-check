Before we 're born , the testes are located in the abdomen . Slowly but steadily they descend lower and lower , until they are situated just above the scrotum . It takes puberty for the testes to fully descend into the scrotum . Painful sensations are sent over a specific pathway , different from the nerves that transmit other sensations . The funny thing is , the brain 's pain center never gets the message that your nuts are now situated a couple of inches lower than where they used to be . So , your brain places the source of the pain in your lower abdomen .
