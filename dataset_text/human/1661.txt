In addition to stopping reflections , oftentimes stages are hollow which allows them to act as acoustic amplifiers ( like a guitar body ) which helps with some ensembles and hurts others , a carpet helps deaden that by decoupling the band from the stage ( which can happen in concert halls designed for classical ensembles ) . The other big thing is with drum kits , the drummer will tend to kick the shit out of his kick drum which makes it skooch forward on smooth surfaces , a carpet helps keep it in place .
