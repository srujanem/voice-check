Vaccines contain an agent that is similar to the microorganism causing a specific disease . These are often attenuated ( dead or weakened ) microbes or parts of the microbe itself like surface proteins . This allows the bodie 's immune system to recognise the agent and kill it , whilst making a record of it , allowing it to kill the actual microorganism should it invade the body in the future .
