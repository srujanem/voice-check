PTSD can be treated , but it 's also very easy to ignore warning signs . If left untreated , it gets worse . Combat soldiers may have more ready access to docs for their physical injuries , and good docs will recognize symptoms of psychological trauma and refer them to specialists . Non - combat vets get an annual physical , and may be able to unconsciously hide their symptoms , or give in to societal pressure against being perceived as " weak . " Also , survivor 's guilt and just having lots of friends die at once can rattle anyone .
