Mir Qasim was an efficient administrator. After verifying the misuse of Dastaks, he declared all business was dutyfree in Bengal. Indians competed against the British in all spheres of business. As a result, the British trade suffered considerably. The combined forces of Mir Qasim, Shah Alam-II, and Shuj-ud-daula faced the British army led by Hector Munro at Buxar in 1764.
