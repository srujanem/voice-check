The British East India Company government entered into an agreement at the level of 'Mahals' with regard to payment of land tax in Uttar Pradesh, in many parts of Madhya Pradesh, Punjab and Delhi. 'Mahal' means taluk. R.M. Bird and James Thompson implemented this system. There were differences in the implementation of this system from region to region. The big and small zamindars were part of this system. Since the company officials fixed more land tax than the expected production from the fields; many zamindars had to lose their ownership of the lands.
