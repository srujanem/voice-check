Charlemagne basically invented the Feudal system as we know it . He and his tribe were a bunch of badasses , who conquered a lot of land . He wanted to keep control of this land , so he put some of his warriors in charge of the lands . They built forts , collected regular tribute from the locals , and so forth . Over time , other places in Europe copied Charlemagne 's system of oaths of fealty and family lands . More interestingly , this system is * entirely * European . Other cultures created nobility in very different ways . In the Islamic world , for example , nobility was simply a matter of wealth - you owned a lot , and were expected to keep an army to defend what you own .
