All the banking transactions in India are controlled by The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) which is known as Mother of Banks or Bankers Bank or Central Bank of India. RBI formulates the monetary policy which should be followed by all banks. Today we have a State bank group, 12 Nationalised banks, 21 Private banks and 46 Foreign banks.
