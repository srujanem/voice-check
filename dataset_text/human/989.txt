A lot had to do with price . Laptops were expensive , and there was no way to do one with a CRT . LCDs were still developing fast , and were very expensive . On the other hand , the CRT makers had long since amortised all their capital costs , in contrast to the LCD makers who were paying for expensive tooling up . TV is a very price - sensitive market , so the CRT makers chased the LCDs down in price and maintained their advantage .
