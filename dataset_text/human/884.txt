Partition Consequences & Refugees: Partition created two independent nations, India and Pakistan. Millions of refugees migrated from Pakistan to India, requiring shelter, food, employment, healthcare, and rehabilitation from the Indian government.

Public Finance & Budget: Public finance deals with government income, expenditure, and debt. Financial year: April 1 to March 31. Budgets: Surplus, Deficit, Balanced. Progressive taxation imposes higher rates on rich.
