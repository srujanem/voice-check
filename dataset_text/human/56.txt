Aligarh Movement & Ramakrishna Mission: Sir Syed Ahmed Khan started 'The Anglo-Oriental College' (later Aligarh Muslim University) to provide modern western education along with religious education to Muslims, supporting female literacy and opposing polygamy. Ramakrishna Paramahamsa was a priest at Dakshineshwar Kali Temple who considered all religions equal. Swami Vivekananda established Ramakrishna Mission in 1897 to spread equality of all religions, social service, and cultural education across the world.
