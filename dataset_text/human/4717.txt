You give your bank your money . They put it in the vault and keep track of how much they have from you . They will from time to time lend your money to other people for mortgages or student loans . Because they can use your money for other things , they will pay you a small percentage on a monthly basis . This is called interest . At any time you can go to the bank and ask for some of your money ( making a Withdrawal ) or give them more money ( making a Deposit . ) People keep money in banks because it 's secure , it 's more convenient than carrying a lot of cash , and once you get a job , you can get your employer to put your salary directly in the bank account so that you do n't have to cash a cheque every two weeks .
