Krishna, Tungabhadra, Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, Dhoni, Bennehalla etc. cause floods during rainy season in Karnataka. Effects of floods: • Floods are causes, loss of shelter, property, land and crops of the people. • Telecommunication, electricity supply and transport facilities get affected. • Besides washing away the fertile upper layers of soil, a lot of trees are destroyed. • Stagnant water in the low lands leads to dangerous diseases.
