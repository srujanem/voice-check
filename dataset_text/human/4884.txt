Inflation is where the buying power of a currency decreases ( causing prices to rise ) - It 's considered normal because steady inflation is the sign of a growing economy caused by the ' wage / cost ' cycle . Employees want higher wages , which causes prices to go up which causes employees to want higher wages , so on and so forth . If inflation is happening too fast , the government can increase interest rates , this causes people to spend less ( because the cost of borrowing increases ) and encourages prices to fall ( or stop rising as fast ) in response . It 's a bit up in the air at the moment because the high level of unemployment is causing people to not spend as much , resulting in lower inflation and consequently very low interest rates to try and prop things up .
