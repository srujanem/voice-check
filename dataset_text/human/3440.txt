If you accept deflation as undesirable , then you tend to accept a little inflation as a preferable alternative . As 0 % is perilously close to deflation , It is better to have a little inflation as a buffer . Defining what " a little " means is entirely arbitrary , but Govts often aim to conduct monetary policy to put inflation somewhere around 2 % . Higher inflation tends to distort the economy in undesirable various ways , such as money piling into particular asset types , making debt more attractive , etc , .
