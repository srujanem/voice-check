In the year 1885, a retired English officer, A.O.Hume established the Indian National Congress. It worked towards creating political awareness and nationalist attitude. It published papers in the local languages and started debates on political problems.

Second World War (1939-1945): Sparked by Germany's attack on Poland. Fought between Axis (Germany, Italy, Japan) and Allies (Britain, France, USSR, USA). Ended in 1945 with Hitler's suicide and atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
