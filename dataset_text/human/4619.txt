Catholic : highly centralized authority in the pope and lots of additional practices . Orthodox : broke from the Catholic Church because they did n't like the authority the pope was taking at around 1000 ad . Also regional cultural differences . Protestant : broke from the Catholic Church due to corruption within the Catholic Church at around 1500 ad . All use functionally the same bible ( different preferred translations ) and generally agree on fundamental theology . The differences are primarily in the customs and traditions of the church . To tell the difference between them , just ask . Unless you 're in Ireland , then keep your mouth shut .
