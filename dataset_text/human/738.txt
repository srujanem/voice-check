Taboo words are not processed by our brains the same way as normal language . You can directly invoke emotion in people using these words which is inherently offensive to the listener . Steven Pinker talks about this a bit in * The Language Instinct * , but , if I remember right , even more thoroughly in * The Blank Slate * ( I hope I 'm remembering correctly and it was these books instead of some other books of his ) . It 's a very fascinating subject to me .
