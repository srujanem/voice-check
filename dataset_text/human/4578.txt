The overview seems to be that Creationism is explicitly that God created the world according to the Bible . Intelligent Design follows the logic of Paley 's watch that suggest that if you find a watch on a deserted island , it is far too complex to be there by chance so there must be someone who put it there . Applied to earth , it is too complex to have happened by chance , so something must have created it . However that something does not have to relate to religion .
