Netaji. As a parallel to the peaceful struggle of Gandhiji, in the early part of the 1930s, Subhash Chandra Bose was engaged in the attempt to organize Indians settled abroad and equip them for fighting against the British. He travelled to Vienna, Berlin, Rome, Istanbul and other countries and inspired the Indians there to support their motherland.
