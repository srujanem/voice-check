Partly because there 's a huge period of time between the sentencing and the execution . On average , US prisoners on death row wait around 190 months ( or ~15 years ) between sentencing and execution ( [ source ] ( URL_0 ) ) . So they 're already being kept in prison for 15 years , and then you have to add in the cost of the execution . You need an execution device , which ca n't be cheap , and you need to keep it in working order ( and if it 's lethal injection , you need the chemicals as well ) . Add to that the staff it takes to execute people and maintain the devices , as well as medical staff to administer a lethal injection . Then there 's the appeals process . Most people on death row are n't well - off enough to hire a lawyer on their own , so they 're likely using state - appointed attorneys , which is more money right there . On top of that , running a court costs money , and an appeal to overturn a death sentence takes a long time ( a big reason why it takes them so long to execute convicts on death row ) . Basically , they already are waiting in jail for 15 years and then you have all that execution stuff on top of it .
