Extremely simplified way of reading binary . Somebody better at explaining can probably write you up a more clear description on why this works , but eh ... this can get you started at least . Say you have the binary number 10001101 . Write down this table : Number : 1 0 0 0 1 1 0 1 Power : 2 ^ 7 2 ^ 6 2 ^ 5 2 ^ 4 2 ^ 3 2 ^ 2 2 ^ 1 2 ^ 0 Result : 128 + 0 + 0 + 0 + 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 141 Basically , you multiply each digit ( since binary , just 1 or 0 ) with the 2^x , x being the position of the digit read from right starting from 0 , and add all results together .
