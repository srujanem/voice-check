The law states that tax breaks will be given to people who use their state insurance exchange ( marketplace ) to purchase insurance . However , many states did not create their own state exchange , preferring instead to use the federal exchange as their state exchange . The law does not specifically state that people who use the federal exchange should receive those same tax breaks . Those opposing the law argue that the tax breaks people have received in those states are unlawful , and should not continue , because the law says nothing about tax breaks for those who use the federal exchange . Those in support of the law argue that the intent of the law is clear , that states using the federal exchange should still be treated as a state exchange . If the Supreme Court rules with the opposition , that the tax breaks should not be granted , it would make health insurance unaffordable for many people , and could effectively doom the entire Obamacare system because too few people would participate in it .
