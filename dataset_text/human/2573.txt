It 's the same with every good that can be traded , be it virtual or real goods . If the demand of a certain good is bigger than the supply the price rises . If the supply is bigger than the demand , the price drops . In the case of Bitcoins , the demand increased much quicker than they could supply new ones . This resulted in an extreme increase of the value of Bitcoin . At the time this person bought his Bitcoins there was nearly no demand so he could get them cheap ( 4000pcs for 200 $ ) . As the demand rose , the price rose so his 4000pcs were worth $ 2.4 million when he sold them .
