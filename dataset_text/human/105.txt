Political anarchy broke out in Punjab after the death of King Ranjit Singh in 1839. In December 1845, war broke out between the British and Punjab. Sense of danger brought Hindus, Sikhs and Muslims together. Defeated due to traitor leaders, they signed the Lahore Agreement in 1846. Later in 1848, Chattar Singh Attariwala and Moolraj rebelled, leading Lord Dalhousie to merge Punjab with the British Empire.
