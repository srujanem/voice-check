Importance of forests : Forest is an important resource to both man and environment. Their importance is as follows : 1) Pure air, food and fodder is provided. 2) It brings good rainfall. 3) It controls soil erosion. 4) It is a shelter house for animals and birds. 5) Medicinal plants
