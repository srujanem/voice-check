Even before the split , Chinese culture was regional . Northern Chinese have a lot in common with Koreans , Manchurians and Mongolians . Southern Chinese blend into South East Asia . Hong Kong people are mostly southern Chinese from Guangdong in term of food , language and lifestyle . During the split , HK got a huge infusion of rich merchant class Chinese from all over China with their gold ( especially Shanghai ) . So there are a lot of Shanghai influence . Hong Kong still place a lot of importance on commerce and corporations . Then there was British colonial influence in government and education . Taiwan have a polynesian native population and Chinese from Fujian as a base . It have some Dutch influence from a brief occupation ( and you can see some natural lighter hair color in Taiwan ) . Japanese occupation in WWII was very gentle comparing to rest of Asia . Japan build schools , roads and hospitals ( older Taiwanese can speak Japanese ) . During the split , Taiwan got upper level professional military ( pilots , officers ) , bureaucracy ( accountants ) and academia ( at least 50 % of all the professors from all the colleges ) from China . In China , you have a 10 years long ban on luxury , religion and secondary education . A lot of history and tradition were lost .
