Humans do n't * NEED * to cook food to eat their food . We can raw meat ... and we do eat raw meat . The fear of contamination often occurs with eating raw meats not immediately after the kill , which is what some / most animals do . But do n't get fooled that animals do n't also get sick from eating " contaminated " raw meat . Many wild animals are hugely infested themselves with parasites , and many die from microbial infections . In general though , animals have evolved to have more robust immune systems to handle these microorganisms better than humans . Also , when proto - humans discovered " cooking " of meat , they learned that cooking not only preserved the meat so that it did not need to be eaten immediately , but it allowed our digestive systems to not work so hard to process and take in that nutrition . We were then able to use that " additional " evolutionary capital to fuel other evolutionary traits like our incredibly large and energy consuming brains .
