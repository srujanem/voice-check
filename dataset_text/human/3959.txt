It depends on whether you mean reaching out , as if for someone 's hand to grab onto , or out to the sides . If out to the sides it 's an instinctual attempt at kinetic force redistribution . Most martial artists are intimately familiar with the technique , and most primates have been observed doing it when fighting . Basically , at the moment just before you make impact , you throw your arms out to the sides and ' slap ' the ground as you land . Doing so channels some of the kinetic energy being delivered vertically into your body outwards horizontally . This lessens the amount your body has to absorb . I was skeptical until I learned how to do it .
