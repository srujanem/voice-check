* A LOT * of props and costumes are rented from movie supply companies . At the end of the production , those items are returned to the rental companies to be made available for rental for other movies . If there are unique items created , the production companies either save them for future possible productions , or sell or give them away . In terms of " Saving Private Ryan " those military uniforms , guns , etc ... were most likely rented from a movie prop / costume company that specializes in military props and returned when done .
