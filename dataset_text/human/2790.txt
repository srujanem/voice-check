Humanity not dying on this rock . Seriously . You and I might not personally make it out of here , but some day , for a variety of plausible reasons , humanity will need options . We can , and should , become better stewards of Earth , but we should also have the sense to diversify . This planet is robust , yet fragile , and we are but one of nature 's offspring . We 're essentially renting a room here , which leaves us at the mercy of Random Acts of Shit , Freak Events of Who - Saw - That - Coming , and the Consequences of Our Actions . I 'd like us to have somewhere else to move to when this apartment block , despite our best efforts , goes downhill . We ca n't just Uber to a goldilocks planet once we realize we 're doomed -- we have to plan ahead . TL;DR options
