You ca n't prove a negative . For example , you ca n't prove that ghosts do n't exist , because you would need to check every point in space at every point in time and show that there is n't a ghost there , which is not possible . Since you ca n't prove they do n't exist , anyone who says they do needs to prove it , because that would be trivial to do . Simply showing me a ghost would be proof that they exist . However , even if there is no proof that ghosts do n't exist , it still does n't prove that they actually do . You would need to actually show me that ghost . What this means is that in legal situations where something ca n't be proven to not exist , the burden of proof is on the other party . Me not being able to prove I did n't commit a crime does n't prove that I did . Someone needs to provide proof that I actually did for a conviction .
