Really simple explanation : Pressure is when you have more or less air above you . The atmosphere is constantly moving around , making pools and pockets of higher / lower pressure air . That being said , what we call wind is really just air moving from high pressure to low pressure . You know when you open a soda can and air fizzes out ? That 's just a gas moving from high pressure to low pressure . The red bumps and blue lines show where the air is moving ; this gives you a general idea of where storm fronts are probably going to be moving . Blue pressure lines mean that a " low pressure front " is moving in , red triangles mean a " high pressure front " is moving in . When two fronts collide , you get storms . Kapiche ?
