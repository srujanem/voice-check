The term " evolution " is often taken to mean " natural selection , " but the two are separate ( though certainly related ) . " Evolution , " in its strictest sense , refers to changes in allele frequency within a population . Alleles are " versions " of a gene ( imagine an allele for red flower petals and one for white flower petals ; they are two separate versions of the " petal color " gene ) . There are several mechanisms by which allele frequencies are altered ; natural selection is only one of these . Others include mutation ( random changes in a gene with time that create new alleles ) , drift , flow . Though of in these terms , then , evolution can never end ; there will always be genetic change , and this change will necessarily result in changes in allele frequencies i.e. evolution .
