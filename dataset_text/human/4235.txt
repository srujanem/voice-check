Modern car painting involves dipping the entire car in multiple baths of chemicals to prep the body and to apply primer and protective coating to every surface . Up to the 1980s ( ? ) cars rusted away in 2 - 3 years ( largely by design ) but today they last for decades ( largely due to government rules that require rust - through warranties of 6 - years ) . Consider that a car is made from very thin steel that would rust away to literally nothing in a year or two if it was not protected by primers and paints .
