Sri Narayana Guru & Periyar: Sri Narayana Guru started Sree Narayana Dharma Paripalana Yogam in 1903 in Kerala, declaring 'One Caste, One Religion and One God for human beings' and building alternate temples for backward communities. He launched the Vaikom Satyagraha in 1924. In Tamil Nadu, E.V. Ramaswami Naicker (Periyar) led the 'Self-Respect Movement' and Dravida Kazhagam, starting the magazine 'Revolt' to champion equality and oppose caste and gender discrimination.
