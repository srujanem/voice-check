Raja Wodeyar (1578-1617) : The credit of expanding the small paleyapattu into a large Mysore goes to him. Srirangapattana was captured from the representative of Vijayanagara and made it as his Capital. Further the Surrounding areas were occupied and expanded his Kingdom. He revived the temples of Srirangapattana, Mysore and Melukote “Rajamudi”, the crown was offered to lord Cheluvanarayana Swamy.
