Gender Discrimination & Gender Minorities: Women leaders include Indira Gandhi, Pratibha Patil, Meira Kumar, and Fathima Beevi. The Parliament passed 'Nari Shakti Vandana Adhiniyam' granting 33% reservation to women in Lok Sabha and Assemblies (106th Amendment Act). Gender Minorities face social ostracism; the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019, SMILE scheme, and National Portal for Transgender Persons provide legal protection and rehabilitation.
