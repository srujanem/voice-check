When you run as administrator , you can easily mess up your system , for example by deleting important files , or by changing settings necessary for your computer to work . Forcing you to run as administrator only when you need to makes sure you know what you 're doing . It 's also a good warning that something might be dangerous : if you download something that 's supposed to be just a tiny game but it asks you to run as administrator , that 's a warning that it might be malware .
