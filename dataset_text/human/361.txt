Dr. B.R. Ambedkar: Dr. Ambedkar started 'Mahad' water movement and 'Kalaram' temple entry movement. He established 'Bahishkruta Hitakarini Sabha', 'Swatantra Karmika Party', and newspapers 'Mukanayaka' and 'Prabuddha Bharata'. He served as Chairman of the Constitution Drafting Committee and India's first Law Minister.

Natural Disasters Management: Cyclones (Bay of Bengal, IMD naming), Floods (Kedarnath 2013, embankment bunds), Landslides, Coastal Erosion (mangrove protection), Earthquakes (seismic zones, light construction).
