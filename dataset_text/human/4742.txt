When someone invests their money - when they spend it on something that will make them more money - they might lose or make money . For example , if you sell flood insurance ( which means you will pay people the cost of repairs if their house floods ) , and there is a flood , you will have to pay out a lot of money . So , as a " hedge " , you would invest in something that would make a lot of money for you if there was a flood . Contractors perhaps , who would rebuild after the flood , or businesses that sell big water pumps , to clean up the flood water . So the money you lose if there is a big flood from your business selling flood insurance is made back from the money you make from your business selling water pumps . A hedge fund is a lump of money used for this purpose . ed : As some people have pointed out , what hedge funds are used for in the real world is just to make money . It may make that money in order to hedge , or it might just do it for it 's own sake , and these days , it 's normally the second one .
