You need to be careful with the term " pyramid scheme " . Usually that terms means an illegal activity where people at the bottom get left with nothing and everything flows upwards ( think gifting clubs ) . Multilevel marketing , at least in most cases is different . Take the example of a certain cosmetics company that drives pink cars . At the lowest level ( consultant ) , the individual keeps 50 % of whatever they sell , the remainder does go up the chain but it 's not really a pyramid " scheme " . In other words , it 's a completely legal business model .
