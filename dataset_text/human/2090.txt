There are Walmart strikes every single year at Christmas time . Walmart employs 1.4 million people in the US alone . If 99 % were happy , and 1 % of the employees were unhappy that is 14,000 unhappy workers . When 14,000 people do something , or are threatening to do something , it gets national attention . ( Clearly these numbers are just to make an example for the sake of showing the scale of employees Walmart has and does not reflect any kind of actual data on employee happiness )
