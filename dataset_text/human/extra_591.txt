Winter and Summer are n't caused by the earth moving towards and away from the sun . Earth 's orbit is elliptical , like all orbits , but it is n't eccentric enough to cause that kind of variation . The season 's are caused by Earth 's axial tilt , which is around 23 degrees . When the northern hemisphere is tilted towards the sun between June and September , it is exposed to more direct solar radiation and temperatures rise . From December to March , the northern hemisphere tilts away from the sun and temperatures fall . Since the southern hemisphere is on the opposite side of the earth , it experiences summer when the north experiences winter and vice - versa .
