Your battery has some intelligence and knows what its capacity is , in milliamp - hours ( mAh ) , which is a measurement of how much current ( milliamps ) the battery can provide for a measured amount of time ( hours ) . I.e. a 2700mAh battery can provide 2700mA for one hour , or 900mA for 3 hours ( 900 * 3 = 2700 ) . The battery measures the current being drawn from it , combines with the amount of time that current was drawn , and subtracts it from its capacity . I.e. after drawing 100mA for one hour from a fully charged 2700mAh battery , you are left with 2600mAh of capacity left ( 2700mAh - 100mA*1h ) , which is ~96 % left ( 2600/2700 = .96296 ) . There 's a little more complexity than above ( internal resistance of the battery , battery chemistry , etc ) that make the above not always hold perfectly true , but as a simple answer , here ya go .
