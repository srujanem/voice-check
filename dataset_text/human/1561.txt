Not all women have a cycle of 28 days . Some are shorter , some longer , some irregular . It is only approximately 28 days . The cycle is due to a rise and fall in hormones . There is no reason as far as I know why it is approx 28 days and not 30,32 , 15 , 7 , 40 etc etc . However , I took many a biology class in college , and 28 days is likely the amount of time to balance the energy required to ovulate and the best frequency for getting pregnant . For example , if women ovulated every week , that s a lot of menses and eggs and tissue . If they ovulated every 3 months , they 'd save energy but it takes a long time to get pregnant ( due to less episodes of a fertile egg ) and lose time to have babies . Of course , if the fertile period lasted longer , that would be ideal , but it did n't work out that way for us humans . Also , many animals have mating cycles based on the moon , likely because its an easy way to tell " time " , so it may be be a holdover from that . But I have no evidence , its just a theory . TL;DR : Its 28 days because of hormones , but the specific duration actually varies among women . This 28 day average is likely an evolutionary balance between energy loss and chance of reproducing .
