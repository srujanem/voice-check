I build systems like this often . Here are a few technical reasons : 1 . People are n't always connected . When you push something to a persons phone , you 're pushing it to a queue that the phone periodically checks , otherwise you 're not going to get that message ( or photo ) delivered if the person is n't connected at the time it sends . 2 . Efficiency . A single phone sending to a copule of hundred people would wreck a senders data plan - they 'd be doing 200 independent " sends " to another phone . 3 . Outside of bluetooth and a few proprietary methods , you _ never _ connect directly to another phone - you always go through an intermediary . The SMS message goes through servers from the carrier , snapchat goes through servers from snapchat .
