Non-Conventional Energy: Renewable sources like Solar energy (Baramar Rajasthan, Chikkaballapur Karnataka) and Wind power (Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Karnataka) conserve exhaustible fossil fuels.

Asian Games History: Conceptualized after WWII by Gurudutt Sondhi of Indian Olympic Council to foster Asian unity. First Asian Games held in New Delhi in 1951, conducted every 4 years by Asian Games Federation.
