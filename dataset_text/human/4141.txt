I ca n't answer your question , but I can point out something about the potential for gaseous / solid life . Living things exist in a state of non - equilibrium thermodynamics where the entropy must be kept low without the system being homogeneous . Gases have high entropic forces because their inter - molecular forces are weak and thus can not easily overcome entropy to form complex * and stable * structures ( conversely , solids have inter - molecular forces that dominate the entropy and thus limit dynamic activity ) . Liquids have the capability of forming low entropy ( i.e. highly ordered ) states while still being able to do stuff ( as opposed to , say , a solid rock which just sits there ): they 're the ideal state of highly ordered , dynamic systems .
