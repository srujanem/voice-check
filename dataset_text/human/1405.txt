[ Long version of what is a hedge fund ] ( URL_0 ) Short version : there 's no real definition of what a hedge fund is , but the rough consensus is that it 's when rich investors pool their investment money together and use aggressive investment strategies to maximize the returns of the investments . Most well known being borrowing a lot of money from banks , several times what the total amount of money in the fund , as a way to amplify the strength of the investment money . But most hedge funds are secretive and do n't openly declare their strategies , to prevent competition . One of the key reasons why they are able to succeed with high returns is because they are unregulated , and can do crazy risky things that other funds ca n't . And they are unregulated because it 's assumed only crazy rich people who do n't mind losing their money would touch hedge funds .
