The Parthenon actually blew up in 1687 : at the time , Greece was part of the Ottoman Empire , which was Muslim , and the Parthenon had been converted into a mosque -- it was apparently still in good repair . But Venice tried to invade Athens and capture the Acropolis . The Ottomans decided to use the Parthenon as an ammunition dump , despite being told that this was the stupidest idea ever . And so the inevitable happened : a Venetian mortar made a direct hit , the ammunition dump exploded , nearly destroying the building , 300 people were killed and the explosion set fire to several homes . The Ottoman Empire went into decline after that , and because nobody worried about historical significance or tourism , nobody bothered trying to rebuild it . Notre Dame , on the other hand , has always been in use and maintained . In modern times , historic buildings bring in the tourists . With modern vehicles churning out less pollution than they used to , historic buildings can be cleaned up to help prevent their decay . But structures that fall into ruin and decay are n't usually rebuilt ; it 's rarely worth the effort , and there may not be reliable records on hand that would allow architects , builders and masons to accurately recreate the original .
