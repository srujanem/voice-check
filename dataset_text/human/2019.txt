There are different ways to conduct a hostile takeover , but a typical Gordon Geckoesque way would be decreasingthe value of the stocks , buying up enough to get influence in the company ( in Sweden generally 10 % of the votes ) , and then either sneakily or openly buying up enough stocks from prefential owners or on the open market to wield control of the company . It often involves shareholders grouping up . When control has been gained , a company can be gutted , directions can be switched and reserves can be liquidated . For example , Volvo at times have had more liquid reserves than they have had share value making it an ideal takeover . At that point , they try to buy back their own stocks or invest somehow to avoid shenanegans .
