Extra fuel is extra weight , which means extra fuel burned and extra money , which others have already said . Reducing weight is also a safety feature , a heavier aircraft requires a longer runway , faster takeoff , faster landing , and speed on the ground can be dangerous . They do n't calculate " exactly " the fuel , they calculate precisely , and then add a hefty safety margin . The [ Gimli Glider ] ( URL_0 ) 's problem was that they calculated incorrectly . If you calculate incorrectly , gravity will have it 's way with you . An airplane filled up to the brim before launch will still run out of gas if you incorrectly calculate how far it will go .
