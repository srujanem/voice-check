That is n't what fragmentation is ; it is n't a question of putting things back when used because they never left . The problem is that files are n't going to stay forever . When data is written to the drive it goes into available space . But when it is removed it leaves a void . Later files are n't going to fit perfectly so they are broken up into pieces which are put into the smaller available holes . If this happens too much it starts becoming time consuming to track down all the pieces to get a full file .
