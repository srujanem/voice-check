In 1995 , cell phones were magic . They were this incredible new technology that was coming into its own , and most people did n't really understand it yet . There were also a lot of misconceptions and wild stories , about cancer , infertility , and interfering with electrical equipment , including planes , hospitals , and , of course gas stations . The gas station myth was exacerbated by a wildly circulated fake email , which caused many to accept it as fact .
