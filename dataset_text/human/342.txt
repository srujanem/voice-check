Tilak declared: 'Swaraj is my birthright and I will get it at any cost'. Lokamanya Tilak utilized his 'Kesari' in Marathi and 'Maratha' newspaper in English for freedom struggle. Through these papers he called for active participation. He wrote Geetha Rahasya in jail.

Regional Cooperation Bodies: Commonwealth of Nations (54 countries, London), SAARC (1985, 8 South Asian nations, Kathmandu), European Union (27 nations, 1992 Maastricht), ASEAN (1967, 10 South East Asian nations), OAU (1963 - African Unity).
