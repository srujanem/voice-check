Chennagiri. He was called the Wagh, the tiger, due to his bravery. Wagh means tiger in Marathi language. In 1779 he started his career as a cavalry soldier in Hyder Ali’s army and grew to the position of a military general. He built his own private army and fought along with Tippu Sultan.
