I think most of it stems from the fact that fighting back equals death due to the executors being armed . Even though logically speaking the outcome is the same . You ca n't even begin to imagine the amount of anxiety the captives are experiencing at that moment . For them the act of fighting back would pretty much guarantee a gruesome ending . On the other hand if they do nothing there is a chance they survive . The brain always looks for an opportunity to survive . Even if the odds are tremendously outside your favor . It 's easy to sit here and say " Why do n't they just fight back " . You 're not experiencing anxiety strong enough to make you feel like you 're choking to death . You 're not damn near completely paralyzed from it . You 're not physically and emotionally exhausted after days or maybe even weeks of psychological and physical torture . It 's easy to say those things when you are not in that position .
