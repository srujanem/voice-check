They are obstruction markers so that aircraft ( particularly helicopters ) can see them before flying into them . In the United States , they are required by the FAA under [ Advisory Circular 70/7460 - 1 K ] ( URL_0 ) . Anything high enough needs to be marked , which is why tall buildings have red flashing lights on them . They are also on maps so a pilot following a pre - planned route will stay well away from them , but news and emergency response helicopter pilots are especially vulnerable to missing thin wires while they 're following the action on the ground .
