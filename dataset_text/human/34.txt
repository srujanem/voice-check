Charter Act 1833 aimed at improving the political situation in India. It also licensed the East India Company to remain in India for another 20 years. The Governor General of Bengal was named as the Governor General of India. The Governor General was vested with powers to direct, control and supervising all trades in India. The Central Government of Bengal had the final right to decide on issues like war, peace and diplomatic relationship with the princely states of India. The Act barred any discrimination based on religion, birth and skin colour.
