If the water is very pure and is in a very clean bottle with a very smooth interior , it can become " supercooled " which means it 's below the freezing point but remains liquid . The reason for this is that ice forms by creating crystals that start around a bit of dust , a crack or other imperfection on a surface , but if none of these exist , it can not start the process of freezing .
