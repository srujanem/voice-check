The purpose of a mascot is to inspire the characteristics associated with said mascot . So things that are strong , fast , fierce , loyal , etc are chosen . These things tend to be predators ( hawks , lions , bulldogs , etc ) , military figures ( Braves , Knights , Minute Men ) , Historical Nations / Groups ( Trojans , Vikings , Seminoles ) , mythical things ( Wizards , Dragons ) , or forces of nature ( Hurricanes , Tornadoes , Tsunamis ) .
