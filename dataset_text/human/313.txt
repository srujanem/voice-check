Textbook Excerpt Part 313:
Causes for the First War of Independence: Political causes: Many kingdoms lost their rights due to the promulgation of Doctrine of Lapse brought about by the British. Satara, Jaipur, Jhansi, Udaipur and other kingdoms came under British control. Dalhousie cancelled the princely titles of the Nawabs of Tanjore and Carnatic kingdoms.

Non-co-operation movement: In 1920, Gandhiji called for non-co-operation. Students left schools, lawyers gave up courts, and Motilal Nehru and C.R.Das gave up law practice. In 1922, angry protestors burned Chauri-Chaura police station, killing 22 police officers. Gandhiji withdrew the movement.
