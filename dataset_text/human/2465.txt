I have a big , shiny aluminum pot and an infrared thermometer . The thermometer _ always _ measures the _ reflection _ of what 's outside the pot , not the pot itself . Aluminum , and shiny metals , _ will _ reflect infrared like a mirror , _ but glass absorbs infrared _ . It 's opaque to it , like a blackboard to visible light . If you use a non - glass mirror , like my pot , it will do precisely what you suppose .
