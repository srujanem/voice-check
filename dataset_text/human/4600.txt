Because species are more a label applied by humans and not set in stone , so to speak . Some species are still close enough genetically to have kids , but in most cases the kids are infertile . So called ring species might relate to this and explain a bit that there are not real species lines . Let 's see if I can explain this in an easy way . Say we have a species of lizards lives north of a big mountain range , over a some thousands of years the they spread out , so some migrate around the mountains to the west and some to the east . The climate of the different sides of the mountain is different so the lizards on the west and east evolve in different ways . They keep spreading over the years until finally they meet again on the south side of the mountains . But as this point the lizards from the west and east are so different that they can no longer interbreed . The west lizards can still breed with the north , and the north still can breed with the east . But the east can not breed with the west . So are they still part of one species or not ?
