These hormones produce anabolic (synthetic) effects on protein and carbohydrate metabolism. 19.2.10 Ovary Females have a pair of ovaries located in the abdomen (Figure 19.1). Ovary is the primary female sex organ which produces one ovum during each menstrual cycle. In addition, ovary also produces two groups of steroid hormones called estrogen and progesterone .
