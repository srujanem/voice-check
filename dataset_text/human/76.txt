India Soils: India has 6 main soil types: 1. Alluvial Soil (spread over Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, fertile for wheat and paddy), 2. Black Soil / Regur (formed from basaltic lava, best suited for cotton in Deccan Trap), 3. Red Soil (covers largest area of Peninsular Plateau), 4. Laterite Soil (formed in areas with >200 cm rainfall), 5. Desert Soil, and 6. Mountain Soil (rich in organic matter and nitrogen).
