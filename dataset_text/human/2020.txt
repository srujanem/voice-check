The advantage of the alleles being different and thus having different blood types would be the same as any other diverse " feature " we have . Diversity makes it so that one single pathogen or mutation could not wipe out the entire species . It could also be due to the fact that diversity is unavoidable , there are plenty of ways your DNA can ultimately be expressed , modified , translated etc . and this may just lead necessarily to more diverse " features " . We see the features we happen to see because those are the ones which are stable and happen to work . Basically , the reasoning is that we have diveristy because without diversity evolution could n't really work the way it did ; and so , partly the reason we have diversity is because evolution did pan out the way it did .
