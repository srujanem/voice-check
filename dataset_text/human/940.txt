The blocky shape of a typical bus is actually quite efficient when you consider fuel efficiency * per person . * A standard car is designed to carry 4 - 5 people , with very few exceptions . In this context , a practical way to make the car more " efficient " is to make it more aerodynamic . With a bus however , it 's much more practical to increase efficiency by adding seats ( the more people a bus can carry , the more fuel efficient it is per person ) ; the blocky shape of a bus can accommodate the most seats on board .
