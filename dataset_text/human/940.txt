Yogasana - Standing Postures: Ardha Chakrasana (half wheel, strengthens neck/chest), Garudasana (eagle posture, balances on one leg), Prasaritha Padotthanasana (forward bend with wide legs), Angushthasana (balancing on big toes).

Queen Laxmibai tied her adopted son to her back and fought in the war. She took Gwalior under her control. When the battle took place again with the British, the Queen laid down her life. Even today, the queen occupies a pride of place in the history of the independence struggle.
