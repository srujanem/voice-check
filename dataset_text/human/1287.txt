According to [ this article ] ( URL_1 ) , the frequency range between 2 kHz and 4kHz is the range that our ears amplify the most . Removing the noise in these ranges was rated to most effectively help with the ' annoyingness ' of the noise , even more so than the higher frequencies . As well , the ' roughness ' of the noise , or how often it changes pitch , also affects how painful the noise can be . Sounds fluctuating between 20 and 200 kHz are the ' roughest ' noises .
