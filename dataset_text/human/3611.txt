One important way your body regulates its temperature is through perspiration . This does n't have to be sweating , just an exudation of moisture through the skin , carrying away heat . Your skin is the largest organ of your body , and all that surface area is important for cooling . So whether the weather is hot or cold , moving air will carry heat away from your skin . When the weather 's hot , that 's a good thing ; if it 's cold , you lose more heat than you want to .
