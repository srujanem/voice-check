Sangolli Rayanna (1829-30): Rayanna was a brave soldier who fought for the independence of Kittur. He developed a sense of nationalism and organized an army of five hundred men. He aimed at looting the treasury and taluk offices of the British. The British thought that Rayanna was being instigated by Rani Chennamma, so they shifted Chennamma to Kusugal prison. The British devised a strategy to capture Rayanna with the help of an Amaldar named Krishnaraya. Rayanna was captured, brought to Dharwad, and hanged to death at Nandagadh on 26th January 1831.
