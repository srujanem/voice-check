Because the courts are hoping to return the original copyright owner to the state they would be at , * but for * the illegal distribution of their works . The judgments are usual calculated by assuming the original copyright owner permanently lost a potential sale for each illegal transfer . The pirate would be responsible for making these sales up . These judgments usually come with some sort of limit to avoid ridiculous payouts . At the same time , these judgments are also usually inflated a bit to * send a message * .
