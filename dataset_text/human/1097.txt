From what I understand it has to do with 3 things . Strength of components - high end motors are made with stronger piston rods , etc . Balance - As you 'd imagine , a slight shimmy at 4k rpm is annoying , at 10k it 's catastrophic , so all rotating parts are balanced to even tighter tolerances . Longevity - I think to a degree the higher RPM you sacrifice power and performance for longevity . a NASCAR engine only has to make it 500 miles , your truck , hopefully 300,000 or more . I might also add other things like fuel pressure , oil pressure , etc all need to be higher at those RPMs . I have little actual experience , just read a bunch .
