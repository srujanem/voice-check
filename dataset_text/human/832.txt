Doctrine of Lapse: This policy was established by Lord Dalhousie during British rule. According to this policy, an Indian king could not transfer his kingdom to his adopted children in the absence of natural heirs. Thus, his kingdom came naturally under British rule.

Khilafat Movement: The British planned to demolish the Ottoman Empire during World War I. Ali brothers started the Khilafat movement in India. Gandhiji declared support to Khilafat movement, making it a joint Hindu-Muslim nation-wide movement against the British.
