As others have pointed out , the winners decide who gets put in court . But the bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki were not remarkable in the war for any reason apart from the type of bomb . Fire bombing of cities in Germany and Japan would cause similar casualties and were done regularly . Shocking to us today , but civilian casualties on that scale were not surprising to anyone in 1945 . In addition , the bombings saved lives overall : both Japanese and Allied . The forecast of US casualties for an invasion of Japan was 1,000,000 men . Japanese casualties ( civilian and military ) would have been higher , probably .
