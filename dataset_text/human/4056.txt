Some of the expense is appeals , as everyone else is going to say , but there are other reasons why a death sentence costs so much . A person with a life sentence will be able to , usually , hold a job in the prison and be able to move about the institution freely . A death sentence inmate will be housed in a separate wing of the prison with other DSI inmates . If the DSI inmate has to leave his cell for an appointment , you need specially trained staff to cuff and escort the inmate . DSI inmates ca n't even walk to the cafeteria themselves . Their food is brought to them . If a DSI inmate has to leave the prison for any reason , you need a team of staff to escort the inmate . What I am getting at is that a DSI inmate needs a lot more staff support to keep the inmate , the staff , and the other inmates safe . More staff means higher costs .
