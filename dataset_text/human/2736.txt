This is probably a reference to the fact that China has stated their intent to install the next Dalai Lama , largely because they want to exert more control over Tibet , a region currently controlled by China that has a separatist movement . For background , the last time the Panchen Lama , the 2nd highest ranking position in Tibetan Buddhism , was selected , the Dalai Lama and the Chinese government each separately named a boy to the position . The one picked by the Dalai Lama was taken by the Chinese govt and has n't been seen since . The Dalai Lama had been the head political figure of the exiled Tibetan govt in addition to a religious one up until recently , when the Dalai Lama gave his political powers to the equivalent of their Prime Minister , a democratically elected leader . This ensured that Tibetans would have an elected leader away from Chinese control . As such , the power the Dalai Lama has is lessened and the current Dalai Lama is unsure whether the position will continue and has said it is up to the Tibetan people ( which , personally , seems to be due to China and their statements regarding the position - his changes to the power structure + making this statement makes it more difficult for China to try and usurp the position ) .
