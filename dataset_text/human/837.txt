An 'Inam Commission' was appointed and all the gifted lands ('Inam lands') were taken back. Due to this, the farmers felt insulted and experienced many financial problems. This too led to the revolt of farmers against the government.

Civil Disobedience Movement: In 1930, Gandhiji submitted 11 demands to Viceroy Irwin, including abolition of salt tax. When Irwin refused, Gandhiji undertook the 'Dandi March' from Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi to manufacture salt, launching Civil Disobedience.
