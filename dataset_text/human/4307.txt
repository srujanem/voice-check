Homing pigeons are special in that they have a natural sense of location that tells them where " home " is . Somewhat similar to how a salmon can return to the place of its birth or other birds know where to fly when the seasons change . You ca n't just take a pigeon off the street and expect it to do as well after training . AFAIK almost all of homing pigeon training involves setting where " home " is and then letting them practice returning from further and further distances .
