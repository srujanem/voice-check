Samuel Johnson was British , and compiled the first dictionary of the language in Britain . He believed that the spelling of a word should reflect its origin , so he left the " u " in words like colour and flavour , along with other spellings like programme . Daniel Webster was American and compiled the first dictionary of the language in the U.S. He felt that spelling should be simplified , so left out extra letters in color and flavor , shortened program , etc . Of course , he obviously did n't reform everything , since we still have through and rough and cough and countless other nonsensical spellings in both British and American English .
