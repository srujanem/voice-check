Child Marriage Prohibition Act 2006: Child marriage (boy < 21, girl < 18) is a crime punishable with 2 years imprisonment and Rs 1 lakh fine. Toll-free helpline 1098 and 47 designated officers in Karnataka enforce protection.

Yogasana - Sitting & Lying Postures: Vajrasana (sitting on heels, erect spine), Bakasana (crane posture, balancing on arms), Supta Virasana, Paryankasana; Lying down: Bhujangasana (cobra, strengthens back), Paripoorna Navasana (boat posture), Simhasana (lion), Mayurasana (peacock).
