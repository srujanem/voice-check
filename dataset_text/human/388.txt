Crop Seasons & Major Crops: Kharif (June-Oct: Paddy, ragi, cotton), Rabi (Oct-March: Wheat, mustard), Jade (summer: pulses, vegetables). West Bengal leads in Paddy; Punjab & UP lead in Wheat. Sugarcane uses Ratoon system.

Self Defence Techniques: Weaponless self-defence: Karate, Judo, Taekwondo, Wrestling. Punching (upward, middle, lower), Open palm hacking/push/pricking, Elbow hits, Kicks (snap, front, side, back, knee). Using everyday items like books or bags as weapons.
