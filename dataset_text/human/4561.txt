Do n't try to think of Final Fantasy as a series of connected stories , but more like a brand or an anthology - sort of like The Twilight Zone . The games share some stylistic & thematic things but there 's no real continuity ( with the exception of a few games that are actually sequels , like X & X-2 ) . With that out of the way , the numbers & sequence of the game should n't really bother you . They just assigned numbers to them in the US in the order they got released over here , which was a slightly different order than they were released in Japan .
