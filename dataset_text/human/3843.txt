Produce that makes it to your typical super market has to first be sorted on a farm , shipped to a store and unloaded . This takes time and can bruise / damage items . So , fruit and veggies are typically picked before they are fully ripe . Many stories tend to avoid items that bruise easily like heirloom tomatoes . Nicer restaurants tend to buy produce that has ripened on the plant . There is a substantial difference in taste . Try a fresh from the vine tomato vs. one of from the store .
