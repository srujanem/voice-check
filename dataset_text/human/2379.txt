The heat is caused by capsaicin , a hydrophobic ( water - hating ) compound , meaning when water comes into contact with capsaicin , the water just bounces right off . The most effective way of ' extinguishing ' the heat would be to emulsify the capsaicin ( i.e. by drinking some milk ) which enables it to be transported away from your tongue . You could also drink washing up liquid , but I do n't think that would be very pleasant .
