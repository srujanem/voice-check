A school district buys a school desk because it 's cheap . A desk manufacturer could make a desk that would not wobble , but it would be more expensive . Desks wobble because the materials they 're made of change their shape . Wood , metal , and plastic can expand , contract , or warp unevenly , so that what was once a desk that sat nicely on four legs now rocks on two . Thanks for being a teacher .
