The currency symbol is on the left to prevent modifying checks and ledgers in old - style , paper - only record keeping . If you 're keeping the books for a business , and write down that you paid out " 10 $ " to another business for supplies , an unscrupulous business partner could steal a bunch of money , and then modify the ledger to say that you paid " 110 $ " by simply tacking a 1 to the front of the number , thus hiding the fact that they 're skimming money . But with the currency symbol on the front , the worst they can do is steal a few pennies by adding some cents at the end .
