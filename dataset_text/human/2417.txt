To touch on what the periodontist said , if one is using proper brushing techniques , it wo n't matter [ too much ] , but there is still a difference . The short answer : sugar all at once is better . Your internal organs wo n't like it , but it 's better for your teeth . The long answer : Sugar , and the by products bacteria in your mouth produce from breaking it down , are acidic . The acid itself , while not beneficial , is n't the only cause of tooth decay in this instance . When your mouth 's PH is out of whack , this affects the rate of production and the effectiveness of the saliva your mouth produces . Why is this important ? Your saliva has something in it called hydroxyaptite that contains phosphate and calcium ions . The concentrations of these ions act as a buffer against tooth decay , but only when their presence in your saliva is at a high enough concentration . Guess what happens the more acidic your mouth becomes ? Its ability to hold hydroxyaptite decreases dramatically , leaving your mouth more vulnerable . So yes , sugar all at once is better . If you buy a Big Gulp soda and sip it over the course of three hours , you 're maintaining a low low PH in your mouth which will dramatically lower your saliva 's ability to fight tooth decay — moreso than if you 'd just drank the entire thing in 20 minutes .
