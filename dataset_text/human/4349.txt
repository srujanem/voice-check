Modern junk food tends to be junk because it has too much bad stuff ( fats , salt , etc . ) , not because it has too little good stuff . It 's so easy for us now to store food in ways which preserve nutrients , and adding nutrients to food , that even n a junk food diet you are unlikely to lack a crucial nutrient . If you picked only one food , say hot dogs , and ate nothing but that * ever * , you might be in trouble . But a varied junk diet is still pretty varied . Plus , if you eat enough calories to be obese , as many junk food lovers do , the food can be light on good nutrients and still have enough total nutrients to get you by .
