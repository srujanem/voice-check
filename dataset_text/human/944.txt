Real genetics is a bit more complicated , but consider this 2x2 table : _ | A | a | A | AA | Aa | a | Aa | aa | Pretend the only 2 hair colours are brown and blonde . Capital A codes for brown , little a codes for blonde ( or ginger or whatever ) . Both parents have the dominant A so are brown haired ( because dominant traits are what is expressed ) , but it 's still possible for them to have a blonde haired baby because they carry the recessive " a " gene . Edit : just to be clear , AA and Aa would produce brown haired kids , aa would be blonde .
