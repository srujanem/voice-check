An incadescent lightbulb works by running electricity through a piece of tungsten ; tungsten is a metal that does n't melt even after it gets very hot . The electricity running through it makes the filament very hot : so hot , in fact , that it glows , just like a piece of hot metal being forged or the embers in a fire . The only problem is that if you did this in air , the metal filament would burn . So lightbulbs are filled with some gas that is n't reactive , so the filament lasts .
