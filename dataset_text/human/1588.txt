Gandhiji protested against this inhuman Act through satyagraha. It gained support throughout the nation. Thousands of Indians participated in these protests against the British. Jallianwallah Bagh Massacre: Punjab witnessed widespread protests against Rowlatt act. As the protests turned violent, the control over Amritsar city was given to General Dyer. Dyer imposed military rule in the city
