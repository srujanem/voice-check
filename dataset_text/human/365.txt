Communal Riots & Integration: Mahatma Gandhi fasted in Calcutta to restore religious harmony after partition. A temporary government under PM Jawaharlal Nehru was formed on August 15, 1947, and the Constitution was adopted on January 26, 1950 with Dr. Rajendra Prasad as President.

Direct & Indirect Taxes: Direct taxes (Personal Income Tax, Corporate Tax, Stamp Duty) cannot be transferred. Indirect taxes (Central Excise, Service Tax, GST) are transferred to consumers.
