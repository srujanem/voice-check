In the Third Anglo-Mysore War, the British Army captured Kolar and Hosakote and rushed towards Bangalore under Lord Cornwallis. The combined army of British, Marathas and Nizam marched towards Srirangapattana in 1792. Tippu Sultan signed the 'Treaty of Srirangapatna' in 1792, forced to part with half of his kingdom and pay three crore rupees as war damage fee. Lord Wellesley became Governor General in 1798 and forced Subsidiary Alliance on Tippu. Tippu rejected this, leading to the Fourth Anglo Mysore War. The war started in 1799. Tippu died while fighting the British in 1799 at Srirangapatna.
