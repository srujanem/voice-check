It is easier for the business controller or travel agent to just look at the base room rate to do comparisons than it is try to figure out what all amenities are likely to be purchased , so hiding the cost from that base rate into the amenities is valuable to the hotel by making the base rate look better to the people who will see and care what it is . Peon business travelers who are relegated to the cheaper hotels are probably told they wo n't be reimbursed for amenities , either . Now the person who sees the price and the person who pays the price are mostly the same person .
