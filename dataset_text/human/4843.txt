It 's similar in effect , but different in structure . A ponzi scheme is an " investment fund " or something similar that promises a very high return , but does n't actually invest the money it receives . Instead , it pays the interest on the earlier customers out of the money of the later customers . Since no new money is actually being taken in , it is inherently unsustainable . Like a pyramid scheme , it is only seemingly profitable so long as it can take in new members .
