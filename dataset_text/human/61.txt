Karnataka Public Service Commission & Police Hierarchy: On 18th May 1951, KPSC was established under Article 315 of the Constitution. Central Armed Forces maintaining law and order include CRPF, BSF, RPF, and CISF. State police administration operates under the Home Minister and Principal Secretary of Home Department, headed by Director General and Inspector General of Police (DG and IGP), followed by DGP, ADGP, IGP, DIG, SP, ASP, DySP, CPI, PSI, ASI, Head Constable, and Police Constable.
