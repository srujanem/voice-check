Women are more [ neotenous ] ( URL_0 ) than men , meaning that they retain more juvenile physical features into adulthood . This includes softer / thinner / smoother skin , as well as smaller stature , higher pitched voices , less body hair , different head and jaw shape , etc . As to * why * this is the case , the answer is less clear . Humans , male and female , are very neotenous compared to our ape cousins ; the going theory is that this is primarily because it allowed for the development of larger brains . But why are women more neotenous than men ? No one 's really sure , but there are a few theories , all related to [ sexual selection ] ( URL_1 ): - Women who retained these features into adulthood were more likely to breed , since these characteristics signalled youthful fertility . - Men were selected to be * less * neotenous than women since less neoteny allows for men to grow larger and more muscular ( along with thicker , denser bones and a higher pain tolerance ) , providing a benefit to males competing with each other for mates . - Women with more neotenous features were more likely to trigger protective instincts in others ( piggybacking on preexisting child - protection instincts ) . It 's possible ( perhaps likely ) that all of these were working at once , but it 's impossible to know for sure . What we do know for sure is that women with more neotenous facial features are consistently rated the most attractive , while men with less neotenous features are similarly rated . * * TL;DR * * Women have softer , smoother skin for the same reason they are shorter , have less body hair , etc .
