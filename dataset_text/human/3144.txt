The Emergency Broadcast System is designed to chain the message across the entire nation from one single source . Every network monitors other networks , and all it would take to initiate the broadcast is for one channel to send the broadcast first . The tones are the digitized ( 300 baud ) version of the message to be passed along . Since other networks are monitoring the broadcast , the digitized information is extracted from the audio , and they then broadcast the same message . During an attack , it is assumed infrastructure ( telephones , Internet etc . ) will be unreliable . Additionally , the system needs to be compatible with dated technology , so for these reasons , they 're using very dated technology . Fun fact : The entire process is automated and can not be overridden by the network . A piece of equipment injects the EBS signal into the transmitter regardless of what program is running . Fun fact X 2 : All cell phones ( that are n't an old brick ) are also equipped to receive EBS signals , and these can not be opted out by the user . These messages can be configured for a region ( tornado warning ) or for the entire nation
