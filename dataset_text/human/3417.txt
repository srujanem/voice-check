Two thoughts . Firstly , it is easy to fall into the thinking that evolution is constantly creating a better species , but it is not . It is a random process and evolving a trait does n't happen because it is the best one , it is simply the one that survived . Secondly , it is often true that the female of a species has more limited power to reproduce compared to the male , but this usually also pairs with the female as the primary caregiver to the offspring . This leads to a male impregnating as many individuals as possible , where a female will nurture a small group . For example , in mammals , a typical litter will be one offspring per pair of nipples as this is as many individuals a female can reasonably sustain .
