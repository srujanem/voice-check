Found [ this ] ( URL_1 ) image which gives an idea of the electronics behind it ( [ source ] ( URL_0 ) ) Basically when you press one of the switches , it " shifts " in one of those resistors which acts like a voltage divider , changing the voltage level on the mic line . When this signal reaches your phone , the signal will go through an ADC ( analog - digital converter ) which will allow your phone to determine which button is being pressed based on the voltage level . This would trigger the software on the phone to raise or lower the volume of its output signal The other potential alternative ( for volume at least ) would be circuitry on the signal line which actually amplifies or reduces the signal , but the circuitry to do this would be more cumbersome than the above and potentially detrimental to the sound quality - I doubt it would be used for inline headphone controls .
