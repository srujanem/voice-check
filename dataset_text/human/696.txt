Humans do not have a temperature sense , we have a * heat flux sense * . That is , we can tell roughly how quickly heat energy is entering or leaving our bodies . [ The fact that different materials have different heat flux under the same temperature differential is not an artifact of human sensing but a fact of the physical universe . And if you 're in winter now , you 're probably glad of this fact because it 's how your house insulation keeps you warm . ] ( URL_0 )
