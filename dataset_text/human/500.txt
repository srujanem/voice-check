First World War (1914-1918): Fought between Triple Entente (Britain, France, Russia, later USA) and Triple Alliance (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Italy). Sparked by assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand. Ended with Treaty of Versailles 1919 and creation of League of Nations.

Volleyball Fundamental Skills: Floating service (ball travels in zig-zag trajectory), Body turn smash (jumping, turning body in air at jump peak), Blocking (Single, Double, Triple block). Libero players cannot smash or block.
