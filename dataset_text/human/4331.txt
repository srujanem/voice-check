Hi there ! Thanks for your question . The solution to this problem was by using a [ synchronization gear ] ( URL_1 ) . It was basically a cam - like system which only allowed the gun to fire when the propeller shaft was in a certain position . You 'd just make sure that you designed the system so that the gun only fired when the propeller was out of the way ! [ Here ] ( URL_0 ) is a video illustrating the mechanism . Interestingly , this style of design means that your gun can only fire at a rate related to the speed at which your propeller is turning !
