To ELI5 it , it used to be a Scottish rite to declare war , and was picked up by the KKK after that idea was included in a novel romanticising the KKK called The Clansman by Thomas Dixon Jr. It 's used partially in the form of a war declaration , and partially the idea is they 're symbolically " lighting up " a cross as a display of faith , not attempting to destroy it .
