All of that is actually quite detailed and filled with math and experiments and countless observations . Nobody has yet found a way to observe anything about what happened before the big bang ; there 's no scientific reason to believe anything in particular about whatever came before ( if anything did ) . It 's not so much that the big bang must have been the beginning , nothing came before it , and if you do n't believe that you 're a science denialist . It 's more that the big bang theory is our best working model of the history of the observable universe - we have very , very good reason to believe that everything was once all squished together at one point at a very high energy level , and that it all radiated out from there pretty damn quick about 13.8 billion years ago .
