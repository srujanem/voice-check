DJing is really just musical curatorship . That 's all . A DJ does n't perform , he just plays music for people to enjoy , and often will put in some effort to craft a playlist ( sometimes on the fly ) that flows nicely , and have one track segue smoothly into the next . So that 's what some electronic music artists do live : they just DJ their own music . The music is 100 % premade , and they just play them in a particular order with a cool light show . You do n't really go to watch the artist so much as you go to hear the music on a big system with a hyped up crowd . Other artists , recognising that this is a little boring to play and to watch , add a live music element . They 'll actually have a keyboard or something with them , and play parts of the music live . Others will have a sampler loaded with tons of cool sounds , which they 'll throw in to keep things interesting . Still others will bring a remixing aspect in , using turntablism or sampling to remix their own music in real time . It depends 100 % on the artist 's desires and 0 % on skill or musicality - though " artists " like David Guetta are pure DJs , just playing prerecorded sets .
