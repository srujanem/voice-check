In a technical sense , yes , transistors are superior to tubes . However , unlike other types of amplifiers that try to reproduce signals as exactly as possible , guitar amps are * supposed * to distort the input signal in certain ways -- and people really liked the distortion that resulted from tubes not being perfect . Yes , you * can * fake it with enough transistors . Hell , you can throw out the whole guitar and build a really excellent synthesizer out of transistors . In some sense playing a physical instrument * at * * all * is vanity . But some people still like tube amps anyway . It does n't have to be logical .
