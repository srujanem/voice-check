Think of mhz as the speed limit on a highway , and cores as the number of lanes . As the mhz increases , processes in your computer can complete faster . As cores increase , it can do more things simultaneously . Some computer workloads ( single threaded ) only care about mhz . They do n't ship things in multiple cars , so it does n't matter how big the highway is ; only the speed limit . Other workloads can use as many threads as its efficient to do so . Most workloads fall somewhere in the middle .
