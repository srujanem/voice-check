Hi I wrote the routing software for a logistics trucking company so I can say in that particular case most of the work is shipping parts from nearby warehouses to a central factory or premises . In fact a super quick Eli5 answer is " they 're doing practically the same thing as you do when ordering online , just with a difference in scale " If you 're interested a typical day is something like : - received request 3 weeks ago for 40 boxes of stuff from warehouse X , 20 boxes from warehouse Y to be dropped off at the car factory K on this date - assign a driver who sets off on a route scanning boxes as he picks them up - at the logistics HQ we send the info along to car factory K with progress on their order - we get the customer to book in the order and we create a receipt for them to sign
