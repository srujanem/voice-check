Quit India Movement 1942: On August 8, 1942, Congress launched Quit India Movement with Gandhiji's call 'Do or Die'. Top leaders were arrested. Jayaprakash Narayan and the Socialist wing led 'The Freedom Struggle Front'. In Mysore, the 'Esoor Incident' took place where villagers declared 'Swatantra Halli'.
