Coastal Plains & Islands: India has 6,100 km mainland coastline divided into West Coast (Malabar, Canara, Konkan) with major ports like Mumbai, Marmugoa, Cochin, New Mangaluru, and East Coast (Utkal, Coromandel) with Chilka and Pulicat lakes. India has 247 islands: 204 in Bay of Bengal (Andaman and Nicobar, volcanic) and 43 in Arabian Sea (Lakshadweep, coral).
