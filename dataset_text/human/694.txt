Badminton Legends: Prakash Padukone ('Gentle Tiger', 1980 All England Champion, Arjuna/Padmashri), Anup Shridhar (3-time National Champion, Ekalavya 2004), Arvind Bhat (2-time National Champion, Scottish Open), Pullela Gopichand (2001 All England Champion, Khel Ratna), P.V. Sindhu (2016 Rio Olympic Silver), Saina Nehwal (2008 World Super Series, Khel Ratna), Jwala Gutta, Aparna Popat (9 consecutive national titles).

Extremists opposed the partition of Bengal in 1905 as 'Divide and Rule' policy. Protests started mounting throughout the country against the division of Bengal. Swadeshi movement called for a ban on western goods and encouraged Indian goods. In 1911 the British government withdrew the Declaration of Partition of Bengal.
