Average mattress sale : $ 700-$1000 or so . Average gross profit margin : 50 % That means each mattress sold will average about $ 400 or so for the store . Minus about $ 50 for the salesman 's commission , that leaves $ 350 per mattress sale on average . A good store will get 10 - 20 sales per week . That 's $ 3500-$7000 a week in profit to cover things like rent , advertising , customer service , etc .
