As we 've seen , it has indeed been ruled illegal in some jurisdictions , but under state anti - discrimination laws , not the Constitution . The Constitution protects people against state action , not private discrimination ; indeed , without civil rights legislation , clubs WOULD be allowed to discriminate on the basis of race . The Supreme Court has long held that racial / national - origin classifications must pass " strict scrutiny , " the strictest standard of review for laws . Sex - based classifications only have to pass " intermediate scrutiny , " which is higher than " rational basis " ( virtually meaning the law just has to make sense ) but lower than strict scrutiny . A basic rationale is that racial classifications are more likely to be suspect than sex - based ones . ( Whites - only bathrooms are heinous , as they target minorities specifically , but male and female bathrooms are far less controversial . ) While there 's a plausible argument against price discrimination on the basis of sex , there have to be specific laws against it .
