You ca n't know if you are truly vaccinated without have blood serology tests done . Vaccinations work by introducing a threat , then your immune system takes note of what that dude looks like in case he shows up again , effectively your immune system makes a wanted poster . Sometimes a vaccine is introduced to your body but your immune system does n't develop the antibodies for it in response , this is called ' not having converted ' . Most people never have serology done to confirm conversion unless they work in health care or have other , high exposure risk , factors in their life .
