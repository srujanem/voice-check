Theosophical Society: Founded by Madam Blavatsky and Colonel H.S. Olcott based on universal brotherhood and study of ideology. Annie Besant, an Irish lady, gave new vitality to the society in India through lectures on Indian culture. She started the newspaper 'New India' and launched the Home Rule movement in 1916. She served as President of the Congress Session in 1917, contributing immensely to Indian philosophy and the freedom struggle.
