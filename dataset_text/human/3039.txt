Brief history : * Cyprus essentially becomes a British colony in 1878 ( and officially in 1925 ) * the British grant Cyprus independence in 1960 * sectarian violence between the Turkish and Greek Cypriots breaks out soon after , and is an ongoing problem * in 1974 , a coup overthrows the Cypriot government , and seeks to become part of Greece * Turkey invades to protect Turks in Cyprus and to prevent Greek annexation * the Cypriot gov't is restored , Turkey stays anyway * Greek Cypriots are kicked out of the Turkish section , Turkish Cypriots move from the Greek section in a mass migration * in 1983 , Turkish Cyprus declares independence , no one but Turkey recognizes it Since then , not much has happened . The has been a UN mandate condemning the Turkish occupation , but Turkey is a much more important ally to the West than Greece is , especially during the Cold War , so that is as far as it went . In Cyprus , the Turks are in the Turkish section , the Greeks are in the Greek section , and it is probably more peaceful than before Cyprus was divided .
