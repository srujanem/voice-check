They started as a series of somewhat - organized protests in major cities around the time when we were trying to figure out that big healthcare bill . The movement was picked up by GOP members like Dick Armey ( yes that 's really his name ) , who brought in much higher - level organizing and funding . Tea Partiers , in theory , want : - Less taxation - Less government spending - Lowering the debt and deficit . It 's a mystery how they expect to complete item c without either sacrificing items a or b , but that 's the basic idea . Socially , they generally want the same things as the Republican party , but they couch views like " abortion should be illegal " and " gay marriage should be illegal " with " the government should not have the power to make it legal . " The main thing is lower taxes , though . They think that the government as it stands is too big and complicated to be effective . Tea partiers would like the government to return to a scale that most citizens can understand . Usually a Tea Partier is a hard - right Republican , although sometimes the Tea Party will run a candidate if they feel the Republican candidate in an area is not conservative enough / will not lower taxes or cut spending enough .
