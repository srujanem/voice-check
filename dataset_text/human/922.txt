Direct & Indirect Taxes: Direct taxes (Personal Income Tax, Corporate Tax, Stamp Duty) cannot be transferred. Indirect taxes (Central Excise, Service Tax, GST) are transferred to consumers.

Communicable Diseases: Malaria (spread by female Anopheles mosquito carrying Plasmodium), Cholera (Vibrio Cholerae bacteria causing rice-water diarrhea), Tuberculosis (Mycobacterium Tuberculosis affecting lungs, diagnosed by Tuberculin test, prevented by BCG vaccine), Typhoid (Salmonella Typhi from contaminated food/water), Influenza (viral air-spread flu).
