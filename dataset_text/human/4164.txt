The more original and authentic an item is the more desirable it becomes . Just having it with the original package and in undamaged conditions makes up about 60 % of the rarity of an item . The about 30 % are unique flaws / discrepancies and the rest is how much someone is willing to pay . It 's statically rare for someone not collecting to have an item and never open the box to at lest look at it . Human curiosity or using an item is why sealed boxes are normally looked for and worth more .
