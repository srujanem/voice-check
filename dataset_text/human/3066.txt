Theoretically , that should be the case . Bail is designed to basically hold something you value hostage so you have an incentive to be at the trial instead of skipping out . What would be a life - changing amount of money for a poor person ( $ 100k bail would essentially force them to put their house up as collateral , the single most important thing they own in the world ) may not cause a rich person to bat an eye .
