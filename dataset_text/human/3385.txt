Large chains actually mitigate food costs by buying in bulk , pre - packaging meals , and by warehousing and distribution . Most large chains are parts of larger conglomerates ( see also : Dardin ) . Small restaurants , and family owned restaurants do this to an extent . These smaller operations purchase from wholesalers who specialize in servicing the smaller " mom and pops " Better known , higher - end restaurants do not mitigate fluctuation . You often see menus that feature short term , and limited choices . You may also see items listed at " market price " . These item 's prices may vary day to day , and you really do n't know what you are paying until the bill arrives . This does not become an issue , as those dining at such establishments already know they are going to spend a large amount going in . If prices change as a result of a short term issue ( tomato shortage due to e - coli for example ) , restaurants can absorb the higher costs on a short term basis by pushing drink sales ( high margin ) or pushing specials featuring different ingredients .
