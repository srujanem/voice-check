If you have a ( big ) city , with good job prospects , people are going to move to the city . Then they stay . Even more jobs / better prospects appear , so more people from the countryside or other towns move to said city and stay there as well . This goes on and on . Large cities such as Tokyo and Osaka are technically expanding -- taking over suburbs or what was once just farm land . It might be easier to accommodate within the large cities instead of trying to attract populations to more remote areas .
