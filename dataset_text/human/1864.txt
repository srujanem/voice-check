Basically it finds repeating patterns and saves them once and then saves where it repeats . For example a picture that is a black background with a blue stripe in the middle . A computer uncompressed will save that as each pixels color ( p1 = black , p2= black .... P500 = blue , etc . ) a compressed file will save it as " these two sections are black and this section is blue . ( p1 - p499 = black , p500- p700= blue , etc . ) and it saves a lot of space .
