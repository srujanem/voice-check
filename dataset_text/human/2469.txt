It 's in fact easy to create the site itself , i.e. design and code base . It could realistically take like a month of work of one or two people . Making the site successful is much more difficult . That includes lots of advertising , marketing , promotion campaigns , maintaining and upgrading the site for a long time , etc . Plus the idea should be good enough so people would actually want to use it . For example , Google+ was very well implemented , but nobody wanted to use it , because it was nothing new compared to Facebook .
