Simon Commission 1927: The British Government appointed Simon Commission without a single Indian member. Congress boycotted it with slogans 'Simon, Go back'. Lala Lajpat Rai died after being injured in a police lathi charge during a protest against Simon Commission.

Horticulture & Floriculture: Horticulture (fruits & vegetables) drives the 'Golden Revolution'. Floriculture grows commercial flowers like rose, jasmine, and orchids. Terminal Markets link producing areas directly to buyers.
