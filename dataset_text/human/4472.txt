It depends on the size of the kitchen . In most restaurants the Chef actually cooks along with the other cooks . Only in really large really fancy kitchens does the head chef not cook . In that case he acts as the manager and quaility control of the kitchen during service . Before service they will usually help with the prep and again act as manager . Apart from that they are the ones that come up with menu , whether that is the standard menu , daily specials or special occasion menus .
