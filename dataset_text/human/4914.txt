" I " is a subject and " me " is an object . The subject is the one performing the action and the object is the one on whom the action is being performed . So if you were to say " Chuck and I went to Taco Bell , " " I " is a subject . * I * went . You can test it out by removing Chuck . " I went to Taco Bell " or " Me went to Taco Bell . " The first one sounds right , and it is . But if you were to say " The nice waitress at Taco Bell gave extra tacos to Chuck and me , " " me " is the object . the tacos were given * to * me . You can test this the same way . " The waitress gave tacos to me " or " the waitress gave tacos to I. " If it sounds right without Chuck , it 's probably right with Chuck .
