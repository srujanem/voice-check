There are lots of types of wind , but generally they go something like this . Imagine a hot air balloon with lots of rope hanging out the side , and people are holding onto the end of the ropes . When the balloon turns on and begins to rise , it pulls on the ropes and people get pulled in too . Air does this too . The sun makes the land really warm , which makes the air right over the land really hot too . This air acts like a hot air balloon , which rises . This pulls in air from all around the place . When the air rushes in , you get wind ! ( I know wind really has to do with pressure and warm / cold fronts , but hey , this is for 5 year olds )
