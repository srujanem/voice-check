If your antivirus software is set to delete infected files , they are generally gone forever . If your antivirus is wrong in what it thought was a virus and it 's set to delete a file , the file you wanted is gone forever . If your antivirus is set to quarantine files it suspects as being viruses , they are held in a storage location where they generally ca n't interact with your computer . You as the user can then make the final call on whether to delete these or not . Generally you can empty your quarantine without issue although best practice would be to review your quarantine prior to emptying it . My experience shows it 's rare a file I have already had gets mis - identified as a virus . Generally it 's the files I newly obtain or am working on currently . For that reason I generally do n't check my quarantine and just empty it occasionally . If I know something is missing I will look for it in quarantine though . I also take good backups of my systems in case something really does go missing and I accidentally wipe it out of quarantine because I was too lazy to read everything in the quarantine in detail .
