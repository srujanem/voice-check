Back when movie palaces would have monster screens the screens would have to be curved so everybody could see the entire screen from across the theater . The Uptown Cinema in Washington , DC still has a monster screen like this , and so do real IMAX theaters . So if you have a huge TV in a wide room the same principle applies . Of course , the curved TVs on the market now are n’t big enough and have too much curvature for this to work well , so it ’s just a gimmick .
