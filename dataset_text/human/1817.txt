If a child marriage takes place, the people who initiated the marriage, and those who conducted or motivated the marriage are liable for imprisonment of two year and a fine of rupees one lakh. As per provisions of this law under column 9, 10 and11; the priest, the photographer, videographer, cook, provider of Shamiyana services, musicians, vehicle drivers and whoever took part in the marriage are liable for punishment.
