Dondiya Wagh (1800): Dondiya was born in a Maratha family of Chennagiri. He was called the Wagh, the tiger, due to his bravery. He started his career as a cavalry soldier in Hyder Ali's army and grew to military general. The British released him from prison after the Fourth Anglo-Mysore War. He built a small army with unhappy soldiers of Tippu's army and captured Bidanoor and Shivamogga forts. Lord Wellesley tried to check this rebellion. When he was caught in between Maratha Army and Nizam's army, the British attacked him near Yapalaperavi and killed him at Konagal.
