The barter system relies primarily on a subjective ( individual ) basis of value , for example , you might think your cow is worth 2 of my pigs + 3 of my chickens , however I might think your cow is only worth 1 pig and 3 chickens . This leads to a long and exhausting process of bargaining until individuals finally come to an agreement and an exchange is made , usually at a compromise . Not to mention the complications of making an exchange if you do n't need chickens and instead need goats , which I do n't have . If everyone has money , everyone can trade . Our current system creates a universal ( objective ) basis of value , so if you bought your cow for $ 100 I know all I need to give you for it is $ 100 .
