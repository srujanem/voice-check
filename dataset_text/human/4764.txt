LY5 : Your email is meant to be read by you . That means you want to make it so other people ca n't read it , but you need to leave a way for you to get in . Security is what we call the efforts the people that make email software go to to ensure only you get to read your mail , even if other people want to . Hackers find ways to overcome the security offered for a particular piece of software ( for example ) . A lot of the time this is nothing more than something called social engineering , or fooling someone into giving them a way in . Other times it means the hacker is smart enough to understand the security in place , and slip by it . Another means of hacking is to use heavy processing power to go through millions or billions of password possibilities until you find the right one . There is definitely no one way to hack a system , the best ones are usually the ones that have n't been done successfully before , because those are normally repaired by the people that make the security .
