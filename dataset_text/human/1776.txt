This kind of behavior is called ‘collective behaviour’. Collective behaviour is an integral part of our social life. The thoughts, feelings and behavior of a large number of disorganized people coming together accidentally constitute collective behaviour. Human behaviour coming under the scope of collective behavior includes mobs, rumours, propaganda, public opinion, revolution, social movements etc. Some of such behavioral patterns are described underneath: Mobs: ‘Assembly of people around a common interest without any expectation or planning is called a mob”.
