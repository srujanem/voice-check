You borrow a Nintendo 64 from a buddy the first day it comes out , and promise to return it as soon as possible . You sell it immediately to a kid on your street , who gives you a ton of cash plus some pogs because of how new and awesome the system is . A year later , you trade those pogs for a brand new Nintendo , and give that back to your buddy . The idea is that you sell ' borrowed ' stocks now , and then buy the stocks to pay back the loan when the stock is at a lower value .
