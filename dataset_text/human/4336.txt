Amazon is a low - margin high - volume business , which means they need a certain threshold of sales to break even . The first quarter of the year is always slow for consumer spending ( people tend to shop conservatively after the holidays ) so Amazon is expecting to recoup the losses in Q2 and Q4 If you make a glass of lemonade for 50 cents and sell it for a dollar , then you 're making a profit after your first sale . BUT , if you have to pay $ 10 / month for your business license ( or warehouse staff weather they 're working or not , or inspection fees , or bank fees or whatever else businesses have to do to make things happen every day ) then you need to sell 20 glasses of lemonade each month to break even . Then your 21st glass makes a profit . If you only sell 19 glasses in December , that 's OK , because you 'll probably sell 50 glasses in August . December is not lemonade season , August is . Amazon works the same way . They make a very small margin on each sale , and they need a LOT of sales to cover their fixed costs . In slow quarters they do n't quite cover their costs fixed costs so they lose money , but they make it up ( and then some ) during the holiday rush . January is not shopping season , December is .
