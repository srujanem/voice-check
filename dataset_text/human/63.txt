Illiteracy & Corruption: Illiteracy is tackled through National Literacy Mission (1988), Sarva Siksha Abhiyan (2001), Right to Education Act 2009 (Article 21A), and Samagra Shiksha. Corruption means inducement to do wrong by bribery or unlawful means. Measures to control corruption include Lokpal, Lokayukta (enacted in Karnataka in 1986), Central Vigilance Commission (CVC), Right to Information Act 2005 (RTI), Sakala, and Janaspandana.
