Tippu Sultan felt that the British were the major obstacle for his policy of expansion. He considered it as his duty to drive the British out of India. He waged war against the British throughout his seventeen years of rule. He knew that hurting the business interest of the British would weaken the political strength of the British. The soldiers of Mysore Army were capable of using modern weapons and doing drills like the European armies. Hyder Ali and Tippu Sultan used missiles during the war for the first time in India. They brought tobacco, sandalwood and others under the state monopoly.
