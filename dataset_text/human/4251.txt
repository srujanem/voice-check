Each eye creates a slightly different image because they are in different places . The overlay of these two images creates the illusion of depth and allows us to know how far things are away from us . This is called stereoscopic vision . In other animals , like deer , the eyes are on the sides of the head . This restricts the use of stereoscopic vision but gives the animals greater peripheral vision , allowing them to see predators . This is sufficient for animals to see depth and any more eyes would not be worth the energy use and risk that they require . Eyes are easily damaged which can lead to infection and they are complex and thus require a large amount of energy to create and maintain from a purely mechanical standpoint . If this mutation ever developed there was no evolutionary advantage for those creatures that manifested a third eye ( or the early mutation : a light sensitive bump ) and so they were not able to breed and pass on this mutation in significant enough numbers to change the population . Spiders and Insects have multiple and/or compound eyes because they evolved under different circumstances .
