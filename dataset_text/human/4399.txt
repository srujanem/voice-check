When I was travelling a lot for my job taking 6 - 12 month projects places like RAC were great . My company ( a major consulting firm with hundreds of thousands of employees ) would pay for short term leases when we had a long - term project . Even if I got a ' furnished ' apartment I 'd still need things like a TV and anything else the appartment did n't have . It never made sense to buy an appliance I already had at home . I did n't need duplicates , I 'd never pay to ship yet another TV back home when the project ended , and I did n't want to literally just throw a TV away . So rentals made a ton of sense , both economically as well as from a convenience perspective .
