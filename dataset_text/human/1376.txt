The common way that a lot of people think of a sail is as if it were a big parachute . With this conceptualization you have a big sheet of cloth and the wind pushes it from behind . This kind of sailboat can only go as fast as the wind pushes it . However , that 's the wrong mental picture . The goal of a sail is actually to get the wind to move along the sail , like the air moves over the wing of an aircraft . Just as an aircraft gets lift from its wings a sailboat gets " lift " from its sail , but that " lift " is directed forward instead of up . This approach does not limit the speed of the boat to the speed of the wind . It even allows the boat to sail nearly directly upwind .
