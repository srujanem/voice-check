In most American schools , it is frowned upon . Ask most administrators ; they would not give good marks to a teacher who yells at their class all the time . It 's generally a sign of a teacher who has lost control of their classroom and is yelling out of frustration or as a last resort . It also depends what you mean by " yelling . " Speaking in a controlled , firm tone every once in a while when students misbehave is n't what I 'd consider yelling . Screaming at a class all the time is yelling and rarely ever works to control an unruly class .
