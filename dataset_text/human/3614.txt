It is because the way Greenland is portrayed . As you might know , the world is spherical . Some say it is smoother than a biljart ball . But if you want to make a flat square , you are going to have a bad time . What a lot of mapmakers do : Squeeze everything around the equator a little bit and then make the northern parts a lot bigger . They expand on the north and south and make the middle seem smaller , making a sphere a cube .
