You have a certain number of fat cells . Even when you lose fat , you keep the same number of fat cells . When you gain weight in the form of fat , what 's happening is that your body is storing energy ( in the form of triglyceride or triglycerol , ca n't remember which ) inside your fat cells . When fat is " liberated " from your fat cells , a process called lipolysis will turn that triglyceride into glycerol and three free fat acids ( FFAs ) . The FFAs enter the blood stream and make their way to the muscles . The muscles say , " ermagerd , gimme " and hand the FFAs off to their mitochondria . A chemical process occurs that splits up the FFAs super long chains of carbon atoms , turning it into something called Acetyl - CoA. This molecule can be used to make adenosine triphosphate ( ATP ) , which is what powers a cell . During all of these processes , the fatty acid is oxidized ( I forget how many times ) . Fatty acid is mostly made of carbon . Carbon + oxygen = CO2 . So one of the ways you get rid of the fat you 've used is to breathe out .
