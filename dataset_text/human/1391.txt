Taxes are not meant to be used as a tool of social engineering , or wielded to punish specific social class or group . Taxes are a means of raising the money needed to run our current government . Most large economies already have very progressive tax rates already , see for example URL_0 . What is the " right " steepness of the marginal tax rate curve ? If you make it too steep you remove the incentive to work and invest in yourself . Note that tax rates increase sharply at relatively low levels of income . A family of two doctors could easily earn 250 - 350k / year . In order to become a doctor you have to spend 10 + years in various schools and underpaid apprenticeship programs and incurred hundreds of thousands in student loans . Is there anything unfair about doctors earning that much , and should we punish them to restore fairness ? Some argue for a completely flat tax rate curve to simplify the process and remove distortionary influences . Do you know who lobbies against this - Intuit and the other tax preparation companies . Endless tinkering with the tax code is a way for politicians to secure favor with some interest group , whether it be wealthy businesses or individuals or unions and the poor . Needless to say this kind of tinkering is enormously wasteful .
