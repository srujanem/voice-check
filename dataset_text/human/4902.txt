I 'm no expert on this subject , but here 's what I 've gathered . A company called Nielsen Media Research is responsible for tracking what programs people are watching . Basically , Nielsen gets 5000 volunteer households to be a representative sample of the entire U.S. Nielsen then uses meters to track when those households have their TVs on and what programs they are tuned in to . They use a computer to send that viewing info to Nielsen 's central computer every night . Using this info , Nielsen is able to determine what channels and what programs are getting the most viewers . Nielsen then makes money by releasing this info to television networks , cable companies , and advertisers . This explanation is oversimplified , but I think it 's the gist of what happens .
