Vande Mataram: National Song composed by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee in Bengali/Sanskrit, praising motherland's green fields, streams, moonlight, strength, and divine beauty.

Indian National Congress: Right from the time of Raja Ram Mohan Roy, the minds of middle class people of India were getting prepared to face new challenges. Organizations like 'The Hindu Mela', 'The East India Association', 'Poona Sarvajanik Sabha' and 'The Indian Association' were prominent.
