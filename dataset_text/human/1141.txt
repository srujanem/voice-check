Basically , our distant ancestors were able to survive and have children , despite lacking super - smell powers . Their children were able to reproduce as well . Our smell is good enough that we can tell when food is spoiled rotten , and when to leave a dangerous area . Humans whose senses of smell could n't even do that , eventually died off from poisoning . There could be some humans with supremely good olfactory senses , and their children may inherit them too . But there is still no reason for those of us with lower smell to be unable to reproduce .
