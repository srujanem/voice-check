I have been to UU churches . The local one here focuses on inclusion , pursuing knowledge and serving the community . I would say the majority of the congregation is ex - Catholic . According to people I know there , " Our dogma is there is no dogma . " What I have found inviting is the openness to new ideas and other ways of thinking and living . I have found more intelligent , thoughtful conversation there than almost anywhere else in my town . They have pagans , Buddhists , agnostics and atheists in their church . Probably others too , I would guess .
