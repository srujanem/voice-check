Hockey Legends & Records: Major Dhyan Chand ('Wizard of Hockey', 3 Olympic golds 1928, 1932, 1936, Padmabhushan, Aug 29 National Sports Day), Ajit Pal Singh (1975 World Cup winning captain), Leslie Claudius (3 golds 1 silver Guinness record), Dhanraj Pillai (4 Olympics, 1999 Khel Ratna), Dr. M.P. Ganesh, Mamta Kharab, Suraj Latha Devi, Helen Mary Innocent.

Dadabhai Naoroji explained about the transfer of Indian wealth to England and called it as Drain Theory. He maintained that encouraging imports and reducing exports created a situation unfavorable to India, leading to drain of wealth. The Age of Moderates is also called the Age of Generous Nationalism.
