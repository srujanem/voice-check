Tribal Movements: The Santala tribal revolt in Bengal and Orissa hillocks fought against permanent landlords and money-lenders. Munda movement led by Birsa Munda and Halagali Hunters rebellion in Karnataka were prominent tribal uprisings.

GIS, GPS & Remote Sensing: GIS (Canada 1960) analyzes spatial earth data. GPS (US satellites, India's Navik system) provides precise location coordinates. Remote sensing gathers satellite imagery for natural disaster management.
