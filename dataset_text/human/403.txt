It is for water pressure , so when you turn on a faucet located below the tower , water comes out . It 's also important for the entire water system to be pressurized so that when pipes leak , it 's mostly clean water leaking out and not dirty water leaking in . There is a little bit of a backup component in that water will still flow and the pipes will remain pressurized in the event of a power outage until water in the tower is depleted .
