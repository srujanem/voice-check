> any human now can be as powerful as other humans because of things like guns and machines Evolution is n't about power , it 's about relative fitness and genetic drift . Relative fitness is simply the number of * viable * offspring you produce compared to someone else . There are lots of ways to change that that do n't involve power or strength . In fact , in many cases , being very sociable and nice can increase your fitness a lot more than being powerful and war - like . While technology generally allows us to change our environment ( or at least protect us from it ) meaning we do n't have to biologically adapt to different environments , there are still plenty of selective pressures , such as diseases , that we can adapt to . Genetic drift is the random changes in [ allele ] ( URL_0 ) frequencies that occur because our population size is not infinite . This means that isolated populations can still change and be genetically different from other populations . While there are fewer and fewer isolated populations , this kind of thing can still occur .
