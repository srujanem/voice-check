Legend has it that coins became round because back in the day , when they were just any old shape , their worth was determined by weight . You could actually split a coin in half and use the two halves as currency ( at half value each ) . But people realized that if you clipped a tiny bit off the edge of the coin , you could keep a bit of the metal while keeping the weight unaffected , eventually making your own currency . As soon as this was discovered , coins were fashioned round so that people could n't do this anymore .
