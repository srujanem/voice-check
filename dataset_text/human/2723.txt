I think the biggest point out of the articles that answers your question is that vanilla came to the U.S. most likely as an ice cream flavoring . It was prized in ice cream and other foods for both its scent and flavor , and because it was considered a delicate and exotic flavor / scent , it was used in moderation . Even today , vanilla ice cream made with real vanilla will be white , because vanilla is incredibly strong and the amount that lends a good level of flavor / scent is not nearly enough to color the otherwise white ice cream significantly .
