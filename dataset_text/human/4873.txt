Monsanto is a huge company , that started with mainly gas and chemical production . I 'd say they are most notably recognized for being the largest producer of Agent Orange during the Vietnam war era , producers of the weed killer Roundup , and the first to genetically modify a plant cell . Part of the controversy that surrounds the company deals with the fact the genetically modified seeds they produce are patented and the company is notorious for suing farmers that are found to be growing any plants that contain traces of monsanto seeds ... even if the wind or other natural forces pushed the seeds onto the farmers land . Another source of frustration comes from the fact that several FDA officials have been major players in the Monsanto organization and have been accused of favoring the company . Some good sources referencing why people have issues with the company are " Food Inc. " and " The World According to Monsanto "
