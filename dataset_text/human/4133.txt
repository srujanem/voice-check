Short answered , you lose it by breathing out more carbon dioxide . When you breath in , you are inhaling nitrogen and oxygen . When you breath out you exhale nitrogen and carbon - dioxide . Carbon - dioxide is heavier than oxygen . When you exercise your body requires more oxygen to feed your muscles to keep them performing their function .
