Iron Ore & Manganese: Iron ore types: Magnetite, Hematite, Limonite, Siderite (found in Singhbhum, Kudremukh, Hospet). Manganese types: Pyrolusite, Psilomelane (used in steel manufacturing). Bauxite is the ore of Aluminium ('Wonder Metal').

Vande Mataram: National Song composed by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee in Bengali/Sanskrit, praising motherland's green fields, streams, moonlight, strength, and divine beauty.
