Airplanes register themselves through flight plans announcing who and what they are . Conceptually those involved could lie ( and transponders faked ) and then the issue would be visual verification . But if a supposedly commercial airline diverted from its flight plan these days without a good reason , chances are a fighter would be scrambled to check on it directly ( for fear of hijackers ) . So either they get spotted when they show up at the airport or when they move away from their expected path . Also it would require the collusion of civilian air authorities who would never be trusted again .
