Rebellion of Kittur – Brave Queen Chennamma (1824): Denying the right of adopted children over the throne was a rule followed in Kittur. After the death of her husband Mallasarja and son Shivalingarudra Sarja, Chennamma adopted a boy named Shivalingappa and started ruling Kittur as queen regent. Thackeray, Collector of Dharwad, attempted to take over Kittur under the Doctrine of Lapse policy. In the battle, Thackeray was shot dead. Later the British attacked Kittur again under Colonel Deak. Chennamma was captured and imprisoned at Bylahongala fort where she passed away.
