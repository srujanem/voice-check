No one really know but some things are certain . The US has been the safest currency in the world . We guarantee our debt ( says so in the 14th Amendment ) . This has been a boon to the US as many other currencies peg themselves to ours . We will lose that advantage overnight . The US would immediately lose its AAA credit rating . The effect of this is to make money more expensive ( interest rates will rise ) . If you want a loan it will cost you more to get it . That may not seem like a very big deal but it is huge . The cost of everything will get more expensive as the effects of that trickle into the economy . In a country already teetering on the edge of recession this will almost certainly drag it back down . Further , the government will start defaulting on payments . It is akin to you having $ 1000 in the bank and having to pay $ 1500 of bills this month . Ordinarily you would have the option of borrowing $ 500 to make good on your debts . If you refuse to borrow ( raise the debt ceiling ) something has to give and will not get paid . What wo n't get paid ? Your guess is as good as anyone else 's . Who makes the decision of what to pay and not to pay is also a question mark . Probably the president but technically this is the job of congress . If they give no direction then it has to be the president . Except he is not allowed to make those calls legally . Say hello to a constitutional crisis . If I recall correctly the US will default on 40 % of its payments . In addition to money becoming more expensive huge swaths of the economy will find they are not getting the money they are due . They can not make payroll , can not fund operations ... you name it . Who takes a bite out of the shit pie is anyone 's guess . The upshot will be a recession that makes the last one look tame . It will almost certainly crash the global economy as well and lead to a global recession . Yes , be afraid .
