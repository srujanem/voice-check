Say you have $ 100,000 left on your mortgage and you sell it for $ 150,000 , you simply pay the remaining $ 100,000 that 's left to the bank , and keep the remaining $ 50,000 . Whoever buys the house from you would have a new mortgage of their own . If your mortgage is worth MORE than the house ( You have $ 200,000 left to pay on a house that is only worth $ 150,000 ) , you have to receive permission from the bank , and you basically give them all the money from the sale and they forgive the remaining $ 50,000 . It 's called a short sale , and is somewhat uncommon because its not very desirable . The owners make no money from selling their home , and the bank loses money on the loan ... but the alternative is usually the owners losing their house to foreclosure .
