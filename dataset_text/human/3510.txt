If you think about all the muscles / parts involved in generating speech ( lips , tongue , glottis , vocal chords , etc . ) , the consonant ' m ' and the vowel ' a ' are far less complex to produce relative to other sounds . The ' ah ' sound is close to the default resting position of the speech - producing muscles / parts in the mouth , and the ' m ' sound is just the closing of the mouth . source : was a baby .
