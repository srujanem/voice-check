Netaji escaped from house arrest to Germany and Japan, taking leadership of the Indian National Army (INA) formed by Rashbehari Bose and Captain Mohan Singh. He gave the call 'Delhi Chalo' and 'Give me blood, I will give you freedom'. Captain Lakshmi commanded the Jhansi Regiment.
