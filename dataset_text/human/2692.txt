Multiplication is repeated additions . So A * B means add A to itself B times . If you have -A * B , you are saying to add -A to itself B times . Because adding a negative to a negative only makes the sum more negative ( further below zero ) , you will get a negative number . If you have A * -B , you are saying to take the ~~inverse~~ opposite of A and add THAT number to itself B number of times . The ~~inverse~~ opposite of A is -A , so A * -B can be rewritten -A * B. So , if you have -A * -B , you are saying to take the ~~inverse~~ opposite of -A and add it to itself B times . Since the ~~inverse~~ opposite of -A is A , you effectively get A * B. And so -1 * -1 means 1 * 1 , which is 1 . Edit : Opposite not inverse .
