Second Anglo-Mysore War (1780-1784): The British attempted to break the 'Madras Treaty'. When Madhava Rao attacked Srirangapattana with the Maratha army, Hyder Ali expected the British to support him as per the Madras Treaty. But the British rejected his request. Mahe, the French colony was under the hold of Hyder Ali. The British attacked Mahe and captured it. This became the reason for the Second Anglo-Mysore War. Hyder Ali captured Kanchipuram and Arcot. In 1781 Hyder Ali was defeated in a battle held in Porto Nova. Hyder Ali died due to illness during the war in 1782. The war was led by his son Tippu Sultan, ending with the 'Treaty of Mangalore' in 1784.
