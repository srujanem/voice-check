Freud talked to a number of women with mental illnesses , and learned that many of them described being sexually abused by their fathers . He hypothesized that the sexual abuse was the cause of the mental illness . But if that were true , it must mean that truly shocking numbers of respectable Victorian men , people who were successful and respected members of the community , were routinely raping their daughters . And that obviously could n't be true ... right ? So he revised his ideas , and came up with the idea that girls desire their fathers ' penises ... and that 's why so many of his patients made up such awful stories about their poor innocent fathers .
