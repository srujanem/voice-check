In America , homeless children with loving families who can care for them live in homeless shelters . Homeless children without loving families who can care for them are taken into the care of the state , and usually sent to live with foster parents . A child begging on the street like that in America would likely be reported to the police , who would investigate and either return her to her parents for a stern talking - to or take her to Child Protective Services to be cared for , depending on which was appropriate .
