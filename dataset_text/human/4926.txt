For a long time since they started , Twitter did not focus on making any money . Like any other company or industry , websites too need money to run and for Twitter it comes from people known as venture capitalists ( those who put some money and become part - owners of the company ) . But unlike other companies like say , a car maker or fruit seller , these venture capitalists determine the value of internet companies by how big the company is rather than how much money it makes . That is , if Twitter has 100 million people who use it , it 's value is much higher than when it had only 10 million users . Because the value of the company ( as seen by these venture capitalists ) was determined by the number of users the service had , the original owners of Twitter kept focusing on increasing the number of users for a long time . For all this while ( I think till mid 2010 ) , Twitter had zero dollars coming to the company as revenue from users . It kept running on money put in by these venture capitalists . Since then , the website has made a few changes . They have advertisers who take up space on certain sections of the website and Twitter makes money from it . For example , there is an area called ' Trending topics ' . One of the 10 in that list is an advertiser and is called a ' Promoted tweet ' , Also , recently , Twitter started displaying a ' promoted tweet ' to users . That is , a user normally sees only those tweets ( messages posted by twitter users ) from those users they follow . But now , one of the displayed message in the list will be from an advertiser . These are some ways they have started making money .
