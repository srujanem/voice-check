Despite the common misconception , exercise actually has very little to do with your weight . * Massive * amounts of exercise can have significant changes of course , but by far the biggest factor on what you weigh is what you eat . Or rather , how much you eat . So even if he exercises very little ( or not at all ) , if he also eats very little , he wo n't gain weight . By the way , the next time you 're hung up about being overweight , do n't think spending forty minutes on the treadmill every day is going to get you out of your slump . You need to eat right . And not fad diets . Real , actual good diets .
