Many are really big , and have a lot of stuff . Enough to send out a few million kg every hundred years for a few million years ( Halley 's comet weighs 220,000,000,000,000 kg , and used to weigh much more ) . Some have run out . And others have n't been comets for very long , and have n't had time to run out of stuff ; they can be knocked into elliptical orbits by collisions or gravitational attraction and become comets .
