When wine is fermented , the alcohols produced are n't all ethanol , which is the " alcohol " we drink . Small amounts of other chemicals , mostly different alcohols that are somewhat more toxic than ethanol , are produced . They also can give the drink an off flavor . The expensive brands are made with higher - quality ingredients and optimized for better flavor - which means getting rid of most of those non - ethanol alcohols . They taste cleaner , and the removal of the junky alcohols means you 're a little less likely to get a hangover . This can be done through distilling , and for wine and beer , taking more care during the brewing / vinting process . Most of the things that contribute to hangovers - alcohol breaking down in your body , dehydration , etc . are the same regardless of which alcohol you drink . But the purity varies and it does matter unless you 're only drinking a small amount . And the sugar content can be higher in cheap stuff , as jabraunlin said , to mask the chemical - y flavor .
