Patents were originally devised to encourage innovation . People and companies are more willing to put the time , effort and money into creating or improving if they know that they will get rewarded for it . Patents ensure that no one can swoop in and steal an idea and all of the resulting profits . That being said , you run into a similar problem if patents were endless - people would stop innovating because the item they are trying to innovate is still owned in exclusivity by someone else . Moreso , there is little incentive for an existing patent holder to improve on a product because they know that no one will be able to create something similar enough to compete .
