The two parties have changed over the years , and both parties have a lot of different kinds of people with different beliefs who call themselves Republicans and Democrats . At this point in history , GENERALLY speaking- * * Republicans * * : * Tend to be more conservative . * Support less restrictions on businesses and as much freedom in the marketplace as possible . * Tend to oppose government intervention or attempts to control the markets . * Support attempts to promote certain morals , values , and religious ideas in the government and society . * Believe in a strict interpretation of the Constitution . * Believe in the power of individual states over the federal government . * * Democrats * * : * Tend to be more liberal . * Support a strong federal government . * Believe society should support its poor , sick , and elderly through government programs and wealth redistribution . * Promote civic freedoms for individuals . * Promote alternative energy . * Tend to be more willing to use government to intervene in marketplaces and/or regulate business practices . * Support a more open interpretation of the Constitution .
