I 'm going to answer your bonus question about blood instead of the original question . Blood is has four major components in people : White blood cells ( called leukocytes ) , red blood cells ( called erythrocytes ) , platelets ( called thrombocytes ) , and plasma . White blood cells are one of your body 's major lines of defense . They literally swallow and destroy things like bacteria , old cells , cancer cells , etc . White blood cells are made by stem cells in your bone marrow . Red blood cells are the cells that carry oxygen from your lungs to the body , and bring carbon dioxide from the body to the lungs for exhalation . These cells are also made by stem cells in your bone marrow . Platelets are responsible for making your blood clot . They 're super sticky , so as they start to pile up on top of each other , they can close up a bleed and allow the tissue to regrow before being broken down or falling off . A scab is a classic example . These guys are made by a cell called the megakaryocyte ( also found in bone marrow , but not from the same cells the other blood cells are made from ) . Plasma is mostly water , but it also has some proteins , sugars , and minerals floating around in it . This is made mostly by you drinking water every day . All these parts are put together inside of your bones . Blood flows to the parts of your bones with blood vessels in them . The cells made in the marrow seep out into this blood , and the old , dying , and broken cells get picked up by the liver and broken down .
