> Can the president be charged with a crime ? In US - style governments , a few government offices protect their holders from criminal prosecution unless the legislature approves . This is to prevent police chiefs from manipulating politics . Typically judges , chief executives ( president or governor ) , and legislators are protected . So are ordinary citizens , but only when they 're about to cast a vote . The only authority to prosecute the President for a federal crime is held by Congress . This is because federal laws are enforced by the executive branch and are subject to the President 's authority . There 's nothing that says a President must arrest themself . Presumably states could try to arrest the President , but the supremacy clause would allow them to release themself . Probably . This has never been tested , as far as I know .
