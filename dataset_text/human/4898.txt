For many years people have disputed whether or not blue balls existed , but today , it 's really not disputed , they do exist . If someone is sexually aroused for a prolonged period of time , vasocongestion ( swelling due to increased blood flow ) can occur in the testicles , leading to pain or discomfort . EDIT : In case you want more proof , there was a case report done in 2000 acknowledging the existence of blue balls in the Journal of the American Academy of Pediatrics .
