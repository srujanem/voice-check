Unions use the collective power of employees to negotiate things like salary and working conditions . They get thier power by having all union members working together , meaning if they do not get what they want they can all not show up to work , effectively temporarily shutting down a business ( the downside for them is they do nt get paid while not showing up to work ) . Unions also often offer benefits such as legal services to its members and often charge a fee for being part of a union . A good and bad union are very subjective , but most would agree what makes a union bad is when the power of a union makes the cost of mainting and obtaining an employee is too expensive for a business to stay in business .
