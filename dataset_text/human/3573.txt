One of the things offered by modern operating systems is called an instruction set . This is basically a way for a programer to use a tool created by the OS programers to accomplish a task that might be common across several programs . Basically it 's preventing the same work being done over and over by different gaming companies . A long while ago MS noticed that video game programers were struggling with communicating to the graphics cards of the time , and there was a lot of duplicate work being done . So MS invented a set of instructions that video card manufacturers can get their cards to respond to . Basically getting all video cards to speak MS 's language . That language became known as Direct X. Direct X is a tool used by game programers to allow the game to talk to the video card . It is proprietary to MS and is one of the reasons that windows has maintained it 's dominance in PC gaming . The new Windows will come with a new Direct X ( version 12 ) . Whether or not there are actual performance improvements remains to be seen .
