The root of the issue is : * * the engine uses energy to pull air through the filter . * * A dirty filter means more resistance which means more energy must be used to pull the air through , reducing the efficiency , and raising fuel usage for the same power output at the same speed . This happens during the downward " intake " stroke , one of the 3 non - power - generating strokes a 4 - stroke motor makes each cycle . Reducing the energy needed for sucking in , and pushing out , via a less restrictive intake and exhaust is an easy way to increase performance AND efficiency . That 's why these are two of the most common modifications you 'll see . The air / fuel mixture has almost nothing to do with it , as almost all cars on the road today are fuel injected , and the system automatically compensates fuel delivery based on the air being taken in .
