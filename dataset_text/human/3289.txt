There 's an interesting line of evidence that folks who crunch a lot of data have discovered in their research that applies to a whole host of things from internet dating profiles to movies and automobiles ; if you want to market something to as many people as possible you ca n't be too eccentric . When people have a large number of choices they want to narrow it down , so they 'll use the tiniest things as a reason to discard a choice ; the last thing you want to do is give anyone a reason to discard your option offhand , and anything that really stands out could be that reason . The investment of capital in producing a new vehicle for market is much higher now than it was in the past , as is evidenced by the relatively small number of automobile manufacturers now as compared to 40 years ago , and every model they turn out has to be a hit or they 'll suffer a huge loss . Thus only the companies that focus exclusively on limited clienteles , Bentley , Ferrari and so on , can afford to get away with a really crazy car anymore .
