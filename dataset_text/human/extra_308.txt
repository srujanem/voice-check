Oils and dirt on hands , for the most part , ca n't dissolve in water . The soap molecule consists of a long chain of carbon atoms that likes to dissolve oils and dirt , and at one end it has a piece that likes to dissolve in water and carries the long , " hydrophobic " piece with it , along with any oils and dirt that it has dissolved . In scientific terms , the soap molecule is " amphipathic . " That means that it has a hydrophobic part and a hydrophilic ( " likes water " ) part .
