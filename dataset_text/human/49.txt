Rebellion of Amara Sullya (1835-37): This rebellion was basically a farmers' rebellion in coastal Karnataka and Kodagu regions. The British dethroned the ruler of Kodagu, Chikkaveerarajendra in 1834. A farmer named Puttabasappa presented himself as Swami Aparampara and took over leadership of the rebellion. Puttabasappa declared that tax on tobacco and salt would be withdrawn. The rebels marched towards Mangalore and looted the Bantwal treasury. The British army captured Puttabasappa and his associates, hanging them to death.
