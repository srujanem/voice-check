Imagine a piece of graph paper . It 's so big that there is no end to it - it goes on forever and ever . Unfortunately , we can see only part of it , and we will never see anything beyond what we can currently see . On the graph paper , there are a bunch of dots spread evenly out . The dots represent " stuff " . Over time , dots close to each other come together and form bigger dots , but if you look at the paper from far enough away , things still look pretty well evenly spread out . Over time , the lines on the graph paper are getting farther apart from each other . The stuff - dots are mostly staying within their boxes on the paper , so the dots are also moving apart from each other , except for the ones that are close enough to " pull " each other together . Given enough time , the dots will be so far away from each other that you could stand on one dot and not see another , and none will be able to pull each other together any more . Do n't worry , that 's a really long time from now . Since the lines are getting farther apart as we go into the future , that means that if you go backwards in time , the lines get closer together . At the beginning of time , all the lines and all the dots were touching , but remember that the paper is still so big it went on forever and ever . The point at which the lines started moving apart was " the big bang " , and that was the start of the Universe .
