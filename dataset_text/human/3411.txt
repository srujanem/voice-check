Physics . When you exhale the air is pretty well the same pressure as atmospheric pressure , meaning the heat transferred into your breath by your lungs is maintained . When you ' blow ' air out it comes out at a higher pressure . All gases cool when they expand so your breath quickly cools down . Try ' blowing ' on your hand right up against your lips , you will find that your breath is still warm . This is because it has n't had time to expand and cool .
