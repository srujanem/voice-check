Poverty, Profiteering & Smuggling: Poverty is the condition where basic necessities of life cannot be met, addressed through MGNREGA, PMGY, NRLM, and NFSA. Profiteering is earning excess profit through unethical business methods like hoarding and black marketing. Smuggling is secretly bringing or sending goods without paying duties, checked through import substitution, EXIM policy, and coastal vigilance.
