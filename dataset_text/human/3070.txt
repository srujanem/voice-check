Evolution favors trades that help a species survive , not traits you as a human deem good . Our intelligence comes at high trade - offs . Most species get several childs and not only one ( or seldom two ) like humans , humans take years to be independent from their parents while in most species they are independent from birth , or after only a short time in most mammals . And here is a big point , the trait to care for their children for a longer time ( several month to years ) evolved in mammals and this allows a longer childhood and a longer learning phase which is necessary to make use of high intelligence . While most Birds ( very close living relatives to dinosaurs ) care for their children , they only do so for a very short amount of time .
