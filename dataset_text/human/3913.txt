First off , soda is hydrating , even if it 's not terribly healthy . Still , it 's worth noting that some liquids that are mostly water are not hydrating . The reasons touch on how the body normally works . Sea water , for instance , is n't good to drink , because it has so much salt . We need the right balance of sodium , chloride , and water to function . Too much sodium ( hypernatremia ) outside of cells leads to water flowing out of cells , resulting in shrinkage and a host of other problems . The brain is particularly sensitive , as sodium is vital to how neurons work -- one symptom of hypernatremia is seizures . Some beverages are diuretics , meaning they stimulate urine production . Alcohol inhibits antidiuretic hormone , which normally makes you hold on to water . Therefore , while vodka is 60 % water , if you drink a lot of it , you 'll wind up thirstier than when you started out , because you 'll pee off lots of free water . On the other hand , weak beer is more than 95 % water , and was one of humanity 's only reliable sources of safe water for thousands of years . Any increase in urine is outweighed by the water . Caffeine is also a weak diuretic , but tolerance builds quickly , so only infrequent consumers are affected . Coffee , tea , and soda are hydrating .
