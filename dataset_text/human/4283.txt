Well , this is kind of an interesting topic . There is some people who absolutely can not eat gluten . This is called [ Coeliac Disease ] ( URL_0 ) . For most everybody else , it is just a normal protein , that is found in wheat and some other grains . Nothing more . There is some slight gluten intolerance , similarly to lactose intolerance too , but those are still in the minority . As many people look for a way to improve their life and find certain things that could be improving their life , they will sometimes blame certain things for their problems , without any real foundation . Since Gluten intolerance is a real problem for some people , a lot of people jumped on the bandwagon and kind of justified it in a way like : " It is bad for some people so it is unnatural " . Thus the trend of Gluten bashing was born . But actually this is quite good in a way too , because people with Coeliacs disease have a way higher variety of foods to choose from , than even a decade ago . So remember , gluten is as harmless as any protein in meat , fish , eggs or anything , except if you have a bad reaction to it . But so are peanuts . Edit : I am not comparing peanut allergies to Coealiacs in terms of severity , it is just a silly analogy
