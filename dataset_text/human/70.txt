India Geographical Position: India is a peninsula in South-East Asia covering 32,87,263 sq. km (2.4% of world total area), ranking 7th largest in area and largest in population. Main land extends from 8°4'N to 37°6'N latitude and 68°7'E to 97°25'E longitude. Land frontier is 15,200 km and total coastline including islands is 7,516.60 km. Indira Point in Great Nicobar is the southernmost tip.
