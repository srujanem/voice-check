Cloud computing means you have a " pool " of resources , like memory or processor power , made up of tons of real life computers somewhere . Now , when you need resources for say , a website , instead of using a single machine , you just take from this " pool " . This method has the advantage of being scalable , meaning , it does n't matter if you want 1 GB of RAM or 128 GB of RAM , you can just take it from the pool , instead of configuring a single computer to do the job .
