Game theory is more of an umbrella topic like " geometry " or " algebra " . It deals with the logic of how games ( or things that can be turned into games ) works . These are logic games usually . Usually it involves other people , and how they react . Like say you and your brother both wanted cookies , but there was only 1 cookie . You could break the cookie up and give him a piece but then he 'll probably get the smaller piece , so he wo nt like that . If he broke the cookie up and gave you a piece you might get the smaller piece . What if you broke the cookie up , but he got to choose the piece he wanted ? All of this is a part of game theory . There are other things like if you and your brother got into a fight and broke the lamp . Then mom came home , and said WHO DID THIS ? If you tell on your brother and he does n't say anything , he gets punished , if you do n't say anything and he does you get punished . However if you both say the other did it , you both get grounded for a month . But if neither of you say anything , you both get in trouble for a day . The chunk of math and logic that deals with stuff like that , is game theory !
