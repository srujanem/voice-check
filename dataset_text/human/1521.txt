There is a hormone in your body called vasopressin ( URL_0 ) . It 's purpose is to regulate how much water is in your system . When you are getting dehydrated , it releases and tells your body not to expel much water in your pee . That 's why your pee is darker when dehydrated ( less water ) . As it turns out , alcohol inhibits vasopressin so your body does n't know that you 're losing water via the many completely clear pees you take . Welp , the next morning when you 're sober and your body realizes how dehydrated you are , a lot of vasopressin is released and you have very dark pee .
