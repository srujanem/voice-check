It will continue to float up until the balloon reaches an altitude at which the air is the same density as the helium and balloon together . That is the maximum height which defines how high it can go . The higher you go , the less air pressure there is , so the helium inside the balloon would expand , stretching your balloon out . It will also escape through the gaps in the rubber polymers . Either your balloon will run out of helium and sink , or it will expand too quickly and pop . It will probably go up a few miles , at least .
