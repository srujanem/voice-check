Prarthana Samaj & Satyashodhak Samaj: Prarthana Samaj was established by Atmaram Panduranga in Mumbai on the principle that service to mankind is service to God, popularized by M.G. Ranade who started a high school for girls. Satyashodhak Samaj was established by Jyotiba Phule, who urged prohibition of liquor, opposed gender inequality and untouchability, opened primary schools for girls, and wrote 'Ghulamagiri' ('Slavery'). His wife Savitribai Phule also worked hard for women's education, starting a girls hostel.
