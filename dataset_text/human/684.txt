Drill and Marching: Open order march (front line 1.5 steps forward, rear line 1.5 steps backward), Closed order march, Right turn, Left turn, Delivering letter/prize to officer, General salute to guest.

Effects of the revolt: The freedom struggle put an end to the administration of the East India Company, and the administration was taken over by to the Queen of England. The administrative responsibility was handed over to the Secretary of Indian Affairs in the British Parliament.
