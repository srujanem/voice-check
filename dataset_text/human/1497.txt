1966, the Indian government initiated the usage of high-yielding variety seeds of wheat in the states of Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Rajasthan and selected districts of Uttar Pradesh. Growing crops using high- yielding grains involves providing specific nutrients, regulated supply of water and protection from diseases. Hence, the use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides and irrigation facilities was also initiated with the usage of high yielding variety seeds. Irrigation facil ities were expanded. As a result, production of wheat increased immensely.
