None of these answers are right , and they are misleading . A server is any computer that is hooked up to the internet to do things . Some servers host websites , some servers host video games , and some servers do nothing but hold information . You could make your own computer a server if you ran a program that hosts one , but doing that is very ineffective because your computer and your internet are not as fast or designed to be a server . Servers for professional uses are individual machines networked together inside a warehouse that does nothing but hold hundreds of thousands of servers . These are usually as small as possible and hooked up to extremely expensive networking equipment on a connection that 's easily ten or more times faster than your download speed and maybe even a thousand times faster than your current uploadload speed . Some servers do the work of dozens of people 's servers . Some servers have extremely expensive , fast processors for doing hard computing , while others simply have massive hard drives for storage . So simply saying that a server is just " a big hard drive " is wrong , but so it just " a really powerful computer . "
