Does the person have a will ? If they do , it forms part of their estate and is distributed . So if they have no relatives , maybe they leave everything to a friend or charity . If the account is owned in joint tenancy with another person , the account automatically transfers to that person by right of survivorship , regardless of whether there 's a will . If there 's no will , the law of the jurisdiction will usually have a breakdown of the " intestacy distribution " . Generally it 's something like parents - > spouse - > kids - > siblings - > cousins . If there are literally no relatives , the state eventually can claim it .
