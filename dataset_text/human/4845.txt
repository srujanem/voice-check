Sharia is simply the idea of basing laws on how Muslims believe the Quran should be interpreted . They believe their god 's laws are supreme over laws of men and should be followed by all citizens in the given country . This naturally evokes a negative reaction from other religious groups . Ironically , these groups may intend to impose their own laws influenced by their own religions . Most Western societies try to use a secular system where no particular religion has any influence over any other in order to make all citizens feel equal under the law .
