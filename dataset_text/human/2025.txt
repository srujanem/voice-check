If you shoot it straight up , it eventually slows , stops , and then falls to the ground , its rate of descent ultimately at its terminal velocity . If you shoot it up but at an angle , it falls ' down ' at up to terminal velocity ( depending on how far it initially traveled up ) but will also have some lateral momentum in the direction you fired it ( i.e. if you shoot it directly to your side , it will fall down at a rate dictated by the acceleration of gravity , but it will be traveling horizontally at ' i shot a gun at you ' speed . Bad idea ) . The latter can certainly be fatal . The former is * probably * not lethal , but definitely unsafe and certainly not worth testing out .
