The purpose is exploration and the testing our capabilities . If at some point in the far future we are going to harvest comets for resources ( which would be more practical for building things in space as it takes far less energy to get resources off a comet as opposed to earth ) this is the first step in a long process of making that happen . Also there is basic exploration . We have never been on a comet before , therefor there is a lot to learn about the makeup and development of comets .
