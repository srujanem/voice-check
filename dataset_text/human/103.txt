The differences among Maratha Chieftains caused the Second Anglo-Maratha War (1803-1805). There was enmity between Yeshwanth Rao Holkar and Daulath Rao Scindia. In 1802, Holkar defeated Scindia and Peshwa Balaji Rao II. The Peshwa appealed to the British for help and accepted the Treaty of Bassein. Lord Wellesley defeated Maratha families between 1803 and 1805.
