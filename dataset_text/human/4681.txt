Fractional reserve banking allows for money creation- this is a tad more complex and the benefits are challenged by more than a few on the relatively far left and right . Way back when , the first bankers were the gold smiths- people entrusted their gold to them because they had the best security for their gold . Maybe they 'd pay a bit for the service . Let 's say one such gold smith had an average of100 lbs of other people 's gold in his vault . Now , this number changes from day to day as people withdraw and deposit- but he notices that never has he had less than 60 lbs . Essentially , this means he only needs to keep 40 lbs of gold on hand- unless something exceptional happens , he 'll be able to give everybody who wants to make a withdrawal their gold . This means he can loan out the remaining 60 , which leads to the benefit described above . But it goes a bit further than that- Let 's say you deposit 10 lbs of gold , and the banker puts 40 % of that aside and loans out the remaining 6 lbs to me . It may not seem like it , but that just created money- sure , there are only 10 lbs of gold in real life , and yet I have 10 lbs of gold and I have 6 , for a total of 16 lbs .
