Typically drivers like the gas cap to be on the side they drive from . This means left - side wheel vehicles often have the tank on the left . Right - side wheel vehicles ( which can include left - side wheel vehicles from manufacturers who sell many of their vehicles in right - side wheel countries ) have the cap on the right .
