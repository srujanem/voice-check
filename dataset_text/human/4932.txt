People pretend they are from a bizarre alien planet or maybe another dimension where things are very different than they are where we live . Then they post problems that they might have if they were really from that kind of place . Sometimes they just make up words that they think sound fun together . Then , people can offer solutions to the problems , also in the character of a strange creature from another world . Humor is derived from the absurdity in the problems and solutions . It is a spinoff of another subreddit , firstworldproblems , in which people make self - deprecating posts about " problems " that they have which in truth only reveal how good they have it compared to those who are suffering from war or malnutrition or other serious problems facing the poor .
