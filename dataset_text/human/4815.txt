Look at the [ definition of degree ] ( URL_0 ) . For measuring , a degree is n't a unit of measurement ( like an inch or a meter is ) , but rather * * it is the marking for the measurement * * . Like how on a yard - stick there is a marking for every inch , on a thermometer there are markings as well . For a thermometer , they set a known point to be the bottom ( let 's say when water freezes ) , and another known point at the top ( let 's say when water boils ) and divided the space between into 100 different pieces with 99 markings between them . When giving a temperature , we say " 17 degrees _ Celsius _ " meaning 17 of those markings from the starting point . Over the years , we 've started to drop the _ Celius _ or _ Fahrenheit _ off because it 's implied . For circles , it 's much the same . Thousands of years ago , the Babylonians divided a circle into 360 pieces and marked off each one . 17 degrees means the same thing it does in temperature : 17 of those markings from the starting point .
