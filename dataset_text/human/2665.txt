Easy answer : its too hot . The deepest man made hole is Kola Superdeep Borehole . That was a Russian experiment to see how deep you could drill into earths crust , reaching down 12,262 meters . At that depth the temperature reached 180 ° C . The scientists estimated that a 15,000 meters depth the temperature would reach 300 ° C , and at that temperature the drill would cease to work and the project was stopped .
