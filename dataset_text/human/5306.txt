India shares land frontier with seven nations. In the North-west, it shares with Afghanistan and Pakistan; in the North with China, Nepal and Bhutan; in the East with Myanmar (Burma) and Bangladesh. Sri Lanka in the south and Maldives in the south-west are also neighbouring countries of India. They have shared water border with India.
