The Sherman was not meant to fight other tanks . It was meant primarily to support infantry formations and destroy enemy fortifications . US armor theory had tank formations primarily used in defeating enemy positions , while rapid and mobile tank destroyer units were meant to engage enemy armor . These units initially had heavy anti tank cannons pulled by trucks , but moved to vehicles like the M10 and M18 . They had light armor , were open top , but fast and had a heavy gun . Obviously in combat the plans often just did not work out and Sherman 's had to engage enemy armor , though more often it was in the form of Pz IV 's and the various German tank destroyer lines , which with the gun upgrades they had installed by the invasion of France the Sherman stood a good chance against .
