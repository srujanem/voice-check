The other answers are right , but here 's an alternative explanation : When you look at .9999999(repeating ) , your brain tricks you into thinking that it 's somehow less than one . But if that were true , then you could subtract .999999(repeating ) from 1 and have something left over . So what happens when we do that ? Well , 1 - 0.9999999 ( repeating ) = 0.000000 ( repeating ) . There will never by a 1 at the end , only 0s . And 0.00000 ( repeating ) is zero .
