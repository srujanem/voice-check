Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel & Princely States: Sardar Patel, the 'Iron Man of India', integrated 562 princely states into the Indian Union under the 1947 Integration Act, offering privy purses to rulers.

Deficit Financing: Deficit financing occurs when government expenditure exceeds revenue. Fiscal Deficit = (Revenue receipts + Non-debt Capital Receipts) – Total Expenditure.
