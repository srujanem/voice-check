Occupied just means it 's under your military control - e.g. Nazi occupied France during WWII . You can also annex land ( as the Germans did with part of the Czech Republic ) and occupy it , but it becomes much the same thing . For land it to become yours ( in a war ) , it needs to be ceded to you through a treaty or instrument of surrender . In rare examples , you may be able to lease some or part of it - this was the arrangement that the UK had with Hong Kong before having to cede it back to the PRC , and the arrangement that the US has with Cuba for Guantanamo Bay .
