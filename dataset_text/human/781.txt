Coal & Petroleum: Coal ('Black Diamond') types: Anthracite, Bituminous, Lignite, Peat (found in Damodar valley, Jharia, Bokaro). Petroleum ('Liquid Gold') first found at Digboi Assam, Ankaleshwar, and Bombay High.

Modern Olympics History: Ancient Olympics in Greece (776 BC - 394 AD). Baron Pierre de Coubertin founded IOC in 1894 in Paris. First Modern Olympics held in Athens in 1896. IOC headquarters in Switzerland. Motto: Citius, Altius, Fortius (Faster, Higher, Stronger). Flag designed 1912 with 5 rings. Torch tradition began 1936 Berlin.
