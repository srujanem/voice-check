Decentralization puts a stop to all kinds of exploitation, upholds human independence and dignity, and nurtures humane values like compassion and co-operation. To enable decentralization, the Panchayat Raj system has been brought into practice. The Indian Government in 1993, through the 73rd Amendment to the Constitution, brought into existence a uniform system of Panchayat Raj institutions
