Too many cars on the road at the same time . Once traffic has hit a saturation point where there are no gaps in which cars can merge , the existing traffic must slow down to provide space for the oncoming traffic . This causes all cars behind to slow down as well . After it repeats a number of times , traffic can slow to a crawl . It happens near the same time because it usually coincides with the time most people are driving to or from work ( many workplaces start at 8 - 9 and end at 5 - 6 ) .
