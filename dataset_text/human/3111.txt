Arresting someone is not just about being within the statute of limitations ; it 's also about evidence . And while I do not discount out of hand the accounts of the various women who have raised allegations , it may just be impossible to gather enough evidence at this late date to convict Mr. Cosby . Plus , he 's not really a flight risk given his age and health , so the attitude may be that the police can get him whenever they want . And he 's highly regarded as well ; no one wants the job of arresting him . It will take some pretty convincing evidence before a DA can be found to press charges .
