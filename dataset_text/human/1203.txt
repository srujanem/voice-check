We do nt fully know how it would happen but the logic is pretty simple as to why it could . If you could get a computer to perfectly simulate a human brain cell , and then extend that to perfectly simulate every brain cell in a humans brain then boom it might have consciousness . Our brain cells follow very simple rules that are very programmable . Out of those simple rules comes our complex consciousness . No reason to think a computer could nt do the same . Obviously though there is a chance there is something more complicated than that to consciousness but I would argue if it happens in the real world we can probably make a simulation of it given a powerful enough computer . There also may be other ways for a computer to achieve consciousness outside of simulating brain cells . We just do nt know very well how self awareness even works .
