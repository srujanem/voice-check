Types of Farming in India: Intensive farming (2-3 crops/year in fertile areas), Subsistence sedentory/shifting farming, Commercial farming (cotton, sugarcane, tobacco), Mixed farming (crops + livestock/poultry), Plantation farming (tea, coffee, rubber).

Drill and Marching: Open order march (front line 1.5 steps forward, rear line 1.5 steps backward), Closed order march, Right turn, Left turn, Delivering letter/prize to officer, General salute to guest.
