Men are perceived as strong , women and children as weak and in need of protection from men . It is traditionally considered honourable to fight the strong and unsportsmanly to fight the weak . For the same reason , it is usually considered less honourable to bomb a hospital than to bomb a stadium , even if the same number of people die . There 's the additional issue that only recently are a handful of nations finally allowing women in combat roles , after a century of them trying , exceptions like Lyudmila Pavlichenko not withstanding ; historically , soldiers have been men . So when you fight men you can claim that you are fighting either actual or potential soldiers -- and fighting soldiers is honourable . Attacking children will always be considered worse than attacking adults , because it is undeniable that children are in every way more vulnerable . Equalising the situation between men and women can be done by allowing women more combat roles and no longer viewing men as strong and women as weak . Both have been goals of the feminist movement since Rosa Luxemburg , and perceptions definitely have changed over the 20th century , but it takes a long time for traditional values to transform .
