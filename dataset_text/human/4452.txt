In the year 1929, under the presidentship of Jawaharlal Nehru, a Congress Session was held in Lahore. During this conference, the Congress declared that ‘Purna Swaraj’ was their aim. On January 26, 1930, the Congress celebrated Independence day all over the country and supported the Lahore declaration.
