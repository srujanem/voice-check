There was a German plan to invade Switzerland and incorporate in into the German Reich and the Italian Empire . The codename was Operation Tannenbaum . It was repeatedly postponed as the troops were needed elsewhere - as other posters noted , the terrain is an impediment to armoured / mechanized warfare , and a very large number of infantry would have been needed . The reason for * that * is that the Swiss were fanatical anti - Nazis who put almost their entire male population under arms and were prepared to fight to the death if an invasion came . The Swiss government communicated to the public that in the case of an invasion , any broadcast claiming that the government had surrendered was to be regarded as enemy propaganda and ignored . To say that invading Switzerland would have served no purpose is rather overstating the point . If the railways and mountain tunnels were captured intact , they would have been useful to the Axis for moving men and materiel . Switzerland was a safe haven for Allied soldiers and airmen . Swiss air defence personnel were ruthless in engaging Axis aircraft that strayed into their airspace but considerably less effective against Allied bombers . The OSS operated relatively freely inside Switzerland . Switzerland sold important military equipment to the Allies . Capturing Switzerland would have served a number of purposes for Germany , not least certain of Hitler 's ideological goals . But it would have been a meatgrinder for the * Wehrmacht * . Stephen P. Halbrook wrote two books on the subject which I highly recommend . * Target Switzerland : Swiss Armed Neutrality in World War II * , and * The Swiss and the Nazis : How the Alpine Republic Survived in the Shadow of the Third Reich * . edit : Remember that " being neutral " did not work for The Netherlands , Belgium , Luxembourg , Denmark or Norway . They were not prepared to defend themselves from the Germans . The Swiss were .
