Lord Cornwallis implemented a new land tax policy in Bengal during 1793 in order to generate steady revenue annually. This was called the Permanent Zamindari System. Under this system, Zamindar became the land owner. The Zamindar was expected to pay the agreed land taxes to the British East India company on a set date every year. He was free to collect any amount of land taxes from the farmers and could retain the excess money collected. This benefitted the Zamindar more.
