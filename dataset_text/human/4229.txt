Each second of a movie contains between 24 - 60 something frames per second , usually 24 - 48 but maybe Peter Jackson will start making animated features , who knows . That is 24 very similar but slightly different versions of a still drawing . This is a little easier with digital techniques but it is still a long and difficult process , requiring a great deal of work not only to make the animation but also to prevent the animation from diverting from the ' true ' representation of the subject . As an example , draw a circle on a piece of paper , then draw another of the same size , then draw another , then another . Now , do that a few thousand times . How many circles are slightly off ? How many have funny bumps , how many are slightly larger , slightly smaller ? Now imagine that your job is to go through someone 's drawings and pick out the ones that go slightly off . Now imagine that you 're the person who has to figure out how to make people drawn on a computer screen seem like human beings , with feelings , voices , weight and depth . It is a great deal more work than picking up an expensive camera and pointing it at a few people who you can just tell to act well .
