Volleyball Fundamental Skills: Floating service (ball travels in zig-zag trajectory), Body turn smash (jumping, turning body in air at jump peak), Blocking (Single, Double, Triple block). Libero players cannot smash or block.

The Moghul sultans, the Nawab of Oudh and other kings were stripped of their kingly status. Consequently, lakhs of soldiers who were dependent on these kings, became unemployed. This became a strong cause for the protest of 1857 against the British.
