One concept to read up on ( if you want more detail ) is the [ optimimum currency area ] ( URL_0 ) which is basically asking the question " What types of regions ought to share currency ? " Robert Mundell came up with a few criteria for such a region , including : 1 . ) Labor mobility ( the same people should be able to get a job anywhere in the region if they want to ) 2 . ) Capital mobility ( you should be able to move money around in the region ) 3 . ) Fiscal transfers ( if , say , one country in the region has some kind of economic shock , the others should pitch in to help out ) People have proposed more criteria . An example of problems that can be caused if you do n't meet all the criteria : A country in the EU has a big crisis ( call it Greece ) . In the olden days , Greece could just print a lot of drachmas and get out of the crisis that way . Now they ca n't , so you need the 3 things above to solve the crisis : 1 ) some Greek people need to move out of Greece and get jobs in other parts of the EU , 2 ) money needs to flow into and out of Greece to allow for efficient investment in places where it 's needed , and 3 ) fiscal transfers to help out in the obvious way .
