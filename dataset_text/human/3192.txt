So the King ( the head of government ) all his ministers and all the representatives are in the same place at the same time . In 1605 , the the King and parliament were all protestants and therefore some Catholics felt like an oppressed minority . A plot emerged to blow up parliament , killing the King and the entire government . The conspirators ( including Guido " Guy " Fawkes ) managed to smuggle hundreds of barrels of gunpowder into the basement . Fawkes was caught on his way to detonate the gunpowder . Modern analysis suggests that had he succeeded , there would have been nothing left but an enormous crater , and the course of European and British history would have been changed forever . That event was on the 5th November 1605 . The fireworks displays and ritual burning of an effigy of Guy Fawkes take place all over the UK , every year as part of the celebrations that the conspirators failed .
