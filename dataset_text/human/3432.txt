Boiled down very far , it is cheaper for a country like Saudi Arabia to produce a barrel of oil than it is for the entire rest of the world ( US Shale included ) . If Saudi Arabia floods the market with oil , the price will drop because so much is available . If the price drops enough , companies will lose money by producing oil , so they will be forced to stop and that leaves the profits to the countries that can still earn money by producing . When enough competition has to quit , they can start shrinking their output which will increase the price . They will be able to control the price of oil and keep it in a region where it maximizes their profits while still keeping others out of market because it is n't worth it for them . For example , Saudi Arabia can create a barrel for say $ 5 where it costs an average US shale company $ 45 dollars . If a barrel sells for $ 100 on the open market , both will make money . If a barrel sells for $ 40 , then only Saudi Arabia makes money . No company will stay in business long if it loses money every time it sells something - either it 'll go broke or shift focus elsewhere . So at $ 100 , both Saudi and US shale sell oil . At $ 40 only Saudi is selling oil . Countries can leverage this against each other because unlike the US , many countries are extremely reliant on oil revenue . As such it is an instrument of power available to those with vast , cheap oil reserves . That 's overly simplified , but is the general concept .
