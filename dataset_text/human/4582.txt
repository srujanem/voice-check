The headphones have a microphone that monitor outside noise . That noise is just sine waves that are made up of peaks and valleys . In order to " cancel out " the noise the headphone process those waves and produce valleys during the peaks and peaks during the valleys . This effectively zeros out the sound to dampen it . Now because it takes time ( though only milliseconds ) to process the signal , it ca n't do this perfectly , however they do a better job at lower frequencies because the wave changes more slowly . Additionally , there are legal reasons that make it so these companies ca n't make their product completely cancel out the human voice ( so you can be alerted during an emergency ) .
