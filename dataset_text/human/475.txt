Technically it 's possible , but unenforceable . In order to get someone to appear in an international human rights court , you first need the co - operation of their home country ( or at least the country they are residing in ) . Without such co - operation , you have no way of compelling the individual to appear in court and you certainly have no way of instituting any form of penalty or sanctions such as imprisonment ( unless of course the individual travels outside the country or countries which are offering him / her protection ) .
