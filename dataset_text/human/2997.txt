Fox News was one of the first major networks to see News as a business rather than as a basic service . They identified their market segment ( a particular subset of religious conservatives ) and pushed it hard . They stopped trying to investigate stories properly and they stopped trying to have balanced news , but they made their slogan " fair and balanced . " Business - wise , it was a major success . MSNBC took a similar strategy and carved out a subset of the US left wing , but since they did it after Fox News , they are n't picked on as much . Basically , Fox News is n't a news program the way we traditionally think of news programs . Instead , it is a news - based entertainment channel with a specific target market . More recently , CNN has become the running joke since , after their market share got cut up from the right ( Fox ) and the left ( MSNBC ) , their balanced approach started to give way to sensationalism . This means that the three big providers of cable news in the USA can all be considered a joke , but Fox News started the trend and has some of the easiest to mock characters , so it gets the brunt of the criticism . It also does n't help them that Colbert and Stewart , two of the most influential political comedians around , got their start around the same time as Fox News took off so they based their format around Fox News and dug right into them .
