The rumour that spread in the army troops at Barackpur led to severe dissatisfaction. When the British officers ordered the Indian soldiers to pull the safety catch with the help of their teeth, the soldiers rejected the command and revolted against their superiors. At this juncture, an Indian soldier named Mangal Pandey killed a British official.

Subhash Chandra Bose & INA: Subhash Chandra Bose secured 4th rank in ICS but joined the freedom struggle, called Netaji. He formed the Congress Socialist Party in 1934 with Nehru, became Congress President in 1938 at Haripur/Tripura, and later formed Forward Bloc in 1939.
