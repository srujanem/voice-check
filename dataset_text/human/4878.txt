Say you 're a baker and you bake cakes . You have two ovens so you can bake two cakes at a time . Sometimes you need to make 3 or 4 cakes at once , but ca n't do that because you only have 2 ovens . So you just bake them one after the other . This is traditional computing . There is a man in town with 50 ovens . Whenever the bakers need to use ovens , they just use his , for a small fee . This way , if we need to bake 4 or 5 cakes at a time , we can , but if we do n't need as many we do n't have to rent as many from the man . Translating ovens to computers , this , in essence , is cloud computing .
