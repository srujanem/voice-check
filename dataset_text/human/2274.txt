Because it was important to that Jesus fulfill the criteria to be Messiah . This means that he had to be descended of the line of David , thus tracing it through Joseph who supposedly is of that line . There were expectations and prophecies concerning just what the Messiah was supposed to be . Much of Christian Theology stems from trying to reconcile Jesus 's death with the expectations of the Messiah . The idea of a spiritual savior and a heavenly kingdom were responses to the fact that Christ failed to meet the expected role of a very real political restoration of the Jewish people .
