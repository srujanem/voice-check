The Marathas captured Savanoor, Gajendraghad, Lakshmeshwara, Hubballi and other places. The combined army marched towards Srirangapattana by capturing fort after fort in 1792. The fort was destroyed during the night. Disturbed by these developments, Tippu Sultan tried to enter into an agreement with the British. It was inevitable for him.
