Government of India Act of 1935 acted as the base for the formation of Indian Constitution. The report submitted in 1928 under the leadership of Motilal Nehru was instrumental in the formation of the act. Most provisions in the Indian Constitution are based on this act. A federal system of Indian Principalities, British governed regions and dominion states was formed. The Reserve Bank of India was established. Dyarchy was established at the centre, while dyarchy was abolished at the provincial level and autonomy was granted. The Federal Court was established.
